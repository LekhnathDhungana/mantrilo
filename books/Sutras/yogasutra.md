# Yogasutra

### 1.1

<vt>Patanjali Yogasutra: Part 1 - Samādhi-pāda - Yoga and its Aims</vt>  
अथ योगानुशासनम् ॥ १.१॥

*<vt>Patanjali Yogasutra: Part 1 - Samādhi-pāda - Yoga and its Aims</vt>  
atha yogānuśāsanam .. 1.1..*



### 1.2

योगश्चित्तवृत्तिनिरोधः ॥ १.२॥

*yogaścittavṛttinirodhaḥ .. 1.2..*

Yoga is holding the mind-stuff (Chitta) back from assuming different forms (Vrittis).

### 1.3

तदा द्रष्टुः स्वरूपेऽवस्थानम् ॥ १.३॥

*tadā draṣṭuḥ svarūpe'vasthānam .. 1.3..*

Then (once all the waves have ended; this has nothing to do with concentration) the seer (Purusha) abides in his own (unmodified) state.

### 1.4

वृत्तिसारूप्यमितरत्र ॥ १.४॥

*vṛttisārūpyamitaratra .. 1.4..*

At other times (apart from concentration) the seer is identified with the modifications.

### 1.5

वृत्तयः पञ्चतय्यः क्लिष्टाऽक्लिष्टाः ॥ १.५॥

*vṛttayaḥ pañcatayyaḥ kliṣṭā'kliṣṭāḥ .. 1.5..*

The modifications fall into five classes, (some) painful and (others) not.

### 1.6

प्रमाणविपर्ययविकल्पनिद्रास्मृतयः ॥ १.६॥

*pramāṇaviparyayavikalpanidrāsmṛtayaḥ .. 1.6..*

(They are) right knowledge, indiscrimination, verbal delusion, sleep, and memory.

### 1.7

प्रत्यक्षानुमानागमाः प्रमाणानि ॥ १.७॥

*pratyakṣānumānāgamāḥ pramāṇāni .. 1.7..*

The proofs are direct perception, inference, and competent evidence.

### 1.8

विपर्ययो मिथ्याज्ञानमतद्रूपप्रतिष्ठम् ॥ १.८॥

*viparyayo mithyājñānamatadrūpapratiṣṭham .. 1.8..*

Indiscrimination is false knowledge, not grounded in the real nature of a thing.

### 1.9

शब्दज्ञानानुपाती वस्तुशून्यो विकल्पः ॥ १.९॥

*śabdajñānānupātī vastuśūnyo vikalpaḥ .. 1.9..*

Verbal delusion arises from words with no (corresponding) reality.

### 1.10

अभावप्रत्ययालम्बना वृत्तिर्निद्रा ॥ १.१०॥

*abhāvapratyayālambanā vṛttirnidrā .. 1.10..*

Sleep is a Vritti that holds the feeling of voidness.

### 1.11

अनुभूतविषयासंप्रमोषः स्मृतिः ॥ १.११॥

*anubhūtaviṣayāsaṃpramoṣaḥ smṛtiḥ .. 1.11..*

Memory occurs when the (Vrittis of) perceived subjects do not slip away (and return to consciousness through impressions).

### 1.12

अभ्यासवैराग्याभ्यां तन्निरोधः ॥ १.१२॥

*abhyāsavairāgyābhyāṃ tannirodhaḥ .. 1.12..*

They are controlled through practice and non-attachment.

### 1.13

तत्र स्थितौ यत्नोऽभ्यासः ॥ १.१३॥

*tatra sthitau yatno'bhyāsaḥ .. 1.13..*

Practice is the continuous struggle to hold them (the Vrittis) fully in check.

### 1.14

स तु दीर्घकालनैरन्तर्यसत्कारासेवितो दृढभूमिः ॥ १.१४॥

*sa tu dīrghakālanairantaryasatkārāsevito dṛḍhabhūmiḥ .. 1.14..*

Long, unbroken effort with great love (for the goal) makes it firmly rooted.

### 1.15

दृष्टानुश्रविकविषयवितृष्णस्य वशीकारसंज्ञा वैराग्यम् ॥ १.१५॥

*dṛṣṭānuśravikaviṣayavitṛṣṇasya vaśīkārasaṃjñā vairāgyam .. 1.15..*

Non-attachment is the state that comes to those who have dropped their thirst for objects, seen or heard of, and that wills to control those objects.

### 1.16

तत्परं पुरुषख्यातेर्गुणवैतृष्ण्यम् ॥ १.१६॥

*tatparaṃ puruṣakhyāterguṇavaitṛṣṇyam .. 1.16..*

Extreme non-attachment gives up even the qualities and arises from knowledge of (the real nature of) the Purusha.

### 1.17

वितर्कविचारानन्दास्मितारूपानुगमात् संप्रज्ञातः ॥ १.१७॥

*vitarkavicārānandāsmitārūpānugamāt saṃprajñātaḥ .. 1.17..*

The concentration called right knowledge is accompanied by reasoning, discrimination, bliss, and unqualified egoism.

### 1.18

विरामप्रत्ययाभ्यासपूर्वः संस्कारशेषोऽन्यः ॥ १.१८॥

*virāmapratyayābhyāsapūrvaḥ saṃskāraśeṣo'nyaḥ .. 1.18..*

Another Samadhi comes through constant practice of stopping all mental activity; in it the Chitta holds only unmanifested impressions.

### 1.19

भवप्रत्ययो विदेहप्रकृतिलयानाम् ॥ १.१९॥

*bhavapratyayo videhaprakṛtilayānām .. 1.19..*

(Without extreme non-attachment, this Samadhi) causes the re-manifestation of the gods and of those merged in nature.

### 1.20

श्रद्धावीर्यस्मृतिसमाधिप्रज्ञापूर्वक इतरेषाम् ॥ १.२०॥

*śraddhāvīryasmṛtisamādhiprajñāpūrvaka itareṣām .. 1.20..*

For others, (this Samadhi) comes through faith, energy, memory, concentration, and discrimination of the real.

### 1.21

तीव्रसंवेगानामासन्नः ॥ १.२१॥

*tīvrasaṃvegānāmāsannaḥ .. 1.21..*

The intensely energetic succeed quickly.

### 1.22

मृदुमध्याधिमात्रत्वात् ततोऽपि विशेषः ॥ १.२२॥

*mṛdumadhyādhimātratvāt tato'pi viśeṣaḥ .. 1.22..*

Yogis' success varies with whether their means are mild, medium, or intense.

### 1.23

ईश्वरप्रणिधानाद्वा ॥ १.२३॥

*īśvarapraṇidhānādvā .. 1.23..*

Or through devotion to Ishvara.

### 1.24

क्लेशकर्मविपाकाशयैरपरामृष्टः पुरुषविशेष ईश्वरः ॥ १.२४॥

*kleśakarmavipākāśayairaparāmṛṣṭaḥ puruṣaviśeṣa īśvaraḥ .. 1.24..*

A particular Purusha, free from misery, karma, its fruits and desire, is Ishvara (the Supreme Ruler).

### 1.25

तत्र निरतिशयं सार्वज्ञबीजम् ॥ १.२५॥

*tatra niratiśayaṃ sārvajñabījam .. 1.25..*

In Him, the all-knowingness that is (only) a germ in others becomes infinite.

### 1.26

स पूर्वेषामपि गुरुः कालेनानवच्छेदात् ॥ १.२६॥

*sa pūrveṣāmapi guruḥ kālenānavacchedāt .. 1.26..*

Not limited by time, He is the Teacher even of the ancient teachers.

### 1.27

तस्य वाचकः प्रणवः ॥ १.२७॥

*tasya vācakaḥ praṇavaḥ .. 1.27..*

Om is the word that manifests Him.

### 1.28

तज्जपस्तदर्थभावनम् ॥ १.२८॥

*tajjapastadarthabhāvanam .. 1.28..*

Repeating this (Om) and meditating on its meaning (is the way).

### 1.29

ततः प्रत्यक्चेतनाधिगमोऽप्यन्तरायाभावश्च ॥ १.२९॥

*tataḥ pratyakcetanādhigamo'pyantarāyābhāvaśca .. 1.29..*

This brings (knowledge of) introspection and destroys obstacles.

### 1.30

व्याधिस्त्यानसंशयप्रमादालस्याविरति-  
भ्रान्तिदर्शनालब्धभूमिकत्वानवस्थितत्वानि  
चित्तविक्षेपास्तेऽन्तरायाः ॥ १.३०॥

*vyādhistyānasaṃśayapramādālasyāvirati-  
bhrāntidarśanālabdhabhūmikatvānavasthitatvāni  
cittavikṣepāste'ntarāyāḥ .. 1.30..*

The distractions that obstruct are disease, mental laziness, doubt, lack of enthusiasm, lethargy, clinging to sense-enjoyments, false perception, failure to reach concentration, and slipping from that state once reached.

### 1.31

दुःखदौर्मनस्याङ्गमेजयत्वश्वासप्रश्वासा विक्षेपसहभुवः ॥ १.३१॥

*duḥkhadaurmanasyāṅgamejayatvaśvāsapraśvāsā vikṣepasahabhuvaḥ .. 1.31..*

Grief, mental distress, trembling of the body, and irregular breathing come with the failure to hold concentration.

### 1.32

तत्प्रतिषेधार्थमेकतत्त्वाभ्यासः ॥ १.३२॥

*tatpratiṣedhārthamekatattvābhyāsaḥ .. 1.32..*

As a remedy, (one should) practise on one subject.

### 1.33

मैत्रीकरुणामुदितोपेक्षाणां सुखदुःखपुण्यापुण्यविषयाणां  
भावनातश्चित्तप्रसादनम् ॥ १.३३॥

*maitrīkaruṇāmuditopekṣāṇāṃ sukhaduḥkhapuṇyāpuṇyaviṣayāṇāṃ  
bhāvanātaścittaprasādanam .. 1.33..*

Thinking with friendship, mercy, gladness, and indifference toward subjects that are happy, unhappy, good, and evil, respectively, calms the Chitta.

### 1.34

प्रच्छर्दनविधारणाभ्यां वा प्राणस्य ॥ १.३४॥

*pracchardanavidhāraṇābhyāṃ vā prāṇasya .. 1.34..*

By expelling and holding the Breath.

### 1.35

विषयवती वा प्रवृत्तिरुत्पन्ना मनसः स्थितिनिबन्धिनी ॥ १.३५॥

*viṣayavatī vā pravṛttirutpannā manasaḥ sthitinibandhinī .. 1.35..*

Forms of concentration that bring extraordinary sense-perceptions make the mind persevere.

### 1.36

विशोका वा ज्योतिष्मती ॥ १.३६॥

*viśokā vā jyotiṣmatī .. 1.36..*

Or (by meditating on) the Effulgent Light, beyond all sorrow.

### 1.37

वीतरागविषयं वा चित्तम् ॥ १.३७॥

*vītarāgaviṣayaṃ vā cittam .. 1.37..*

Or (by meditating on) the heart that has dropped all attachment to sense-objects.

### 1.38

स्वप्ननिद्राज्ञानालम्बनं वा ॥ १.३८॥

*svapnanidrājñānālambanaṃ vā .. 1.38..*

Knowledge arising in sleep, taken as an object of meditation, also serves.

### 1.39

यथाभिमतध्यानाद्वा ॥ १.३९॥

*yathābhimatadhyānādvā .. 1.39..*

Or by meditating on anything one finds good.

### 1.40

परमाणु परममहत्त्वान्तोऽस्य वशीकारः ॥ १.४०॥

*paramāṇu paramamahattvānto'sya vaśīkāraḥ .. 1.40..*

Meditating in this way, the Yogi's mind becomes unobstructed, from the atomic to the infinite.

### 1.41

क्षीणवृत्तेरभिजातस्येव मणेर्ग्रहीतृग्रहणग्राह्येषु  
तत्स्थतदञ्जनता समापत्तिः ॥ १.४१॥

*kṣīṇavṛtterabhijātasyeva maṇergrahītṛgrahaṇagrāhyeṣu  
tatsthatadañjanatā samāpattiḥ .. 1.41..*

The Yogi whose Vrittis have thus lost their power (are controlled) gains concentratedness and sameness in the receiver, (the instrument of) receiving, and the received (the Self, the mind, and external objects), like a crystal (before objects of different colours).

### 1.42

तत्र शब्दार्थज्ञानविकल्पैः संकीर्णा सवितर्का समापत्तिः ॥ १.४२॥

*tatra śabdārthajñānavikalpaiḥ saṃkīrṇā savitarkā samāpattiḥ .. 1.42..*

When sound, meaning, and resulting knowledge are mixed together, it is (called) Samadhi with question.

### 1.43

स्मृतिपरिशुद्धौ स्वरूपशून्येवार्थमात्रनिर्भासा निर्वितर्का ॥ १.४३॥

*smṛtipariśuddhau svarūpaśūnyevārthamātranirbhāsā nirvitarkā .. 1.43..*

The Samadhi called "without question" (comes) when memory is purified, or free of qualities, and expresses only the meaning (of the object meditated on).

### 1.44

एतयैव सविचारा निर्विचारा च सूक्ष्मविषया व्याख्याता ॥ १.४४॥

*etayaiva savicārā nirvicārā ca sūkṣmaviṣayā vyākhyātā .. 1.44..*

This process also explains (the concentrations) with and without discrimination, whose objects are finer.

### 1.45

सूक्ष्मविषयत्वं चालिङ्गपर्यवसानम् ॥ १.४५॥

*sūkṣmaviṣayatvaṃ cāliṅgaparyavasānam .. 1.45..*

Pradhāna is the limit of the finer objects.

### 1.46

ता एव सबीजः समाधिः ॥ १.४६॥

*tā eva sabījaḥ samādhiḥ .. 1.46..*

These concentrations have seed.

### 1.47

निर्विचारवैशारद्येऽध्यात्मप्रसादः ॥ १.४७॥

*nirvicāravaiśāradye'dhyātmaprasādaḥ .. 1.47..*

When the concentration "without discrimination" is purified, the Chitta becomes firmly fixed.

### 1.48

ऋतम्भरा तत्र प्रज्ञा ॥ १.४८॥

*ṛtambharā tatra prajñā .. 1.48..*

The knowledge in that state is called "filled with Truth."

### 1.49

श्रुतानुमानप्रज्ञाभ्यामन्यविषया विशेषार्थत्वात् ॥ १.४९॥

*śrutānumānaprajñābhyāmanyaviṣayā viśeṣārthatvāt .. 1.49..*

Knowledge from testimony and inference concerns common objects. Knowledge from the Samadhi just mentioned is far higher, reaching where inference and testimony cannot go.

### 1.50

तज्जः संस्कारोऽन्यसंस्कारप्रतिबन्धी ॥ १.५०॥

*tajjaḥ saṃskāro'nyasaṃskārapratibandhī .. 1.50..*

The impression left by this Samadhi blocks all other impressions.

### 1.51

तस्यापि निरोधे सर्वनिरोधान्निर्बीजः समाधिः ॥ १.५१॥  
॥ इति पतञ्जलि-विरचिते योग-सूत्रे प्रथमः समाधि-पादः ॥

*tasyāpi nirodhe sarvanirodhānnirbījaḥ samādhiḥ .. 1.51..  
.. iti patañjali-viracite yoga-sūtre prathamaḥ samādhi-pādaḥ ..*

When even this (impression, which blocks all others) is restrained, everything is restrained, and the "seedless" Samadhi comes.

### 2.1

<vt>Patanjali Yogasutra: Part 2 - Sādhana-pāda - Yoga and its Practice</vt>  
तपःस्वाध्यायेश्वरप्रणिधानानि क्रियायोगः ॥ २.१॥

*<vt>Patanjali Yogasutra: Part 2 - Sādhana-pāda - Yoga and its Practice</vt>  
tapaḥsvādhyāyeśvarapraṇidhānāni kriyāyogaḥ .. 2.1..*

Kriyā-Yoga consists of mortification, study, and offering the results of one's work to God.

### 2.2

समाधिभावनार्थः क्लेशतनूकरणार्थश्च ॥ २.२॥

*samādhibhāvanārthaḥ kleśatanūkaraṇārthaśca .. 2.2..*

(It serves) the practice of Samadhi and the reduction of the pain-bearing obstructions.

### 2.3

अविद्यास्मितारागद्वेषाभिनिवेशाः क्लेशाः ॥ २.३॥

*avidyāsmitārāgadveṣābhiniveśāḥ kleśāḥ .. 2.3..*

Ignorance, egoism, attachment, aversion and clinging to life: these are the obstructions that bring pain.

### 2.4

अविद्या क्षेत्रमुत्तरेषां प्रसुप्ततनुविच्छिन्नोदाराणाम् ॥ २.४॥

*avidyā kṣetramuttareṣāṃ prasuptatanuvicchinnodārāṇām .. 2.4..*

Ignorance is the field in which all the others grow, whether dormant, weakened, overpowered, or expanded.

### 2.5

अनित्याशुचिदुःखानात्मसु नित्यशुचिसुखात्मख्यातिरविद्या ॥ २.५॥

*anityāśuciduḥkhānātmasu nityaśucisukhātmakhyātiravidyā .. 2.5..*

Ignorance mistakes the non-eternal, impure, painful, and non-Self for the eternal, pure, happy, and the Atman or Self (respectively).

### 2.6

दृग्दर्शनशक्त्योरेकात्मतेवास्मिता ॥ २.६॥

*dṛgdarśanaśaktyorekātmatevāsmitā .. 2.6..*

Egoism is identifying the seer with the instrument of seeing.

### 2.7

सुखानुशयी रागः ॥ २.७॥

*sukhānuśayī rāgaḥ .. 2.7..*

Attachment is what dwells on pleasure.

### 2.8

दुःखानुशयी द्वेषः ॥ २.८॥

*duḥkhānuśayī dveṣaḥ .. 2.8..*

Aversion is what dwells on pain.

### 2.9

स्वरसवाही विदुषोऽपि तथारूढोऽभिनिवेशः ॥ २.९॥

*svarasavāhī viduṣo'pi tathārūḍho'bhiniveśaḥ .. 2.9..*

Clinging to life flows by its own nature and is established even in the learned.

### 2.10

ते प्रतिप्रसवहेयाः सूक्ष्माः ॥ २.१०॥

*te pratiprasavaheyāḥ sūkṣmāḥ .. 2.10..*

The fine Samskaras are overcome by resolving them into their causal state.

### 2.11

ध्यानहेयास्तद्वृत्तयः ॥ २.११॥

*dhyānaheyāstadvṛttayaḥ .. 2.11..*

Their gross modifications are to be cast off through meditation.

### 2.12

क्लेशमूलः कर्माशयो दृष्टादृष्टजन्मवेदनीयः ॥ २.१२॥

*kleśamūlaḥ karmāśayo dṛṣṭādṛṣṭajanmavedanīyaḥ .. 2.12..*

These pain-bearing obstructions are the root of the "receptacle of works," which is experienced in this visible life or in the unseen life.

### 2.13

सति मूले तद्विपाको जात्यायुर्भोगाः ॥ २.१३॥

*sati mūle tadvipāko jātyāyurbhogāḥ .. 2.13..*

While the root remains, it ripens as species, life, and the experience of pleasure and pain.

### 2.14

ते ह्लादपरितापफलाः पुण्यापुण्यहेतुत्वात् ॥ २.१४॥

*te hlādaparitāpaphalāḥ puṇyāpuṇyahetutvāt .. 2.14..*

Virtue yields them as pleasure; vice yields them as pain.

### 2.15

परिणामतापसंस्कारदुःखैर्गुणवृत्तिविरोधाच्च  
दुःखमेव सर्वं विवेकिनः ॥ २.१५॥

*pariṇāmatāpasaṃskāraduḥkhairguṇavṛttivirodhācca  
duḥkhameva sarvaṃ vivekinaḥ .. 2.15..*

For the discriminating, everything is painful, as it were. All things bring pain as a consequence, as anticipated loss of happiness, as new craving born of impressions of happiness, and as the counteraction of qualities.

### 2.16

हेयं दुःखमनागतम् ॥ २.१६॥

*heyaṃ duḥkhamanāgatam .. 2.16..*

Misery yet to come is to be avoided.

### 2.17

द्रष्टृदृश्ययोः संयोगो हेयहेतुः ॥ २.१७॥

*draṣṭṛdṛśyayoḥ saṃyogo heyahetuḥ .. 2.17..*

What is to be avoided is caused by the junction of seer and seen.

### 2.18

प्रकाशक्रियास्थितिशीलं भूतेन्द्रियात्मकं  
भोगापवर्गार्थं दृश्यम् ॥ २.१८॥

*prakāśakriyāsthitiśīlaṃ bhūtendriyātmakaṃ  
bhogāpavargārthaṃ dṛśyam .. 2.18..*

The experienced consists of elements and organs. Its nature is illumination, action and inertia, and it exists for the experience and release of the experiencer.

### 2.19

विशेषाविशेषलिङ्गमात्रालिङ्गानि गुणपर्वाणि ॥ २.१९॥

*viśeṣāviśeṣaliṅgamātrāliṅgāni guṇaparvāṇi .. 2.19..*

The qualities have four states: defined, undefined, indicated only, and signless.

### 2.20

द्रष्टा दृशिमात्रः शुद्धोऽपि प्रत्ययानुपश्यः ॥ २.२०॥

*draṣṭā dṛśimātraḥ śuddho'pi pratyayānupaśyaḥ .. 2.20..*

The seer is intelligence alone. Though pure, it sees by way of the colouring of the intellect.

### 2.21

तदर्थ एव दृश्यस्यात्मा ॥ २.२१॥

*tadartha eva dṛśyasyātmā .. 2.21..*

The experienced exists, by its nature, for him.

### 2.22

कृतार्थं प्रति नष्टमप्यनष्टं तदन्यसाधारणत्वात् ॥ २.२२॥

*kṛtārthaṃ prati naṣṭamapyanaṣṭaṃ tadanyasādhāraṇatvāt .. 2.22..*

For one who has reached the goal it is destroyed; yet it is not destroyed, being shared with others.

### 2.23

स्वस्वामिशक्त्योः स्वरूपोपलब्धिहेतुः संयोगः ॥ २.२३॥

*svasvāmiśaktyoḥ svarūpopalabdhihetuḥ saṃyogaḥ .. 2.23..*

Junction brings about realisation of the nature of both powers, the experienced and its Lord.

### 2.24

तस्य हेतुरविद्या ॥ २.२४॥

*tasya heturavidyā .. 2.24..*

Its cause is ignorance.

### 2.25

तदभावात् संयोगाभावो हानं तद्दृशेः कैवल्यम् ॥ २.२५॥

*tadabhāvāt saṃyogābhāvo hānaṃ taddṛśeḥ kaivalyam .. 2.25..*

Without ignorance there is no junction, the thing to be avoided. That is the independence of the seer.

### 2.26

विवेकख्यातिरविप्लवा हानोपायः ॥ २.२६॥

*vivekakhyātiraviplavā hānopāyaḥ .. 2.26..*

Ignorance is destroyed by unbroken practice of discrimination.

### 2.27

तस्य सप्तधा प्रान्तभूमिः प्रज्ञा ॥ २.२७॥

*tasya saptadhā prāntabhūmiḥ prajñā .. 2.27..*

His knowledge reaches the sevenfold highest ground.

### 2.28

योगाङ्गानुष्ठानादशुद्धिक्षये ज्ञानदीप्तिरा विवेकख्यातेः ॥ २.२८॥

*yogāṅgānuṣṭhānādaśuddhikṣaye jñānadīptirā vivekakhyāteḥ .. 2.28..*

As practice of the various parts of Yoga destroys impurities, knowledge grows radiant, up to discrimination.

### 2.29

यमनियमासनप्राणायामप्रत्याहारधारणाध्यानसमाधयोऽष्टावङ्गानि ॥ २.२९॥

*yamaniyamāsanaprāṇāyāmapratyāhāradhāraṇādhyānasamādhayo'ṣṭāvaṅgāni .. 2.29..*

The eight limbs of Yoga are Yama, Niyama, Āsana, Prānāyāma, Pratyāhāra, Dhāranā, Dhyāna and Samādhi.

### 2.30

अहिंसासत्यास्तेयब्रह्मचर्यापरिग्रहा यमाः ॥ २.३०॥

*ahiṃsāsatyāsteyabrahmacaryāparigrahā yamāḥ .. 2.30..*

The Yamas are non-killing, truthfulness, non-stealing, continence and non-receiving.

### 2.31

जातिदेशकालसमयानवच्छिन्नाः सार्वभौमा महाव्रतम् ॥ २.३१॥

*jātideśakālasamayānavacchinnāḥ sārvabhaumā mahāvratam .. 2.31..*

When not limited by time, place, purpose or caste rules, these are the universal great vows.

### 2.32

शौचसंतोषतपःस्वाध्यायेश्वरप्रणिधानानि नियमाः ॥ २.३२॥

*śaucasaṃtoṣatapaḥsvādhyāyeśvarapraṇidhānāni niyamāḥ .. 2.32..*

The Niyamas are internal and external purification, contentment, mortification, study and worship of God.

### 2.33

वितर्कबाधने प्रतिपक्षभावनम् ॥ २.३३॥

*vitarkabādhane pratipakṣabhāvanam .. 2.33..*

To block thoughts hostile to Yoga, bring up opposing thoughts.

### 2.34

वितर्का हिंसादयः कृतकारितानुमोदिता लोभक्रोधमोहपूर्वका  
मृदुमध्याधिमात्रा दुःखाज्ञानानन्तफला इति प्रतिपक्षभावनम् ॥ २.३४॥

*vitarkā hiṃsādayaḥ kṛtakāritānumoditā lobhakrodhamohapūrvakā  
mṛdumadhyādhimātrā duḥkhājñānānantaphalā iti pratipakṣabhāvanam .. 2.34..*

Killing, falsehood and the like obstruct Yoga, whether done, caused or approved; whether from avarice, anger or ignorance; whether slight, middling or great. They end in infinite ignorance and misery. Reflecting so is the method of thinking the contrary.

### 2.35

अहिंसाप्रतिष्ठायां तत्सन्निधौ वैरत्यागः ॥ २.३५॥

*ahiṃsāpratiṣṭhāyāṃ tatsannidhau vairatyāgaḥ .. 2.35..*

Once non-killing is established, all enmity in others ceases in his presence.

### 2.36

सत्यप्रतिष्ठायां क्रियाफलाश्रयत्वम् ॥ २.३६॥

*satyapratiṣṭhāyāṃ kriyāphalāśrayatvam .. 2.36..*

Established in truthfulness, the Yogi gains the power to obtain the fruits of work, for himself and others, without the works.

### 2.37

अस्तेयप्रतिष्ठायां सर्वरत्नोपस्थानम् ॥ २.३७॥

*asteyapratiṣṭhāyāṃ sarvaratnopasthānam .. 2.37..*

Once non-stealing is established, all wealth comes to the Yogi.

### 2.38

ब्रह्मचर्यप्रतिष्ठायां वीर्यलाभः ॥ २.३८॥

*brahmacaryapratiṣṭhāyāṃ vīryalābhaḥ .. 2.38..*

Once continence is established, energy is gained.

### 2.39

अपरिग्रहस्थैर्ये जन्मकथंतासम्बोधः ॥ २.३९॥

*aparigrahasthairye janmakathaṃtāsambodhaḥ .. 2.39..*

Fixed in non-receiving, he gains memory of past life.

### 2.40

शौचात् स्वाङ्गजुगुप्सा परैरसंसर्गः ॥ २.४०॥

*śaucāt svāṅgajugupsā parairasaṃsargaḥ .. 2.40..*

Once internal and external cleanliness are established, disgust for one's own body arises, along with non-intercourse with others.

### 2.41

सत्त्वशुद्धि सौमनस्यैकाग्र्येन्द्रिय जयात्मदर्शन-योग्यत्वानि च ॥ २.४१॥

*sattvaśuddhi saumanasyaikāgryendriya jayātmadarśana-yogyatvāni ca .. 2.41..*

Purification of the Sattva also arises, as do cheerfulness of mind, concentration, mastery of the organs, and fitness to realise the Self.

### 2.42

संतोषादनुत्तमसुखलाभः ॥ २.४२॥

*saṃtoṣādanuttamasukhalābhaḥ .. 2.42..*

Contentment brings supreme happiness.

### 2.43

कायेन्द्रियसिद्धिरशुद्धिक्षयात् तपसः ॥ २.४३॥

*kāyendriyasiddhiraśuddhikṣayāt tapasaḥ .. 2.43..*

Mortification destroys impurity and so brings powers to the organs and body.

### 2.44

स्वाध्यायाद् इष्टदेवतासंप्रयोगः ॥ २.४४॥

*svādhyāyād iṣṭadevatāsaṃprayogaḥ .. 2.44..*

Repeating the Mantra brings realisation of the intended deity.

### 2.45

समाधिसिद्धिरीश्वरप्रणिधानात् ॥ २.४५॥

*samādhisiddhirīśvarapraṇidhānāt .. 2.45..*

Samadhi comes through sacrificing all to Ishvara.

### 2.46

स्थिरसुखम् आसनम् ॥ २.४६॥

*sthirasukham āsanam .. 2.46..*

Posture is what is firm and pleasant.

### 2.47

प्रयत्नशैथिल्यानन्तसमापत्तिभ्याम् ॥ २.४७॥

*prayatnaśaithilyānantasamāpattibhyām .. 2.47..*

Posture becomes firm and pleasant by lessening the natural tendency to restlessness and meditating on the unlimited.

### 2.48

ततो द्वन्द्वानभिघातः ॥ २.४८॥

*tato dvandvānabhighātaḥ .. 2.48..*

When the seat is mastered, dualities no longer obstruct.

### 2.49

तस्मिन्सति श्वासप्रश्वासयोर्गतिविच्छेदः प्राणायामः ॥ २.४९॥

*tasminsati śvāsapraśvāsayorgativicchedaḥ prāṇāyāmaḥ .. 2.49..*

After this comes control of the movement of exhalation and inhalation.

### 2.50

बाह्याभ्यन्तरस्तम्भवृत्तिर्देशकालसंख्याभिः  
परिदृष्टो दीर्घसूक्ष्मः ॥ २.५०॥

*bāhyābhyantarastambhavṛttirdeśakālasaṃkhyābhiḥ  
paridṛṣṭo dīrghasūkṣmaḥ .. 2.50..*

Its modifications are external, internal or motionless; they are regulated by place, time and number, and are long or short.

### 2.51

बाह्याभ्यन्तरविषयाक्षेपी चतुर्थः ॥ २.५१॥

*bāhyābhyantaraviṣayākṣepī caturthaḥ .. 2.51..*

The fourth restrains the Prana through reflection on an external or internal object.

### 2.52

ततः क्षीयते प्रकाशावरणम् ॥ २.५२॥

*tataḥ kṣīyate prakāśāvaraṇam .. 2.52..*

From that, the covering over the light of the Chitta thins away.

### 2.53

धारणासु च योग्यता मनसः ॥ २.५३॥

*dhāraṇāsu ca yogyatā manasaḥ .. 2.53..*

The mind grows fit for Dharana.

### 2.54

स्वविषयासंप्रयोगे चित्तस्वरूपानुकार इवेन्द्रियाणां प्रत्याहारः ॥ २.५४॥

*svaviṣayāsaṃprayoge cittasvarūpānukāra ivendriyāṇāṃ pratyāhāraḥ .. 2.54..*

The organs are drawn in when they leave their own objects and take on, as it were, the form of the mind-stuff.

### 2.55

ततः परमा वश्यतेन्द्रियाणाम् ॥ २.५५॥  
॥ इति पतञ्जलि-विरचिते योग-सूत्रे द्वितीयः साधन-पादः ॥

*tataḥ paramā vaśyatendriyāṇām .. 2.55..  
.. iti patañjali-viracite yoga-sūtre dvitīyaḥ sādhana-pādaḥ ..*

From that comes supreme mastery of the organs.

### 3.1

<vt>Patanjali Yogasutra: Part 3 - Vibhūti-Pāda - Powers</vt>  
देशबन्धश्चित्तस्य धारणा ॥ ३.१॥

*<vt>Patanjali Yogasutra: Part 3 - Vibhūti-Pāda - Powers</vt>  
deśabandhaścittasya dhāraṇā .. 3.1..*

Dhāranā is fixing the mind on a particular object.

### 3.2

तत्र प्रत्ययैकतानता ध्यानम् ॥ ३.२॥

*tatra pratyayaikatānatā dhyānam .. 3.2..*

Dhyāna is an unbroken flow of knowledge in that object.

### 3.3

तदेवार्थमात्रनिर्भासं स्वरूपशून्यमिव समाधिः ॥ ३.३॥

*tadevārthamātranirbhāsaṃ svarūpaśūnyamiva samādhiḥ .. 3.3..*

When that drops all forms and reflects the meaning alone, it is Samādhi.

### 3.4

त्रयमेकत्र संयमः ॥ ३.४॥

*trayamekatra saṃyamaḥ .. 3.4..*

These three, practised on one object, are Samyama.

### 3.5

तज्जयात्प्रज्ञालोकः ॥ ३.५॥

*tajjayātprajñālokaḥ .. 3.5..*

Mastery of that brings the light of knowledge.

### 3.6

तस्य भूमिषु विनियोगः ॥ ३.६॥

*tasya bhūmiṣu viniyogaḥ .. 3.6..*

It should be applied in stages.

### 3.7

त्रयमन्तरङ्गं पूर्वेभ्यः ॥ ३.७॥

*trayamantaraṅgaṃ pūrvebhyaḥ .. 3.7..*

These three are more internal than the preceding ones.

### 3.8

तदपि बहिरङ्गं निर्बीजस्य ॥ ३.८॥

*tadapi bahiraṅgaṃ nirbījasya .. 3.8..*

Yet even they are external to seedless Samadhi.

### 3.9

व्युत्थाननिरोधसंस्कारयोरभिभवप्रादुर्भावौ  
निरोधक्षणचित्तान्वयो निरोधपरिणामः ॥ ३.९॥

*vyutthānanirodhasaṃskārayorabhibhavaprādurbhāvau  
nirodhakṣaṇacittānvayo nirodhapariṇāmaḥ .. 3.9..*

When the disturbed impressions of the mind are suppressed and impressions of control arise, the mind persisting in that moment of control is said to attain the controlling modifications.

### 3.10

तस्य प्रशान्तवाहिता संस्कारात् ॥ ३.१०॥

*tasya praśāntavāhitā saṃskārāt .. 3.10..*

Habit steadies its flow.

### 3.11

सर्वार्थतैकाग्रतयोः क्षयोदयौ चित्तस्य समाधिपरिणामः ॥ ३.११॥

*sarvārthataikāgratayoḥ kṣayodayau cittasya samādhipariṇāmaḥ .. 3.11..*

When the power of taking in all sorts of objects is destroyed and the power of concentrating on one object is manifested, the Chitta gains the modification called Samadhi.

### 3.12

ततः पुनः शान्तोदितौ तुल्यप्रत्ययौ चित्तस्यैकाग्रतापरिणामः ॥ ३.१२॥

*tataḥ punaḥ śāntoditau tulyapratyayau cittasyaikāgratāpariṇāmaḥ .. 3.12..*

The Chitta is one-pointed when the past impression and the present one are alike.

### 3.13

एतेन भूतेन्द्रियेषु धर्मलक्षणावस्थापरिणामा व्याख्याताः ॥ ३.१३॥

*etena bhūtendriyeṣu dharmalakṣaṇāvasthāpariṇāmā vyākhyātāḥ .. 3.13..*

This explains the threefold transformation of form, time and state, in fine or gross matter and in the organs.

### 3.14

शान्तोदिताव्यपदेश्य धर्मानुपाती धर्मी ॥ ३.१४॥

*śāntoditāvyapadeśya dharmānupātī dharmī .. 3.14..*

The qualified is what transformation acts upon, whether past, present or yet to be manifested.

### 3.15

क्रमान्यत्वं परिणामान्यत्वे हेतुः ॥ ३.१५॥

*kramānyatvaṃ pariṇāmānyatve hetuḥ .. 3.15..*

The succession of changes causes manifold evolution.

### 3.16

परिणामत्रयसंयमाद् अतीतानागतज्ञानम् ॥ ३.१६॥

*pariṇāmatrayasaṃyamād atītānāgatajñānam .. 3.16..*

Samyama on the three kinds of change gives knowledge of past and future.

### 3.17

शब्दार्थप्रत्ययानामित रेतराध्यासात्  
सङ्करस्तत्प्रविभाग संयमात्सर्वभूतरुतज्ञानम् ॥ ३.१७॥

*śabdārthapratyayānāmita retarādhyāsāt  
saṅkarastatpravibhāgasaṃyamātsarvabhūtarutajñānam .. 3.17..*

Word, meaning, and knowledge are ordinarily confused. Samyama on them gives knowledge of all animal sounds.

### 3.18

संस्कारसाक्षात्करणात्पूर्वजातिज्ञानम् ॥ ३.१८॥

*saṃskārasākṣātkaraṇāt pūrvajātijñānam .. 3.18..*

Perceiving the impressions gives knowledge of past life.

### 3.19

प्रत्ययस्य परचित्तज्ञानम् ॥ ३.१९॥

*pratyayasya paracittajñānam .. 3.19..*

Samyama on the signs in another person's body gives knowledge of that person's mind.

### 3.20

न च तत्सालम्बनं तस्याविषयीभूतत्वात् ॥ ३.२०॥

*na ca tatsālambanaṃ tasyāviṣayībhūtatvāt .. 3.20..*

But not of the mind's contents, since those are not the object of the Samyama.

### 3.21

कायरूपसंयमात्तद्ग्राह्यशक्तिस्तम्भे  
चक्षुःप्रकाशासंप्रयोगेऽन्तर्धानम् ॥ ३.२१॥

*kāyarūpasaṃyamāttadgrāhyaśaktistambhe  
cakṣuḥprakāśāsaṃprayoge'ntardhānam .. 3.21..*

With Samyama on the form of the body, its perceptibility is blocked and the power of manifestation in the eye is cut off, so the Yogi's body becomes unseen.

### 3.22

एतेन शब्दाद्यन्तर्धानमुक्तम्॥३.२२॥

*etena śabdādyantardhānamuktam .. 3.22..*

This also explains how spoken words and similar things can disappear or be concealed.

### 3.23

सोपक्रमं निरुपक्रमं च कर्म  
तत्संयमादपरान्तज्ञानमरिष्टेभ्यो वा ॥

*sopakramaṃ nirupakramaṃ ca karma  
tatsaṃyamādaparāntajñānamariṣṭebhyo vā .. 3.23..*

Karma is of two kinds: soon to bear fruit and late to bear fruit. Through Samyama on these, or through the portents called Arishta, Yogis know the exact time they will leave their bodies.

### 3.24

मैत्र्यादिषु बलानि ॥

*maitryādiṣu balāni .. 3.24..*

Samyama on friendship, mercy, and the like (I.33) makes the Yogi excel in those qualities.

### 3.25

बलेषु हस्तिबलादीनि ॥

*baleṣu hastibalādīni .. 3.25..*

Samyama on the strength of the elephant and other creatures brings their strength to the Yogi.

### 3.26

प्रवृत्त्यालोक न्यासात्सूक्ष्म व्यवहितविप्रकृष्टज्ञानम् ॥

*pravṛttyālokanya asātsūkṣma vyavahitaviprakṛṣṭajñānam .. 3.26..*

Samyama on the Effulgent Light (I.36) gives knowledge of what is fine, obstructed, and remote.

### 3.27

भुवनज्ञानं सूर्ये संयमात् ॥

*bhuvanajñānaṃ sūrye saṃyamāt .. 3.27..*

Samyama on the sun gives knowledge of the world.

### 3.28

चन्द्रे ताराव्यूहज्ञानम् ॥

*candre tārāvyūhajñānam .. 3.28..*

On the moon: knowledge of the clusters of stars.

### 3.29

ध्रुवे तद्गतिज्ञानम् ॥

*dhruve tadgatijñānam .. 3.29..*

On the pole-star: knowledge of the movements of the stars.

### 3.30

नाभिचक्रे कायव्यूहज्ञानम् ॥

*nābhicakre kāyavyūhajñānam .. 3.30..*

On the navel circle: knowledge of how the body is constituted.

### 3.31

कण्ठकूपे क्षुत्पिपासानिवृत्तिः ॥

*kaṇṭhakūpe kṣutpipāsānivṛttiḥ .. 3.31..*

On the hollow of the throat: hunger ceases.

### 3.32

कूर्मनाड्यां स्थैर्यम् ॥

*kūrmanāḍyāṃ sthairyam .. 3.32..*

On the nerve called Kurma: the body becomes fixed.

### 3.33

मूर्धज्योतिषि सिद्धदर्शनम् ॥

*mūrdhajyotiṣi siddhadarśanam .. 3.33..*

On the light coming from the top of the head: sight of the Siddhas.

### 3.34

प्रातिभाद्वा सर्वम् ॥

*prātibhādvā sarvam .. 3.34..*

Or all knowledge, through the power of Prātibha.

### 3.35

हृदये चित्तसंवित् ॥

*hṛdaye cittasaṃvit .. 3.35..*

In the heart: knowledge of minds.

### 3.36

सत्त्व पुरुषयोरत्यन्तासंकीर्णयोः प्रत्ययाविशेषो भोगः  
परार्थत्वात्स्वार्थसंयमात्पुरुषज्ञानम् ॥

*sattva puruṣayoratyanta asaṃkīrṇayoḥ pratyayāviśeṣo bhogaḥ  
parārthatvātsvārthasaṃyamātpuruṣajñānam .. 3.36..*

Enjoyment arises from failing to distinguish the soul from Sattva, which are wholly different, since Sattva acts for another. Samyama on the self-centred one gives knowledge of the Purusha.

### 3.37

ततः प्रातिभश्रावण वेदनादर्शास्वादवार्ता जायन्ते ॥

*tataḥ prātibhaśrāvaṇa vedanādarśāsvādavārtā jāyante .. 3.37..*

From that arise the knowledge belonging to Pratibha and supernatural hearing, touch, sight, taste, and smell.

### 3.38

ते समाधावुपसर्गा व्युत्थाने सिद्धयः ॥

*te samādhāvupasargā vyutthāne siddhayaḥ .. 3.38..*

These obstruct Samadhi, but in the worldly state they are powers.

### 3.39

बन्ध कारणशैथिल्यात्प्रचार संवेदनाच्च  
चित्तस्य परशरीरावेशः ॥

*bandha kāraṇaśaithilyātpracāra saṃvedanācca  
cittasya paraśarīrāveśaḥ .. 3.39..*

When the cause of the Chitta's bondage has loosened, the Yogi, knowing its channels of activity (the nerves), enters another's body.

### 3.40

उदानजयाज्जल पङ्ककण्टकादिष्वसङ्ग उत्क्रान्तिश्च ॥

*udānajayājjala paṅkakaṇṭakādiṣvasaṅga utkrāntiśca .. 3.40..*

Conquering the current called Udāna, the Yogi does not sink in water or swamps, can walk on thorns and the like, and can die at will.

### 3.41

समानजयाज्ज्वलनम् ॥

*samānajayājjvalanam .. 3.41..*

Conquering the current Samāna, he is surrounded by a blaze of light.

### 3.42

श्रोत्राकाशयोः सम्बन्धसंयमाद्दिव्यं श्रोत्रम् ॥

*śrotrākāśayoḥ sambandhasaṃyamāddivyaṃ śrotram .. 3.42..*

Samyama on the relation between the ear and the Akasha gives divine hearing.

### 3.43

कायाकाशयोः सम्बन्धसंयमाल्लघुतूल-  
समापत्तेश्चाकाशगमनम् ॥

*kāyākāśayoḥ sambandhasaṃyamāllaghutūla-  
samāpatteścākāśagamanam .. 3.43..*

Through Samyama on the relation between the Akasha and the body, and by becoming as light as cotton-wool and the like through meditating on them, the Yogi travels through the skies.

### 3.44

बहिरकल्पिता वृत्तिर्महाविदेहा ततः प्रकाशावरणक्षयः ॥

*bahirakalpitā vṛttirmahāvidehā tataḥ prakāśāvaraṇakṣayaḥ .. 3.44..*

Samyama on the "real modifications" of the mind outside the body, called great disembodiedness, removes the covering over light.

### 3.45

स्थूल स्वरूपसूक्ष्मान्वयार्थ वत्त्वसंयमाद्भूतजयः ॥

*sthūla svarūpasūkṣma anvayārtha vattvasaṃyamādbhūtajayaḥ .. 3.45..*

Mastery of the elements comes from Samyama on their gross and fine forms, their essential traits, the Gunas inherent in them, and their contribution to the soul's experience.

### 3.46

ततोऽणिमादिप्रादुर्भावः कायसम्पत्तद्धर्मानभिघातश्च ॥

*tato'ṇimādiprādurbhāvaḥ kāyasampattaddharmānabhighātaśca .. 3.46..*

From that come minuteness and the other powers, the "glorification of the body," and the indestructibility of the body's qualities.

### 3.47

रूपलावण्य बलवज्रसंहननत्वानि कायसम्पत् ॥

*rūpalāvaṇya balavajra saṃhananatvāni kāyasampat .. 3.47..*

The "glorification of the body" means beauty, complexion, strength, and adamantine hardness.

### 3.48

ग्रहण स्वरूपा स्मितान्वयार्थवत्त्व संयमादिन्द्रियजयः ॥

*grahaṇa svarūpa asmitānvayārthavattva saṃyamādindriyajayaḥ .. 3.48..*

Conquest of the organs comes from Samyama on their objectivity and power of illumination, on egoism, on the Gunas inherent in them, and on their contribution to the soul's experience.

### 3.49

ततो मनोजवित्वं विकरणभावः प्रधानजयश्च ॥

*tato manojavitvaṃ vikaraṇabhāvaḥ pradhānajayaśca .. 3.49..*

From that the body gains rapid movement like the mind's, the organs gain power independent of the body, and nature is conquered.

### 3.50

सत्त्वपुरुषान्यताख्याति मात्रस्य सर्वभावाधिष्ठातृत्वं  
सर्वज्ञातृत्वं च ॥

*sattvapuruṣānyatākhyāti mātrasya sarvabhāvādhiṣṭhātṛtvaṃ  
sarvajñātṛtvaṃ ca .. 3.50..*

Samyama on the discrimination between Sattva and Purusha brings omnipotence and omniscience.

### 3.51

तद्वैराग्यादपि दोषबीजक्षये कैवल्यम् ॥

*tadvairāgyādapi doṣabījakṣaye kaivalyam .. 3.51..*

Giving up even these powers destroys the very seed of evil, which leads to Kaivalya.

### 3.52

स्थान्युपनिमन्त्रणे सङ्गस्मयाकरणं पुनरनिष्टप्रसङ्गात् ॥

*sthānyupanimantraṇe saṅgasmayākaraṇaṃ punaraniṣṭaprasaṅgāt .. 3.52..*

The Yogi should not be tempted or flattered by the advances of celestial beings, for fear of evil returning.

### 3.53

क्षणतत्क्रमयोः संयमाद्विवेकजं ज्ञानम् ॥

*kṣaṇatatkramayoḥ saṃyamādvivekajaṃ jñānam .. 3.53..*

Samyama on a particle of time and on what precedes and follows it brings discrimination.

### 3.54

जातिलक्षण देशैरन्यतानवच्छेदात् तुल्ययोस्ततः प्रतिपत्तिः ॥

*jātilakṣaṇa deśairanyatānavacchedāt tulyayostataḥ pratipattiḥ .. 3.54..*

Even things that cannot be told apart by species, sign, or place are distinguished by this Samyama.

### 3.55

तारकं सर्वविषयं सर्वथाविषयम् अक्रमं  
चेति विवेकजं ज्ञानम् ॥

*tārakaṃ sarvaviṣayaṃ sarvathāviṣayam akramaṃ  
ceti vivekajaṃ jñānam .. 3.55..*

The saving knowledge is that knowledge of discrimination which covers all objects, in all their variations, at once.

### 3.56

सत्त्वपुरुषयोः शुद्धिसाम्ये कैवल्यमिति ॥  
॥ इति पतञ्जलि-विरचिते योग-सूत्रे तृतीयो विभूति-पादः ॥

*sattvapuruṣayoḥ śuddhisāmye kaivalyamiti .. 3.56..  
.. iti patañjali-viracite yoga-sūtre tṛtīyo vibhūti-pādaḥ ..*

When Sattva and Purusha are alike in purity, Kaivalya comes.

### 4.1

<vt>Patanjali Yogasutra: Part 4 - Kaivalya-pāda - Liberation</vt>  
जन्मौषधिमन्त्रतपःसमाधिजाः सिद्धयः ॥ ४.१॥

*<vt>Patanjali Yogasutra: Part 4 - Kaivalya-pāda - Liberation</vt>  
janmauṣadhimantratapaḥsamādhijāḥ siddhayaḥ .. 4.1..*

Birth, chemical means, the power of words, mortification, or concentration can each bring the Siddhis (powers).

### 4.2

जात्यन्तरपरिणामः प्रकृत्यापूरात् ॥ ४.२॥

*jātyantarapariṇāmaḥ prakṛtyāpūrāt .. 4.2..*

Change into another species comes through the filling in of nature.

### 4.3

निमित्तमप्रयोजकं प्रकृतीनां वरणभेदस्तु  
ततः क्षेत्रिकवत् ॥ ४.३॥

*nimittamaprayojakaṃ prakṛtīnāṃ varaṇabhedastu  
tataḥ kṣetrikavat .. 4.3..*

Good and bad deeds do not directly cause the transformations of nature. They break the obstacles to its evolution, as a farmer breaks the barriers to water, which then flows down by its own nature.

### 4.4

निर्माणचित्तान्यस्मितामात्रात् ॥ ४.४॥

*nirmāṇacittānyasmitāmātrāt .. 4.4..*

The created minds arise from egoism alone.

### 4.5

प्रवृत्तिभेदे प्रयोजकं चित्तमेकमनेकेषाम् ॥ ४.५॥

*pravṛttibhede prayojakaṃ cittamekamanekeṣām .. 4.5..*

Though the created minds act in various ways, the one original mind controls them all.

### 4.6

तत्र ध्यानजमनाशयम् ॥ ४.६॥

*tatra dhyānajamanāśayam .. 4.6..*

Among the various Chittas, the one attained by Samadhi is free of desire.

### 4.7

कर्माशुक्लाकृष्णं योगिनस्त्रिविधमितरेषाम् ॥ ४.७॥

*karmāśuklākṛṣṇaṃ yoginastrividhamitareṣām .. 4.7..*

For Yogis, works are neither black nor white. For others they are of three kinds: black, white, and mixed.

### 4.8

ततस्तद्विपाकानुगुणानामेवाभिव्यक्तिर्वासनानाम् ॥ ४.८॥

*tatastadvipākānuguṇānāmevābhivyaktirvāsanānām .. 4.8..*

From these three kinds of work, each state manifests only the desires that suit that state. (The others are held in abeyance for the time being.)

### 4.9

जातिदेशकालव्यवहितानामप्यानन्तर्यं  
स्मृतिसंस्कारयोरेकरूपत्वात् ॥ ४.९॥

*jātideśakālavyavahitānāmapyānantaryaṃ  
smṛtisaṃskārayorekarūpatvāt .. 4.9..*

Desires follow in sequence even when separated by species, space, and time, because memory and impressions are identical.

### 4.10

तासामनादित्वं चाशिषो नित्यत्वात् ॥ ४.१०॥

*tāsāmanāditvaṃ cāśiṣo nityatvāt .. 4.10..*

Since the thirst for happiness is eternal, desires have no beginning.

### 4.11

हेतुफलाश्रयालम्बनैः संगृहीतत्वादेषामभावे तदभावः ॥ ४.११॥

*hetuphalāśrayālambanaiḥ saṃgṛhītatvādeṣāmabhāve tadabhāvaḥ .. 4.11..*

Desires are held together by cause, effect, support, and objects. Without these, they cease.

### 4.12

अतीतानागतं स्वरूपतोऽस्त्यध्वभेदाद्धर्माणाम् ॥ ४.१२॥

*atītānāgataṃ svarūpato'styadhvabhedāddharmāṇām .. 4.12..*

Past and future exist in their own nature, since qualities take different paths.

### 4.13

ते व्यक्तसूक्ष्मा गुणात्मानः ॥ ४.१३॥

*te vyaktasūkṣmā guṇātmānaḥ .. 4.13..*

They are either manifest or fine, being of the nature of the Gunas.

### 4.14

परिणामैकत्वाद्वस्तुतत्त्वम् ॥ ४.१४॥

*pariṇāmaikatvādvastutattvam .. 4.14..*

Things are unified because their changes are unified.

### 4.15

वस्तुसाम्ये चित्तभेदात्तयोर्विभक्तः पन्थाः ॥ ४.१५॥

*vastusāmye cittabhedāttayorvibhaktaḥ panthāḥ .. 4.15..*

Since perception and desire differ with regard to the same object, mind and object differ in nature.

### 4.16

न चैकचित्ततन्त्रं वस्तु तदप्रमाणकं तदा किं स्यात् ॥ ४.१६॥

*na caikacittatantraṃ vastu tadapramāṇakaṃ tadā kiṃ syāt .. 4.16..*

An object cannot be said to depend on the perception of a single mind. If it did, it could be called non-existent whenever that mind was not perceiving it.

### 4.17

तदुपरागापेक्षित्वाच्चित्तस्य वस्तु ज्ञाताज्ञातम् ॥ ४.१७॥

*taduparāgāpekṣitvāccittasya vastu jñātājñātam .. 4.17..*

Things are known or unknown to the mind according to the colouring they give it.

### 4.18

सदा ज्ञाताश्चित्तवृत्तयस्तत्प्रभोः पुरुषस्यापरिणामित्वात् ॥ ४.१८॥

*sadā jñātāścittavṛttayastatprabhoḥ puruṣasyāpariṇāmitvāt .. 4.18..*

The states of the mind are always known, since the mind's lord, the Purusha, does not change.

### 4.19

न तत्स्वाभासं दृश्यत्वात् ॥ ४.१९॥

*na tatsvābhāsaṃ dṛśyatvāt .. 4.19..*

The mind is not self-luminous, since it is an object.

### 4.20

एकसमये चोभयानवधारणम् ॥ ४.२०॥

*ekasamaye cobhayānavadhāraṇam .. 4.20..*

Because it cannot perceive both at once.

### 4.21

चित्तान्तरदृश्ये बुद्धिबुद्धेरतिप्रसङ्गः स्मृतिसङ्करश्च ॥ ४.२१॥

*cittāntaradṛśye buddhibuddheratiprasaṅgaḥ smṛtisaṅkaraśca .. 4.21..*

If a further perceiving mind is assumed, such assumptions never end, and memory falls into confusion.

### 4.22

चितेरप्रतिसंक्रमायास्तदाकारापत्तौ स्वबुद्धिसंवेदनम् ॥ ४.२२॥

*citerapratisaṃkramāyāstadākārāpattau svabuddhisaṃvedanam .. 4.22..*

The essence of knowledge (the Purusha) never changes; the mind becomes conscious when it takes on that form.

### 4.23

द्रष्टृदृश्योपरक्तं चित्तं सर्वार्थम् ॥ ४.२३॥

*draṣṭṛdṛśyoparaktaṃ cittaṃ sarvārtham .. 4.23..*

Tinged by both seer and seen, the mind can understand everything.

### 4.24

तदसंख्येयवासनाभिश्चित्रमपि परार्थं संहत्यकारित्वात् ॥ ४.२४॥

*tadasaṃkhyeyavāsanābhiścitramapi parārthaṃ saṃhatyakāritvāt .. 4.24..*

Though patterned by countless desires, the mind works for another (the Purusha), since it works in combination.

### 4.25

विशेषदर्शिन आत्मभावभावनाविनिवृत्तिः ॥ ४.२५॥

*viśeṣadarśina ātmabhāvabhāvanāvinivṛttiḥ .. 4.25..*

For one who discriminates, seeing the mind as Atman comes to an end.

### 4.26

तदा विवेकनिम्नं कैवल्यप्राग्भारं चित्तम् ॥ ४.२६॥

*tadā vivekanimnaṃ kaivalyaprāgbhāraṃ cittam .. 4.26..*

Then the mind, intent on discrimination, reaches the prior state of Kaivalya (isolation).

### 4.27

तच्छिद्रेषु प्रत्ययान्तराणि संस्कारेभ्यः ॥ ४.२७॥

*tacchidreṣu pratyayāntarāṇi saṃskārebhyaḥ .. 4.27..*

Thoughts that arise to obstruct this come from impressions.

### 4.28

हानमेषां क्लेशवदुक्तम् ॥ ४.२८॥

*hānameṣāṃ kleśavaduktam .. 4.28..*

They are destroyed in the same way as ignorance, egoism, and the rest, as stated earlier (II.10).

### 4.29

प्रसंख्यानेऽप्यकुसीदस्य सर्वथा  
विवेकख्यातेर्धर्ममेघः समाधिः ॥ ४.२९॥

*prasaṃkhyāne'pyakusīdasya sarvathā  
vivekakhyāterdharmameghaḥ samādhiḥ .. 4.29..*

One who gives up the fruits even on reaching right discriminating knowledge of the essences gains, through perfect discrimination, the Samadhi called the cloud of virtue.

### 4.30

ततः क्लेशकर्मनिवृत्तिः ॥ ४.३०॥

*tataḥ kleśakarmanivṛttiḥ .. 4.30..*

From that, pain and works cease.

### 4.31

तदा सर्वावरणमलापेतस्य ज्ञानस्यानन्त्याज्ज्ञेयमल्पम् ॥ ४.३१॥

*tadā sarvāvaraṇamalāpetasya jñānasyānantyājjñeyamalpam .. 4.31..*

When knowledge, stripped of coverings and impurities, becomes infinite, what can be known becomes small.

### 4.32

ततः कृतार्थानां परिणामक्रमसमाप्तिर्गुणानाम् ॥ ४.३२॥

*tataḥ kṛtārthānāṃ pariṇāmakramasamāptirguṇānām .. 4.32..*

Then the successive transformations of the qualities end, their goal being reached.

### 4.33

क्षणप्रतियोगी परिणामापरान्तनिर्ग्राह्यः क्रमः ॥ ४.३३॥

*kṣaṇapratiyogī pariṇāmāparāntanirgrāhyaḥ kramaḥ .. 4.33..*

Succession is the changes that relate to moments and are perceived at the far end of a series.

### 4.34

पुरुषार्थशून्यानां गुणानां प्रतिप्रसवः कैवल्यं  
स्वरूपप्रतिष्ठा वा चितिशक्तिरिति ॥ ४.३४॥  
*****  
॥ इति पतञ्जलि-विरचिते योग-सूत्रे चतुर्थः कैवल्य-पादः ॥  
॥ इति श्री पातञ्जल-योग-सूत्राणि ॥

*puruṣārthaśūnyānāṃ guṇānāṃ pratiprasavaḥ kaivalyaṃ  
svarūpapratiṣṭhā vā citiśaktiriti .. 4.34..  
*****  
.. iti patañjali-viracite yoga-sūtre caturthaḥ kaivalya-pādaḥ ..  
.. iti śrī pātañjala-yoga-sūtrāṇi ..*

Kaivalya is the qualities resolving in reverse order, with no motive of action left for the Purusha; or it is the power of knowledge established in its own nature.
