# Chapter 3 – Karma Yoga (The Yoga of Action)

*Yoga of Action*

### 3.1

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
ज्यायसी चेत्कर्मणस्ते मता बुद्धिर्जनार्दन ।  
तत्किं कर्मणि घोरे मां नियोजयसि केशव ॥ ३-१॥

*arjuna uvāca  
jyāyasī cetkarmaṇaste matā buddhirjanārdana  
tatkiṃ karmaṇi ghore māṃ niyojayasi keśava 3-1*

Arjuna said: If you hold that knowledge is greater than action, O Janardana, then why, O Keshava, do you urge me into this dreadful action?

### 3.2

व्यामिश्रेणेव वाक्येन बुद्धिं मोहयसीव मे ।  
तदेकं वद निश्चित्य येन श्रेयोऽहमाप्नुयाम् ॥ ३-२॥

*vyāmiśreṇeva vākyena buddhiṃ mohayasīva me  
tadekaṃ vada niścitya yena śreyo'hamāpnuyām 3-2*

With these seemingly contradictory words you appear to confuse my understanding. So tell me plainly the one path by which I can surely reach the Highest.

### 3.3

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
लोकेऽस्मिन् द्विविधा निष्ठा पुरा प्रोक्ता मयानघ ।  
ज्ञानयोगेन साङ्ख्यानां कर्मयोगेन योगिनाम् ॥ ३-३॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
loke'smin dvividhā niṣṭhā purā proktā mayānagha  
jñānayogena sāṅkhyānāṃ karmayogena yoginām 3-3*

The Blessed Lord said: In this world there are two paths, as I explained before, O sinless one: the Path of Knowledge followed by the Sankhyans and the Path of Action followed by the Yogins.

### 3.4

न कर्मणामनारम्भान् न्नैष्कर्म्यं पुरुषोऽश्नुते ।  
न च संन्यसनादेव सिद्धिं समधिगच्छति ॥ ३-४॥

*na karmaṇāmanārambhān naiṣkarmyaṃ puruṣo'śnute  
na ca saṃnyasanādeva siddhiṃ samadhigacchati 3-4*

A person does not reach the state beyond action simply by not acting, nor does anyone attain Perfection through renunciation alone.

### 3.5

न हि कश्चित्क्षणमपि जातु तिष्ठत्यकर्मकृत् ।  
कार्यते ह्यवशः कर्म सर्वः प्रकृतिजैर्गुणैः ॥ ३-५॥

*na hi kaścitkṣaṇamapi jātu tiṣṭhatyakarmakṛt  
kāryate hyavaśaḥ karma sarvaḥ prakṛtijairguṇaiḥ 3-5*

Truly, no one can stay even for a moment without acting, because everyone is driven to act, helplessly, by the qualities that arise from Prakriti.

### 3.6

कर्मेन्द्रियाणि संयम्य य आस्ते मनसा स्मरन् ।  
इन्द्रियार्थान्विमूढात्मा मिथ्याचारः स उच्यते ॥ ३-६॥

*karmendriyāṇi saṃyamya ya āste manasā smaran  
indriyārthānvimūḍhātmā mithyācāraḥ sa ucyate 3-6*

Someone who holds back the organs of action but sits dwelling on sense objects in the mind is self-deceived and is called a hypocrite.

### 3.7

यस्त्विन्द्रियाणि मनसा नियम्यारभतेऽर्जुन ।  
कर्मेन्द्रियैः कर्मयोगमसक्तः स विशिष्यते ॥ ३-७॥

*yastvindriyāṇi manasā niyamyārabhate'rjuna  
karmendriyaiḥ karmayogamasaktaḥ sa viśiṣyate 3-7*

But the person who controls the senses with the mind, O Arjuna, and puts the organs of action to work in Karma Yoga without attachment, is the superior one.

### 3.8

नियतं कुरु कर्म त्वं कर्म ज्यायो ह्यकर्मणः ।  
शरीरयात्रापि च ते न प्रसिद्ध्येदकर्मणः ॥ ३-८॥

*niyataṃ kuru karma tvaṃ karma jyāyo hyakarmaṇaḥ  
śarīrayātrāpi ca te na prasiddhyedakarmaṇaḥ 3-8*

Carry out the duty that is yours to perform, because action is better than inaction. Without action you could not even keep your body alive.

### 3.9

यज्ञार्थात्कर्मणोऽन्यत्र लोकोऽयं कर्मबन्धनः ।  
तदर्थं कर्म कौन्तेय मुक्तसङ्गः समाचर ॥ ३-९॥

*yajñārthātkarmaṇo'nyatra loko'yaṃ karmabandhanaḥ  
tadarthaṃ karma kaunteya muktasaṅgaḥ samācara 3-9*

The world is bound by every action except those done as sacrifice. Therefore, O son of Kunti, act only for that purpose (for yajna), free from all attachment.

### 3.10

सहयज्ञाः प्रजाः सृष्ट्वा पुरोवाच प्रजापतिः ।  
अनेन प्रसविष्यध्वमेष वोऽस्त्विष्टकामधुक् ॥ ३-१०॥

*sahayajñāḥ prajāḥ sṛṣṭvā purovāca prajāpatiḥ  
anena prasaviṣyadhvameṣa vo'stviṣṭakāmadhuk 3-10*

At the beginning of creation, Prajapati (the Creator) brought forth humanity along with sacrifice and said: 'Through this you shall prosper; let this be the cow that yields all you wish for' (Kamadhuk, the mythological wish-granting cow).

### 3.11

देवान्भावयतानेन ते देवा भावयन्तु वः ।  
परस्परं भावयन्तः श्रेयः परमवाप्स्यथ ॥ ३-११॥

*devānbhāvayatānena te devā bhāvayantu vaḥ  
parasparaṃ bhāvayantaḥ śreyaḥ paramavāpsyatha 3-11*

Through sacrifice you sustain the gods, and may those devas sustain you in turn. Sustaining one another in this way, you will reach the Highest Good.

### 3.12

इष्टान्भोगान्हि वो देवा दास्यन्ते यज्ञभाविताः ।  
तैर्दत्तानप्रदायैभ्यो यो भुङ्क्ते स्तेन एव सः ॥ ३-१२॥

*iṣṭānbhogānhi vo devā dāsyante yajñabhāvitāḥ  
tairdattānapradāyaibhyo yo bhuṅkte stena eva saḥ 3-12*

The devas, sustained by sacrifice, will grant you the things you desire. Anyone who enjoys what the devas give without offering anything back to them is nothing but a thief.

### 3.13

यज्ञशिष्टाशिनः सन्तो मुच्यन्ते सर्वकिल्बिषैः ।  
भुञ्जते ते त्वघं पापा ये पचन्त्यात्मकारणात् ॥ ३-१३॥

*yajñaśiṣṭāśinaḥ santo mucyante sarvakilbiṣaiḥ  
bhuñjate te tvaghaṃ pāpā ye pacantyātmakāraṇāt 3-13*

The righteous who eat what remains after sacrifice are released from all sins. But the sinful who cook only for themselves eat nothing but sin.

### 3.14

अन्नाद्भवन्ति भूतानि पर्जन्यादन्नसम्भवः ।  
यज्ञाद्भवति पर्जन्यो यज्ञः कर्मसमुद्भवः ॥ ३-१४॥

*annādbhavanti bhūtāni parjanyādannasambhavaḥ  
yajñādbhavati parjanyo yajñaḥ karmasamudbhavaḥ 3-14*

Living beings come from food; food is produced by rain; rain comes from sacrifice; and sacrifice is born of action.

### 3.15

कर्म ब्रह्मोद्भवं विद्धि ब्रह्माक्षरसमुद्भवम् ।  
तस्मात्सर्वगतं ब्रह्म नित्यं यज्ञे प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ ३-१५॥

*karma brahmodbhavaṃ viddhi brahmākṣarasamudbhavam  
tasmātsarvagataṃ brahma nityaṃ yajñe pratiṣṭhitam 3-15*

Understand that action originates in Brahmaji (the creator), and Brahmaji originates in the Imperishable. Therefore the all-pervading Brahman (the God-principle) is always present in sacrifice.

### 3.16

एवं प्रवर्तितं चक्रं नानुवर्तयतीह यः ।  
अघायुरिन्द्रियारामो मोघं पार्थ स जीवति ॥ ३-१६॥

*evaṃ pravartitaṃ cakraṃ nānuvartayatīha yaḥ  
aghāyurindriyārāmo moghaṃ pārtha sa jīvati 3-16*

Whoever does not keep turning this wheel that has been set in motion lives a sinful life, delighting in the senses. Such a person lives for nothing, O son of Pritha.

### 3.17

यस्त्वात्मरतिरेव स्यादात्मतृप्तश्च मानवः ।  
आत्मन्येव च सन्तुष्टस्तस्य कार्यं न विद्यते ॥ ३-१७॥

*yastvātmaratireva syādātmatṛptaśca mānavaḥ  
ātmanyeva ca santuṣṭastasya kāryaṃ na vidyate 3-17*

But the person who delights only in the Self, who is satisfied with the Self and content in the Self alone, truly has nothing further to do.

### 3.18

नैव तस्य कृतेनार्थो नाकृतेनेह कश्चन ।  
न चास्य सर्वभूतेषु कश्चिदर्थव्यपाश्रयः ॥ ३-१८॥

*naiva tasya kṛtenārtho nākṛteneha kaścana  
na cāsya sarvabhūteṣu kaścidarthavyapāśrayaḥ 3-18*

Such a person has no stake at all in what is done or left undone in this world, and does not depend on any being for anything.

### 3.19

तस्मादसक्तः सततं कार्यं कर्म समाचर ।  
असक्तो ह्याचरन्कर्म परमाप्नोति पूरुषः ॥ ३-१९॥

*tasmādasaktaḥ satataṃ kāryaṃ karma samācara  
asakto hyācarankarma paramāpnoti pūruṣaḥ 3-19*

So always perform the actions that ought to be done, without attachment. By acting without attachment a person reaches the Supreme.

### 3.20

कर्मणैव हि संसिद्धिमास्थिता जनकादयः ।  
लोकसङ्ग्रहमेवापि सम्पश्यन्कर्तुमर्हसि ॥ ३-२०॥

*karmaṇaiva hi saṃsiddhimāsthitā janakādayaḥ  
lokasaṅgrahamevāpi sampaśyankartumarhasi 3-20*

Janaka and others reached Perfection through action alone. You too should act, even if only to protect and guide the people.

### 3.21

यद्यदाचरति श्रेष्ठस्तत्तदेवेतरो जनः ।  
स यत्प्रमाणं कुरुते लोकस्तदनुवर्तते ॥ ३-२१॥

*yadyadācarati śreṣṭhastattadevetaro janaḥ  
sa yatpramāṇaṃ kurute lokastadanuvartate 3-21*

Whatever a great person does, others do as well (they imitate it). Whatever standard such a person sets, the world (the people) follows.

### 3.22

न मे पार्थास्ति कर्तव्यं त्रिषु लोकेषु किञ्चन ।  
नानवाप्तमवाप्तव्यं वर्त एव च कर्मणि ॥ ३-२२॥

*na me pārthāsti kartavyaṃ triṣu lokeṣu kiñcana  
nānavāptamavāptavyaṃ varta eva ca karmaṇi 3-22*

There is nothing in the three worlds, O Partha, that I need to do, and nothing I lack that I need to obtain. Yet I still engage in action.

### 3.23

यदि ह्यहं न वर्तेयं जातु कर्मण्यतन्द्रितः ।  
मम वर्त्मानुवर्तन्ते मनुष्याः पार्थ सर्वशः ॥ ३-२३॥

*yadi hyahaṃ na varteyaṃ jātu karmaṇyatandritaḥ  
mama vartmānuvartante manuṣyāḥ pārtha sarvaśaḥ 3-23*

For if I did not always engage in action without pause, people everywhere would follow My example, O son of Pritha.

### 3.24

उत्सीदेयुरिमे लोका न कुर्यां कर्म चेदहम् ।  
सङ्करस्य च कर्ता स्यामुपहन्यामिमाः प्रजाः ॥ ३-२४॥

*utsīdeyurime lokā na kuryāṃ karma cedaham  
saṅkarasya ca kartā syāmupahanyāmimāḥ prajāḥ 3-24*

If I stopped acting, these worlds would fall to ruin. I would be the cause of the mixing of castes and would bring about the destruction of these beings.

### 3.25

सक्ताः कर्मण्यविद्वांसो यथा कुर्वन्ति भारत ।  
कुर्याद्विद्वांस्तथासक्तश्चिकीर्षुर्लोकसङ्ग्रहम् ॥ ३-२५॥

*saktāḥ karmaṇyavidvāṃso yathā kurvanti bhārata  
kuryādvidvāṃstathāsaktaś cikīrṣurlokasaṅgraham 3-25*

Just as the ignorant act out of attachment to their work, O Bharata, so the wise should act without attachment, seeking the good of the world.

### 3.26

न बुद्धिभेदं जनयेदज्ञानां कर्मसङ्गिनाम् ।  
जोषयेत्सर्वकर्माणि विद्वान्युक्तः समाचरन् ॥ ३-२६॥

*na buddhibhedaṃ janayedajñānāṃ karmasaṅginām  
joṣayetsarvakarmāṇi vidvānyuktaḥ samācaran 3-26*

The wise should not disturb the minds of the ignorant who are attached to action. Instead, they should encourage them in all their work while themselves performing it with devotion.

### 3.27

प्रकृतेः क्रियमाणानि गुणैः कर्माणि सर्वशः ।  
अहङ्कारविमूढात्मा कर्ताहमिति मन्यते ॥ ३-२७॥

*prakṛteḥ kriyamāṇāni guṇaiḥ karmāṇi sarvaśaḥ  
ahaṅkāravimūḍhātmā kartāhamiti manyate 3-27*

In every case, all actions are carried out simply by the qualities of Nature (the gunas). The person whose mind is clouded by egoism thinks, 'I am the doer.'

### 3.28

तत्त्ववित्तु महाबाहो गुणकर्मविभागयोः ।  
गुणा गुणेषु वर्तन्त इति मत्वा न सज्जते ॥ ३-२८॥

*tattvavittu mahābāho guṇakarmavibhāgayoḥ  
guṇā guṇeṣu vartanta iti matvā na sajjate 3-28*

But the one who knows the truth, O mighty-armed, about how the qualities divide and how they function, who understands that it is the gunas as senses that move among the gunas as objects, is not attached.

### 3.29

प्रकृतेर्गुणसम्मूढाः सज्जन्ते गुणकर्मसु ।  
तानकृत्स्नविदो मन्दान्कृत्स्नविन्न विचालयेत् ॥ ३-२९॥

*prakṛterguṇasammūḍhāḥ sajjante guṇakarmasu  
tānakṛtsnavido mandānkṛtsnavinna vicālayet 3-29*

Those who are deluded by the qualities of nature (the gunas) become attached to the activities of those qualities. The person of perfect knowledge should not unsettle the foolish, whose knowledge is incomplete.

### 3.30

मयि सर्वाणि कर्माणि संन्यस्याध्यात्मचेतसा ।  
निराशीर्निर्ममो भूत्वा युध्यस्व विगतज्वरः ॥ ३-३०॥

*mayi sarvāṇi karmāṇi saṃnyasyādhyātmacetasā  
nirāśīrnirmamo bhūtvā yudhyasva vigatajvaraḥ 3-30*

Surrender all your actions to Me, with your mind fixed on the Self, free from expectation and from egoism (the sense of ownership), free from mental fever, and fight!

### 3.31

ये मे मतमिदं नित्यमनुतिष्ठन्ति मानवाः ।  
श्रद्धावन्तोऽनसूयन्तो मुच्यन्ते तेऽपि कर्मभिः ॥ ३-३१॥

*ye me matamidaṃ nityamanutiṣṭhanti mānavāḥ  
śraddhāvanto'nasūyanto mucyante te'pi karmabhiḥ 3-31*

Those who constantly follow this teaching of Mine, with faith and without finding fault, are also set free from the bondage of action.

### 3.32

ये त्वेतदभ्यसूयन्तो नानुतिष्ठन्ति मे मतम् ।  
सर्वज्ञानविमूढांस्तान्विद्धि नष्टानचेतसः ॥ ३-३२॥

*ye tvetadabhyasūyanto nānutiṣṭhanti me matam  
sarvajñānavimūḍhāṃstān viddhi naṣṭānacetasaḥ 3-32*

But those who sneer at My teaching and do not practise it, confused about all knowledge and lacking discrimination, understand them to be headed for destruction.

### 3.33

सदृशं चेष्टते स्वस्याः प्रकृतेर्ज्ञानवानपि ।  
प्रकृतिं यान्ति भूतानि निग्रहः किं करिष्यति ॥ ३-३३॥

*sadṛśaṃ ceṣṭate svasyāḥ prakṛterjñānavānapi  
prakṛtiṃ yānti bhūtāni nigrahaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati 3-33*

Even a wise person acts according to their own nature. All beings follow their nature; what can suppression accomplish?

### 3.34

इन्द्रियस्येन्द्रियस्यार्थे रागद्वेषौ व्यवस्थितौ ।  
तयोर्न वशमागच्छेत्तौ ह्यस्य परिपन्थिनौ ॥ ३-३४॥

*indriyasyendriyasyārthe rāgadveṣau vyavasthitau  
tayorna vaśamāgacchettau hyasya paripanthinau 3-34*

Attraction and aversion toward sense objects reside in the senses. No one should fall under their control, because they are a person's enemies.

### 3.35

श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात् ।  
स्वधर्मे निधनं श्रेयः परधर्मो भयावहः ॥ ३-३५॥

*śreyānsvadharmo viguṇaḥ paradharmātsvanuṣṭhitāt  
svadharme nidhanaṃ śreyaḥ paradharmo bhayāvahaḥ 3-35*

One's own duty, even if it lacks merit, is better than another's duty performed well. It is better to die doing one's own duty; another's duty is full of fear (it brings real danger).

### 3.36

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
अथ केन प्रयुक्तोऽयं पापं चरति पूरुषः ।  
अनिच्छन्नपि वार्ष्णेय बलादिव नियोजितः ॥ ३-३६॥

*arjuna uvāca  
atha kena prayukto'yaṃ pāpaṃ carati pūruṣaḥ  
anicchannapi vārṣṇeya balādiva niyojitaḥ 3-36*

Arjuna said: But what drives a person to commit sin, O Varshneya, even against their own will, as if compelled by force?

### 3.37

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
काम एष क्रोध एष रजोगुणसमुद्भवः ।  
महाशनो महापाप्मा विद्ध्येनमिह वैरिणम् ॥ ३-३७॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
kāma eṣa krodha eṣa rajoguṇasamudbhavaḥ  
mahāśano mahāpāpmā viddhyenamiha vairiṇam 3-37*

The Blessed Lord said: It is desire, it is anger, born of the quality of passion (rajas), all-consuming and the source of all sin. Know this to be the enemy here in this world.

### 3.38

धूमेनाव्रियते वह्निर्यथादर्शो मलेन च ।  
यथोल्बेनावृतो गर्भस्तथा तेनेदमावृतम् ॥ ३-३८॥

*dhūmenāvriyate vahniryathādarśo malena ca  
yatholbenāvṛto garbhastathā tenedamāvṛtam 3-38*

Just as smoke covers fire, dust covers a mirror, and the womb covers the embryo, so this (wisdom) is covered by that (desire or anger).

### 3.39

आवृतं ज्ञानमेतेन ज्ञानिनो नित्यवैरिणा ।  
कामरूपेण कौन्तेय दुष्पूरेणानलेन च ॥ ३-३९॥

*āvṛtaṃ jñānametena jñānino nityavairiṇā  
kāmarūpeṇa kaunteya duṣpūreṇānalena ca 3-39*

O son of Kunti, wisdom is covered over by this constant enemy of the wise, which takes the form of desire and, like fire, is never satisfied.

### 3.40

इन्द्रियाणि मनो बुद्धिरस्याधिष्ठानमुच्यते ।  
एतैर्विमोहयत्येष ज्ञानमावृत्य देहिनम् ॥ ३-४०॥

*indriyāṇi mano buddhirasyādhiṣṭhānamucyate  
etairvimohayatyeṣa jñānamāvṛtya dehinam 3-40*

The senses, the mind, and the intellect are said to be where it dwells. Working through these, it veils a person's wisdom and deludes the embodied soul.

### 3.41

तस्मात्त्वमिन्द्रियाण्यादौ नियम्य भरतर्षभ ।  
पाप्मानं प्रजहि ह्येनं ज्ञानविज्ञाननाशनम् ॥ ३-४१॥

*tasmāttvamindriyāṇyādau niyamya bharatarṣabha  
pāpmānaṃ prajahi hyenaṃ jñānavijñānanāśanam 3-41*

Therefore, O best of the Bharatas, first bring the senses under control, and then destroy this sinful thing, which ruins both knowledge and wisdom.

### 3.42

इन्द्रियाणि पराण्याहुरिन्द्रियेभ्यः परं मनः ।  
मनसस्तु परा बुद्धिर्यो बुद्धेः परतस्तु सः ॥ ३-४२॥

*indriyāṇi parāṇyāhurindriyebhyaḥ paraṃ manaḥ  
manasastu parā buddhiryo buddheḥ paratastu saḥ 3-42*

It is said that the senses are higher than the body; the mind is higher than the senses; the intellect is higher than the mind; and higher even than the intellect is He (the Atman).

### 3.43

एवं बुद्धेः परं बुद्ध्वा संस्तभ्यात्मानमात्मना ।  
जहि शत्रुं महाबाहो कामरूपं दुरासदम् ॥ ३-४३॥   
  
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु  
ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे  
कर्मयोगो नाम तृतीयोऽध्यायः ॥ ३॥

*evaṃ buddheḥ paraṃ buddhvā saṃstabhyātmānamātmanā  
jahi śatruṃ mahābāho kāmarūpaṃ durāsadam 3-43  
  
oṃ tatsaditi śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu  
brahmavidyāyāṃ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṃvāde  
karmayogo nāma tṛtīyo'dhyāyaḥ 3*

Knowing Him who is beyond the intellect, and steadying the self by the Self, slay the enemy that takes the form of desire, O mighty-armed, hard though it certainly is to conquer.

So ends the third discourse, called "The Yoga of Action," in the Upanishads of the glorious Bhagavad Gita, the science of the Eternal, the scripture of Yoga, the dialogue between Sri Krishna and Arjuna.
