# Chapter 1 – Arjuna Viṣāda Yoga (The Yoga of Arjuna’s Despondency)

*Yoga of Arjuna's Grief*

### 1.1

धृतराष्ट्र उवाच ।  
धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे समवेता युयुत्सवः ।  
मामकाः पाण्डवाश्चैव किमकुर्वत सञ्जय ॥ १-१॥

*dhṛtarāṣṭra uvāca  
dharmakṣetre kurukṣetre samavetā yuyutsavaḥ  
māmakāḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva kimakurvata sañjaya 1-1*

Dhritarashtra said: Sanjaya, tell me what my sons and the sons of Pandu did once they had gathered, eager for battle, on the sacred field of Kurukshetra.

### 1.2

सञ्जय उवाच ।   
दृष्ट्वा तु पाण्डवानीकं व्यूढं दुर्योधनस्तदा ।  
आचार्यमुपसङ्गम्य राजा वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ १-२॥

*sañjaya uvāca  
dṛṣṭvā tu pāṇḍavānīkaṃ vyūḍhaṃ duryodhanastadā  
ācāryamupasaṅgamya rājā vacanamabravīt 1-2*

Sanjaya said: When King Duryodhana saw the Pandava army arranged in battle formation, he went up to his teacher Drona and spoke these words.

### 1.3

पश्यैतां पाण्डुपुत्राणामाचार्य महतीं चमूम् ।  
व्यूढां द्रुपदपुत्रेण तव शिष्येण धीमता ॥ १-३॥

*paśyaitāṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāmācārya mahatīṃ camūm  
vyūḍhāṃ drupadaputreṇa tava śiṣyeṇa dhīmatā 1-3*

Teacher, look at this great army of the sons of Pandu, drawn up for battle by your gifted pupil, the son of Drupada.

### 1.4

अत्र शूरा महेष्वासा भीमार्जुनसमा युधि ।  
युयुधानो विराटश्च द्रुपदश्च महारथः ॥ १-४॥

*atra śūrā maheṣvāsā bhīmārjunasamā yudhi  
yuyudhāno virāṭaśca drupadaśca mahārathaḥ 1-4*

In it stand heroes and powerful archers who match Bhima and Arjuna in combat: Yuyudhana, Virata and Drupada, each of whom leads eleven thousand archers.

### 1.5

धृष्टकेतुश्चेकितानः काशिराजश्च वीर्यवान् ।  
पुरुजित्कुन्तिभोजश्च शैब्यश्च नरपुङ्गवः ॥ १-५॥

*dhṛṣṭaketuścekitānaḥ kāśirājaśca vīryavān  
purujitkuntibhojaśca śaibyaśca narapuṅgavaḥ 1-5*

There are Dhrishtaketu, Chekitana and the courageous king of Kashi, along with Purujit, Kuntibhoja and Saibya, the finest of men.

### 1.6

युधामन्युश्च विक्रान्त उत्तमौजाश्च वीर्यवान् ।  
सौभद्रो द्रौपदेयाश्च सर्व एव महारथाः ॥ १-६॥

*yudhāmanyuśca vikrānta uttamaujāśca vīryavān  
saubhadro draupadeyāśca sarva eva mahārathāḥ 1-6*

There are the powerful Yudhamanyu and the brave Uttamaujas, the son of Subhadra and the sons of Draupadi, every one of them a commander of a division.

### 1.7

अस्माकं तु विशिष्टा ये तान्निबोध द्विजोत्तम ।  
नायका मम सैन्यस्य संज्ञार्थं तान्ब्रवीमि ते ॥ १-७॥

*asmākaṃ tu viśiṣṭā ye tānnibodha dvijottama  
nāyakā mama sainyasya saṃjñārthaṃ tānbravīmi te 1-7*

Now, best of the twice-born, learn too the names of the most outstanding men on our own side, the leaders of my army. I list them so that you know who they are.

### 1.8

भवान्भीष्मश्च कर्णश्च कृपश्च समितिञ्जयः ।  
अश्वत्थामा विकर्णश्च सौमदत्तिस्तथैव च ॥ १-८॥

*bhavānbhīṣmaśca karṇaśca kṛpaśca samitiñjayaḥ  
aśvatthāmā vikarṇaśca saumadattistathaiva ca 1-8*

You yourself, and Bhishma, and Karna, and Kripa who is always victorious in war; Aswatthama, Vikarna, and the son of Somadatta as well.

### 1.9

अन्ये च बहवः शूरा मदर्थे त्यक्तजीविताः ।  
नानाशस्त्रप्रहरणाः सर्वे युद्धविशारदाः ॥ १-९॥

*anye ca bahavaḥ śūrā madarthe tyaktajīvitāḥ  
nānāśastrapraharaṇāḥ sarve yuddhaviśāradāḥ 1-9*

And there are many other heroes, ready to lay down their lives for my sake, carrying every kind of weapon and missile, all of them expert in warfare.

### 1.10

अपर्याप्तं तदस्माकं बलं भीष्माभिरक्षितम् ।  
पर्याप्तं त्विदमेतेषां बलं भीमाभिरक्षितम् ॥ १-१०॥

*aparyāptaṃ tadasmākaṃ balaṃ bhīṣmābhirakṣitam  
paryāptaṃ tvidameteṣāṃ balaṃ bhīmābhirakṣitam 1-10*

This army of ours, guarded by Bhishma, is not enough, while that army of theirs, guarded by Bhima, is enough. Alternatively: this army of ours, protected by Bhishma, is without limit, while that army of theirs, protected by Bhima, is limited.

### 1.11

अयनेषु च सर्वेषु यथाभागमवस्थिताः ।  
भीष्ममेवाभिरक्षन्तु भवन्तः सर्व एव हि ॥ १-११॥

*ayaneṣu ca sarveṣu yathābhāgamavasthitāḥ  
bhīṣmamevābhirakṣantu bhavantaḥ sarva eva hi 1-11*

So all of you, each holding his assigned post in the various divisions of the army, must guard Bhishma above everyone else.

### 1.12

तस्य सञ्जनयन्हर्षं कुरुवृद्धः पितामहः ।  
सिंहनादं विनद्योच्चैः शङ्खं दध्मौ प्रतापवान् ॥ १-१२॥

*tasya sañjanayanharṣaṃ kuruvṛddhaḥ pitāmahaḥ  
siṃhanādaṃ vinadyoccaiḥ śaṅkhaṃ dadhmau pratāpavān 1-12*

To lift Duryodhana's spirits, his illustrious grandsire Bhishma, the eldest of the Kauravas, then let out a roar like a lion's and blew his conch.

### 1.13

ततः शङ्खाश्च भेर्यश्च पणवानकगोमुखाः ।  
सहसैवाभ्यहन्यन्त स शब्दस्तुमुलोऽभवत् ॥ १-१३॥

*tataḥ śaṅkhāśca bheryaśca paṇavānakagomukhāḥ  
sahasaivābhyahanyanta sa śabdastumulo'bhavat 1-13*

Right after Bhishma, conches and kettledrums, tabors, drums and cow-horns all burst into sound at once, and the noise was overwhelming.

### 1.14

ततः श्वेतैर्हयैर्युक्ते महति स्यन्दने स्थितौ ।  
माधवः पाण्डवश्चैव दिव्यौ शङ्खौ प्रदध्मतुः ॥ १-१४॥

*tataḥ śvetairhayairyukte mahati syandane sthitau  
mādhavaḥ pāṇḍavaścaiva divyau śaṅkhau pradadhmatuḥ 1-14*

Then Madhava and the son of Pandu, seated in their splendid chariot drawn by white horses, blew their divine conches too.

### 1.15

पाञ्चजन्यं हृषीकेशो देवदत्तं धनञ्जयः ।  
पौण्ड्रं दध्मौ महाशङ्खं भीमकर्मा वृकोदरः ॥ १-१५॥

*pāñcajanyaṃ hṛṣīkeśo devadattaṃ dhanañjayaḥ  
pauṇḍraṃ dadhmau mahāśaṅkhaṃ bhīmakarmā vṛkodaraḥ 1-15*

Hrishikesha blew the Panchajanya, Dhananjaya (Arjuna) blew the Devadatta, and Vrikodara (Bhima), who performs fearsome deeds, blew his great conch called Paundra.

### 1.16

अनन्तविजयं राजा कुन्तीपुत्रो युधिष्ठिरः ।  
नकुलः सहदेवश्च सुघोषमणिपुष्पकौ ॥ १-१६॥

*anantavijayaṃ rājā kuntīputro yudhiṣṭhiraḥ  
nakulaḥ sahadevaśca sughoṣamaṇipuṣpakau 1-16*

King Yudhisthira, Kunti's son, blew the Anantavijaya; Nakula and Sahadeva blew the Sughosha and the Manipushpaka.

### 1.17

काश्यश्च परमेष्वासः शिखण्डी च महारथः ।  
धृष्टद्युम्नो विराटश्च सात्यकिश्चापराजितः ॥ १-१७॥

*kāśyaśca parameṣvāsaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca mahārathaḥ  
dhṛṣṭadyumno virāṭaśca sātyakiścāparājitaḥ 1-17*

The king of Kashi, a superb archer; Shikhandi, the mighty commander of eleven thousand archers; Dhristadyumna, Virata, and the undefeated Satyaki;

### 1.18

द्रुपदो द्रौपदेयाश्च सर्वशः पृथिवीपते ।  
सौभद्रश्च महाबाहुः शङ्खान्दध्मुः पृथक्पृथक् ॥ १-१८॥

*drupado draupadeyāśca sarvaśaḥ pṛthivīpate  
saubhadraśca mahābāhuḥ śaṅkhāndadhmuḥ pṛthakpṛthak 1-18*

Drupada, the sons of Draupadi, and the strong-armed son of Subhadra, O Lord of the Earth, each blew his own conch.

### 1.19

स घोषो धार्तराष्ट्राणां हृदयानि व्यदारयत् ।  
नभश्च पृथिवीं चैव तुमुलोऽभ्यनुनादयन् ॥ १-१९॥

*sa ghoṣo dhārtarāṣṭrāṇāṃ hṛdayāni vyadārayat  
nabhaśca pṛthivīṃ caiva tumulo'bhyanunādayan 1-19*

That thunderous sound tore at the hearts of Dhritarashtra's people and made both sky and earth echo with it.

### 1.20

अथ व्यवस्थितान्दृष्ट्वा धार्तराष्ट्रान् कपिध्वजः ।  
प्रवृत्ते शस्त्रसम्पाते धनुरुद्यम्य पाण्डवः ।  
हृषीकेशं तदा वाक्यमिदमाह महीपते ॥ १-२०॥

*atha vyavasthitāndṛṣṭvā dhārtarāṣṭrān kapidhvajaḥ  
pravṛtte śastrasampāte dhanurudyamya pāṇḍavaḥ  
hṛṣīkeśaṃ tadā vākyamidamāha mahīpate1-20*

Then, O Lord of the Earth, seeing Dhritarashtra's forces drawn up and the weapons about to fly, Arjuna, the son of Pandu, whose banner bore a monkey, lifted his bow and said these words to Krishna (Hrishikesha).

### 1.21

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
सेनयोरुभयोर्मध्ये रथं स्थापय मेऽच्युत ॥ १-२१॥

*arjuna uvāca  
senayorubhayormadhye rathaṃ sthāpaya me'cyuta 1-21*

Arjuna said: Achyuta, drive my chariot between the two armies so that I can look at the men standing here ready to fight, and learn, on the eve of this battle, whom I must fight against.

### 1.22

यावदेतान्निरीक्षेऽहं योद्धुकामानवस्थितान् ।  
कैर्मया सह योद्धव्यमस्मिन् रणसमुद्यमे ॥ १-२२॥

*yāvadetānnirīkṣe'haṃ yoddhukāmānavasthitān  
kairmayā saha yoddhavyamasmin raṇasamudyame 1-22*

Achyuta, drive my chariot between the two armies so that I can look at the men standing here ready to fight, and learn, on the eve of this battle, whom I must fight against.

### 1.23

योत्स्यमानानवेक्षेऽहं य एतेऽत्र समागताः ।  
धार्तराष्ट्रस्य दुर्बुद्धेर्युद्धे प्रियचिकीर्षवः ॥ १-२३॥

*yotsyamānānavekṣe'haṃ ya ete'tra samāgatāḥ  
dhārtarāṣṭrasya durbuddheryuddhe priyacikīrṣavaḥ 1-23*

I want to see those who have gathered here for the fight, hoping to please the wicked sons of Dhritarashtra in battle.

### 1.24

सञ्जय उवाच ।  
एवमुक्तो हृषीकेशो गुडाकेशेन भारत ।  
सेनयोरुभयोर्मध्ये स्थापयित्वा रथोत्तमम् ॥ १-२४॥

*sañjaya uvāca  
evamukto hṛṣīkeśo guḍākeśena bhārata  
senayorubhayormadhye sthāpayitvā rathottamam 1-24*

Sanjaya said: Spoken to in this way by Gudakesha, O Bharata, Hrishikesha drove that finest of chariots to a place between the two armies;

### 1.25

भीष्मद्रोणप्रमुखतः सर्वेषां च महीक्षिताम् ।  
उवाच पार्थ पश्यैतान् समवेतान्कुरूनिति॥ १-२५॥

*bhīṣmadroṇapramukhataḥ sarveṣāṃ ca mahīkṣitām  
uvāca pārtha paśyaitān samavetānkurūniti 1-25*

and there, before Bhishma and Drona and all the kings of the earth, he said: Partha, look at these Kurus assembled here.

### 1.26

तत्रापश्यत्स्थितान्पार्थः पितॄनथ पितामहान् ।  
आचार्यान्मातुलान्भ्रातॄन् पुत्रान्पौत्रान्सखींस्तथा ॥ १-२६॥

*tatrāpaśyatsthitānpārthaḥ pitṝnatha pitāmahān  
ācāryānmātulānbhrātṝn putrānpautrānsakhīṃstathā 1-26*

Then Partha saw, standing in both armies, fathers, grandfathers, teachers, maternal uncles, brothers, sons, grandsons, and friends as well.

### 1.27

श्वशुरान्सुहृदश्चैव सेनयोरुभयोरपि ।  
तान्समीक्ष्य स कौन्तेयः सर्वान्बन्धूनवस्थितान् ।  
कृपया परयाविष्टो विषीदन्निदमब्रवीत्  ॥ १-२७॥

*śvaśurānsuhṛdaścaiva senayorubhayorapi  
tānsamīkṣya sa kaunteyaḥ sarvānbandhūnavasthitān  
kṛpayā parayāviṣṭo viṣīdannidamabravīt 1-27*

He saw fathers-in-law and companions too in both armies. Seeing all these relatives lined up for battle, the son of Kunti, overcome with deep compassion, spoke these sorrowful words.

### 1.28

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
दृष्ट्वेमं स्वजनं कृष्ण युयुत्सुं समुपस्थितम् ॥ १-२८॥

*arjuna uvāca  
dṛṣṭvemaṃ svajanaṃ kṛṣṇa yuyutsuṃ samupasthitam 1-28*

Arjuna said: Krishna, when I see my own kinsmen standing here, eager for battle,

### 1.29

सीदन्ति मम गात्राणि मुखं च परिशुष्यति ।  
वेपथुश्च शरीरे मे रोमहर्षश्च जायते ॥ १-२९॥

*sīdanti mama gātrāṇi mukhaṃ ca pariśuṣyati  
vepathuśca śarīre me romaharṣaśca jāyate 1-29*

my limbs give way and my mouth goes dry; my body trembles and my hair stands on end.

### 1.30

गाण्डीवं स्रंसते हस्तात्त्वक्चैव परिदह्यते ।  
न च शक्नोम्यवस्थातुं भ्रमतीव च मे मनः ॥ १-३०॥

*gāṇḍīvaṃ sraṃsate hastāttvakcaiva paridahyate  
na ca śaknomyavasthātuṃ bhramatīva ca me manaḥ 1-30*

The Gandiva bow slips from my grip and my skin burns all over. I cannot even stand, and my mind seems to be spinning.

### 1.31

निमित्तानि च पश्यामि विपरीतानि केशव ।  
न च श्रेयोऽनुपश्यामि हत्वा स्वजनमाहवे ॥ १-३१॥

*nimittāni ca paśyāmi viparītāni keśava  
na ca śreyo'nupaśyāmi hatvā svajanamāhave 1-31*

I see bad omens too, Keshava, and I can see nothing good coming from killing my own relatives in battle.

### 1.32

न काङ्क्षे विजयं कृष्ण न च राज्यं सुखानि च ।  
किं नो राज्येन गोविन्द किं भोगैर्जीवितेन वा ॥ १-३२॥

*na kāṅkṣe vijayaṃ kṛṣṇa na ca rājyaṃ sukhāni ca  
kiṃ no rājyena govinda kiṃ bhogairjīvitena vā 1-32*

Krishna, I have no wish for victory, or for a kingdom, or for pleasures. What use is a kingdom to us, Govinda? What use are pleasures, or even life itself?

### 1.33

येषामर्थे काङ्क्षितं नो राज्यं भोगाः सुखानि च ।  
त इमेऽवस्थिता युद्धे प्राणांस्त्यक्त्वा धनानि च ॥ १-३३॥

*yeṣāmarthe kāṅkṣitaṃ no rājyaṃ bhogāḥ sukhāni ca  
ta ime'vasthitā yuddhe prāṇāṃstyaktvā dhanāni ca 1-33*

The very people for whose sake we want a kingdom, enjoyments and pleasures are standing here in battle, having given up their lives and their wealth.

### 1.34

आचार्याः पितरः पुत्रास्तथैव च पितामहाः ।  
मातुलाः श्वशुराः पौत्राः श्यालाः सम्बन्धिनस्तथा ॥ १-३४॥

*ācāryāḥ pitaraḥ putrāstathaiva ca pitāmahāḥ  
mātulāḥ śvaśurāḥ pautrāḥ śyālāḥ sambandhinastathā 1-34*

Teachers, fathers, sons and grandfathers; maternal uncles, fathers-in-law, grandsons, brothers-in-law and other relatives.

### 1.35

एतान्न हन्तुमिच्छामि घ्नतोऽपि मधुसूदन ।  
अपि त्रैलोक्यराज्यस्य हेतोः किं नु महीकृते ॥ १-३५॥

*etānna hantumicchāmi ghnato'pi madhusūdana  
api trailokyarājyasya hetoḥ kiṃ nu mahīkṛte 1-35*

Madhusudana, I do not want to kill them even if they kill me, not even to rule all three worlds, let alone for this earth.

### 1.36

निहत्य धार्तराष्ट्रान्नः का प्रीतिः स्याज्जनार्दन ।  
पापमेवाश्रयेदस्मान् हत्वैतानाततायिनः ॥ १-३६॥

*nihatya dhārtarāṣṭrānnaḥ kā prītiḥ syājjanārdana  
pāpamevāśrayedasmān hatvaitānātatāyinaḥ 1-36*

What joy could we find, Janardana, in killing the sons of Dhritarashtra? Killing these criminals would bring us nothing but sin.

### 1.37

तस्मान्नार्हा वयं हन्तुं धार्तराष्ट्रान्स्वबान्धवान् ।  
स्वजनं हि कथं हत्वा सुखिनः स्याम माधव ॥ १-३७॥

*tasmānnārhā vayaṃ hantuṃ dhārtarāṣṭrānsvabāndhavān  
svajanaṃ hi kathaṃ hatvā sukhinaḥ syāma mādhava 1-37*

So we should not kill the sons of Dhritarashtra, who are our own relatives. How could we ever be happy, Madhava, after killing our own people?

### 1.38

यद्यप्येते न पश्यन्ति लोभोपहतचेतसः ।  
कुलक्षयकृतं दोषं मित्रद्रोहे च पातकम् ॥ १-३८॥

*yadyapyete na paśyanti lobhopahatacetasaḥ  
kulakṣayakṛtaṃ doṣaṃ mitradrohe ca pātakam 1-38*

Even if these men, their judgment clouded by greed, see nothing wrong in destroying families in society and no sin in betraying friends,

### 1.39

कथं न ज्ञेयमस्माभिः पापादस्मान्निवर्तितुम् ।  
कुलक्षयकृतं दोषं प्रपश्यद्भिर्जनार्दन ॥ १-३९॥

*kathaṃ na jñeyamasmābhiḥ pāpādasmānnivartitum  
kulakṣayakṛtaṃ doṣaṃ prapaśyadbhirjanārdana 1-39*

why should we, who plainly see the evil in destroying family units, not learn to turn away from this sin, Janardana?

### 1.40

कुलक्षये प्रणश्यन्ति कुलधर्माः सनातनाः ।  
धर्मे नष्टे कुलं कृत्स्नमधर्मोऽभिभवत्युत ॥ १-४०॥

*kulakṣaye praṇaśyanti kuladharmāḥ sanātanāḥ  
dharme naṣṭe kulaṃ kṛtsnamadharmo'bhibhavatyuta 1-40*

When a family is destroyed, its age-old religious traditions die with it; and once spirituality is lost, impiety takes hold of the entire family.

### 1.41

अधर्माभिभवात्कृष्ण प्रदुष्यन्ति कुलस्त्रियः ।  
स्त्रीषु दुष्टासु वार्ष्णेय जायते वर्णसङ्करः ॥ १-४१॥

*adharmābhibhavātkṛṣṇa praduṣyanti kulastriyaḥ  
strīṣu duṣṭāsu vārṣṇeya jāyate varṇasaṅkaraḥ 1-41*

When impiety spreads, Krishna, the women of the family become corrupted; and when the women are corrupted, descendant of the Vrishnis, the castes become mixed (varna-samkara).

### 1.42

सङ्करो नरकायैव कुलघ्नानां कुलस्य च ।  
पतन्ति पितरो ह्येषां लुप्तपिण्डोदकक्रियाः ॥ १-४२॥

*saṅkaro narakāyaiva kulaghnānāṃ kulasya ca  
patanti pitaro hyeṣāṃ luptapiṇḍodakakriyāḥ 1-42*

This mixing of castes sends the destroyers of the family to hell, and their ancestors fall too, deprived of their offerings of pinda (rice-balls) and water libations.

### 1.43

दोषैरेतैः कुलघ्नानां वर्णसङ्करकारकैः ।  
उत्साद्यन्ते जातिधर्माः कुलधर्माश्च शाश्वताः ॥ १-४३॥

*doṣairetaiḥ kulaghnānāṃ varṇasaṅkarakārakaiḥ  
utsādyante jātidharmāḥ kuladharmāśca śāśvatāḥ 1-43*

Through these wrongful acts of the family's destroyers, which bring about the mixing of castes, the everlasting religious traditions of caste and family are wiped out.

### 1.44

उत्सन्नकुलधर्माणां मनुष्याणां जनार्दन ।  
नरके नियतं वासो भवतीत्यनुशुश्रुम ॥ १-४४॥

*utsannakuladharmāṇāṃ manuṣyāṇāṃ janārdana  
narake niyataṃ vāso bhavatītyanuśuśruma 1-44*

We have heard, Janardana, that people whose family religious practices have been destroyed must inevitably live in hell for an unknown length of time.

### 1.45

अहो बत महत्पापं कर्तुं व्यवसिता वयम् ।  
यद्राज्यसुखलोभेन हन्तुं स्वजनमुद्यताः ॥ १-४५॥

*aho bata mahatpāpaṃ kartuṃ vyavasitā vayam  
yadrājyasukhalobhena hantuṃ svajanamudyatāḥ 1-45*

What a tragedy! We have set ourselves on a terrible sin: out of greed for the pleasures of a kingdom, we are ready to kill our own relatives.

### 1.46

यदि मामप्रतीकारमशस्त्रं शस्त्रपाणयः ।  
धार्तराष्ट्रा रणे हन्युस्तन्मे क्षेमतरं भवेत् ॥ १-४६॥

*yadi māmapratīkāramaśastraṃ śastrapāṇayaḥ  
dhārtarāṣṭrā raṇe hanyustanme kṣemataraṃ bhavet 1-46*

It would be better for me if the sons of Dhritarashtra, weapons in hand, killed me in battle while I stood unarmed and offered no resistance.

### 1.47

सञ्जय उवाच ।  
एवमुक्त्वार्जुनः सङ्ख्ये रथोपस्थ उपाविशत् ।  
विसृज्य सशरं चापं शोकसंविग्नमानसः ॥ १-४७॥   
  
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु  
ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे  
अर्जुनविषादयोगो नाम प्रथमोऽध्यायः ॥ १॥

*sañjaya uvāca  
evamuktvārjunaḥ saṅkhye rathopastha upāviśat  
visṛjya saśaraṃ cāpaṃ śokasaṃvignamānasaḥ 1-47  
  
oṃ tatsaditi śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu  
brahmavidyāyāṃ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṃvāde  
arjunaviṣādayogo nāma prathamo'dhyāyaḥ 1*

Sanjaya said: Having spoken this way on the battlefield, Arjuna threw down his bow and arrows and sank onto the seat of the chariot, his mind overwhelmed by grief.

So ends the first discourse, called "The Yoga of Arjuna's Despondency," in the Upanishads of the glorious Bhagavad Gita, the science of the Eternal, the scripture of Yoga, the dialogue between Sri Krishna and Arjuna.
