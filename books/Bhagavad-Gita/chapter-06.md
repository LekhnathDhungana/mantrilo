# Chapter 6 – Dhyāna Yoga (The Yoga of Meditation)

*Yoga of Meditation*

### 6.1

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
अनाश्रितः कर्मफलं कार्यं कर्म करोति यः ।  
स संन्यासी च योगी च न निरग्निर्न चाक्रियः ॥ ६-१॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
anāśritaḥ karmaphalaṃ kāryaṃ karma karoti yaḥ  
sa saṃnyāsī ca yogī ca na niragnirna cākriyaḥ 6-1*

The Blessed Lord said: The person who carries out the duty laid upon him without leaning on the results of his actions is both a SAMNYASIN and a YOGIN. The one who has merely given up the sacred fire and all activity is not.

### 6.2

यं संन्यासमिति प्राहुर्योगं तं विद्धि पाण्डव ।  
न ह्यसंन्यस्तसङ्कल्पो योगी भवति कश्चन ॥ ६-२॥

*yaṃ saṃnyāsamiti prāhuryogaṃ taṃ viddhi pāṇḍava  
na hyasaṃnyastasaṅkalpo yogī bhavati kaścana 6-2*

O Pandava, understand that what people call renunciation is in fact YOGA. No one can truly become a YOGI without first giving up thoughts.

### 6.3

आरुरुक्षोर्मुनेर्योगं कर्म कारणमुच्यते ।  
योगारूढस्य तस्यैव शमः कारणमुच्यते ॥ ६-३॥

*ārurukṣormuneryogaṃ karma kāraṇamucyate  
yogārūḍhasya tasyaiva śamaḥ kāraṇamucyate 6-3*

For the MUNI, the sage who wants to reach YOGA, action is described as the path. For that same sage once YOGA has been reached, inaction, or stillness, is described as the path.

### 6.4

यदा हि नेन्द्रियार्थेषु न कर्मस्वनुषज्जते ।  
सर्वसङ्कल्पसंन्यासी योगारूढस्तदोच्यते ॥ ६-४॥

*yadā hi nendriyārtheṣu na karmasvanuṣajjate  
sarvasaṅkalpasaṃnyāsī yogārūḍhastadocyate 6-4*

When a person has let go of all thoughts and feels no attachment to the objects of the senses or to actions, that person is said to have reached YOGA.

### 6.5

उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानं नात्मानमवसादयेत् ।  
आत्मैव ह्यात्मनो बन्धुरात्मैव रिपुरात्मनः ॥ ६-५॥

*uddharedātmanātmānaṃ nātmānamavasādayet  
ātmaiva hyātmano bandhurātmaiva ripurātmanaḥ 6-5*

A person should raise himself up through his own Self and never drag himself down. This Self alone is one's own friend, and this Self alone is one's own enemy.

### 6.6

बन्धुरात्मात्मनस्तस्य येनात्मैवात्मना जितः ।  
अनात्मनस्तु शत्रुत्वे वर्तेतात्मैव शत्रुवत् ॥ ६-६॥

*bandhurātmātmanastasya yenātmaivātmanā jitaḥ  
anātmanastu śatrutve vartetātmaiva śatruvat 6-6*

For the one who has mastered himself through the Self, the Self is a friend. For the one who has not mastered himself, the Self behaves like an enemy, just as an outside foe would.

### 6.7

जितात्मनः प्रशान्तस्य परमात्मा समाहितः ।  
शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु तथा मानापमानयोः ॥ ६-७॥

*jitātmanaḥ praśāntasya paramātmā samāhitaḥ  
śītoṣṇasukhaduḥkheṣu tathā mānāpamānayoḥ 6-7*

The Supreme Self of a person who is self-controlled and at peace stays even in cold and heat, in pleasure and pain, and in honour and dishonour alike.

### 6.8

ज्ञानविज्ञानतृप्तात्मा कूटस्थो विजितेन्द्रियः ।  
युक्त इत्युच्यते योगी समलोष्टाश्मकाञ्चनः ॥ ६-८॥

*jñānavijñānatṛptātmā kūṭastho vijitendriyaḥ  
yukta ityucyate yogī samaloṣṭāśmakāñcanaḥ 6-8*

The YOGI who is content with knowledge and wisdom, who stays unshaken, who has mastered the senses, and for whom a clod of earth, a stone, and gold are all the same, is said to be harmonised (that is, to have reached NIRVIKALPA SAMADHI).

### 6.9

सुहृन्मित्रार्युदासीन मध्यस्थद्वेष्यबन्धुषु ।  
साधुष्वपि च पापेषु समबुद्धिर्विशिष्यते ॥ ६-९॥

*suhṛnmitrāryudāsīna madhyasthadveṣyabandhuṣu  
sādhuṣvapi ca pāpeṣu samabuddhirviśiṣyate 6-9*

The one who holds the same attitude toward well-wishers, friends, enemies, the indifferent, the neutral, the hateful, relatives, the righteous, and the unrighteous stands above the rest.

### 6.10

योगी युञ्जीत सततमात्मानं रहसि स्थितः ।  
एकाकी यतचित्तात्मा निराशीरपरिग्रहः ॥ ६-१०॥

*yogī yuñjīta satatamātmānaṃ rahasi sthitaḥ  
ekākī yatacittātmā nirāśīraparigrahaḥ 6-10*

The YOGI should keep working to steady the mind, staying in a solitary place by himself, with body and mind under control, and free from hope and greed.

### 6.11

शुचौ देशे प्रतिष्ठाप्य स्थिरमासनमात्मनः ।  
नात्युच्छ्रितं नातिनीचं चैलाजिनकुशोत्तरम् ॥ ६-११॥

*śucau deśe pratiṣṭhāpya sthiramāsanamātmanaḥ  
nātyucchritaṃ nātinīcaṃ cailājinakuśottaram 6-11*

In a clean place he should set up a firm seat of his own, neither too high nor too low, made of a cloth, a skin, and KUSHA grass laid one on top of the other . . .

### 6.12

तत्रैकाग्रं मनः कृत्वा यतचित्तेन्द्रियक्रियः ।  
उपविश्यासने युञ्ज्याद्योगमात्मविशुद्धये ॥ ६-१२॥

*tatraikāgraṃ manaḥ kṛtvā yatacittendriyakriyaḥ  
upaviśyāsane yuñjyādyogamātmaviśuddhaye 6-12*

Sitting there on that seat, with the mind brought to a single point and the activity of mind and senses under control, he should practise YOGA in order to purify the self.

### 6.13

समं कायशिरोग्रीवं धारयन्नचलं स्थिरः ।  
सम्प्रेक्ष्य नासिकाग्रं स्वं दिशश्चानवलोकयन् ॥ ६-१३॥

*samaṃ kāyaśirogrīvaṃ dhārayannacalaṃ sthiraḥ  
samprekṣya nāsikāgraṃ svaṃ diśaścānavalokayan 6-13*

He should hold body, head, and neck upright, steady, and motionless, with his gaze fixed on the tip of his nose and not wandering about.

### 6.14

प्रशान्तात्मा विगतभी र्ब्रह्मचारिव्रते स्थितः ।  
मनः संयम्य मच्चित्तो युक्त आसीत मत्परः ॥ ६-१४॥

*praśāntātmā vigatabhīr brahmacārivrate sthitaḥ  
manaḥ saṃyamya maccitto yukta āsīta matparaḥ 6-14*

With a calm mind, free from fear, steadfast in the vow of BRAHMACHARYA, with the mind under control, thinking of Me and keeping balanced, he should sit with Me as his highest goal.

### 6.15

युञ्जन्नेवं सदात्मानं योगी नियतमानसः ।  
शान्तिं निर्वाणपरमां मत्संस्थामधिगच्छति ॥ ६-१५॥

*yuñjannevaṃ sadātmānaṃ yogī niyatamānasaḥ  
śāntiṃ nirvāṇaparamāṃ matsaṃsthāmadhigacchati 6-15*

By keeping the mind steady in this way at all times, the YOGI with his mind under control reaches the Peace that rests in Me, and that Peace ends in complete liberation (NIRVANA or MOKSHA).

### 6.16

नात्यश्नतस्तु योगोऽस्ति न चैकान्तमनश्नतः ।  
न चातिस्वप्नशीलस्य जाग्रतो नैव चार्जुन ॥ ६-१६॥

*nātyaśnatastu yogo'sti na caikāntamanaśnataḥ  
na cātisvapnaśīlasya jāgrato naiva cārjuna 6-16*

YOGA is truly out of reach for the one who overeats and for the one who does not eat at all, for the one who sleeps too much and for the one who stays awake all the time, O Arjuna.

### 6.17

युक्ताहारविहारस्य युक्तचेष्टस्य कर्मसु ।  
युक्तस्वप्नावबोधस्य योगो भवति दुःखहा ॥ ६-१७॥

*yuktāhāravihārasya yuktaceṣṭasya karmasu  
yuktasvapnāvabodhasya yogo bhavati duḥkhahā 6-17*

For the person who is moderate in food and recreation, moderate in effort while acting, and moderate in sleep and waking, YOGA becomes the destroyer of pain.

### 6.18

यदा विनियतं चित्तमात्मन्येवावतिष्ठते ।  
निःस्पृहः सर्वकामेभ्यो युक्त इत्युच्यते तदा ॥ ६-१८॥

*yadā viniyataṃ cittamātmanyevāvatiṣṭhate  
niḥspṛhaḥ sarvakāmebhyo yukta ityucyate tadā 6-18*

When the fully mastered mind rests in the Self alone and is free of craving for every object of desire, then that person is said to be united (YUKTAH).

### 6.19

यथा दीपो निवातस्थो नेङ्गते सोपमा स्मृता ।  
योगिनो यतचित्तस्य युञ्जतो योगमात्मनः ॥ ६-१९॥

*yathā dīpo nivātastho neṅgate sopamā smṛtā  
yogino yatacittasya yuñjato yogamātmanaḥ 6-19*

A lamp set in a place without wind does not flicker. This is the image used for the YOGI whose mind is controlled and who is practising the YOGA of the Self (or who is absorbed in the YOGA of the Self).

### 6.20

यत्रोपरमते चित्तं निरुद्धं योगसेवया ।  
यत्र चैवात्मनात्मानं पश्यन्नात्मनि तुष्यति ॥ ६-२०॥

*yatroparamate cittaṃ niruddhaṃ yogasevayā  
yatra caivātmanātmānaṃ paśyannātmani tuṣyati 6-20*

When the mind, held in check by the practice of YOGA, grows quiet, and when, seeing the Self through the self, he finds contentment in his own Self;

### 6.21

सुखमात्यन्तिकं यत्तद् बुद्धिग्राह्यमतीन्द्रियम् ।  
वेत्ति यत्र न चैवायं स्थितश्चलति तत्त्वतः ॥ ६-२१॥

*sukhamātyantikaṃ yattad buddhigrāhyamatīndriyam  
vetti yatra na caivāyaṃ sthitaścalati tattvataḥ 6-21*

When he (the YOGI) experiences that boundless bliss which the (pure) intellect can grasp but which lies beyond the senses, and, settled in it, never strays from the Reality;

### 6.22

यं लब्ध्वा चापरं लाभं मन्यते नाधिकं ततः ।  
यस्मिन्स्थितो न दुःखेन गुरुणापि विचाल्यते ॥ ६-२२॥

*yaṃ labdhvā cāparaṃ lābhaṃ manyate nādhikaṃ tataḥ  
yasminsthito na duḥkhena guruṇāpi vicālyate 6-22*

When, having gained it, he regards no other gain as greater, and, settled in it, he is not shaken even by heavy sorrow;

### 6.23

तं विद्याद् दुःखसंयोगवियोगं योगसंज्ञितम् ।  
स निश्चयेन योक्तव्यो योगोऽनिर्विण्णचेतसा ॥ ६-२३॥

*taṃ vidyād duḥkhasaṃyogaviyogaṃ yogasaṃjñitam  
sa niścayena yoktavyo yogo'nirviṇṇacetasā 6-23*

Know this: YOGA is the cutting away of the bond with pain. This YOGA is to be practised with resolve, with a mind that is steady and never gives in to despair.

### 6.24

सङ्कल्पप्रभवान्कामांस्त्यक्त्वा सर्वानशेषतः ।  
मनसैवेन्द्रियग्रामं विनियम्य समन्ततः ॥ ६-२४॥

*saṅkalpaprabhavānkāmāṃs tyaktvā sarvānaśeṣataḥ  
manasaivendriyagrāmaṃ viniyamya samantataḥ 6-24*

Giving up entirely every desire that springs from SANKALPA, and fully holding back the whole band of senses with the mind on every side,

### 6.25

शनैः शनैरुपरमेद् बुद्ध्या धृतिगृहीतया ।  
आत्मसंस्थं मनः कृत्वा न किञ्चिदपि चिन्तयेत् ॥ ६-२५॥

*śanaiḥ śanairuparamed buddhyā dhṛtigṛhītayā  
ātmasaṃsthaṃ manaḥ kṛtvā na kiñcidapi cintayet 6-25*

step by step he should arrive at stillness through a firmly held intellect. With the mind settled in the Self, he should think of nothing at all.

### 6.26

यतो यतो निश्चरति मनश्चञ्चलमस्थिरम् ।  
ततस्ततो नियम्यैतदात्मन्येव वशं नयेत् ॥ ६-२६॥

*yato yato niścarati manaścañcalamasthiram  
tatastato niyamyaitadātmanyeva vaśaṃ nayet 6-26*

Whatever makes the restless, unsteady mind drift away, he should pull it back from that cause and bring it once more under the sole control of the Self.

### 6.27

प्रशान्तमनसं ह्येनं योगिनं सुखमुत्तमम् ।  
उपैति शान्तरजसं ब्रह्मभूतमकल्मषम् ॥ ६-२७॥

*praśāntamanasaṃ hyenaṃ yoginaṃ sukhamuttamam  
upaiti śāntarajasaṃ brahmabhūtamakalmaṣam 6-27*

Supreme Bliss truly comes to this YOGI whose mind is fully at peace, whose passion has been stilled, who is free from sin, and who has become BRAHMAN.

### 6.28

युञ्जन्नेवं सदात्मानं योगी विगतकल्मषः ।  
सुखेन ब्रह्मसंस्पर्शमत्यन्तं सुखमश्नुते ॥ ६-२८॥

*yuñjannevaṃ sadātmānaṃ yogī vigatakalmaṣaḥ  
sukhena brahmasaṃsparśamatyantaṃ sukhamaśnute 6-28*

By engaging the mind in this way (in the practice of YOGA), the YOGI, freed from sins, easily enjoys the limitless Bliss that comes from contact with BRAHMAN.

### 6.29

सर्वभूतस्थमात्मानं सर्वभूतानि चात्मनि ।  
ईक्षते योगयुक्तात्मा सर्वत्र समदर्शनः ॥ ६-२९॥

*sarvabhūtasthamātmānaṃ sarvabhūtāni cātmani  
īkṣate yogayuktātmā sarvatra samadarśanaḥ 6-29*

With his mind brought into harmony through YOGA, he sees the Self dwelling in every being and every being dwelling in the Self. He sees the same thing wherever he looks.

### 6.30

यो मां पश्यति सर्वत्र सर्वं च मयि पश्यति ।  
तस्याहं न प्रणश्यामि स च मे न प्रणश्यति ॥ ६-३०॥

*yo māṃ paśyati sarvatra sarvaṃ ca mayi paśyati  
tasyāhaṃ na praṇaśyāmi sa ca me na praṇaśyati 6-30*

Whoever sees Me everywhere and sees everything in Me is never lost to Me, and I am never lost to him.

### 6.31

सर्वभूतस्थितं यो मां भजत्येकत्वमास्थितः ।  
सर्वथा वर्तमानोऽपि स योगी मयि वर्तते ॥ ६-३१॥

*sarvabhūtasthitaṃ yo māṃ bhajatyekatvamāsthitaḥ  
sarvathā vartamāno'pi sa yogī mayi vartate 6-31*

The YOGI who, grounded in unity, worships Me as the one dwelling in all beings lives in Me, no matter how he happens to live.

### 6.32

आत्मौपम्येन सर्वत्र समं पश्यति योऽर्जुन ।  
सुखं वा यदि वा दुःखं स योगी परमो मतः ॥ ६-३२॥

*ātmaupamyena sarvatra samaṃ paśyati yo'rjuna  
sukhaṃ vā yadi vā duḥkhaṃ sa yogī paramo mataḥ 6-32*

O Arjuna, the one who, through the sameness of the Self, sees equality everywhere, whether in pleasure or in pain, is considered the highest YOGI.

### 6.33

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
योऽयं योगस्त्वया प्रोक्तः साम्येन मधुसूदन ।  
एतस्याहं न पश्यामि चञ्चलत्वात्स्थितिं स्थिराम् ॥ ६-३३॥

*arjuna uvāca  
yo'yaṃ yogastvayā proktaḥ sāmyena madhusūdana  
etasyāhaṃ na paśyāmi cañcalatvātsthitiṃ sthirām 6-33*

Arjuna said: O slayer of Madhu, this YOGA of Equanimity that You have taught, I cannot see it lasting steadily, because the mind is so restless.

### 6.34

चञ्चलं हि मनः कृष्ण प्रमाथि बलवद् दृढम् ।  
तस्याहं निग्रहं मन्ये वायोरिव सुदुष्करम् ॥ ६-३४॥

*cañcalaṃ hi manaḥ kṛṣṇa pramāthi balavad dṛḍham  
tasyāhaṃ nigrahaṃ manye vāyoriva suduṣkaram 6-34*

O Krishna, the mind is truly restless, turbulent, strong, and stubborn. I think it is as hard to hold in check as the wind.

### 6.35

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
असंशयं महाबाहो मनो दुर्निग्रहं चलम् ।  
अभ्यासेन तु कौन्तेय वैराग्येण च गृह्यते ॥ ६-३५॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
asaṃśayaṃ mahābāho mano durnigrahaṃ calam  
abhyāsena tu kaunteya vairāgyeṇa ca gṛhyate 6-35*

The Blessed Lord said: There is no doubt, O mighty-armed one, that the mind is restless and hard to control. Yet, O son of Kunti, it can be held in check through practice and through dispassion.

### 6.36

असंयतात्मना योगो दुष्प्राप इति मे मतिः ।  
वश्यात्मना तु यतता शक्योऽवाप्तुमुपायतः ॥ ६-३६॥

*asaṃyatātmanā yogo duṣprāpa iti me matiḥ  
vaśyātmanā tu yatatā śakyo'vāptumupāyataḥ 6-36*

In My view, YOGA is hard to reach for someone whose self is uncontrolled. But the self-controlled person who keeps striving can reach it by the (right) means.

### 6.37

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
अयतिः श्रद्धयोपेतो योगाच्चलितमानसः ।  
अप्राप्य योगसंसिद्धिं कां गतिं कृष्ण गच्छति ॥ ६-३७॥

*arjuna uvāca  
ayatiḥ śraddhayopeto yogāccalitamānasaḥ  
aprāpya yogasaṃsiddhiṃ kāṃ gatiṃ kṛṣṇa gacchati 6-37*

Arjuna said: O Krishna, what becomes of the person who has faith but cannot control himself, whose mind drifts away from YOGA, and who therefore fails to reach perfection in YOGA? Where does he end up?

### 6.38

कच्चिन्नोभयविभ्रष्टश्छिन्नाभ्रमिव नश्यति ।  
अप्रतिष्ठो महाबाहो विमूढो ब्रह्मणः पथि ॥ ६-३८॥

*kaccinnobhayavibhraṣṭaś chinnābhramiva naśyati  
apratiṣṭho mahābāho vimūḍho brahmaṇaḥ pathi 6-38*

O mighty-armed one, having fallen from both, does he not perish like a torn cloud, with no support, lost and confused on the path of BRAHMAN?

### 6.39

एतन्मे संशयं कृष्ण छेत्तुमर्हस्यशेषतः ।  
त्वदन्यः संशयस्यास्य छेत्ता न ह्युपपद्यते ॥ ६-३९॥

*etanme saṃśayaṃ kṛṣṇa chettumarhasyaśeṣataḥ  
tvadanyaḥ saṃśayasyāsya chettā na hyupapadyate 6-39*

O Krishna, please remove this doubt of mine completely, for no one but You is able to clear it away.

### 6.40

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
पार्थ नैवेह नामुत्र विनाशस्तस्य विद्यते ।  
न हि कल्याणकृत्कश्चिद् दुर्गतिं तात गच्छति ॥ ६-४०॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
pārtha naiveha nāmutra vināśastasya vidyate  
na hi kalyāṇakṛtkaścid durgatiṃ tāta gacchati 6-40*

The Blessed Lord said: O Partha, there is no ruin for such a person, neither in this world nor in the next. My son, no one who strives to do good ever comes to a bad end.

### 6.41

प्राप्य पुण्यकृतां लोकानुषित्वा शाश्वतीः समाः ।  
शुचीनां श्रीमतां गेहे योगभ्रष्टोऽभिजायते ॥ ६-४१॥

*prāpya puṇyakṛtāṃ lokānuṣitvā śāśvatīḥ samāḥ  
śucīnāṃ śrīmatāṃ gehe yogabhraṣṭo'bhijāyate 6-41*

The one who has fallen from YOGA reaches the worlds of the righteous, lives there for many long years, and is then born again into a pure and prosperous household.

### 6.42

अथवा योगिनामेव कुले भवति धीमताम् ।  
एतद्धि दुर्लभतरं लोके जन्म यदीदृशम् ॥ ६-४२॥

*athavā yogināmeva kule bhavati dhīmatām  
etaddhi durlabhataraṃ loke janma yadīdṛśam 6-42*

Or he may even be born into a family of wise YOGIS. A birth like that is indeed very hard to come by in this world.

### 6.43

तत्र तं बुद्धिसंयोगं लभते पौर्वदेहिकम् ।  
यतते च ततो भूयः संसिद्धौ कुरुनन्दन ॥ ६-४३॥

*tatra taṃ buddhisaṃyogaṃ labhate paurvadehikam  
yatate ca tato bhūyaḥ saṃsiddhau kurunandana 6-43*

There he regains the knowledge he had gathered in his earlier body and strives harder than before for Perfection, O son of the Kurus.

### 6.44

पूर्वाभ्यासेन तेनैव ह्रियते ह्यवशोऽपि सः ।  
जिज्ञासुरपि योगस्य शब्दब्रह्मातिवर्तते ॥ ६-४४॥

*pūrvābhyāsena tenaiva hriyate hyavaśo'pi saḥ  
jijñāsurapi yogasya śabdabrahmātivartate 6-44*

That earlier practice carries him forward whether he wills it or not. Even the person who simply wants to know YOGA passes beyond the SHABDA BRAHMAN.

### 6.45

प्रयत्नाद्यतमानस्तु योगी संशुद्धकिल्बिषः ।  
अनेकजन्मसंसिद्धस्ततो याति परां गतिम् ॥ ६-४५॥

*prayatnādyatamānastu yogī saṃśuddhakilbiṣaḥ  
anekajanmasaṃsiddhas tato yāti parāṃ gatim 6-45*

But the YOGI who strives with diligence, cleansed of sins and (gradually) perfected over many births, then reaches the highest Goal.

### 6.46

तपस्विभ्योऽधिको योगी ज्ञानिभ्योऽपि मतोऽधिकः ।  
कर्मिभ्यश्चाधिको योगी तस्माद्योगी भवार्जुन ॥ ६-४६॥

*tapasvibhyo'dhiko yogī jñānibhyo'pi mato'dhikaḥ  
karmibhyaścādhiko yogī tasmādyogī bhavārjuna 6-46*

The YOGI is held to be greater than the ascetics, greater even than the men of knowledge (mere scholars), and greater than the men of action. Therefore, O Arjuna, (strive to) be a YOGI.

### 6.47

योगिनामपि सर्वेषां मद्गतेनान्तरात्मना ।  
श्रद्धावान्भजते यो मां स मे युक्ततमो मतः ॥ ६-४७॥   
  
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु  
ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे  
आत्मसंयमयोगो नाम षष्ठोऽध्यायः ॥ ६॥

*yogināmapi sarveṣāṃ madgatenāntarātmanā  
śraddhāvānbhajate yo māṃ sa me yuktatamo mataḥ 6-47  
  
oṃ tatsaditi śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu  
brahmavidyāyāṃ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṃvāde  
ātmasaṃyamayogo nāma ṣaṣṭho'dhyāyaḥ 6*

And of all YOGIS, the one who is full of faith, whose inner self is merged in Me, and who worships Me, is in My view the most devout.

Thus, in the Upanishads of the glorious Bhagavad Gita, the science of the Eternal, the scripture of Yoga, the dialogue between Sri Krishna and Arjuna, ends the sixth discourse, entitled "The Yoga of Meditation."
