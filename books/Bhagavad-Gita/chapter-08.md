# Chapter 8 – Akṣara-Brahma Yoga (The Yoga of the Imperishable Absolute)

*Yoga of Imperishable Brahman*

### 8.1

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
किं तद् ब्रह्म किमध्यात्मं किं कर्म पुरुषोत्तम ।  
अधिभूतं च किं प्रोक्तमधिदैवं किमुच्यते ॥ ८-१॥

*arjuna uvāca  
kiṃ tad brahma kimadhyātmaṃ kiṃ karma puruṣottama  
adhibhūtaṃ ca kiṃ proktamadhidaivaṃ kimucyate 8-1*

Arjuna said: What is that BRAHMAN? What is the ADHYATMA? What is action? O best of men, what is said to be the ADHIBHUTA, and what is meant by ADHIDAIVA?

### 8.2

अधियज्ञः कथं कोऽत्र देहेऽस्मिन्मधुसूदन ।  
प्रयाणकाले च कथं ज्ञेयोऽसि नियतात्मभिः ॥ ८-२॥

*adhiyajñaḥ kathaṃ ko'tra dehe'sminmadhusūdana  
prayāṇakāle ca kathaṃ jñeyo'si niyatātmabhiḥ 8-2*

O destroyer of Madhu, who is the ADHIYAJNA here in this body, and in what way? And how are You to be known by the self-controlled at the hour of death?

### 8.3

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
अक्षरं ब्रह्म परमं स्वभावोऽध्यात्ममुच्यते ।  
भूतभावोद्भवकरो विसर्गः कर्मसंज्ञितः ॥ ८-३॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
akṣaraṃ brahma paramaṃ svabhāvo'dhyātmamucyate  
bhūtabhāvodbhavakaro visargaḥ karmasaṃjñitaḥ 8-3*

The Blessed Lord said: BRAHMAN is the Imperishable, the Supreme. His essential nature is called Self-knowledge. The creative force that brings beings forth into manifestation is called work.

### 8.4

अधिभूतं क्षरो भावः पुरुषश्चाधिदैवतम् ।  
अधियज्ञोऽहमेवात्र देहे देहभृतां वर ॥ ८-४॥

*adhibhūtaṃ kṣaro bhāvaḥ puruṣaścādhidaivatam  
adhiyajño'hamevātra dehe dehabhṛtāṃ vara 8-4*

The ADHIBHUTA (the elements) is My perishable nature, and the Indweller (the essence) is the ADHIDAIVATA. O best of embodied beings, I alone am the ADHIYAJNA here in this body.

### 8.5

अन्तकाले च मामेव स्मरन्मुक्त्वा कलेवरम् ।  
यः प्रयाति स मद्भावं याति नास्त्यत्र संशयः ॥ ८-५॥

*antakāle ca māmeva smaranmuktvā kalevaram  
yaḥ prayāti sa madbhāvaṃ yāti nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ 8-5*

Whoever leaves the body and departs at the moment of death remembering Me alone reaches My being. There is no doubt about this.

### 8.6

यं यं वापि स्मरन्भावं त्यजत्यन्ते कलेवरम् ।  
तं तमेवैति कौन्तेय सदा तद्भावभावितः ॥ ८-६॥

*yaṃ yaṃ vāpi smaranbhāvaṃ tyajatyante kalevaram  
taṃ tamevaiti kaunteya sadā tadbhāvabhāvitaḥ 8-6*

O Kaunteya (son of Kunti), whatever being a person thinks of at the end when leaving the body, to that being alone he goes, because of his constant dwelling on it.

### 8.7

तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु मामनुस्मर युध्य च ।   
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर् र्मामेवैष्यस्यसंशयम् ॥ ८- ७॥

*tasmātsarveṣu kāleṣu māmanusmara yudhya ca  
mayyarpitamanobuddhir māmevaiṣyasya saṃśayaḥ 8-7*

So remember Me at all times and fight, with mind and intellect fixed on (or absorbed in) Me. You will surely come to Me alone.

### 8.8

अभ्यासयोगयुक्तेन चेतसा नान्यगामिना ।  
परमं पुरुषं दिव्यं याति पार्थानुचिन्तयन् ॥ ८-८॥

*abhyāsayogayuktena cetasā nānyagāminā  
paramaṃ puruṣaṃ divyaṃ yāti pārthānucintayan 8-8*

O Partha, with the mind made steady by the discipline of regular meditation, turning toward nothing else, and constantly meditating on the Supreme PURUSHA, the Resplendent One, a person goes (to Him).

### 8.9

कविं पुराणमनुशासितार-  
मणोरणीयंसमनुस्मरेद्यः ।  
सर्वस्य धातारमचिन्त्यरूप-  
मादित्यवर्णं तमसः परस्तात् ॥ ८-९॥

*kaviṃ purāṇamanuśāsitāra-  
maṇoraṇīyaṃsamanusmaredyaḥ  
sarvasya dhātāramacintyarūpa-  
mādityavarṇaṃ tamasaḥ parastāt 8-9*

Whoever meditates on the All-knowing, the Ancient, the Ruler (of the whole world), smaller than the atom, the Support of everything, whose Form cannot be conceived, shining like the Sun and beyond the darkness (of ignorance) . . .

### 8.10

प्रयाणकाले मनसाऽचलेन  
भक्त्या युक्तो योगबलेन चैव ।  
भ्रुवोर्मध्ये प्राणमावेश्य सम्यक्  
स तं परं पुरुषमुपैति दिव्यम् ॥ ८-१०॥

*prayāṇakāle manasā'calena  
bhaktyā yukto yogabalena caiva  
bhruvormadhye prāṇamāveśya samyak  
sa taṃ paraṃ puruṣamupaiti divyam 8-10*

At the time of death, with an unwavering mind full of devotion, and using the power of YOGA to settle the whole PRANA (breath) between the two eyebrows, he (the seeker) reaches the Supreme Resplendent PURUSHA.

### 8.11

यदक्षरं वेदविदो वदन्ति  
विशन्ति यद्यतयो वीतरागाः ।  
यदिच्छन्तो ब्रह्मचर्यं चरन्ति  
तत्ते पदं सङ्ग्रहेण प्रवक्ष्ये ॥ ८-११॥

*yadakṣaraṃ vedavido vadanti  
viśanti yadyatayo vītarāgāḥ  
yadicchanto brahmacaryaṃ caranti  
tatte padaṃ saṅgraheṇa pravakṣye 8-11*

That which the knowers of the VEDA call the Imperishable; That which the self-controlled and desire-free enter; That for whose sake BRAHMACHARYA is practised: that Goal I will describe to you briefly.

### 8.12

सर्वद्वाराणि संयम्य मनो हृदि निरुध्य च ।  
मूर्ध्न्याधायात्मनः प्राणमास्थितो योगधारणाम् ॥ ८-१२॥

*sarvadvārāṇi saṃyamya mano hṛdi nirudhya ca  
mūrdhnyādhāyātmanaḥ prāṇamāsthito yogadhāraṇām 8-12*

Having shut all the gates, having held the mind within the heart, having fixed the life-breath in the head, and absorbed in the practice of concentration,

### 8.13

ओमित्येकाक्षरं ब्रह्म व्याहरन्मामनुस्मरन् ।  
यः प्रयाति त्यजन्देहं स याति परमां गतिम् ॥ ८-१३॥

*omityekākṣaraṃ brahma vyāharanmāmanusmaran  
yaḥ prayāti tyajandehaṃ sa yāti paramāṃ gatim 8-13*

the person who departs, leaving the body while pronouncing the single syllable OM, the (symbol of) BRAHMAN, and remembering Me, reaches the Supreme Goal.

### 8.14

अनन्यचेताः सततं यो मां स्मरति नित्यशः ।  
तस्याहं सुलभः पार्थ नित्ययुक्तस्य योगिनः ॥ ८-१४॥

*ananyacetāḥ satataṃ yo māṃ smarati nityaśaḥ  
tasyāhaṃ sulabhaḥ pārtha nityayuktasya yoginaḥ 8-14*

O Partha, I am easy to reach for that always-steadfast YOGI who remembers Me constantly every day and thinks of nothing else.

### 8.15

मामुपेत्य पुनर्जन्म दुःखालयमशाश्वतम् ।  
नाप्नुवन्ति महात्मानः संसिद्धिं परमां गताः ॥ ८-१५॥

*māmupetya punarjanma duḥkhālayamaśāśvatam  
nāpnuvanti mahātmānaḥ saṃsiddhiṃ paramāṃ gatāḥ 8-15*

Having reached Me, these MAHATMAS (great souls) are not born again into this impermanent home of pain, for they have attained the Highest Perfection, MOKSHA.

### 8.16

आब्रह्मभुवनाल्लोकाः पुनरावर्तिनोऽर्जुन ।  
मामुपेत्य तु कौन्तेय पुनर्जन्म न विद्यते ॥ ८-१६॥

*ābrahmabhuvanāllokāḥ punarāvartino'rjuna  
māmupetya tu kaunteya punarjanma na vidyate 8-16*

O Arjuna, all the worlds, right up to the world of BRAHMAJI, are subject to rebirth. But the one who reaches Me, O Kaunteya, is never born again.

### 8.17

सहस्रयुगपर्यन्तमहर्यद् ब्रह्मणो विदुः ।  
रात्रिं युगसहस्रान्तां तेऽहोरात्रविदो जनाः ॥ ८-१७॥

*sahasrayugaparyantamaharyad brahmaṇo viduḥ  
rātriṃ yugasahasrāntāṃ te'horātravido janāḥ 8-17*

Those who understand that the day of BRAHMA lasts a thousand YUGAS (aeons), and that the night likewise lasts a thousand YUGAS (aeons), truly know what day and night are.

### 8.18

अव्यक्ताद् व्यक्तयः सर्वाः प्रभवन्त्यहरागमे ।  
रात्र्यागमे प्रलीयन्ते तत्रैवाव्यक्तसंज्ञके ॥ ८-१८॥

*avyaktād vyaktayaḥ sarvāḥ prabhavantyaharāgame  
rātryāgame pralīyante tatraivāvyaktasaṃjñake 8-18*

When the day arrives, everything manifest comes forth from the unmanifest. When night arrives, it all dissolves back into that very thing called the unmanifest.

### 8.19

भूतग्रामः स एवायं भूत्वा भूत्वा प्रलीयते ।  
रात्र्यागमेऽवशः पार्थ प्रभवत्यहरागमे ॥ ८-१९॥

*bhūtagrāmaḥ sa evāyaṃ bhūtvā bhūtvā pralīyate  
rātryāgame'vaśaḥ pārtha prabhavatyaharāgame 8-19*

O Partha, this same multitude of beings comes into existence over and over and is dissolved (into the unmanifest), helplessly, when night comes, and comes forth again when day comes.

### 8.20

परस्तस्मात्तु भावोऽन्योऽव्यक्तोऽव्यक्तात्सनातनः ।  
यः स सर्वेषु भूतेषु नश्यत्सु न विनश्यति ॥ ८-२०॥

*parastasmāttu bhāvo'nyo'vyakto'vyaktātsanātanaḥ  
yaḥ sa sarveṣu bhūteṣu naśyatsu na vinaśyati 8-20*

But beyond that unmanifest (AVYAKTA) there truly exists another Unmanifest, which is Eternal and is not destroyed when all beings are destroyed.

### 8.21

अव्यक्तोऽक्षर इत्युक्तस्तमाहुः परमां गतिम् ।  
यं प्राप्य न निवर्तन्ते तद्धाम परमं मम ॥ ८-२१॥

*avyakto'kṣara ityuktastamāhuḥ paramāṃ gatim  
yaṃ prāpya na nivartante taddhāma paramaṃ mama 8-21*

What is called the Unmanifest and the Imperishable is said to be the Highest Goal (path). Those who reach It never come back. That is My highest abode (state).

### 8.22

पुरुषः स परः पार्थ भक्त्या लभ्यस्त्वनन्यया ।  
यस्यान्तःस्थानि भूतानि येन सर्वमिदं ततम् ॥ ८-२२॥

*puruṣaḥ sa paraḥ pārtha bhaktyā labhyastvananyayā  
yasyāntaḥsthāni bhūtāni yena sarvamidaṃ tatam 8-22*

O Partha, that Highest PURUSHA, within whom all beings live and by whom all this is pervaded, can be reached by unwavering devotion to Him alone.

### 8.23

यत्र काले त्वनावृत्तिमावृत्तिं चैव योगिनः ।  
प्रयाता यान्ति तं कालं वक्ष्यामि भरतर्षभ ॥ ८-२३॥

*yatra kāle tvanāvṛttimāvṛttiṃ caiva yoginaḥ  
prayātā yānti taṃ kālaṃ vakṣyāmi bharatarṣabha 8-23*

Now, O Chief of the Bharatas, I will tell you the time (path) at which YOGINS depart never to return, and also the time (path) at which they depart only to return.

### 8.24

अग्निर्ज्योतिरहः शुक्लः षण्मासा उत्तरायणम् ।  
तत्र प्रयाता गच्छन्ति ब्रह्म ब्रह्मविदो जनाः ॥ ८-२४॥

*agnirjyotirahaḥ śuklaḥ ṣaṇmāsā uttarāyaṇam  
tatra prayātā gacchanti brahma brahmavido janāḥ 8-24*

Fire, light, daytime, the bright fortnight, the six months of the sun's northern course: those who know BRAHMAN and go by this path go to BRAHMAN.

### 8.25

धूमो रात्रिस्तथा कृष्णः षण्मासा दक्षिणायनम् ।  
तत्र चान्द्रमसं ज्योतिर्योगी प्राप्य निवर्तते ॥ ८-२५॥

*dhūmo rātristathā kṛṣṇaḥ ṣaṇmāsā dakṣiṇāyanam  
tatra cāndramasaṃ jyotiryogī prāpya nivartate 8-25*

Smoke, night, the dark fortnight, the six months of the sun's southern course: the YOGI who goes by these reaches the Moon, the lunar light, and comes back.

### 8.26

शुक्लकृष्णे गती ह्येते जगतः शाश्वते मते ।  
एकया यात्यनावृत्तिमन्ययावर्तते पुनः ॥ ८-२६॥

*śuklakṛṣṇe gatī hyete jagataḥ śāśvate mate  
ekayā yātyanāvṛttimanyayāvartate punaḥ 8-26*

The Path of Light and the Path of Darkness are both considered to be eternal paths open to the world. By the first, the Path of Light, a person goes and does not return. By the second, the Path of Darkness, he returns again.

### 8.27

नैते सृती पार्थ जानन्योगी मुह्यति कश्चन ।  
तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु योगयुक्तो भवार्जुन ॥ ८-२७॥

*naite sṛtī pārtha jānanyogī muhyati kaścana  
tasmātsarveṣu kāleṣu yogayukto bhavārjuna 8-27*

O Partha, no YOGIN who knows these paths is ever deluded. Therefore, O Arjuna, be steadfast in YOGA at all times.

### 8.28

वेदेषु यज्ञेषु तपःसु चैव  
दानेषु यत्पुण्यफलं प्रदिष्टम् ।  
अत्येति तत्सर्वमिदं विदित्वा  
योगी परं स्थानमुपैति चाद्यम् ॥ ८-२८॥   
  
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु  
ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे  
अक्षरब्रह्मयोगो नामाष्टमोऽध्यायः ॥ ८॥

*vedeṣu yajñeṣu tapaḥsu caiva  
dāneṣu yatpuṇyaphalaṃ pradiṣṭam  
atyeti tatsarvamidaṃ viditvā  
yogī paraṃ sthānamupaiti cādyam 8-28  
  
oṃ tatsaditi śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu  
brahmavidyāyāṃ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṃvāde  
akṣarabrahmayogo nāmāṣṭamo'dhyāyaḥ 8*

Whatever rewards of merit (the scriptures) declare to come from studying the VEDAS, performing sacrifices, practising austerities, and giving in charity, the YOGIN goes beyond them all. Having understood this (the two paths), he reaches the Supreme, Primeval (Essence).

Thus, in the Upanishads of the glorious Bhagavad Gita, the science of the Eternal, the scripture of Yoga, the dialogue between Sri Krishna and Arjuna, ends the eighth discourse, entitled "The Yoga of the Imperishable Absolute."
