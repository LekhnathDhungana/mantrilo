# Chapter 18 – Mokṣa-Sannyāsa Yoga (The Yoga of Liberation and Renunciation)

*Yoga of Liberation and Renunciation*

### 18.1

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
संन्यासस्य महाबाहो तत्त्वमिच्छामि वेदितुम् ।  
त्यागस्य च हृषीकेश पृथक्केशिनिषूदन ॥ १८-१॥

*arjuna uvāca  
saṃnyāsasya mahābāho tattvamicchāmi veditum  
tyāgasya ca hṛṣīkeśa pṛthakkeśiniṣūdana 18-1*

Arjuna said: O mighty-armed one, O Hrishikesa, O slayer of Keshi (Krishna), I want to understand, each on its own, the real nature or truth of Renunciation and also of Abandonment.

### 18.2

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
काम्यानां कर्मणां न्यासं संन्यासं कवयो विदुः ।  
सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं प्राहुस्त्यागं विचक्षणाः ॥ १८-२॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
kāmyānāṃ karmaṇāṃ nyāsaṃ saṃnyāsaṃ kavayo viduḥ  
sarvakarmaphalatyāgaṃ prāhustyāgaṃ vicakṣaṇāḥ 18-2*

The Blessed Lord said: The Sages understand SAMNYASA to mean giving up actions driven by desire. The wise call giving up the fruits of all actions TYAAGA.

### 18.3

त्याज्यं दोषवदित्येके कर्म प्राहुर्मनीषिणः ।  
यज्ञदानतपःकर्म न त्याज्यमिति चापरे ॥ १८-३॥

*tyājyaṃ doṣavadityeke karma prāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ  
yajñadānatapaḥkarma na tyājyamiti cāpare 18-3*

Some thinkers say that all action should be given up as something evil. Others say that acts of sacrifice, giving, and austerity should never be given up.

### 18.4

निश्चयं श‍ृणु मे तत्र त्यागे भरतसत्तम ।  
त्यागो हि पुरुषव्याघ्र त्रिविधः सम्प्रकीर्तितः ॥ १८-४॥

*niścayaṃ śṛṇu me tatra tyāge bharatasattama  
tyāgo hi puruṣavyāghra trividhaḥ samprakīrtitaḥ 18-4*

O best of the Bharatas, hear from Me the settled truth about this abandonment. Abandonment, O best of men, has indeed been described as being of three kinds.

### 18.5

यज्ञदानतपःकर्म न त्याज्यं कार्यमेव तत् ।  
यज्ञो दानं तपश्चैव पावनानि मनीषिणाम् ॥ १८-५॥

*yajñadānatapaḥkarma na tyājyaṃ kāryameva tat  
yajño dānaṃ tapaścaiva pāvanāni manīṣiṇām 18-5*

Acts of sacrifice, charity, and austerity are not to be given up; they should be carried out. Worship, charity, and austerity purify even the wise.

### 18.6

एतान्यपि तु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा फलानि च ।  
कर्तव्यानीति मे पार्थ निश्चितं मतमुत्तमम् ॥ १८-६॥

*etānyapi tu karmāṇi saṅgaṃ tyaktvā phalāni ca  
kartavyānīti me pārtha niścitaṃ matamuttamam 18-6*

Yet even these actions, O Partha, should be done after setting aside attachment and the desire for results. This is My firm and considered view.

### 18.7

नियतस्य तु संन्यासः कर्मणो नोपपद्यते ।  
मोहात्तस्य परित्यागस्तामसः परिकीर्तितः ॥ १८-७॥

*niyatasya tu saṃnyāsaḥ karmaṇo nopapadyate  
mohāttasya parityāgastāmasaḥ parikīrtitaḥ 18-7*

It is not right to give up actions that are obligatory. Giving them up out of delusion is said to be TAMASIC (dull).

### 18.8

दुःखमित्येव यत्कर्म कायक्लेशभयात्त्यजेत् ।  
स कृत्वा राजसं त्यागं नैव त्यागफलं लभेत् ॥ १८-८॥

*duḥkhamityeva yatkarma kāyakleśabhayāttyajet  
sa kṛtvā rājasaṃ tyāgaṃ naiva tyāgaphalaṃ labhet 18-8*

The person who gives up action because it is painful, out of fear of physical hardship, performs a RAJASIC (passionate) abandonment and does not gain the fruit of abandonment.

### 18.9

कार्यमित्येव यत्कर्म नियतं क्रियतेऽर्जुन ।  
सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा फलं चैव स त्यागः सात्त्विको मतः ॥ १८-९॥

*kāryamityeva yatkarma niyataṃ kriyate'rjuna  
saṅgaṃ tyaktvā phalaṃ caiva sa tyāgaḥ sāttviko mataḥ 18-9*

O Arjuna, when an obligatory action is done simply because it ought to be done, with attachment and the desire for results both let go, that abandonment is considered SATTVIC (pure).

### 18.10

न द्वेष्ट्यकुशलं कर्म कुशले नानुषज्जते ।  
त्यागी सत्त्वसमाविष्टो मेधावी छिन्नसंशयः ॥ १८-१०॥

*na dveṣṭyakuśalaṃ karma kuśale nānuṣajjate  
tyāgī sattvasamāviṣṭo medhāvī chinnasaṃśayaḥ 18-10*

The one who abandons in this way, steeped in purity, intelligent, with every doubt cut away, neither dislikes unpleasant work nor clings to pleasant work.

### 18.11

न हि देहभृता शक्यं त्यक्तुं कर्माण्यशेषतः ।  
यस्तु कर्मफलत्यागी स त्यागीत्यभिधीयते ॥ १८-११॥

*na hi dehabhṛtā śakyaṃ tyaktuṃ karmāṇyaśeṣataḥ  
yastu karmaphalatyāgī sa tyāgītyabhidhīyate 18-11*

It is simply not possible for an embodied being to give up action completely. But the one who gives up the fruits of action is truly called a relinquisher (TYAAGI).

### 18.12

अनिष्टमिष्टं मिश्रं च त्रिविधं कर्मणः फलम् ।  
भवत्यत्यागिनां प्रेत्य न तु संन्यासिनां क्वचित् ॥ १८-१२॥

*aniṣṭamiṣṭaṃ miśraṃ ca trividhaṃ karmaṇaḥ phalam  
bhavatyatyāgināṃ pretya na tu saṃnyāsināṃ kvacit 18-12*

The threefold fruit of action, bad, good, and mixed, comes after death only to those who lack the spirit of abandonment. It never comes to those who have relinquished completely.

### 18.13

पञ्चैतानि महाबाहो कारणानि निबोध मे ।  
साङ्ख्ये कृतान्ते प्रोक्तानि सिद्धये सर्वकर्मणाम् ॥ १८-१३॥

*pañcaitāni mahābāho kāraṇāni nibodha me  
sāṅkhye kṛtānte proktāni siddhaye sarvakarmaṇām 18-13*

O mighty-armed one, learn from Me these five causes behind the accomplishment of every action, as set out in the SANKHYA (UPANISHAD) system, which is the culmination of all action.

### 18.14

अधिष्ठानं तथा कर्ता करणं च पृथग्विधम् ।  
विविधाश्च पृथक्चेष्टा दैवं चैवात्र पञ्चमम् ॥ १८-१४॥

*adhiṣṭhānaṃ tathā kartā karaṇaṃ ca pṛthagvidham  
vividhāśca pṛthakceṣṭā daivaṃ caivātra pañcamam 18-14*

The seat (the body), the doer (the ego), the various organs of perception, the different functions of the various organs of action, and the presiding deity as the fifth.

### 18.15

शरीरवाङ्मनोभिर्यत्कर्म प्रारभते नरः ।  
न्याय्यं वा विपरीतं वा पञ्चैते तस्य हेतवः ॥ १८-१५॥

*śarīravāṅmanobhiryat karma prārabhate naraḥ  
nyāyyaṃ vā viparītaṃ vā pañcaite tasya hetavaḥ 18-15*

Whatever action a person carries out with body, speech, or mind, whether right or wrong, these five are its causes.

### 18.16

तत्रैवं सति कर्तारमात्मानं केवलं तु यः ।  
पश्यत्यकृतबुद्धित्वान्न स पश्यति दुर्मतिः ॥ १८-१६॥

*tatraivaṃ sati kartāramātmānaṃ kevalaṃ tu yaḥ  
paśyatyakṛtabuddhitvānna sa paśyati durmatiḥ 18-16*

This being so, the person who, through an untrained understanding, regards his Self, which stands alone (never conditioned by the engine), as the doer, has a distorted intelligence and does not see.

### 18.17

यस्य नाहङ्कृतो भावो बुद्धिर्यस्य न लिप्यते ।  
हत्वाऽपि स इमाँल्लोकान्न हन्ति न निबध्यते ॥ १८-१७॥

*yasya nāhaṅkṛto bhāvo buddhiryasya na lipyate  
hatvā'pi sa imām̐llokānna hanti na nibadhyate 18-17*

The one who is free from the sense of ego, whose intelligence is not stained (by good or evil), does not really slay even if he slays these people, nor is he bound (by the action).

### 18.18

ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं परिज्ञाता त्रिविधा कर्मचोदना ।  
करणं कर्म कर्तेति त्रिविधः कर्मसङ्ग्रहः ॥ १८-१८॥

*jñānaṃ jñeyaṃ parijñātā trividhā karmacodanā  
karaṇaṃ karma karteti trividhaḥ karmasaṅgrahaḥ 18-18*

Knowledge, the thing known, and the knower are the threefold impulse behind action. The organs, the action, and the agent are the threefold basis of action.

### 18.19

ज्ञानं कर्म च कर्ता च त्रिधैव गुणभेदतः ।  
प्रोच्यते गुणसङ्ख्याने यथावच्छृणु तान्यपि ॥ १८-१९॥

*jñānaṃ karma ca kartā ca tridhaiva guṇabhedataḥ  
procyate guṇasaṅkhyāne yathāvacchṛṇu tānyapi 18-19*

The Science of Temperaments (gunas) declares that knowledge, action, and the actor are each of three kinds only, according to the differences in temperament. Listen to these as well, in due order.

### 18.20

सर्वभूतेषु येनैकं भावमव्ययमीक्षते ।  
अविभक्तं विभक्तेषु तज्ज्ञानं विद्धि सात्त्विकम् ॥ १८-२०॥

*sarvabhūteṣu yenaikaṃ bhāvamavyayamīkṣate  
avibhaktaṃ vibhakteṣu tajjñānaṃ viddhi sāttvikam 18-20*

The knowledge by which one sees the single indestructible reality in all beings, undivided within the divided, know that knowledge to be SATTVIC (Pure).

### 18.21

पृथक्त्वेन तु यज्ज्ञानं नानाभावान्पृथग्विधान् ।  
वेत्ति सर्वेषु भूतेषु तज्ज्ञानं विद्धि राजसम् ॥ १८-२१॥

*pṛthaktvena tu yajjñānaṃ nānābhāvānpṛthagvidhān  
vetti sarveṣu bhūteṣu tajjñānaṃ viddhi rājasam 18-21*

But the knowledge that sees in all beings separate entities of different kinds, distinct from one another, know that knowledge to be RAJASIC (Passionate).

### 18.22

यत्तु कृत्स्नवदेकस्मिन्कार्ये सक्तमहैतुकम् ।  
अतत्त्वार्थवदल्पं च तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ १८-२२॥

*yattu kṛtsnavadekasminkārye saktamahaitukam  
atattvārthavadalpaṃ ca tattāmasamudāhṛtam 18-22*

And the knowledge that fastens onto one single effect as though it were everything, without reason, without any basis in truth, and narrow, is said to be TAMASIC (Dull).

### 18.23

नियतं सङ्गरहितमरागद्वेषतः कृतम् ।  
अफलप्रेप्सुना कर्म यत्तत्सात्त्विकमुच्यते ॥ १८-२३॥

*niyataṃ saṅgarahitamarāgadveṣataḥ kṛtam  
aphalaprepsunā karma yattatsāttvikamucyate 18-23*

An action that is prescribed, free from attachment, done without liking or disliking by someone who does not want the result, is declared SATTVIC (pure).

### 18.24

यत्तु कामेप्सुना कर्म साहङ्कारेण वा पुनः ।  
क्रियते बहुलायासं तद्राजसमुदाहृतम् ॥ १८-२४॥

*yattu kāmepsunā karma sāhaṅkāreṇa vā punaḥ  
kriyate bahulāyāsaṃ tadrājasamudāhṛtam 18-24*

But an action done by someone craving the satisfaction of desires or personal gain, done with egoism or with great strain, is declared RAJASIC (Passionate).

### 18.25

अनुबन्धं क्षयं हिंसामनपेक्ष्य च पौरुषम् ।  
मोहादारभ्यते कर्म यत्तत्तामसमुच्यते ॥ १८-२५॥

*anubandhaṃ kṣayaṃ hiṃsāmanapekṣya ca pauruṣam  
mohādārabhyate karma yattattāmasamucyate 18-25*

An action undertaken out of delusion, with no thought for the consequences, the loss, the harm, or one's own ability, is declared TAMASIC (dull).

### 18.26

मुक्तसङ्गोऽनहंवादी धृत्युत्साहसमन्वितः ।  
सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योर्निर्विकारः कर्ता सात्त्विक उच्यते ॥ १८-२६॥

*muktasaṅgo'nahaṃvādī dhṛtyutsāhasamanvitaḥ  
siddhyasiddhyornirvikāraḥ kartā sāttvika ucyate 18-26*

An agent who is free from attachment, without ego, full of steadiness and enthusiasm, and unmoved by success or failure is called SATTVIC (pure).

### 18.27

रागी कर्मफलप्रेप्सुर्लुब्धो हिंसात्मकोऽशुचिः ।  
हर्षशोकान्वितः कर्ता राजसः परिकीर्तितः ॥ १८-२७॥

*rāgī karmaphalaprepsurlubdho hiṃsātmako'śuciḥ  
harṣaśokānvitaḥ kartā rājasaḥ parikīrtitaḥ 18-27*

An agent who is passionate, eager for the fruits of actions, greedy, harmful, impure, and swinging between joy and grief is said to be RAJASIC (passionate).

### 18.28

अयुक्तः प्राकृतः स्तब्धः शठो नैष्कृतिकोऽलसः ।  
विषादी दीर्घसूत्री च कर्ता तामस उच्यते ॥ १८-२८॥

*ayuktaḥ prākṛtaḥ stabdhaḥ śaṭho naiṣkṛtiko'lasaḥ  
viṣādī dīrghasūtrī ca kartā tāmasa ucyate 18-28*

An agent who is unsteady, coarse, stubborn, deceitful, spiteful, lazy, gloomy, and given to putting things off is said to be TAMASIC (Dull).

### 18.29

बुद्धेर्भेदं धृतेश्चैव गुणतस्त्रिविधं श‍ृणु ।  
प्रोच्यमानमशेषेण पृथक्त्वेन धनञ्जय ॥ १८-२९॥

*buddherbhedaṃ dhṛteścaiva guṇatastrividhaṃ śṛṇu  
procyamānamaśeṣeṇa pṛthaktvena dhanañjaya 18-29*

O Dhananjaya, hear the threefold division of understanding and of fortitude according to the qualities, as I set them out fully and one by one.

### 18.30

प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं च कार्याकार्ये भयाभये ।  
बन्धं मोक्षं च या वेत्ति बुद्धिः सा पार्थ सात्त्विकी ॥ १८-३०॥

*pravṛttiṃ ca nivṛttiṃ ca kāryākārye bhayābhaye  
bandhaṃ mokṣaṃ ca yā vetti buddhiḥ sā pārtha sāttvikī 18-30*

O Partha, the understanding that knows the paths of work and renunciation, what should and should not be done, fear and fearlessness, bondage and liberation, is SATTVIC (pure).

### 18.31

यया धर्ममधर्मं च कार्यं चाकार्यमेव च ।  
अयथावत्प्रजानाति बुद्धिः सा पार्थ राजसी ॥ १८-३१॥

*yayā dharmamadharmaṃ ca kāryaṃ cākāryameva ca  
ayathāvatprajānāti buddhiḥ sā pārtha rājasī 18-31*

O Partha, the intellect (understanding) through which one misjudges DHARMA and ADHARMA, and also what should and should not be done, is RAJASIC (passionate).

### 18.32

अधर्मं धर्ममिति या मन्यते तमसावृता ।  
सर्वार्थान्विपरीतांश्च बुद्धिः सा पार्थ तामसी ॥ १८-३२॥

*adharmaṃ dharmamiti yā manyate tamasāvṛtā  
sarvārthānviparītāṃśca buddhiḥ sā pārtha tāmasī 18-32*

O Partha, the intellect (understanding) that, wrapped in darkness, takes ADHARMA for DHARMA and sees everything upside down, is TAMASIC (dull).

### 18.33

धृत्या यया धारयते मनःप्राणेन्द्रियक्रियाः ।  
योगेनाव्यभिचारिण्या धृतिः सा पार्थ सात्त्विकी ॥ १८-३३॥

*dhṛtyā yayā dhārayate manaḥprāṇendriyakriyāḥ  
yogenāvyabhicāriṇyā dhṛtiḥ sā pārtha sāttvikī 18-33*

O Partha, the unwavering fortitude by which, through YOGA, the activities of the mind, the PRANA, and the senses are held in check is SATTVIC (pure).

### 18.34

यया तु धर्मकामार्थान्धृत्या धारयतेऽर्जुन ।  
प्रसङ्गेन फलाकाङ्क्षी धृतिः सा पार्थ राजसी ॥ १८-३४॥

*yayā tu dharmakāmārthāndhṛtyā dhārayate'rjuna  
prasaṅgena phalākāṅkṣī dhṛtiḥ sā pārtha rājasī 18-34*

But, O Arjuna, the fortitude by which one clings to duty, pleasure, and wealth out of attachment and craving for the fruits of actions, that fortitude, O Partha, is RAJASIC (passionate).

### 18.35

यया स्वप्नं भयं शोकं विषादं मदमेव च ।  
न विमुञ्चति दुर्मेधा धृतिः सा पार्थ तामसी ॥ १८-३५॥

*yayā svapnaṃ bhayaṃ śokaṃ viṣādaṃ madameva ca  
na vimuñcati durmedhā dhṛtiḥ sā pārtha tāmasī 18-35*

O Partha, the persistence by which a foolish person refuses to let go of sleep, fear, grief, depression, and also arrogance (conceit), that fortitude is TAMASIC (dull).

### 18.36

सुखं त्विदानीं त्रिविधं श‍ृणु मे भरतर्षभ ।  
अभ्यासाद्रमते यत्र दुःखान्तं च निगच्छति ॥ १८-३६॥

*sukhaṃ tvidānīṃ trividhaṃ śṛṇu me bharatarṣabha  
abhyāsādramate yatra duḥkhāntaṃ ca nigacchati 18-36*

And now, O best of the Bharatas, hear from Me about the three kinds of pleasure, in which one takes delight through practice and so surely comes to the end of pain.

### 18.37

यत्तदग्रे विषमिव परिणामेऽमृतोपमम् ।  
तत्सुखं सात्त्विकं प्रोक्तमात्मबुद्धिप्रसादजम् ॥ १८-३७॥

*yattadagre viṣamiva pariṇāme'mṛtopamam  
tatsukhaṃ sāttvikaṃ proktamātmabuddhiprasādajam 18-37*

The pleasure that seems like poison at first but is like nectar in the end is declared SATTVIC (pure). It is born of the purity of one's own mind that comes from Self-realisation.

### 18.38

विषयेन्द्रियसंयोगाद्यत्तदग्रेऽमृतोपमम् ।  
परिणामे विषमिव तत्सुखं राजसं स्मृतम् ॥ १८-३८॥

*viṣayendriyasaṃyogādyat tadagre'mṛtopamam  
pariṇāme viṣamiva tatsukhaṃ rājasaṃ smṛtam 18-38*

The pleasure that comes from contact between the sense organs and their objects, which is like nectar at first but like poison in the end, is declared RAJASIC (passionate).

### 18.39

यदग्रे चानुबन्धे च सुखं मोहनमात्मनः ।  
निद्रालस्यप्रमादोत्थं तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ १८-३९॥

*yadagre cānubandhe ca sukhaṃ mohanamātmanaḥ  
nidrālasyapramādotthaṃ tattāmasamudāhṛtam 18-39*

The pleasure that deludes the Self both at the start and afterwards, arising from sleep, laziness, and carelessness, is declared TAMASIC (Dull).

### 18.40

न तदस्ति पृथिव्यां वा दिवि देवेषु वा पुनः ।  
सत्त्वं प्रकृतिजैर्मुक्तं यदेभिः स्यात्त्रिभिर्गुणैः ॥ १८-४०॥

*na tadasti pṛthivyāṃ vā divi deveṣu vā punaḥ  
sattvaṃ prakṛtijairmuktaṃ yadebhiḥ syāttribhirguṇaiḥ 18-40*

There is no being on earth, nor even in heaven among the DEVAS (heavenly beings), who is completely free from the three qualities born of PRAKRITI (matter).

### 18.41

ब्राह्मणक्षत्रियविशां शूद्राणां च परन्तप ।  
कर्माणि प्रविभक्तानि स्वभावप्रभवैर्गुणैः ॥ १८-४१॥

*brāhmaṇakṣatriyaviśāṃ śūdrāṇāṃ ca parantapa  
karmāṇi pravibhaktāni svabhāvaprabhavairguṇaiḥ 18-41*

O Parantapa, the duties of scholars (BRAHMANAS), of leaders (KSHATRIYAS), of traders (VAISHYAS), and also of workers (SHUDRAS) are allotted according to the qualities that arise from their own nature.

### 18.42

शमो दमस्तपः शौचं क्षान्तिरार्जवमेव च ।  
ज्ञानं विज्ञानमास्तिक्यं ब्रह्मकर्म स्वभावजम् ॥ १८-४२॥

*śamo damastapaḥ śaucaṃ kṣāntirārjavameva ca  
jñānaṃ vijñānamāstikyaṃ brahmakarma svabhāvajam 18-42*

Calmness, self-control, austerity, purity, forgiveness, and also honesty, knowledge, realisation, and belief in God are the duties of the BRAHMANAS, born of (their own) nature.

### 18.43

शौर्यं तेजो धृतिर्दाक्ष्यं युद्धे चाप्यपलायनम् ।  
दानमीश्वरभावश्च क्षात्रं कर्म स्वभावजम् ॥ १८-४३॥

*śauryaṃ tejo dhṛtirdākṣyaṃ yuddhe cāpyapalāyanam  
dānamīśvarabhāvaśca kṣātraṃ karma svabhāvajam 18-43*

Courage, brilliance, steadiness, skill, not running from battle, generosity, and leadership are the duties of the KSHATRIYAS, born of (their own) nature.

### 18.44

कृषिगौरक्ष्यवाणिज्यं वैश्यकर्म स्वभावजम् ।  
परिचर्यात्मकं कर्म शूद्रस्यापि स्वभावजम् ॥ १८-४४॥

*kṛṣigaurakṣyavāṇijyaṃ vaiśyakarma svabhāvajam  
paricaryātmakaṃ karma śūdrasyāpi svabhāvajam 18-44*

Farming, raising cattle, and trade are the duties of the VAISHYAS, born of (their own) nature. Service is the duty of the SHUDRAS, born of (their own) nature.

### 18.45

स्वे स्वे कर्मण्यभिरतः संसिद्धिं लभते नरः ।  
स्वकर्मनिरतः सिद्धिं यथा विन्दति तच्छृणु ॥ १८-४५॥

*sve sve karmaṇyabhirataḥ saṃsiddhiṃ labhate naraḥ  
svakarmanirataḥ siddhiṃ yathā vindati tacchṛṇu 18-45*

By devoting himself to his own duty, a person reaches Perfection. Listen to how he reaches Perfection while engaged in his own duty.

### 18.46

यतः प्रवृत्तिर्भूतानां येन सर्वमिदं ततम् ।  
स्वकर्मणा तमभ्यर्च्य सिद्धिं विन्दति मानवः ॥ १८-४६॥

*yataḥ pravṛttirbhūtānāṃ yena sarvamidaṃ tatam  
svakarmaṇā tamabhyarcya siddhiṃ vindati mānavaḥ 18-46*

By worshipping, through his own duty, the One from whom all beings have evolved and by whom all this is pervaded, a person reaches Perfection.

### 18.47

श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात् ।  
स्वभावनियतं कर्म कुर्वन्नाप्नोति किल्बिषम् ॥ १८-४७॥

*śreyānsvadharmo viguṇaḥ paradharmātsvanuṣṭhitāt  
svabhāvaniyataṃ karma kurvannāpnoti kilbiṣam 18-47*

One's own duty, even when it lacks merit, is better than another's duty done well. The one who does the duty set by his own nature incurs no sin.

### 18.48

सहजं कर्म कौन्तेय सदोषमपि न त्यजेत् ।  
सर्वारम्भा हि दोषेण धूमेनाग्निरिवावृताः ॥ १८-४८॥

*sahajaṃ karma kaunteya sadoṣamapi na tyajet  
sarvārambhā hi doṣeṇa dhūmenāgnirivāvṛtāḥ 18-48*

O Kaunteya, one should not give up the duty one is born to, even if it has faults. For is not every undertaking wrapped in some evil, just as fire is wrapped in smoke?

### 18.49

असक्तबुद्धिः सर्वत्र जितात्मा विगतस्पृहः ।  
नैष्कर्म्यसिद्धिं परमां संन्यासेनाधिगच्छति ॥ १८-४९॥

*asaktabuddhiḥ sarvatra jitātmā vigataspṛhaḥ  
naiṣkarmyasiddhiṃ paramāṃ saṃnyāsenādhigacchati 18-49*

The one whose intellect is unattached in every situation, who has mastered himself, and from whom desire has gone, reaches through renunciation the Supreme State of Freedom from action.

### 18.50

सिद्धिं प्राप्तो यथा ब्रह्म तथाप्नोति निबोध मे ।  
समासेनैव कौन्तेय निष्ठा ज्ञानस्य या परा ॥ १८-५०॥

*siddhiṃ prāpto yathā brahma tathāpnoti nibodha me  
samāsenaiva kaunteya niṣṭhā jñānasya yā parā 18-50*

O Kaunteya, learn from Me in brief how the person who has reached perfection attains BRAHMAN (the Eternal), that Supreme State of Knowledge.

### 18.51

बुद्ध्या विशुद्धया युक्तो धृत्यात्मानं नियम्य च ।  
शब्दादीन्विषयांस्त्यक्त्वा रागद्वेषौ व्युदस्य च ॥ १८-५१॥

*buddhyā viśuddhayā yukto dhṛtyātmānaṃ niyamya ca  
śabdādīnviṣayāṃstyaktvā rāgadveṣau vyudasya ca 18-51*

Possessing a pure intellect, controlling the self with steadiness, letting go of sound and the other sense objects, and casting off attraction and hatred;

### 18.52

विविक्तसेवी लघ्वाशी यतवाक्कायमानसः ।  
ध्यानयोगपरो नित्यं वैराग्यं समुपाश्रितः ॥ १८-५२॥

*viviktasevī laghvāśī yatavākkāyamānasaḥ  
dhyānayogaparo nityaṃ vairāgyaṃ samupāśritaḥ 18-52*

living in solitude, eating little, with speech, body, and mind under control, always engaged in meditation and concentration, and taking shelter in dispassion;

### 18.53

अहङ्कारं बलं दर्पं कामं क्रोधं परिग्रहम् ।  
विमुच्य निर्ममः शान्तो ब्रह्मभूयाय कल्पते ॥ १८-५३॥

*ahaṅkāraṃ balaṃ darpaṃ kāmaṃ krodhaṃ parigraham  
vimucya nirmamaḥ śānto brahmabhūyāya kalpate 18-53*

having given up egoism, power, arrogance, desire, anger, and the drive to possess, free from any sense of "mine," and therefore at peace, he is fit to become BRAHMAN.

### 18.54

ब्रह्मभूतः प्रसन्नात्मा न शोचति न काङ्क्षति ।  
समः सर्वेषु भूतेषु मद्भक्तिं लभते पराम् ॥ १८-५४॥

*brahmabhūtaḥ prasannātmā na śocati na kāṅkṣati  
samaḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣu madbhaktiṃ labhate parām 18-54*

Having become BRAHMAN, calm in the Self, he neither grieves nor craves. Treating all beings alike, he gains supreme devotion to Me.

### 18.55

भक्त्या मामभिजानाति यावान्यश्चास्मि तत्त्वतः ।  
ततो मां तत्त्वतो ज्ञात्वा विशते तदनन्तरम् ॥ १८-५५॥

*bhaktyā māmabhijānāti yāvānyaścāsmi tattvataḥ  
tato māṃ tattvato jñātvā viśate tadanantaram 18-55*

Through devotion he comes to know Me in Essence, what I am and who I am. Then, having known Me in My Essence, he enters the Supreme straight away.

### 18.56

सर्वकर्माण्यपि सदा कुर्वाणो मद्व्यपाश्रयः ।  
मत्प्रसादादवाप्नोति शाश्वतं पदमव्ययम् ॥ १८-५६॥

*sarvakarmāṇyapi sadā kurvāṇo madvyapāśrayaḥ  
matprasādādavāpnoti śāśvataṃ padamavyayam 18-56*

Performing all actions while always taking shelter in Me, he gains by My grace the Eternal, Indestructible State or Abode.

### 18.57

चेतसा सर्वकर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य मत्परः ।  
बुद्धियोगमुपाश्रित्य मच्चित्तः सततं भव ॥ १८-५७॥

*cetasā sarvakarmāṇi mayi saṃnyasya matparaḥ  
buddhiyogamupāśritya maccittaḥ satataṃ bhava 18-57*

Giving up all actions to Me in your mind, holding Me as the Highest Goal, and relying on the YOGA of discrimination, keep your mind fixed on Me always.

### 18.58

मच्चित्तः सर्वदुर्गाणि मत्प्रसादात्तरिष्यसि ।  
अथ चेत्त्वमहङ्कारान्न श्रोष्यसि विनङ्क्ष्यसि ॥ १८-५८॥

*maccittaḥ sarvadurgāṇi matprasādāttariṣyasi  
atha cettvamahaṅkārānna śroṣyasi vinaṅkṣyasi 18-58*

With your mind fixed on Me, you will overcome every obstacle by My grace. But if, out of egoism, you refuse to listen to Me, you will be destroyed.

### 18.59

यदहङ्कारमाश्रित्य न योत्स्य इति मन्यसे ।  
मिथ्यैष व्यवसायस्ते प्रकृतिस्त्वां नियोक्ष्यति ॥ १८-५९॥

*yadahaṅkāramāśritya na yotsya iti manyase  
mithyaiṣa vyavasāyaste prakṛtistvāṃ niyokṣyati 18-59*

If, filled with egoism, you think, "I will not fight," that resolve of yours is pointless, for your nature will force you.

### 18.60

स्वभावजेन कौन्तेय निबद्धः स्वेन कर्मणा ।  
कर्तुं नेच्छसि यन्मोहात्करिष्यस्यवशोऽपि तत् ॥ १८-६०॥

*svabhāvajena kaunteya nibaddhaḥ svena karmaṇā  
kartuṃ necchasi yanmohātkariṣyasyavaśo'pi tat 18-60*

O son of Kunti, bound by your own KARMA (action), which springs from your own nature, you will helplessly do even the very thing that, through delusion, you do not wish to do.

### 18.61

ईश्वरः सर्वभूतानां हृद्देशेऽर्जुन तिष्ठति ।  
भ्रामयन्सर्वभूतानि यन्त्रारूढानि मायया ॥ १८-६१॥

*īśvaraḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ hṛddeśe'rjuna tiṣṭhati  
bhrāmayansarvabhūtāni yantrārūḍhāni māyayā 18-61*

O Arjuna, the Lord lives in the hearts of all beings, and through His power of illusion He sets every being turning, as though it were mounted on a machine.

### 18.62

तमेव शरणं गच्छ सर्वभावेन भारत ।  
तत्प्रसादात्परां शान्तिं स्थानं प्राप्स्यसि शाश्वतम् ॥ १८-६२॥

*tameva śaraṇaṃ gaccha sarvabhāvena bhārata  
tatprasādātparāṃ śāntiṃ sthānaṃ prāpsyasi śāśvatam 18-62*

Take shelter in Him with your whole being, O Bharata. Through His grace you will gain Supreme Peace and the Eternal Abode.

### 18.63

इति ते ज्ञानमाख्यातं गुह्याद्गुह्यतरं मया ।  
विमृश्यैतदशेषेण यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ १८-६३॥

*iti te jñānamākhyātaṃ guhyādguhyataraṃ mayā  
vimṛśyaitadaśeṣeṇa yathecchasi tathā kuru 18-63*

So I have revealed to you this Wisdom, a secret deeper than every other secret. Think it over completely, and then act however you choose.

### 18.64

सर्वगुह्यतमं भूयः श‍ृणु मे परमं वचः ।  
इष्टोऽसि मे दृढमिति ततो वक्ष्यामि ते हितम् ॥ १८-६४॥

*sarvaguhyatamaṃ bhūyaḥ śṛṇu me paramaṃ vacaḥ  
iṣṭo'si me dṛḍhamiti tato vakṣyāmi te hitam 18-64*

Listen once more to My highest word, the most secret of all. Because you are very dear to Me, I will tell you what is for your good.

### 18.65

मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु ।  
मामेवैष्यसि सत्यं ते प्रतिजाने प्रियोऽसि मे ॥ १८-६५॥

*manmanā bhava madbhakto madyājī māṃ namaskuru  
māmevaiṣyasi satyaṃ te pratijāne priyo'si me 18-65*

Set your mind on Me, be devoted to Me, offer sacrifice to Me, and bow down to Me. Then you will surely come to Me alone. I promise you this truly, because you are dear to Me.

### 18.66

सर्वधर्मान्परित्यज्य मामेकं शरणं व्रज ।  
अहं त्वा सर्वपापेभ्यो मोक्षयिष्यामि मा शुचः ॥ १८-६६॥

*sarvadharmānparityajya māmekaṃ śaraṇaṃ vraja  
ahaṃ tvā sarvapāpebhyo mokṣayiṣyāmi mā śucaḥ 18-66*

Letting go of all dharmas (of body, mind, and intellect), take refuge in Me alone. I will free you from every sin. Do not grieve.

### 18.67

इदं ते नातपस्काय नाभक्ताय कदाचन ।  
न चाशुश्रूषवे वाच्यं न च मां योऽभ्यसूयति ॥ १८-६७॥

*idaṃ te nātapaskāya nābhaktāya kadācana  
na cāśuśrūṣave vācyaṃ na ca māṃ yo'bhyasūyati 18-67*

You must never speak of this to anyone who lacks austerity or devotion, or who does no service, or who has no wish to hear it, or who speaks ill of Me.

### 18.68

य इदं परमं गुह्यं मद्भक्तेष्वभिधास्यति ।  
भक्तिं मयि परां कृत्वा मामेवैष्यत्यसंशयः ॥ १८-६८॥

*ya idaṃ paramaṃ guhyaṃ madbhakteṣvabhidhāsyati  
bhaktiṃ mayi parāṃ kṛtvā māmevaiṣyatyasaṃśayaḥ 18-68*

Whoever, out of supreme devotion to Me, teaches this highest secret to My devotees will without doubt come to Me.

### 18.69

न च तस्मान्मनुष्येषु कश्चिन्मे प्रियकृत्तमः ।  
भविता न च मे तस्मादन्यः प्रियतरो भुवि ॥ १८-६९॥

*na ca tasmānmanuṣyeṣu kaścinme priyakṛttamaḥ  
bhavitā na ca me tasmādanyaḥ priyataro bhuvi 18-69*

No one among human beings does Me a dearer service than such a person, and no one on earth will ever be dearer to Me.

### 18.70

अध्येष्यते च य इमं धर्म्यं संवादमावयोः ।  
ज्ञानयज्ञेन तेनाहमिष्टः स्यामिति मे मतिः ॥ १८-७०॥

*adhyeṣyate ca ya imaṃ dharmyaṃ saṃvādamāvayoḥ  
jñānayajñena tenāhamiṣṭaḥ syāmiti me matiḥ 18-70*

And whoever studies this sacred dialogue of ours will, by doing so, have worshipped Me through the sacrifice of wisdom. That is My conviction.

### 18.71

श्रद्धावाननसूयश्च श‍ृणुयादपि यो नरः ।  
सोऽपि मुक्तः शुभाँल्लोकान्प्राप्नुयात्पुण्यकर्मणाम् ॥ १८-७१॥

*śraddhāvānanasūyaśca śṛṇuyādapi yo naraḥ  
so'pi muktaḥ śubhām̐llokān prāpnuyātpuṇyakarmaṇām 18-71*

And the person who simply listens to this with faith and without ill will is also set free, and will reach the happy worlds of those who perform righteous deeds.

### 18.72

कच्चिदेतच्छ्रुतं पार्थ त्वयैकाग्रेण चेतसा ।  
कच्चिदज्ञानसम्मोहः प्रनष्टस्ते धनञ्जय ॥ १८-७२॥

*kaccidetacchrutaṃ pārtha tvayaikāgreṇa cetasā  
kaccidajñānasammohaḥ pranaṣṭaste dhanañjaya 18-72*

O son of Pritha, have you listened to this with a single-pointed mind? O Dhananjaya, has the confusion born of your ignorance been cleared away?

### 18.73

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
नष्टो मोहः स्मृतिर्लब्धा त्वत्प्रसादान्मयाच्युत ।  
स्थितोऽस्मि गतसन्देहः करिष्ये वचनं तव ॥ १८-७३॥

*arjuna uvāca  
naṣṭo mohaḥ smṛtirlabdhā tvatprasādānmayācyuta  
sthito'smi gatasandehaḥ kariṣye vacanaṃ tava 18-73*

Arjuna said: My delusion is gone. Through your grace, O Achyuta, I have regained my memory (knowledge). I stand firm; my doubts have vanished. I will act according to your word.

### 18.74

सञ्जय उवाच ।  
इत्यहं वासुदेवस्य पार्थस्य च महात्मनः ।  
संवादमिममश्रौषमद्भुतं रोमहर्षणम् ॥ १८-७४॥

*sañjaya uvāca  
ityahaṃ vāsudevasya pārthasya ca mahātmanaḥ  
saṃvādamimamaśrauṣamadbhutaṃ romaharṣaṇam 18-74*

Sanjaya said: In this way I heard this marvelous dialogue between Vaasudeva and the noble-souled Paartha, a dialogue that makes the hair stand on end.

### 18.75

व्यासप्रसादाच्छ्रुतवानेतद्गुह्यमहं परम् ।  
योगं योगेश्वरात्कृष्णात्साक्षात्कथयतः स्वयम् ॥ १८-७५॥

*vyāsaprasādācchrutavān etadguhyamahaṃ param  
yogaṃ yogeśvarātkṛṣṇāt sākṣātkathayataḥ svayam 18-75*

By the grace of Vyasa I heard this supreme and most secret Yoga directly from Krishna, the Lord of Yoga, as He Himself declared it.

### 18.76

राजन्संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य संवादमिममद्भुतम् ।  
केशवार्जुनयोः पुण्यं हृष्यामि च मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ १८-७६॥

*rājansaṃsmṛtya saṃsmṛtya saṃvādamimamadbhutam  
keśavārjunayoḥ puṇyaṃ hṛṣyāmi ca muhurmuhuḥ 18-76*

O King, as I recall this wonderful and holy dialogue between Keshava and Arjuna, I rejoice again and again.

### 18.77

तच्च संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य रूपमत्यद्भुतं हरेः ।  
विस्मयो मे महान् राजन्हृष्यामि च पुनः पुनः ॥ १८-७७॥

*tacca saṃsmṛtya saṃsmṛtya rūpamatyadbhutaṃ hareḥ  
vismayo me mahān rājanhṛṣyāmi ca punaḥ punaḥ 18-77*

And as I recall, over and over, that most wonderful Form of Hari, my amazement is great, O king, and I rejoice again and again.

### 18.78

यत्र योगेश्वरः कृष्णो यत्र पार्थो धनुर्धरः ।  
तत्र श्रीर्विजयो भूतिर्ध्रुवा नीतिर्मतिर्मम ॥ १८-७८॥   
  
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु  
ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे  
मोक्षसंन्यासयोगो नाम अष्टादशोऽध्यायः ॥ १८॥

*yatra yogeśvaraḥ kṛṣṇo yatra pārtho dhanurdharaḥ  
tatra śrīrvijayo bhūtirdhruvā nītirmatirmama 18-78  
  
oṃ tatsaditi śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu  
brahmavidyāyāṃ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṃvāde  
mokṣasaṃnyāsayogo nāma aṣṭādaśo'dhyāyaḥ 18*

Wherever Krishna, the Lord of Yoga, is present, and wherever Paartha the archer is present, there you will find prosperity, victory, happiness, and firm (steady, sound) policy. This is my conviction.

Thus, in the Upanishads of the glorious Bhagavad Gita, the science of the Eternal, the scripture of Yoga, the dialogue between Sri Krishna and Arjuna, ends the eighteenth discourse, entitled The Yoga of Liberation by Renunciation.
