# Chapter 9 – Rāja-Vidya Rāja-Guhya Yoga (The Yoga of Sovereign Knowledge and the Supreme Secret)

*Yoga of Royal Knowledge and Royal Secret*

### 9.1

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
इदं तु ते गुह्यतमं प्रवक्ष्याम्यनसूयवे ।  
ज्ञानं विज्ञानसहितं यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात् ॥ ९-१॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
idaṃ tu te guhyatamaṃ pravakṣyāmyanasūyave  
jñānaṃ vijñānasahitaṃ yajjñātvā mokṣyase'śubhāt 9-1*

The Blessed Lord said: Because you do not find fault, I will now reveal to you the deepest secret of all: the most profound knowledge joined with direct experience (realisation). Once you have understood it, you will be released from the sorrows of life.

### 9.2

राजविद्या राजगुह्यं पवित्रमिदमुत्तमम् ।  
प्रत्यक्षावगमं धर्म्यं सुसुखं कर्तुमव्ययम् ॥ ९-२॥

*rājavidyā rājaguhyaṃ pavitramidamuttamam  
pratyakṣāvagamaṃ dharmyaṃ susukhaṃ kartumavyayam 9-2*

This is the royal science and the royal secret, the highest purifier. It can be known directly through intuition, it accords with DHARMA, it is very easy to practise, and it never perishes.

### 9.3

अश्रद्दधानाः पुरुषा धर्मस्यास्य परन्तप ।  
अप्राप्य मां निवर्तन्ते मृत्युसंसारवर्त्मनि ॥ ९-३॥

*aśraddadhānāḥ puruṣā dharmasyāsya parantapa  
aprāpya māṃ nivartante mṛtyusaṃsāravartmani 9-3*

O Parantapa, those who have no faith in this DHARMA (the Divine Self) fail to reach Me and come back to the road of rebirth, which is full of death.

### 9.4

मया ततमिदं सर्वं जगदव्यक्तमूर्तिना ।  
मत्स्थानि सर्वभूतानि न चाहं तेष्ववस्थितः ॥ ९-४॥

*mayā tatamidaṃ sarvaṃ jagadavyaktamūrtinā  
matsthāni sarvabhūtāni na cāhaṃ teṣvavasthitaḥ 9-4*

In My Unmanifest form (aspect) I pervade this entire world. Every being exists within Me, yet I do not reside in them.

### 9.5

न च मत्स्थानि भूतानि पश्य मे योगमैश्वरम् ।  
भूतभृन्न च भूतस्थो ममात्मा भूतभावनः ॥ ९-५॥

*na ca matsthāni bhūtāni paśya me yogamaiśvaram  
bhūtabhṛnna ca bhūtastho mamātmā bhūtabhāvanaḥ 9-5*

And in truth, beings do not exist in Me either. See My Divine YOGA: I uphold all beings, yet I do not dwell in them. I Myself am the efficient cause of every being.

### 9.6

यथाकाशस्थितो नित्यं वायुः सर्वत्रगो महान् ।  
तथा सर्वाणि भूतानि मत्स्थानीत्युपधारय ॥ ९-६॥

*yathākāśasthito nityaṃ vāyuḥ sarvatrago mahān  
tathā sarvāṇi bhūtāni matsthānītyupadhāraya 9-6*

Just as the great wind, which moves everywhere, always rests in space (the AKASHA), understand that in the same way all beings rest in Me.

### 9.7

सर्वभूतानि कौन्तेय प्रकृतिं यान्ति मामिकाम् ।  
कल्पक्षये पुनस्तानि कल्पादौ विसृजाम्यहम् ॥ ९-७॥

*sarvabhūtāni kaunteya prakṛtiṃ yānti māmikām  
kalpakṣaye punastāni kalpādau visṛjāmyaham 9-7*

O Kaunteya (Son of Kunti), when a KALPA ends, all beings pass into My PRAKRITI (nature). When the next KALPA begins, I send them out once more.

### 9.8

प्रकृतिं स्वामवष्टभ्य विसृजामि पुनः पुनः ।  
भूतग्राममिमं कृत्स्नमवशं प्रकृतेर्वशात् ॥ ९-८॥

*prakṛtiṃ svāmavaṣṭabhya visṛjāmi punaḥ punaḥ  
bhūtagrāmamimaṃ kṛtsnamavaśaṃ prakṛtervaśāt 9-8*

Giving life to My PRAKRITI, I send forth this whole helpless crowd of beings again and again, through the power of nature (PRAKRITI).

### 9.9

न च मां तानि कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनञ्जय ।  
उदासीनवदासीनमसक्तं तेषु कर्मसु ॥ ९-९॥

*na ca māṃ tāni karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanañjaya  
udāsīnavadāsīnam asaktaṃ teṣu karmasu 9-9*

Dhananjaya, I sit as if unconcerned, with no attachment to these acts, and so these acts do not bind Me.

### 9.10

मयाध्यक्षेण प्रकृतिः सूयते सचराचरम् ।  
हेतुनानेन कौन्तेय जगद्विपरिवर्तते ॥ ९-१०॥

*mayādhyakṣeṇa prakṛtiḥ sūyate sacarācaram  
hetunānena kaunteya jagadviparivartate 9-10*

With Me presiding over her, PRAKRITI (nature) brings forth everything that moves and everything that does not move. For this reason, O Kaunteya, the world keeps turning.

### 9.11

अवजानन्ति मां मूढा मानुषीं तनुमाश्रितम् ।  
परं भावमजानन्तो मम भूतमहेश्वरम् ॥ ९-११॥

*avajānanti māṃ mūḍhā mānuṣīṃ tanumāśritam  
paraṃ bhāvamajānanto mama bhūtamaheśvaram 9-11*

Foolish people ignore Me when I live in a human body. They do not recognise My Higher nature as the Great Lord of every being.

### 9.12

मोघाशा मोघकर्माणो मोघज्ञाना विचेतसः ।  
राक्षसीमासुरीं चैव प्रकृतिं मोहिनीं श्रिताः ॥ ९-१२॥

*moghāśā moghakarmāṇo moghajñānā vicetasaḥ  
rākṣasīmāsurīṃ caiva prakṛtiṃ mohinīṃ śritāḥ 9-12*

Their hopes are empty, their actions are empty, their knowledge is empty, and they lack sense. They are truly gripped by the deluding nature of RAKSHASAS and ASURAS.

### 9.13

महात्मानस्तु मां पार्थ दैवीं प्रकृतिमाश्रिताः ।  
भजन्त्यनन्यमनसो ज्ञात्वा भूतादिमव्ययम् ॥ ९-१३॥

*mahātmānastu māṃ pārtha daivīṃ prakṛtimāśritāḥ  
bhajantyananyamanaso jñātvā bhūtādimavyayam 9-13*

But the MAHATMAS (great souls), O Partha, who share in My divine nature, worship Me with a mind fixed on nothing else, knowing Me as the Imperishable Source of all beings.

### 9.14

सततं कीर्तयन्तो मां यतन्तश्च दृढव्रताः ।  
नमस्यन्तश्च मां भक्त्या नित्ययुक्ता उपासते ॥ ९-१४॥

*satataṃ kīrtayanto māṃ yatantaśca dṛḍhavratāḥ  
namasyantaśca māṃ bhaktyā nityayuktā upāsate 9-14*

They constantly sing My glory, make earnest effort, hold firmly to their vows, bow down before Me, and remain always steady; in this way they worship Me with devotion.

### 9.15

ज्ञानयज्ञेन चाप्यन्ये यजन्तो मामुपासते ।  
एकत्वेन पृथक्त्वेन बहुधा विश्वतोमुखम् ॥ ९-१५॥

*jñānayajñena cāpyanye yajanto māmupāsate  
ekatvena pṛthaktvena bahudhā viśvatomukham 9-15*

Still others worship Me by offering the sacrifice of Wisdom, seeing Me as the One, as the distinct, and as the manifold, I who face in every direction in all My forms.

### 9.16

अहं क्रतुरहं यज्ञः स्वधाहमहमौषधम् ।  
मन्त्रोऽहमहमेवाज्यमहमग्निरहं हुतम् ॥ ९-१६॥

*ahaṃ kraturahaṃ yajñaḥ svadhāhamahamauṣadham  
mantro'hamahamevājyam ahamagnirahaṃ hutam 9-16*

I am the KRATU; I am the sacrifice; I am the offering (food) given to the PITRIS (ancestors); I am the healing herb and every plant; I am the MANTRA; I am the clarified butter as well; I am the fire; I am the oblation.

### 9.17

पिताहमस्य जगतो माता धाता पितामहः ।  
वेद्यं पवित्रमोङ्कार ऋक्साम यजुरेव च ॥ ९-१७॥

*pitāhamasya jagato mātā dhātā pitāmahaḥ  
vedyaṃ pavitramoṅkāra ṛksāma yajureva ca 9-17*

I am the Father of this world, and its Mother, its supporter and its grandsire. I am the one Thing worth knowing, the Purifier, the syllable OM, and also the RIK, the SAMA and the YAJUH.

### 9.18

गतिर्भर्ता प्रभुः साक्षी निवासः शरणं सुहृत् ।  
प्रभवः प्रलयः स्थानं निधानं बीजमव्ययम् ॥ ९-१८॥

*gatirbhartā prabhuḥ sākṣī nivāsaḥ śaraṇaṃ suhṛt  
prabhavaḥ pralayaḥ sthānaṃ nidhānaṃ bījamavyayam 9-18*

The Goal, the Supporter, the Lord, the Witness: all these are Me. I am the Abode, the Shelter and the Friend; the Origin and the Dissolution; the Foundation, the Treasure-house and the Seed that never perishes.

### 9.19

तपाम्यहमहं वर्षं निगृह्णाम्युत्सृजामि च ।  
अमृतं चैव मृत्युश्च सदसच्चाहमर्जुन ॥ ९-१९॥

*tapāmyahamahaṃ varṣaṃ nigṛhṇāmyutsṛjāmi ca  
amṛtaṃ caiva mṛtyuśca sadasaccāhamarjuna 9-19*

As the Sun, I give out heat; I hold back the rain and I release it. I am Immortality and I am also death; I am both Existence and Non-existence, O Arjuna.

### 9.20

त्रैविद्या मां सोमपाः पूतपापा  
यज्ञैरिष्ट्वा स्वर्गतिं प्रार्थयन्ते ।  
ते पुण्यमासाद्य सुरेन्द्रलोक-  
मश्नन्ति दिव्यान्दिवि देवभोगान् ॥ ९-२०॥

*traividyā māṃ somapāḥ pūtapāpā  
yajñairiṣṭvā svargatiṃ prārthayante  
te puṇyamāsādya surendraloka-  
maśnanti divyāndivi devabhogān 9-20*

Those who know the three VEDAS, who drink the SOMA and are cleansed of sin, worship Me through sacrifices and ask for the road to heaven. They arrive in the holy world of the Lord of the gods and there taste the Divine pleasures of the gods.

### 9.21

ते तं भुक्त्वा स्वर्गलोकं विशालं  
क्षीणे पुण्ये मर्त्यलोकं विशन्ति ।  
एवं त्रयीधर्ममनुप्रपन्ना  
गतागतं कामकामा लभन्ते ॥ ९-२१॥

*te taṃ bhuktvā svargalokaṃ viśālaṃ  
kṣīṇe puṇye martyalokaṃ viśanti  
evaṃ trayīdharmamanuprapannā  
gatāgataṃ kāmakāmā labhante 9-21*

After enjoying that vast heavenly world, they re-enter the world of mortals once their merit runs out. So, by following the commands of the three VEDAS and longing for the objects of desire, they end up in the condition of going and returning (SAMSARA).

### 9.22

अनन्याश्चिन्तयन्तो मां ये जनाः पर्युपासते ।  
तेषां नित्याभियुक्तानां योगक्षेमं वहाम्यहम् ॥ ९-२२॥

*ananyāścintayanto māṃ ye janāḥ paryupāsate  
teṣāṃ nityābhiyuktānāṃ yogakṣemaṃ vahāmyaham 9-22*

For those who worship Me alone, with thoughts of nothing else, and who are always self-controlled, I provide what they do not yet have (YOGA) and I protect what they already have (KSHEMA).

### 9.23

येऽप्यन्यदेवता भक्ता यजन्ते श्रद्धयान्विताः ।  
तेऽपि मामेव कौन्तेय यजन्त्यविधिपूर्वकम् ॥ ९-२३॥

*ye'pyanyadevatā bhaktā yajante śraddhayānvitāḥ  
te'pi māmeva kaunteya yajantyavidhipūrvakam 9-23*

Even those devotees who worship other gods with faith are in fact worshipping Me alone, O son of Kunti, though by the wrong method.

### 9.24

अहं हि सर्वयज्ञानां भोक्ता च प्रभुरेव च ।  
न तु मामभिजानन्ति तत्त्वेनातश्च्यवन्ति ते ॥ ९-२४॥

*ahaṃ hi sarvayajñānāṃ bhoktā ca prabhureva ca  
na tu māmabhijānanti tattvenātaścyavanti te 9-24*

For I alone am the enjoyer in every sacrifice and the Lord of them all. But they do not know Me in My true Essence, and so they fall (they come back to this mortal world).

### 9.25

यान्ति देवव्रता देवान्पितॄन्यान्ति पितृव्रताः ।  
भूतानि यान्ति भूतेज्या यान्ति मद्याजिनोऽपि माम् ॥ ९-२५॥

*yānti devavratā devānpitṝnyānti pitṛvratāḥ  
bhūtāni yānti bhūtejyā yānti madyājino'pi mām 9-25*

Those who worship the DEVAS, or gods, go to the DEVAS. Those who worship the PITRIS, or ancestors, go to the ancestors. Those who worship the BHUTAS, or elements, go to the BHUTAS. But those who worship Me come to Me.

### 9.26

पत्रं पुष्पं फलं तोयं यो मे भक्त्या प्रयच्छति ।  
तदहं भक्त्युपहृतमश्नामि प्रयतात्मनः ॥ ९-२६॥

*patraṃ puṣpaṃ phalaṃ toyaṃ yo me bhaktyā prayacchati  
tadahaṃ bhaktyupahṛtamaśnāmi prayatātmanaḥ 9-26*

When someone offers Me a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or water with devotion, I accept that gift, offered with devotion by a pure heart.

### 9.27

यत्करोषि यदश्नासि यज्जुहोषि ददासि यत् ।  
यत्तपस्यसि कौन्तेय तत्कुरुष्व मदर्पणम् ॥ ९-२७॥

*yatkaroṣi yadaśnāsi yajjuhoṣi dadāsi yat  
yattapasyasi kaunteya tatkuruṣva madarpaṇam 9-27*

O Kaunteya, whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in sacrifice, whatever you give away, whatever austerity you practise, do it all as an offering to Me.

### 9.28

शुभाशुभफलैरेवं मोक्ष्यसे कर्मबन्धनैः ।  
संन्यासयोगयुक्तात्मा विमुक्तो मामुपैष्यसि ॥ ९-२८॥

*śubhāśubhaphalairevaṃ mokṣyase karmabandhanaiḥ  
saṃnyāsayogayuktātmā vimukto māmupaiṣyasi 9-28*

In this way you will be released from the bonds of action that produce good and bad results. With your mind firmly set in the YOGA of renunciation, you will be free and will come to Me.

### 9.29

समोऽहं सर्वभूतेषु न मे द्वेष्योऽस्ति न प्रियः ।  
ये भजन्ति तु मां भक्त्या मयि ते तेषु चाप्यहम् ॥ ९-२९॥

*samo'haṃ sarvabhūteṣu na me dveṣyo'sti na priyaḥ  
ye bhajanti tu māṃ bhaktyā mayi te teṣu cāpyaham 9-29*

I am the same toward every being; no one is hateful to Me and no one is dear. But those who worship Me with devotion live in Me, and I live in them too.

### 9.30

अपि चेत्सुदुराचारो भजते मामनन्यभाक् ।  
साधुरेव स मन्तव्यः सम्यग्व्यवसितो हि सः ॥ ९-३०॥

*api cetsudurācāro bhajate māmananyabhāk  
sādhureva sa mantavyaḥ samyagvyavasito hi saḥ 9-30*

Even if a person of the worst conduct worships Me with devotion to no one else (with single-pointed focus), that person should indeed be counted as righteous, because he has made the right resolve.

### 9.31

क्षिप्रं भवति धर्मात्मा शश्वच्छान्तिं निगच्छति ।  
कौन्तेय प्रतिजानीहि न मे भक्तः प्रणश्यति ॥ ९-३१॥

*kṣipraṃ bhavati dharmātmā śaśvacchāntiṃ nigacchati  
kaunteya pratijānīhi na me bhaktaḥ praṇaśyati 9-31*

He quickly becomes righteous and gains Eternal Peace. O Kaunteya, know this for certain: My devotee is never lost.

### 9.32

मां हि पार्थ व्यपाश्रित्य येऽपि स्युः पापयोनयः ।  
स्त्रियो वैश्यास्तथा शूद्रास्तेऽपि यान्ति परां गतिम् ॥ ९-३२॥

*māṃ hi pārtha vyapāśritya ye'pi syuḥ pāpayonayaḥ  
striyo vaiśyāstathā śūdrāste'pi yānti parāṃ gatim 9-32*

For even those who may be of sinful birth, O Partha, WOMEN, VAISHYAS and SHUDRAS alike, reach the Supreme Goal when they take refuge in Me.

### 9.33

किं पुनर्ब्राह्मणाः पुण्या भक्ता राजर्षयस्तथा ।  
अनित्यमसुखं लोकमिमं प्राप्य भजस्व माम् ॥ ९-३३॥

*kiṃ punarbrāhmaṇāḥ puṇyā bhaktā rājarṣayastathā  
anityamasukhaṃ lokamimaṃ prāpya bhajasva mām 9-33*

How much more easily, then, do the holy BRAHMINS and the devoted royal saints reach that goal. Having come into this impermanent and joyless world, worship Me with devotion.

### 9.34

मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु ।  
मामेवैष्यसि युक्त्वैवमात्मानं मत्परायणः ॥ ९-३४॥   
  
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु  
ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे  
राजविद्याराजगुह्ययोगो नाम नवमोऽध्यायः ॥ ९॥

*manmanā bhava madbhakto madyājī māṃ namaskuru  
māmevaiṣyasi yuktvaivamātmānaṃ matparāyaṇaḥ 9-34  
  
oṃ tatsaditi śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu  
brahmavidyāyāṃ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṃvāde  
rājavidyārājaguhyayogo nāma navamo'dhyāyaḥ 9*

Fix your mind on Me. Be devoted to Me, sacrifice to Me, and bow down to Me. Having joined your whole Self with Me in this way, and taking Me as the Supreme Goal, you will come to Me.

Thus, in the Upanishads of the glorious Bhagavad Gita, the science of the Eternal, the scripture of Yoga, the dialogue between Sri Krishna and Arjuna, ends the ninth discourse, entitled "The Yoga of Sovereign Knowledge and the Supreme Secret."
