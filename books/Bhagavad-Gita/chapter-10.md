# Chapter 10 – Vibhūti Yoga (The Yoga of Divine Glories)

*Yoga of Divine Glories*

### 10.1

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
भूय एव महाबाहो श‍ृणु मे परमं वचः ।  
यत्तेऽहं प्रीयमाणाय वक्ष्यामि हितकाम्यया ॥ १०-१॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
bhūya eva mahābāho śṛṇu me paramaṃ vacaḥ  
yatte'haṃ prīyamāṇāya vakṣyāmi hitakāmyayā 10-1*

The Blessed Lord said: Listen once more, mighty-armed one, to My supreme teaching. Because I want your good, I will speak it to you, who take joy in hearing Me.

### 10.2

न मे विदुः सुरगणाः प्रभवं न महर्षयः ।  
अहमादिर्हि देवानां महर्षीणां च सर्वशः ॥ १०-२॥

*na me viduḥ suragaṇāḥ prabhavaṃ na maharṣayaḥ  
ahamādirhi devānāṃ maharṣīṇāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ 10-2*

Neither the gods in heaven nor the great rishis know where I come from, for in every respect I am the origin of all the devas and the rishis.

### 10.3

यो मामजमनादिं च वेत्ति लोकमहेश्वरम् ।  
असम्मूढः स मर्त्येषु सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥ १०-३॥

*yo māmajamanādiṃ ca vetti lokamaheśvaram  
asammūḍhaḥ sa martyeṣu sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate 10-3*

Whoever among mortals knows Me as unborn and without beginning, as the great Lord of the worlds, is free from delusion and released from every sin.

### 10.4

बुद्धिर्ज्ञानमसम्मोहः क्षमा सत्यं दमः शमः ।  
सुखं दुःखं भवोऽभावो भयं चाभयमेव च ॥ १०-४॥

*buddhirjñānamasammohaḥ kṣamā satyaṃ damaḥ śamaḥ  
sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ bhavo'bhāvo bhayaṃ cābhayameva ca 10-4*

Intellect, wisdom, freedom from delusion, forgiveness, truth, self-control, calm, happiness, pain, birth and death, fear and fearlessness too, . . .

### 10.5

अहिंसा समता तुष्टिस्तपो दानं यशोऽयशः ।  
भवन्ति भावा भूतानां मत्त एव पृथग्विधाः ॥ १०-५॥

*ahiṃsā samatā tuṣṭistapo dānaṃ yaśo'yaśaḥ  
bhavanti bhāvā bhūtānāṃ matta eva pṛthagvidhāḥ 10-5*

Non-violence, evenness of mind, contentment, austerity, generosity, fame, and disgrace: all these varied qualities found in beings come from Me alone.

### 10.6

महर्षयः सप्त पूर्वे चत्वारो मनवस्तथा ।  
मद्भावा मानसा जाता येषां लोक इमाः प्रजाः ॥ १०-६॥

*maharṣayaḥ sapta pūrve catvāro manavastathā  
madbhāvā mānasā jātā yeṣāṃ loka imāḥ prajāḥ 10-6*

The seven great rishis, the four ancients before them, and the Manus, who share My powers, were born from My mind. From them have come all the creatures in the world, and by them they are sustained.

### 10.7

एतां विभूतिं योगं च मम यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ।  
सोऽविकम्पेन योगेन युज्यते नात्र संशयः ॥ १०-७॥

*etāṃ vibhūtiṃ yogaṃ ca mama yo vetti tattvataḥ  
so'vikampena yogena yujyate nātra saṃśayaḥ 10-7*

Whoever truly knows these many manifestations of My being (the macrocosm) and this yoga-power of Mine (the microcosm) becomes firmly settled in unshakable yoga. Of this there is no doubt.

### 10.8

अहं सर्वस्य प्रभवो मत्तः सर्वं प्रवर्तते ।  
इति मत्वा भजन्ते मां बुधा भावसमन्विताः ॥ १०-८॥

*ahaṃ sarvasya prabhavo mattaḥ sarvaṃ pravartate  
iti matvā bhajante māṃ budhā bhāvasamanvitāḥ 10-8*

I am the origin of everything; from Me all things unfold. Understanding this, the wise, filled with loving awareness, worship Me.

### 10.9

मच्चित्ता मद्गतप्राणा बोधयन्तः परस्परम् ।  
कथयन्तश्च मां नित्यं तुष्यन्ति च रमन्ति च ॥ १०-९॥

*maccittā madgataprāṇā bodhayantaḥ parasparam  
kathayantaśca māṃ nityaṃ tuṣyanti ca ramanti ca 10-9*

With their minds resting entirely in Me and their senses absorbed in Me, they enlighten one another and speak of Me constantly, and in this they find contentment and joy.

### 10.10

तेषां सततयुक्तानां भजतां प्रीतिपूर्वकम् ।  
ददामि बुद्धियोगं तं येन मामुपयान्ति ते ॥ १०-१०॥

*teṣāṃ satatayuktānāṃ bhajatāṃ prītipūrvakam  
dadāmi buddhiyogaṃ taṃ yena māmupayānti te 10-10*

To those who are always steadfast and worship Me with love, I grant the buddhi yoga by which they reach Me.

### 10.11

तेषामेवानुकम्पार्थमहमज्ञानजं तमः ।  
नाशयाम्यात्मभावस्थो ज्ञानदीपेन भास्वता ॥ १०-११॥

*teṣāmevānukampārthamahamajñānajaṃ tamaḥ  
nāśayāmyātmabhāvastho jñānadīpena bhāsvatā 10-11*

Out of pure compassion for them, I, who live within their hearts, dispel the darkness born of ignorance with the shining lamp of knowledge.

### 10.12

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
परं ब्रह्म परं धाम पवित्रं परमं भवान् ।  
पुरुषं शाश्वतं दिव्यमादिदेवमजं विभुम् ॥ १०-१२॥

*arjuna uvāca  
paraṃ brahma paraṃ dhāma pavitraṃ paramaṃ bhavān  
puruṣaṃ śāśvataṃ divyamādidevamajaṃ vibhum 10-12*

Arjuna said: You are the supreme Brahman, the supreme dwelling place, the supreme purifier, the eternal and divine Purusha, the God of all gods, unborn and present everywhere.

### 10.13

आहुस्त्वामृषयः सर्वे देवर्षिर्नारदस्तथा ।  
असितो देवलो व्यासः स्वयं चैव ब्रवीषि मे ॥ १०-१३॥

*āhustvāmṛṣayaḥ sarve devarṣirnāradastathā  
asito devalo vyāsaḥ svayaṃ caiva bravīṣi me 10-13*

All the rishis have declared You to be this, as have the deva-rishi Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa; and now You Yourself are telling me the same.

### 10.14

सर्वमेतदृतं मन्ये यन्मां वदसि केशव ।  
न हि ते भगवन्व्यक्तिं विदुर्देवा न दानवाः ॥ १०-१४॥

*sarvametadṛtaṃ manye yanmāṃ vadasi keśava  
na hi te bhagavanvyaktiṃ vidurdevā na dānavāḥ 10-14*

I accept everything You tell me as true, O Keshava. Truly, Blessed Lord, neither the devas nor the danavas know Your manifestation, Your real identity.

### 10.15

स्वयमेवात्मनात्मानं वेत्थ त्वं पुरुषोत्तम ।  
भूतभावन भूतेश देवदेव जगत्पते ॥ १०-१५॥

*svayamevātmanātmānaṃ vettha tvaṃ puruṣottama  
bhūtabhāvana bhūteśa devadeva jagatpate 10-15*

Indeed, You alone know Yourself, through Yourself, O Purushottama (supreme Purusha), source of beings, Lord of beings, God of gods, ruler of the world.

### 10.16

वक्तुमर्हस्यशेषेण दिव्या ह्यात्मविभूतयः ।  
याभिर्विभूतिभिर्लोकानिमांस्त्वं व्याप्य तिष्ठसि ॥ १०-१६॥

*vaktumarhasyaśeṣeṇa divyā hyātmavibhūtayaḥ  
yābhirvibhūtibhirlokānimāṃstvaṃ vyāpya tiṣṭhasi 10-16*

Please tell me fully, holding nothing back, about Your divine glories, the ones by which You pervade and dwell in all these worlds.

### 10.17

कथं विद्यामहं योगिंस्त्वां सदा परिचिन्तयन् ।  
केषु केषु च भावेषु चिन्त्योऽसि भगवन्मया ॥ १०-१७॥

*kathaṃ vidyāmahaṃ yogiṃstvāṃ sadā paricintayan  
keṣu keṣu ca bhāveṣu cintyo'si bhagavanmayā 10-17*

How am I, meditating on You always, to know You, O Yogin? In which forms and in which things, Blessed Lord, should I think of You?

### 10.18

विस्तरेणात्मनो योगं विभूतिं च जनार्दन ।  
भूयः कथय तृप्तिर्हि श‍ृण्वतो नास्ति मेऽमृतम् ॥ १०-१८॥

*vistareṇātmano yogaṃ vibhūtiṃ ca janārdana  
bhūyaḥ kathaya tṛptirhi śṛṇvato nāsti me'mṛtam 10-18*

Tell me again and in detail, O Janardana, of Your yoga-power and Your glory that fills all things, for I cannot get enough of hearing Your speech, which is like nectar that gives life.

### 10.19

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
हन्त ते कथयिष्यामि दिव्या ह्यात्मविभूतयः ।  
प्राधान्यतः कुरुश्रेष्ठ नास्त्यन्तो विस्तरस्य मे ॥ १०-१९॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi divyā hyātmavibhūtayaḥ  
prādhānyataḥ kuruśreṣṭha nāstyanto vistarasya me 10-19*

The Blessed Lord said: Alas! I will now describe My divine glories to you, the ones that pervade all things, choosing only the chief among them, O best of the Kurus, for there is no end to the details of My extent.

### 10.20

अहमात्मा गुडाकेश सर्वभूताशयस्थितः ।  
अहमादिश्च मध्यं च भूतानामन्त एव च ॥ १०-२०॥

*ahamātmā guḍākeśa sarvabhūtāśayasthitaḥ  
ahamādiśca madhyaṃ ca bhūtānāmanta eva ca 10-20*

I am the Self, O Gudakesha, seated in the hearts of all beings. I am the beginning, the middle, and the end of all beings as well.

### 10.21

आदित्यानामहं विष्णुर्ज्योतिषां रविरंशुमान् ।  
मरीचिर्मरुतामस्मि नक्षत्राणामहं शशी ॥ १०-२१॥

*ādityānāmahaṃ viṣṇurjyotiṣāṃ raviraṃśumān  
marīcirmarutāmasmi nakṣatrāṇāmahaṃ śaśī 10-21*

Among the twelve Adityas I am Vishnu; among sources of light I am the radiant sun; among the Maruts I am Marichi; among the stars I am the moon.

### 10.22

वेदानां सामवेदोऽस्मि देवानामस्मि वासवः ।  
इन्द्रियाणां मनश्चास्मि भूतानामस्मि चेतना ॥ १०-२२॥

*vedānāṃ sāmavedo'smi devānāmasmi vāsavaḥ  
indriyāṇāṃ manaścāsmi bhūtānāmasmi cetanā 10-22*

Among the Vedas I am the Sama-Veda; among the gods I am Vasava; among the senses I am the mind; and in living beings I am the intelligence.

### 10.23

रुद्राणां शङ्करश्चास्मि वित्तेशो यक्षरक्षसाम् ।  
वसूनां पावकश्चास्मि मेरुः शिखरिणामहम् ॥ १०-२३॥

*rudrāṇāṃ śaṅkaraścāsmi vitteśo yakṣarakṣasām  
vasūnāṃ pāvakaścāsmi meruḥ śikhariṇāmaham 10-23*

Among the Rudras I am Shankara; among the yakshas and rakshasas I am Kubera, the lord of wealth; among the Vasus I am Pavaka (Agni); and among mountains I am Meru.

### 10.24

पुरोधसां च मुख्यं मां विद्धि पार्थ बृहस्पतिम् ।  
सेनानीनामहं स्कन्दः सरसामस्मि सागरः ॥ १०-२४॥

*purodhasāṃ ca mukhyaṃ māṃ viddhi pārtha bṛhaspatim  
senānīnāmahaṃ skandaḥ sarasāmasmi sāgaraḥ 10-24*

Among household priests, O Partha, know Me to be the chief of them, Brihaspati; among commanders of armies I am Skanda; among bodies of water I am the ocean.

### 10.25

महर्षीणां भृगुरहं गिरामस्म्येकमक्षरम् ।  
यज्ञानां जपयज्ञोऽस्मि स्थावराणां हिमालयः ॥ १०-२५॥

*maharṣīṇāṃ bhṛgurahaṃ girāmasmyekamakṣaram  
yajñānāṃ japayajño'smi sthāvarāṇāṃ himālayaḥ 10-25*

Among the great rishis I am Bhrigu; among words I am the single syllable Om; among sacrifices I am the sacrifice of silent repetition (japa-yajna); among things that do not move I am the Himalayas.

### 10.26

अश्वत्थः सर्ववृक्षाणां देवर्षीणां च नारदः ।  
गन्धर्वाणां चित्ररथः सिद्धानां कपिलो मुनिः ॥ १०-२६॥

*aśvatthaḥ sarvavṛkṣāṇāṃ devarṣīṇāṃ ca nāradaḥ  
gandharvāṇāṃ citrarathaḥ siddhānāṃ kapilo muniḥ 10-26*

Among all trees I am the ashwattha tree; among divine rishis, Narada; among gandharvas, Chitraratha; among perfected beings, the muni Kapila.

### 10.27

उच्चैःश्रवसमश्वानां विद्धि माममृतोद्भवम् ।  
ऐरावतं गजेन्द्राणां नराणां च नराधिपम् ॥ १०-२७॥

*uccaiḥśravasamaśvānāṃ viddhi māmamṛtodbhavam  
airāvataṃ gajendrāṇāṃ narāṇāṃ ca narādhipam 10-27*

Among horses know Me as Ucchahi-shravas, born of amrita; among royal elephants, Airavata; and among human beings, the king.

### 10.28

आयुधानामहं वज्रं धेनूनामस्मि कामधुक् ।  
प्रजनश्चास्मि कन्दर्पः सर्पाणामस्मि वासुकिः ॥ १०-२८॥

*āyudhānāmahaṃ vajraṃ dhenūnāmasmi kāmadhuk  
prajanaścāsmi kandarpaḥ sarpāṇāmasmi vāsukiḥ 10-28*

Among weapons I am the thunderbolt; among cows I am Kamadhuk; I am Kandarpa, the cause of offspring; among serpents I am Vasuki.

### 10.29

अनन्तश्चास्मि नागानां वरुणो यादसामहम् ।  
पितॄणामर्यमा चास्मि यमः संयमतामहम् ॥ १०-२९॥

*anantaścāsmi nāgānāṃ varuṇo yādasāmaham  
pitṝṇāmaryamā cāsmi yamaḥ saṃyamatāmaham 10-29*

Among the nagas I am Ananta; among the deities of the waters I am Varuna; among the ancestors I am Aryama; and among those who govern I am Yama.

### 10.30

प्रह्लादश्चास्मि दैत्यानां कालः कलयतामहम् ।  
मृगाणां च मृगेन्द्रोऽहं वैनतेयश्च पक्षिणाम् ॥ १०-३०॥

*prahlādaścāsmi daityānāṃ kālaḥ kalayatāmaham  
mṛgāṇāṃ ca mṛgendro'haṃ vainateyaśca pakṣiṇām 10-30*

Among the daityas I am Prahlada; among those that measure, I am Time; among animals I am the lion, lord of beasts; and among birds I am Vainateya (Garuda).

### 10.31

पवनः पवतामस्मि रामः शस्त्रभृतामहम् ।  
झषाणां मकरश्चास्मि स्रोतसामस्मि जाह्नवी ॥ १०-३१॥

*pavanaḥ pavatāmasmi rāmaḥ śastrabhṛtāmaham  
jhaṣāṇāṃ makaraścāsmi srotasāmasmi jāhnavī 10-31*

Among purifiers I am the wind; among those who bear arms I am Rama; among fish I am the shark; among rivers I am the Ganges.

### 10.32

सर्गाणामादिरन्तश्च मध्यं चैवाहमर्जुन ।  
अध्यात्मविद्या विद्यानां वादः प्रवदतामहम् ॥ १०-३२॥

*sargāṇāmādirantaśca madhyaṃ caivāhamarjuna  
adhyātmavidyā vidyānāṃ vādaḥ pravadatāmaham 10-32*

Of all created things, O Arjuna, I am the beginning, the middle, and the end as well. Among sciences I am the science of the Self, and in every debate I am the reasoning.

### 10.33

अक्षराणामकारोऽस्मि द्वन्द्वः सामासिकस्य च ।  
अहमेवाक्षयः कालो धाताहं विश्वतोमुखः ॥ १०-३३॥

*akṣarāṇāmakāro'smi dvandvaḥ sāmāsikasya ca  
ahamevākṣayaḥ kālo dhātāhaṃ viśvatomukhaḥ 10-33*

Among letters I am the letter A; among compound words I am the dual compound (the co-ordinate type). I am indeed inexhaustible, everlasting Time, and I am the dispenser of the fruits of action, whose faces look in every direction.

### 10.34

मृत्युः सर्वहरश्चाहमुद्भवश्च भविष्यताम् ।  
कीर्तिः श्रीर्वाक्च नारीणां स्मृतिर्मेधा धृतिः क्षमा ॥ १०-३४॥

*mṛtyuḥ sarvaharaścāhamudbhavaśca bhaviṣyatām  
kīrtiḥ śrīrvākca nārīṇāṃ smṛtirmedhā dhṛtiḥ kṣamā 10-34*

I am Death, which swallows everything, and I am the rise of those who are destined to rise. Among feminine qualities I am fame, prosperity, speech, memory, intelligence, steadiness, and forgiveness.

### 10.35

बृहत्साम तथा साम्नां गायत्री छन्दसामहम् ।  
मासानां मार्गशीर्षोऽहमृतूनां कुसुमाकरः ॥ १०-३५॥

*bṛhatsāma tathā sāmnāṃ gāyatrī chandasāmaham  
māsānāṃ mārgaśīrṣo'hamṛtūnāṃ kusumākaraḥ 10-35*

Among hymns, too, I am the Brihat-saman; among metres I am the Gayatri; among months I am Margashirsha (the part of December-January); among seasons I am spring, the season of flowers.

### 10.36

द्यूतं छलयतामस्मि तेजस्तेजस्विनामहम् ।  
जयोऽस्मि व्यवसायोऽस्मि सत्त्वं सत्त्ववतामहम् ॥ १०-३६॥

*dyūtaṃ chalayatāmasmi tejastejasvināmaham  
jayo'smi vyavasāyo'smi sattvaṃ sattvavatāmaham 10-36*

I am the gambling of cheats; I am the brilliance of the brilliant; I am victory; I am the effort of those who are determined; I am the goodness in the good.

### 10.37

वृष्णीनां वासुदेवोऽस्मि पाण्डवानां धनञ्जयः ।  
मुनीनामप्यहं व्यासः कवीनामुशना कविः ॥ १०-३७॥

*vṛṣṇīnāṃ vāsudevo'smi pāṇḍavānāṃ dhanañjayaḥ  
munīnāmapyahaṃ vyāsaḥ kavīnāmuśanā kaviḥ 10-37*

Among the Vrishnis I am Vaasudeva; among the Pandavas I am Dhananjaya; among the munis I am Vyasa; and among poets I am Ushana, the great seer.

### 10.38

दण्डो दमयतामस्मि नीतिरस्मि जिगीषताम् ।  
मौनं चैवास्मि गुह्यानां ज्ञानं ज्ञानवतामहम् ॥ १०-३८॥

*daṇḍo damayatāmasmi nītirasmi jigīṣatām  
maunaṃ caivāsmi guhyānāṃ jñānaṃ jñānavatāmaham 10-38*

Among those who punish I am the sceptre; among those who seek to win I am statesmanship; among secrets I am silence; and among those who know I am knowledge itself.

### 10.39

यच्चापि सर्वभूतानां बीजं तदहमर्जुन ।  
न तदस्ति विना यत्स्यान्मया भूतं चराचरम् ॥ १०-३९॥

*yaccāpi sarvabhūtānāṃ bījaṃ tadahamarjuna  
na tadasti vinā yatsyānmayā bhūtaṃ carācaram 10-39*

Whatever is the seed of every being, that too am I, O Arjuna. There is no being, moving or unmoving, that can exist apart from Me.

### 10.40

नान्तोऽस्ति मम दिव्यानां विभूतीनां परन्तप ।  
एष तूद्देशतः प्रोक्तो विभूतेर्विस्तरो मया ॥ १०-४०॥

*nānto'sti mama divyānāṃ vibhūtīnāṃ parantapa  
eṣa tūddeśataḥ prokto vibhūtervistaro mayā 10-40*

My divine glories have no end, O Parantapa. What I have given you is only a short account of some of their particulars.

### 10.41

यद्यद्विभूतिमत्सत्त्वं श्रीमदूर्जितमेव वा ।  
तत्तदेवावगच्छ त्वं मम तेजोंऽशसम्भवम् ॥ १०-४१॥

*yadyadvibhūtimatsattvaṃ śrīmadūrjitameva vā  
tattadevāvagaccha tvaṃ mama tejoṃ'śasambhavam 10-41*

Whatever in any being is glorious, prosperous, or powerful, know that it springs from a fragment of My splendour.

### 10.42

अथवा बहुनैतेन किं ज्ञातेन तवार्जुन ।  
विष्टभ्याहमिदं कृत्स्नमेकांशेन स्थितो जगत् ॥ १०-४२॥   
  
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु  
ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे  
विभूतियोगो नाम दशमोऽध्यायः ॥ १०॥

*athavā bahunaitena kiṃ jñātena tavārjuna  
viṣṭabhyāhamidaṃ kṛtsnamekāṃśena sthito jagat  10-42  
  
oṃ tatsaditi śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu  
brahmavidyāyāṃ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṃvāde  
vibhūtiyogo nāma daśamo'dhyāyaḥ 10*

But what use is it to you, Arjuna, to know all these details? With a single fraction of Myself I stand supporting this entire world.

So ends the tenth discourse, called "The Yoga of Divine Glories," in the Upanishads of the glorious Bhagavad Gita, the science of the Eternal, the scripture of Yoga, the dialogue between Sri Krishna and Arjuna.
