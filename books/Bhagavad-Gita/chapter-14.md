# Chapter 14 – Guṇa-Traya Vibhāga Yoga (The Yoga of the Division of the Three Guṇas)

*Yoga of the Division of the Three Guṇas*

### 14.1

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
परं भूयः प्रवक्ष्यामि ज्ञानानां ज्ञानमुत्तमम् ।  
यज्ज्ञात्वा मुनयः सर्वे परां सिद्धिमितो गताः ॥ १४-१॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
paraṃ bhūyaḥ pravakṣyāmi jñānānāṃ jñānamuttamam  
yajjñātvā munayaḥ sarve parāṃ siddhimito gatāḥ 14-1*

The Blessed Lord said: Once more I will explain to you that Supreme Knowledge, the finest of all forms of knowledge. Every sage who has come to know it has reached Supreme Perfection after this life.

### 14.2

इदं ज्ञानमुपाश्रित्य मम साधर्म्यमागताः ।  
सर्गेऽपि नोपजायन्ते प्रलये न व्यथन्ति च ॥ १४-२॥

*idaṃ jñānamupāśritya mama sādharmyamāgatāḥ  
sarge'pi nopajāyante pralaye na vyathanti ca 14-2*

Those who take shelter in this Knowledge and so arrive at My own Being are not born when Creation begins, nor are they troubled when dissolution comes.

### 14.3

मम योनिर्महद् ब्रह्म तस्मिन्गर्भं दधाम्यहम् ।  
सम्भवः सर्वभूतानां ततो भवति भारत ॥ १४-३॥

*mama yonirmahad brahma tasmingarbhaṃ dadhāmyaham  
sambhavaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ tato bhavati bhārata 14-3*

The great BRAHMAN (MULA PRAKRITI) is My womb. Into it I place the seed, and from that, O Bharata, all beings are born.

### 14.4

सर्वयोनिषु कौन्तेय मूर्तयः सम्भवन्ति याः ।  
तासां ब्रह्म महद्योनिरहं बीजप्रदः पिता ॥ १४-४॥

*sarvayoniṣu kaunteya mūrtayaḥ sambhavanti yāḥ  
tāsāṃ brahma mahadyonirahaṃ bījapradaḥ pitā 14-4*

O Kaunteya, whatever forms arise in any womb at all, the great BRAHMA is the womb that holds them, and I am the Father who supplies the seed.

### 14.5

सत्त्वं रजस्तम इति गुणाः प्रकृतिसम्भवाः ।  
निबध्नन्ति महाबाहो देहे देहिनमव्ययम् ॥ १४-५॥

*sattvaṃ rajastama iti guṇāḥ prakṛtisambhavāḥ  
nibadhnanti mahābāho dehe dehinamavyayam 14-5*

Purity, passion and inertia: these qualities (GUNAS), O mighty-armed one, spring from PRAKRITI, and they tie the Indestructible Embodied one firmly to the body.

### 14.6

तत्र सत्त्वं निर्मलत्वात्प्रकाशकमनामयम् ।  
सुखसङ्गेन बध्नाति ज्ञानसङ्गेन चानघ ॥ १४-६॥

*tatra sattvaṃ nirmalatvāt prakāśakamanāmayam  
sukhasaṅgena badhnāti jñānasaṅgena cānagha 14-6*

Among these, SATTWA, being free of stain, is luminous and healthy (it does not obstruct). O sinless one, it binds by creating attachment to happiness and attachment to knowledge.

### 14.7

रजो रागात्मकं विद्धि तृष्णासङ्गसमुद्भवम् ।  
तन्निबध्नाति कौन्तेय कर्मसङ्गेन देहिनम् ॥ १४-७॥

*rajo rāgātmakaṃ viddhi tṛṣṇāsaṅgasamudbhavam  
tannibadhnāti kaunteya karmasaṅgena dehinam 14-7*

Understand that RAJAS has the nature of passion; it is the source of craving and attachment. O Kaunteya, it binds the embodied one tightly through attachment to action.

### 14.8

तमस्त्वज्ञानजं विद्धि मोहनं सर्वदेहिनाम् ।  
प्रमादालस्यनिद्राभि स्तन्निबध्नाति भारत ॥ १४-८॥

*tamastvajñānajaṃ viddhi mohanaṃ sarvadehinām  
pramādālasyanidrābhi stannibadhnāti bhārata 14-8*

But understand that TAMAS is born of ignorance and deludes every embodied being. O Bharata, it binds tightly through heedlessness, laziness and sleep.

### 14.9

सत्त्वं सुखे सञ्जयति रजः कर्मणि भारत ।  
ज्ञानमावृत्य तु तमः प्रमादे सञ्जयत्युत ॥ १४-९॥

*sattvaṃ sukhe sañjayati rajaḥ karmaṇi bhārata  
jñānamāvṛtya tu tamaḥ pramāde sañjayatyuta 14-9*

SATTWA attaches a person to happiness, and RAJAS to action, O Bharata; TAMAS, by covering over knowledge, attaches a person to heedlessness.

### 14.10

रजस्तमश्चाभिभूय सत्त्वं भवति भारत ।  
रजः सत्त्वं तमश्चैव तमः सत्त्वं रजस्तथा ॥ १४-१०॥

*rajastamaścābhibhūya sattvaṃ bhavati bhārata  
rajaḥ sattvaṃ tamaścaiva tamaḥ sattvaṃ rajastathā 14-10*

At one time, O Bharata, SATTWA rises and prevails, having overcome RAJAS and inertia (TAMAS); at another time RAJAS prevails, having overcome SATTWA and inertia; and at another, inertia (TAMAS) prevails, having overcome SATTWA and RAJAS.

### 14.11

सर्वद्वारेषु देहेऽस्मिन्प्रकाश उपजायते ।  
ज्ञानं यदा तदा विद्याद्विवृद्धं सत्त्वमित्युत ॥ १४-११॥

*sarvadvāreṣu dehe'sminprakāśa upajāyate  
jñānaṃ yadā tadā vidyādvivṛddhaṃ sattvamityuta 14-11*

When the light of intelligence shines out through every gate (every sense) of this body, you may know that SATTWA is in control.

### 14.12

लोभः प्रवृत्तिरारम्भः कर्मणामशमः स्पृहा ।  
रजस्येतानि जायन्ते विवृद्धे भरतर्षभ ॥ १४-१२॥

*lobhaḥ pravṛttirārambhaḥ karmaṇāmaśamaḥ spṛhā  
rajasyetāni jāyante vivṛddhe bharatarṣabha 14-12*

Greed, activity, the launching of new undertakings, restlessness and longing: these appear when RAJAS is in control, O best of the Bharatas.

### 14.13

अप्रकाशोऽप्रवृत्तिश्च प्रमादो मोह एव च ।  
तमस्येतानि जायन्ते विवृद्धे कुरुनन्दन ॥ १४-१३॥

*aprakāśo'pravṛttiśca pramādo moha eva ca  
tamasyetāni jāyante vivṛddhe kurunandana 14-13*

Darkness, inertness, heedlessness and delusion: these appear when TAMAS is in control, O descendant of Kuru.

### 14.14

यदा सत्त्वे प्रवृद्धे तु प्रलयं याति देहभृत् ।  
तदोत्तमविदां लोकानमलान्प्रतिपद्यते ॥ १४-१४॥

*yadā sattve pravṛddhe tu pralayaṃ yāti dehabhṛt  
tadottamavidāṃlokān amalānpratipadyate 14-14*

If the embodied one dies while SATTWA is in control, he goes to the spotless worlds of those who know the Highest.

### 14.15

रजसि प्रलयं गत्वा कर्मसङ्गिषु जायते ।  
तथा प्रलीनस्तमसि मूढयोनिषु जायते ॥ १४-१५॥

*rajasi pralayaṃ gatvā karmasaṅgiṣu jāyate  
tathā pralīnastamasi mūḍhayoniṣu jāyate 14-15*

One who dies while RAJAS prevails is born among people attached to action; one who dies while TAMAS prevails is born in the womb of the senseless.

### 14.16

कर्मणः सुकृतस्याहुः सात्त्विकं निर्मलं फलम् ।  
रजसस्तु फलं दुःखमज्ञानं तमसः फलम् ॥ १४-१६॥

*karmaṇaḥ sukṛtasyāhuḥ sāttvikaṃ nirmalaṃ phalam  
rajasastu phalaṃ duḥkhamajñānaṃ tamasaḥ phalam 14-16*

The fruit of good action, it is said, is SATTVIC and pure. The fruit of RAJAS is truly pain, and the fruit of TAMAS is ignorance.

### 14.17

सत्त्वात्सञ्जायते ज्ञानं रजसो लोभ एव च ।  
प्रमादमोहौ तमसो भवतोऽज्ञानमेव च ॥ १४-१७॥

*sattvātsañjāyate jñānaṃ rajaso lobha eva ca  
pramādamohau tamaso bhavato'jñānameva ca 14-17*

From SATTWA comes knowledge; from RAJAS comes greed; from TAMAS come heedlessness, delusion and ignorance as well.

### 14.18

ऊर्ध्वं गच्छन्ति सत्त्वस्था मध्ये तिष्ठन्ति राजसाः ।  
जघन्यगुणवृत्तिस्था अधो गच्छन्ति तामसाः ॥ १४-१८॥

*ūrdhvaṃ gacchanti sattvasthā madhye tiṣṭhanti rājasāḥ  
jaghanyaguṇavṛttisthā adho gacchanti tāmasāḥ 14-18*

Those established in SATTWA rise upward; the RAJASIC remain in the middle; and the TAMASIC, who live in the workings of the lowest GUNA, sink downward.

### 14.19

नान्यं गुणेभ्यः कर्तारं यदा द्रष्टानुपश्यति ।  
गुणेभ्यश्च परं वेत्ति मद्भावं सोऽधिगच्छति ॥ १४-१९॥

*nānyaṃ guṇebhyaḥ kartāraṃ yadā draṣṭānupaśyati  
guṇebhyaśca paraṃ vetti madbhāvaṃ so'dhigacchati 14-19*

When the Seer perceives no doer other than the GUNAS, and knows the One who is above the GUNAS, he reaches My Being.

### 14.20

गुणानेतानतीत्य त्रीन्देही देहसमुद्भवान् ।  
जन्ममृत्युजरादुःखैर्विमुक्तोऽमृतमश्नुते ॥ १४-२०॥

*guṇānetānatītya trīndehī dehasamudbhavān  
janmamṛtyujarāduḥkhair vimukto'mṛtamaśnute 14-20*

When the embodied one has passed beyond these three GUNAS, from which the body is produced, he is set free from birth, death, old age and pain, and he gains Immortality.

### 14.21

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
कैर्लिङ्गैस्त्रीन्गुणानेता नतीतो भवति प्रभो ।  
किमाचारः कथं चैतांस्त्रीन्गुणानतिवर्तते ॥ १४-२१॥

*arjuna uvāca  
kairliṅgaistrīnguṇānetā natīto bhavati prabho  
kimācāraḥ kathaṃ caitāṃ strīnguṇānativartate 14-21*

Arjuna said: O Lord, what are the signs of someone who has passed beyond the three GUNAS? How does he behave, and how does he move past these three GUNAS?

### 14.22

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
प्रकाशं च प्रवृत्तिं च मोहमेव च पाण्डव ।  
न द्वेष्टि सम्प्रवृत्तानि न निवृत्तानि काङ्क्षति ॥ १४-२२॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
prakāśaṃ ca pravṛttiṃ ca mohameva ca pāṇḍava  
na dveṣṭi sampravṛttāni na nivṛttāni kāṅkṣati 14-22*

The Blessed Lord said: O Pandava, he does not dislike light, activity or delusion when they are present, nor does he yearn for them when they are gone.

### 14.23

उदासीनवदासीनो गुणैर्यो न विचाल्यते ।  
गुणा वर्तन्त इत्येवं योऽवतिष्ठति नेङ्गते ॥ १४-२३॥

*udāsīnavadāsīno guṇairyo na vicālyate  
guṇā vartanta ityevaṃ yo'vatiṣṭhati neṅgate 14-23*

He sits as if unconcerned and is not shaken by the GUNAS; knowing that it is only the GUNAS that are at work, he stays centred in himself and does not waver . . .

### 14.24

समदुःखसुखः स्वस्थः समलोष्टाश्मकाञ्चनः ।  
तुल्यप्रियाप्रियो धीरस्तुल्यनिन्दात्मसंस्तुतिः ॥ १४-२४॥

*samaduḥkhasukhaḥ svasthaḥ samaloṣṭāśmakāñcanaḥ  
tulyapriyāpriyodhīrah stulyanindātmasaṃstutiḥ 14-24*

He is the same in pleasure and in pain; he rests in the Self; a lump of earth, a jewel and gold are all equal to him; what is pleasant and what is unpleasant are alike to him; he is steady; blame and praise of himself are the same to him . . .

### 14.25

मानापमानयो स्तुल्यस्तुल्यो मित्रारिपक्षयोः ।  
सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी गुणातीतः स उच्यते ॥ १४-२५॥

*mānāpamānayostulya stulyo mitrāripakṣayoḥ  
sarvārambhaparityāgī guṇātītaḥ sa ucyate 14-25*

He is the same in honour and in disgrace, the same toward friend and enemy, and he has given up all undertakings. Such a person is said to have passed beyond the GUNAS.

### 14.26

मां च योऽव्यभिचारेण भक्तियोगेन सेवते ।  
स गुणान्समतीत्यैतान् ब्रह्मभूयाय कल्पते ॥ १४-२६॥

*māṃ ca yo'vyabhicāreṇa bhaktiyogena sevate  
sa guṇānsamatītyaitān brahmabhūyāya kalpate 14-26*

And whoever serves Me with devotion that never wavers passes beyond the GUNAS and becomes fit to be BRAHMAN.

### 14.27

ब्रह्मणो हि प्रतिष्ठाहममृतस्याव्ययस्य च ।  
शाश्वतस्य च धर्मस्य सुखस्यैकान्तिकस्य च ॥ १४-२७॥   
  
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु  
ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे  
गुणत्रयविभागयोगो नाम चतुर्दशोऽध्यायः ॥ १४॥

*brahmaṇo hi pratiṣṭhāham amṛtasyāvyayasya ca  
śāśvatasya ca dharmasya sukhasyaikāntikasya ca 14-27  
  
oṃ tatsaditi śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu  
brahmavidyāyāṃ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṃvāde  
guṇatrayavibhāgayogo nāma caturdaśo'dhyāyaḥ 14*

Brahman, the Immortal and Unchanging, has its abode in Me, as do everlasting Dharma and Absolute Bliss.

So ends the fourteenth discourse, titled "The Yoga of the Division of the Three Guṇas," in the Upanishads of the glorious Bhagavad Gita, the science of the Eternal, the scripture of Yoga, the dialogue between Sri Krishna and Arjuna.
