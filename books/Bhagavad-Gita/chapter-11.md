# Chapter 11 – Viśvarūpa-Darśana Yoga (The Yoga of the Cosmic Vision)

*Yoga of the Cosmic Vision*

### 11.1

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
मदनुग्रहाय परमं गुह्यमध्यात्मसंज्ञितम् ।  
यत्त्वयोक्तं वचस्तेन मोहोऽयं विगतो मम ॥ ११-१॥

*arjuna uvāca  
madanugrahāya paramaṃ guhyamadhyātmasaṃjñitam  
yattvayoktaṃ vacastena moho'yaṃ vigato mama 11-1*

Arjuna said: Out of compassion for me You have spoken this teaching, the highest secret about the Self, and hearing it my delusion has left me.

### 11.2

भवाप्ययौ हि भूतानां श्रुतौ विस्तरशो मया ।  
त्वत्तः कमलपत्राक्ष माहात्म्यमपि चाव्ययम् ॥ ११-२॥

*bhavāpyayau hi bhūtānāṃ śrutau vistaraśo mayā  
tvattaḥ kamalapatrākṣa māhātmyamapi cāvyayam 11-2*

O Lotus-eyed Krishna, I have heard from You in full how beings come into existence and how they pass away, and I have heard of Your greatness that never runs out.

### 11.3

एवमेतद्यथात्थ त्वमात्मानं परमेश्वर ।  
द्रष्टुमिच्छामि ते रूपमैश्वरं पुरुषोत्तम ॥ ११-३॥

*evametadyathāttha tvamātmānaṃ parameśvara  
draṣṭumicchāmi te rūpamaiśvaraṃ puruṣottama 11-3*

O Supreme Lord, You have described Yourself just so, and now, O Purushottama, I want to see Your divine form with my own eyes.

### 11.4

मन्यसे यदि तच्छक्यं मया द्रष्टुमिति प्रभो ।  
योगेश्वर ततो मे त्वं दर्शयात्मानमव्ययम् ॥ ११-४॥

*manyase yadi tacchakyaṃ mayā draṣṭumiti prabho  
yogeśvara tato me tvaṃ darśayātmānamavyayam 11-4*

O Lord, if You judge that I am able to look upon it, then, O Master of Yogas, please reveal to me Your imperishable Self in its own form.

### 11.5

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
पश्य मे पार्थ रूपाणि शतशोऽथ सहस्रशः ।  
नानाविधानि दिव्यानि नानावर्णाकृतीनि च ॥ ११-५॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
paśya me pārtha rūpāṇi śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ  
nānāvidhāni divyāni nānāvarṇākṛtīni ca 11-5*

The Blessed Lord said: Look, O Partha, at My forms, hundreds and thousands of them, divine, of many kinds, in every colour and shape.

### 11.6

पश्यादित्यान्वसून्रुद्रानश्विनौ मरुतस्तथा ।  
बहून्यदृष्टपूर्वाणि पश्याश्चर्याणि भारत ॥ ११-६॥

*paśyādityānvasūnrudrānaśvinau marutastathā  
bahūnyadṛṣṭapūrvāṇi paśyāścaryāṇi bhārata 11-6*

Look at the Adityas, the Vasus, the Rudras, the two Ashwins, and the Maruts. Look, O Bharata, at many marvels that no one has ever seen before.

### 11.7

इहैकस्थं जगत्कृत्स्नं पश्याद्य सचराचरम् ।  
मम देहे गुडाकेश यच्चान्यद् द्रष्टुमिच्छसि ॥ ११-७॥

*ihaikasthaṃ jagatkṛtsnaṃ paśyādya sacarācaram  
mama dehe guḍākeśa yaccānyad draṣṭumicchasi 11-7*

O Gudakesha, see here in this one body of Mine the entire universe gathered together, everything that moves and everything that stands still, along with anything else you wish to see.

### 11.8

न तु मां शक्यसे द्रष्टुमनेनैव स्वचक्षुषा ।  
दिव्यं ददामि ते चक्षुः पश्य मे योगमैश्वरम् ॥ ११-८॥

*na tu māṃ śakyase draṣṭumanenaiva svacakṣuṣā  
divyaṃ dadāmi te cakṣuḥ paśya me yogamaiśvaram 11-8*

But you cannot see Me with the eyes you have now. I grant you divine sight; look upon My sovereign Yoga.

### 11.9

सञ्जय उवाच ।  
एवमुक्त्वा ततो राजन्महायोगेश्वरो हरिः ।  
दर्शयामास पार्थाय परमं रूपमैश्वरम् ॥ ११-९॥

*sañjaya uvāca  
evamuktvā tato rājanmahāyogeśvaro hariḥ  
darśayāmāsa pārthāya paramaṃ rūpamaiśvaram 11-9*

Sanjaya said: Having said this, O King, Hari, the great Lord of Yoga, revealed to Partha His supreme form as the Lord of all.

### 11.10

अनेकवक्त्रनयनमनेकाद्भुतदर्शनम् ।  
अनेकदिव्याभरणं दिव्यानेकोद्यतायुधम् ॥ ११-१०॥

*anekavaktranayanamanekādbhutadarśanam  
anekadivyābharaṇaṃ divyānekodyatāyudham 11-10*

It had countless mouths and eyes, countless astonishing sights, countless divine ornaments, and countless divine weapons raised aloft.

### 11.11

दिव्यमाल्याम्बरधरं दिव्यगन्धानुलेपनम् ।  
सर्वाश्चर्यमयं देवमनन्तं विश्वतोमुखम् ॥ ११-११॥

*divyamālyāmbaradharaṃ divyagandhānulepanam  
sarvāścaryamayaṃ devamanantaṃ viśvatomukham 11-11*

It wore heavenly garlands and robes and was anointed with heavenly perfumes; it was wholly marvellous, radiant, without limit, and faced in every direction.

### 11.12

दिवि सूर्यसहस्रस्य भवेद्युगपदुत्थिता ।  
यदि भाः सदृशी सा स्याद्भासस्तस्य महात्मनः ॥ ११-१२॥

*divi sūryasahasrasya bhavedyugapadutthitā  
yadi bhāḥ sadṛśī sā syādbhāsastasya mahātmanaḥ 11-12*

If a thousand suns were to blaze up together in the sky at the same instant, their brilliance might resemble the brilliance of that Mighty Being.

### 11.13

तत्रैकस्थं जगत्कृत्स्नं प्रविभक्तमनेकधा ।  
अपश्यद्देवदेवस्य शरीरे पाण्डवस्तदा ॥ ११-१३॥

*tatraikasthaṃ jagatkṛtsnaṃ pravibhaktamanekadhā  
apaśyaddevadevasya śarīre pāṇḍavastadā 11-13*

There, in the body of the God of gods, the son of Pandu then saw the whole universe, with all its countless divisions, gathered into one.

### 11.14

ततः स विस्मयाविष्टो हृष्टरोमा धनञ्जयः ।  
प्रणम्य शिरसा देवं कृताञ्जलिरभाषत ॥ ११-१४॥

*tataḥ sa vismayāviṣṭo hṛṣṭaromā dhanañjayaḥ  
praṇamya śirasā devaṃ kṛtāñjalirabhāṣata 11-14*

Then Dhananjaya, struck with awe, his hair bristling, bowed his head before the God and, pressing his palms together, began to speak.

### 11.15

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
पश्यामि देवांस्तव देव देहे  
सर्वांस्तथा भूतविशेषसङ्घान् ।  
ब्रह्माणमीशं कमलासनस्थ-  
मृषींश्च सर्वानुरगांश्च दिव्यान् ॥ ११-१५॥

*arjuna uvāca  
paśyāmi devāṃstava deva dehe  
sarvāṃstathā bhūtaviśeṣasaṅghān  
brahmāṇamīśaṃ kamalāsanastha-  
mṛṣīṃśca sarvānuragāṃśca divyān 11-15*

Arjuna said: O God, in Your body I see all the gods and also crowds of beings of every kind: Brahma the Lord of creation seated on the lotus, all the rishis, and the heavenly serpents.

### 11.16

अनेकबाहूदरवक्त्रनेत्रं  
पश्यामि त्वां सर्वतोऽनन्तरूपम् ।  
नान्तं न मध्यं न पुनस्तवादिं  
पश्यामि विश्वेश्वर विश्वरूप ॥ ११-१६॥

*anekabāhūdaravaktranetraṃ  
paśyāmi tvāṃ sarvato'nantarūpam  
nāntaṃ na madhyaṃ na punastavādiṃ  
paśyāmi viśveśvara viśvarūpa 11-16*

I see You everywhere in a form without limit, with arms, bellies, mouths, and eyes beyond number. I can find in You no end, no middle, and no beginning, O Lord of the universe, O Cosmic Form.

### 11.17

किरीटिनं गदिनं चक्रिणं च  
तेजोराशिं सर्वतो दीप्तिमन्तम् ।  
पश्यामि त्वां दुर्निरीक्ष्यं समन्ताद्  
दीप्तानलार्कद्युतिमप्रमेयम् ॥ ११-१७॥

*kirīṭinaṃ gadinaṃ cakriṇaṃ ca  
tejorāśiṃ sarvato dīptimantam  
paśyāmi tvāṃ durnirīkṣyaṃ samantād  
dīptānalārkadyutimaprameyam 11-17*

I see You wearing a crown and carrying a club and a discus, a mass of light shining in all directions, so dazzling that it is hard to look at, flaring on every side like blazing fire and the sun, and beyond comprehension.

### 11.18

त्वमक्षरं परमं वेदितव्यं  
त्वमस्य विश्वस्य परं निधानम् ।  
त्वमव्ययः शाश्वतधर्मगोप्ता  
सनातनस्त्वं पुरुषो मतो मे ॥ ११-१८॥

*tvamakṣaraṃ paramaṃ veditavyaṃ  
tvamasya viśvasya paraṃ nidhānam  
tvamavyayaḥ śāśvatadharmagoptā  
sanātanastvaṃ puruṣo mato me 11-18*

You are the Imperishable, the Supreme that must be known. You are the great storehouse of this universe. You are the undying guardian of the eternal dharma. As I see it, You are the ancient Purusha.

### 11.19

अनादिमध्यान्तम् अनन्तवीर्यम्  
अनन्तबाहुं शशिसूर्यनेत्रम् ।  
पश्यामि त्वां दीप्तहुताशवक्त्रं  
स्वतेजसा विश्वमिदं तपन्तम् ॥ ११-१९॥

*anādimadhyāntam anantavīryam  
anantabāhuṃ śaśisūryanetram  
paśyāmi tvāṃ dīptahutāśavaktraṃ  
svatejasā viśvamidaṃ tapantam 11-19*

I see You with no beginning, middle, or end, with boundless power and countless arms; the sun and moon are Your eyes, blazing fire is Your mouth, and Your radiance scorches the whole universe.

### 11.20

द्यावापृथिव्योरिदमन्तरं हि  
व्याप्तं त्वयैकेन दिशश्च सर्वाः ।  
दृष्ट्वाद्भुतं रूपमुग्रं तवेदं  
लोकत्रयं प्रव्यथितं महात्मन् ॥ ११-२०॥

*dyāvāpṛthivyoridamantaraṃ hi  
vyāptaṃ tvayaikena diśaśca sarvāḥ  
dṛṣṭvādbhutaṃ rūpamugraṃ tavedaṃ  
lokatrayaṃ pravyathitaṃ mahātman 11-20*

You alone fill the space between earth and heaven and every direction of the sky. Seeing this wondrous and terrifying form of Yours, O great-souled One, the three worlds shake with fear.

### 11.21

अमी हि त्वां सुरसङ्घा विशन्ति  
केचिद्भीताः प्राञ्जलयो गृणन्ति ।  
स्वस्तीत्युक्त्वा महर्षिसिद्धसङ्घाः  
स्तुवन्ति त्वां स्तुतिभिः पुष्कलाभिः ॥ ११-२१॥

*amī hi tvāṃ surasaṅghā viśanti  
kecidbhītāḥ prāñjalayo gṛṇanti  
svastītyuktvā maharṣisiddhasaṅghāḥ  
stuvanti tvāṃ stutibhiḥ puṣkalābhiḥ 11-21*

These crowds of devas are indeed entering into You; some, afraid, praise You with palms joined, while groups of great rishis and siddhas cry 'May all be well' and glorify You with lofty hymns.

### 11.22

रुद्रादित्या वसवो ये च साध्या  
विश्वेऽश्विनौ मरुतश्चोष्मपाश्च ।  
गन्धर्वयक्षासुरसिद्धसङ्घा  
वीक्षन्ते त्वां विस्मिताश्चैव सर्वे ॥ ११-२२॥

*rudrādityā vasavo ye ca sādhyā  
viśve'śvinau marutaścoṣmapāśca  
gandharvayakṣā surasiddhasaṅghā  
vīkṣante tvāṃ vismitāścaiva sarve 11-22*

The Rudras, the Adityas, the Vasus, the Sadhyas, the Vishwe-devas, the two Ashwins, the Maruts, the Ushmapas, and the crowds of Gandharvas, Yakshas, Asuras, and Siddhas all gaze at You in utter amazement.

### 11.23

रूपं महत्ते बहुवक्त्रनेत्रं  
महाबाहो बहुबाहूरुपादम् ।  
बहूदरं बहुदंष्ट्राकरालं  
दृष्ट्वा लोकाः प्रव्यथितास्तथाहम् ॥ ११-२३॥

*rūpaṃ mahatte bahuvaktranetraṃ  
mahābāho bahubāhūrupādam  
bahūdaraṃ bahudaṃṣṭrākarālaṃ  
dṛṣṭvā lokāḥ pravyathitāstathāham 11-23*

O Mighty-armed One, seeing Your immense form with its many mouths and eyes, its many arms, thighs, and feet, its many bellies, and its many terrible tusks, the worlds are stricken with terror, and so am I.

### 11.24

नभःस्पृशं दीप्तमनेकवर्णं  
व्यात्ताननं दीप्तविशालनेत्रम् ।  
दृष्ट्वा हि त्वां प्रव्यथितान्तरात्मा  
धृतिं न विन्दामि शमं च विष्णो ॥ ११-२४॥

*nabhaḥspṛśaṃ dīptamanekavarṇaṃ  
vyāttānanaṃ dīptaviśālanetram  
dṛṣṭvā hi tvāṃ pravyathitāntarātmā  
dhṛtiṃ na vindāmi śamaṃ ca viṣṇo 11-24*

When I see You reaching up to the sky, blazing in many colours, with mouths gaping and huge flaming eyes, my heart is seized with fear, and I can find neither courage nor calm, O Vishnu.

### 11.25

दंष्ट्राकरालानि च ते मुखानि  
दृष्ट्वैव कालानलसन्निभानि ।  
दिशो न जाने न लभे च शर्म  
प्रसीद देवेश जगन्निवास ॥ ११-२५॥

*daṃṣṭrākarālāni ca te mukhāni  
dṛṣṭvaiva kālānalasannibhāni  
diśo na jāne na labhe ca śarma  
prasīda deveśa jagannivāsa 11-25*

Seeing Your mouths with their fearsome tusks blazing like the fires of pralaya, I lose all sense of direction and find no peace. Be merciful, O Lord of the devas, O Refuge of the universe.

### 11.26

अमी च त्वां धृतराष्ट्रस्य पुत्राः  
सर्वे सहैवा वनिपालसङ्घैः ।  
भीष्मो द्रोणः सूतपुत्रस्तथासौ  
सहास्मदीयैरपि योधमुख्यैः ॥ ११-२६॥

*amī ca tvāṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putrāḥ  
sarve sahaivā vanipālasaṅghaiḥ  
bhīṣmo droṇaḥ sūtaputrastathāsau  
sahāsmadīyairapi yodhamukhyaiḥ 11-26*

All the sons of Dhritarashtra together with the crowds of earthly kings, Bhishma, Drona, and Karna the charioteer's son, along with the chief warriors on our own side,

### 11.27

वक्त्राणि ते त्वरमाणा विशन्ति  
दंष्ट्राकरालानि भयानकानि ।  
केचिद्विलग्ना दशनान्तरेषु  
सन्दृश्यन्ते चूर्णितैरुत्तमाङ्गैः ॥ ११-२७॥

*vaktrāṇi te tvaramāṇā viśanti  
daṃṣṭrākarālāni bhayānakāni  
kecidvilagnā daśanāntareṣu  
sandṛśyante cūrṇitairuttamāṅgaiḥ 11-27*

are rushing headlong into Your mouths with their dreadful teeth, frightful to see. Some can be seen caught between the teeth, their heads ground to pulp.

### 11.28

यथा नदीनां बहवोऽम्बुवेगाः  
समुद्रमेवाभिमुखा द्रवन्ति ।  
तथा तवामी नरलोकवीरा  
विशन्ति वक्त्राण्यभिविज्वलन्ति ॥ ११-२८॥

*yathā nadīnāṃ bahavo'mbuvegāḥ  
samudramevābhimukhā dravanti  
tathā tavāmī naralokavīrā  
viśanti vaktrāṇyabhivijvalanti 11-28*

Just as the many streams of rivers run toward the ocean, so these heroes of the human world pour into Your blazing mouths.

### 11.29

यथा प्रदीप्तं ज्वलनं पतङ्गा  
विशन्ति नाशाय समृद्धवेगाः ।  
तथैव नाशाय विशन्ति लोका-  
स्तवापि वक्त्राणि समृद्धवेगाः ॥ ११-२९॥

*yathā pradīptaṃ jvalanaṃ pataṅgā  
viśanti nāśāya samṛddhavegāḥ  
tathaiva nāśāya viśanti lokā-  
stavāpi vaktrāṇi samṛddhavegāḥ 11-29*

Arjuna says: Just as moths dart into a roaring fire and perish, so these beings race headlong into Your mouths to their own destruction.

### 11.30

लेलिह्यसे ग्रसमानः समन्ताल्-  
लोकान्समग्रान्वदनैर्ज्वलद्भिः ।  
तेजोभिरापूर्य जगत्समग्रं  
भासस्तवोग्राः प्रतपन्ति विष्णो ॥ ११-३०॥

*lelihyase grasamānaḥ samantāl-  
lokānsamagrān vadanairjvaladbhiḥ  
tejobhirāpūrya jagatsamagraṃ  
bhāsastavogrāḥ pratapanti viṣṇo 11-30*

With Your flaming mouths You swallow all the worlds on every side, licking them up as if savouring them. Your fierce rays fill the whole universe with light and scorch it, O Vishnu.

### 11.31

आख्याहि मे को भवानुग्ररूपो  
नमोऽस्तु ते देववर प्रसीद ।  
विज्ञातुमिच्छामि भवन्तमाद्यं  
न हि प्रजानामि तव प्रवृत्तिम् ॥ ११-३१॥

*ākhyāhi me ko bhavānugrarūpo  
namo'stu te devavara prasīda  
vijñātumicchāmi bhavantamādyaṃ  
na hi prajānāmi tava pravṛttim 11-31*

Tell me who You are, You of such a fierce form. I bow to You, O Supreme God; be merciful. I want to understand You, the Primal Being, for I do not grasp what You intend.

### 11.32

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
कालोऽस्मि लोकक्षयकृत्प्रवृद्धो  
लोकान्समाहर्तुमिह प्रवृत्तः ।  
ऋतेऽपि त्वां न भविष्यन्ति सर्वे  
येऽवस्थिताः प्रत्यनीकेषु योधाः ॥ ११-३२॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
kālo'smi lokakṣayakṛtpravṛddho  
lokānsamāhartumiha pravṛttaḥ  
ṛte'pi tvāṃ na bhaviṣyanti sarve  
ye'vasthitāḥ pratyanīkeṣu yodhāḥ 11-32*

The Blessed Lord said: I am Time, the mighty destroyer of worlds, and I have now set about destroying these worlds. Even if you do nothing, not one of the warriors drawn up in the opposing armies will survive.

### 11.33

तस्मात्त्वमुत्तिष्ठ यशो लभस्व  
जित्वा शत्रून् भुङ्क्ष्व राज्यं समृद्धम् ।  
मयैवैते निहताः पूर्वमेव  
निमित्तमात्रं भव सव्यसाचिन् ॥ ११-३३॥

*tasmāttvamuttiṣṭha yaśo labhasva  
jitvā śatrūn bhuṅkṣva rājyaṃ samṛddham  
mayaivaite nihatāḥ pūrvameva  
nimittamātraṃ bhava savyasācin 11-33*

So rise and win glory. Defeat your enemies and enjoy a prosperous kingdom. I Myself have already killed them; you, O left-handed archer, are only to be the instrument.

### 11.34

द्रोणं च भीष्मं च जयद्रथं च  
कर्णं तथान्यानपि योधवीरान् ।  
मया हतांस्त्वं जहि मा व्यथिष्ठा  
युध्यस्व जेतासि रणे सपत्नान् ॥ ११-३४॥

*droṇaṃ ca bhīṣmaṃ ca jayadrathaṃ ca  
karṇaṃ tathānyānapi yodhavīrān  
mayā hatāṃstvaṃ jahi mā vyathiṣṭhā  
yudhyasva jetāsi raṇe sapatnān 11-34*

Drona, Bhishma, Jayadratha, Karna, and the other brave fighters have already been killed by Me. Strike them down; do not be troubled by fear. Fight, and you will defeat your enemies in battle.

### 11.35

सञ्जय उवाच ।  
एतच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं केशवस्य  
कृताञ्जलिर्वेपमानः किरीटी ।  
नमस्कृत्वा भूय एवाह कृष्णं  
सगद्गदं भीतभीतः प्रणम्य ॥ ११-३५॥

*sañjaya uvāca  
etacchrutvā vacanaṃ keśavasya  
kṛtāñjalirvepamānaḥ kirīṭī  
namaskṛtvā bhūya evāha kṛṣṇaṃ  
sagadgadaṃ bhītabhītaḥ praṇamya 11-35*

Sanjaya said: On hearing these words of Keshava (Krishna), the crowned one (Arjuna), trembling and bowing low, joined his palms and, overcome with fear, spoke to Krishna again in a faltering voice.

### 11.36

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
स्थाने हृषीकेश तव प्रकीर्त्या  
जगत्प्रहृष्यत्यनुरज्यते च ।  
रक्षांसि भीतानि दिशो द्रवन्ति  
सर्वे नमस्यन्ति च सिद्धसङ्घाः ॥ ११-३६॥

*arjuna uvāca  
sthāne hṛṣīkeśa tava prakīrtyā  
jagatprahṛṣyat yanurajyate ca  
rakṣāṃsi bhītāni diśo dravanti  
sarve namasyanti ca siddhasaṅghāḥ 11-36*

Arjuna said: It is only right, O Hrishikesha (Krishna), that the world rejoices and delights in praising You, that the rakshasas scatter in fear to every direction, and that all the hosts of siddhas bow before You.

### 11.37

कस्माच्च ते न नमेरन्महात्मन्  
गरीयसे ब्रह्मणोऽप्यादिकर्त्रे ।  
अनन्त देवेश जगन्निवास  
त्वमक्षरं सदसत्तत्परं यत् ॥ ११-३७॥

*kasmācca te na nameranmahātman  
garīyase brahmaṇo'pyādikartre  
ananta deveśa jagannivāsa  
tvamakṣaraṃ sadasattatparaṃ yat 11-37*

And why would they not bow to You, O great-souled One, who are greater than all, the first cause even of Brahma? O Infinite Being, O Lord of lords, O Refuge of the universe, You are the Imperishable, that which lies beyond both the manifest and the unmanifest.

### 11.38

त्वमादिदेवः पुरुषः पुराण-  
स्त्वमस्य विश्वस्य परं निधानम् ।  
वेत्तासि वेद्यं च परं च धाम  
त्वया ततं विश्वमनन्तरूप ॥ ११-३८॥

*tvamādidevaḥ puruṣaḥ purāṇa-  
stvamasya viśvasya paraṃ nidhānam  
vettāsi vedyaṃ ca paraṃ ca dhāma  
tvayā tataṃ viśvamanantarūpa 11-38*

You are the first God, the ancient Purusha, the ultimate shelter of this universe. You are the knower, what is to be known, and the highest goal. O Being of endless forms, You pervade the whole universe.

### 11.39

वायुर्यमोऽग्निर्वरुणः शशाङ्कः  
प्रजापतिस्त्वं प्रपितामहश्च ।  
नमो नमस्तेऽस्तु सहस्रकृत्वः  
पुनश्च भूयोऽपि नमो नमस्ते ॥ ११-३९॥

*vāyuryamo'gnirvaruṇaḥ śaśāṅkaḥ  
prajāpatistvaṃ prapitāmahaśca  
namo namaste'stu sahasrakṛtvaḥ  
punaśca bhūyo'pi namo namaste 11-39*

You are Vayu, Yama, Agni, Varuna, the Moon, Prajapati, and the great-grandfather of all. Homage to You, homage a thousand times over, and homage to You yet again!

### 11.40

नमः पुरस्तादथ पृष्ठतस्ते  
नमोऽस्तु ते सर्वत एव सर्व ।  
अनन्तवीर्यामितविक्रमस्त्वं  
सर्वं समाप्नोषि ततोऽसि सर्वः ॥ ११-४०॥

*namaḥ purastādatha pṛṣṭhataste  
namo'stu te sarvata eva sarva  
anantavīryāmitavikramastvaṃ  
sarvaṃ samāpnoṣi tato'si sarvaḥ 11-40*

Homage to You from in front and from behind! Homage to You from every side, O All! Your power is without end and Your might without measure; You fill everything, and so You are everything.

### 11.41

सखेति मत्वा प्रसभं यदुक्तं  
हे कृष्ण हे यादव हे सखेति ।  
अजानता महिमानं तवेदं  
मया प्रमादात्प्रणयेन वापि ॥ ११-४१॥

*sakheti matvā prasabhaṃ yaduktaṃ  
he kṛṣṇa he yādava he sakheti  
ajānatā mahimānaṃ tavedaṃ  
mayā pramādātpraṇayena vāpi 11-41*

Whatever I said thoughtlessly, out of carelessness or affection, calling You 'Krishna,' 'Yadava,' or 'friend,' treating You as a mere companion and ignorant of this greatness of Yours,

### 11.42

यच्चावहासार्थमसत्कृतोऽसि  
विहारशय्यासनभोजनेषु ।  
एकोऽथवाप्यच्युत तत्समक्षं  
तत्क्षामये त्वामहमप्रमेयम् ॥ ११-४२॥

*yaccāvahāsārthamasatkṛto'si  
vihāraśayyāsanabhojaneṣu  
eko'thavāpyacyuta tatsamakṣaṃ  
tatkṣāmaye tvāmahamaprameyam 11-42*

and however I may have slighted You in jest, while playing, resting, sitting, or eating, whether alone with You, O Achyuta, or in front of others, for all of that, O Immeasurable One, I beg Your forgiveness.

### 11.43

पितासि लोकस्य चराचरस्य  
त्वमस्य पूज्यश्च गुरुर्गरीयान् ।  
न त्वत्समोऽस्त्यभ्यधिकः कुतोऽन्यो  
लोकत्रयेऽप्यप्रतिमप्रभाव ॥ ११-४३॥

*pitāsi lokasya carācarasya  
tvamasya pūjyaśca gururgarīyān  
na tvatsamo'styabhyadhikaḥ kuto'nyo  
lokatraye'pyapratimaprabhāva 11-43*

You are the Father of this world of moving and unmoving things, the one this world should worship, the greatest Guru. No one is Your equal; how then could anyone in the three worlds surpass You, O Being of unmatched power?

### 11.44

तस्मात्प्रणम्य प्रणिधाय कायं  
प्रसादये त्वामहमीशमीड्यम् ।  
पितेव पुत्रस्य सखेव सख्युः  
प्रियः प्रियायार्हसि देव सोढुम् ॥ ११-४४॥

*tasmātpraṇamya praṇidhāya kāyaṃ  
prasādaye tvāmahamīśamīḍyam  
piteva putrasya sakheva sakhyuḥ  
priyaḥ priyāyārhasi deva soḍhum 11-44*

So I bow down and lay my body at Your feet, asking Your pardon, adorable Lord. As a father pardons his son, a friend his friend, and a lover his beloved, so, O Deva, please pardon me.

### 11.45

अदृष्टपूर्वं हृषितोऽस्मि दृष्ट्वा  
भयेन च प्रव्यथितं मनो मे ।  
तदेव मे दर्शय देव रूपं  
प्रसीद देवेश जगन्निवास ॥ ११-४५॥

*adṛṣṭapūrvaṃ hṛṣito'smi dṛṣṭvā  
bhayena ca pravyathitaṃ mano me  
tadeva me darśaya deva rūpaṃ  
prasīda deveśa jagannivāsa 11-45*

I am overjoyed to have seen what no one has seen before, and yet fear unsettles my mind. Show me, O God, only Your earlier form; be merciful, O God of gods, O Refuge of the universe.

### 11.46

किरीटिनं गदिनं चक्रहस्तं  
इच्छामि त्वां द्रष्टुमहं तथैव ।  
तेनैव रूपेण चतुर्भुजेन  
सहस्रबाहो भव विश्वमूर्ते ॥ ११-४६॥

*kirīṭinaṃ gadinaṃ cakrahastaṃ  
icchāmi tvāṃ draṣṭumahaṃ tathaiva  
tenaiva rūpeṇa caturbhujena  
sahasrabāho bhava viśvamūrte 11-46*

I long to see You as You were before, wearing the crown and holding the mace and the discus, in that earlier four-armed form alone, O Thousand-armed One, O Universal Form.

### 11.47

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
मया प्रसन्नेन तवार्जुनेदं  
रूपं परं दर्शितमात्मयोगात् ।  
तेजोमयं विश्वमनन्तमाद्यं  
यन्मे त्वदन्येन न दृष्टपूर्वम् ॥ ११-४७॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
mayā prasannena tavārjunedaṃ  
rūpaṃ paraṃ darśitamātmayogāt  
tejomayaṃ viśvamanantamādyaṃ  
yanme tvadanyena na dṛṣṭapūrvam 11-47*

The Blessed Lord said: Out of grace toward you, O Arjuna, and by My own power of Yoga, I have revealed this supreme form, radiant, primal, and infinite, this universal form of Mine that no one but you has ever seen.

### 11.48

न वेदयज्ञाध्ययनैर्न दानै-  
र्न च क्रियाभिर्न तपोभिरुग्रैः ।  
एवंरूपः शक्य अहं नृलोके  
द्रष्टुं त्वदन्येन कुरुप्रवीर ॥ ११-४८॥

*na vedayajñādhyayanairna dānai-  
rna ca kriyābhirna tapobhirugraiḥ  
evaṃrūpaḥ śakya ahaṃ nṛloke  
draṣṭuṃ tvadanyena kurupravīra 11-48*

Not by studying the Vedas, not by sacrifices, not by rites, and not by harsh austerities can anyone in the world of men see Me in this form, except you alone, O great hero of the Kurus.

### 11.49

मा ते व्यथा मा च विमूढभावो  
दृष्ट्वा रूपं घोरमीदृङ्ममेदम् ।  
व्यपेतभीः प्रीतमनाः पुनस्त्वं  
तदेव मे रूपमिदं प्रपश्य ॥ ११-४९॥

*mā te vyathā mā ca vimūḍhabhāvo  
dṛṣṭvā rūpaṃ ghoramīdṛṅmamedam  
vyapetabhīḥ prītamanāḥ punastvaṃ  
tadeva me rūpamidaṃ prapaśya 11-49*

Do not be frightened or confused at the sight of this terrible form of Mine. Let your fear go, be glad at heart, and look once more on this other form of Mine.

### 11.50

सञ्जय उवाच ।  
इत्यर्जुनं वासुदेवस्तथोक्त्वा  
स्वकं रूपं दर्शयामास भूयः ।  
आश्वासयामास च भीतमेनं  
भूत्वा पुनः सौम्यवपुर्महात्मा ॥ ११-५०॥

*sañjaya uvāca  
ityarjunaṃ vāsudevastathoktvā  
svakaṃ rūpaṃ darśayāmāsa bhūyaḥ  
āśvāsayāmāsa ca bhītamenaṃ  
bhūtvā punaḥ saumyavapurmahātmā 11-50*

Sanjaya said: Having spoken this way to Arjuna, Vaasudeva showed His own form once more, and the great-souled One, taking on His gentle appearance, comforted the terrified Arjuna.

### 11.51

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
दृष्ट्वेदं मानुषं रूपं तव सौम्यं जनार्दन ।  
इदानीमस्मि संवृत्तः सचेताः प्रकृतिं गतः ॥ ११-५१॥

*arjuna uvāca  
dṛṣṭvedaṃ mānuṣaṃ rūpaṃ tava saumyaṃ janārdana  
idānīmasmi saṃvṛttaḥ sacetāḥ prakṛtiṃ gataḥ 11-51*

Arjuna said: O Janardana, now that I see this gentle human form of Yours, I am calm again and have come back to myself.

### 11.52

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
सुदुर्दर्शमिदं रूपं दृष्टवानसि यन्मम ।  
देवा अप्यस्य रूपस्य नित्यं दर्शनकाङ्क्षिणः ॥ ११-५२॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
sudurdarśamidaṃ rūpaṃ dṛṣṭavānasi yanmama  
devā apyasya rūpasya nityaṃ darśanakāṅkṣiṇaḥ 11-52*

The Blessed Lord said: This form of Mine that you have just seen is very hard to see indeed. Even the gods constantly yearn to behold it.

### 11.53

नाहं वेदैर्न तपसा न दानेन न चेज्यया ।  
शक्य एवंविधो द्रष्टुं दृष्टवानसि मां यथा ॥ ११-५३॥

*nāhaṃ vedairna tapasā na dānena na cejyayā  
śakya evaṃvidho draṣṭuṃ dṛṣṭavānasi māṃ yathā 11-53*

Not through the Vedas, not through austerity, not through gifts, and not through sacrifices can I be seen in this form as you have seen Me (in your present state of mind).

### 11.54

भक्त्या त्वनन्यया शक्य अहमेवंविधोऽर्जुन ।  
ज्ञातुं द्रष्टुं च तत्त्वेन प्रवेष्टुं च परन्तप ॥ ११-५४॥

*bhaktyā tvananyayā śakya ahamevaṃvidho'rjuna  
jñātuṃ draṣṭuṃ ca tattvena praveṣṭuṃ ca parantapa 11-54*

But through devotion directed to Me alone, O Parantapa (scorcher of your enemies), I can in this form be truly known, seen, and entered into.

### 11.55

मत्कर्मकृन्मत्परमो मद्भक्तः सङ्गवर्जितः ।  
निर्वैरः सर्वभूतेषु यः स मामेति पाण्डव ॥ ११-५५॥   
  
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु  
ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे  
विश्वरूपदर्शनयोगो नामैकादशोऽध्यायः ॥ ११॥

*matkarmakṛnmatparamo madbhaktaḥ saṅgavarjitaḥ  
nirvairaḥ sarvabhūteṣu yaḥ sa māmeti pāṇḍava  11-55  
  
oṃ tatsaditi śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu  
brahmavidyāyāṃ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṃvāde  
viśvarūpadarśanayogo nāmaikādaśo'dhyāyaḥ 11*

Whoever performs all actions for My sake, holds Me as the highest, is devoted to Me, is free of attachment, and bears no hostility toward any creature, that person comes to Me, O Pandava.

So ends the eleventh discourse, called "The Yoga of the Cosmic Vision," in the Upanishads of the glorious Bhagavad Gita, the knowledge of the Eternal, the scripture of Yoga, the dialogue between Sri Krishna and Arjuna.
