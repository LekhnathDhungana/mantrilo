# Chapter 4 – Jñāna-Karma-Sannyāsa Yoga (The Yoga of Knowledge and Renunciation of Action)

*Yoga of Renunciation of Action in Knowledge*

### 4.1

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
इमं विवस्वते योगं प्रोक्तवानहमव्ययम् ।  
विवस्वान्मनवे प्राह मनुरिक्ष्वाकवेऽब्रवीत् ॥ ४-१॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
imaṃ vivasvate yogaṃ proktavānahamavyayam  
vivasvānmanave prāha manurikṣvākave'bravīt 4-1*

The Blessed Lord said: I passed this imperishable Yoga on to Vivasvan. Vivasvan gave it to Manu, and Manu gave it to Ikshvaku.

### 4.2

एवं परम्पराप्राप्तमिमं राजर्षयो विदुः ।  
स कालेनेह महता योगो नष्टः परन्तप ॥ ४-२॥

*evaṃ paramparāprāptamimaṃ rājarṣayo viduḥ  
sa kāleneha mahatā yogo naṣṭaḥ parantapa 4-2*

The royal sages received this knowledge, handed down from one to the next in an unbroken line. But over a long stretch of time, O Parantapa (burner of the foes), this Yoga has been lost in the world.

### 4.3

स एवायं मया तेऽद्य योगः प्रोक्तः पुरातनः ।  
भक्तोऽसि मे सखा चेति रहस्यं ह्येतदुत्तमम् ॥ ४-३॥

*sa evāyaṃ mayā te'dya yogaḥ proktaḥ purātanaḥ  
bhakto'si me sakhā ceti rahasyaṃ hyetaduttamam 4-3*

Today I have taught you that very same ancient Yoga, because you are My devotee and My friend. It is the highest secret.

### 4.4

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
अपरं भवतो जन्म परं जन्म विवस्वतः ।  
कथमेतद्विजानीयां त्वमादौ प्रोक्तवानिति ॥ ४-४॥

*arjuna uvāca  
aparaṃ bhavato janma paraṃ janma vivasvataḥ  
kathametadvijānīyāṃ tvamādau proktavāniti 4-4*

Arjuna said: You were born recently, while Vivaswan (the Sun) was born long before. How, then, am I to understand that You taught this Yoga at the beginning?

### 4.5

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
बहूनि मे व्यतीतानि जन्मानि तव चार्जुन ।  
तान्यहं वेद सर्वाणि न त्वं वेत्थ परन्तप ॥ ४-५॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
bahūni me vyatītāni janmāni tava cārjuna  
tānyahaṃ veda sarvāṇi na tvaṃ vettha parantapa 4-5*

The Blessed Lord said: I have gone through many births, Arjuna, and so have you. I remember them all; you do not, O Parantapa (scorcher of foes).

### 4.6

अजोऽपि सन्नव्ययात्मा भूतानामीश्वरोऽपि सन् ।  
प्रकृतिं स्वामधिष्ठाय सम्भवाम्यात्ममायया ॥ ४-६॥

*ajo'pi sannavyayātmā bhūtānāmīśvaro'pi san  
prakṛtiṃ svāmadhiṣṭhāya sambhavāmyātmamāyayā 4-6*

Although I am unborn and My nature never perishes, and although I am the Lord of all beings, I still take birth through My own Maya, keeping My own Nature under My command.

### 4.7

यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत ।  
अभ्युत्थानमधर्मस्य तदात्मानं सृजाम्यहम् ॥ ४-७॥

*yadā yadā hi dharmasya glānirbhavati bhārata  
abhyutthānamadharmasya tadātmānaṃ sṛjāmyaham 4-7*

Whenever righteousness declines and unrighteousness gains ground, O Bharata, I bring Myself into manifestation.

### 4.8

परित्राणाय साधूनां विनाशाय च दुष्कृताम् ।  
धर्मसंस्थापनार्थाय सम्भवामि युगे युगे ॥ ४-८॥

*paritrāṇāya sādhūnāṃ vināśāya ca duṣkṛtām  
dharmasaṃsthāpanārthāya sambhavāmi yuge yuge 4-8*

Age after age I am born to protect the good, to destroy the wicked, and to set righteousness firmly in place.

### 4.9

जन्म कर्म च मे दिव्यमेवं यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ।  
त्यक्त्वा देहं पुनर्जन्म नैति मामेति सोऽर्जुन ॥ ४-९॥

*janma karma ca me divyamevaṃ yo vetti tattvataḥ  
tyaktvā dehaṃ punarjanma naiti māmeti so'rjuna 4-9*

Whoever truly understands My divine birth and My divine action is not born again after leaving the body. That person comes to Me, Arjuna.

### 4.10

वीतरागभयक्रोधा मन्मया मामुपाश्रिताः ।  
बहवो ज्ञानतपसा पूता मद्भावमागताः ॥ ४-१०॥

*vītarāgabhayakrodhā manmayā māmupāśritāḥ  
bahavo jñānatapasā pūtā madbhāvamāgatāḥ 4-10*

Many, released from attachment, fear, and anger, absorbed in Me and sheltering in Me, have been purified by the Fire of Knowledge and have reached My Being.

### 4.11

ये यथा मां प्रपद्यन्ते तांस्तथैव भजाम्यहम् ।  
मम वर्त्मानुवर्तन्ते मनुष्याः पार्थ सर्वशः ॥ ४-११॥

*ye yathā māṃ prapadyante tāṃstathaiva bhajāmyaham  
mama vartmānuvartante manuṣyāḥ pārtha sarvaśaḥ 4-11*

However people come to Me, that is how I reward them. In every way they walk, O son of Pritha, it is My path they are treading.

### 4.12

काङ्क्षन्तः कर्मणां सिद्धिं यजन्त इह देवताः ।  
क्षिप्रं हि मानुषे लोके सिद्धिर्भवति कर्मजा ॥ ४-१२॥

*kāṅkṣantaḥ karmaṇāṃ siddhiṃ yajanta iha devatāḥ  
kṣipraṃ hi mānuṣe loke siddhirbhavati karmajā 4-12*

Those who want their actions in this world to bear fruit offer sacrifices to the gods, because results from action come quickly in the world of objects.

### 4.13

चातुर्वर्ण्यं मया सृष्टं गुणकर्मविभागशः ।  
तस्य कर्तारमपि मां विद्ध्यकर्तारमव्ययम् ॥ ४-१३॥

*cāturvarṇyaṃ mayā sṛṣṭaṃ guṇakarmavibhāgaśaḥ  
tasya kartāramapi māṃ viddhyakartāramavyayam 4-13*

I created the four castes according to the division of guna and karma. Yet although I am their author, know Me as the one who does nothing and never changes.

### 4.14

न मां कर्माणि लिम्पन्ति न मे कर्मफले स्पृहा ।  
इति मां योऽभिजानाति कर्मभिर्न स बध्यते ॥ ४-१४॥

*na māṃ karmāṇi limpanti na me karmaphale spṛhā  
iti māṃ yo'bhijānāti karmabhirna sa badhyate 4-14*

Actions leave no stain on Me, and I have no craving for the fruits of action. Whoever knows Me in this way is not bound by what he does.

### 4.15

एवं ज्ञात्वा कृतं कर्म पूर्वैरपि मुमुक्षुभिः ।  
कुरु कर्मैव तस्मात्त्वं पूर्वैः पूर्वतरं कृतम् ॥ ४-१५॥

*evaṃ jñātvā kṛtaṃ karma pūrvairapi mumukṣubhiḥ  
kuru karmaiva tasmāttvaṃ pūrvaiḥ pūrvataraṃ kṛtam 4-15*

Knowing this, the seekers of freedom in ancient times still performed action. So you too should act, as those of old did long ago.

### 4.16

किं कर्म किमकर्मेति कवयोऽप्यत्र मोहिताः ।  
तत्ते कर्म प्रवक्ष्यामि यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात् ॥ ४-१६॥

*kiṃ karma kimakarmeti kavayo'pyatra mohitāḥ  
tatte karma pravakṣyāmi yajjñātvā mokṣyase'śubhāt 4-16*

What is action? What is inaction? Even the wise are confused about this. So I will explain to you what action is (the nature of action and inaction), and by understanding it you will be freed from evil (from samsara, the wheel of birth and death).

### 4.17

कर्मणो ह्यपि बोद्धव्यं बोद्धव्यं च विकर्मणः ।  
अकर्मणश्च बोद्धव्यं गहना कर्मणो गतिः ॥ ४-१७॥

*karmaṇo hyapi boddhavyaṃ boddhavyaṃ ca vikarmaṇaḥ  
akarmaṇaśca boddhavyaṃ gahanā karmaṇo gatiḥ 4-17*

One truly needs to understand (the real nature of) right action, and also of forbidden (or unlawful) action and of inaction. The nature (the path) of action is hard to fathom.

### 4.18

कर्मण्यकर्म यः पश्येदकर्मणि च कर्म यः ।  
स बुद्धिमान्मनुष्येषु स युक्तः कृत्स्नकर्मकृत् ॥ ४-१८॥

*karmaṇyakarma yaḥ paśyedakarmaṇi ca karma yaḥ  
sa buddhimānmanuṣyeṣu sa yuktaḥ kṛtsnakarmakṛt 4-18*

Whoever sees inaction within action and action within inaction is wise among people. Such a one is a yogi and has truly accomplished all action.

### 4.19

यस्य सर्वे समारम्भाः कामसङ्कल्पवर्जिताः ।  
ज्ञानाग्निदग्धकर्माणं तमाहुः पण्डितं बुधाः ॥ ४-१९॥

*yasya sarve samārambhāḥ kāmasaṅkalpavarjitāḥ  
jñānāgnidagdhakarmāṇaṃ tamāhuḥ paṇḍitaṃ budhāḥ 4-19*

When all of a person's undertakings are free of desire and personal purpose, and his actions have been burned away by the Fire of Knowledge, the wise call him a Sage.

### 4.20

त्यक्त्वा कर्मफलासङ्गं नित्यतृप्तो निराश्रयः ।  
कर्मण्यभिप्रवृत्तोऽपि नैव किञ्चित्करोति सः ॥ ४-२०॥

*tyaktvā karmaphalāsaṅgaṃ nityatṛpto nirāśrayaḥ  
karmaṇyabhipravṛtto'pi naiva kiñcitkaroti saḥ 4-20*

Having let go of attachment to the fruits of action, always content and relying on nothing, he does nothing at all even while he is busy acting.

### 4.21

निराशीर्यतचित्तात्मा त्यक्तसर्वपरिग्रहः ।  
शारीरं केवलं कर्म कुर्वन्नाप्नोति किल्बिषम् ॥ ४-२१॥

*nirāśīryatacittātmā tyaktasarvaparigrahaḥ  
śārīraṃ kevalaṃ karma kurvannāpnoti kilbiṣam 4-21*

Free of expectation, with mind and self under control, having given up every possession, performing action with the body alone, he commits no sin.

### 4.22

यदृच्छालाभसन्तुष्टो द्वन्द्वातीतो विमत्सरः ।  
समः सिद्धावसिद्धौ च कृत्वापि न निबध्यते ॥ ४-२२॥

*yadṛcchālābhasantuṣṭo dvandvātīto vimatsaraḥ  
samaḥ siddhāvasiddhau ca kṛtvāpi na nibadhyate 4-22*

Satisfied with whatever comes without effort, beyond the pairs of opposites and free of envy, steady alike in success and failure, he acts but is not bound.

### 4.23

गतसङ्गस्य मुक्तस्य ज्ञानावस्थितचेतसः ।  
यज्ञायाचरतः कर्म समग्रं प्रविलीयते ॥ ४-२३॥

*gatasaṅgasya muktasya jñānāvasthitacetasaḥ  
yajñāyācarataḥ karma samagraṃ pravilīyate 4-23*

For one who is without attachment, who is liberated, whose mind rests in knowledge, and who acts only as a sacrifice, every action dissolves completely.

### 4.24

ब्रह्मार्पणं ब्रह्म हविर्ब्रह्माग्नौ ब्रह्मणा हुतम् ।  
ब्रह्मैव तेन गन्तव्यं ब्रह्मकर्मसमाधिना ॥ ४-२४॥

*brahmārpaṇaṃ brahma havirbrahmāgnau brahmaṇā hutam  
brahmaiva tena gantavyaṃ brahmakarmasamādhinā 4-24*

Brahman is the offering; Brahman is the clarified butter and the rest that make up the oblation; Brahman pours the offering into the fire that is Brahman. One who sees Brahman in every action will surely reach Brahman.

### 4.25

दैवमेवापरे यज्ञं योगिनः पर्युपासते ।  
ब्रह्माग्नावपरे यज्ञं यज्ञेनैवोपजुह्वति ॥ ४-२५॥

*daivamevāpare yajñaṃ yoginaḥ paryupāsate  
brahmāgnāvapare yajñaṃ yajñenaivopajuhvati 4-25*

Some yogis offer sacrifice only to the devas (deva-yajna). Others offer the sacrifice itself as a sacrifice, through the Self, into the Fire of Brahman (brahma-yajna).

### 4.26

श्रोत्रादीनीन्द्रियाण्यन्ये संयमाग्निषु जुह्वति ।  
शब्दादीन्विषयानन्य इन्द्रियाग्निषु जुह्वति ॥ ४-२६॥

*śrotrādīnīndriyāṇyanye saṃyamāgniṣu juhvati  
śabdādīnviṣayānanya indriyāgniṣu juhvati 4-26*

Some offer hearing and the other senses into the fires of restraint. Others offer sound and the other sense objects into the fires of the senses.

### 4.27

सर्वाणीन्द्रियकर्माणि प्राणकर्माणि चापरे ।  
आत्मसंयमयोगाग्नौ जुह्वति ज्ञानदीपिते ॥ ४-२७॥

*sarvāṇīndriyakarmāṇi prāṇakarmāṇi cāpare  
ātmasaṃyamayogāgnau juhvati jñānadīpite 4-27*

Still others offer every function of the senses and every function of the breath (vital energy) into the fire of the Yoga of self-restraint, a fire lit by knowledge.

### 4.28

द्रव्ययज्ञास्तपोयज्ञा योगयज्ञास्तथापरे ।  
स्वाध्यायज्ञानयज्ञाश्च यतयः संशितव्रताः ॥ ४-२८॥

*dravyayajñāstapoyajñā yogayajñāstathāpare  
svādhyāyajñānayajñāśca yatayaḥ saṃśitavratāḥ 4-28*

Others offer wealth, austerity, and Yoga as their sacrifice, while ascetics of firm vows and self-discipline offer scriptural study and knowledge as theirs.

### 4.29

अपाने जुह्वति प्राणं प्राणेऽपानं तथापरे ।  
प्राणापानगती रुद्ध्वा प्राणायामपरायणाः ॥ ४-२९॥

*apāne juhvati prāṇaṃ prāṇe'pānaṃ tathāpare  
prāṇāpānagatī ruddhvā prāṇāyāmaparāyaṇāḥ 4-29*

Others, intent solely on breath control, offer the outgoing breath into the incoming and the incoming into the outgoing, holding back the flow of both.

### 4.30

अपरे नियताहाराः प्राणान्प्राणेषु जुह्वति ।  
सर्वेऽप्येते यज्ञविदो यज्ञक्षपितकल्मषाः ॥ ४-३०॥

*apare niyatāhārāḥ prāṇānprāṇeṣu juhvati  
sarve'pyete yajñavido yajñakṣapitakalmaṣāḥ 4-30*

Others, keeping their diet carefully regulated, offer the vital airs into the Vital Air. All of these understand sacrifice, and sacrifice has destroyed their sins.

### 4.31

यज्ञशिष्टामृतभुजो यान्ति ब्रह्म सनातनम् ।  
नायं लोकोऽस्त्ययज्ञस्य कुतोऽन्यः कुरुसत्तम ॥ ४-३१॥

*yajñaśiṣṭāmṛtabhujo yānti brahma sanātanam  
nāyaṃ loko'styayajñasya kuto'nyaḥ kurusattama 4-31*

Those who eat the nectar that remains after the sacrifice go to the eternal Brahman. For one who performs no sacrifice, not even this world is his; how then could the other (world) be, O best of the Kurus?

### 4.32

एवं बहुविधा यज्ञा वितता ब्रह्मणो मुखे ।  
कर्मजान्विद्धि तान्सर्वानेवं ज्ञात्वा विमोक्ष्यसे ॥ ४-३२॥

*evaṃ bahuvidhā yajñā vitatā brahmaṇo mukhe  
karmajānviddhi tānsarvānevaṃ jñātvā vimokṣyase 4-32*

In this way sacrifices of many kinds are laid out before Brahman (literally, at the mouth or face of Brahman). Know that all of them arise from action, and with that knowledge you will be set free.

### 4.33

श्रेयान्द्रव्यमयाद्यज्ञाज्ज्ञानयज्ञः परन्तप ।  
सर्वं कर्माखिलं पार्थ ज्ञाने परिसमाप्यते ॥ ४-३३॥

*śreyāndravyamayādyajñājjñānayajñaḥ parantapa  
sarvaṃ karmākhilaṃ pārtha jñāne parisamāpyate 4-33*

The sacrifice of knowledge is greater than sacrifice with material objects, O Parantapa. Every action without exception, O Partha, comes to its fulfillment in Knowledge.

### 4.34

तद्विद्धि प्रणिपातेन परिप्रश्नेन सेवया ।  
उपदेक्ष्यन्ति ते ज्ञानं ज्ञानिनस्तत्त्वदर्शिनः ॥ ४-३४॥

*tadviddhi praṇipātena paripraśnena sevayā  
upadekṣyanti te jñānaṃ jñāninastattvadarśinaḥ 4-34*

Understand that the wise who have realized the Truth will teach you (that) Knowledge when you approach them with deep prostration, with questions, and with service.

### 4.35

यज्ज्ञात्वा न पुनर्मोहमेवं यास्यसि पाण्डव ।  
येन भूतान्यशेषेण द्रक्ष्यस्यात्मन्यथो मयि ॥ ४-३५॥ var अशेषाणि

*yajjñātvā na punarmohamevaṃ yāsyasi pāṇḍava  
yena bhūtānyaśeṣeṇa drakṣyasyātmanyatho mayi 4-35*

Once you know it, O Pandava, you will never again fall into delusion like this. Through it you will see all beings in your own Self, and in Me as well.

### 4.36

अपि चेदसि पापेभ्यः सर्वेभ्यः पापकृत्तमः ।  
सर्वं ज्ञानप्लवेनैव वृजिनं सन्तरिष्यसि ॥ ४-३६॥

*api cedasi pāpebhyaḥ sarvebhyaḥ pāpakṛttamaḥ  
sarvaṃ jñānaplavenaiva vṛjinaṃ santariṣyasi 4-36*

Even if you were the worst sinner of all, you would still cross beyond every sin on the raft of Knowledge.

### 4.37

यथैधांसि समिद्धोऽग्निर्भस्मसात्कुरुतेऽर्जुन ।  
ज्ञानाग्निः सर्वकर्माणि भस्मसात्कुरुते तथा ॥ ४-३७॥

*yathaidhāṃsi samiddho'gnirbhasmasātkurute'rjuna  
jñānāgniḥ sarvakarmāṇi bhasmasātkurute tathā 4-37*

Just as a blazing fire turns fuel to ashes, Arjuna, so the Fire of Knowledge turns all actions to ashes.

### 4.38

न हि ज्ञानेन सदृशं पवित्रमिह विद्यते ।  
तत्स्वयं योगसंसिद्धः कालेनात्मनि विन्दति ॥ ४-३८॥

*na hi jñānena sadṛśaṃ pavitramiha vidyate  
tatsvayaṃ yogasaṃsiddhaḥ kālenātmani vindati 4-38*

Nothing in this world purifies as Knowledge does. One who has become perfect in Yoga discovers it in the Self, in due time.

### 4.39

श्रद्धावाँल्लभते ज्ञानं तत्परः संयतेन्द्रियः ।  
ज्ञानं लब्ध्वा परां शान्तिमचिरेणाधिगच्छति ॥ ४-३९॥

*śraddhāvām̐llabhate jñānaṃ tatparaḥ saṃyatendriyaḥ  
jñānaṃ labdhvā parāṃ śāntimacireṇādhigacchati 4-39*

A person full of faith, devoted to It, with the senses mastered, gains (this) Knowledge. Having gained it, he soon reaches the Supreme Peace.

### 4.40

अज्ञश्चाश्रद्दधानश्च संशयात्मा विनश्यति ।  
नायं लोकोऽस्ति न परो न सुखं संशयात्मनः ॥ ४-४०॥

*ajñaścāśraddadhānaśca saṃśayātmā vinaśyati  
nāyaṃ loko'sti na paro na sukhaṃ saṃśayātmanaḥ 4-40*

The ignorant person, without faith and full of self-doubt, goes to ruin. For the doubter there is neither this world, nor the next, nor any happiness.

### 4.41

योगसंन्यस्तकर्माणं ज्ञानसञ्छिन्नसंशयम् ।  
आत्मवन्तं न कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनञ्जय ॥ ४-४१॥

*yogasaṃnyastakarmāṇaṃ jñānasañchinnasaṃśayam  
ātmavantaṃ na karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanañjaya 4-41*

One who has renounced action through Yoga, whose doubts have been torn apart by Knowledge, and who is master of himself, is not bound by actions, O Dhananjaya.

### 4.42

तस्मादज्ञानसम्भूतं हृत्स्थं ज्ञानासिनात्मनः ।  
छित्त्वैनं संशयं योगमातिष्ठोत्तिष्ठ भारत ॥ ४-४२॥   
  
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु  
ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे  
ज्ञानकर्मसंन्यासयोगो नाम चतुर्थोऽध्यायः ॥ ४॥

*tasmādajñānasambhūtaṃ hṛtsthaṃ jñānāsinātmanaḥ  
chittvainaṃ saṃśayaṃ yogamātiṣṭhottiṣṭha bhārata  4-42  
  
oṃ tatsaditi śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu  
brahmavidyāyāṃ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṃvāde  
jñānakarmasaṃnyāsayogo nāma caturtho'dhyāyaḥ 4*

So take the sword of Knowledge and cut through this doubt about the Self, born of ignorance and lodged in your heart. Take refuge in Yoga. Stand up, O Bharata.

So ends the fourth discourse, called "The Yoga of Knowledge and Renunciation of Action," in the Upanishads of the glorious Bhagavad Gita, the science of the Eternal, the scripture of Yoga, the dialogue between Sri Krishna and Arjuna.
