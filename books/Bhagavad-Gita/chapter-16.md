# Chapter 16 – Daivāsura Sampad Vibhāga Yoga (The Yoga of Divine and Demonic Qualities)

*Yoga of Divine and Demonic Qualities*

### 16.1

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
अभयं सत्त्वसंशुद्धिर्ज्ञानयोगव्यवस्थितिः ।  
दानं दमश्च यज्ञश्च स्वाध्यायस्तप आर्जवम् ॥ १६-१॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
abhayaṃ sattvasaṃśuddhihi jñānayogavyavasthitiḥ  
dānaṃ damaśca yajñaśca svādhyāyastapa ārjavam 16-1*

The Blessed Lord said: Freedom from fear, a pure heart, firm commitment to the Yoga of Knowledge, giving to others, mastery over the senses, sacrifice, study of the Shastras, and honesty . . .

### 16.2

अहिंसा सत्यमक्रोधस्त्यागः शान्तिरपैशुनम् ।  
दया भूतेष्वलोलुप्त्वं मार्दवं ह्रीरचापलम् ॥ १६-२॥

*ahiṃsā satyamakrodhastyāgaḥ śāntirapaiśunam  
dayā bhūteṣvaloluptvaṃ mārdavaṃ hrīracāpalam 16-2*

Doing no harm, truthfulness, freedom from anger, renunciation, calmness, freedom from deceit, compassion toward living beings, freedom from greed, gentleness, modesty, freedom from restlessness . . .

### 16.3

तेजः क्षमा धृतिः शौचमद्रोहो नातिमानिता ।  
भवन्ति सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातस्य भारत ॥ १६-३॥

*tejaḥ kṣamā dhṛtiḥ śaucamadroho nātimānitā  
bhavanti sampadaṃ daivīmabhijātasya bhārata 16-3*

Energy, forgiveness, endurance, purity, freedom from hatred, freedom from pride: these, O Bharata, are the marks of one born to the divine condition.

### 16.4

दम्भो दर्पोऽभिमानश्च क्रोधः पारुष्यमेव च ।  
अज्ञानं चाभिजातस्य पार्थ सम्पदमासुरीम् ॥ १६-४॥

*dambho darpo'bhimānaśca krodhaḥ pāruṣyameva ca  
ajñānaṃ cābhijātasya pārtha sampadamāsurīm 16-4*

Hypocrisy, arrogance, self-conceit, anger, and also harshness and ignorance: these, O Partha, mark one born to the demonic condition.

### 16.5

दैवी सम्पद्विमोक्षाय निबन्धायासुरी मता ।  
मा शुचः सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातोऽसि पाण्डव ॥ १६-५॥

*daivī sampadvimokṣāya nibandhāyāsurī matā  
mā śucaḥ sampadaṃ daivīmabhijāto'si pāṇḍava 16-5*

The divine nature is considered to lead to liberation, the demonic to bondage. Do not grieve, O Pandava; you were born with divine qualities.

### 16.6

द्वौ भूतसर्गौ लोकेऽस्मिन्दैव आसुर एव च ।  
दैवो विस्तरशः प्रोक्त आसुरं पार्थ मे श‍ृणु ॥ १६-६॥

*dvau bhūtasargau loke'smindaiva āsura eva ca  
daivo vistaraśaḥ prokta āsuraṃ pārtha me śṛṇu 16-6*

In this world there are two kinds of beings, the divine and the demonic. The divine kind has already been described in detail; now hear from Me, O Partha, about the demonic.

### 16.7

प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं च जना न विदुरासुराः ।  
न शौचं नापि चाचारो न सत्यं तेषु विद्यते ॥ १६-७॥

*pravṛttiṃ ca nivṛttiṃ ca janā na vidurāsurāḥ  
na śaucaṃ nāpi cācāro na satyaṃ teṣu vidyate 16-7*

Demonic people do not understand what should be done and what should be avoided. No purity, no right conduct, and no truth can be found in them.

### 16.8

असत्यमप्रतिष्ठं ते जगदाहुरनीश्वरम् ।  
अपरस्परसम्भूतं किमन्यत्कामहैतुकम् ॥ १६-८॥

*asatyamapratiṣṭhaṃ te jagadāhuranīśvaram  
aparasparasambhūtaṃ kimanyatkāmahaitukam 16-8*

They declare that the universe has no truth in it, no moral foundation, and no God; that it is not produced by any orderly chain of causes, but arises from lust alone. What else could there be?

### 16.9

एतां दृष्टिमवष्टभ्य नष्टात्मानोऽल्पबुद्धयः ।  
प्रभवन्त्युग्रकर्माणः क्षयाय जगतोऽहिताः ॥ १६-९॥

*etāṃ dṛṣṭimavaṣṭabhyan aṣṭātmāno'lpabuddhayaḥ  
prabhavant yugrakarmāṇaḥ kṣayāya jagato'hitāḥ 16-9*

Clinging to this view, these lost souls, feeble in understanding and violent in action, rise up as enemies of the world, bent on its destruction.

### 16.10

काममाश्रित्य दुष्पूरं दम्भमानमदान्विताः ।  
मोहाद्गृहीत्वासद्ग्राहान्प्रवर्तन्तेऽशुचिव्रताः ॥ १६-१०॥

*kāmamāśritya duṣpūraṃ dambhamānamadānvitāḥ  
mohādgṛhītvāsadgrāhān pravartante'śucivratāḥ 16-10*

Full of desires that can never be satisfied, steeped in hypocrisy, pride, and arrogance, holding wicked notions out of delusion, they act with impure intentions.

### 16.11

चिन्तामपरिमेयां च प्रलयान्तामुपाश्रिताः ।  
कामोपभोगपरमा एतावदिति निश्चिताः ॥ १६-११॥

*cintāmaparimeyāṃ ca pralayāntāmupāśritāḥ  
kāmopabhogaparamā etāvaditi niścitāḥ 16-11*

They surrender to countless anxieties that end only at death. They treat the satisfaction of lust as the highest goal and are convinced that this is all there is.

### 16.12

आशापाशशतैर्बद्धाः कामक्रोधपरायणाः ।  
ईहन्ते कामभोगार्थमन्यायेनार्थसञ्चयान् ॥ १६-१२॥

*āśāpāśaśatairbaddhāḥ kāmakrodhaparāyaṇāḥ  
īhante kāmabhogārtham anyāyenārthasañcayān 16-12*

Tied down by a hundred cords of hope, given over to lust and anger, they struggle to pile up wealth by unlawful means in order to satisfy their senses.

### 16.13

इदमद्य मया लब्धमिमं प्राप्स्ये मनोरथम् ।  
इदमस्तीदमपि मे भविष्यति पुनर्धनम् ॥ १६-१३॥

*idamadya mayā labdhamimaṃ prāpsye manoratham  
idamastīdamapi me bhaviṣyati punardhanam 16-13*

"Today I have gained this. This wish I will fulfill. This is mine, and that wealth too will be mine in the future."

### 16.14

असौ मया हतः शत्रुर्हनिष्ये चापरानपि ।  
ईश्वरोऽहमहं भोगी सिद्धोऽहं बलवान्सुखी ॥ १६-१४॥

*asau mayā hataḥ śatrurhaniṣye cāparānapi  
īśvaro'hamahaṃ bhogī siddho'haṃ balavānsukhī 16-14*

"I have killed that enemy, and I will destroy the others as well. I am the master. I am the one who enjoys. I am successful, powerful, and happy."

### 16.15

आढ्योऽभिजनवानस्मि कोऽन्योऽस्ति सदृशो मया ।  
यक्ष्ये दास्यामि मोदिष्य इत्यज्ञानविमोहिताः ॥ १६-१५॥

*āḍhyo'bhijanavānasmi ko'nyo'sti sadṛśo mayā  
yakṣye dāsyāmi modiṣya ityajñānavimohitāḥ 16-15*

"I am wealthy and of noble birth. Who else can compare with me? I will give gifts; I will enjoy myself." In this way they are led astray by ignorance.

### 16.16

अनेकचित्तविभ्रान्ता मोहजालसमावृताः ।  
प्रसक्ताः कामभोगेषु पतन्ति नरकेऽशुचौ ॥ १६-१६॥

*anekacittavibhrāntā mohajālasamāvṛtāḥ  
prasaktāḥ kāmabhogeṣu patanti narake'śucau 16-16*

Confused by endless fantasies, caught in the net of delusion, hooked on the satisfaction of lust, they sink into a foul hell.

### 16.17

आत्मसम्भाविताः स्तब्धा धनमानमदान्विताः ।  
यजन्ते नामयज्ञैस्ते दम्भेनाविधिपूर्वकम् ॥ १६-१७॥

*ātmasambhāvitāḥ stabdhā dhanamānamadānvitāḥ  
yajante nāmayajñaiste dambhenāvidhipūrvakam 16-17*

Conceited, obstinate, swollen with pride and intoxicated by wealth, they perform sacrifices that are such in name only, for show, and in defiance of what scripture prescribes.

### 16.18

अहङ्कारं बलं दर्पं कामं क्रोधं च संश्रिताः ।  
मामात्मपरदेहेषु प्रद्विषन्तोऽभ्यसूयकाः ॥ १६-१८॥

*ahaṅkāraṃ balaṃ darpaṃ kāmaṃ krodhaṃ ca saṃśritāḥ  
māmātmaparadeheṣu pradviṣanto'bhyasūyakāḥ 16-18*

Clinging to egoism, power, arrogance, lust, and anger, these spiteful people hate Me, who dwell in their own bodies and in the bodies of others.

### 16.19

तानहं द्विषतः क्रूरान्संसारेषु नराधमान् ।  
क्षिपाम्यजस्रमशुभानासुरीष्वेव योनिषु ॥ १६-१९॥

*tānahaṃ dviṣataḥ krūrānsaṃsāreṣu narādhamān  
kṣipāmyajasramaśubhān  āsurīṣveva yoniṣu 16-19*

These cruel and hateful people, the lowest of humanity in this world, these wrongdoers, I cast again and again into demonic wombs alone.

### 16.20

आसुरीं योनिमापन्ना मूढा जन्मनि जन्मनि ।  
मामप्राप्यैव कौन्तेय ततो यान्त्यधमां गतिम् ॥ १६-२०॥

*āsurīṃ yonimāpannā mūḍhā janmani janmani  
māmaprāpyaiva kaunteya tato yāntyadhamāṃ gatim 16-20*

Entering demonic wombs birth after birth, deluded and never reaching Me, they fall, O Kaunteya, into a state even lower than that.

### 16.21

त्रिविधं नरकस्येदं द्वारं नाशनमात्मनः ।  
कामः क्रोधस्तथा लोभस्तस्मादेतत्त्रयं त्यजेत् ॥ १६-२१॥

*trividhaṃ narakasyedaṃ dvāraṃ nāśanamātmanaḥ  
kāmaḥ krodhastathā lobhastasmādetattrayaṃ tyajet 16-21*

Lust, anger, and greed are the three gates of hell that destroy the Self. For this reason one should give up all three.

### 16.22

एतैर्विमुक्तः कौन्तेय तमोद्वारैस्त्रिभिर्नरः ।  
आचरत्यात्मनः श्रेयस्ततो याति परां गतिम् ॥ १६-२२॥

*etairvimuktaḥ kaunteya tamodvāraistribhirnaraḥ  
ācaratyātmanaḥ śreyastato yāti parāṃ gatim 16-22*

A person who has escaped these three gates to darkness, O Kaunteya, does what is truly good for himself, and so reaches the Supreme Goal.

### 16.23

यः शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य वर्तते कामकारतः ।  
न स सिद्धिमवाप्नोति न सुखं न परां गतिम् ॥ १६-२३॥

*yaḥ śāstravidhimutsṛjya vartate kāmakārataḥ  
na sa siddhimavāpnoti na sukhaṃ na parāṃ gatim 16-23*

One who throws aside the rules of the scriptures and acts driven by desire reaches neither perfection, nor happiness, nor the Supreme Goal.

### 16.24

तस्माच्छास्त्रं प्रमाणं ते कार्याकार्यव्यवस्थितौ ।  
ज्ञात्वा शास्त्रविधानोक्तं कर्म कर्तुमिहार्हसि ॥ १६-२४॥   
  
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु  
ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे  
दैवासुरसम्पद्विभागयोगो नाम षोडशोऽध्यायः ॥ १६॥

*tasmācchāstraṃ pramāṇaṃ te kāryākāryavyavasthitau  
jñātvā śāstravidhānoktaṃ karma kartumihārhasi 16-24  
  
oṃ tatsaditi śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu  
brahmavidyāyāṃ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṃvāde  
daivāsurasampadvibhāgayogo nāma ṣoḍaśo'dhyāyaḥ 16*

Therefore let the scriptures be your authority in deciding what should and should not be done. Once you know what the scriptural commands say, you should act accordingly here in this world.

Thus, in the Upanishads of the glorious Bhagavad Gita, the science of the Eternal, the scripture of Yoga, the dialogue between Sri Krishna and Arjuna, ends the sixteenth discourse, entitled "The Yoga of Divine and Demonic Qualities."
