# Chapter 5 – Karma-Sannyāsa Yoga (The Yoga of Renunciation)

*Yoga of Renunciation*

### 5.1

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
संन्यासं कर्मणां कृष्ण पुनर्योगं च शंससि ।  
यच्छ्रेय एतयोरेकं तन्मे ब्रूहि सुनिश्चितम् ॥ ५-१॥

*arjuna uvāca  
saṃnyāsaṃ karmaṇāṃ kṛṣṇa punaryogaṃ ca śaṃsasi  
yacchreya etayorekaṃ tanme brūhi suniścitam 5-1*

Arjuna said: O Krishna, you praise giving up actions, and then you also praise Yoga, the performance of actions. Tell me definitely which of these two is better.

### 5.2

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
संन्यासः कर्मयोगश्च निःश्रेयसकरावुभौ ।  
तयोस्तु कर्मसंन्यासात्कर्मयोगो विशिष्यते ॥ ५-२॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
saṃnyāsaḥ karmayogaśca niḥśreyasakarāvubhau  
tayostu karmasaṃnyāsātkarmayogo viśiṣyate 5-2*

The Blessed Lord said: Both renouncing action and the Yoga of action lead to the highest bliss. Yet of the two, the Yoga of action stands higher than the renunciation of action.

### 5.3

ज्ञेयः स नित्यसंन्यासी यो न द्वेष्टि न काङ्क्षति ।  
निर्द्वन्द्वो हि महाबाहो सुखं बन्धात्प्रमुच्यते ॥ ५-३॥

*jñeyaḥ sa nityasaṃnyāsī yo na dveṣṭi na kāṅkṣati  
nirdvandvo hi mahābāho sukhaṃ bandhātpramucyate 5-3*

A person who neither hates nor craves should be recognized as a permanent Samnyasi. O Mighty-armed, being free from the pairs of opposites, such a person is easily released from bondage.

### 5.4

साङ्ख्ययोगौ पृथग्बालाः प्रवदन्ति न पण्डिताः ।  
एकमप्यास्थितः सम्यगुभयोर्विन्दते फलम् ॥ ५-४॥

*sāṅkhyayogau pṛthagbālāḥ pravadanti na paṇḍitāḥ  
ekamapyāsthitaḥ samyagubhayorvindate phalam 5-4*

Only the childish, not the wise, treat Sankhya (Knowledge) and Yoga (the Yoga of action) as separate things. Whoever is truly grounded in even one of them gains the fruit of both.

### 5.5

यत्साङ्ख्यैः प्राप्यते स्थानं तद्योगैरपि गम्यते ।  
एकं साङ्ख्यं च योगं च यः पश्यति स पश्यति ॥ ५-५॥

*yatsāṅkhyaiḥ prāpyate sthānaṃ tadyogairapi gamyate  
ekaṃ sāṅkhyaṃ ca yogaṃ ca yaḥ paśyati sa paśyati 5-5*

The state reached by the Sankhyas (Jnanis) is reached by the Yogins (Karma-Yogins) as well. The one who truly sees is the one who sees Sankhya and Yoga as one.

### 5.6

संन्यासस्तु महाबाहो दुःखमाप्तुमयोगतः ।  
योगयुक्तो मुनिर्ब्रह्म नचिरेणाधिगच्छति ॥ ५-६॥

*saṃnyāsastu mahābāho duḥkhamāptumayogataḥ  
yogayukto munirbrahma nacireṇādhigacchati 5-6*

But without Yoga, O mighty-armed, renunciation is difficult to reach. The person who is harmonized through Yoga and steady in contemplation quickly attains Brahman.

### 5.7

योगयुक्तो विशुद्धात्मा विजितात्मा जितेन्द्रियः ।  
सर्वभूतात्मभूतात्मा कुर्वन्नपि न लिप्यते ॥ ५-७॥

*yogayukto viśuddhātmā vijitātmā jitendriyaḥ  
sarvabhūtātmabhūtātmā kurvannapi na lipyate 5-7*

One who is devoted to the path of action, whose mind is thoroughly pure, who has mastered the self, who has brought the senses under control, and who recognizes the Self within as the Self in all beings, remains untainted even while acting.

### 5.8

नैव किञ्चित्करोमीति युक्तो मन्येत तत्त्ववित् ।  
पश्यञ्श‍ृण्वन्स्पृशञ्जिघ्रन्नश्नन्गच्छन्स्वपञ्श्वसन् ॥ ५-८॥

*naiva kiñcitkaromīti yukto manyeta tattvavit  
paśyañ śṛṇvan spṛśañ jighrann aśnan gacchan svapañ śvasan 5-8*

"I am not doing anything at all." This is how the harmonized knower of Truth would think, even while seeing, hearing, touching, smelling, eating, moving, sleeping, and breathing,

### 5.9

प्रलपन्विसृजन्गृह्णन्नुन्मिषन्निमिषन्नपि ।  
इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेषु वर्तन्त इति धारयन् ॥ ५-९॥

*pralapan visṛjan gṛhṇann unmiṣan nimiṣann api  
indriyāṇīndriyārtheṣu vartanta iti dhārayan 5-9*

while speaking, releasing, grasping, opening and closing the eyes, firmly aware that it is only the senses that are moving among the objects of sense.

### 5.10

ब्रह्मण्याधाय कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा करोति यः ।  
लिप्यते न स पापेन पद्मपत्रमिवाम्भसा ॥ ५-१०॥

*brahmaṇyādhāya karmāṇi saṅgaṃ tyaktvā karoti yaḥ  
lipyate na sa pāpena padmapatramivāmbhasā 5-10*

One who acts while dedicating every action to Brahman and letting go of attachment is not stained by sin, just as a lotus leaf stays untouched by the water resting on it.

### 5.11

कायेन मनसा बुद्ध्या केवलैरिन्द्रियैरपि ।  
योगिनः कर्म कुर्वन्ति सङ्गं त्यक्त्वात्मशुद्धये ॥ ५-११॥

*kāyena manasā buddhyā kevalairindriyairapi  
yoginaḥ karma kurvanti saṅgaṃ tyaktvātmaśuddhaye 5-11*

Having given up attachment, Yogis act only with the body, mind, intellect, and senses, and they do so for the purification of the self (the ego).

### 5.12

युक्तः कर्मफलं त्यक्त्वा शान्तिमाप्नोति नैष्ठिकीम् ।  
अयुक्तः कामकारेण फले सक्तो निबध्यते ॥ ५-१२॥

*yuktaḥ karmaphalaṃ tyaktvā śāntimāpnoti naiṣṭhikīm  
ayuktaḥ kāmakāreṇa phale sakto nibadhyate 5-12*

The united one (the well-poised, the harmonized), having let go of the fruit of action, reaches Eternal Peace. The one who is not united (the unsteady, the unbalanced), driven by desire and clinging to the fruit, is bound.

### 5.13

सर्वकर्माणि मनसा संन्यस्यास्ते सुखं वशी ।  
नवद्वारे पुरे देही नैव कुर्वन्न कारयन् ॥ ५-१३॥

*sarvakarmāṇi manasā saṃnyasyāste sukhaṃ vaśī  
navadvāre pure dehī naiva kurvanna kārayan 5-13*

Renouncing all actions in the mind and fully in command of the self, the embodied being dwells at ease in the city of nine gates, neither acting nor making others (the body and senses) act.

### 5.14

न कर्तृत्वं न कर्माणि लोकस्य सृजति प्रभुः ।  
न कर्मफलसंयोगं स्वभावस्तु प्रवर्तते ॥ ५-१४॥

*na kartṛtvaṃ na karmāṇi lokasya sṛjati prabhuḥ  
na karmaphalasaṃyogaṃ svabhāvastu pravartate 5-14*

The Lord does not create agency for the world, nor actions, nor the joining of actions with their fruits. It is Nature that does the acting.

### 5.15

नादत्ते कस्यचित्पापं न चैव सुकृतं विभुः ।  
अज्ञानेनावृतं ज्ञानं तेन मुह्यन्ति जन्तवः ॥ ५-१५॥

*nādatte kasyacitpāpaṃ na caiva sukṛtaṃ vibhuḥ  
ajñānenāvṛtaṃ jñānaṃ tena muhyanti jantavaḥ 5-15*

The Lord accepts neither the wrongdoing nor even the merit of anyone. Knowledge is covered over by ignorance, and this is why beings are deluded.

### 5.16

ज्ञानेन तु तदज्ञानं येषां नाशितमात्मनः ।  
तेषामादित्यवज्ज्ञानं प्रकाशयति तत्परम् ॥ ५-१६॥

*jñānena tu tadajñānaṃ yeṣāṃ nāśitamātmanaḥ  
teṣāmādityavajjñānaṃ prakāśayati tatparam 5-16*

But for those whose ignorance has been destroyed by Knowledge of the Self, that Knowledge, like the sun, reveals the Supreme (Brahman).

### 5.17

तद्बुद्धयस्तदात्मानस्तन्निष्ठास्तत्परायणाः ।  
गच्छन्त्यपुनरावृत्तिं ज्ञाननिर्धूतकल्मषाः ॥ ५-१७॥

*tadbuddhayas tadātmānas tanniṣṭhās tatparāyaṇāḥ  
gacchantyapunarāvṛttiṃ jñānanirdhūtakalmaṣāḥ 5-17*

With their intellect absorbed in That, their very Self being That, firmly established in That, and holding That as their highest goal, they go to the place from which there is no return, their sins dissolved by Knowledge.

### 5.18

विद्याविनयसम्पन्ने ब्राह्मणे गवि हस्तिनि ।  
शुनि चैव श्वपाके च पण्डिताः समदर्शिनः ॥ ५-१८॥

*vidyāvinayasampanne brāhmaṇe gavi hastini  
śuni caiva śvapāke ca paṇḍitāḥ samadarśinaḥ 5-18*

Sages regard with the same eye a Brahmana who has learning and humility, a cow, an elephant, and even a dog and an outcaste.

### 5.19

इहैव तैर्जितः सर्गो येषां साम्ये स्थितं मनः ।  
निर्दोषं हि समं ब्रह्म तस्माद् ब्रह्मणि ते स्थिताः ॥ ५-१९॥

*ihaiva tairjitaḥ sargo yeṣāṃ sāmye sthitaṃ manaḥ  
nirdoṣaṃ hi samaṃ brahma tasmād brahmaṇi te sthitāḥ 5-19*

Right here (in this world), birth (indeed everything) is conquered by those whose minds rest in equality. Brahman is truly spotless and equal, and so they are established in Brahman.

### 5.20

न प्रहृष्येत्प्रियं प्राप्य नोद्विजेत्प्राप्य चाप्रियम् ।  
स्थिरबुद्धिरसम्मूढो ब्रह्मविद् ब्रह्मणि स्थितः ॥ ५-२०॥

*na prahṛṣyetpriyaṃ prāpya nodvijetprāpya cāpriyam  
sthirabuddhirasammūḍho brahmavid brahmaṇi sthitaḥ 5-20*

Settled in Brahman, with a steady intellect and free of delusion, the knower of Brahman neither rejoices when something pleasant comes nor grieves when something unpleasant comes.

### 5.21

बाह्यस्पर्शेष्वसक्तात्मा विन्दत्यात्मनि यत्सुखम् ।  
स ब्रह्मयोगयुक्तात्मा सुखमक्षयमश्नुते ॥ ५-२१॥

*bāhyasparśeṣvasaktātmā vindatyātmani yatsukham  
sa brahmayogayuktātmā sukhamakṣayamaśnute 5-21*

With the self detached from outer contacts, such a person finds happiness in the Self. With the self absorbed in meditation on Brahman, that person reaches unending happiness.

### 5.22

ये हि संस्पर्शजा भोगा दुःखयोनय एव ते ।  
आद्यन्तवन्तः कौन्तेय न तेषु रमते बुधः ॥ ५-२२॥

*ye hi saṃsparśajā bhogā duḥkhayonaya eva te  
ādyantavantaḥ kaunteya na teṣu ramate budhaḥ 5-22*

Pleasures that arise from contact with objects only produce pain, because they have a beginning and an end. O son of Kunti, the wise take no delight in them.

### 5.23

शक्नोतीहैव यः सोढुं प्राक्शरीरविमोक्षणात् ।  
कामक्रोधोद्भवं वेगं स युक्तः स सुखी नरः ॥ ५-२३॥

*śaknotīhaiva yaḥ soḍhuṃ prākśarīravimokṣaṇāt  
kāmakrodhodbhavaṃ vegaṃ sa yuktaḥ sa sukhī naraḥ 5-23*

Whoever, while still here (in this world) and before release from the body (death), can withstand the surge that arises from desire and anger is a Yogin and is a happy person.

### 5.24

योऽन्तःसुखोऽन्तरारामस्तथान्तर्ज्योतिरेव यः ।  
स योगी ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं ब्रह्मभूतोऽधिगच्छति ॥ ५-२४॥

*yo'ntaḥsukho'ntarārāmastathāntarjyotireva yaḥ  
sa yogī brahmanirvāṇaṃ brahmabhūto'dhigacchati 5-24*

The one who is happy within, who delights within, and who is lit from within, that Yogi reaches Absolute Freedom, or Moksha, and becomes Brahman.

### 5.25

लभन्ते ब्रह्मनिर्वाणमृषयः क्षीणकल्मषाः ।  
छिन्नद्वैधा यतात्मानः सर्वभूतहिते रताः ॥ ५-२५॥

*labhante brahmanirvāṇamṛṣayaḥ kṣīṇakalmaṣāḥ  
chinnadvaidhā yatātmānaḥ sarvabhūtahite ratāḥ 5-25*

Absolute Freedom, or Moksha, is gained by those Rishis whose sins have been wiped out, whose dualities have been torn apart, who are self-controlled, and who are devoted to the good of all beings.

### 5.26

कामक्रोधवियुक्तानां यतीनां यतचेतसाम् ।  
अभितो ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं वर्तते विदितात्मनाम् ॥ ५-२६॥

*kāmakrodhaviyuktānāṃ yatīnāṃ yatacetasām  
abhito brahmanirvāṇaṃ vartate viditātmanām 5-26*

For self-controlled ascetics who are free of desire and anger, who have mastered their thoughts, and who have realized the Self, Absolute Freedom (Brahmic Bliss) is present on every side.

### 5.27

स्पर्शान्कृत्वा बहिर्बाह्यांश्चक्षुश्चैवान्तरे भ्रुवोः ।  
प्राणापानौ समौ कृत्वा नासाभ्यन्तरचारिणौ ॥ ५-२७॥

*sparśān kṛtvā bahir bāhyāṃś cakṣuścaivāntare bhruvoḥ  
prāṇāpānau samau kṛtvā nāsābhyantaracāriṇau 5-27*

Shutting out (all) outer contacts, fixing the gaze (as it were) between the eyebrows, and evening out the outgoing and incoming breaths that move within the nostrils,

### 5.28

यतेन्द्रियमनोबुद्धि र्मुनिर्मोक्षपरायणः ।  
विगतेच्छाभयक्रोधो यः सदा मुक्त एव सः ॥ ५-२८॥

*yatendriya manobuddhir munirmokṣaparāyaṇaḥ  
vigatecchābhayakrodho yaḥ sadā mukta eva saḥ 5-28*

with the senses, mind, and intellect (always) under control, with liberation as the supreme goal, and free from desire, fear, and anger, the sage is truly liberated forever.

### 5.29

भोक्तारं यज्ञतपसां सर्वलोकमहेश्वरम् ।  
सुहृदं सर्वभूतानां ज्ञात्वा मां शान्तिमृच्छति ॥ ५-२९॥   
  
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु  
ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे  
संन्यासयोगो नाम पञ्चमोऽध्यायः ॥ ५॥

*bhoktāraṃ yajñatapasāṃ sarvalokamaheśvaram  
suhṛdaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ jñātvā māṃ śāntimṛcchati  5-29  
  
oṃ tatsaditi śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu  
brahmavidyāyāṃ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṃvāde  
saṃnyāsayogo nāma pañcamo'dhyāyaḥ 5*

Knowing Me as the Enjoyer of sacrifices and austerities, the Great Lord of all the worlds, and the friend of every being, that person reaches Peace.

Thus, in the Upanishads of the glorious Bhagavad Gita, the science of the Eternal, the scripture of Yoga, the dialogue between Sri Krishna and Arjuna, ends the fifth discourse, entitled "The Yoga of Renunciation."
