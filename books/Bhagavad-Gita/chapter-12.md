# Chapter 12 – Bhakti Yoga (The Yoga of Devotion)

*Yoga of Devotion*

### 12.1

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
एवं सततयुक्ता ये भक्तास्त्वां पर्युपासते ।  
ये चाप्यक्षरमव्यक्तं तेषां के योगवित्तमाः ॥ १२-१॥

*arjuna uvāca  
evaṃ satatayuktā ye bhaktāstvāṃ paryupāsate  
ye cāpyakṣaramavyaktaṃ teṣāṃ ke yogavittamāḥ 12-1*

Those devotees who, always steadfast, worship You in this way, and those others who worship the imperishable and unmanifested: which of these two groups has the better understanding of yoga?

### 12.2

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
मय्यावेश्य मनो ये मां नित्ययुक्ता उपासते ।  
श्रद्धया परयोपेताः ते मे युक्ततमा मताः ॥ १२-२॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
mayyāveśya mano ye māṃ nityayuktā upāsate  
śraddhayā parayopetāḥ te me yuktatamā matāḥ 12-2*

The Blessed Lord said: Those who fix their minds on Me and worship Me, always steadfast and possessed of the highest faith, are in My view the foremost in yoga.

### 12.3

ये त्वक्षरमनिर्देश्यमव्यक्तं पर्युपासते ।  
सर्वत्रगमचिन्त्यञ्च कूटस्थमचलन्ध्रुवम् ॥ १२-३॥

*ye tvakṣaramanirdeśyam avyaktaṃ paryupāsate  
sarvatragamacintyañca kūṭasthamacalandhruvam 12-3*

But those who worship the imperishable, the indefinable, the unmanifest, the all-pervading, the unthinkable, the unchanging, the unmoving, and the eternal, . . .

### 12.4

सन्नियम्येन्द्रियग्रामं सर्वत्र समबुद्धयः ।  
ते प्राप्नुवन्ति मामेव सर्वभूतहिते रताः ॥ १२-४॥

*sanniyamyendriyagrāmaṃ sarvatra samabuddhayaḥ  
te prāpnuvanti māmeva sarvabhūtahite ratāḥ 12-4*

who have brought all their senses under control, who are even-minded in every situation and always delight in the good of all beings: they too, in truth, come to Me.

### 12.5

क्लेशोऽधिकतरस्तेषामव्यक्तासक्तचेतसाम् ।  
अव्यक्ता हि गतिर्दुःखं देहवद्भिरवाप्यते ॥ १२-५॥

*kleśo'dhikatarasteṣām avyaktāsaktacetasām  
avyaktā hi gatirduḥkhaṃ dehavadbhiravāpyate 12-5*

Those whose minds are set on the Unmanifest have the harder task, because the Unmanifest, as a goal, is very difficult for embodied beings to reach.

### 12.6

ये तु सर्वाणि कर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य मत्पराः ।  
अनन्येनैव योगेन मां ध्यायन्त उपासते ॥ १२-६॥

*ye tu sarvāṇi karmāṇi mayi saṃnyasya matparāḥ  
ananyenaiva yogena māṃ dhyāyanta upāsate 12-6*

But those who worship Me, surrendering all their actions to Me, holding Me as their highest goal, and meditating on Me with undivided devotion (yoga) . . .

### 12.7

तेषामहं समुद्धर्ता मृत्युसंसारसागरात् ।  
भवामि नचिरात्पार्थ मय्यावेशितचेतसाम् ॥ १२-७॥

*teṣāmahaṃ samuddhartā mṛtyusaṃsārasāgarāt  
bhavāmi nacirātpārtha mayyāveśitacetasām 12-7*

for these, whose minds rest in Me, O Partha, I soon become the rescuer who lifts them out of the ocean of finite experience, which is samsara.

### 12.8

मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय ।  
निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः ॥ १२-८॥

*mayyeva mana ādhatsva mayi buddhiṃ niveśaya  
nivasiṣyasi mayyeva ata ūrdhvaṃ na saṃśayaḥ 12-8*

Set your mind on Me alone and rest your intellect in Me. From then on, without any doubt, you will live in Me alone.

### 12.9

अथ चित्तं समाधातुं न शक्नोषि मयि स्थिरम् ।  
अभ्यासयोगेन ततो मामिच्छाप्तुं धनञ्जय ॥ १२-९॥

*atha cittaṃ samādhātuṃ na śaknoṣi mayi sthiram  
abhyāsayogena tato māmicchāptuṃ dhanañjaya 12-9*

If you cannot hold your mind steadily on Me, then try to reach Me, O Dhananjaya, through the yoga of constant practice.

### 12.10

अभ्यासेऽप्यसमर्थोऽसि मत्कर्मपरमो भव ।  
मदर्थमपि कर्माणि कुर्वन्सिद्धिमवाप्स्यसि ॥ १२-१०॥

*abhyāse'pyasamartho'si matkarmaparamo bhava  
madarthamapi karmāṇi kurvansiddhimavāpsyasi 12-10*

If you cannot manage even abhyasa-yoga, then devote yourself to acting for My sake. Even by performing actions for My sake you will reach perfection.

### 12.11

अथैतदप्यशक्तोऽसि कर्तुं मद्योगमाश्रितः ।  
सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं ततः कुरु यतात्मवान् ॥ १२-११॥

*athaitadapyaśakto'si kartuṃ madyogamāśritaḥ  
sarvakarmaphalatyāgaṃ tataḥ kuru yatātmavān 12-11*

And if you cannot do even this, then take shelter in Me, master yourself, and give up the fruits of all your actions.

### 12.12

श्रेयो हि ज्ञानमभ्यासाज्ज्ञानाद्ध्यानं विशिष्यते ।  
ध्यानात्कर्मफलत्यागस्त्यागाच्छान्तिरनन्तरम् ॥ १२-१२॥

*śreyo hi jñānamabhyāsāj jñānāddhyānaṃ viśiṣyate  
dhyānātkarmaphalatyāgas tyāgācchāntiranantaram 12-12*

Knowledge is certainly better than practice; meditation is better than knowledge; giving up the fruits of action is better than meditation; and peace follows at once upon that giving up.

### 12.13

अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्रः करुण एव च ।  
निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी ॥ १२-१३॥

*adveṣṭā sarvabhūtānāṃ maitraḥ karuṇa eva ca  
nirmamo nirahaṅkāraḥ samaduḥkhasukhaḥ kṣamī 12-13*

One who hates no living being, who is friendly and compassionate toward all, who is free of attachment and the sense of "I," who stays balanced in pleasure and pain, and who is forgiving . . .

### 12.14

सन्तुष्टः सततं योगी यतात्मा दृढनिश्चयः ।  
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥ १२-१४॥

*santuṣṭaḥ satataṃ yogī yatātmā dṛḍhaniścayaḥ  
mayyarpitamanobuddhiryo madbhaktaḥ sa me priyaḥ 12-14*

who is always content, steady in meditation, self-controlled, firm in conviction, with mind and intellect given over to Me: that devotee of Mine is dear to Me.

### 12.15

यस्मान्नोद्विजते लोको लोकान्नोद्विजते च यः ।  
हर्षामर्षभयोद्वेगैर्मुक्तो यः स च मे प्रियः ॥ १२-१५॥

*yasmānnodvijate loko lokānnodvijate ca yaḥ  
harṣāmarṣabhayodvegair mukto yaḥ sa ca me priyaḥ 12-15*

One who does not disturb the world and whom the world cannot disturb, who is free of elation, envy, fear, and anxiety: that one is dear to Me.

### 12.16

अनपेक्षः शुचिर्दक्ष उदासीनो गतव्यथः ।  
सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥ १२-१६॥

*anapekṣaḥ śucirdakṣa udāsīno gatavyathaḥ  
sarvārambhaparityāgī yo madbhaktaḥ sa me priyaḥ 12-16*

One who wants nothing, who is pure, alert, detached, and untroubled, who has given up all new undertakings: such a person, devoted to Me, is dear to Me.

### 12.17

यो न हृष्यति न द्वेष्टि न शोचति न काङ्क्षति ।  
शुभाशुभपरित्यागी भक्तिमान्यः स मे प्रियः ॥ १२-१७॥

*yo na hṛṣyati na dveṣṭi na śocati na kāṅkṣati  
śubhāśubhaparityāgī bhaktimānyaḥ sa me priyaḥ 12-17*

One who neither rejoices nor hates, neither grieves nor craves, who has let go of both good and evil and is full of devotion, is dear to Me.

### 12.18

समः शत्रौ च मित्रे च तथा मानापमानयोः ।  
शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु समः सङ्गविवर्जितः ॥ १२-१८॥

*samaḥ śatrau ca mitre ca tathā mānāpamānayoḥ  
śītoṣṇasukhaduḥkheṣu samaḥ saṅgavivarjitaḥ 12-18*

One who is the same toward enemy and friend, the same in honour and disgrace, the same in cold and heat, in pleasure and pain, and who is free of attachment . . .

### 12.19

तुल्यनिन्दास्तुतिर्मौनी सन्तुष्टो येन केनचित् ।  
अनिकेतः स्थिरमतिर्भक्तिमान्मे प्रियो नरः ॥ १२-१९॥

*tulyanindāstutirmaunī santuṣṭo yena kenacit  
aniketaḥ sthiramatir bhaktimānme priyo naraḥ 12-19*

to whom blame and praise are alike, who is silent, satisfied with whatever comes, without a fixed home, steady of mind, and full of devotion: that person is dear to Me.

### 12.20

ये तु धर्म्यामृतमिदं यथोक्तं पर्युपासते ।  
श्रद्दधाना मत्परमा भक्तास्तेऽतीव मे प्रियाः ॥ १२-२०॥   
  
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु  
ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे  
भक्तियोगो नाम द्वादशोऽध्यायः ॥ १२॥

*ye tu dharmyāmṛtamidaṃ yathoktaṃ paryupāsate  
śraddadhānā matparamā bhaktāste'tīva me priyāḥ 12-20  
  
oṃ tatsaditi śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu  
brahmavidyāyāṃ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṃvāde  
bhaktiyogo nāma dvādaśo'dhyāyaḥ 12*

Those who follow this immortal dharma (the law of life) as I have described it, who have faith and hold Me as their highest goal: such devotees are exceedingly dear to Me.

So ends the twelfth discourse, called "The Yoga of Devotion," in the Upanishads of the glorious Bhagavad Gita, the science of the Eternal, the scripture of Yoga, the dialogue between Sri Krishna and Arjuna.
