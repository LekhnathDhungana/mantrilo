# Chapter 17 – Śraddhā-Traya Vibhāga Yoga (The Yoga of the Threefold Faith)

*Yoga of the Threefold Faith*

### 17.1

अर्जुन उवाच ।  
ये शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य यजन्ते श्रद्धयान्विताः ।  
तेषां निष्ठा तु का कृष्ण सत्त्वमाहो रजस्तमः ॥ १७-१॥

*arjuna uvāca  
ye śāstravidhimutsṛjya yajante śraddhayānvitāḥ  
teṣāṃ niṣṭhā tu kā kṛṣṇa sattvamāho rajastamaḥ 17-1*

Arjuna said: Krishna, what about those who ignore the rules of the scriptures but still offer sacrifice with faith? What is their state? Is it sattwa, rajas or tamas?

### 17.2

श्रीभगवानुवाच ।  
त्रिविधा भवति श्रद्धा देहिनां सा स्वभावजा ।  
सात्त्विकी राजसी चैव तामसी चेति तां श‍ृणु ॥ १७-२॥

*śrībhagavānuvāca  
trividhā bhavati śraddhā dehināṃ sā svabhāvajā  
sāttvikī rājasī caiva tāmasī ceti tāṃ śṛṇu 17-2*

The Blessed Lord said: The faith of embodied beings, which springs from their own nature, is of three kinds: sattvic (pure), rajasic (passionate) and tamasic (dull or dark). Listen as I explain it.

### 17.3

सत्त्वानुरूपा सर्वस्य श्रद्धा भवति भारत ।  
श्रद्धामयोऽयं पुरुषो यो यच्छ्रद्धः स एव सः ॥ १७-३॥

*sattvānurūpā sarvasya śraddhā bhavati bhārata  
śraddhāmayo'yaṃ puruṣo yo yacchraddhaḥ sa eva saḥ 17-3*

Bharata, each person's faith matches his nature. A human being is made of his faith; whatever his faith is, that is what he is.

### 17.4

यजन्ते सात्त्विका देवान्यक्षरक्षांसि राजसाः ।  
प्रेतान्भूतगणांश्चान्ये यजन्ते तामसा जनाः ॥ १७-४॥

*yajante sāttvikā devān yakṣarakṣāṃsi rājasāḥ  
pretānbhūtagaṇāṃścānye yajante tāmasā janāḥ 17-4*

Sattvic, or pure, people worship the gods (devas). Rajasic, or passionate, people worship the yakshas and rakshasas. The rest, the tamasic people of dull or dark nature, worship ghosts (pretas) and the hosts of bhutas, the nature spirits.

### 17.5

अशास्त्रविहितं घोरं तप्यन्ते ये तपो जनाः ।  
दम्भाहङ्कारसंयुक्ताः कामरागबलान्विताः ॥ १७-५॥

*aśāstravihitaṃ ghoraṃ tapyante ye tapo janāḥ  
dambhāhaṅkārasaṃyuktāḥ kāmarāgabalānvitāḥ 17-5*

Those who undergo severe austerities that the scriptures do not prescribe, who are full of hypocrisy and egoism, and who are driven by the force of lust and attachment,

### 17.6

कर्षयन्तः शरीरस्थं भूतग्राममचेतसः ।  
मां चैवान्तःशरीरस्थं तान्विद्ध्यासुरनिश्चयान् ॥ १७-६॥

*karṣayantaḥ śarīrasthaṃ bhūtagrāmamacetasaḥ  
māṃ caivāntaḥśarīrasthaṃ tānviddhyāsuraniścayān 17-6*

mindlessly tormenting all the elements of the body, and tormenting Me too, who dwell within the body: know that such people are demonic in their resolve.

### 17.7

आहारस्त्वपि सर्वस्य त्रिविधो भवति प्रियः ।  
यज्ञस्तपस्तथा दानं तेषां भेदमिमं श‍ृणु ॥ १७-७॥

*āhārastvapi sarvasya trividho bhavati priyaḥ  
yajñastapastathā dānaṃ teṣāṃ bhedamimaṃ śṛṇu 17-7*

The food that each person prefers is also of three kinds, and so are sacrifice, austerity and charity. Hear now how they differ.

### 17.8

आयुःसत्त्वबलारोग्य सुखप्रीतिविवर्धनाः ।  
रस्याः स्निग्धाः स्थिरा हृद्या आहाराः सात्त्विकप्रियाः ॥ १७-८॥

*āyuḥsattvabalārogya sukhaprītivivardhanāḥ  
rasyāḥ snigdhāḥ sthirā hṛdyā āhārāḥ sāttvikapriyāḥ 17-8*

Foods that increase life, purity, strength, health, happiness and cheerfulness (good appetite), and that are flavorful, rich in oil, nourishing and pleasing, are the foods the sattvic (pure) enjoy.

### 17.9

कट्वम्ललवणात्युष्ण तीक्ष्णरूक्षविदाहिनः ।  
आहारा राजसस्येष्टा दुःखशोकामयप्रदाः ॥ १७-९॥

*kaṭvamlalavaṇātyuṣṇa tīkṣṇarūkṣavidāhinaḥ  
āhārā rājasasyeṣṭā duḥkhaśokāmayapradāḥ 17-9*

Foods that are bitter, sour, salty, extremely hot, pungent, dry and scorching are preferred by the rajasic; they bring pain, sorrow and sickness.

### 17.10

यातयामं गतरसं पूति पर्युषितं च यत् ।  
उच्छिष्टमपि चामेध्यं भोजनं तामसप्रियम् ॥ १७-१०॥

*yātayāmaṃ gatarasaṃ pūti paryuṣitaṃ ca yat  
ucchiṣṭamapi cāmedhyaṃ bhojanaṃ tāmasapriyam 17-10*

Food that is stale, tasteless, foul-smelling and decayed, leftover and impure, is what the tamasic enjoy.

### 17.11

अफलाकाङ्क्षिभिर्यज्ञो विधिदृष्टो य इज्यते ।  
यष्टव्यमेवेति मनः समाधाय स सात्त्विकः ॥ १७-११॥

*aphalākāṅkṣibhiryajño vidhidṛṣṭo ya ijyate  
yaṣṭavyameveti manaḥ samādhāya sa sāttvikaḥ 17-11*

A sacrifice that people offer without any wish for reward, according to the prescribed rules, and with firm faith that sacrifice is a duty, is sattvic or pure.

### 17.12

अभिसन्धाय तु फलं दम्भार्थमपि चैव यत् ।  
इज्यते भरतश्रेष्ठ तं यज्ञं विद्धि राजसम् ॥ १७-१२॥

*abhisandhāya tu phalaṃ dambhārthamapi caiva yat  
ijyate bharataśreṣṭha taṃ yajñaṃ viddhi rājasam 17-12*

Best of the Bharatas, a sacrifice offered in hope of reward or for the sake of show is to be recognized as a rajasic yajna.

### 17.13

विधिहीनमसृष्टान्नं मन्त्रहीनमदक्षिणम् ।  
श्रद्धाविरहितं यज्ञं तामसं परिचक्षते ॥ १७-१३॥

*vidhihīnamasṛṣṭānnaṃ mantrahīnamadakṣiṇam  
śraddhāvirahitaṃ yajñaṃ tāmasaṃ paricakṣate 17-13*

A sacrifice that goes against the rules, in which no food is shared, which lacks mantras and gifts, and which is performed without faith, is declared to be tamasic.

### 17.14

देवद्विजगुरुप्राज्ञपूजनं शौचमार्जवम् ।  
ब्रह्मचर्यमहिंसा च शारीरं तप उच्यते ॥ १७-१४॥

*devadvijaguruprājñapūjanaṃ śaucamārjavam  
brahmacaryamahiṃsā ca śārīraṃ tapa ucyate 17-14*

Reverence toward the gods, the twice-born, teachers and the wise; purity, honesty, celibacy and non-violence: these are called austerity of the body.

### 17.15

अनुद्वेगकरं वाक्यं सत्यं प्रियहितं च यत् ।  
स्वाध्यायाभ्यसनं चैव वाङ्मयं तप उच्यते ॥ १७-१५॥

*anudvegakaraṃ vākyaṃ satyaṃ priyahitaṃ ca yat  
svādhyāyābhyasanaṃ caiva vāṅmayaṃ tapa ucyate 17-15*

Speech that stirs no agitation, that is truthful, pleasant and helpful, together with the regular study of the Vedas, makes up the austerity of speech.

### 17.16

मनः प्रसादः सौम्यत्वं मौनमात्मविनिग्रहः ।  
भावसंशुद्धिरित्येतत्तपो मानसमुच्यते ॥ १७-१६॥

*manaḥ prasādaḥ saumyatvaṃ maunamātmavinigrahaḥ  
bhāvasaṃśuddhirityetat tapomānasamucyate 17-16*

Calmness of mind, kindness of heart, silence, self-restraint and purity of character: together these are called austerity of the mind.

### 17.17

श्रद्धया परया तप्तं तपस्तत्त्रिविधं नरैः ।  
अफलाकाङ्क्षिभिर्युक्तैः सात्त्विकं परिचक्षते ॥ १७-१७॥

*śraddhayā parayā taptaṃ tapastattrividhaṃ naraiḥ  
aphalākāṅkṣibhiryuktaiḥ sāttvikaṃ paricakṣate 17-17*

When steadfast people practice this threefold austerity with the deepest faith and no wish for reward, it is called sattvic.

### 17.18

सत्कारमानपूजार्थं तपो दम्भेन चैव यत् ।  
क्रियते तदिह प्रोक्तं राजसं चलमध्रुवम् ॥ १७-१८॥

*satkāramānapūjārthaṃ tapo dambhena caiva yat  
kriyate tadiha proktaṃ rājasaṃ calamadhruvam 17-18*

Austerity practiced for the sake of being well received, honored and revered, and practiced with hypocrisy, is said here to be rajasic; it is unstable and does not last.

### 17.19

मूढग्राहेणात्मनो यत्पीडया क्रियते तपः ।  
परस्योत्सादनार्थं वा तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ १७-१९॥

*mūḍhagrāheṇātmano yatpīḍayā kriyate tapaḥ  
parasyotsādanārthaṃ vā tattāmasamudāhṛtam 17-19*

Austerity carried out through self-torture, based on some misguided idea, or in order to harm someone else, is declared to be tamasic.

### 17.20

दातव्यमिति यद्दानं दीयतेऽनुपकारिणे ।  
देशे काले च पात्रे च तद्दानं सात्त्विकं स्मृतम् ॥ १७-२०॥

*dātavyamiti yaddānaṃ dīyate'nupakāriṇe  
deśe kāle ca pātre ca taddānaṃ sāttvikaṃ smṛtam 17-20*

A gift that is given because giving is understood to be a duty, at the right time and place, to a deserving person from whom nothing is expected in return, is considered sattvic.

### 17.21

यत्तु प्रत्युपकारार्थं फलमुद्दिश्य वा पुनः ।  
दीयते च परिक्लिष्टं तद्दानं राजसं स्मृतम् ॥ १७-२१॥

*yattu pratyupakārārthaṃ phalamuddiśya vā punaḥ  
dīyate ca parikliṣṭaṃ taddānaṃ rājasaṃ smṛtam 17-21*

A gift given in the hope of getting something back, or with an eye to a future reward, or given grudgingly, is considered rajasic.

### 17.22

अदेशकाले यद्दानमपात्रेभ्यश्च दीयते ।  
असत्कृतमवज्ञातं तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ १७-२२॥

*adeśakāle yaddānamapātrebhyaśca dīyate  
asatkṛtamavajñātaṃ tattāmasamudāhṛtam 17-22*

A gift given at the wrong place and time, to unworthy people, without respect or with contempt, is declared to be tamasic.

### 17.23

ॐतत्सदिति निर्देशो ब्रह्मणस्त्रिविधः स्मृतः ।  
ब्राह्मणास्तेन वेदाश्च यज्ञाश्च विहिताः पुरा ॥ १७-२३॥

*oṃtatsaditi nirdeśo brahmaṇastrividhaḥ smṛtaḥ  
brāhmaṇāstena vedāśca yajñāśca vihitāḥ purā 17-23*

"Om Tat Sat" has been declared the threefold name of Brahman. By it, in ancient times, the Brahmanas, the Vedas and the yajnas (sacrifices) were brought into being.

### 17.24

तस्मादोमित्युदाहृत्य यज्ञदानतपःक्रियाः ।  
प्रवर्तन्ते विधानोक्ताः सततं ब्रह्मवादिनाम् ॥ १७-२४॥

*tasmādomityudāhṛtya yajñadānatapaḥkriyāḥ  
pravartante vidhānoktāḥ satataṃ brahmavādinām 17-24*

For this reason, students of Brahman always begin their scripturally ordained acts of sacrifice, giving and austerity by uttering Om.

### 17.25

तदित्यनभिसन्धाय फलं यज्ञतपःक्रियाः ।  
दानक्रियाश्च विविधाः क्रियन्ते मोक्षकाङ्क्षिभिः ॥ १७-२५॥

*tadityanabhisandhāya phalaṃ yajñatapaḥkriyāḥ  
dānakriyāśca vividhāḥ kriyante mokṣakāṅkṣibhiḥ 17-25*

Seekers of liberation utter "Tat" as they perform acts of sacrifice and austerity and the various acts of giving, without aiming at any reward.

### 17.26

सद्भावे साधुभावे च सदित्येतत्प्रयुज्यते ।  
प्रशस्ते कर्मणि तथा सच्छब्दः पार्थ युज्यते ॥ १७-२६॥

*sadbhāve sādhubhāve ca sadityetatprayujyate  
praśaste karmaṇi tathā sacchabdaḥ pārtha yujyate 17-26*

The word "Sat" is used to mean Reality and Goodness; and, Partha, the word "Sat" is also used for an auspicious act.

### 17.27

यज्ञे तपसि दाने च स्थितिः सदिति चोच्यते ।  
कर्म चैव तदर्थीयं सदित्येवाभिधीयते ॥ १७-२७॥

*yajñe tapasi dāne ca sthitiḥ saditi cocyate  
karma caiva tadarthīyaṃ sadityevābhidhīyate 17-27*

Steadiness in sacrifice, austerity and giving is also called "Sat," and any action connected with these (done for the sake of the Supreme) is likewise called "Sat."

### 17.28

अश्रद्धया हुतं दत्तं तपस्तप्तं कृतं च यत् ।  
असदित्युच्यते पार्थ न च तत्प्रेत्य नो इह ॥ १७-२८॥   
  
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु  
ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे  
श्रद्धात्रयविभागयोगो नाम सप्तदशोऽध्यायः ॥ १७॥

*aśraddhayā hutaṃ dattaṃ tapastaptaṃ kṛtaṃ ca yat  
asadityucyate pārtha na ca tatpretya no iha 17-28  
  
oṃ tatsaditi śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu  
brahmavidyāyāṃ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṃvāde  
śraddhātrayavibhāgayogo nāma saptadaśo'dhyāyaḥ 17*

Partha, whatever is sacrificed, given or done, and whatever austerity is undertaken, without faith, is called "Asat." It counts for nothing, either here or in the hereafter (after death).

So ends the seventeenth discourse, called "The Yoga of the Threefold Faith," in the Upanishads of the glorious Bhagavad Gita, the science of the Eternal, the scripture of Yoga, the dialogue between Sri Krishna and Arjuna.
