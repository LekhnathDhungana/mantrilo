# Manisha Panchakam

### 1

अनुष्टुप् छन्दः   
सत्याचार्यस्य गमने कदाचिन्मुक्तिदायकम् ।  
काशीक्षेत्रं प्रति सह गौर्या मार्गे तु शङ्करम् ॥

*anuṣṭup chandaḥ   
satyācāryasya gamane kadācinmuktidāyakam .  
kāśīkṣetraṃ prati saha gauryā mārge tu śaṅkaram ..*

Anushtup Metre
The great teacher (Shankaracharya) was once on his way to the holy land of Kashi, the giver of liberation, when he came upon Shankara (Shiva) on the road, with Gauri (Parvati) beside him.

### 2

अन्त्यवेषधरं दृष्ट्वा गच्छ गच्छेति चाब्रवीत् ।  
शङ्करःसोऽपि चाण्डलस्तं पुनः प्राह शङ्करम् ॥

*antyaveṣadharaṃ dṛṣṭvā gaccha gaccheti cābravīt .  
śaṅkaraḥso'pi cāṇḍalastaṃ punaḥ prāha śaṅkaram ..*

Seeing him in the dress of an outcaste, Shankaracharya said, "Go away, go away." That chandala then spoke to Shankara (in reply).

### 3

अन्नमयादन्नमयमथवा चैतन्यमेव चैतन्यात् ।  
यतिवर दूरीकर्तुं वाञ्छसि किं ब्रूहि गच्छ गच्छेति ।

*annamayādannamayamathavā caitanyameva caitanyāt .  
yativara dūrīkartuṃ vāñchasi kiṃ brūhi gaccha gaccheti .*

The Chandala asks: O greatest of the twice-born! When you say "Go, go," what exactly do you want to move away? Should a body made of food move away from another body made of food? Or should consciousness move away from consciousness?

### 4

शार्दूल विक्रीडित छन्द -  
किं गङ्गाम्बुनि बिम्बितेऽम्बरमणौ चण्डालवाटीपयः  
पूरे चान्तरमस्ति काञ्चनघटीमृत्कुम्भयोर्वाम्बरे।  
प्रत्यग्वस्तुनि निस्तरङ्गसहजानन्दावबोधाम्बुधौ  
विप्रोऽयंश्वपचोऽयमित्यपि महान्कोऽयंविभेदभ्रमः

*śārdūla vikrīḍita chanda -  
kiṃ gaṅgāmbuni bimbite'mbaramaṇau caṇḍālavāṭīpayaḥ  
pūre cāntaramasti kāñcanaghaṭīmṛtkumbhayorvāmbare.  
pratyagvastuni nistaraṅgasahajānandāvabodhāmbudhau  
vipro'yaṃśvapaco'yamityapi mahānko'yaṃvibhedabhramaḥ*

Shardulavikridita metre
Does the "jewel of the sky" change at all when it is reflected in the waters of the Ganga rather than in a roadside ditch in a slum? Does space itself differ, whether it is inside a golden pot or a mud pot? In the inner Self, that ocean of self-existing Blissful Consciousness free of the ripples of thought, how can there be this deluded distinction: "this one is a brāhmaṇa and that one is a dog-eater"?

### 5

जाग्रत्स्वप्नसुषुप्तिषु स्फुटतरा या संविदुज्जृम्भते  
या ब्रह्मादिपिपीलिकान्ततनुषु प्रोता जगत्साक्षिणी ।  
सैवाहं न च दृश्यवस्त्विति दृढप्रज्ञापि यस्यास्ति चे-  
च्चाण्डालोऽस्तु स तु द्विजोऽस्तु गुरुरित्येषा मनीषा मम ॥ १ ॥

*jāgratsvapnasuṣuptiṣu sphuṭatarā yā saṃvidujjṛmbhate  
yā brahmādipipīlikāntatanuṣu protā jagatsākṣiṇī .  
saivāhaṃ na ca dṛśyavastviti dṛḍhaprajñāpi yasyāsti ce-  
ccāṇḍālo'stu sa tu dvijo'stu gururityeṣā manīṣā mama .. 1 ..*

Sri Shankara answers: This truly is my deep conviction. The person who has realised that he is not what is seen, but the one Consciousness that lights up every experience in waking, dream, and deep sleep, the one Consciousness that is the only witness of the whole play of the universe, the one Consciousness that is the very spark of life in all forms from the Creator down to the ant, that person alone is my Guru, whether he is a sweeper or a brāhmaṇa.

### 6

ब्रह्मैवाहमिदं जगच्च सकलं चिन्मात्रविस्तारितं  
सर्वं चैतदविद्यया त्रिगुणयाऽशेषं मया कल्पितम् ।  
इत्थं यस्य दृढा मतिः सुखतरे नित्ये परे निर्मले  
चाण्डालोऽस्तु स तु द्विजोऽस्तु गुरुरित्येषा मनीषा मम ॥ २ ॥

*brahmaivāhamidaṃ jagacca sakalaṃ cinmātravistāritaṃ  
sarvaṃ caitadavidyayā triguṇayā'śeṣaṃ mayā kalpitam .  
itthaṃ yasya dṛḍhā matiḥ sukhatare nitye pare nirmale  
cāṇḍālo'stu sa tu dvijo'stu gururityeṣā manīṣā mama .. 2 ..*

The person who, directly experiencing the spotless Supreme-Bliss-Eternal, has come to the firm understanding that the whole universe is only a vast play of pure Consciousness, entirely projected by his "ignorance" as expressed in the three moods of his mind, while he himself is only that Brahman: that person alone is my Guru, whether he is a sweeper or a brāhmaṇa.

### 7

शश्वन्नश्वरमेव विश्वमखिलं निश्चित्य वाचा गुरो-  
र्नित्यं ब्रह्म निरन्तरं विमृशता निर्व्याजशान्तात्मना ।  
भूतं भावि च दुष्कृतं प्रदहता संविन्मये पावके  
प्रारब्धाय समर्पितं स्ववपुरित्येषा मनीषा मम ॥ ३ ॥

*śaśvannaśvarameva viśvamakhilaṃ niścitya vācā guro-  
rnityaṃ brahma nirantaraṃ vimṛśatā nirvyājaśāntātmanā .  
bhūtaṃ bhāvi ca duṣkṛtaṃ pradahatā saṃvinmaye pāvake  
prārabdhāya samarpitaṃ svavapurityeṣā manīṣā mama .. 3 ..*

The person who has reflected at length on his teacher's words that this changing world is forever in flux; who has tamed his mind into true quiet and poise; who has freed his mind of all dissimilar thoughts and brought it to contemplate Brahman constantly; who has burnt up all his past and future residual vāsanās in the fire of pure Consciousness; who has handed over his body to live through and use up its present destiny: that person alone is my Guru, "whether he is a sweeper or a brāhmaṇa."

### 8

या तिर्यङ्नरदेवताभिरहमित्यन्तः स्फुटा गृह्यते  
यद्भासा हृदयाक्षदेहविषया भान्ति स्वतोऽचेतनाः ।  
तां भास्यैः पिहितार्कमण्डलनिभां स्फूर्तिं सदा भावय-  
न्योगी निर्वृतमानसो हि गुरुरित्येषा मनीषा मम ॥ ४ ॥

*yā tiryaṅnaradevatābhirahamityantaḥ sphuṭā gṛhyate  
yadbhāsā hṛdayākṣadehaviṣayā bhānti svato'cetanāḥ .  
tāṃ bhāsyaiḥ pihitārkamaṇḍalanibhāṃ sphūrtiṃ sadā bhāvaya-  
nyogī nirvṛtamānaso hi gururityeṣā manīṣā mama .. 4 ..*

A yogī lives with a hushed mind, meditating on That which shows itself as "I--I," the sense of individual subjecthood in all creatures, whether plant, animal, human, or angel; That by whose Light the mind, senses, and body are stirred to activity, although all of them are made of inert, insentient matter; That which lights up everything, like the sun behind a bank of clouds. Such a one alone is my Guru: this is my firm conviction.

### 9

यत्सौख्याम्बुधिलेशलेशत इमे शक्रादयो निर्वृता  
यच्चित्ते नितरां प्रशान्तकलने लब्ध्वा मुनिर्निर्वृतः ।  
यस्मिन्नित्यसुखाम्बुधौ गलितधीर्ब्रह्मैव न ब्रह्मविद्  
यः कश्चित्स सुरेन्द्रवन्दितपदो नूनं मनीषा मम ॥ ५ ॥

*yatsaukhyāmbudhileśaleśata ime śakrādayo nirvṛtā  
yaccitte nitarāṃ praśāntakalane labdhvā munirnirvṛtaḥ .  
yasminnityasukhāmbudhau galitadhīrbrahmaiva na brahmavid  
yaḥ kaścitsa surendravanditapado nūnaṃ manīṣā mama .. 5 ..*

In its moments of complete quiet, a yogī's mind reaches that Ocean of Bliss of which one tiny droplet is enough to make Indra and the others content and happy. The one who has dissolved his individual intellect in this eternal Ocean of Bliss truly is Brahman, and not merely a Knower of Brahman. That rare one, whose feet are worshipped even by the King of Gods himself, alone is my Guru: this is my firm conviction.

### 10

दासस्तेऽहं देहदृष्ट्याऽस्मि शंभो  
जातस्तेंऽशो जीवदृष्ट्या त्रिदृष्टे ।  
सर्वस्याऽऽत्मन्नात्मदृष्ट्या त्वमेवे-  
त्येवं मे धीर्निश्चिता सर्वशास्त्रैः ॥

*dāsaste'haṃ dehadṛṣṭyā'smi śaṃbho  
jātasteṃ'śo jīvadṛṣṭyā tridṛṣṭe .  
sarvasyā''tmannātmadṛṣṭyā tvameve-  
tyevaṃ me dhīrniścitā sarvaśāstraiḥ ..*

O Shambhu, seen from the standpoint of the body, I am your servant. Seen from the standpoint of the individual soul, O three-eyed one, I am a part born of you. But seen from the standpoint of the Self, you alone are everything. All the scriptures have firmly established this understanding in me.

### 11

॥ इति श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ मनीषापञ्चकं सम्पूर्णम् ॥

*.. iti śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavataḥ kṛtau manīṣāpañcakaṃ sampūrṇam ..*

Here concludes the Manisha Panchakam, composed by the revered Shankaracharya.
