# Sadhana Panchakam

### 1

वेदो नित्यमधीयतां तदुदितं कर्म स्वनुष्ठीयतां  
तेनेशस्य विधीयतामपचितिः काम्ये मतिस्त्यज्यताम् ।  
पापौघः परिधूयतां भवसुखे दोषोऽनुसन्धीयता-  
मात्मेच्छा व्यवसीयतां निजगृहात्तूर्णं विनिर्गम्यताम् ॥ १॥

*vedo nityamadhīyatāṃ taduditaṃ karma svanuṣṭhīyatāṃ  
teneśasya vidhīyatāmapacitiḥ kāmye matistyajyatām .  
pāpaughaḥ paridhūyatāṃ bhavasukhe doṣo'nusandhīyatā-  
mātmecchā vyavasīyatāṃ nijagṛhāttūrṇaṃ vinirgamyatām .. 1..*

Make study of the Vedas a daily habit.
Carry out with care the duties (karmas) they prescribe.
Offer all of those actions (karmas) to the Lord as worship.
Let go of every desire in the mind.
Cleanse the heaps of sin from your heart.
See that the pleasures of sense-objects (samsar) are shot through with pain.
Search for the Self with steady effort.
Break free from the bondage of 'home'.

### 2

सङ्गः सत्सु विधीयतां भगवतो भक्तिर्दृढाऽऽधीयतां  
शान्त्यादिः परिचीयतां दृढतरं कर्माशु सन्त्यज्यताम् ।  
सद्विद्वानुपसृप्यतां प्रतिदिनं तत्पादुका सेव्यतां  
ब्रह्मैकाक्षरमर्थ्यतां श्रुतिशिरोवाक्यं समाकर्ण्यताम् ॥ २॥

*saṅgaḥ satsu vidhīyatāṃ bhagavato bhaktirdṛḍhā''dhīyatāṃ  
śāntyādiḥ paricīyatāṃ dṛḍhataraṃ karmāśu santyajyatām .  
sadvidvānupasṛpyatāṃ pratidinaṃ tatpādukā sevyatāṃ  
brahmaikākṣaramarthyatāṃ śrutiśirovākyaṃ samākarṇyatām .. 2..*

Keep the company of the wise.
Stand firm in devotion to the Lord.
Develop virtues such as Shanti and the rest.
Give up all actions driven by desire.
Go for shelter to a Perfect Master (Sat-Guru).
Serve His Lotus feet each day.
Worship "Om," the Immutable.
Listen deeply to the declarations of the Upanishads.

### 3

वाक्यार्थश्च विचार्यतां श्रुतिशिरःपक्षः समाश्रीयतां  
दुस्तर्कात्सुविरम्यतां श्रुतिमतस्तर्कोऽनुसन्धीयताम् ।  
ब्रह्मास्मीति विभाव्यतामहरहर्गर्वः परित्यज्यतां  
देहेऽहम्मतिरुज्झ्यतां बुधजनैर्वादः परित्यज्यताम् ॥ ३॥

*vākyārthaśca vicāryatāṃ śrutiśiraḥpakṣaḥ samāśrīyatāṃ  
dustarkātsuviramyatāṃ śrutimatastarko'nusandhīyatām .  
brahmāsmīti vibhāvyatāmaharahargarvaḥ parityajyatāṃ  
dehe'hammatirujjhyatāṃ budhajanairvādaḥ parityajyatām .. 3..*

Keep reflecting on what the Upanishadic teachings mean, and take refuge in the Truth of Brahman.
Stay away from twisted arguments, and follow the discerning reasoning of the Sruti (Upanishads).
Remain always immersed in the attitude (bhav) "I am Brahman."
Let go of pride.
Drop the deluded misconception "I am the body."
Abandon completely the habit of arguing with the wise.

### 4

क्षुद्व्याधिश्च चिकित्स्यतां प्रतिदिनं भिक्षौषधं भुज्यतां  
स्वाद्वन्नं न तु याच्यतां विधिवशात् प्राप्तेन सन्तुष्यताम् ।  
शीतोष्णादि विषह्यतां न तु वृथा वाक्यं समुच्चार्यता-  
मौदासीन्यमभीप्स्यतां जनकृपानैष्ठुर्यमुत्सृज्यताम् ॥ ४ ॥

*kṣudvyādhiśca cikitsyatāṃ pratidinaṃ bhikṣauṣadhaṃ bhujyatāṃ  
svādvannaṃ na tu yācyatāṃ vidhivaśāt prāptena santuṣyatām .  
śītoṣṇādi viṣahyatāṃ na tu vṛthā vākyaṃ samuccāryatā-  
maudāsīnyamabhīpsyatāṃ janakṛpānaiṣṭhuryamutsṛjyatām .. 4 ..*

Treat the disease of hunger.
Take the medicine of Bhiksha-food every day.
Do not beg for tasty food.
Live content on whatever falls to your lot as He has ordained.
Bear all the pairs of opposites, such as heat and cold.
Avoid pointless talk.
Stay indifferent.
Keep yourself clear of the net of other people's kindness.

### 5

एकान्ते सुखमास्यतां परतरे चेतः समाधीयतां  
पूर्णात्मा सुसमीक्ष्यतां जगदिदं तद्बाधितं दृश्यताम् ।  
प्राक्कर्म प्रविलाप्यतां चितिबलान्नाप्युत्तरैः श्लिष्यतां  
प्रारब्धं त्विह भुज्यतामथ परब्रह्मात्मना स्थीयताम् ॥ ५॥

*ekānte sukhamāsyatāṃ paratare cetaḥ samādhīyatāṃ  
pūrṇātmā susamīkṣyatāṃ jagadidaṃ tadbādhitaṃ dṛśyatām .  
prākkarma pravilāpyatāṃ citibalānnāpyuttaraiḥ śliṣyatāṃ  
prārabdhaṃ tviha bhujyatāmatha parabrahmātmanā sthīyatām .. 5..*

Live happily in solitude.
Still your mind in the Supreme Lord.
Realize the all-pervading Self and see it everywhere.
Understand that the finite Universe is a projection of the Self.
Overcome the effects of deeds from earlier lives through right action now.
Through wisdom, become detached from future actions (Agami).
Live through and use up "Prarabdha," the fruits of past actions.
After that, remain absorbed in the bhav "I am Brahman"!
