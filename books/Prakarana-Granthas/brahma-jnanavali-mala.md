# Brahma Jnanavali Mala

### 1

सकृच्छ्रवणमात्रेण ब्रह्मज्ञानं यतो भवेत् ।  
ब्रह्मज्ञानावलीमाला सर्वेषां मोक्षसिद्धये ॥ १॥

*sakṛcchravaṇamātreṇa brahmajñānaṃ yato bhavet .  
brahmajñānāvalīmālā sarveṣāṃ mokṣasiddhaye .. 1..*

Hearing this work, the Brahma Jnanavali Mala, only once brings knowledge of Brahman, and it makes liberation possible for everyone.

### 2

असङ्गोऽहम् असङ्गोऽहम् असङ्गोऽहं पुनः पुनः ।  
सच्चिदानन्दरूपोऽहम् अहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ २॥

*asaṅgo'ham asaṅgo'ham asaṅgo'haṃ punaḥ punaḥ .  
saccidānandarūpo'ham aham evāham avyayaḥ .. 2..*

I have no attachment, none at all, and I am forever free of every kind of clinging. My nature is Existence-Consciousness-Bliss. I am the Self itself, imperishable and never changing.

### 3

नित्यशुद्धविमुक्तोऽहं निराकारोऽहम् अव्ययः ।  
भूमानन्दस्वरूपोऽहम् अहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ ३॥

*nityaśuddhavimukto'haṃ nirākāro'ham avyayaḥ .  
bhūmānandasvarūpo'ham aham evāham avyayaḥ .. 3..*

I am eternal. I am pure, beyond the control of Maya. I am always liberated. I have no form, I cannot perish, and I do not change. Infinite bliss is my nature. I am the Self itself, imperishable and changeless.

### 4

नित्योऽहं निरवद्योऽहं निराकारोऽहम् उच्यते ।  
परमानन्दरूपोऽहम् अहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ ४॥

*nityo'haṃ niravadyo'haṃ nirākāro'ham ucyate .  
paramānandarūpo'ham aham evāham avyayaḥ .. 4..*

I am eternal and without flaw. I have no form, I cannot perish, and I do not change. Supreme bliss is my nature. I am the Self itself, imperishable and changeless.

### 5

शुद्धचैतन्यरूपोऽहम् आत्मारामोऽहम् एव च ।  
अखण्डानन्दरूपोऽहम् अहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ ५॥

*śuddhacaitanyarūpo'ham ātmārāmo'ham eva ca .  
akhaṇḍānandarūpo'ham aham evāham avyayaḥ .. 5..*

I am pure consciousness, delighting in my own Self. My nature is undivided, concentrated bliss. I am the Self itself, imperishable and changeless.

### 6

प्रत्यक्चैतन्यरूपोऽहं शान्तोऽहं प्रकृतेः परः ।  
शाश्वतानन्दरूपोऽहम् अहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ ६॥

*pratyakcaitanyarūpo'haṃ śānto'haṃ prakṛteḥ paraḥ .  
śāśvatānandarūpo'ham aham evāham avyayaḥ .. 6..*

I am the consciousness that dwells within. I am at peace, untouched by any agitation. I lie beyond Prakriti (Maya). Eternal bliss is my nature. I am the Self itself, imperishable and changeless.

### 7

तत्त्वातीतः परात्माहं मध्यातीतः परः शिवः ।  
मायातीतः परंज्योतिर् अहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ ७॥

*tattvātītaḥ parātmāhaṃ madhyātītaḥ paraḥ śivaḥ .  
māyātītaḥ paraṃjyotir aham evāham avyayaḥ .. 7..*

I am the supreme Self, higher than all the categories such as Prakriti, mahat, and ahankara. I am the supreme auspicious One, higher than all those in the middle. I lie beyond Maya. I am the supreme light. I am the Self itself, imperishable and changeless.

### 8

नानारूपव्यतीतोऽहं चिदाकारोऽहम् अच्युतः ।  
सुखरूपस्वरूपोऽहम् अहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ ८॥

*nānārūpavyatīto'haṃ cidākāro'ham acyutaḥ .  
sukharūpasvarūpo'ham aham evāham avyayaḥ .. 8..*

I lie beyond every distinct form. Pure consciousness is my nature. Decline can never touch me. Bliss is my nature. I am the Self itself, imperishable and changeless.

### 9

मायातत्कार्यदेहादि मम नास्त्येव सर्वदा ।  
स्वप्रकाशैकरूपोऽहम् अहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ ९॥

*māyātatkāryadehādi mama nāstyeva sarvadā .  
svaprakāśaikarūpo'ham aham evāham avyayaḥ .. 9..*

For me there is no Maya and none of its products, such as the body. I am the single, self-shining reality. I am the Self itself, imperishable and changeless.

### 10

गुणत्रयव्यतीतोऽहं ब्रह्मादीनां च साक्ष्यहम् ।  
अनन्तानन्दरूपोऽहम् अहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ १०॥

*guṇatrayavyatīto'haṃ brahmādīnāṃ ca sākṣyaham .  
anantānandarūpo'ham aham evāham avyayaḥ .. 10..*

I stand beyond the three gunas: sattva, rajas, and tamas. Even Brahma and the others have me as their witness. Infinite bliss is my nature. I am the Self itself, imperishable and changeless.

### 11

अन्तर्यामिस्वरूपोऽहं कूटस्थः सर्वगोऽस्म्यहम् ।  
परमात्मस्वरूपोऽहम् अहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ ११॥

*antaryāmisvarūpo'haṃ kūṭasthaḥ sarvago'smyaham .  
paramātmasvarūpo'ham aham evāham avyayaḥ .. 11..*

I am the controller within. I never alter and I pervade everything. I myself am the supreme Self. I am the Self itself, imperishable and changeless.

### 12

निष्कलोऽहं निष्क्रियोऽहं सर्वात्माद्यः सनातनः ।  
अपरोक्षस्वरूपोऽहम् अहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ १२॥

*niṣkalo'haṃ niṣkriyo'haṃ sarvātmādyaḥ sanātanaḥ .  
aparokṣasvarūpo'ham aham evāham avyayaḥ .. 12..*

I have no parts and I perform no action. I am the self of everyone. I am the first, the ancient and eternal one. I am the self known by direct intuition. I am the Self itself, imperishable and changeless.

### 13

द्वन्द्वादिसाक्षिरूपोऽहम् अचलोऽहं सनातनः ।  
सर्वसाक्षिस्वरूपोऽहम् अहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ १३॥

*dvandvādisākṣirūpo'ham acalo'haṃ sanātanaḥ .  
sarvasākṣisvarūpo'ham aham evāham avyayaḥ .. 13..*

All pairs of opposites have me as their witness. I cannot be moved. I am eternal. I witness everything. I am the Self itself, imperishable and changeless.

### 14

प्रज्ञानघन एवाहं विज्ञानघन एव च ।  
अकर्ताहम् अभोक्ताहम् अहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ १४॥

*prajñānaghana evāhaṃ vijñānaghana eva ca .  
akartāham abhoktāham aham evāham avyayaḥ .. 14..*

I am a solid mass of awareness and consciousness. I neither act nor experience. I am the Self itself, imperishable and changeless.

### 15

निराधारस्वरूपोऽहं सर्वाधारोऽहम् एव च ।  
आप्तकामस्वरूपोऽहम् अहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ १५॥

*nirādhārasvarūpo'haṃ sarvādhāro'ham eva ca .  
āptakāmasvarūpo'ham aham evāham avyayaḥ .. 15..*

Nothing supports me, and I support everything. I have no desires that need fulfilling. I am the Self itself, imperishable and changeless.

### 16

तापत्रयविनिर्मुक्तो देहत्रयविलक्षणः ।  
अवस्थात्रयसाक्ष्यस्मि चाहम् एवाहम् अव्ययः ॥ १६॥

*tāpatrayavinirmukto dehatrayavilakṣaṇaḥ .  
avasthātrayasākṣyasmi cāham evāham avyayaḥ .. 16..*

The three kinds of affliction cannot touch me: those arising in the body, those coming from other beings, and those caused by higher powers. I am distinct from the gross, subtle, and causal bodies. I witness the three states of waking, dream, and deep sleep. I am the Self itself, imperishable and changeless.

### 17

दृग्दृश्यौ द्वौ पदार्थौ स्तः परस्परविलक्षणौ ।  
दृग्ब्रह्म दृश्यं मायेति सर्ववेदान्तडिण्डिमः ॥ १७॥

*dṛgdṛśyau dvau padārthau staḥ parasparavilakṣaṇau .  
dṛgbrahma dṛśyaṃ māyeti sarvavedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ .. 17..*

Two things stand apart from each other: the seer and the seen. Brahman is the seer and Maya is the seen. All of Vedanta declares this.

### 18

अहं साक्षीति यो विद्याद् विविच्यैवं पुनः पुनः ।  
स एव मुक्तः सो विद्वान् इति वेदान्तडिण्डिमः ॥ १८॥

*ahaṃ sākṣīti yo vidyād vivicyaivaṃ punaḥ punaḥ .  
sa eva muktaḥ so vidvān iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ .. 18..*

Whoever, through repeated contemplation, comes to realize that he is only a witness is the one who is liberated and enlightened. Vedanta declares this.

### 19

घटकुड्यादिकं सर्वं मृत्तिकामात्रम् एव च ।  
तद्वद् ब्रह्म जगत् सर्वम् इति वेदान्तडिण्डिमः ॥ १९॥

*ghaṭakuḍyādikaṃ sarvaṃ mṛttikāmātram eva ca .  
tadvad brahma jagat sarvam iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ .. 19..*

A pot, a wall, and such things are nothing other than clay. In the same way, the whole universe is nothing other than Brahman. Vedanta declares this.

### 20

ब्रह्म सत्यं जगन्मिथ्या जीवो ब्रह्मैव नापरः ।  
अनेन वेद्यं सच्छास्त्रम् इति वेदान्तडिण्डिमः ॥ २०॥

*brahma satyaṃ jaganmithyā jīvo brahmaiva nāparaḥ .  
anena vedyaṃ sacchāstram iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ .. 20..*

Brahman is real. The universe is mithya, meaning it cannot be classed as either real or unreal. The jiva is Brahman itself, not something else. Take this as the correct Sastra. Vedanta declares this.

### 21

अन्तर्ज्योतिर् बहिर्ज्योतिः प्रत्यग्ज्योतिः परात्परः ।  
ज्योतिर्ज्योतिः स्वयंज्योतिर् आत्मज्योतिः शिवोऽस्म्यहम् ॥ २१॥

*antarjyotir bahirjyotiḥ pratyagjyotiḥ parātparaḥ .  
jyotirjyotiḥ svayaṃjyotir ātmajyotiḥ śivo'smyaham .. 21..*

I am the auspicious one, the light within and the light without, the light that dwells inside, higher than the highest, the light of all lights, shining by itself, the light that is the Self.
