# Svarupanusandhanashtakam

### 1

तपोयज्ञदानादिभिः शुद्धबुद्धि-  
र्विरक्तो नृपादेः पदे तुच्छबुद्ध्या ।  
परित्यज्य सर्वं यदाप्नोति तत्त्वं  
परं ब्रह्म नित्यं तदेवाहमस्मि ॥ १॥

*tapoyajñadānādibhiḥ śuddhabuddhi-  
rvirakto nṛpādeḥ pade tucchabuddhyā .  
parityajya sarvaṃ yadāpnoti tattvaṃ  
paraṃ brahma nityaṃ tadevāhamasmi ..1..*

Some purify their intellect through austerity, sacrifice, charity and similar practices, grow dispassionate, count even the rank of kings as trivial, and give up everything to reach the Truth. The supreme, eternal Brahman that such a person attains is what I myself am.

### 2

दयालुं गुरुं ब्रह्मनिष्ठं प्रशान्तं  
समाराध्य मत्या विचार्य स्वरूपम् ।  
यदाप्नोति तत्त्वं निदिध्यास विद्वान्-  
परं ब्रह्म नित्यं तदेवाहमस्मि ॥ २॥

*dayāluṃ guruṃ brahmaniṣṭhaṃ praśāntaṃ  
samārādhya matyā vicārya svarūpam .  
yadāpnoti tattvaṃ nididhyāsa vidvān-  
paraṃ brahma nityaṃ tadevāhamasmi ..2..*

The wise one worships with devotion the compassionate Guru, who is utterly serene and established in Brahman, examines his own true nature with the intellect, and meditates deeply. The Reality he then attains is the supreme, eternal Brahman. I am that very One.

### 3

यदानन्दरूपं प्रकाशस्वरूपं  
निरस्तप्रपञ्चं परिच्छेदहीनम् ।  
अहम्ब्रह्मवृत्त्यैकगम्यं तुरीयं  
परं ब्रह्म नित्यं तदेवाहमस्मि ॥ ३॥

*yadānandarūpaṃ prakāśasvarūpaṃ  
nirastaprapañcaṃ paricchedahīnam .  
ahambrahmavṛttyaikagamyaṃ turīyaṃ  
paraṃ brahma nityaṃ tadevāhamasmi ..3..*

Its nature is bliss and its very essence is pure, luminous consciousness. In It the whole manifold universe dissolves. It is completely free of limitation and division, and can be reached only through the mental modification "I am Brahman." It is the Fourth (Turīya), beyond the three states, the supreme, eternal Brahman. That alone is what I am.

### 4

यदज्ञानतो भाति विश्वं समस्तं  
विनष्टं च सद्यो यदात्मप्रबोधे ।  
मनोवागतीतं विशुद्धं विमुक्तं  
परं ब्रह्म नित्यं तदेवाहमस्मि ॥ ४॥

*yadajñānato bhāti viśvaṃ samastaṃ  
vinaṣṭaṃ ca sadyo yadātmaprabodhe .  
manovāgatītaṃ viśuddhaṃ vimuktaṃ  
paraṃ brahma nityaṃ tadevāhamasmi ..4..*

Because It is not known, the entire universe appears; when the Self is awakened to, the universe dissolves at once. It lies beyond mind and speech, entirely pure and perfectly free. That eternal, supreme Brahman alone is what I am.

### 5

निषेधे कृते नेति नेतीति वाक्यैः  
समाधिस्थितानां यदाभाति पूर्णम् ।  
अवस्थात्रयातीतमद्वैतमेकं  
परं ब्रह्म नित्यं तदेवाहमस्मि ॥ ५॥

*niṣedhe kṛte neti netīti vākyaiḥ  
samādhisthitānāṃ yadābhāti pūrṇam .  
avasthātrayātītamadvaitamekaṃ  
paraṃ brahma nityaṃ tadevāhamasmi ..5..*

When negation has been carried through to the end by the words "not this, not this" (neti neti), what shines out as the Full to those established in samādhi is beyond the three states, non-dual and one. That supreme, eternal Brahman alone is what I am.

### 6

यदानन्दलेशैः समानन्दि विश्वं  
यदाभाति सत्त्वे तदाभाति सर्वम् ।  
यदालोकने रूपमन्यत्समस्तं  
परं ब्रह्म नित्यं तदेवाहमस्मि ॥ ६॥

*yadānandaleśaiḥ samānandi viśvaṃ  
yadābhāti sattve tadābhāti sarvam .  
yadālokane rūpamanyatsamastaṃ  
paraṃ brahma nityaṃ tadevāhamasmi ..6..*

A mere fragment of Its bliss makes the whole universe rejoice. Simply because It shines, in existence itself, all things shine. In Its sight every other form is seen. I am that very One, the eternal, supreme Brahman.

### 7

अनन्तं विभुं निर्विकल्पं निरीहं  
शिवं सङ्गहीनं यदोङ्कारगम्यम् ।  
निराकारमत्युज्ज्वलं मृत्युहीनं  
परं ब्रह्म नित्यं तदेवाहमस्मि ॥ ७॥

*anantaṃ vibhuṃ nirvikalpaṃ nirīhaṃ  
śivaṃ saṅgahīnaṃ yadoṅkāragamyam .  
nirākāramatyujjvalaṃ mṛtyuhīnaṃ  
paraṃ brahma nityaṃ tadevāhamasmi ..7..*

It is infinite, all-pervading, without distinctions and without desire. It is the auspicious one, unattached, reached through the syllable Om. It has no form, is supremely radiant and knows no death. That eternal, supreme Brahman alone is what I am.

### 8

यदानन्द सिन्धौ निमग्नः पुमान्स्या-  
दविद्याविलासः समस्तप्रपञ्चः ।  
तदा नः स्फुरत्यद्भुतं यन्निमित्तं  
परं ब्रह्म नित्यं तदेवाहमस्मि ॥ ८॥

*yadānanda sindhau nimagnaḥ pumānsyā-  
davidyāvilāsaḥ samastaprapañcaḥ .  
tadā naḥ sphuratyadbhutaṃ yannimittaṃ  
paraṃ brahma nityaṃ tadevāhamasmi ..8..*

When a person is wholly immersed in the ocean of bliss, the entire manifest universe, which is only the play of ignorance, dissolves for us. That wondrous reality, because of which all this shines, is the eternal, supreme Brahman. I truly am That.

### 9

स्वरूपानुसन्धानरूपां स्तुतिं यः  
पठेदादराद्भक्तिभावो मनुष्यः ।  
श्रुणोतीह वा नित्यमुद्युक्तचित्तो  
भवेद्विष्णुरत्रैव वेदप्रमाणात् ॥ ९॥

*svarūpānusandhānarūpāṃ stutiṃ yaḥ  
paṭhedādarādbhaktibhāvo manuṣyaḥ .  
śruṇotīha vā nityamudyuktacitto  
bhavedviṣṇuratraiva vedapramāṇāt ..9..*

The devout person who reverently recites this hymn, which is an inquiry into one's own true nature, or who listens to it here each day with an eager and attentive mind, will become Viṣṇu himself in this very world, on the authority of the Vedas.

### 10

इति श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य  
श्रीगोविन्दभगवत्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य  
श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ  
स्वरूपानुसन्धानाष्टकं सम्पूर्णम् ॥

*iti śrīmatparamahaṃsaparivrājakācāryasya  
śrīgovindabhagavatpūjyapādaśiṣyasya  
śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavataḥ kṛtau  
svarūpānusandhānāṣṭakaṃ sampūrṇam ..*

Here ends the Svarūpānusandhānāṣṭaka (the eight verses of contemplation on one's true nature), composed by the revered Śaṅkara Bhagavat, the venerable supreme renunciant, wandering mendicant and teacher, disciple of the revered Govinda Bhagavatpāda, whose feet are worthy of worship.
