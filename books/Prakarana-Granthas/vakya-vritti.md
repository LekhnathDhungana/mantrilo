# Vakya Vritti

### 1

वाक्यवृत्तिः  
सर्गस्थितिप्रलयहेतुमचिन्त्यशक्तिं  
विश्वेश्वरं विदितविश्वमनन्तमूर्तिम्।  
निर्मुक्तबन्धनमपारसुखाम्बुराशिं  
श्रीवल्लभं विमलबोधघनं नमामि।।1।।

*Vākyavṛttiḥ  
Sargasthitipralayahetumacintyaśaktiṃ  
Viśveśvaraṃ viditaviśvamanantamūrtim.  
Nirmuktabandhanamapārasukhāmburāśiṃ  
Śrīvallabhaṃ vimalabodhaghanaṃ namāmi..1..*

I bow to that Pure Divine Consciousness, an ocean of happiness without a shore. It is the All-pervading One (Vishnu), the Beloved of Shri, the all-knowing Lord of the Universe. It takes on endless forms and yet is always free, and by a power beyond understanding It becomes (apparently) the Cause of the universe's creation, maintenance, and dissolution.

### 2

यस्य प्रसादादहमेव विष्णु  
र्मय्येव सर्वं परिकल्पितं च।  
इत्थं विजानामि सदात्मरूपं  
तस्याङ्घ्रिपद्मं प्रणतोऽस्मि नित्यम्।।2।।

*Yasya prasādādahameva viṣṇu  
Rmayyeva sarvaṃ parikalpitaṃ ca.  
Itthaṃ vijānāmi sadātmarūpaṃ  
Tasyāṅghripadmaṃ praṇato'smi nityam..2..*

Again and again I prostrate at the feet of my Guru. Through the Guru's grace I have come to realize that "I alone am the All-pervading Essence (Vishnu)" and that "the whole world of multiplicity is a superimposition on myself."

### 3

तापत्रयार्कसंतप्तः कश्चिदुद्विग्नमानसः।  
शमादिसाधनैर्युक्तः सद्गुरुं परिपृच्छति।।3।।

*Tāpatrayārkasaṃtaptaḥ kaścidudvignamānasaḥ.  
Śamādisādhanairyuktaḥ sadguruṃ paripṛcchati..3..*

Burned by the blazing sun of the three miseries, a student who is weary of the world and restless for release, and who has developed all the means to liberation, especially virtues such as self-control, puts a question to a noble teacher:

### 4

अनायासेन येनास्मान्मुच्येय भवबन्धनात्।  
तन्मे संक्षिप्य भगवन्केवलं कृपया वद।।4।।

*Anāyāsena yenāsmānmucyeya bhavabandhanāt.  
Tanme saṃkṣipya bhagavankevalaṃ kṛpayā vada..4..*

"Holy Teacher, purely out of your grace and mercy, please explain to me briefly how I can easily be freed from the sorrows of this bondage to change."

### 5

साध्वी ते वचनव्यक्तिः प्रतिभाति वदामि ते।  
इदं तदिति विस्पष्टं सावधानमनाः श्रृणु।।5।।

*Sādhvī te vacanavyaktiḥ pratibhāti vadāmi te.  
Idaṃ taditi vispaṣṭaṃ sāvadhānamanāḥ śrṛṇu..5..*

The teacher said: "Your question is sound and very clearly put. I will answer it fully, so that it becomes as vivid to you as something you are seeing close at hand."

### 6

तत्त्वमस्यादिवाक्योत्थं यज्जीवपरमात्मनोः।  
तादात्म्यविषयं ज्ञानं तदिदं मुक्तिसाधनम्।।6।।

*Tattvamasyādivākyotthaṃ yajjīvaparamātmanoḥ.  
Tādātmyaviṣayaṃ jñānaṃ tadidaṃ muktisādhanam..6..*

The immediate means to liberation is direct knowledge of the complete identity of the individual Self and the Universal Self, a knowledge that arises from Vedic statements such as "Thou art that."

### 7

को जीवः कः परश्चात्मा तादात्म्यं वा कथं तयोः।  
तत्त्वमस्यादिवाक्यं वा कथं तत्प्रतिपादयेत्।।7।।

*Ko jīvaḥ kaḥ paraścātmā tādātmyaṃ vā kathaṃ tayoḥ.  
Tattvamasyādivākyaṃ vā kathaṃ tatpratipādayet..7..*

The disciple said: "What is the individual Self? What, then, is the Universal Self? How can the two be identical? And how can statements like 'That thou art' discuss and prove this identity?"

### 8

अत्र ब्रूमः समाधानं कोऽन्यो जीवस्त्वमेव हि।  
यस्त्वं पृच्छसि मां कोऽहं ब्रह्मैवासि न संशयः।।8।।

*Atra brūmaḥ samādhānaṃ ko'nyo jīvastvameva hi.  
Yastvaṃ pṛcchasi māṃ ko'haṃ brahmaivāsi na saṃśayaḥ..8..*

The teacher said: "I will answer your question. Who could the individual Self (Jiva) be except you, the one asking me, 'Who am I?' There is no doubt about this. You alone are Brahman."

### 9

पदार्थमेव जानामि नाद्यापि भगवन्स्फुटम्।  
अहं ब्रह्मेति वाक्यार्थं प्रतिपद्ये कथं वद।।9।।

*Padārthameva jānāmi nādyāpi bhagavansphuṭam.  
Ahaṃ brahmeti vākyārthaṃ pratipadye kathaṃ vada..9..*

The disciple said: I do not clearly grasp even the meaning of the words. How, then, can I understand the significance of the sentence "I am Brahman"?

### 10

सत्यमाह भवानत्र विगानं नैव विद्यते।  
हेतुः पदार्थबोधो हि वाक्यार्थावगतेरिह।।10।।

*Satyamāha bhavānatra vigānaṃ naiva vidyate.  
Hetuḥ padārthabodho hi vākyārthāvagateriha..10..*

The teacher said: "You spoke the truth in your complaint. Knowing and understanding the meaning of the words in a sentence is indeed what makes it possible to understand the sentence's full significance. There are no two opinions on this."

### 11

अन्तःकरणतद्वृत्तिसाक्षी चैतन्यविग्रहः।  
आनन्दरूपः सत्यः सन्किं नात्मानं प्रपद्यसे।।11।।

*Antaḥkaraṇatadvṛttisākṣī caitanyavigrahaḥ.  
Ānandarūpaḥ satyaḥ sankiṃ nātmānaṃ prapadyase..11..*

"Why do you not recognize your own Self, the very embodiment of Eternal Bliss-Essence, the Witnessing Light that illumines the inner instruments and their functions?"

### 12

सत्यानन्दस्वरूपं धीसाक्षिणं ज्ञानविग्रहम्।  
चिन्तयात्मतया नित्यं त्यक्त्वा देहादिगां धियम्।।12।।

*Satyānandasvarūpaṃ dhīsākṣiṇaṃ jñānavigraham.  
Cintayātmatayā nityaṃ tyaktvā dehādigāṃ dhiyam..12..*

"Drop the mistaken intellectual notion that the Self is the body and so on. Always meditate on yourself and think of yourself as eternal Knowledge-Bliss, the Witness of the intellect, a pure mass of Knowledge."

### 13

रूपादिमान्यतः पिण्डस्ततो नात्मा घटादिवत्।  
वियदादिमहाभूतविकारत्वाच्च कुम्भवत्।।13।।

*Rūpādimānyataḥ piṇḍastato nātmā ghaṭādivat.  
Viyadādimahābhūtavikāratvācca kumbhavat..13..*

"The body is not the Self. Like a pot and similar things, the body has form and other such features. And again like a pot, the body is a modification of the great elements, such as Akash."

### 14

अनात्मा यदि पिण्डोऽयमुक्तहेतुबलान्मतः।  
करामलकवत्साक्षादात्मानं प्रतिपादय।।14।।

*Anātmā yadi piṇḍo'yamuktahetubalānmataḥ.  
Karāmalakavatsākṣādātmānaṃ pratipādaya..14..*

The disciple said: "If these arguments establish that the gross body is 'not-Self', then please explain the Self fully and point to It directly, as clearly as a fruit held in the hand."

### 15

घटद्रष्टा घटाद्भिन्नः सर्वथा न घटो यथा।  
देहद्रष्टा तथा देहो नाहमित्यवधारय।।15।।

*Ghaṭadraṣṭā ghaṭādbhinnaḥ sarvathā na ghaṭo yathā.  
Dehadraṣṭā tathā deho nāhamityavadhāraya..15..*

The teacher said: "The one who perceives a pot is always clearly other than the pot and can never be the pot. In the same way, you who perceive your body are distinct from it and can never be the body. Settle this firmly within yourself."

### 16

एवमिन्द्रियदृङ् नाहमिन्द्रियाणीति निश्चिनु।  
मनो बुद्धिस्तथा प्राणो नाहमित्यवधारय।।16।।

*Evamindriyadṛṅ nāhamindriyāṇīti niścinu.  
Mano buddhistathā prāṇo nāhamityavadhāraya..16..*

"Likewise, be sure within yourself that you, the seer of the senses, are not the senses. Determine also that you are neither the mind, nor the intellect, nor the vital air (Prana)."

### 17

संघातोऽपि तथा नाहमिति दृश्यविलक्षणम्।  
द्रष्टारमनुमानेन निपुणं संप्रधारय।।17।।

*Saṃghāto'pi tathā nāhamiti dṛśyavilakṣaṇam.  
Draṣṭāramanumānena nipuṇaṃ saṃpradhāraya..17..*

"In the same way, be sure that you are not the combination of the gross and subtle bodies. Through intelligent inference, determine that you, the 'seer', are entirely distinct from the 'seen'."

### 18

देहेन्द्रियादयो भावा हानादिव्यापृतिक्षमाः।  
यस्य संनिधिमात्रेण सोऽहमित्यवधारय।।18।।

*Dehendriyādayo bhāvā hānādivyāpṛtikṣamāḥ.  
Yasya saṃnidhimātreṇa so'hamityavadhāraya..18..*

"'I am He', the One through whose presence alone inert things such as the body and the senses are able to function by accepting and rejecting."

### 19

अनापन्नविकारः सन्नयस्कान्तवदेव यः।  
बुद्ध्यादींश्चालयेत्प्रत्यक्सोऽहमित्यवधारय।।19।।

*Anāpannavikāraḥ sannayaskāntavadeva yaḥ.  
Buddhyādīṃścālayetpratyakso'hamityavadhāraya..19..*

"'I am He', the One changeless, Innermost Self that moves the intellect and the rest, as a magnet moves iron filings."

### 20

अजडात्मवदाभान्ति यत्सांनिध्याज्जडा अपि।  
देहेन्द्रियमनःप्राणाः सोऽहमित्यवधारय।।20।।

*Ajaḍātmavadābhānti yatsāṃnidhyājjaḍā api.  
Dehendriyamanaḥprāṇāḥ so'hamityavadhāraya..20..*

"'I am He', the One Entity in whose living presence the body, senses, mind, and Pranas, though inert in themselves, seem conscious and active, as if they were the Self."

### 21

अगमन्मे मनोऽन्यत्र सांप्रतं च स्थिरीकृतम्।  
एवं यो वेद धीवृत्तिं सोऽहमित्यवधारय।।21।।

*Agamanme mano'nyatra sāṃprataṃ ca sthirīkṛtam.  
Evaṃ yo veda dhīvṛttiṃ so'hamityavadhāraya..21..*

"'He am I', the One Consciousness, the Self that illumines such changes in my mind as 'my mind wandered elsewhere, but now it has been brought to rest'. 'He am I' (So'ham)."

### 22

स्वप्नजागरिते सुप्ति भावाभावौ धियां तथा।  
यो वेत्त्यविक्रियः साक्षात्सोऽहमित्यवधारय।।22।।

*Svapnajāgarite supti bhāvābhāvau dhiyāṃ tathā.  
Yo vettyavikriyaḥ sākṣātso'hamityavadhāraya..22..*

"'He am I', the One Consciousness, the Changeless Self that is known directly, that illumines the three states of waking, dream, and deep sleep, and that illumines the rise and fall of the intellect and its functions. 'He am I' (So'ham)."

### 23

घटावभासको दीपो घटादन्यो यथेष्यते।  
देहावभासको देही तथाहं बोधविग्रहः।।23।।

*Ghaṭāvabhāsako dīpo ghaṭādanyo yatheṣyate.  
Dehāvabhāsako dehī tathāhaṃ bodhavigrahaḥ..23..*

"Know yourself as the One Self, a uniform mass of Consciousness. It illumines the body and so is quite distinct from it, just as a lamp lighting a pot is always different from the pot it lights. 'I am a mass of Consciousness' (Aham bodhavigraha)."

### 24

पुत्रवित्तादयो भावा यस्य शेषतया प्रियाः।  
द्रष्टा सर्वप्रियतमः सोऽहमित्यवधारय।।24।।

*Putravittādayo bhāvā yasya śeṣatayā priyāḥ.  
Draṣṭā sarvapriyatamaḥ so'hamityavadhāraya..24..*

"Know yourself as the One for whose sake people and things such as children and wealth are dear, the sole seer and the dearest of all. 'He am I': determine this and realize So'ham."

### 25

परप्रेमास्पदतया मा न भूवमहं सदा।  
भूयासमिति यो द्रष्टा सोऽहमित्यवधारय।।25।।

*Parapremāspadatayā mā na bhūvamahaṃ sadā.  
Bhūyāsamiti yo draṣṭā so'hamityavadhāraya..25..*

"Know yourself as the One about whom there is always the concern, 'May I always be; may I never cease to be', because this Seer is the dearest of all. 'He am I': assert this and realize it."

### 26

यः साक्षिलक्षणो बोधस्त्वंपदार्थः स उच्यते।  
साक्षित्वमपि बोद्धृत्वमविकारितयात्मनः।।26।।

*Yaḥ sākṣilakṣaṇo bodhastvaṃpadārthaḥ sa ucyate.  
Sākṣitvamapi boddhṛtvamavikāritayātmanaḥ..26..*

"The Consciousness, the Self, which appears as the Witness, is what the word 'thou' refers to. Since It is free of all change, even the act of witnessing is nothing other than the Self's power to illumine."

### 27

देहेन्द्रियमनःप्राणाहंकृतिभ्यो विलक्षणः।  
प्रोज्झिताशेषषड्भावविकारस्त्वंपदाभिधः।।27।।

*Dehendriyamanaḥprāṇāhaṃkṛtibhyo vilakṣaṇaḥ.  
Projjhitāśeṣaṣaḍbhāvavikārastvaṃpadābhidhaḥ..27..*

"The Self is entirely distinct from the body, senses, mind, Prana, and Ego. It is therefore completely free from the six modifications that all material things must undergo. This Self is the indicative meaning of the term 'thou'."

### 28

त्वमर्थमेवं निश्चित्य तदर्थं चिन्तयेत्पुनः।  
अतद्व्यावृत्तिरूपेण साक्षाद्विधिमुखेन च।।28।।

*Tvamarthamevaṃ niścitya tadarthaṃ cintayetpunaḥ.  
Atadvyāvṛttirūpeṇa sākṣādvidhimukhena ca..28..*

"Having settled the meaning of the term 'thou' in this way, one should reflect on what the word 'that' means, using both the method of negation and the direct method of scriptural assertion."

### 29

निरस्ताशेषसंसारदोषोऽस्थूलादिलक्षणः।  
अदृश्यत्वादिगुणकः पराकृततमोमलः।।29।।

*Nirastāśeṣasaṃsāradoṣo'sthūlādilakṣaṇaḥ.  
Adṛśyatvādiguṇakaḥ parākṛtatamomalaḥ..29..*

"'That' which is free from all the impurities of Samsara, 'that' which the Upanishads define as 'not large' and so on, as having qualities such as imperceptibility, and which is beyond all the darkness produced by ignorance."

### 30

निरस्तातिशयानन्दः सत्यः प्रज्ञानविग्रहः।  
सत्तास्वलक्षणः पूर्णः परमात्मेति गीयते।।30।।

*Nirastātiśayānandaḥ satyaḥ prajñānavigrahaḥ.  
Sattāsvalakṣaṇaḥ pūrṇaḥ paramātmeti gīyate..30..*

"It has no bliss greater than Itself; It is a pure embodiment of Eternal Consciousness; 'existence' is its defining mark. This All-Pervading Being is what the term 'that' indicates. So the scriptures declare in their songs."

### 31

सर्वज्ञत्वं परेशत्वं तथा संपूर्णशक्तिता।  
वेदैः समर्थ्यते यस्य तद्ब्रह्मेत्यवधारय।।31।।

*Sarvajñatvaṃ pareśatvaṃ tathā saṃpūrṇaśaktitā.  
Vedaiḥ samarthyate yasya tadbrahmetyavadhāraya..31..*

"That which the Vedas establish as All-knowing, All-powerful, and the Supreme Lord is Itself the Infinite Brahman. Make sure of that Brahman in your own understanding."

### 32

यज्ज्ञानात्सर्वविज्ञानं श्रुतिषु प्रतिपादितम्।  
मृदाद्यनेकदृष्टान्तैस्तद्ब्रह्मेत्यवधारय।।32।।

*Yajjñānātsarvavijñānaṃ śrutiṣu pratipāditam.  
Mṛdādyanekadṛṣṭāntaistadbrahmetyavadhāraya..32..*

"That which the scriptures have described, using examples such as clay, as the one thing by knowing which all else becomes known: make sure of that Brahman in your understanding."

### 33

यदानन्त्यं प्रतिज्ञाय श्रुतिस्तत्सिद्धये जगौ।  
तत्कार्यत्वं प्रपञ्चस्य तद्ब्रह्मेत्यवधारय।।33।।

*Yadānantyaṃ pratijñāya śrutistatsiddhaye jagau.  
Tatkāryatvaṃ prapañcasya tadbrahmetyavadhāraya..33..*

"That which the scriptures set out to prove to be limitless, calling the World of Plurality Its effects in support of this claim: make sure of that Brahman in your understanding."

### 34

विजिज्ञास्यतया यच्च वेदान्तेषु मुमुक्षुभिः।  
समर्थ्यतेऽतियत्नेन तद्ब्रह्मेत्यवधारय।।34।।

*Vijijñāsyatayā yacca vedānteṣu mumukṣubhiḥ.  
Samarthyate'tiyatnena tadbrahmetyavadhāraya..34..*

"That which the Upanishads clearly establish as the only object of deep contemplation for sincere seekers of liberation: make sure of that Brahman in your understanding."

### 35

जीवात्मना प्रवेशश्च नियन्तृत्वं च तान्प्रति।  
श्रूयते यस्य वेदेषु तद्ब्रह्मेत्यवधारय।।35।।

*Jīvātmanā praveśaśca niyantṛtvaṃ ca tānprati.  
Śrūyate yasya vedeṣu tadbrahmetyavadhāraya..35..*

"That which the Vedas speak of as having entered each creature as its individual self, and which the same sources make known as their controller: make sure of that Brahman in your understanding."

### 36

कर्मणां फलदातृत्वं यस्यैव श्रूयते श्रुतौ।  
जीवानां हेतुकर्तृत्वं तद्ब्रह्मेत्यवधारय।।36।।

*Karmaṇāṃ phaladātṛtvaṃ yasyaiva śrūyate śrutau.  
Jīvānāṃ hetukartṛtvaṃ tadbrahmetyavadhāraya..36..*

"That which the Upanishads declare to be the sole giver of the rewards of all action, and the very agent (prompter) in every action done by each individual ego: make sure of that Brahman in your understanding."

### 37

तत्त्वंपदार्थौ निर्णीतौ वाक्यार्थश्चिन्त्यतेऽधुना।  
तादात्म्यमत्र वाक्यार्थस्तयोरेव पदार्थयोः।।37।।

*Tattvaṃpadārthau nirṇītau vākyārthaścintyate'dhunā.  
Tādātmyamatra vākyārthastayoreva padārthayoḥ..37..*

"The meanings of the terms 'that' and 'thou' have now been discussed and finally settled. Next we will discuss the meaning of the commandment (Mahavakya) 'That thou art'. It shows nothing but the complete identity of the meanings of 'that' and 'thou'."

### 38

संसर्गो वा विशिष्टो वा वाक्यार्थो नात्र संमतः।  
अखण्डैकरसत्वेन वाक्यार्थो विदुषां मतः।।38।।

*Saṃsargo vā viśiṣṭo vā vākyārtho nātra saṃmataḥ.  
Akhaṇḍaikarasatvena vākyārtho viduṣāṃ mataḥ..38..*

"The meaning of the sentence (the commandment 'That thou art') is not reached either through a 'sequence of meaning' or as something 'qualified by something else'. According to the wise, its only meaning is an indivisible Being consisting of Bliss alone."

### 39

प्रत्यग्बोधो य आभाति सोऽद्वयानन्दलक्षणः।  
अद्वयानन्दरूपश्च प्रत्यग्बोधैकलक्षणः।।39।।

*Pratyagbodho ya ābhāti so'dvayānandalakṣaṇaḥ.  
Advayānandarūpaśca pratyagbodhaikalakṣaṇaḥ..39..*

"What appears (anjati) within as the Witnessing Consciousness (the individual Self) has the nature of Bliss, One-without-a-second. And the Bliss within is nothing other than the individual Self, the Witnessing Consciousness within."

### 40

इत्थमन्योन्यतादात्म्यप्रतिपत्तिर्यदा भवेत्।  
अब्रह्मत्वं त्वमर्थस्य व्यावर्तेत तदैव हि।।40।।

*Itthamanyonyatādātmyapratipattiryadā bhavet.  
Abrahmatvaṃ tvamarthasya vyāvarteta tadaiva hi..40..*

"When the mutual identity of the two words 'thou' and 'that' is understood as explained above, the idea 'I am not Brahman', held by 'thou', comes to an immediate end."

### 41

तदर्थस्य च पारोक्ष्यं यद्येवं किं ततः श्रृणु।  
पूर्णानन्दैकरूपेण प्रत्यग्बोधोऽवतिष्ठते।।41।।

*Tadarthasya ca pārokṣyaṃ yadyevaṃ kiṃ tataḥ śrṛṇu.  
Pūrṇānandaikarūpeṇa pratyagbodho'vatiṣṭhate..41..*

"If, as stated, the deep meaning of the term 'that' is 'Mass of Bliss, without a second', and of 'thou' is 'Witnessing Consciousness', what follows? Listen: the Inner Self, the Consciousness that illumines all thoughts, abides as the All-full, One Mass of Bliss, without a second."

### 42

तत्त्वमस्यादिवाक्यं च तादात्म्यप्रतिपादने।  
लक्ष्यौ तत्त्वंपदार्थौ द्वावुपादाय प्रवर्तते।।42।।

*Tattvamasyādivākyaṃ ca tādātmyapratipādane.  
Lakṣyau tattvaṃpadārthau dvāvupādāya pravartate..42..*

"Great statements such as 'That thou art' establish the identity of what the two terms 'thou' and 'That' refer to in their deeper, indicative meaning."

### 43

हित्वा द्वौ शबलौ वाच्यौ वाक्यं वाक्यार्थबोधने।  
यथा प्रवर्ततेऽस्माभिस्तथा व्याख्यातमादरात्।।43।।

*Hitvā dvau śabalau vācyau vākyaṃ vākyārthabodhane.  
Yathā pravartate'smābhistathā vyākhyātamādarāt..43..*

"How the great statement sets aside the two qualified meanings and reveals what it truly means, this we have already commented on with care."

### 44

आलम्बनतया भाति योऽस्मत्प्रत्ययशब्दयोः।  
अन्तःकरणसंभिन्नबोधः स त्वंपदाभिधः।।44।।

*Ālambanatayā bhāti yo'smatpratyayaśabdayoḥ.  
Antaḥkaraṇasaṃbhinnabodhaḥ sa tvaṃpadābhidhaḥ..44..*

"That which shines as the object of the idea and the word 'I' is Consciousness expressing itself in the inner instruments. This is the direct word-meaning of 'thou' (twam)."

### 45

मायोपाधिर्जगद्योनिः सर्वज्ञत्वादिलक्षणः।  
पारोक्ष्यशबलः सत्याद्यात्मकस्तत्पदाभिधः।।45।।

*Māyopādhirjagadyoniḥ sarvajñatvādilakṣaṇaḥ.  
Pārokṣyaśabalaḥ satyādyātmakastatpadābhidhaḥ..45..*

"The Consciousness that is expressed through Maya and so becomes the 'cause of the Universe', that is described as omnipresent and so on, that is known only indirectly (meditate), and that has the nature of existence and the like: that Eswara is the word-meaning of the term 'That'."

### 46

प्रत्यक्परोक्षतैकस्य सद्वितीयत्वपूर्णता।  
विरुध्यते यतस्तस्माल्लक्षणा संप्रवर्तते।।46।।

*Pratyakparokṣataikasya sadvitīyatvapūrṇatā.  
Virudhyate yatastasmāllakṣaṇā saṃpravartate..46..*

"If we insist on the identity of 'that' and 'thou' on the basis of the word-meanings of these terms, we will have to assign opposite natures to one and the same thing: being known mediately and being known immediately, and also both 'the existence of duality' and 'absolute oneness'. Identity between such opposites is impossible, so the suggestive meaning, 'explanation by implication', has to be accepted."

### 47

मानान्तरविरोधे तु मुख्यार्थस्यापरिग्रहे।  
मुख्यार्थेनाविनाभूते प्रतीतिर्लक्षणोच्यते।।47।।

*Mānāntaravirodhe tu mukhyārthasyāparigrahe.  
Mukhyārthenāvinābhūte pratītirlakṣaṇocyate..47..*

"If the direct word-meaning conflicts with what other proofs and evidence show, then the sense consistent with that word-meaning, which the term intelligently suggests, is the one to accept. This is its suggestive meaning (lakshana)."

### 48

तत्त्वमस्यादिवाक्येषु लक्षणा भागलक्षणा।  
सोऽयमित्यादिवाक्यस्थपदयोरिव नापरा।।48।।

*Tattvamasyādivākyeṣu lakṣaṇā bhāgalakṣaṇā.  
So'yamityādivākyasthapadayoriva nāparā..48..*

"In statements such as 'that thou art', the method of rejecting and accepting must be used, as in the sentence 'He is this man'. No other method can be applied."

### 49

अहं ब्रह्मेति वाक्यार्थबोधो यावद्दृढीभवेत्।  
शमादिसहितस्तावदभ्यस्येच्छ्रवणादिकम्।।49।।

*Ahaṃ brahmeti vākyārthabodho yāvaddṛḍhībhavet.  
Śamādisahitastāvadabhyasyecchravaṇādikam..49..*

"Until the direct personal experience of 'I am Brahman' is gained, we must live by values such as self-control, and must keep up the practice of listening to teachers or reading the scriptures, and of daily reflection and meditation on those ideas."

### 50

श्रुत्याचार्यप्रसादेन दृढो बोधो यदा भवेत्।  
निरस्ताशेषसंसारनिदानः पुरुषस्तदा।।50।।

*Śrutyācāryaprasādena dṛḍho bodho yadā bhavet.  
Nirastāśeṣasaṃsāranidānaḥ puruṣastadā..50..*

"When, through the grace of a spiritual teacher, a seeker gains a clear and direct experience of the Supreme Self as taught in the scriptures, that realized person becomes free of all 'ignorance', the foundation of the entire experience of this world of plurality."

### 51

विशीर्णकार्यकरणो भूतसूक्ष्मैरनावृतः।  
विमुक्तकर्मनिगलः सद्य एव विमुच्यते।।51।।

*Viśīrṇakāryakaraṇo bhūtasūkṣmairanāvṛtaḥ.  
Vimuktakarmanigalaḥ sadya eva vimucyate..51..*

"No longer conditioned by the gross and subtle bodies, free from the embrace of the gross and subtle elements, and released from the charm of actions, such a person is liberated at once."

### 52

प्रारब्धकर्मवेगेण जीवन्मुक्तो यदा भवेत्।  
किञ्चित्कालमनारब्धकर्मबन्धस्य संक्षये।।52।।

*Prārabdhakarmavegeṇa jīvanmukto yadā bhavet.  
Kiñcitkālamanārabdhakarmabandhasya saṃkṣaye..52..*

"Because of the compelling force of those actions that have already begun to bear results (Prarabdha), the one liberated in life remains for some time until they are exhausted."

### 53

निरस्तातिशयानन्दं वैष्णवं परमं पदम्।  
पुनरावृत्तिरहितं कैवल्यं प्रतिपद्यते।।53।।

*Nirastātiśayānandaṃ vaiṣṇavaṃ paramaṃ padam.  
Punarāvṛttirahitaṃ kaivalyaṃ pratipadyate..53..*

"The one liberated in life reaches the State of Absolute Oneness, the endless, immeasurable Bliss known as the Supreme Abode of Vishnu, from which there is no return."

### 54

इति श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य  
श्रीगोविन्दभगवत्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य  
श्रीमच्छंकरभगवतः कृतौ  
वाक्यवृत्तिः समाप्ता।।

*Iti śrīmatparamahaṃsaparivrājakācāryasya  
Śrīgovindabhagavatpūjyapādaśiṣyasya  
Śrīmacchaṃkarabhagavataḥ kṛtau  
Vākyavṛttiḥ samāptā..*
