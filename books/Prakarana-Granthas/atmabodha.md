# Atmabodha

### 1

तपोभिः क्षीणपापानां शान्तानां वीतरागिणाम्  
मुमुक्षूणामपेक्ष्योऽयमात्मबोधो विधीयते ॥१॥

*tapobhiḥ kṣīṇapāpānāṃ śāntānāṃ vītarāgiṇām  
mumukṣūṇāmapekṣyo'yamātmabodho vidhīyate ..1..*

I am writing the ATMA-BODHA, this treatise on Knowledge of the Self, for those who have been purified by austerities, who are calm and peaceful in heart, who have no cravings, and who long for liberation.

### 2

बोधोऽन्यसाधनेभ्यो हि साक्षान्मोक्षैकसाधनम्  
पाकस्य वह्निवज्ज्ञानं विना मोक्षो न सिध्यति ॥२॥

*bodho'nyasādhanebhyo hi sākṣānmokṣaikasādhanam  
pākasya vahnivajjñānaṃ vinā mokṣo na sidhyati ..2..*

Fire is what directly does the cooking; in the same way, there is no emancipation without Knowledge. Among all the forms of discipline, Knowledge of the Self is the one direct means to liberation.

### 3

अविरोधितया कर्म नाविद्यां विनिवर्तयेत्  
विद्याऽविद्यां निहन्त्येव तेजस्तिमिरसङ्घवत् ॥३॥

*avirodhitayā karma nāvidyāṃ vinivartayet  
vidyā'vidyāṃ nihantyeva tejastimirasaṅghavat ..3..*

Action cannot remove ignorance, because action is not opposed to it or in conflict with it. Knowledge does remove ignorance, as light removes deep darkness.

### 4

परिच्छिन्न इवाज्ञानात्तन्नाशे सति केवलः  
स्वयं प्रकाशते ह्यात्मा मेघापायेंऽशुमानिव ॥४॥

*paricchinna ivājñānāttannāśe sati kevalaḥ  
svayaṃ prakāśate hyātmā meghāpāyeṃ'śumāniva ..4..*

Ignorance makes the Soul seem finite. Once ignorance is gone, the Self, which allows no multiplicity, shows itself by itself, as the Sun does when the clouds drift away.

### 5

अज्ञानकलुषं जीवं ज्ञानाभ्यासाद्विनिर्मलम्  
कृत्वा ज्ञानं स्वयं नश्येज्जलं कतकरेणुवत् ॥५॥

*ajñānakaluṣaṃ jīvaṃ jñānābhyāsādvinirmalam  
kṛtvā jñānaṃ svayaṃ naśyejjalaṃ katakareṇuvat ..5..*

Steady practice of knowledge cleanses the Self ('Jivatman') of the stain of ignorance, and then knowledge itself vanishes, as the powder of the 'Kataka-nut' sinks away after clearing muddy water.

### 6

संसारः स्वप्नतुल्यो हि रागद्वेषादिसङ्कुलः  
स्वकाले सत्यवद्भाति प्रबोधे सत्यसद्भवेत् ॥६॥

*saṃsāraḥ svapnatulyo hi rāgadveṣādisaṅkulaḥ  
svakāle satyavadbhāti prabodhe satyasadbhavet ..6..*

This world, filled with attachments, aversions and the like, resembles a dream. While it lasts it seems real; on waking (when true wisdom dawns) it is seen to be unreal.

### 7

तावत्सत्यं जगद्भाति शुक्तिकारजतं यथा  
यावन्न ज्ञायते ब्रह्म सर्वाधिष्ठानमद्वयम् ॥७॥

*tāvatsatyaṃ jagadbhāti śuktikārajataṃ yathā  
yāvanna jñāyate brahma sarvādhiṣṭhānamadvayam ..7..*

The Jagat seems true (Satyam) only until Brahman, the substratum and basis of this whole creation, is realised. It is like silver falsely seen in mother-of-pearl.

### 8

उपादानेऽखिलाधारे जगन्ति परमेश्वरे  
सर्गस्थितिलयान् यान्ति बुद्बुदानीव वारिणि ॥८॥

*upādāne'khilādhāre jaganti parameśvare  
sargasthitilayān yānti budbudānīva vāriṇi ..8..*

The worlds arise, remain and dissolve in the Supreme Self, the material cause and support of everything, just as bubbles do in water.

### 9

सच्चिदात्मन्यनुस्यूते नित्ये विष्णौ प्रकल्पिताः  
व्यक्तयो विविधाः सर्वा हाटके कटकादिवत् ॥९॥

*saccidātmanyanusyūte nitye viṣṇau prakalpitāḥ  
vyaktayo vividhāḥ sarvā hāṭake kaṭakādivat ..9..*

This whole manifested world of things and beings is projected by imagination onto its substratum, the Eternal All-pervading Vishnu, whose nature is Existence-Intelligence. In the same way, many different ornaments are all made of one gold.

### 10

यथाकाशो हृषीकेशो नानोपाधिगतो विभुः  
तद्भेदाद्भिन्नवद्भाति तन्नाशे केवलो भवेत् ॥१०॥

*yathākāśo hṛṣīkeśo nānopādhigato vibhuḥ  
tadbhedādbhinnavadbhāti tannāśe kevalo bhavet ..10..*

The All-pervading Akasa seems varied because it is associated with various conditionings (Upadhis), each different from the others; when those limiting adjuncts are destroyed, space is one. The Omnipresent Truth is the same: it seems varied through its association with the various Upadhis, and it becomes one when they are destroyed.

### 11

नानोपाधिवशादेव जातिवर्णाश्रमादयः  
आत्मन्यारोपितास्तोये रसवर्णादि भेदवत् ॥११॥

*nānopādhivaśādeva jātivarṇāśramādayaḥ  
ātmanyāropitāstoye rasavarṇādi bhedavat ..11..*

Through association with different conditionings (Upadhis), ideas such as caste, colour and position get superimposed on the Atman, just as flavour, colour and so on get superimposed on water.

### 12

पंचीकृतमहाभूतसंभवं कर्मसंचितम्  
शरीरं सुखदुःखानां भोगायतनमुच्यते ॥१२॥

*paṃcīkṛtamahābhūtasaṃbhavaṃ karmasaṃcitam  
śarīraṃ sukhaduḥkhānāṃ bhogāyatanamucyate ..12..*

The gross body is the tent of experiences, the medium through which pleasure and pain are felt. Each person's past actions determine it, and it is made of the Five elements after they have undergone "five-fold self-division and mutual combination" (Pancheekarana).

### 13

पंचप्राणमनोबुद्धिदशेन्द्रियसमन्वितम्  
अपंचीकृतभूतोत्थं सूक्ष्माङ्गं भोगसाधनम् ॥१३॥

*paṃcaprāṇamanobuddhidaśendriyasamanvitam  
apaṃcīkṛtabhūtotthaṃ sūkṣmāṅgaṃ bhogasādhanam ..13..*

The five Pranas, the ten organs, the Manas and the Buddhi are formed from the rudimentary elements (Tanmatras) before their "five-fold division and mutual combination with one another" (Pancheekarana). Together these are the subtle body, the individual's instruments of experience.

### 14

अनाद्यविद्याऽनिर्वाच्या कारणोपाधिरुच्यते  
उपाधित्रितयादन्यमात्मानमवधारयेत् ॥१४॥

*anādyavidyā'nirvācyā kāraṇopādhirucyate  
upādhitritayādanyamātmānamavadhārayet ..14..*

Avidya, indescribable and without beginning, is the Causal Body. Be certain that the Atman is something other than these three conditioning bodies (Upadhis).

### 15

पंचकोशादियोगेन तत्तन्मय इव स्थितः  
शुद्धात्मा नीलवस्त्रादियोगेन स्फटिको यथा ॥१५॥

*paṃcakośādiyogena tattanmaya iva sthitaḥ  
śuddhātmā nīlavastrādiyogena sphaṭiko yathā ..15..*

When identified with the five sheaths, the Immaculate Atman seems to have taken on their qualities, as a crystal seems to take on the colour of whatever is near it (blue cloth and so on).

### 16

वपुस्तुषादिभिः कोशैर्युक्तं युक्त्यवघाततः  
आत्मानमन्तरं शुद्धं विविच्यात्तण्डुलं यथा ॥१६॥

*vapustuṣādibhiḥ kośairyuktaṃ yuktyavaghātataḥ  
ātmānamantaraṃ śuddhaṃ vivicyāttaṇḍulaṃ yathā ..16..*

By discriminating self-analysis and logical thought, separate the Pure Self within from the sheaths, the way rice is separated from the husk, bran and other coverings.

### 17

सदा सर्वगतोऽप्यात्मा न सर्वत्रावभासते  
बुद्धावेवावभासेत स्वच्छेषु प्रतिबिम्बवत् ॥१७॥

*sadā sarvagato'pyātmā na sarvatrāvabhāsate  
buddhāvevāvabhāseta svaccheṣu pratibimbavat ..17..*

Though All-pervading, the Atman does not shine in everything. It shows itself only in the inner equipment, the intellect (Buddhi), like a reflection in a clean mirror.

### 18

देहेन्द्रियमनोबुद्धिप्रकृतिभ्यो विलक्षणम्  
तद्वृत्तिसाक्षिणं विद्यादात्मानं राजवत्सदा ॥१८॥

*dehendriyamanobuddhiprakṛtibhyo vilakṣaṇam  
tadvṛttisākṣiṇaṃ vidyādātmānaṃ rājavatsadā ..18..*

Understand that the Atman is always like the King: separate from the body, senses, mind and intellect, which together make up matter (Prakriti), and the witness of what they do.

### 19

व्यापृतेष्विन्द्रियेष्वात्मा व्यापारीवाविवेकिनाम्  
दृश्यतेऽभ्रेषु धावत्सु धावन्निव यथा शशी ॥१९॥

*vyāpṛteṣvindriyeṣvātmā vyāpārīvāvivekinām  
dṛśyate'bhreṣu dhāvatsu dhāvanniva yathā śaśī ..19..*

When clouds move across the sky, the moon seems to be racing along. Likewise, to someone without discrimination, the Atman seems active when viewed through the workings of the sense organs.

### 20

आत्मचैतन्यमाश्रित्य देहेन्द्रियमनोधियः।  
स्वकीयार्थेषु वर्तन्ते सूर्यालोकं यथा जनाः ॥२०॥

*ātmacaitanyamāśritya dehendriyamanodhiyaḥ.  
svakīyārtheṣu vartante sūryālokaṃ yathā janāḥ ..20..*

The body, senses, mind and intellect carry out their separate activities by relying on the vital energy of Consciousness (Atma Chaitanya), just as people do their work by relying on the light of the Sun.

### 21

देहेन्द्रियगुणान्कर्माण्यमले सच्चिदात्मनि  
अध्यस्यन्त्यविवेकेन गगने नीलतादिवत् ॥२१॥

*dehendriyaguṇānkarmāṇyamale saccidātmani  
adhyasyantyavivekena gagane nīlatādivat ..21..*

Lacking the power of discrimination, fools superimpose all the varied functions of the body and senses on the Atman, the Absolute Existence-Knowledge (Sat-Chit), just as they ascribe blue colour and the like to the sky.

### 22

अज्ञानान्मानसोपाधेः कर्तृत्वादीनि चात्मनि।  
कल्प्यन्तेऽम्बुगते चन्द्रे चलनादि यथाम्भसः ॥२२॥

*ajñānānmānasopādheḥ kartṛtvādīni cātmani.  
kalpyante'mbugate candre calanādi yathāmbhasaḥ ..22..*

Out of ignorance, the rippling that belongs to the water is credited to the moon reflected and dancing on it. In the same deluded way, being the doer, being the enjoyer and other limitations, which really belong to the mind, are taken to be the nature of the Self (Atman).

### 23

रागेच्छासुखदुःखादि बुद्धौ सत्यां प्रवर्तते  
सुषुप्तौ नास्ति तन्नाशे तस्माद्बुद्धेस्तु नात्मनः ॥२३॥

*rāgecchāsukhaduḥkhādi buddhau satyāṃ pravartate  
suṣuptau nāsti tannāśe tasmādbuddhestu nātmanaḥ ..23..*

Attachment, desire, pleasure, pain and the rest are noticed for as long as the Buddhi or mind is functioning. In deep sleep, when the mind ceases to exist, they are not noticed. So they belong only to the mind and not to the Atman.

### 24

प्रकाशोऽर्कस्य तोयस्य शैत्यमग्नेर्यथोष्णता  
स्वभावः सच्चिदानन्दनित्यनिर्मलतात्मनः ॥२४॥

*prakāśo'rkasya toyasya śaityamagneryathoṣṇatā  
svabhāvaḥ saccidānandanityanirmalatātmanaḥ ..24..*

Luminosity is the nature of the Sun, coolness the nature of water, and heat the nature of fire; in the same way, the nature of the Atman is Eternity, Purity, Reality, Consciousness and Bliss.

### 25

आत्मनः सच्चिदंशश्च बुद्धेर्वृत्तिरिति द्वयम्  
संयोज्य चाविवेकेन जानामीति प्रवर्तते ॥२५॥

*ātmanaḥ saccidaṃśaśca buddhervṛttiriti dvayam  
saṃyojya cāvivekena jānāmīti pravartate ..25..*

The notion "I know" comes from confusedly blending two things: the Existence-Knowledge aspect of the Self and the thought-wave of the intellect.

### 26

आत्मनो विक्रिया नास्ति बुद्धेर्बोधो न जात्विति  
जीवः सर्वमलं ज्ञात्वा ज्ञाता द्रष्टेति मुह्यति ॥२६॥

*ātmano vikriyā nāsti buddherbodho na jātviti  
jīvaḥ sarvamalaṃ jñātvā jñātā draṣṭeti muhyati ..26..*

The Atman never does anything, and the intellect has no power of its own to experience 'I know.' Yet the individuality in us mistakenly believes that it is the seer and the knower.

### 27

रज्जुसर्पवदात्मानं जीवं ज्ञात्वा भयं वहेत्  
नाहं जीवः परात्मेति ज्ञातं चेन्निर्भयो भवेत् ॥२७॥

*rajjusarpavadātmānaṃ jīvaṃ jñātvā bhayaṃ vahet  
nāhaṃ jīvaḥ parātmeti jñātaṃ cennirbhayo bhavet ..27..*

A person who takes a rope for a snake is seized by fear; so is one who takes himself to be the ego (Jiva). The ego-centred individuality in us becomes fearless again on realising that It is not a Jiva but is Itself the Supreme Soul.

### 28

आत्मावभासयत्येको बुद्ध्यादीनीन्द्रियाण्यपि  
दीपो घटादिवत्स्वात्मा जडैस्तैर्नावभास्यते ॥२८॥

*ātmāvabhāsayatyeko buddhyādīnīndriyāṇyapi  
dīpo ghaṭādivatsvātmā jaḍaistairnāvabhāsyate ..28..*

As a lamp lights up a jar or a pot, the Atman lights up the mind, the sense organs and so on. Being inert, these material objects cannot light up themselves.

### 29

स्वबोधे नान्यबोधेच्छा बोधरूपतयात्मनः  
न दीपस्यान्यदीपेच्छा यथा स्वात्मप्रकाशने ॥२९॥

*svabodhe nānyabodhecchā bodharūpatayātmanaḥ  
na dīpasyānyadīpecchā yathā svātmaprakāśane ..29..*

A burning lamp needs no second lamp to reveal its light. So too the Atman, being Knowledge itself, needs no other knowledge in order to be known.

### 30

निषिध्य निखिलोपाधीन्नेति नेतीति वाक्यतः  
विद्यादैक्यं महावाक्यैर्जीवात्मपरमात्मनोः ॥३०॥

*niṣidhya nikhilopādhīnneti netīti vākyataḥ  
vidyādaikyaṃ mahāvākyairjīvātmaparamātmanoḥ ..30..*

By negating the conditionings (Upadhis) with the help of the scriptural statement 'It is not this, It is not this,' one must realise the oneness of the individual soul and the Supreme Soul, as the great Mahavakyas point out.

### 31

आविद्यकं शरीरादि दृश्यं बुद्बुदवत्क्षरम्  
एतद्विलक्षणं विद्यादहं ब्रह्मेति निर्मलम् ॥३१॥

*āvidyakaṃ śarīrādi dṛśyaṃ budbudavatkṣaram  
etadvilakṣaṇaṃ vidyādahaṃ brahmeti nirmalam ..31..*

The body and everything else up to the "Causal Body" (Ignorance) are objects of perception, and they perish as easily as bubbles. Through discrimination, realise: I am the 'Pure Brahman,' forever entirely apart from all of these.

### 32

देहान्यत्वान्न मे जन्मजराकार्श्यलयादयः  
शब्दादिविषयैः सङ्गो निरिन्द्रियतया न च ॥३२॥

*dehānyatvānna me janmajarākārśyalayādayaḥ  
śabdādiviṣayaiḥ saṅgo nirindriyatayā na ca ..32..*

I am not the body, and therefore birth, wrinkling, senility, death and such changes do not touch me. Sense objects such as sound and taste have nothing to do with me, because I have no sense organs.

### 33

अमनस्त्वान्न मे दुःखरागद्वेषभयादयः  
अप्राणो ह्यमनाः शुभ्र इत्यादि श्रुतिशासनात् ॥३३॥

*amanastvānna me duḥkharāgadveṣabhayādayaḥ  
aprāṇo hyamanāḥ śubhra ityādi śrutiśāsanāt ..33..*

I am not the mind, and so I am free of sorrow, attachment, malice and fear. For the great scripture, the Upanishads, commands: "HE is without breath and without mind, Pure, etc."

### 34

निर्गुणो निष्क्रियो नित्यो निर्विकल्पो निरंजनः  
निर्विकारो निराकारो नित्यमुक्तोऽस्मि निर्मलः ॥३४॥

*nirguṇo niṣkriyo nityo nirvikalpo niraṃjanaḥ  
nirvikāro nirākāro nityamukto'smi nirmalaḥ ..34..*

I have no attributes and no actions. I am Eternal (Nitya), with no desire or thought (Nirvikalpa), no stain (Niranjana), no change (Nirvikara) and no form (Nirakara); I am ever-liberated (Nitya Mukta) and ever-pure (Nirmala).

### 35

अहमाकाशवत्सर्वं बहिरन्तर्गतोऽच्युतः  
सदा सर्वसमः सिद्धो निःसङ्गो निर्मलोऽचलः ॥३५॥

*ahamākāśavatsarvaṃ bahirantargato'cyutaḥ  
sadā sarvasamaḥ siddho niḥsaṅgo nirmalo'calaḥ ..35..*

Like space, I fill everything inside and out. Unchanging and the same in all, I am at all times pure, unattached, stainless and motionless.

### 36

नित्यशुद्धविमुक्तैकमखण्डानन्दमद्वयम्  
सत्यं ज्ञानमनन्तं यत्परं ब्रह्माहमेव तत् ॥३६॥

*nityaśuddhavimuktaikamakhaṇḍānandamadvayam  
satyaṃ jñānamanantaṃ yatparaṃ brahmāhameva tat ..36..*

I truly am that Supreme Brahman alone: Eternal, Pure and Free, One, indivisible and non-dual, whose nature is Changeless Knowledge and Infinity.

### 37

एवं निरन्तराभ्यस्ता ब्रह्मैवास्मीति वासना  
हरत्यविद्याविक्षेपान् रोगानिव रसायनम् ॥३७॥

*evaṃ nirantarābhyastā brahmaivāsmīti vāsanā  
haratyavidyāvikṣepān rogāniva rasāyanam ..37..*

The impression "I am Brahman," built up in this way through constant practice, wipes out ignorance and the agitation it brings, as medicine or Rasayana wipes out disease.

### 38

विविक्तदेश आसीनो विरागो विजितेन्द्रियः  
भावयेदेकमात्मानं तमनन्तमनन्यधीः ॥३८॥

*viviktadeśa āsīno virāgo vijitendriyaḥ  
bhāvayedekamātmānaṃ tamanantamananyadhīḥ ..38..*

Sit in a solitary place, clear the mind of desires, restrain the senses, and meditate with steady attention on the Atman, which is One without a second.

### 39

आत्मन्येवाखिलं दृश्यं प्रविलाप्य धिया सुधीः  
भावयेदेकमात्मानं निर्मलाकाशवत्सदा ॥३९॥

*ātmanyevākhilaṃ dṛśyaṃ pravilāpya dhiyā sudhīḥ  
bhāvayedekamātmānaṃ nirmalākāśavatsadā ..39..*

The wise person should intelligently merge the whole world of objects in the Atman alone, and should constantly think of the Self as never tainted by anything, like space.

### 40

रूपवर्णादिकं सर्वं विहाय परमार्थवित्  
परिपूर्णचिदानन्दस्वरूपेणावतिष्ठते ॥४०॥

*rūpavarṇādikaṃ sarvaṃ vihāya paramārthavit  
paripūrṇacidānandasvarūpeṇāvatiṣṭhate ..40..*

One who has realised the Supreme lets go of all identification with objects of name and form. From then on he lives as an embodiment of Infinite Consciousness and Bliss. He becomes the Self.

### 41

ज्ञातृज्ञानज्ञेयभेदः परे नात्मनि विद्यते  
चिदानन्दैकरूपत्वाद्दीप्यते स्वयमेव तत् ॥४१॥

*jñātṛjñānajñeyabhedaḥ pare nātmani vidyate  
cidānandaikarūpatvāddīpyate svayameva tat ..41..*

In the Supreme Self there are no distinctions such as "Knower," "Knowledge" and "Object of Knowledge." Being endless Bliss by nature, It permits no such divisions within Itself. It alone shines by Itself.

### 42

एवमात्मारणौ ध्यानमथने सततं कृते  
उदितावगतिर्ज्वाला सर्वाज्ञानेन्धनं दहेत् ॥४२॥

*evamātmāraṇau dhyānamathane satataṃ kṛte  
uditāvagatirjvālā sarvājñānendhanaṃ dahet ..42..*

When the lower and higher aspects of the Self are thoroughly churned together, the fire of knowledge is born. Its great blaze will consume all the fuel of ignorance in us.

### 43

अरुणेनेव बोधेन पूर्वं सन्तमसे हृते  
तत आविर्भवेदात्मा स्वयमेवांशुमानिव ॥४३॥

*aruṇeneva bodhena pūrvaṃ santamase hṛte  
tata āvirbhavedātmā svayamevāṃśumāniva ..43..*

The Lord of early dawn (Aruna) has already carried off the thick darkness by the time the sun soon rises. In the same way, the Divine Consciousness of the Self rises once right knowledge has already slain the darkness in the heart.

### 44

आत्मा तु सततं प्राप्तोऽप्यप्राप्तवदविद्यया  
तन्नाशे प्राप्तवद्भाति स्वकण्ठाभरणं यथा ॥४४॥

*ātmā tu satataṃ prāpto'pyaprāptavadavidyayā  
tannāśe prāptavadbhāti svakaṇṭhābharaṇaṃ yathā ..44..*

The Atman is a Reality that is always present, yet ignorance keeps it from being realised. When ignorance is destroyed, the Atman is realised. It is like an ornament thought lost while it is still on one's own neck.

### 45

स्थाणौ पुरुषवद्भ्रान्त्या कृता ब्रह्मणि जीवता  
जीवस्य तात्त्विके रूपे तस्मिन्दृष्टे निवर्तते ॥४५॥

*sthāṇau puruṣavadbhrāntyā kṛtā brahmaṇi jīvatā  
jīvasya tāttvike rūpe tasmindṛṣṭe nivartate ..45..*

Because of ignorance, Brahman seems to be a 'Jiva,' as a post seems to be a ghost. When the real nature of the 'Jiva' is realised as the Self, the ego-centred individuality is destroyed.

### 46

तत्त्वस्वरूपानुभवादुत्पन्नं ज्ञानमंजसा  
अहं ममेति चाज्ञानं बाधते दिग्भ्रमादिवत् ॥४६॥

*tattvasvarūpānubhavādutpannaṃ jñānamaṃjasā  
ahaṃ mameti cājñānaṃ bādhate digbhramādivat ..46..*

Ignorance, marked by the notions 'I' and 'Mine,' is destroyed by the knowledge that comes from realising the true nature of the Self, just as correct information removes a mistaken idea about the directions.

### 47

सम्यग्विज्ञानवान् योगी स्वात्मन्येवाखिलं जगत्  
एकं च सर्वमात्मानमीक्षते ज्ञानचक्षुषा ॥४७॥

*samyagvijñānavān yogī svātmanyevākhilaṃ jagat  
ekaṃ ca sarvamātmānamīkṣate jñānacakṣuṣā ..47..*

The Yogi of perfect realisation and enlightenment sees, through his "eye of wisdom" (Gyana Chakshush), the whole universe in his own Self, and regards everything as his own Self and nothing else.

### 48

आत्मैवेदं जगत्सर्वमात्मनोऽन्यन्न विद्यते  
मृदो यद्वद्घटादीनि स्वात्मानं सर्वमीक्षते ॥४८॥

*ātmaivedaṃ jagatsarvamātmano'nyanna vidyate  
mṛdo yadvadghaṭādīni svātmānaṃ sarvamīkṣate ..48..*

Nothing at all exists apart from the Atman; the tangible universe truly is Atman. Pots and jars are made of clay and cannot be called anything but clay. So too, for the enlightened soul, all that is perceived is the Self.

### 49

जीवन्मुक्तस्तु तद्विद्वान्पूर्वोपाधिगुणांस्त्यजेत्  
सच्चिदानन्दरूपत्वात् भवेद्भ्रमरकीटवत् ॥४९॥

*jīvanmuktastu tadvidvānpūrvopādhiguṇāṃstyajet  
saccidānandarūpatvāt bhavedbhramarakīṭavat ..49..*

A liberated person who has Self-knowledge gives up the traits of the equipments (Upadhis) already described. Because his nature is Sat-chit-ananda, he truly becomes Brahman, as the worm grows into a wasp.

### 50

तीर्त्वा मोहार्णवं हत्वा रागद्वेषादिराक्षसान्  
योगी शान्तिसमायुक्त आत्मारामो विराजते ॥५०॥

*tīrtvā mohārṇavaṃ hatvā rāgadveṣādirākṣasān  
yogī śāntisamāyukta ātmārāmo virājate ..50..*

Having crossed the ocean of delusion and slain the monsters of likes and dislikes, the Yogi who is united with peace lives in the glory of his own realised Self, as an Atmaram.

### 51

बाह्यानित्यसुखासक्तिं हित्वात्मसुखनिर्वृतः  
घटस्थदीपवत्स्वस्थः स्वान्तरेव प्रकाशते ॥५१॥

*bāhyānityasukhāsaktiṃ hitvātmasukhanirvṛtaḥ  
ghaṭasthadīpavatsvasthaḥ svāntareva prakāśate ..51..*

The Jivan Mukta, abiding in himself, drops all attachment to illusory external happiness. Content with the bliss that comes from the Atman, he shines within like a lamp set inside a jar.

### 52

उपाधिस्थोऽपि तद्धर्मैरलिप्तो व्योमवन्मुनिः  
सर्वविन्मूढवत्तिष्ठेदसक्तो वायुवच्चरेत् ॥५२॥

*upādhistho'pi taddharmairalipto vyomavanmuniḥ  
sarvavinmūḍhavattiṣṭhedasakto vāyuvaccaret ..52..*

Though he lives in the conditionings (Upadhis), the contemplative one always stays unconcerned with anything; or he may wander about like the wind, wholly unattached.

### 53

उपाधिविलयाद्विष्णौ निर्विशेषं विशेन्मुनिः  
जले जलं वियद्व्योम्नि तेजस्तेजसि वा यथा ॥५३॥

*upādhivilayādviṣṇau nirviśeṣaṃ viśenmuniḥ  
jale jalaṃ viyadvyomni tejastejasi vā yathā ..53..*

When the Upadhis are destroyed, the contemplative one is completely absorbed in 'Vishnu,' the All-pervading Spirit, like water into water, space into space and light into light.

### 54

यल्लाभान्नापरो लाभो यत्सुखान्नापरं सुखम्  
यज्ज्ञानान्नापरं ज्ञानं तद्ब्रह्मेत्यवधारयेत् ॥५४॥

*yallābhānnāparo lābho yatsukhānnāparaṃ sukham  
yajjñānānnāparaṃ jñānaṃ tadbrahmetyavadhārayet ..54..*

Realise That to be Brahman: gaining it leaves nothing further to gain, its blessedness leaves no other blessing to wish for, and knowing it leaves nothing further to know.

### 55

यद्दृष्ट्वा नापरं दृश्यं यद्भूत्वा न पुनर्भवः  
यज्ज्ञात्वा नापरं ज्ञेयं तद्ब्रह्मेत्यवधारयेत् ॥५५॥

*yaddṛṣṭvā nāparaṃ dṛśyaṃ yadbhūtvā na punarbhavaḥ  
yajjñātvā nāparaṃ jñeyaṃ tadbrahmetyavadhārayet ..55..*

Realise that to be Brahman: once it is seen, nothing is left to see; once one has become it, one is not born again in this world; once it is known, nothing else is left to know.

### 56

तिर्यगूर्ध्वमधः पूर्णं सच्चिदानन्दमद्वयम्  
अनन्तं नित्यमेकं यत्तद्ब्रह्मेत्यवधारयेत् ॥५६॥

*tiryagūrdhvamadhaḥ pūrṇaṃ saccidānandamadvayam  
anantaṃ nityamekaṃ yattadbrahmetyavadhārayet ..56..*

Realise that to be Brahman which is Existence-Knowledge-Bliss-Absolute, Non-dual, Infinite, Eternal and One, and which fills all the quarters, above, below and everything between.

### 57

अतद्व्यावृत्तिरूपेण वेदान्तैर्लक्ष्यतेऽद्वयम्  
अखण्डानन्दमेकं यत्तद्ब्रह्मेत्यवधारयेत् ॥५७॥

*atadvyāvṛttirūpeṇa vedāntairlakṣyate'dvayam  
akhaṇḍānandamekaṃ yattadbrahmetyavadhārayet ..57..*

Realise that to be Brahman which is Non-dual, Indivisible, One and Blissful, and which Vedanta points to as the Immutable Substratum, realised after every tangible object has been negated.

### 58

अखण्डानन्दरूपस्य तस्यानन्दलवाश्रिताः  
ब्रह्माद्यास्तारतम्येन भवन्त्यानन्दिनोऽखिलाः ॥५८॥

*akhaṇḍānandarūpasya tasyānandalavāśritāḥ  
brahmādyāstāratamyena bhavantyānandino'khilāḥ ..58..*

Deities such as Brahma taste no more than a particle of the unlimited Bliss of Brahman, and they enjoy it according to their share of that particle.

### 59

तद्युक्तमखिलं वस्तु व्यवहारस्तदन्वितः  
तस्मात्सर्वगतं ब्रह्म क्षीरे सर्पिरिवाखिले ॥५९॥

*tadyuktamakhilaṃ vastu vyavahārastadanvitaḥ  
tasmātsarvagataṃ brahma kṣīre sarpirivākhile ..59..*

Brahman pervades all objects, and all actions are possible because of Brahman. So Brahman permeates everything, as butter permeates milk.

### 60

अनण्वस्थूलमह्रस्वमदीर्घमजमव्ययम्  
अरूपगुणवर्णाख्यं तद्ब्रह्मेत्यवधारयेत् ॥६०॥

*anaṇvasthūlamahrasvamadīrghamajamavyayam  
arūpaguṇavarṇākhyaṃ tadbrahmetyavadhārayet ..60..*

Realise that to be Brahman which is neither subtle nor gross, neither short nor long, without birth or change, and without form, qualities, colour or name.

### 61

यद्भासा भास्यतेऽर्कादि भास्यैर्यत्तु न भास्यते  
येन सर्वमिदं भाति तद्ब्रह्मेत्यवधारयेत् ॥६१॥

*yadbhāsā bhāsyate'rkādi bhāsyairyattu na bhāsyate  
yena sarvamidaṃ bhāti tadbrahmetyavadhārayet ..61..*

Realise that to be Brahman by whose light shining orbs such as the Sun and the Moon are lit up, while their light does not light it up.

### 62

स्वयमन्तर्बहिर्व्याप्य भासयन्नखिलं जगत्  
ब्रह्म प्रकाशते वह्निप्रतप्तायसपिण्डवत् ॥६२॥

*svayamantarbahirvyāpya bhāsayannakhilaṃ jagat  
brahma prakāśate vahniprataptāyasapiṇḍavat ..62..*

The Supreme Brahman fills the whole universe, outside and inside, and shines of Itself, like the fire that saturates a red-hot iron ball and glows by itself.

### 63

जगद्विलक्षणं ब्रह्म ब्रह्मणोऽन्यन्न किंचन  
ब्रह्मान्यद्भाति चेन्मिथ्या यथा मरुमरीचिका ॥६३॥

*jagadvilakṣaṇaṃ brahma brahmaṇo'nyanna kiṃcana  
brahmānyadbhāti cenmithyā yathā marumarīcikā ..63..*

Brahman is other than this universe, and yet nothing exists that is not Brahman. If anything besides Brahman seems to exist, it is as unreal as a mirage.

### 64

दृश्यते श्रूयते यद्यद्ब्रह्मणोऽन्यन्न तद्भवेत्  
तत्त्वज्ञानाच्च तद्ब्रह्म सच्चिदानन्दमद्वयम् ॥६४॥

*dṛśyate śrūyate yadyadbrahmaṇo'nyanna tadbhavet  
tattvajñānācca tadbrahma saccidānandamadvayam ..64..*

Everything seen or heard is Brahman and nothing else. On gaining knowledge of the Reality, one sees the Universe as the non-dual Brahman, Existence-Knowledge-Bliss-Absolute.

### 65

सर्वगं सच्चिदात्मानं ज्ञानचक्षुर्निरीक्षते  
अज्ञानचक्षुर्नेक्षेत भास्वन्तं भानुमन्धवत् ॥६५॥

*sarvagaṃ saccidātmānaṃ jñānacakṣurnirīkṣate  
ajñānacakṣurnekṣeta bhāsvantaṃ bhānumandhavat ..65..*

The Atman is Pure Consciousness and is always present everywhere, yet only the eye of wisdom perceives It. Someone whose sight is clouded by ignorance does not see It, as the blind do not see the brilliant Sun.

### 66

श्रवणादिभिरुद्दीप्तज्ञानाग्निपरितापितः  
जीवः सर्वमलान्मुक्तः स्वर्णवद्द्योतते स्वयम् ॥६६॥

*śravaṇādibhiruddīptajñānāgniparitāpitaḥ  
jīvaḥ sarvamalānmuktaḥ svarṇavaddyotate svayam ..66..*

Heated in the fire of knowledge, which is kindled by hearing and so on, the 'Jiva' is freed of impurities and shines of itself, like gold.

### 67

हृदाकाशोदितो ह्यात्मा बोधभानुस्तमोऽपहृत्  
सर्वव्यापी सर्वधारी भाति भासयतेऽखिलम् ॥६७॥

*hṛdākāśodito hyātmā bodhabhānustamo'pahṛt  
sarvavyāpī sarvadhārī bhāti bhāsayate'khilam ..67..*

The Atman, the Sun of Knowledge rising in the sky of the heart, destroys the darkness of ignorance. It pervades and sustains all; it shines, and it makes everything shine.

### 68

दिग्देशकालाद्यनपेक्ष्य सर्वगं  
शीतादिहृन्नित्यसुखं निरंजनम्  
यः स्वात्मतीर्थं भजते विनिष्क्रियः  
स सर्ववित्सर्वगतोऽमृतो भवेत् ॥६८॥

*digdeśakālādyanapekṣya sarvagaṃ  
śītādihṛnnityasukhaṃ niraṃjanam  
yaḥ svātmatīrthaṃ bhajate viniṣkriyaḥ  
sa sarvavitsarvagato'mṛto bhavet ..68..*

One who gives up all activity, who is free of every limit of time, space and direction, and who worships his own Atman, present everywhere, destroyer of heat and cold, Eternal Bliss and stainless, becomes All-knowing and All-pervading and then attains Immortality.

### 69

इति श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य श्रीगोविन्दभगव़  
त्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ  
आत्मबोधः संपूर्णः।।

*iti śrīmatparamahaṃsaparivrājakācāryasya śrīgovindabhagav̈at  
pūjyapādaśiṣyasya śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavataḥ kṛtau  
ātmabodhaḥ saṃpūrṇaḥ..*

Here the Ātmabodha comes to a close. Its author is the revered Śrī Śaṅkarabhagavatpāda, disciple of the worshipful teacher Govinda Bhagavatpāda, a great Paramahaṃsa and wandering ascetic.
