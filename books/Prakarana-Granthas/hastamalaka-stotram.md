# Hastamalaka Stotram

### 0

(इन्द्रवज्रा छन्द -)  
कस्त्वं शिशो कस्य कुतोऽसि गन्ता  
किं नाम ते त्वं कुत आगतोऽसि ।  
एतन्मयोक्तं वद चार्भक त्वं  
मत्प्रीतये प्रीति विवर्धनोऽसि ॥

*(indravajrā chanda -)  
kastvaṃ śiśo kasya kuto'si gantā  
kiṃ nāma te tvaṃ kuta āgato'si .  
etanmayoktaṃ vada cārbhaka tvaṃ  
matprītaye prīti vivardhano'si ..*

Child, who are you? To whom do you belong? Where are you headed? What are you called, and where have you come from? Tell me all of this, little one. You have delighted my heart, and you are joy itself in living form.

### 0

हस्तामलक उवाच ।  
नाहं मनुष्यो न च देव-यक्षौ  
न ब्राह्मण-क्षत्रिय-वैश्य-शूद्राः ।  
न ब्रह्मचारी न गृही वनस्थो  
भिक्षुर्न चाहं निजबोध रूपः ॥

*hastāmalaka uvāca .  
nāhaṃ manuṣyo na ca deva-yakṣau  
na brāhmaṇa-kṣatriya-vaiśya-śūdrāḥ .  
na brahmacārī na gṛhī vanastho  
bhikṣurna cāhaṃ nijabodha rūpaḥ ..*

Hastamalaka replied: I am not a human being or a divine being, not a yaksha or a spirit. I am not a Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, or Shudra. I am not a student, a householder, a forest-dweller, or a monk. I am pure awareness itself, whose very nature is Self-knowledge (nijabodha rupa).

### 1

(भुजङ्गप्रयात छन्द -)  
निमित्तं मनश्चक्षुरादि प्रवृत्तौ  
निरस्ताखिलोपाधिराकाशकल्पः ।  
रविर्लोकचेष्टानिमित्तं यथा यः  
स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥१॥

*(bhujaṅgaprayāta chanda -)  
nimittaṃ manaścakṣurādi pravṛttau  
nirastākhilopādhirākāśakalpaḥ .  
ravirlokaceṣṭānimittaṃ yathā yaḥ  
sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo'hamātmā .. 1..*

As the sun drives the activities of all beings on earth, the Atma (Self) drives the working of the mind, the eye, and every other organ. Its nature is eternal Consciousness, and that is what I am. Apart from the limiting adjuncts (the body, mind, and sense-organs), I, the Self, am like space.

### 2

यमग्न्युष्णवन्नित्यबोध स्वरूपं  
मनश्चक्षुरादीन्यबोधात्मकानि ।  
प्रवर्तन्त आश्रित्य निष्कम्पमेकं  
स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥२॥

*yamagnyuṣṇavannityabodha svarūpaṃ  
manaścakṣurādīnyabodhātmakāni .  
pravartanta āśritya niṣkampamekaṃ  
sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo'hamātmā .. 2..*

The mind, the eye, and the other organs are all insentient, and they function by relying on the Self. Heat is the very nature of fire, and in the same way eternal consciousness is the very nature of the Self, which never changes and is one alone (non-dual). I am that Self.

### 3

मुखाभासको दर्पणे दृश्यमानो  
मुखत्वात् पृथक्त्वेन नैवास्ति वस्तु ।  
चिदाभासको धीषु जीवोऽपि तद्वत्  
स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥३॥

*mukhābhāsako darpaṇe dṛśyamāno  
mukhatvāt pṛthaktvena naivāsti vastu .  
cidābhāsako dhīṣu jīvo'pi tadvat  
sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo'hamātmā .. 3..*

A face seen in a mirror has no real existence apart from the face it reflects. Likewise the Jiva, merely the reflection of the Self or Pure Consciousness in the intellect (or mind), has no separate existence apart from the Self. I am that Self, whose nature is eternal consciousness.

### 4

यथा दर्पणाभाव आभासहानौ  
मुखं विद्यते कल्पनाहीनमेकम् ।  
तथा धी वियोगे निराभासको यः  
स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥ ४॥

*yathā darpaṇābhāva ābhāsahānau  
mukhaṃ vidyate kalpanāhīnamekam .  
tathā dhī viyoge nirābhāsako yaḥ  
sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo'hamātmā .. 4..*

Take away the mirror and the reflected face is gone; only the face remains, with no false appearance in the form of a reflection. In the same way, when the mind (the reflecting medium) is gone, the Atma stands free of every wrong notion the reflection had produced.

### 5

मनश्चक्षुरादेर्वियुक्तः स्वयं यो  
मनश्चक्षुरादेर्मनश्चक्षुरादिः ।  
मनश्चक्षुरादेरगम्यस्वरूपः  
स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥ ७॥

*manaścakṣurāderviyuktaḥ svayaṃ yo  
manaścakṣurādermanaścakṣurādiḥ .  
manaścakṣurāderagamyasvarūpaḥ  
sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo'hamātmā .. 5..*

I am the Self whose nature is eternal consciousness. It is distinct from the mind, the eye, and the other organs, yet it is the mind of the mind, the eye of the eye, the ear of the ear, and so on. Even so, the mind and the sense-organs cannot reach it.

### 6

य एको विभाति स्वतः शुद्धचेताः  
प्रकाशस्वरूपोऽपि नानेव धीषु  
शरावोदकस्थो यथा भानुरेकः  
स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥६॥

*ya eko vibhāti svataḥ śuddhacetāḥ  
prakāśasvarūpo'pi nāneva dhīṣu  
śarāvodakastho yathā bhānurekaḥ  
sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo'hamātmā .. 6..*

Being self-luminous, the Self shines on its own for those whose minds have become entirely pure. It is one, yet in different intellects it looks like many separate things, just as the one sun looks like many when reflected in different pots of water. I am that Self, whose nature is eternal consciousness.

### 7

यथाऽनेकचक्षुः-प्रकाशो रविर्न  
क्रमेण प्रकाशीकरोति प्रकाश्यम् ।  
अनेका धियो यस्तथैकः प्रबोधः  
स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥ ७॥

*yathā'nekacakṣuḥ-prakāśo ravirna  
krameṇa prakāśīkaroti prakāśyam .  
anekā dhiyo yastathaikaḥ prabodhaḥ  
sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo'hamātmā .. 7..*

The sun gives light to every eye, and it does not show things to one person after another in turn; all eyes can see at the same moment. In the same way the one Self gives consciousness to all intellects at once.

### 8

विवस्वत् प्रभातं यथा रूपमक्षं  
प्रगृह्णाति नाभातमेवं विवस्वान् ।  
यदाभात आभासयत्यक्षमेकः  
स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥८॥

*vivasvat prabhātaṃ yathā rūpamakṣaṃ  
pragṛhṇāti nābhātamevaṃ vivasvān .  
yadābhāta ābhāsayatyakṣamekaḥ  
sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo'hamātmā .. 8..*

The eye sees clearly only what the sun lights up, and cannot see what the sun does not light. Yet the sun can enable the eye to see objects only because the Self illumines the sun itself.

### 9

यथा सूर्य एकोऽप्स्वनेकश्चलासु  
स्थिरास्वप्यनन्यद्विभाव्यस्वरूपः  
चलासु प्रभिन्नः सुधीष्वेक एव  
स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥ ९॥

*yathā sūrya eko'psvanekaścalāsu  
sthirāsvapyananyadvibhāvyasvarūpaḥ  
calāsu prabhinnaḥ sudhīṣveka eva  
sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo'hamātmā .. 9..*

One sun shows up as many reflections in various containers of water, some moving and some still, yet the nature of the water does not affect it. Similarly the one Self, though reflected in many ever-changing intellects, stays changeless and is not stained by their character.

### 10

घनच्छन्नदृष्टिर्घनच्छन्नमर्कम्  
यथा निष्प्रभं मन्यते चातिमूढः ।  
तथा बद्धवद्भाति यो मूढ-दृष्टेः  
स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥  १०॥

*ghanacchannadṛṣṭir ghanacchannamarkam  
yathā niṣprabhaṃ manyate cātimūḍhaḥ .  
tathā baddhavadbhāti yo mūḍha-dṛṣṭeḥ  
sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo'hamātmā .. 10..*

A deeply ignorant person whose sight is blocked by a cloud supposes that the clouded sun has lost its brilliance. In the same way, to someone deluded by Avidya the Atma seems to be bound. I am that Atma, whose nature is eternal consciousness.

### 11

समस्तेषु वस्तुष्वनुस्यूतमेकं  
समस्तानि वस्तूनि यन्न स्पृशन्ति ।  
वियद्वत्सदा शुद्धमच्छस्वरूपं  
स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥ ११॥

*samasteṣu vastuṣvanusyūtamekaṃ  
samastāni vastūni yanna spṛśanti .  
viyadvatsadā śuddhamacchasvarūpaṃ  
sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo'hamātmā .. 11..*

One Self pervades everything in this universe, yet nothing can stain it. It is forever pure like space, untouched by the impurity of attachment and aversion, and immortal. I am that Self, whose nature is eternal consciousness.

### 12

उपाधौ यथा भेदता सन्मणीनां  
तथा भेदता बुद्धिभेदेषु तेऽपि ।  
यथा चन्द्रिकाणां जले चञ्चलत्वं  
तथा चञ्चलत्वं तवापीह विष्णो ॥ १२॥

*upādhau yathā bhedatā sanmaṇīnāṃ  
tathā bhedatā buddhibhedeṣu te'pi .  
yathā candrikāṇāṃ jale cañcalatvaṃ  
tathā cañcalatvaṃ tavāpīha viṣṇo .. 12..*

O all-pervading Lord! A crystal takes on different looks because of different limiting adjuncts, and You too seem different because You are reflected in different intellects. Reflections of the moon in different vessels of water move as the water moves, and You too seem to undergo change through Your association with different intellects.
