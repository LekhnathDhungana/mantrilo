# Tattvabodha

### 0

<vt>Invocation</vt>  
वासुदेवेन्द्रयोगीन्द्रं नत्वा ज्ञानप्रदं गुरुम् ।  
मुमुक्षूणां हितार्थाय तत्त्वबोधोभिधीयते ॥

*<vt>Invocation</vt>  
vāsudevendrayogīndram natvā jñānapradam gurum |  
mumukṣūņām hitārthāya tattvabodhobhidhīyate ||*

After bowing to Shri Vasudevendra, king among yogis, the guru who grants knowledge, the Tattvabodha is set out for the benefit of seekers.
This opening shloka serves as an invocation, or mangalacharya, to the Guru. The remainder of the text is in prose.

### 1

साधनचतुष्टयसम्पन्नाधिकारिणां मोक्षसाधनभूतं  
तत्त्वविवेकप्रकारं वक्ष्यामः ।

*sadhana catuștaya sampannādhikāriņām mokṣasādhanabhūtam |  
tattvaviveka prakāram vakşyāmaḥ ||*

For those who possess the four qualifications, we will explain the method of discrimination that leads to liberation.

### 2

<vt>Sādhanacatuștayam (The four-fold qualifications)</vt>  
साधनचतुष्टयं किम् ?  
नित्यानित्यवस्तुविवेकः ।  
इहामुत्रार्थफलभोगविरागः ।  
शमादिषट्कसम्पत्तिः ।  
मुमुक्षुत्वं चेति ।

*<vt>Sādhanacatuștayam (The four-fold qualifications)</vt>  
sādhanacatuṣṭayaṃ kim ?  
nityānityavastuvivekaḥ .  
ihāmutrārthaphalabhogavirāgaḥ .  
śamādiṣaṭkasampattiḥ .  
mumukṣutvaṃ ceti .*

What are the four qualifications? The ability to tell the permanent from the impermanent; detachment from enjoying the results of one's actions in this world and the next; the set of six attainments (inner wealth) that begins with shama; and a longing for liberation.

### 3

<vt>Nityānityavastuvivekaḥ (Discrimination)</vt>  
नित्यानित्यवस्तुविवेकः कः ?  
नित्यवस्त्वेकं ब्रह्म तद्व्यतिरिक्तं सर्वमनित्यम् ।  
अयमेव नित्यानित्यवस्तुविवेकः ।

*<vt>Nityānityavastuvivekaḥ (Discrimination)</vt>  
nityānityavastuvivekaḥ kaḥ ?  
nityavastvekaṃ brahma tadvyatiriktaṃ sarvamanityam .  
ayameva nityānityavastuvivekaḥ .*

What does discrimination between the permanent and the impermanent mean? Reality alone is everlasting; all else passes away. This conviction by itself is discrimination between the permanent and the impermanent.

### 4

<vt>Virāgaḥ (Vairagya - Dispassion)</vt>  
विरागः कः ?  
इहस्वर्गभोगेषु इच्छाराहित्यम् ।

*<vt>Virāgaḥ (Vairagya - Dispassion)</vt>  
virāgaḥ kaḥ ?  
ihasvargabhogeṣu icchārāhityam .*

What is dispassion? It is having no desire for enjoyments (the results of one's actions) either in this world or in heaven.

### 5

<vt>Śamādisādhanasampattiḥ (The six-fold wealth)</vt>  
शमादिसाधनसम्पत्तिः का ?  
शमो दम उपरमस्तितिक्षा श्रद्धा समाधानं च इति ।  
शमः कः ?  
मनोनिग्रहः ।  
दमः कः ?  
चक्षुरादिबाह्येन्द्रियनिग्रहः ।  
उपरमः कः ?  
स्वधर्मानुष्ठानमेव ।  
तितिक्षा का ?  
शीतोष्णसुखदुःखादिसहिष्णुत्वम् ।  
श्रद्धा कीदृशी ?  
गुरुवेदान्तवाक्यादिषु विश्वासः श्रद्धा ।  
समाधानं किम् ?  
चित्तैकाग्रता ।

*<vt>Śamādisādhanasampattiḥ (The six-fold wealth)</vt>  
śamādisādhanasampattiḥ kā ?  
śamo dama uparamastitikṣā śraddhā samādhānaṃ ca iti .  
samaḥ kah? mano nigrahaḥ|  
damaḥ kah? cakṣurādibāhyendriyanigrahaḥ .  
uparamaḥ kah? svadharmānuṣṭhānameva .  
titikṣā kā?śītoṣṇasukhaduḥkhādisahiṣṇutvam .  
śraddhā kīdṛśī ? guruvedāntavākyādiṣu viśvāsaḥ śraddhā .  
samādhānaṃ kim ? cittaikāgratā .*

What is the inner wealth that begins with sama? It consists of sama, dhama, uparama, titiksha, shraddha, and samadhana.
What is sama? It is control, or mastery, of the mind.
What is dhama? It is restraint of the outer sense organs, such as the eyes.
What is uparama, or uparati? It is strict adherence to one's own dharma (duty).
What is titiksha? It is the ability to bear heat and cold, pleasure and pain, and so on.
What is shraddha like? Shraddha is trust in the words and teachings of the guru and of Vedanta (the scriptures).
What is samadhana? It is one-pointedness of mind.

### 6

<vt>Mumuksutvam (Liberation)</vt>  
मुमुक्षुत्वं किम् ?  
मोक्षो मे भूयाद् इति इच्छा ।  
एतत् साधनचतुष्टयम् ।  
ततस्तत्त्वविवेकस्याधिकारिणो भवन्ति ।

*<vt>Mumuksutvam (Liberation)</vt>  
mumukṣutvaṃ kim ?  
mokṣo me bhūyād iti icchā .  
etat sādhanacatuṣṭayam .  
tatastattvavivekasyādhikāriṇo bhavanti .*

What is mumukṣutvam? It is the intense wish, 'May I attain liberation.' That is mumuksutvam.
These are the four qualifications. With them, people become fit to inquire into the Truth.

### 7

<vt>Tattvaviveka (Enquiry into truth)</vt>  
तत्त्वविवेकः कः ?  
आत्मा सत्यं तदन्यत् सर्वं मिथ्येति ।आत्मा कः ?  
स्थूलसूक्ष्मकारणशरीराद्व्यतिरिक्तः पञ्चकोशातीतः सन्  
अवस्थात्रयसाक्षी सच्चिदानन्दस्वरूपः सन्  
यस्तिष्ठति स आत्मा ।

*<vt>Tattvaviveka (Enquiry into truth)</vt>  
tattvavivekaḥ kaḥ ?  
ātmā satyaṃ tadanyat sarvaṃ mithyeti .ātmā kaḥ ?  
sthūlasūkṣmakāraṇaśarīrādvyatiriktaḥ pañcakośātītaḥ san  
avasthātrayasākṣī saccidānandasvarūpaḥ san  
yastiṣṭhati sa ātmā .*

What is inquiry into the truth? It is the firm conviction that the Self is real and that everything other than the Self is unreal.
What is the Self? The Self is that which is distinct from the gross, subtle, and causal bodies, beyond the five sheaths, the witness of the three states of consciousness, and of the nature of existence-consciousness-bliss.

### 8

<vt>Śarīratrayam (The three bodies) - Sthūlaśarīraṃ - The Gross Body</vt>  
स्थूलशरीरं किम् ?  
पञ्चीकृतपञ्चमहाभूतैः कृतं सत्कर्मजन्यं  
सुखदुःखादिभोगायतनं शरीरम्  
अस्ति जायते वर्धते विपरिणमते अपक्षीयते विनश्यतीति  
षड्विकारवदेतत्स्थूलशरीरम् ।

*<vt>Śarīratrayam (The three bodies) - Sthūlaśarīraṃ - The Gross Body</vt>  
sthūlaśarīraṃ kim ?  
pañcīkṛtapañcamahābhūtaiḥ kṛtaṃ satkarmajanyaṃ  
sukhaduḥkhādibhogāyatanaṃ śarīram  
asti jāyate vardhate vipariṇamate apakṣīyate vinaśyatīti  
ṣaḍvikāravadetatsthūlaśarīram .*

What is the Gross Body?
The gross body is made of the five great elements after they have undergone panchikarana. It is born from good deeds of the past, it is the vehicle for experiences such as joy and sorrow, and it passes through six changes: potential existence, birth, growth, maturity, decay, and death.

### 9

सूक्ष्मशरीरं किम् ?  
अपञ्चीकृतपञ्चमहाभूतैः कृतं सत्कर्मजन्यं  
सुखदुःखादिभोगसाधनं  
पञ्चज्ञानेन्द्रियाणि पञ्चकर्मेन्द्रियाणि पञ्चप्राणादयः  
मनश्चैकं बुद्धिश्चैका  
एवं सप्तदशाकलाभिः सह यत्तिष्ठति तत्सूक्ष्मशरीरम् ।

*sūkṣmaśarīraṃ kim ?  
apañcīkṛtapañcamahābhūtaiḥ kṛtaṃ satkarmajanyaṃ  
sukhaduḥkhādibhogasādhanaṃ  
pañcajñānendriyāṇi pañcakarmendriyāṇi pañcaprāṇādayaḥ  
manaścaikaṃ buddhiścaikā  
evaṃ saptadaśākalābhiḥ saha yattiṣṭhati tatsūkṣmaśarīram .*

What is the subtle body? The subtle body is made of the five great elements before grossification. It is born from good deeds of the past and is the instrument through which joy, sorrow, and the like are experienced. It consists of seventeen parts: the five organs of perception, the five organs of action, the five pranas, the mind, and the intellect.

### 10

<vt>Jnana Indriya - The five sense organs of Perception</vt>  
श्रोत्रं त्वक् चक्षुः रसना घ्राणम् इति पञ्च ज्ञानेन्द्रियाणि ।  
श्रोत्रस्य दिग्देवता ।  
त्वचो वायुः ।  
चक्षुषः सूर्यः ।  
रसनाया वरुणः ।  
घ्राणस्य अश्विनौ ।  
इति ज्ञानेन्द्रियदेवताः ।  
श्रोत्रस्य विषयः शब्दग्रहणम् ।  
त्वचो विषयः स्पर्शग्रहणम् ।  
चक्षुषो विषयः रूपग्रहणम् ।  
रसनाया विषयः रसग्रहणम् ।  
घ्राणस्य विषयः गन्धग्रहणम् इति ।

*<vt>Jnana Indriya - The five sense organs of Perception</vt>  
śrotraṃ tvak cakṣuḥ rasanā ghrāṇam iti pañca jñānendriyāṇi .  
śrotrasya digdevatā .  
tvaco vāyuḥ .  
cakṣuṣaḥ sūryaḥ .  
rasanāyā varuṇaḥ .  
ghrāṇasya aśvinau .  
iti jñānendriyadevatāḥ .  
śrotrasya viṣayaḥ śabdagrahaṇam .  
tvaco viṣayaḥ sparśagrahaṇam .  
cakṣuṣo viṣayaḥ rūpagrahaṇam .  
rasanāyā viṣayaḥ rasagrahaṇam .  
ghrāṇasya viṣayaḥ gandhagrahaṇam iti .*

The five organs of perception are the ears, skin, eyes, tongue, and nose. Their presiding deities are space for the ears, air for the skin, the sun for the eyes, water for the tongue, and the Ashvini kumaras for the nose. Their fields of experience are: the ear perceives sound, the skin perceives touch, the eyes perceive sight, the tongue perceives taste, and the nose perceives smell.

### 11

<vt>Karma Indriya - The five sense organs of Action</vt>  
वाक्पाणिपादपायूपस्थानीति पञ्चकर्मेन्द्रियाणि ।  
वाचो देवता वह्निः ।  
हस्तयोरिन्द्रः ।  
पादयोर्विष्णुः ।  
पायोर्मृत्युः ।  
उपस्थस्य प्रजापतिः ।  
इति कर्मेन्द्रियदेवताः ।  
वाचो विषयः भाषणम् ।  
पाण्योर्विषयः वस्तुग्रहणम् ।  
पादयोर्विषयः गमनम् ।  
पायोर्विषयः मलत्यागः ।  
उपस्थस्य विषयः आनन्द इति ।

*<vt>Karma Indriya - The five sense organs of Action</vt>  
vākpāṇipādapāyūpasthānīti pañcakarmendriyāṇi .  
vāco devatā vahniḥ .  
hastayorindraḥ .  
pādayorviṣṇuḥ .  
pāyormṛtyuḥ .  
upasthasya prajāpatiḥ .  
iti karmendriyadevatāḥ .  
vāco viṣayaḥ bhāṣaṇam .  
pāṇyorviṣayaḥ vastugrahaṇam .  
pādayorviṣayaḥ gamanam .  
pāyorviṣayaḥ malatyāgaḥ .  
upasthasya viṣayaḥ ānanda iti .*

The five organs of action are speech, the hands, the legs, the anus, and the genitals. Their presiding deities are agni (fire) for speech, Indra for the hands, Vishnu for the legs, Yama for the anus, and Prajapati for the genitals. Speech functions to speak, the hands to grasp, the legs to move about, the anus to expel waste, and the genitals to give pleasure (procreation).

### 12

<vt>Kārana Sharira - The Causal Body</vt>  
कारणशरीरं किम् ?  
अनिर्वाच्यानाद्यविद्यारूपं शरीरद्वयस्य कारणमात्रं  
सत्स्वरूपाऽज्ञानं निर्विकल्पकरूपं यदस्ति तत्कारणशरीरम् ।

*<vt>Kārana Sharira - The Causal Body</vt>  
kāraṇaśarīraṃ kim ?  
anirvācyānādyavidyārūpaṃ śarīradvayasya kāraṇamātraṃ  
satsvarūpā'jñānaṃ nirvikalpakarūpaṃ yadasti tatkāraṇaśarīram .*

The causal body is that which cannot be explained, has no beginning, takes the form of ignorance, is the sole cause of the two bodies (gross and subtle), is unaware of one's true nature, and is free of duality.

### 13

<vt>AVASTHA TRAYA - The Three States</vt>  
अवस्थात्रयं किम् ?  
जाग्रत्स्वप्नसुषुप्त्यवस्थाः ।

*<vt>AVASTHA TRAYA - The Three States</vt>  
avasthātrayaṃ kim ?  
jāgratsvapnasuṣuptyavasthāḥ .*

What are the three states? They are waking, dreaming, and deep sleep.

### 14

<vt>jāgrad avasthā - The waking state</vt>  
जाग्रदवस्था का ?  
श्रोत्रादिज्ञानेन्द्रियैः शब्दादिविषयैश्च ज्ञायते इति यत्  
सा जाग्रदावस्था ।  
स्थूल शरीराभिमानी आत्मा विश्व इत्युच्यते ।

*<vt>jāgrad avasthā - The waking state</vt>  
jāgradavasthā kā ?  
śrotrādijñānendriyaiḥ śabdādiviṣayaiśca jñāyate iti yat  
sā jāgradāvasthā .  
sthūla śarīrābhimānī ātmā viśva ityucyate .*

What is the waking state? It is the state of experience in which sense objects such as sound are perceived through sense organs such as the ears. The Self identified with the gross body is then called vishva.

### 15

<vt>swapnā avasthā - The dream state</vt>  
स्वप्नावस्था केति चेत् ?  
जाग्रदवस्थायां यद्दृष्टं यद् श्रुतम्  
तज्जनितवासनया निद्रासमये यः प्रपञ्चः प्रतीयते सा  
स्वप्नावस्था ।  
सूक्ष्मशरीराभिमानी आत्मा तैजस इत्युच्यते ।

*<vt>swapnā avasthā - The dream state</vt>  
svapnāvasthā keti cet ?  
jāgradavasthāyāṃ yaddṛṣṭaṃ yad śrutam  
tajjanitavāsanayā nidrāsamaye yaḥ prapañcaḥ pratīyate sā  
svapnāvasthā .  
sūkṣmaśarīrābhimānī ātmā taijasa ityucyate .*

To the question 'What is the dream state?' the answer is this: the world projected during sleep out of impressions left by what was seen and heard while awake is called the dream state. The Self identified with the subtle body is called taiijas.

### 16

<vt>suṣupti avasthā - The deep sleep state</vt>  
अतः सुषुप्त्यवस्था का ?  
अहं किमपि न जानामि सुखेन मया निद्राऽनुभूयत इति  
सुषुप्त्यवस्था ।  
कारणशरीराभिमानी आत्मा प्राज्ञ इत्युच्यते ।

*<vt>suṣupti avasthā - The deep sleep state</vt>  
ataḥ suṣuptyavasthā kā ?  
ahaṃ kimapi na jānāmi sukhena mayā nidrā'nubhūyata iti  
suṣuptyavasthā .  
kāraṇaśarīrābhimānī ātmā prājña ityucyate .*

What, then, is the deep sleep state? It is the state of which one afterwards says, 'I knew nothing; I slept well.' The Self identified with the causal body is called prajna.

### 17

<vt>pañca kośā - The five sheaths</vt>  
पञ्च कोशाः के ?  
अन्नमयः प्राणमयः मनोमयः विज्ञानमयः आनन्दमयश्चेति ।

*<vt>pañca kośā - The five sheaths</vt>  
pañca kośāḥ ke ?  
annamayaḥ prāṇamayaḥ manomayaḥ vijñānamayaḥ ānandamayaśceti .*

Which five sheaths are meant?
The sheaths are named Annamaya, Pranamaya, Manomaya, Vijnanamaya, and Anandamaya.

### 18

<vt>annamaya kośā - The food sheath</vt>  
अन्नमयः कः ?  
अन्नरसेनैव भूत्वा अन्नरसेनैव वृद्धिं प्राप्य अन्नरूपपृथिव्यां  
यद्विलीयते तदन्नमयः कोशः स्थूलशरीरम् ।

*<vt>annamaya kośā - The food sheath</vt>  
annamayaḥ kaḥ ?  
annarasenaiva bhūtvā annarasenaiva vṛddhiṃ prāpya annarūpapṛthivyāṃ  
yadvilīyate tadannamayaḥ kośaḥ sthūlaśarīram .*

What is the annamaya kośā?
That which is born from the essence of food, is sustained by the essence of food, and dissolves back into the earth, itself of the nature of food, is called the food sheath, or the gross body.

### 19

<vt>prāṇamaya kośā - The vital air sheath</vt>  
प्राणमयः कः ?  
प्राणाद्याः पञ्चवायवः वागादीन्द्रियपञ्चकं प्राणमयः कोशः ।

*<vt>prāṇamaya kośā - The vital air sheath</vt>  
prāṇamayaḥ kaḥ ?  
prāṇādyāḥ pañcavāyavaḥ vāgādīndriyapañcakaṃ prāṇamayaḥ kośaḥ .*

What is the Prāṇamayaḥ kośaḥ?
The five physiological functions, prana and the rest, together with the five organs of action, speech and the rest, make up the vital air sheath.

### 20

<vt>manomaya kośā - The mental sheath</vt>  
मनोमयः कोशः कः ?  
मनश्च ज्ञानेन्द्रियपञ्चकं मिलित्वा यो भवति स मनोमयः कोशः ।

*<vt>manomaya kośā - The mental sheath</vt>  
manomayaḥ kośaḥ kaḥ ?  
manaśca jñānendriyapañcakaṃ militvā yo bhavati sa manomayaḥ kośaḥ .*

What is the Manomayaḥ kośaḥ?
The mind together with the five organs of perception makes up the mental sheath.

### 21

<vt>vijñānamaya kośā - The intellectual sheath</vt>  
विज्ञानमयः कः ?  
बुद्धिज्ञानेन्द्रियपञ्चकं मिलित्वा यो भवति स विज्ञानमयः कोशः

*<vt>vijñānamaya kośā - The intellectual sheath</vt>  
vijñānamayaḥ kaḥ ?  
buddhijñānendriyapañcakaṃ militvā yo bhavati sa vijñānamayaḥ kośaḥ .*

What is the Vijñānamayaḥ kośaḥ?
The intellect together with the five organs of perception is the intellectual sheath. It is subtle and pervades the three sheaths before it. It governs the other three. It is made up of the intellect and the five organs of perception. The five senses belong to both the mental and the intellectual sheaths, because perception involves both the mind and the intellect.

### 22

<vt>ānandamaya kośā - The intellectual sheath</vt>  
आनन्दमयः कः ?  
एवमेव कारणशरीरभूताविद्यास्थमलिनसत्त्वं  
प्रियादिवृत्तिसहितं सत् आनन्दमयः कोशः ।  
एतत्कोशपञ्चकम् ।

*<vt>ānandamaya kośā - The intellectual sheath</vt>  
ānandamayaḥ kaḥ ?  
evameva kāraṇa śarīra bhūtāvidyāstha malina sattvaṃ  
priyādivṛttisahitaṃ sat ānandamayaḥ kośaḥ .  
etatkośapañcakam .*

What is the Anandamaya kośaḥ?
The bliss sheath rests on ignorance, which has the form of the causal body; it is impure in nature and joined with thought-modes such as priya.
These are the five sheaths.

### 23

<vt>pañcakośātita - Beyond the five sheaths</vt>  
मदीयं शरीरं मदीयाः प्राणाः मदीयं मनश्च  
मदीया बुद्धिर्मदीयं अज्ञानमिति स्वेनैव ज्ञायते  
तद्यथा मदीयत्वेन ज्ञातं कटककुण्डल गृहादिकं  
स्वस्माद्भिन्नं तथा पञ्चकोशादिकं स्वस्माद्भिन्नम्  
मदीयत्वेन ज्ञातमात्मा न भवति ॥

*<vt>pañcakośātita - Beyond the five sheaths</vt>  
madīyaṃ śarīraṃ madīyāḥ prāṇāḥ madīyaṃ manaśca  
madīyā buddhirmadīyaṃ ajñānamiti svenaiva jñāyate  
tadyathā madīyatvena jñātaṃ kaṭakakuṇḍala gṛhādikaṃ  
svasmādbhinnaṃ tathā pañcakośādikaṃ svasmādbhinnam  
madīyatvena jñātamātmā na bhavati ..*

Just as bangles, earrings, a house, and other things one calls 'mine' are distinct from the knowing 'me,' so too the five sheaths and the rest are known by the Self as 'my body, my pranas, my mind, my intellect, my knowledge.' They are therefore not the Self.

### 24

<vt>ātmān - The nature of the Self</vt>  
आत्मा तर्हि कः ?  
सच्चिदानन्दस्वरूपः ।  
सत्किम् ?  
कालत्रयेऽपि तिष्ठतीति सत् ।  
चित्किम् ?  
ज्ञानस्वरूपः ।  
आनन्दः कः ?  
सुखस्वरूपः ।  
एवं सच्चिदानन्दस्वरूपं स्वात्मानं विजानीयात् ।

*<vt>ātmān - The nature of the Self</vt>  
ātmā tarhi kaḥ ?  
saccidānandasvarūpaḥ .  
satkim ?  
kālatraye'pi tiṣṭhatīti sat .  
citkim ?  
jñānasvarūpaḥ .  
ānandaḥ kaḥ ?  
sukhasvarūpaḥ .  
evaṃ saccidānandasvarūpaṃ svātmānaṃ vijānīyāt .*

What, then, is the Self?
It is of the nature of existence, consciousness, and bliss.
What is existence? Existence is that which stays unchanged through the three periods of time (past, present, and future).
What is consciousness? It is of the nature of absolute knowledge.
What is bliss? It is of the nature of absolute happiness.
In this way one should know oneself to be of the nature of existence-consciousness-bliss.

### 25

<vt>jagat - The Universe</vt>  
अथ चतुर्विंशतितत्त्वोत्पत्तिप्रकारं वक्ष्यामः ।

*<vt>jagat - The Universe</vt>  
atha caturviṃśatitattvotpattiprakāraṃ vakṣyāmaḥ .*

We will now describe how the twenty-four factors come into being.

### 26

<vt>māyā</vt>  
ब्रह्माश्रया सत्त्वरजस्तमोगुणात्मिका माया अस्ति ।

*<vt>māyā</vt>  
brahmāśrayā sattvarajastamoguṇātmikā māyā asti .*

Resting on Brahman there exists maya, which is made of the three qualities sattva, rajas, and tamas.

### 27

<vt>Evolution of the five elements</vt>  
ततः आकाशः सम्भूतः ।  
आकाशाद् वायुः ।  
वायोस्तेजः ।  
तेजस आपः ।  
अभ्धयः पृथिवी ।

*<vt>Evolution of the five elements</vt>  
tataḥ ākāśaḥ sambhūtaḥ .  
ākāśād vāyuḥ .  
vāyostejaḥ .  
tejasa āpaḥ .  
abhdhayaḥ pṛthivī .*

From that (maya), space arose.
From space, air.
From air, fire.
From fire, water.
From water, earth.

### 28

<vt>Evolution of the Sattva aspect - The organs of perception</vt>  
एतेषां पञ्चतत्त्वानां मध्ये  
आकाशस्य सात्विकांशात् श्रोत्रेन्द्रियं सम्भूतम् ।  
वायोः सात्विकांशात् त्वगिन्द्रियं सम्भूतम् ।  
अग्नेः सात्विकांशात् चक्षुरिन्द्रियं सम्भूतम् ।  
जलस्य सात्विकांशात् रसनेन्द्रियं सम्भूतम् ।  
पृथिव्याः सात्विकांशात् घ्राणेन्द्रियं सम्भूतम् ।

*<vt>Evolution of the Sattva aspect - The organs of perception</vt>  
eteṣāṃ pañcatattvānāṃ madhye  
ākāśasya sātvikāṃśāt śrotrendriyaṃ sambhūtam .  
vāyoḥ sātvikāṃśāt tvagindriyaṃ sambhūtam .  
agneḥ sātvikāṃśāt cakṣurindriyaṃ sambhūtam .  
jalasya sātvikāṃśāt rasanendriyaṃ sambhūtam .  
pṛthivyāḥ sātvikāṃśāt ghrāṇendriyaṃ sambhūtam .*

Among these five great elements,
from the sattvic part of space came the organ of hearing, the ear.
From the sattvic part of air came the organ of touch, the skin.
From the sattvic part of fire came the organ of sight, the eye.
From the sattvic part of water came the organ of taste, the tongue.
From the sattvic part of earth came the organ of smell, the nose.

### 29

<vt>Antahkarana - The inner instruments</vt>  
एतेषां पञ्चतत्त्वानां समष्टिसात्विकांशात्  
मनोबुद्ध्यहङ्कार चित्तान्तःकरणानि सम्भूतानि ।  
सङ्कल्पविकल्पात्मकं मनः ।  
निश्चयात्मिका बुद्धिः ।  
अहंकर्ता अहंकारः ।  
चिन्तनकर्तृ चित्तम् ।  
मनसो देवता चन्द्रमाः ।  
बुद्धे ब्रह्मा ।  
अहंकारस्य रुद्रः ।  
चित्तस्य वासुदेवः ।

*<vt>Antahkarana - The inner instruments</vt>  
eteṣāṃ pañcatattvānāṃ samaṣṭisātvikāṃśāt  
manobuddhyahaṅkāra cittāntaḥkaraṇāni sambhūtāni .  
saṅkalpavikalpātmakaṃ manaḥ .  
niścayātmikā buddhiḥ .  
ahaṅkartā ahaṅkāraḥ .  
cintanakartṛ cittam .  
manaso devatā candramāḥ .  
buddhe brahmā .  
ahaṅkārasya rudraḥ .  
cittasya vāsudevaḥ .*

From the combined sattvic part of these five elements
the inner instrument is formed: mind, intellect, ego, and memory.
The mind has the nature of indecision.
The intellect has the nature of decision.
The ego has the nature of the sense of being the doer.
Memory has the nature of thinking, or recollection.
The presiding deity of the mind is the moon,
of the intellect, Brahma,
of the ego, Rudra,
and of memory, Vasudeva.

### 30

<vt>Evolution of the Rajas aspect</vt>  
एतेषां पञ्चतत्त्वानां मध्ये  
आकाशस्य राजसांशात् वागिन्द्रियं सम्भूतम् ।  
वायोः राजसांशात् पाणीन्द्रियं सम्भूतम् ।  
वन्हेः राजसांशात् पादेन्द्रियं सम्भूतम् ।  
जलस्य राजसांशात् उपस्थेन्द्रियं सम्भूतम् ।  
पृथिव्या राजसांशात् गुदेन्द्रियं सम्भूतम् ।  
एतेषां समष्टिराजसांशात् पञ्चप्राणाः सम्भूताः ।

*<vt>Evolution of the Rajas aspect</vt>  
eteṣāṃ pañcatattvānāṃ madhye  
ākāśasya rājasāṃśāt vāgindriyaṃ sambhūtam .  
vāyoḥ rājasāṃśāt pāṇīndriyaṃ sambhūtam .  
vanheḥ rājasāṃśāt pādendriyaṃ sambhūtam .  
jalasya rājasāṃśāt upasthendriyaṃ sambhūtam .  
pṛthivyā rājasāṃśāt gudendriyaṃ sambhūtam .  
eteṣāṃ samaṣṭirājasāṃśāt pañcaprāṇāḥ sambhūtāḥ .*

Among these five elements,
from the rajas part of space the organ of speech is formed.
From the rajas part of air the organ of grasping, the hands, is formed.
From the rajas part of fire the organ of movement, the legs, is formed.
From the rajas part of water the organ of procreation is formed.
From the rajas part of earth the anus is formed.
From the combined rajas part of these five elements the five vital airs, the pranas, are formed.

### 31

<vt>Evolution of the Tamasic aspect</vt>  
एतेषां पञ्चतत्त्वानां तामसांशात्  
पञ्चीकृतपञ्चतत्त्वानि भवन्ति ।  
पञ्चीकरणं कथम् इति चेत् ।  
एतेषां पञ्चमहाभूतानां तामसांशस्वरूपम्  
एकमेकं भूतं द्विधा विभज्य एकमेकमर्धं पृथक्  
तूष्णीं व्यवस्थाप्य अपरमपरमर्धं चतुर्धां विभज्य  
स्वार्धमन्येषु अर्धेषु स्वभागचतुष्टयसंयोजनं कार्यम् ।  
तदा पञ्चीकरणं भवति ।  
एतेभ्यः पञ्चीकृतपञ्चमहाभूतेभ्यः स्थूलशरीरं भवति ।  
एवं पिण्डब्रह्माण्डयोरैक्यं सम्भूतम् ।

*<vt>Evolution of the Tamasic aspect</vt>  
eteṣāṃ pañcatattvānāṃ tāmasāṃśāt  
pañcīkṛtapañcatattvāni bhavanti .  
pañcīkaraṇaṃ katham iti cet .  
eteṣāṃ pañcamahābhūtānāṃ tāmasāṃśasvarūpam  
ekamekaṃ bhūtaṃ dvidhā vibhajya ekamekamardhaṃ pṛthak  
tūṣṇīṃ vyavasthāpya aparamaparamardhaṃ caturdhāṃ vibhajya  
svārdhamanyeṣu ardheṣu svabhāgacatuṣṭayasaṃyojanaṃ kāryam .  
tadā pañcīkaraṇaṃ bhavati .  
etebhyaḥ pañcīkṛtapañcamahābhūtebhyaḥ sthūlaśarīraṃ bhavati .  
evaṃ piṇḍabrahmāṇḍayoraikyaṃ sambhūtam .*

From the tamas part of these five elements the grossified five elements arise.
If one asks how this panchikarana happens, it is as follows.
- The tamas part of each of the five elements splits into two equal halves.
- One half of each stays whole.
- The other half of each splits into four equal parts.
- Then to the whole half of each element is joined one-eighth from each of the other four elements.
- With that, panchikarana is complete.
- From these five grossified elements the gross body is formed.
In this way the microcosm and the macrocosm are identical.

### 32

<vt>Jiva and Ishwara</vt>  
स्थूलशरीराभिमानि जीवनामकं ब्रह्मप्रतिबिम्बं भवति ।  
स एव जीवः प्रकृत्या स्वस्मात् ईश्वरं भिन्नत्वेन जानाति ।  
अविद्योपाधिः सन् आत्मा जीव इत्युच्यते ।  
मायोपाधिः सन् ईश्वर इत्युच्यते ।  
एवं उपाधिभेदात् जीवेश्वरभेददृष्टिः यावत्पर्यन्तं तिष्ठति  
तावत्पर्यन्तं जन्ममरणादिरूपसंसारो न निवर्तते ।  
तस्मात्कारणान्न जीवेश्वरयोर्भेदबुद्धिः स्वीकार्या ।

*<vt>Jiva and Ishwara</vt>  
sthūlaśarīrābhimāni jīvanāmakaṃ brahmapratibimbaṃ bhavati .  
sa eva jīvaḥ prakṛtyā svasmāt īśvaraṃ bhinnatvena jānāti .  
avidyopādhiḥ san ātmā jīva ityucyate .  
māyopādhiḥ san īśvara ityucyate .  
evaṃ upādhibhedāt jīveśvarabhedadṛṣṭiḥ yāvatparyantaṃ tiṣṭhati  
tāvatparyantaṃ janmamaraṇādirūpasaṃsāro na nivartate .  
tasmātkāraṇānna jīveśvarayorbhedabuddhiḥ svīkāryā .*

The reflection of Brahman that identifies itself with the gross body is called the jiva. By nature this jiva regards Ishwara as different from itself. The Self conditioned by ignorance (maya) is called Ishwara. As long as the notion persists that the jiva and Ishwara are different, a notion arising from the difference in their conditioning, there is no release from samsara, which takes the form of repeated birth, death, and so on. For this reason the idea that the jiva differs from Ishwara should not be accepted.

### 33

<vt>TAT TVAM ASI - An enquiry into the statement 'THAT THOU ART'</vt>  
ननु साहंकारस्य किंचिज्ज्ञस्य जीवस्य निरहंकारस्य सर्वज्ञस्य  
ईश्वरस्य तत्त्वमसीति महावाक्यात् कथमभेदबुद्धिः स्यादुभयोः  
विरुद्धधर्माक्रान्तत्वात् ।  
इति चेन्न । स्थूलसूक्ष्मशरीराभिमानी त्वंपदवाच्यार्थः ।  
उपाधिविनिर्मुक्तं समाधिदशासम्पन्नं शुद्धं चैतन्यं  
त्वंपदलक्ष्यार्थः ।  
एवं सर्वज्ञत्वादिविशिष्ट ईश्वरः तत्पदवाच्यार्थः ।  
उपाधिशून्यं शुद्धचैतन्यं तत्पदलक्ष्यार्थः ।  
एवं च जीवेश्वरयो चैतन्यरूपेणाऽभेदे बाधकाभावः ।

*<vt>TAT TVAM ASI - An enquiry into the statement 'THAT THOU ART'</vt>  
nanu sāhaṅkārasya kiñcijjñasya jīvasya nirahaṅkārasya sarvajñasya  
īśvarasya tattvamasīti mahāvākyāt kathamabhedabuddhiḥ syādubhayoḥ  
viruddhadharmākrāntatvāt .  
iti cenna . sthūlasūkṣmaśarīrābhimānī tvampadavācyārthaḥ .  
upādhivinirmuktaṃ samādhidaśāsampannaṃ śuddhaṃ caitanyaṃ  
tvampadalakṣyārthaḥ .  
evaṃ sarvajñatvādiviśiṣṭa īśvaraḥ tatpadavācyārthaḥ .  
upādhiśūnyaṃ śuddhacaitanyaṃ tatpadalakṣyārthaḥ .  
evaṃ ca jīveśvarayo caitanyarūpeṇā'bhede bādhakābhāvaḥ .*

But the jiva has an ego and limited knowledge, while Ishwara has no ego and knows everything. How then can there be identity, as the mahavakya 'That thou art' declares, between two things with opposite characteristics?
If such a doubt arises, the answer is no, it is not so. The literal meaning of the word 'thou' is the one identified with the gross and subtle bodies. The implied meaning of 'thou' is pure awareness, free of all conditioning, which is recognized in the state of samadhi. Likewise, the literal meaning of 'That' is Ishwara with omniscience and the other attributes; the implied meaning of 'That' is pure awareness, free of all conditioning. So, from the standpoint of awareness, there is no contradiction in the identity of the jiva and Ishwara.

### 34

<vt>jīvanmuktāḥ</vt>  
एवं च वेदान्तवाक्यैः सद्गुरूपदेशेन च सर्वेष्वपि  
भूतेषु येषां  
ब्रह्मबुद्धिरुत्पन्ना ते जीवन्मुक्ताः इत्यर्थः ।ननु जीवन्मुक्तः कः ?  
यथा देहोऽहं पुरुषोऽहं ब्राह्मणोऽहं शूद्रोऽहमस्मीति  
दृढनिश्चयस्तथा नाहं ब्राह्मणः न शूद्रः न पुरुषः  
किन्तु असंगः सच्चिदानन्द स्वरूपः प्रकाशरूपः सर्वान्तर्यामी  
चिदाकाशरूपोऽस्मीति दृढनिश्चय  
रूपोऽपरोक्षज्ञानवान् जीवन्मुक्तः ॥  
ब्रह्मैवाहमस्मीत्यपरोक्षज्ञानेन निखिलकर्मबन्धविनिर्मुक्तः  
स्यात् ।

*<vt>jīvanmuktāḥ</vt>  
evaṃ ca vedāntavākyaiḥ sadgurūpadeśena ca sarveṣvapi  
bhūteṣu yeṣāṃ brahmabuddhirutpannā te jīvanmuktāḥ ityarthaḥ .  
nanu jīvanmuktaḥ kaḥ ?  
yathā deho'haṃ puruṣo'haṃ brāhmaṇo'haṃ śūdro'hamasmīti  
dṛḍhaniścayastathā nāhaṃ brāhmaṇaḥ na śūdraḥ na puruṣaḥ  
kintu asaṅgaḥ saccidānanda svarūpaḥ prakāśarūpaḥ sarvāntaryāmī  
cidākāśarūpo'smīti dṛḍhaniścaya  
rūpo'parokṣajñānavān jīvanmuktaḥ ..  
brahmaivāhamasmītyaparokṣajñānena  
nikhilakarmabandhavinirmuktaḥ syāt .*

So, through the words of Vedanta and the teaching of the satguru, those in whom the vision of the truth in all beings has arisen are liberated while still alive (jīvanmuktah).
Who, then, is a jīvanmuktah? Just as a person firmly believes 'I am the body,' 'I am a man,' 'I am a Brahmin,' 'I am a Shudra,' so one who has firmly determined through immediate knowledge (aparoksha jnana) 'I am not a Brahmin,' 'I am not a Shudra,' 'I am not a man,' but rather 'I am unattached, of the nature of existence-consciousness-bliss, self-luminous, dwelling in all, formless awareness,' is a jīvanmuktah.
Through the immediate knowledge that 'I am Brahman alone,' one is released from the bondage of all karmas.

### 35

<vt>Karma - The Actions</vt>  
कर्माणि कतिविधानि सन्तीति चेत्  
आगामिसञ्चितप्रारब्धभेदेन त्रिविधानि सन्ति ।

*<vt>Karma - The Actions</vt>  
karmāṇi katividhāni santīti cet  
āgāmisañcitaprārabdhabhedena trividhāni santi .*

If someone asks how many kinds of karma there are, the answer is three: agami, sanchita, and prarabdha.

### 36

<vt>Agami karma</vt>  
ज्ञानोत्पत्त्यनन्तरं ज्ञानिदेहकृतं पुण्यपापरूपं कर्म  
यदस्ति तदागामीत्यभिधीयते ।

*<vt>Agami karma</vt>  
jñānotpattyanantaraṃ jñānidehakṛtaṃ puṇyapāparūpaṃ karma  
yadasti tadāgāmītyabhidhīyate .*

Agami is the result of actions, good or bad, performed by the body of the realized person (jnani) after knowledge has dawned.

### 37

<vt>Sanchita karma</vt>  
सञ्चितं कर्म किम् ?  
अनन्तकोटिजन्मनां बीजभूतं सत् यत्कर्मजातं पूर्वार्जितं  
तिष्ठति तत् सञ्चितं ज्ञेयम् ।

*<vt>Sanchita karma</vt>  
sañcitaṃ karma kim ?  
anantakoṭijanmanāṃ bījabhūtaṃ sat yatkarmajātaṃ pūrvārjitaṃ  
tiṣṭhati tat sañcitaṃ jñeyam .*

Sanchita (accumulated) karma is the result of actions done in all previous births, lying in seed form, ready to produce countless crores of future births.

### 38

<vt>Prārabdha karma</vt>  
प्रारब्धं कर्म किमिति चेत् ।  
इदं शरीरमुत्पाद्य इह लोके एवं सुखदुःखादिप्रदं यत्कर्म  
तत्प्रारब्धं  
भोगेन नष्टं भवति प्रारब्धकर्मणां भोगादेव क्षय इति ।

*<vt>Prārabdha karma</vt>  
prārabdhaṃ karma kimiti cet .  
idaṃ śarīramutpādya iha loke evaṃ sukhaduḥkhādipradaṃ yatkarma  
tatprārabdhaṃ  
bhogena naṣṭaṃ bhavati prārabdhakarmaṇāṃ bhogādeva kṣaya iti .*

Prarabdha karma consists of the actions that, having produced this body, bear fruit in this very world as happiness or misery, and that can be exhausted only by being enjoyed or suffered.

### 39

<vt>Freedom from bondage of actions</vt>  
सञ्चितं कर्म ब्रह्मैवाहमिति निश्चयात्मकज्ञानेन नश्यति ।  
आगामि कर्म अपि ज्ञानेन नश्यति किंच आगामि कर्मणां  
नलिनीदलगतजलवत् ज्ञानिनां सम्बन्धो नास्ति ।

*<vt>Freedom from bondage of actions</vt>  
sañcitaṃ karma brahmaivāhamiti niścayātmakajñānena naśyati .  
āgāmi karma api jñānena naśyati kiñca āgāmi karmaṇāṃ  
nalinīdalagatajalavat jñānināṃ sambandho nāsti .*

Sanchita karma is destroyed by the firm knowledge 'I am Brahman' alone.
Agami karma is also destroyed by knowledge, and the wise person is untouched by it, just as a lotus leaf is untouched by the water on it (padma patram ivambhasa).

### 40

किंच ये ज्ञानिनं स्तुवन्ति भजन्ति अर्चयन्ति तान्प्रति  
ज्ञानिकृतं आगामि पुण्यं गच्छति ।  
ये ज्ञानिनं निन्दन्ति द्विषन्ति दुःखप्रदानं कुर्वन्ति तान्प्रति  
ज्ञानिकृतं सर्वमागामि क्रियमाणं यदवाच्यं कर्म  
पापात्मकं तद्गच्छति ।  
सुहृदः पुण्यकृतं दुर्हृदः पापकृत्यं गृह्णन्ति ।

*kiñca ye jñāninaṃ stuvanti bhajanti arcayanti tānprati  
jñānikṛtaṃ āgāmi puṇyaṃ gacchati .  
ye jñāninaṃ nindanti dviṣanti duḥkhapradānaṃ kurvanti tānprati  
jñānikṛtaṃ sarvamāgāmi kriyamāṇaṃ yadavācyaṃ karma  
pāpātmakaṃ tadgacchati .  
suhṛdaḥ puṇyakṛtaṃ durhṛdaḥ pāpakṛtyaṃ gṛhṇanti .*

Moreover, the results of the actions done by the wise person go to those who praise, serve, and worship him. The results of all his blameworthy and sinful actions go to those who criticize, hate, or hurt him.

### 41

<vt>Conclusion</vt>  
तथा चात्मवित्संसारं तीर्त्वा ब्रह्मानन्दमिहैव प्राप्नोति ।  
तरति शोकमात्मवित् इति श्रुतेः ।  
तनुं त्यजतु वा काश्यां श्वपचस्य गृहेऽथ वा ।  
ज्ञानसम्प्राप्तिसमये मुक्ताऽसौ विगताशयः । इति स्मृतेश्च ।

*<vt>Conclusion</vt>  
tathā cātmavitsaṃsāraṃ tīrtvā brahmānandamihaiva prāpnoti .  
tarati śokamātmavit iti śruteḥ .  
tanuṃ tyajatu vā kāśyāṃ śvapacasya gṛhe'tha vā .  
jñānasamprāptisamaye muktā'sau vigatāśayaḥ . iti smṛteśca .*

Thus the knower of the Self, having crossed samsara, reaches supreme bliss right here. The shruti declares that the knower of the Self passes beyond all sorrow. Let the wise person drop the body in Kashi or in the house of a dog-eater; it makes no difference, because at the very moment of gaining knowledge he is liberated, freed from all the results of his actions. The smritis affirm this as well.

### 42

<vt>Colophon</vt>  
इति श्रीशङ्करभगवत्पादाचार्यप्रणीतः तत्त्वबोधप्रकरणं समाप्तम् ।

*<vt>Colophon</vt>  
iti śrīśaṅkarabhagavatpādācāryapraṇītaḥ tattvabodhaprakaraṇaṃ samāptam .*

Here ends the Tattvabodha, composed by the revered Ādi Śaṅkarācārya.
