# Avadhuta Gita

### 1.1

<vt>अथ प्रथमोऽध्यायः</vt>  
ईश्वरानुग्रहादेव पुंसामद्वैतवासना ।  
महद्भयपरित्राणाद्विप्राणामुपजायते ॥ १-१॥

*<vt>Chapter 1</vt>  
īśvarānugrahādeva puṃsāmadvaitavāsanā .  
mahadbhayaparitrāṇādviprāṇāmupajāyate ..1-1..*

Only by God's grace do the wise come to long for the realization of non-dual Brahman, and through that longing they are released from great fear.

### 1.2

येनेदं पूरितं सर्वमात्मनैवात्मनात्मनि ।  
निराकारं कथं वन्दे ह्यभिन्नं शिवमव्ययम् ॥ १-२॥

*yenedaṃ pūritaṃ sarvamātmanaivātmanātmani .  
nirākāraṃ kathaṃ vande hyabhinnaṃ śivamavyayam ..1-2..*

Truly, the whole universe is pervaded by the Atman. And since the Atman is veiled only by Itself, how could I offer worship to that formless, undivided, changeless Supreme Bliss?

### 1.3

पञ्चभूतात्मकं विश्वं मरीचिजलसन्निभम् ।  
कस्याप्यहो नमस्कुर्यामहमेको निरञ्जनः ॥ १-३॥

*pañcabhūtātmakaṃ viśvaṃ marīcijalasannibham .  
kasyāpyaho namaskuryāmahameko nirañjanaḥ ..1-3..*

This universe, made of the five elements (space, air, fire, water, and earth), is like the water of a mirage. Being myself that one stainless Atman, to whom could I bow?

### 1.4

आत्मैव केवलं सर्वं भेदाभेदो न विद्यते ।  
अस्ति नास्ति कथं ब्रूयां विस्मयः प्रतिभाति मे ॥ १-४॥

*ātmaiva kevalaṃ sarvaṃ bhedābhedo na vidyate .  
asti nāsti kathaṃ brūyāṃ vismayaḥ pratibhāti me ..1-4..*

In truth the Atman alone is everything, and within It there is neither division nor absence of division. To me it seems baffling to say either "the Atman exists" or "the Atman does not exist."

### 1.5

वेदान्तसारसर्वस्वं ज्ञानं विज्ञानमेव च ।  
अहमात्मा निराकारः सर्वव्यापी स्वभावतः ॥ १-५॥

*vedāntasārasarvasvaṃ jñānaṃ vijñānameva ca .  
ahamātmā nirākāraḥ sarvavyāpī svabhāvataḥ ..1-5..*

The heart of all Vedanta is the knowledge and direct realization of the Atman. By my very nature I am that formless Atman that pervades everything.

### 1.6

यो वै सर्वात्मको देवो निष्कलो गगनोपमः ।  
स्वभावनिर्मलः शुद्धः स एवायं न संशयः ॥ १-६॥

*yo vai sarvātmako devo niṣkalo gaganopamaḥ .  
svabhāvanirmalaḥ śuddhaḥ sa evāyaṃ na saṃśayaḥ ..1-6..*

Without any doubt I am that Atman: present everywhere, self-luminous, undivided like space, pure in Its own nature, and never dimmed by maya.

### 1.7

अहमेवाव्ययोऽनन्तः शुद्धविज्ञानविग्रहः ।  
सुखं दुःखं न जानामि कथं कस्यापि वर्तते ॥ १-७॥

*ahamevāvyayo'nantaḥ śuddhavijñānavigrahaḥ .  
sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ na jānāmi kathaṃ kasyāpi vartate ..1-7..*

Truly I am indestructible and infinite, pure consciousness itself. I do not know how happiness and sorrow are experienced, or who it is that experiences them.

### 1.8

न मानसं कर्म शुभाशुभं मे  
न कायिकं कर्म शुभाशुभं मे ।  
न वाचिकं कर्म शुभाशुभं मे  
ज्ञानामृतं शुद्धमतीन्द्रियोऽहम् ॥ १-८॥

*na mānasaṃ karma śubhāśubhaṃ me  
na kāyikaṃ karma śubhāśubhaṃ me .  
na vācikaṃ karma śubhāśubhaṃ me  
jñānāmṛtaṃ śuddhamatīndriyo'ham ..1-8..*

For me there is no act of mind, good or bad; for me there is no act of body, good or bad; for me there is no act of speech, good or bad. I am undying consciousness, pure and beyond all.

### 1.9

मनो वै गगनाकारं मनो वै सर्वतोमुखम् ।  
मनोऽतीतं मनः सर्वं न मनः परमार्थतः ॥ १-९॥

*mano vai gaganākāraṃ mano vai sarvatomukham .  
mano'tītaṃ manaḥ sarvaṃ na manaḥ paramārthataḥ ..1-9..*

The mind is truly like space: it seems to face every direction, it seems to go beyond everything, it seems to be everything. Yet in reality the mind does not exist at all.

### 1.10

अहमेकमिदं सर्वं व्योमातीतं निरन्तरम् ।  
पश्यामि कथमात्मानं प्रत्यक्षं वा तिरोहितम् ॥ १-१०॥

*ahamekamidaṃ sarvaṃ vyomātītaṃ nirantaram .  
paśyāmi kathamātmānaṃ pratyakṣaṃ vā tirohitam ..1-10..*

I am one, and I am in all things. I am limitless and beyond space. How could I see my own Self coming into being or passing away?

### 1.11

त्वमेवमेकं हि कथं न बुध्यसे  
समं हि सर्वेषु विमृष्टमव्ययम् ।  
सदोदितोऽसि त्वमखण्डितः प्रभो  
दिवा च नक्तं च कथं हि मन्यसे ॥ १-११॥

*tvamevamekaṃ hi kathaṃ na budhyase  
samaṃ hi sarveṣu vimṛṣṭamavyayam .  
sadodito'si tvamakhaṇḍitaḥ prabho  
divā ca naktaṃ ca kathaṃ hi manyase ..1-11..*

Why do you not grasp that you yourself are that absolute One? You are the eternal Atman, reflecting all things alike. You are the Lord whom nothing can refute, forever shining. How then can you think in terms of day (light) or night (darkness)?

### 1.12

आत्मानं सततं विद्धि सर्वत्रैकं निरन्तरम् ।  
अहं ध्याता परं ध्येयमखण्डं खण्ड्यते कथम् ॥ १-१२॥

*ātmānaṃ satataṃ viddhi sarvatraikaṃ nirantaram .  
ahaṃ dhyātā paraṃ dhyeyamakhaṇḍaṃ khaṇḍyate katham ..1-12..*

Stay aware of the Atman at all times; It is unbroken and everywhere the same. You say, "I am the one who meditates," and "the Supreme One is what I meditate upon." Why do you split what cannot be split?

### 1.13

न जातो न मृतोऽसि त्वं न ते देहः कदाचन ।  
सर्वं ब्रह्मेति विख्यातं ब्रवीति बहुधा श्रुतिः ॥ १-१३॥

*na jāto na mṛto'si tvaṃ na te dehaḥ kadācana .  
sarvaṃ brahmeti vikhyātaṃ bravīti bahudhā śrutiḥ ..1-13..*

You were never born and you will never die. You have never possessed a body. In many different ways the Upanishads proclaim this declared truth: "All is Brahman."

### 1.14

स बाह्याभ्यन्तरोऽसि त्वं शिवः सर्वत्र सर्वदा ।  
इतस्ततः कथं भ्रान्तः प्रधावसि पिशाचवत् ॥ १-१४॥

*sa bāhyābhyantaro'si tvaṃ śivaḥ sarvatra sarvadā .  
itastataḥ kathaṃ bhrāntaḥ pradhāvasi piśācavat ..1-14..*

You are always present, both inside and outside. You are that Supreme Bliss, at every time and in every place. Why then do you rush here and there like a bewildered ghost?

### 1.15

संयोगश्च वियोगश्च वर्तते न च ते न मे ।  
न त्वं नाहं जगन्नेदं सर्वमात्मैव केवलम् ॥ १-१५॥

*saṃyogaśca viyogaśca vartate na ca te na me .  
na tvaṃ nāhaṃ jagannedaṃ sarvamātmaiva kevalam ..1-15..*

Between you and me there can be no joining and no parting. In reality neither you nor I nor this world exists. The Atman alone remains.

### 1.16

शब्दादिपञ्चकस्यास्य नैवासि त्वं न ते पुनः ।  
त्वमेव परमं तत्त्वमतः किं परितप्यसे ॥ १-१६॥

*śabdādipañcakasyāsya naivāsi tvaṃ na te punaḥ .  
tvameva paramaṃ tattvamataḥ kiṃ paritapyase ..1-16..*

You do not belong to the five sense-objects, such as sound, touch, form, taste, and smell, and they in turn do not belong to you. You are indeed that Supreme Reality. What reason, then, do you have for grief?

### 1.17

जन्म मृत्युर्न ते चित्तं बन्धमोक्षौ शुभाशुभौ ।  
कथं रोदिषि रे वत्स नामरूपं न ते न मे ॥ १-१७॥

*janma mṛtyurna te cittaṃ bandhamokṣau śubhāśubhau .  
kathaṃ rodiṣi re vatsa nāmarūpaṃ na te na me ..1-17..*

You have no birth and no death, no memory, no bondage and no liberation, no good and no evil. Why do you cry, my dear one? Name and form belong neither to you nor to me.

### 1.18

अहो चित्त कथं भ्रान्तः प्रधावसि पिशाचवत् ।  
अभिन्नं पश्य चात्मानं रागत्यागात्सुखी भव ॥ १-१८॥

*aho citta kathaṃ bhrāntaḥ pradhāvasi piśācavat .  
abhinnaṃ paśya cātmānaṃ rāgatyāgātsukhī bhava ..1-18..*

O mind, why do you roam about like a restless ghost? Realize the Atman, in which there are no distinctions. Let go of every craving and be happy.

### 1.19

त्वमेव तत्त्वं हि विकारवर्जितं  
निष्कम्पमेकं हि विमोक्षविग्रहम् ।  
न ते च रागो ह्यथवा विरागः  
कथं हि सन्तप्यसि कामकामतः ॥ १-१९॥

*tvameva tattvaṃ hi vikāravarjitaṃ  
niṣkampamekaṃ hi vimokṣavigraham .  
na te ca rāgo hyathavā virāgaḥ  
kathaṃ hi santapyasi kāmakāmataḥ ..1-19..*

Truly, you are that unchanging Reality, which is still, one, and freedom itself. You feel neither passion nor dispassion. Why then do you torment yourself by craving objects of lust?

### 1.20

वदन्ति श्रुतयः सर्वाः निर्गुणं शुद्धमव्ययम् ।  
अशरीरं समं तत्त्वं तन्मां विद्धि न संशयः ॥ १-२०॥

*vadanti śrutayaḥ sarvāḥ nirguṇaṃ śuddhamavyayam .  
aśarīraṃ samaṃ tattvaṃ tanmāṃ viddhi na saṃśayaḥ ..1-20..*

Every Upanishad declares that Brahman, the Supreme Reality, is without attributes, pure, unchanging, bodiless, and equally present in all things. Know that I am that Brahman. Have no doubt about this.

### 1.21

साकारमनृतं विद्धि निराकारं निरन्तरम् ।  
एतत्तत्त्वोपदेशेन न पुनर्भवसम्भवः ॥ १-२१॥

*sākāramanṛtaṃ viddhi nirākāraṃ nirantaram .  
etattattvopadeśena na punarbhavasambhavaḥ ..1-21..*

Know this: whatever has form is unreal (since it is limited by its form); whatever is formless is eternal. One who lives out this truth in his own life will not be born again.

### 1.22

एकमेव समं तत्त्वं वदन्ति हि विपश्चितः ।  
रागत्यागात्पुनश्चित्तमेकानेकं न विद्यते ॥ १-२२॥

*ekameva samaṃ tattvaṃ vadanti hi vipaścitaḥ .  
rāgatyāgātpunaścittamekānekaṃ na vidyate ..1-22..*

The sages declare that Reality is one and never changes. Once craving is given up, both unity and diversity vanish from the mind.

### 1.23

अनात्मरूपं च कथं समाधि-  
रात्मस्वरूपं च कथं समाधिः ।  
अस्तीति नास्तीति कथं समाधि-  
र्मोक्षस्वरूपं यदि सर्वमेकम् ॥ १-२३॥

*anātmarūpaṃ ca kathaṃ samādhi-  
rātmasvarūpaṃ ca kathaṃ samādhiḥ .  
astīti nāstīti kathaṃ samādhi-  
rmokṣasvarūpaṃ yadi sarvamekam ..1-23..*

How can a man reach samadhi while he takes himself to be something other than the Atman? Yet samadhi is also impossible for a man who thinks of himself as the Atman. How can samadhi be reached while a man thinks that the Atman both exists and does not exist? And what need is there to reach samadhi at all, if everything is one and free by nature?

### 1.24

विशुद्धोऽसि समं तत्त्वं विदेहस्त्वमजोऽव्ययः ।  
जानामीह न जानामीत्यात्मानं मन्यसे कथम् ॥ १-२४॥

*viśuddho'si samaṃ tattvaṃ videhastvamajo'vyayaḥ .  
jānāmīha na jānāmītyātmānaṃ manyase katham ..1-24..*

You are the Reality, forever pure and unchanging. You have no body, no birth, and no death. Why do you think in terms such as "I know the Atman" or "I do not know It"?

### 1.25

तत्त्वमस्यादिवाक्येन स्वात्मा हि प्रतिपादितः ।  
नेति नेति श्रुतिर्ब्रूयादनृतं पाञ्चभौतिकम् ॥ १-२५॥

*tattvamasyādivākyena svātmā hi pratipāditaḥ .  
neti neti śrutirbrūyādanṛtaṃ pāñcabhautikam ..1-25..*

The Atman, the true nature of existence, is established by the great Vedic sayings such as "Thou art That" and "I am Brahman." The body, made of the five elements, is unreal; the Upanishads declare this with the words "Not this, not this."

### 1.26

आत्मन्येवात्मना सर्वं त्वया पूर्णं निरन्तरम् ।  
ध्याता ध्यानं न ते चित्तं निर्लज्जं ध्यायते कथम् ॥ १-२६॥

*ātmanyevātmanā sarvaṃ tvayā pūrṇaṃ nirantaram .  
dhyātā dhyānaṃ na te cittaṃ nirlajjaṃ dhyāyate katham ..1-26..*

Truly, everything in this universe is filled completely by your Atman alone. So in your mind there is neither a meditator nor any meditation. How can you go on meditating without feeling any shame?

### 1.27

शिवं न जानामि कथं वदामि  
शिवं न जानामि कथं भजामि ।  
अहं शिवश्चेत्परमार्थतत्त्वं  
समस्वरूपं गगनोपमं च ॥ १-२७॥

*śivaṃ na jānāmi kathaṃ vadāmi  
śivaṃ na jānāmi kathaṃ bhajāmi .  
ahaṃ śivaścetparamārthatattvaṃ  
samasvarūpaṃ gaganopamaṃ ca ..1-27..*

How can I speak of or worship that Supreme Bliss, which I cannot know as an object of knowledge? For I myself am that Supreme Bliss, the Ultimate Reality, complete by nature and pervading all like space.

### 1.28

नाहं तत्त्वं समं तत्त्वं कल्पनाहेतुवर्जितम् ।  
ग्राह्यग्राहकनिर्मुक्तं स्वसंवेद्यं कथं भवेत् ॥ १-२८॥

*nāhaṃ tattvaṃ samaṃ tattvaṃ kalpanāhetuvarjitam .  
grāhyagrāhakanirmuktaṃ svasaṃvedyaṃ kathaṃ bhavet ..1-28..*

I am not the cosmic principles. I am that same Reality, beyond inference and reasoning, free of both subject and object. How could I know my own Self?

### 1.29

अनन्तरूपं न हि वस्तु किञ्चि-  
त्तत्त्वस्वरूपं न हि वस्तु किञ्चित् ।  
आत्मैकरूपं परमार्थतत्त्वं  
न हिंसको वापि न चाप्यहिंसा ॥ १-२९॥

*anantarūpaṃ na hi vastu kiñci-  
ttattvasvarūpaṃ na hi vastu kiñcit .  
ātmaikarūpaṃ paramārthatattvaṃ  
na hiṃsako vāpi na cāpyahiṃsā ..1-29..*

No object can be infinite by nature. Matter cannot be Reality Itself. Truly, the Atman alone is the Supreme Reality. It is neither harmful nor harmless.

### 1.30

विशुद्धोऽसि समं तत्त्वं विदेहमजमव्ययम् ।  
विभ्रमं कथमात्मार्थे विभ्रान्तोऽहं कथं पुनः ॥ १-३०॥

*viśuddho'si samaṃ tattvaṃ videhamajamavyayam .  
vibhramaṃ kathamātmārthe vibhrānto'haṃ kathaṃ punaḥ ..1-30..*

You are that pure, bodiless, unborn, indestructible, unchanging Reality. How could there be any confusion about the Atman? And how, then, can you say, "I am confused"?

### 1.31

घटे भिन्ने घटाकाशं सुलीनं भेदवर्जितम् ।  
शिवेन मनसा शुद्धो न भेदः प्रतिभाति मे ॥ १-३१॥

*ghaṭe bhinne ghaṭākāśaṃ sulīnaṃ bhedavarjitam .  
śivena manasā śuddho na bhedaḥ pratibhāti me ..1-31..*

When a jar breaks, the space inside the jar merges completely into undivided space. In the same way, when the mind is purified, it merges into the Supreme Bliss. Hence I perceive no diversity.

### 1.32

न घटो न घटाकाशो न जीवो जीवविग्रहः ।  
केवलं ब्रह्म संविद्धि वेद्यवेदकवर्जितम् ॥ १-३२॥

*na ghaṭo na ghaṭākāśo na jīvo jīvavigrahaḥ .  
kevalaṃ brahma saṃviddhi vedyavedakavarjitam ..1-32..*

Distinctions like jar and jar-space, or individual body and individual soul, do not exist in Brahman. Realize that Absolute Brahman, which can neither be known nor be made known.

### 1.33

सर्वत्र सर्वदा सर्वमात्मानं सततं ध्रुवम् ।  
सर्वं शून्यमशून्यं च तन्मां विद्धि न संशयः ॥ १-३३॥

*sarvatra sarvadā sarvamātmānaṃ satataṃ dhruvam .  
sarvaṃ śūnyamaśūnyaṃ ca tanmāṃ viddhi na saṃśayaḥ ..1-33..*

The Atman exists at all times, in all places, and in all things. It is eternal and unchanging. Everything in this world is empty, and at the same time it is full of the Atman. Realize: I am that Atman. Have no doubt about it.

### 1.34

वेदा न लोका न सुरा न यज्ञा  
वर्णाश्रमो नैव कुलं न जातिः ।  
न धूममार्गो न च दीप्तिमार्गो  
ब्रह्मैकरूपं परमार्थतत्त्वम् ॥ १-३४॥

*vedā na lokā na surā na yajñā  
varṇāśramo naiva kulaṃ na jātiḥ .  
na dhūmamārgo na ca dīptimārgo  
brahmaikarūpaṃ paramārthatattvam ..1-34..*

In Brahman there are no Vedas, no worlds, no gods, no sacrifices; no stages of life, no castes, no race, no lineage; neither the path of smoke nor the path of light. Only that same Brahman, the Supreme Reality, exists.

### 1.35

व्याप्यव्यापकनिर्मुक्तः त्वमेकः सफलं यदि ।  
प्रत्यक्षं चापरोक्षं च ह्यात्मानं मन्यसे कथम् ॥ १-३५॥

*vyāpyavyāpakanirmuktaḥ tvamekaḥ saphalaṃ yadi .  
pratyakṣaṃ cāparokṣaṃ ca hyātmānaṃ manyase katham ..1-35..*

If you have realized that Absolute One, free of both pervader and pervaded, how can you regard the Atman as either perceptible or imperceptible?

### 1.36

अद्वैतं केचिदिच्छन्ति द्वैतमिच्छन्ति चापरे ।  
समं तत्त्वं न विन्दन्ति द्वैताद्वैतविवर्जितम् ॥ १-३६॥

*advaitaṃ kecidicchanti dvaitamicchanti cāpare .  
samaṃ tattvaṃ na vindanti dvaitādvaitavivarjitam ..1-36..*

Some choose to be non-dualists and others choose to be dualists, but none of them truly knows that same Brahman, which is free of both duality and non-duality.

### 1.37

श्वेतादिवर्णरहितं शब्दादिगुणवर्जितम् ।  
कथयन्ति कथं तत्त्वं मनोवाचामगोचरम् ॥ १-३७॥

*śvetādivarṇarahitaṃ śabdādiguṇavarjitam .  
kathayanti kathaṃ tattvaṃ manovācāmagocaram ..1-37..*

Brahman has no colours such as white, blue, or yellow; It has no qualities such as sound, touch, or smell. How can people describe Brahman, which lies beyond mind and speech?

### 1.38

यदाऽनृतमिदं सर्वं देहादिगगनोपमम् ।  
तदा हि ब्रह्म संवेत्ति न ते द्वैतपरम्परा ॥ १-३८॥

*yadā'nṛtamidaṃ sarvaṃ dehādigaganopamam .  
tadā hi brahma saṃvetti na te dvaitaparamparā ..1-38..*

When everything in this world, the body included, becomes unreal and empty like space, then one truly knows Brahman. For such a person there is no longer any procession of dualities.

### 1.39

परेण सहजात्मापि ह्यभिन्नः प्रतिभाति मे ।  
व्योमाकारं तथैवैकं ध्याता ध्यानं कथं भवेत् ॥ १-३९॥

*pareṇa sahajātmāpi hyabhinnaḥ pratibhāti me .  
vyomākāraṃ tathaivaikaṃ dhyātā dhyānaṃ kathaṃ bhavet ..1-39..*

It seems to me that my own inner self and the Supreme Self are truly one and the same, exactly like a single unbroken space. How then can there be a meditator and a meditation?

### 1.40

यत्करोमि यदश्नामि यज्जुहोमि ददामि यत् ।  
एतत्सर्वं न मे किञ्चिद्विशुद्धोऽहमजोऽव्ययः ॥ १-४०॥

*yatkaromi yadaśnāmi yajjuhomi dadāmi yat .  
etatsarvaṃ na me kiñcidviśuddho'hamajo'vyayaḥ ..1-40..*

Whatever I do, whatever I eat, whatever I offer in sacrifice, whatever I give away: none of it belongs to me. I am forever pure, unborn, and unchanging.

### 1.41

सर्वं जगद्विद्धि निराकृतीदं  
सर्वं जगद्विद्धि विकारहीनम् ।  
सर्वं जगद्विद्धि विशुद्धदेहं  
सर्वं जगद्विद्धि शिवैकरूपम् ॥ १-४१॥

*sarvaṃ jagadviddhi nirākṛtīdaṃ  
sarvaṃ jagadviddhi vikārahīnam .  
sarvaṃ jagadviddhi viśuddhadehaṃ  
sarvaṃ jagadviddhi śivaikarūpam ..1-41..*

Know this entire universe to be without form. Know this entire universe to be without change. Know this entire universe to be pure existence itself. Know this entire universe to be of the nature of Supreme Bliss.

### 1.42

तत्त्वं त्वं न हि सन्देहः किं जानाम्यथवा पुनः ।  
असंवेद्यं स्वसंवेद्यमात्मानं मन्यसे कथम् ॥ १-४२॥

*tattvaṃ tvaṃ na hi sandehaḥ kiṃ jānāmyathavā punaḥ .  
asaṃvedyaṃ svasaṃvedyamātmānaṃ manyase katham ..1-42..*

Beyond doubt, you are that Supreme Reality. Why then do you wonder, "Do I know the Atman or not?" or "Is It something experienced, or is It beyond comprehension?"

### 1.43

मायाऽमाया कथं तात छायाऽछाया न विद्यते ।  
तत्त्वमेकमिदं सर्वं व्योमाकारं निरञ्जनम् ॥ १-४३॥

*māyā'māyā kathaṃ tāta chāyā'chāyā na vidyate .  
tattvamekamidaṃ sarvaṃ vyomākāraṃ nirañjanam ..1-43..*

My dear one, just as darkness and light cannot exist together, how could ignorance and knowledge both be present in Brahman? All of this is that one spotless, formless Reality.

### 1.44

आदिमध्यान्तमुक्तोऽहं न बद्धोऽहं कदाचन ।  
स्वभावनिर्मलः शुद्ध इति मे निश्चिता मतिः ॥ १-४४॥

*ādimadhyāntamukto'haṃ na baddho'haṃ kadācana .  
svabhāvanirmalaḥ śuddha iti me niścitā matiḥ ..1-44..*

I have no beginning, no middle, and no end. I am never bound. By nature I am pure and perfect; of this I am firmly convinced.

### 1.45

महदादि जगत्सर्वं न किञ्चित्प्रतिभाति मे ।  
ब्रह्मैव केवलं सर्वं कथं वर्णाश्रमस्थितिः ॥ १-४५॥

*mahadādi jagatsarvaṃ na kiñcitpratibhāti me .  
brahmaiva kevalaṃ sarvaṃ kathaṃ varṇāśramasthitiḥ ..1-45..*

I perceive nothing, from the cosmic intelligence down to the manifest universe. Truly, all of this is Brahman. How can caste and the stages of life continue to apply?

### 1.46

जानामि सर्वथा सर्वमहमेको निरन्तरम् ।  
निरालम्बमशून्यं च शून्यं व्योमादिपञ्चकम् ॥ १-४६॥

*jānāmi sarvathā sarvamahameko nirantaram .  
nirālambamaśūnyaṃ ca śūnyaṃ vyomādipañcakam ..1-46..*

I know in every way that I am the one Reality, pervading all, independent, and existing forever. The five elements, space, air, fire, water, and earth, are all unreal.

### 1.47

न षण्ढो न पुमान्न स्त्री न बोधो नैव कल्पना ।  
सानन्दो वा निरानन्दमात्मानं मन्यसे कथम् ॥ १-४७॥

*na ṣaṇḍho na pumānna strī na bodho naiva kalpanā .  
sānando vā nirānandamātmānaṃ manyase katham ..1-47..*

The Atman is not male, not female, and not neuter. It is not an object of perception or inference. Why then do you brood over whether the Atman is blissful or without bliss?

### 1.48

षडङ्गयोगान्न तु नैव शुद्धं  
मनोविनाशान्न तु नैव शुद्धम् ।  
गुरूपदेशान्न तु नैव शुद्धं  
स्वयं च तत्त्वं स्वयमेव बुद्धम् ॥ १-४८॥

*ṣaḍaṅgayogānna tu naiva śuddhaṃ  
manovināśānna tu naiva śuddham .  
gurūpadeśānna tu naiva śuddhaṃ  
svayaṃ ca tattvaṃ svayameva buddham ..1-48..*

The Atman cannot be purified by practising the six limbs of yoga, by destroying the mind, or by a teacher's instruction. The Atman is Reality Itself and Purity Itself.

### 1.49

न हि पञ्चात्मको देहो विदेहो वर्तते न हि ।  
आत्मैव केवलं सर्वं तुरीयं च त्रयं कथम् ॥ १-४९॥

*na hi pañcātmako deho videho vartate na hi .  
ātmaiva kevalaṃ sarvaṃ turīyaṃ ca trayaṃ katham ..1-49..*

The Atman neither has a body made of the five elements nor is It bodiless. Truly, everything is the Atman. How can the three states, and the Fourth, be attributed to the Atman?

### 1.50

न बद्धो नैव मुक्तोऽहं न चाहं ब्रह्मणः पृथक् ।  
न कर्ता न च भोक्ताहं व्याप्यव्यापकवर्जितः ॥ १-५०॥

*na baddho naiva mukto'haṃ na cāhaṃ brahmaṇaḥ pṛthak .  
na kartā na ca bhoktāhaṃ vyāpyavyāpakavarjitaḥ ..1-50..*

In truth I am neither bound nor free, nor am I separate from Brahman. I am neither the doer nor the one who enjoys, neither the pervader nor the pervaded.

### 1.51

यथा जलं जले न्यस्तं सलिलं भेदवर्जितम् ।  
प्रकृतिं पुरुषं तद्वदभिन्नं प्रतिभाति मे ॥ १-५१॥

*yathā jalaṃ jale nyastaṃ salilaṃ bhedavarjitam .  
prakṛtiṃ puruṣaṃ tadvadabhinnaṃ pratibhāti me ..1-51..*

Just as water poured into water remains the same undivided water, so prakriti (matter) and Purusha (Spirit) both appear to me as one and the same.

### 1.52

यदि नाम न मुक्तोऽसि न बद्धोऽसि कदाचन ।  
साकारं च निराकारमात्मानं मन्यसे कथम् ॥ १-५२॥

*yadi nāma na mukto'si na baddho'si kadācana .  
sākāraṃ ca nirākāramātmānaṃ manyase katham ..1-52..*

If you are indeed not free, then neither have you ever been bound. How then can you think of yourself as having form (when bound) or as formless (when liberated)?

### 1.53

जानामि ते परं रूपं प्रत्यक्षं गगनोपमम् ।  
यथा परं हि रूपं यन्मरीचिजलसन्निभम् ॥ १-५३॥

*jānāmi te paraṃ rūpaṃ pratyakṣaṃ gaganopamam .  
yathā paraṃ hi rūpaṃ yanmarīcijalasannibham ..1-53..*

I know your supreme form, which is ever present and pervades everything like space. Likewise I know your lower form, which is indeed like the water of a mirage.

### 1.54

न गुरुर्नोपदेशश्च न चोपाधिर्न मे क्रिया ।  
विदेहं गगनं विद्धि विशुद्धोऽहं स्वभावतः ॥ १-५४॥

*na gururnopadeśaśca na copādhirna me kriyā .  
videhaṃ gaganaṃ viddhi viśuddho'haṃ svabhāvataḥ ..1-54..*

I have no guru and no teaching, no attributes and no action. Know for certain that I am without body, like space, and absolutely pure by nature.

### 1.55

विशुद्धोऽस्य शरीरोऽसि न ते चित्तं परात्परम् ।  
अहं चात्मा परं तत्त्वमिति वक्तुं न लज्जसे ॥ १-५५॥

*viśuddho'sya śarīro'si na te cittaṃ parātparam .  
ahaṃ cātmā paraṃ tattvamiti vaktuṃ na lajjase ..1-55..*

You are absolutely pure. You have no body and no mind, and you are beyond maya. Do not be ashamed to declare, "I am the Atman, the Supreme Reality."

### 1.56

कथं रोदिषि रे चित्त ह्यात्मैवात्मात्मना भव ।  
पिब वत्स कलातीतमद्वैतं परमामृतम् ॥ १-५६॥

*kathaṃ rodiṣi re citta hyātmaivātmātmanā bhava .  
piba vatsa kalātītamadvaitaṃ paramāmṛtam ..1-56..*

O mind, why do you weep? You truly are the Atman. Become one with It. Drink, my dear one, the supreme nectar of the limitless ocean of non-dual Brahman.

### 1.57

नैव बोधो न चाबोधो न बोधाबोध एव च ।  
यस्येदृशः सदा बोधः स बोधो नान्यथा भवेत् ॥ १-५७॥

*naiva bodho na cābodho na bodhābodha eva ca .  
yasyedṛśaḥ sadā bodhaḥ sa bodho nānyathā bhavet ..1-57..*

In the Atman there is no knowledge, no ignorance, and no mixture of the two. Whoever holds this knowledge at all times becomes knowledge Itself and nothing else.

### 1.58

ज्ञानं न तर्को न समाधियोगो  
न देशकालौ न गुरूपदेशः ।  
स्वभावसंवित्तरहं च तत्त्व-  
माकाशकल्पं सहजं ध्रुवं च ॥ १-५८॥

*jñānaṃ na tarko na samādhiyogo  
na deśakālau na gurūpadeśaḥ .  
svabhāvasaṃvittarahaṃ ca tattva-  
mākāśakalpaṃ sahajaṃ dhruvaṃ ca ..1-58..*

Knowledge of the Self does not rest on reasoning, on the practice of meditation, on a guru's instruction, or on anything within space and time. By nature I am that absolute Knowledge, the Reality that is innate, eternal, and as boundless as space.

### 1.59

न जातोऽहं मृतो वापि न मे कर्म शुभाशुभम् ।  
विशुद्धं निर्गुणं ब्रह्म बन्धो मुक्तिः कथं मम ॥ १-५९॥

*na jāto'haṃ mṛto vāpi na me karma śubhāśubham .  
viśuddhaṃ nirguṇaṃ brahma bandho muktiḥ kathaṃ mama ..1-59..*

I was never born and I will never die. Nor do I perform any action, good or bad. I am that pure Brahman without attributes. How could there be bondage or liberation for me?

### 1.60

यदि सर्वगतो देवः स्थिरः पूर्णो निरन्तरः ।  
अन्तरं हि न पश्यामि स बाह्याभ्यन्तरः कथम् ॥ १-६०॥

*yadi sarvagato devaḥ sthiraḥ pūrṇo nirantaraḥ .  
antaraṃ hi na paśyāmi sa bāhyābhyantaraḥ katham ..1-60..*

Since Brahman is present everywhere, luminous, motionless, full, and unbroken, I see no diversity at all. How could It be inside or outside?

### 1.61

स्फुरत्येव जगत्कृत्स्नमखण्डितनिरन्तरम् ।  
अहो मायामहामोहो द्वैताद्वैतविकल्पना ॥ १-६१॥

*sphuratyeva jagatkṛtsnamakhaṇḍitanirantaram .  
aho māyāmahāmoho dvaitādvaitavikalpanā ..1-61..*

To me the entire universe appears as one undivided, unbroken whole. Ah, what a dreadful delusion of maya has produced the split between duality and non-duality!

### 1.62

साकारं च निराकारं नेति नेतीति सर्वदा ।  
भेदाभेदविनिर्मुक्तो वर्तते केवलः शिवः ॥ १-६२॥

*sākāraṃ ca nirākāraṃ neti netīti sarvadā .  
bhedābhedavinirmukto vartate kevalaḥ śivaḥ ..1-62..*

Every object, whether it has a form or has none, is always denied with the words "Not this, not this." What alone exists is the Supreme Beatitude, free from both difference and sameness.

### 1.63

न ते च माता च पिता च बन्धुः  
न ते च पत्नी न सुतश्च मित्रम् ।  
न पक्षपाती न विपक्षपातः  
कथं हि सन्तप्तिरियं हि चित्ते ॥ १-६३॥

*na te ca mātā ca pitā ca bandhuḥ  
na te ca patnī na sutaśca mitram .  
na pakṣapātī na vipakṣapātaḥ  
kathaṃ hi santaptiriyaṃ hi citte ..1-63..*

You have no mother, no father, no relative, no wife, no child, no friend. You have neither favoritism nor indifference. Why, then, is there so much sorrow in your mind?

### 1.64

दिवा नक्तं न ते चित्तं उदयास्तमयौ न हि ।  
विदेहस्य शरीरत्वं कल्पयन्ति कथं बुधाः ॥ १-६४॥

*divā naktaṃ na te cittaṃ udayāstamayau na hi .  
videhasya śarīratvaṃ kalpayanti kathaṃ budhāḥ ..1-64..*

O mind, for you there is no day and no night, no rising and no setting. How could the wise picture a bodiless being as having a body?

### 1.65

नाविभक्तं विभक्तं च न हि दुःखसुखादि च ।  
न हि सर्वमसर्वं च विद्धि चात्मानमव्ययम् ॥ १-६५॥

*nāvibhaktaṃ vibhaktaṃ ca na hi duḥkhasukhādi ca .  
na hi sarvamasarvaṃ ca viddhi cātmānamavyayam ..1-65..*

Know the unchanging Atman, which is neither the whole nor a part, neither undivided nor divided. It lies beyond both happiness and misery.

### 1.66

नाहं कर्ता न भोक्ता च न मे कर्म पुराऽधुना ।  
न मे देहो विदेहो वा निर्ममेति ममेति किम् ॥ १-६६॥

*nāhaṃ kartā na bhoktā ca na me karma purā'dhunā .  
na me deho videho vā nirmameti mameti kim ..1-66..*

I am not the one who acts, nor the one who enjoys. In me there is no action, past or present. Since I hold no notion of having a body or of being without one, how could I call it mine or not mine?

### 1.67

न मे रागादिको दोषो दुःखं देहादिकं न मे ।  
आत्मानं विद्धि मामेकं विशालं गगनोपमम् ॥ १-६७॥

*na me rāgādiko doṣo duḥkhaṃ dehādikaṃ na me .  
ātmānaṃ viddhi māmekaṃ viśālaṃ gaganopamam ..1-67..*

I am free of weaknesses such as passion and anger. I am free of the misery that belongs to the body, the mind, and the rest. Know that I am that Atman, one and as vast as space.

### 1.68

सखे मनः किं बहुजल्पितेन  
सखे मनः सर्वमिदं वितर्क्यम् ।  
यत्सारभूतं कथितं मया ते  
त्वमेव तत्त्वं गगनोपमोऽसि ॥ १-६८॥

*sakhe manaḥ kiṃ bahujalpitena  
sakhe manaḥ sarvamidaṃ vitarkyam .  
yatsārabhūtaṃ kathitaṃ mayā te  
tvameva tattvaṃ gaganopamo'si ..1-68..*

My friend the mind, what good is all this empty talk? My friend the mind, all of it is mere speculation. I have told you the essence: you truly are the Reality, limitless as space.

### 1.69

येन केनापि भावेन यत्र कुत्र मृता अपि ।  
योगिनस्तत्र लीयन्ते घटाकाशमिवाम्बरे ॥ १-६९॥

*yena kenāpi bhāvena yatra kutra mṛtā api .  
yoginastatra līyante ghaṭākāśamivāmbare ..1-69..*

However and wherever yogis die, they merge into Brahman, just as the space inside a jar joins the boundless space when the jar breaks.

### 1.70

तीर्थे चान्त्यजगेहे वा नष्टस्मृतिरपि त्यजन् ।  
समकाले तनुं मुक्तः कैवल्यव्यापको भवेत् ॥ १-७०॥

*tīrthe cāntyajagehe vā naṣṭasmṛtirapi tyajan .  
samakāle tanuṃ muktaḥ kaivalyavyāpako bhavet ..1-70..*

A yogi may die at a sacred site or in the home of an outcaste, or even die unconscious; but the moment he leaves the body he becomes one with the Absolute Brahman.

### 1.71

धर्मार्थकाममोक्षांश्च द्विपदादिचराचरम् ।  
मन्यन्ते योगिनः सर्वं मरीचिजलसन्निभम् ॥ १-७१॥

*dharmārthakāmamokṣāṃśca dvipadādicarācaram .  
manyante yoginaḥ sarvaṃ marīcijalasannibham ..1-71..*

To the yogis, everything, duty, wealth, pleasures, liberation, and all things moving and unmoving such as people and trees, is like water in a mirage.

### 1.72

अतीतानागतं कर्म वर्तमानं तथैव च ।  
न करोमि न भुञ्जामि इति मे निश्चला मतिः ॥ १-७२॥

*atītānāgataṃ karma vartamānaṃ tathaiva ca .  
na karomi na bhuñjāmi iti me niścalā matiḥ ..1-72..*

I did not act in the past, I do not act now, and I will not act in the future. Nor did I, do I, or will I enjoy the fruits of such actions. Of this I am firmly convinced.

### 1.73

शून्यागारे समरसपूत-  
स्तिष्ठन्नेकः सुखमवधूतः ।  
चरति हि नग्नस्त्यक्त्वा गर्वं  
विन्दति केवलमात्मनि सर्वम् ॥ १-७३॥

*śūnyāgāre samarasapūta-  
stiṣṭhannekaḥ sukhamavadhūtaḥ .  
carati hi nagnastyaktvā garvaṃ  
vindati kevalamātmani sarvam ..1-73..*

The Avadhuta dwells happily by himself in a solitary place, cleansed by the unbroken bliss of Brahman. Having given up the ego, the wandering Avadhuta roams about and finds everything within his own Self.

### 1.74

त्रितयतुरीयं न हि नहि यत्र  
विन्दति केवलमात्मनि तत्र ।  
धर्माधर्मौ न हि नहि यत्र  
बद्धो मुक्तः कथमिह तत्र ॥ १-७४॥

*tritayaturīyaṃ na hi nahi yatra  
vindati kevalamātmani tatra .  
dharmādharmau na hi nahi yatra  
baddho muktaḥ kathamiha tatra ..1-74..*

Where there is no threefold state and not even the Fourth (the Transcendental), there one reaches the Absolute Atman. Where there is neither virtue nor vice, how could bondage or liberation exist?

### 1.75

विन्दति विन्दति न हि नहि मन्त्रं  
छन्दोलक्षणं न हि नहि तन्त्रम् ।  
समरसमग्नो भावितपूतः  
प्रलपितमेतत्परमवधूतः ॥ १-७५॥

*vindati vindati na hi nahi mantraṃ  
chandolakṣaṇaṃ na hi nahi tantram .  
samarasamagno bhāvitapūtaḥ  
pralapitametatparamavadhūtaḥ ..1-75..*

Brahman cannot be reached by repeating mantras, reciting the Vedas, or performing tantric rites. The great Avadhuta, having purified himself through meditation and become absorbed in the unbroken bliss of Brahman, sang this Gita spontaneously.

### 1.76

सर्वशून्यमशून्यं च सत्यासत्यं न विद्यते ।  
स्वभावभावतः प्रोक्तं शास्त्रसंवित्तिपूर्वकम् ॥ १-७६॥

*sarvaśūnyamaśūnyaṃ ca satyāsatyaṃ na vidyate .  
svabhāvabhāvataḥ proktaṃ śāstrasaṃvittipūrvakam ..1-76..*

Everything is empty and at the same time full. In Brahman there is neither truth nor falsehood. The Avadhuta declares this from his own experience and also from his knowledge of the scriptures.

### 2.1

<vt>अथ द्वितीयोऽध्यायः</vt>  
बालस्य वा विषयभोगरतस्य वापि  
मूर्खस्य सेवकजनस्य गृहस्थितस्य ।  
एतद्गुरोः किमपि नैव न चिन्तनीयं  
रत्नं कथं त्यजति कोऽप्यशुचौ प्रविष्टम् ॥ २-१॥

*<vt>Chapter 2</vt>  
bālasya vā viṣayabhogaratasya vāpi  
mūrkhasya sevakajanasya gṛhasthitasya .  
etadguroḥ kimapi naiva na cintanīyaṃ  
ratnaṃ kathaṃ tyajati ko'pyaśucau praviṣṭam ..2-1..*

A guru may be young, or someone who enjoys worldly pleasures; he may be unlettered, a servant, or a householder. None of this should count. Does anyone throw away a gem because it fell in the dirt?

### 2.2

नैवात्र काव्यगुण एव तु चिन्तनीयो  
ग्राह्यः परं गुणवता खलु सार एव ।  
सिन्दूरचित्ररहिता भुवि रूपशून्या  
पारं न किं नयति नौरिह गन्तुकामान् ॥ २-२॥

*naivātra kāvyaguṇa eva tu cintanīyo  
grāhyaḥ paraṃ guṇavatā khalu sāra eva .  
sindūracitrarahitā bhuvi rūpaśūnyā  
pāraṃ na kiṃ nayati nauriha gantukāmān ..2-2..*

A guru's teaching should not be judged by its literary quality. Intelligent people take the essence. Can a boat not carry passengers across the ocean even if it is unpainted and ugly?

### 2.3

प्रयत्नेन विना येन निश्चलेन चलाचलम् ।  
ग्रस्तं स्वभावतः शान्तं चैतन्यं गगनोपमम् ॥ २-३॥

*prayatnena vinā yena niścalena calācalam .  
grastaṃ svabhāvataḥ śāntaṃ caitanyaṃ gaganopamam ..2-3..*

The motionless Brahman holds everything that moves and everything that does not, without any effort. By nature It is calm, conscious, and all-pervading like space.

### 2.4

अयत्नाछालयेद्यस्तु एकमेव चराचरम् ।  
सर्वगं तत्कथं भिन्नमद्वैतं वर्तते मम ॥ २-४॥

*ayatnāchālayedyastu ekameva carācaram .  
sarvagaṃ tatkathaṃ bhinnamadvaitaṃ vartate mama ..2-4..*

Truly, that all-present Brahman alone directs, without effort, all that moves and all that stands still. How, then, could that non-dual Brahman be different from me?

### 2.5

अहमेव परं यस्मात्सारात्सारतरं शिवम् ।  
गमागमविनिर्मुक्तं निर्विकल्पं निराकुलम् ॥ २-५॥

*ahameva paraṃ yasmātsārātsārataraṃ śivam .  
gamāgamavinirmuktaṃ nirvikalpaṃ nirākulam ..2-5..*

Because I am that Supreme Beatitude, I am beyond what is essential and what is not. I am free from birth and death, from doubt and confusion.

### 2.6

सर्वावयवनिर्मुक्तं तथाहं त्रिदशार्चितम् ।  
सम्पूर्णत्वान्न गृह्णामि विभागं त्रिदशादिकम् ॥ २-६॥

*sarvāvayavanirmuktaṃ tathāhaṃ tridaśārcitam .  
sampūrṇatvānna gṛhṇāmi vibhāgaṃ tridaśādikam ..2-6..*

Having no constituent parts, I am worshipped by the gods. Since I am that undivided Being, I recognize no distinctions among the gods (such as Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva).

### 2.7

प्रमादेन न सन्देहः किं करिष्यामि वृत्तिमान् ।  
उत्पद्यन्ते विलीयन्ते बुद्बुदाश्च यथा जले ॥ २-७॥

*pramādena na sandehaḥ kiṃ kariṣyāmi vṛttimān .  
utpadyante vilīyante budbudāśca yathā jale ..2-7..*

Ignorance cannot raise any doubt in me. Why should I be concerned with the waves of the mind? They rise and vanish like bubbles on water.

### 2.8

महदादीनि भूतानि समाप्यैवं सदैव हि ।  
मृदुद्रव्येषु तीक्ष्णेषु गुडेषु कटुकेषु च ॥ २-८॥

*mahadādīni bhūtāni samāpyaivaṃ sadaiva hi .  
mṛdudravyeṣu tīkṣṇeṣu guḍeṣu kaṭukeṣu ca ..2-8..*

Just as softness, hardness, sweetness, and bitterness are always bound to the things that have them, so cosmic intelligence and the other elements are always pervaded by Brahman.

### 2.9

कटुत्वं चैव शैत्यत्वं मृदुत्वं च यथा जले ।  
प्रकृतिः पुरुषस्तद्वदभिन्नं प्रतिभाति मे ॥ २-९॥

*kaṭutvaṃ caiva śaityatvaṃ mṛdutvaṃ ca yathā jale .  
prakṛtiḥ puruṣastadvadabhinnaṃ pratibhāti me ..2-9..*

Just as water may be hot, cold, or lukewarm and still remain water, so, it seems to me, prakriti (matter) and Purusha (Spirit) are one and the same.

### 2.10

सर्वाख्यारहितं यद्यत्सूक्ष्मात्सूक्ष्मतरं परम् ।  
मनोबुद्धीन्द्रियातीतमकलङ्कं जगत्पतिम् ॥ २-१०॥

*sarvākhyārahitaṃ yadyatsūkṣmātsūkṣmataraṃ param .  
manobuddhīndriyātītamakalaṅkaṃ jagatpatim ..2-10..*

Because Brahman is finer than the finest, It has no name. It is beyond the senses, the mind, and the intellect. It is the ever-radiant Lord of the universe.

### 2.11

ईदृशं सहजं यत्र अहं तत्र कथं भवेत् ।  
त्वमेव हि कथं तत्र कथं तत्र चराचरम् ॥ २-११॥

*īdṛśaṃ sahajaṃ yatra ahaṃ tatra kathaṃ bhavet .  
tvameva hi kathaṃ tatra kathaṃ tatra carācaram ..2-11..*

When Brahman is such a self-existent reality, how can there be an "I" or a "you," or this universe of moving and unmoving things?

### 2.12

गगनोपमं तु यत्प्रोक्तं तदेव गगनोपमम् ।  
चैतन्यं दोषहीनं च सर्वज्ञं पूर्णमेव च ॥ २-१२॥

*gaganopamaṃ tu yatproktaṃ tadeva gaganopamam .  
caitanyaṃ doṣahīnaṃ ca sarvajñaṃ pūrṇameva ca ..2-12..*

Brahman has been likened to space, and indeed, like space, It pervades everything. Brahman is pure consciousness, all-knowing, and complete.

### 2.13

पृथिव्यां चरितं नैव मारुतेन च वाहितम् ।  
वरिणा पिहितं नैव तेजोमध्ये व्यवस्थितम् ॥ २-१३॥

*pṛthivyāṃ caritaṃ naiva mārutena ca vāhitam .  
variṇā pihitaṃ naiva tejomadhye vyavasthitam ..2-13..*

Truly, Brahman does not walk upon the earth, nor is It borne by the wind; It is not sunk in water, nor does It live in fire.

### 2.14

आकाशं तेन संव्याप्तं न तद्व्याप्तं च केनचित् ।  
स बाह्याभ्यन्तरं तिष्ठत्यवच्छिन्नं निरन्तरम् ॥ २-१४॥

*ākāśaṃ tena saṃvyāptaṃ na tadvyāptaṃ ca kenacit .  
sa bāhyābhyantaraṃ tiṣṭhatyavacchinnaṃ nirantaram ..2-14..*

Space is wholly pervaded by Brahman, yet nothing pervades Brahman. It dwells within and without, undivided and unbroken.

### 2.15

सूक्ष्मत्वात्तददृश्यत्वान्निर्गुणत्वाच्च योगिभिः ।  
आलम्बनादि यत्प्रोक्तं क्रमादालम्बनं भवेत् ॥ २-१५॥

*sūkṣmatvāttadadṛśyatvānnirguṇatvācca yogibhiḥ .  
ālambanādi yatproktaṃ kramādālambanaṃ bhavet ..2-15..*

Since Brahman is subtle, unseen, and without qualities, whatever methods the yogis have laid down for realizing It should be followed, step by step.

### 2.16

सतताऽभ्यासयुक्तस्तु निरालम्बो यदा भवेत् ।  
तल्लयाल्लीयते नान्तर्गुणदोषविवर्जितः ॥ २-१६॥

*satatā'bhyāsayuktastu nirālambo yadā bhavet .  
tallayāllīyate nāntarguṇadoṣavivarjitaḥ ..2-16..*

Through steady practice of yoga the mind stops clinging to any object; and when objects fall away, the mind dissolves into the Final Cause, leaving good and evil behind.

### 2.17

विषविश्वस्य रौद्रस्य मोहमूर्च्छाप्रदस्य च ।  
एकमेव विनाशाय ह्यमोघं सहजामृतम् ॥ २-१७॥

*viṣaviśvasya raudrasya mohamūrcchāpradasya ca .  
ekameva vināśāya hyamoghaṃ sahajāmṛtam ..2-17..*

For the deadly poison of worldliness, which brings on the stupor of delusion, there is only one unfailing remedy: the ambrosia of Brahman that is one's own nature.

### 2.18

भावगम्यं निराकारं साकारं दृष्टिगोचरम् ।  
भावाभावविनिर्मुक्तमन्तरालं तदुच्यते ॥ २-१८॥

*bhāvagamyaṃ nirākāraṃ sākāraṃ dṛṣṭigocaram .  
bhāvābhāvavinirmuktamantarālaṃ taducyate ..2-18..*

The formless can be thought of, and a form can be pictured. But Brahman is neither being nor non-being. It may be called the Innermost One.

### 2.19

बाह्यभावं भवेद्विश्वमन्तः प्रकृतिरुच्यते ।  
अन्तरादन्तरं ज्ञेयं नारिकेलफलाम्बुवत् ॥ २-१९॥

*bāhyabhāvaṃ bhavedviśvamantaḥ prakṛtirucyate .  
antarādantaraṃ jñeyaṃ nārikelaphalāmbuvat ..2-19..*

The outer expression is this universe; the inner expression is called prakriti, or maya. Realize Brahman, which is said to be the innermost of the inner, like the water (milk) inside the kernel of a coconut.

### 2.20

भ्रान्तिज्ञानं स्थितं बाह्यं सम्यग्ज्ञानं च मध्यगम् ।  
मध्यान्मध्यतरं ज्ञेयं नारिकेलफलाम्बुवत् ॥ २-२०॥

*bhrāntijñānaṃ sthitaṃ bāhyaṃ samyagjñānaṃ ca madhyagam .  
madhyānmadhyataraṃ jñeyaṃ nārikelaphalāmbuvat ..2-20..*

Knowledge concerned with the outer world is false. True knowledge arises within (in the pure mind). Realize Brahman, the innermost of the inner self, like the water (milk) inside the kernel of a coconut.

### 2.21

पौर्णमास्यां यथा चन्द्र एक एवातिनिर्मलः ।  
तेन तत्सदृशं पश्येद्द्विधादृष्टिर्विपर्ययः ॥ २-२१॥

*paurṇamāsyāṃ yathā candra eka evātinirmalaḥ .  
tena tatsadṛśaṃ paśyeddvidhādṛṣṭirviparyayaḥ ..2-21..*

Just as on a full-moon night one sees the moon as single and brilliant, so one should see Brahman as one and radiant. Seeing duality arises from mistaken perception.

### 2.22

अनेनैव प्रकारेण बुद्धिभेदो न सर्वगः ।  
दाता च धीरतामेति गीयते नामकोटिभिः ॥ २-२२॥

*anenaiva prakāreṇa buddhibhedo na sarvagaḥ .  
dātā ca dhīratāmeti gīyate nāmakoṭibhiḥ ..2-22..*

(Since dualistic vision comes from mistaken perception,) a person of scattered intellect cannot realize the all-present Brahman. Only the pure reach the tranquil state of Brahman, and such a person's name is praised by many.

### 2.23

गुरुप्रज्ञाप्रसादेन मूर्खो वा यदि पण्डितः ।  
यस्तु सम्बुध्यते तत्त्वं विरक्तो भवसागरात् ॥ २-२३॥

*guruprajñāprasādena mūrkho vā yadi paṇḍitaḥ .  
yastu sambudhyate tattvaṃ virakto bhavasāgarāt ..2-23..*

Whether learned or unlettered, anyone who realizes that Truth through the grace of the guru and the grace of his own intellect is set free from the stormy ocean of maya.

### 2.24

रागद्वेषविनिर्मुक्तः सर्वभूतहिते रतः ।  
दृढबोधश्च धीरश्च स गच्छेत्परमं पदम् ॥ २-२४॥

*rāgadveṣavinirmuktaḥ sarvabhūtahite rataḥ .  
dṛḍhabodhaśca dhīraśca sa gacchetparamaṃ padam ..2-24..*

One who reaches the Supreme Reality is free of attachment and aversion, devoted to the good of all beings, serene, and steady in intellect.

### 2.25

घटे भिन्ने घटाकाश आकाशे लीयते यथा ।  
देहाभावे तथा योगी स्वरूपे परमात्मनि ॥ २-२५॥

*ghaṭe bhinne ghaṭākāśa ākāśe līyate yathā .  
dehābhāve tathā yogī svarūpe paramātmani ..2-25..*

Just as the space in a jar becomes one with cosmic space when the jar is destroyed, so the yogi, after death, merges into his true nature, the Supreme Self.

### 2.26

उक्तेयं कर्मयुक्तानां मतिर्यान्तेऽपि सा गतिः ।  
न चोक्ता योगयुक्तानां मतिर्यान्तेऽपि सा गतिः ॥ २-२६॥

*ukteyaṃ karmayuktānāṃ matiryānte'pi sā gatiḥ .  
na coktā yogayuktānāṃ matiryānte'pi sā gatiḥ ..2-26..*

Of those who follow the path of action it is said that they attain whatever state they long for at the moment of death. But this has not been said of those who follow yoga.

### 2.27

या गतिः कर्मयुक्तानां सा च वागिन्द्रियाद्वदेत् ।  
योगिनां या गतिः क्वापि ह्यकथ्या भवतोर्जिता ॥ २-२७॥

*yā gatiḥ karmayuktānāṃ sā ca vāgindriyādvadet .  
yogināṃ yā gatiḥ kvāpi hyakathyā bhavatorjitā ..2-27..*

The destination of those who follow action can be put into words; but the goal of the yogis cannot be expressed, because it is not something to be acquired.

### 2.28

एवं ज्ञात्वा त्वमुं मार्गं योगिनां नैव कल्पितम् ।  
विकल्पवर्जनं तेषां स्वयं सिद्धिः प्रवर्तते ॥ २-२८॥

*evaṃ jñātvā tvamuṃ mārgaṃ yogināṃ naiva kalpitam .  
vikalpavarjanaṃ teṣāṃ svayaṃ siddhiḥ pravartate ..2-28..*

Knowing this, one should not imagine any particular path for the yogis. They abandon desire and doubt, and so their perfection comes about of itself.

### 2.29

तीर्थे वान्त्यजगेहे वा यत्र कुत्र मृतोऽपि वा ।  
न योगी पश्यते गर्भं परे ब्रह्मणि लीयते ॥ २-२९॥

*tīrthe vāntyajagehe vā yatra kutra mṛto'pi vā .  
na yogī paśyate garbhaṃ pare brahmaṇi līyate ..2-29..*

Wherever a yogi dies, whether in a holy place or in the home of an outcaste, he never again enters a mother's womb. He merges into Supreme Brahman.

### 2.30

सहजमजमचिन्त्यं यस्तु पश्येत्स्वरूपं  
घटति यदि यथेष्टं लिप्यते नैव दोषैः ।  
सकृदपि तदभावात्कर्म किञ्चिन्नकुर्यात्  
तदपि न च विबद्धः संयमी वा तपस्वी ॥ २-३०॥

*sahajamajamacintyaṃ yastu paśyetsvarūpaṃ  
ghaṭati yadi yatheṣṭaṃ lipyate naiva doṣaiḥ .  
sakṛdapi tadabhāvātkarma kiñcinnakuryāt  
tadapi na ca vibaddhaḥ saṃyamī vā tapasvī ..2-30..*

One who has realized the Self, which is innate, unborn, and beyond conception, is not stained by any evil, however he may act. And once free from ignorance, he can perform no action at all. For this reason the self-controlled mendicant is never bound. (Bhagavad Gita 5.10)

### 2.31

निरामयं निष्प्रतिमं निराकृतिं  
निराश्रयं निर्वपुषं निराशिषम् ।  
निर्द्वन्द्वनिर्मोहमलुप्तशक्तिकं  
तमीशमात्मानमुपैति शाश्वतम् ॥ २-३१॥

*nirāmayaṃ niṣpratimaṃ nirākṛtiṃ  
nirāśrayaṃ nirvapuṣaṃ nirāśiṣam .  
nirdvandvanirmohamaluptaśaktikaṃ  
tamīśamātmānamupaiti śāśvatam ..2-31..*

The yogi reaches that eternal Supreme Atman, which is pure, without equal, formless, without support, without body, without desire, beyond the pairs of opposites, free of delusion, and undiminished in power.

### 2.32

वेदो न दीक्षा न च मुण्डनक्रिया  
गुरुर्न शिष्यो न च यन्त्रसम्पदः ।  
मुद्रादिकं चापि न यत्र भासते  
तमीशमात्मानमुपैति शाश्वतम् ॥ २-३२॥

*vedo na dīkṣā na ca muṇḍanakriyā  
gururna śiṣyo na ca yantrasampadaḥ .  
mudrādikaṃ cāpi na yatra bhāsate  
tamīśamātmānamupaiti śāśvatam ..2-32..*

The yogi reaches that eternal Supreme Atman, which neither the Vedas, nor initiation, nor shaving of the head, nor guru, nor disciple, nor symbolic diagrams, nor mudras (hand gestures) can bring to light.

### 2.33

न शाम्भवं शाक्तिकमानवं न वा  
पिण्डं च रूपं च पदादिकं न वा ।  
आरम्भनिष्पत्तिघटादिकं च नो  
तमीशमात्मानमुपैति शाश्वतम् ॥ २-३३॥

*na śāmbhavaṃ śāktikamānavaṃ na vā  
piṇḍaṃ ca rūpaṃ ca padādikaṃ na vā .  
ārambhaniṣpattighaṭādikaṃ ca no  
tamīśamātmānamupaiti śāśvatam ..2-33..*

The yogi reaches that eternal Supreme Atman, which did not arise from Shiva, Shakti, or Manu. It is not a lump of flesh; It has no form and no limbs such as feet or hands. It is not a thing like a jar, which can be shaped and completed.

### 2.34

यस्य स्वरूपात्सचराचरं जग-  
दुत्पद्यते तिष्ठति लीयतेऽपि वा ।  
पयोविकारादिव फेनबुद्बुदा-  
स्तमीशमात्मानमुपैति शाश्वतम् ॥ २-३४॥

*yasya svarūpātsacarācaraṃ jaga-  
dutpadyate tiṣṭhati līyate'pi vā .  
payovikārādiva phenabudbudā-  
stamīśamātmānamupaiti śāśvatam ..2-34..*

The yogi reaches that eternal Supreme Atman, from whose essence both the moving and the unmoving worlds arise, in which they rest, and into which they dissolve again, just as foam and bubbles form from changes in water and then sink back into it.

### 2.35

नासानिरोधो न च दृष्टिरासनं  
बोधोऽप्यबोधोऽपि न यत्र भासते ।  
नाडीप्रचारोऽपि न यत्र किञ्चि-  
त्तमीशमात्मानमुपैति शाश्वतम् ॥ २-३५॥

*nāsānirodho na ca dṛṣṭirāsanaṃ  
bodho'pyabodho'pi na yatra bhāsate .  
nāḍīpracāro'pi na yatra kiñci-  
ttamīśamātmānamupaiti śāśvatam ..2-35..*

The yogi reaches that eternal Supreme Atman, which is beyond knowledge and ignorance, and which is not revealed by disciplines such as controlling the breath, fixing the gaze, practicing postures, or working the nerve-currents (that is, the movement of the ida, pingala, and sushumna).

### 2.36

नानात्वमेकत्वमुभत्वमन्यता  
अणुत्वदीर्घत्वमहत्त्वशून्यता ।  
मानत्वमेयत्वसमत्ववर्जितं  
तमीशमात्मानमुपैति शाश्वतम् ॥ २-३६॥

*nānātvamekatvamubhatvamanyatā  
aṇutvadīrghatvamahattvaśūnyatā .  
mānatvameyatvasamatvavarjitaṃ  
tamīśamātmānamupaiti śāśvatam ..2-36..*

The yogi reaches that eternal Supreme Atman, which is neither many nor one, neither both nor something else. It is free of smallness, largeness, vastness, and emptiness. It has no measure, no dimension, and no sameness.

### 2.37

सुसंयमी वा यदि वा न संयमी  
सुसङ्ग्रही वा यदि वा न सङ्ग्रही ।  
निष्कर्मको वा यदि वा सकर्मक-  
स्तमीशमात्मानमुपैति शाश्वतम् ॥ २-३७॥

*susaṃyamī vā yadi vā na saṃyamī  
susaṅgrahī vā yadi vā na saṅgrahī .  
niṣkarmako vā yadi vā sakarmaka-  
stamīśamātmānamupaiti śāśvatam ..2-37..*

The yogi reaches that eternal Supreme Atman, whether or not he is a self-restrained ascetic, whether or not he owns great wealth, whether he is active or inactive.

### 2.38

मनो न बुद्धिर्न शरीरमिन्द्रियं  
तन्मात्रभूतानि न भूतपञ्चकम् ।  
अहङ्कृतिश्चापि वियत्स्वरूपकं  
तमीशमात्मानमुपैति शाश्वतम् ॥ २-३८॥

*mano na buddhirna śarīramindriyaṃ  
tanmātrabhūtāni na bhūtapañcakam .  
ahaṅkṛtiścāpi viyatsvarūpakaṃ  
tamīśamātmānamupaiti śāśvatam ..2-38..*

The yogi reaches that eternal Supreme Atman, which is not the body, the senses, the mind, the intellect, or the ego. Nor is It the five subtle elements, the five gross elements, or anything of the nature of space.

### 2.39

विधौ निरोधे परमात्मतां गते  
न योगिनश्चेतसि भेदवर्जिते ।  
शौचं न वाशौचमलिङ्गभावना  
सर्वं विधेयं यदि वा निषिध्यते ॥ २-३९॥

*vidhau nirodhe paramātmatāṃ gate  
na yoginaścetasi bhedavarjite .  
śaucaṃ na vāśaucamaliṅgabhāvanā  
sarvaṃ vidheyaṃ yadi vā niṣidhyate ..2-39..*

When the yogi reaches that Supreme Atman, he passes beyond the commands and prohibitions of the scriptures. In the undifferentiated mind of such a yogi there is no notion of purity or impurity, nor can any evil thought arise. What is forbidden to others is permitted to him, because he stands beyond all rules.

### 2.40

मनो वचो यत्र न शक्तमीरितुं  
नूनं कथं तत्र गुरूपदेशता ।  
इमां कथामुक्तवतो गुरोस्त-  
द्युक्तस्य तत्त्वं हि समं प्रकाशते ॥ २-४०॥

*mano vaco yatra na śaktamīrituṃ  
nūnaṃ kathaṃ tatra gurūpadeśatā .  
imāṃ kathāmuktavato gurosta-  
dyuktasya tattvaṃ hi samaṃ prakāśate ..2-40..*

The mind cannot grasp Brahman, and speech cannot express It, let alone the instruction of a guru. Yet Brahman is fully revealed to one who has absorbed the instruction of the guru who proclaimed that Truth.

### 3.1

<vt>अथ तृतीयोऽध्यायः</vt>  
गुणविगुणविभागो वर्तते नैव किञ्चित्  
रतिविरतिविहीनं निर्मलं निष्प्रपञ्चम् ।  
गुणविगुणविहीनं व्यापकं विश्वरूपं  
कथमहमिह वन्दे व्योमरूपं शिवं वै ॥ ३-१॥

*<vt>Chapter 3</vt>  
guṇaviguṇavibhāgo vartate naiva kiñcit  
rativirativihīnaṃ nirmalaṃ niṣprapañcam .  
guṇaviguṇavihīnaṃ vyāpakaṃ viśvarūpaṃ  
kathamahamiha vande vyomarūpaṃ śivaṃ vai ..3-1..*

In Brahman the division into merit and demerit is entirely absent. Brahman is pure, unmanifest, and free from both passion and dispassion. How can I worship that infinite Supreme Beatitude, which is neither with attributes nor without them, and which pervades and is present everywhere?

### 3.2

श्वेतादिवर्णरहितो नियतं शिवश्च  
कार्यं हि कारणमिदं हि परं शिवश्च ।  
एवं विकल्परहितोऽहमलं शिवश्च  
स्वात्मानमात्मनि सुमित्र कथं नमामि ॥ ३-२॥

*śvetādivarṇarahito niyataṃ śivaśca  
kāryaṃ hi kāraṇamidaṃ hi paraṃ śivaśca .  
evaṃ vikalparahito'hamalaṃ śivaśca  
svātmānamātmani sumitra kathaṃ namāmi ..3-2..*

The Supreme Beatitude is eternal and free of colors such as white, red, and black. Truly It is both cause and effect. I am indeed that Brahman, free from all diversity. O dear friend, how can I, the Self, bow to the Self?

### 3.3

निर्मूलमूलरहितो हि सदोदितोऽहं  
निर्धूमधूमरहितो हि सदोदितोऽहम् ।  
निर्दीपदीपरहितो हि सदोदितोऽहं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-३॥

*nirmūlamūlarahito hi sadodito'haṃ  
nirdhūmadhūmarahito hi sadodito'ham .  
nirdīpadīparahito hi sadodito'haṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-3..*

I am uncreated and apart from creation, for I am always present. I am unclouded and free of the cloud of maya, for I am always manifest. I borrow no light from any other light, and I am also apart from luminous things, for I shine by myself. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, limitless as space.

### 3.4

निष्कामकाममिह नाम कथं वदामि  
निःसङ्गसङ्गमिह नाम कथं वदामि ।  
निःसारसाररहितं च कथं वदामि  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-४॥

*niṣkāmakāmamiha nāma kathaṃ vadāmi  
niḥsaṅgasaṅgamiha nāma kathaṃ vadāmi .  
niḥsārasārarahitaṃ ca kathaṃ vadāmi  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-4..*

Truly, how can I say that Brahman has desire, or that It is free of desire? How can I say whether It is attached or detached? How can I say that It is unreal, or that It lacks substance? I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, limitless as space.

### 3.5

अद्वैतरूपमखिलं हि कथं वदामि  
द्वैतस्वरूपमखिलं हि कथं वदामि ।  
नित्यं त्वनित्यमखिलं हि कथं वदामि  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-५॥

*advaitarūpamakhilaṃ hi kathaṃ vadāmi  
dvaitasvarūpamakhilaṃ hi kathaṃ vadāmi .  
nityaṃ tvanityamakhilaṃ hi kathaṃ vadāmi  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-5..*

How could I say whether Brahman is non-dual or dual in nature? How could I say whether It is eternal or not eternal? I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.6

स्थूलं हि नो न हि कृशं न गतागतं हि  
आद्यन्तमध्यरहितं न परापरं हि ।  
सत्यं वदामि खलु वै परमार्थतत्त्वं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-६॥

*sthūlaṃ hi no na hi kṛśaṃ na gatāgataṃ hi  
ādyantamadhyarahitaṃ na parāparaṃ hi .  
satyaṃ vadāmi khalu vai paramārthatattvaṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-6..*

Brahman is neither gross nor subtle. It neither appears nor vanishes. It has no beginning, no middle, and no end. In Brahman there is nothing higher or lower. I am declaring the Truth, the Ultimate Reality itself. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.7

संविद्धि सर्वकरणानि नभोनिभानि  
संविद्धि सर्वविषयांश्च नभोनिभांश्च ।  
संविद्धि चैकममलं न हि बन्धमुक्तं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-७॥

*saṃviddhi sarvakaraṇāni nabhonibhāni  
saṃviddhi sarvaviṣayāṃśca nabhonibhāṃśca .  
saṃviddhi caikamamalaṃ na hi bandhamuktaṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-7..*

Be certain of this: the sense organs and their objects are like clouds in the sky. Recognize the Shining One as neither bound nor free. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.8

दुर्बोधबोधगहनो न भवामि तात  
दुर्लक्ष्यलक्ष्यगहनो न भवामि तात ।  
आसन्नरूपगहनो न भवामि तात  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-८॥

*durbodhabodhagahano na bhavāmi tāta  
durlakṣyalakṣyagahano na bhavāmi tāta .  
āsannarūpagahano na bhavāmi tāta  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-8..*

Dear one, I am neither beyond the intellect's grasp nor out of its reach. I am neither imperceptible nor beyond perception. I am neither close to sight nor impenetrable to it. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.9

निष्कर्मकर्मदहनो ज्वलनो भवामि  
निर्दुःखदुःखदहनो ज्वलनो भवामि ।  
निर्देहदेहदहनो ज्वलनो भवामि  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-९॥

*niṣkarmakarmadahano jvalano bhavāmi  
nirduḥkhaduḥkhadahano jvalano bhavāmi .  
nirdehadehadahano jvalano bhavāmi  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-9..*

I am the fire of knowledge that consumes every action of the actionless Atman. I am the fire of knowledge that destroys every sorrow of the sorrowless Atman. I am the fire of knowledge that burns away every body of the bodiless Atman. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.10

निष्पापपापदहनो हि हुताशनोऽहं  
निर्धर्मधर्मदहनो हि हुताशनोऽहम् ।  
निर्बन्धबन्धदहनो हि हुताशनोऽहं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-१०॥

*niṣpāpapāpadahano hi hutāśano'haṃ  
nirdharmadharmadahano hi hutāśano'ham .  
nirbandhabandhadahano hi hutāśano'haṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-10..*

I am the fire of knowledge that wipes away every sin of the sinless Atman. I am the fire of knowledge that destroys every attribute of the attributeless Atman. I am the fire of knowledge that annihilates every bond of the unbound Atman. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.11

निर्भावभावरहितो न भवामि वत्स  
निर्योगयोगरहितो न भवामि वत्स ।  
निश्चित्तचित्तरहितो न भवामि वत्स  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-११॥

*nirbhāvabhāvarahito na bhavāmi vatsa  
niryogayogarahito na bhavāmi vatsa .  
niścittacittarahito na bhavāmi vatsa  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-11..*

My dear, I am neither free of thought nor possessed of thought. I am neither separated nor joined. I am neither without a mind nor in possession of one. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.12

निर्मोहमोहपदवीति न मे विकल्पो  
निःशोकशोकपदवीति न मे विकल्पः ।  
निर्लोभलोभपदवीति न मे विकल्पो  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-१२॥

*nirmohamohapadavīti na me vikalpo  
niḥśokaśokapadavīti na me vikalpaḥ .  
nirlobhalobhapadavīti na me vikalpo  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-12..*

In my Self there are no alternatives such as delusion or its absence, grief or its absence, greed or its absence. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.13

संसारसन्ततिलता न च मे कदाचित्  
सन्तोषसन्ततिसुखो न च मे कदाचित् ।  
अज्ञानबन्धनमिदं न च मे कदाचित्  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-१३॥

*saṃsārasantatilatā na ca me kadācit  
santoṣasantatisukho na ca me kadācit .  
ajñānabandhanamidaṃ na ca me kadācit  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-13..*

I am never caught by the creeper of endless birth and death. Neither the vast joy of contentment nor the bondage of ignorance exists in me. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.14

संसारसन्ततिरजो न च मे विकारः  
सन्तापसन्ततितमो न च मे विकारः ।  
सत्त्वं स्वधर्मजनकं न च मे विकारो  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-१४॥

*saṃsārasantatirajo na ca me vikāraḥ  
santāpasantatitamo na ca me vikāraḥ .  
sattvaṃ svadharmajanakaṃ na ca me vikāro  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-14..*

The continuation of the world is a modification of rajas, not of me. The chain of suffering is a modification of tamas, not of me. Happiness is a modification of sattva, not of me. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.15

सन्तापदुःखजनको न विधिः कदाचित्  
सन्तापयोगजनितं न मनः कदाचित् ।  
यस्मादहङ्कृतिरियं न च मे कदाचित्  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-१५॥

*santāpaduḥkhajanako na vidhiḥ kadācit  
santāpayogajanitaṃ na manaḥ kadācit .  
yasmādahaṅkṛtiriyaṃ na ca me kadācit  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-15..*

I have never done any action that could produce anguish and misery. I have never had a mind that could operate through contact with pain and pleasure. There is no root of ego in me. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.16

निष्कम्पकम्पनिधनं न विकल्पकल्पं  
स्वप्नप्रबोधनिधनं न हिताहितं हि ।  
निःसारसारनिधनं न चराचरं हि  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-१६॥

*niṣkampakampanidhanaṃ na vikalpakalpaṃ  
svapnaprabodhanidhanaṃ na hitāhitaṃ hi .  
niḥsārasāranidhanaṃ na carācaraṃ hi  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-16..*

Stillness and vibration, doubt and resolve, both end in Brahman. The waking and dreaming states, good and evil, both come to rest in Brahman. Strength and weakness, motion and rest, both dissolve in Brahman. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.17

नो वेद्यवेदकमिदं न च हेतुतर्क्यं  
वाचामगोचरमिदं न मनो न बुद्धिः ।  
एवं कथं हि भवतः कथयामि तत्त्वं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-१७॥

*no vedyavedakamidaṃ na ca hetutarkyaṃ  
vācāmagocaramidaṃ na mano na buddhiḥ .  
evaṃ kathaṃ hi bhavataḥ kathayāmi tattvaṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-17..*

Brahman is neither the knower nor the thing known. It lies beyond speculation and logical reasoning. It is beyond the reach of speech, mind, and intellect. So how can I tell you about Brahman, the Ultimate Reality? I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.18

निर्भिन्नभिन्नरहितं परमार्थतत्त्व-  
मन्तर्बहिर्न हि कथं परमार्थतत्त्वम् ।  
प्राक्सम्भवं न च रतं न हि वस्तु किञ्चित्  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-१८॥

*nirbhinnabhinnarahitaṃ paramārthatattva-  
mantarbahirna hi kathaṃ paramārthatattvam .  
prāksambhavaṃ na ca rataṃ na hi vastu kiñcit  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-18..*

Brahman is neither undivided nor divided. It truly has no inside and no outside. Brahman is not something that came into being in the past and will later cease to be. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.19

रागादिदोषरहितं त्वहमेव तत्त्वं  
दैवादिदोषरहितं त्वहमेव तत्त्वम् ।  
संसारशोकरहितं त्वहमेव तत्त्वं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-१९॥

*rāgādidoṣarahitaṃ tvahameva tattvaṃ  
daivādidoṣarahitaṃ tvahameva tattvam .  
saṃsāraśokarahitaṃ tvahameva tattvaṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-19..*

Truly I am Brahman, free of passion, jealousy, hatred, and the rest. I am that Reality without the sufferings that come from physical, earthly, and supernatural causes. I am that Truth untouched by the grief and misery of the world. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.20

स्थानत्रयं यदि च नेति कथं तुरीयं  
कालत्रयं यदि च नेति कथं दिशश्च ।  
शान्तं पदं हि परमं परमार्थतत्त्वं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-२०॥

*sthānatrayaṃ yadi ca neti kathaṃ turīyaṃ  
kālatrayaṃ yadi ca neti kathaṃ diśaśca .  
śāntaṃ padaṃ hi paramaṃ paramārthatattvaṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-20..*

How can there be a Fourth [Transcendental state] when Brahman has no three states [waking, dreaming, and sleeping]? How can the four directions of space exist without the threefold division of time? Truly, Brahman is the supreme home of peace. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.21

दीर्घो लघुः पुनरितीह नमे विभागो  
विस्तारसंकटमितीह न मे विभागः ।  
कोणं हि वर्तुलमितीह न मे विभागो  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-२१॥

*dīrgho laghuḥ punaritīha name vibhāgo  
vistārasaṃkaṭamitīha na me vibhāgaḥ .  
koṇaṃ hi vartulamitīha na me vibhāgo  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-21..*

Again, in my Self there are no divisions such as long or short, wide or narrow, round or angular. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.22

मातापितादि तनयादि न मे कदाचित्  
जातं मृतं न च मनो न च मे कदाचित् ।  
निर्व्याकुलं स्थिरमिदं परमार्थतत्त्वं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-२२॥

*mātāpitādi tanayādi na me kadācit  
jātaṃ mṛtaṃ na ca mano na ca me kadācit .  
nirvyākulaṃ sthiramidaṃ paramārthatattvaṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-22..*

I have no mother, no father, and no children. I have no birth, no death, and no desire. Truly, the Supreme Reality is unshakable and calm. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.23

शुद्धं विशुद्धमविचारमनन्तरूपं  
निर्लेपलेपमविचारमनन्तरूपम् ।  
निष्खण्डखण्डमविचारमनन्तरूपं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-२३॥

*śuddhaṃ viśuddhamavicāramanantarūpaṃ  
nirlepalepamavicāramanantarūpam .  
niṣkhaṇḍakhaṇḍamavicāramanantarūpaṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-23..*

Brahman is radiant, utterly pure, infinite, and beyond the intellect. It is foolish to think of the infinite Brahman as either unpolluted or polluted, undivided or divided. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.24

ब्रह्मादयः सुरगणाः कथमत्र सन्ति  
स्वर्गादयो वसतयः कथमत्र सन्ति ।  
यद्येकरूपममलं परमार्थतत्त्वं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-२४॥

*brahmādayaḥ suragaṇāḥ kathamatra santi  
svargādayo vasatayaḥ kathamatra santi .  
yadyekarūpamamalaṃ paramārthatattvaṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-24..*

If the Supreme Reality is one and the same and utterly pure, how can there be so many gods, such as Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva, or so many realms, such as Brahmaloka, heaven, and earth? I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.25

निर्नेति नेति विमलो हि कथं वदामि  
निःशेषशेषविमलो हि कथं वदामि ।  
निर्लिङ्गलिङ्गविमलो हि कथं वदामि  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-२५॥

*nirneti neti vimalo hi kathaṃ vadāmi  
niḥśeṣaśeṣavimalo hi kathaṃ vadāmi .  
nirliṅgaliṅgavimalo hi kathaṃ vadāmi  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-25..*

How can I say that the Supreme Reality is not this or not that, that It is endless or has an end, that It is bodiless or has a body? I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.26

निष्कर्मकर्मपरमं सततं करोमि  
निःसङ्गसङ्गरहितं परमं विनोदम् ।  
निर्देहदेहरहितं सततं विनोदं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-२६॥

*niṣkarmakarmaparamaṃ satataṃ karomi  
niḥsaṅgasaṅgarahitaṃ paramaṃ vinodam .  
nirdehadeharahitaṃ satataṃ vinodaṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-26..*

Though I perform no action, I am always engaged in the highest activity. Being unattached, I enjoy the bliss of non-attachment. Being bodiless, I enjoy the happiness of having no body. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.27

मायाप्रपञ्चरचना न च मे विकारः ।  
कौटिल्यदम्भरचना न च मे विकारः ।  
सत्यानृतेति रचना न च मे विकारो  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-२७॥

*māyāprapañcaracanā na ca me vikāraḥ .  
kauṭilyadambharacanā na ca me vikāraḥ .  
satyānṛteti racanā na ca me vikāro  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-27..*

The beautiful creation of maya produces no change in my Self. Neither crookedness and deceit nor truth and falsehood have any effect on me. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.28

सन्ध्यादिकालरहितं न च मे वियोगो-  
ह्यन्तः प्रबोधरहितं बधिरो न मूकः ।  
एवं विकल्परहितं न च भावशुद्धं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-२८॥

*sandhyādikālarahitaṃ na ca me viyogo-  
hyantaḥ prabodharahitaṃ badhiro na mūkaḥ .  
evaṃ vikalparahitaṃ na ca bhāvaśuddhaṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-28..*

Though I am beyond divisions of time such as evening and morning, I am never absent. Though I have no differentiated knowledge, I am not deaf or mute. Since I am free of ignorance, I need no purification of the mind. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.29

निर्नाथनाथरहितं हि निराकुलं वै  
निश्चित्तचित्तविगतं हि निराकुलं वै ।  
संविद्धि सर्वविगतं हि निराकुलं वै  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-२९॥

*nirnāthanātharahitaṃ hi nirākulaṃ vai  
niścittacittavigataṃ hi nirākulaṃ vai .  
saṃviddhi sarvavigataṃ hi nirākulaṃ vai  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-29..*

I have no master and I am no master, so I am free of perplexity. I have gone beyond the mind, which has stopped functioning, so I have no perplexity. Know for certain that all dualities have ended for me, so I have no perplexity. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.30

कान्तारमन्दिरमिदं हि कथं वदामि  
संसिद्धसंशयमिदं हि कथं वदामि ।  
एवं निरन्तरसमं हि निराकुलं वै  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-३०॥

*kāntāramandiramidaṃ hi kathaṃ vadāmi  
saṃsiddhasaṃśayamidaṃ hi kathaṃ vadāmi .  
evaṃ nirantarasamaṃ hi nirākulaṃ vai  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-30..*

How can I say that Brahman is like a forest or a temple? How can I declare that Brahman is proven or doubtful? Truly, It is all-pervading, unaffected, and motionless. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.31

निर्जीवजीवरहितं सततं विभाति  
निर्बीजबीजरहितं सततं विभाति ।  
निर्वाणबन्धरहितं सततं विभाति  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-३१॥

*nirjīvajīvarahitaṃ satataṃ vibhāti  
nirbījabījarahitaṃ satataṃ vibhāti .  
nirvāṇabandharahitaṃ satataṃ vibhāti  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-31..*

Having neither matter nor life, Brahman shines forever. Having neither origin nor seed, It exists forever. Being neither free nor bound, Brahman shines forever. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.32

सम्भूतिवर्जितमिदं सततं विभाति  
संसारवर्जितमिदं सततं विभाति ।  
संहारवर्जितमिदं सततं विभाति  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-३२॥

*sambhūtivarjitamidaṃ satataṃ vibhāti  
saṃsāravarjitamidaṃ satataṃ vibhāti .  
saṃhāravarjitamidaṃ satataṃ vibhāti  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-32..*

Having no origin, Brahman shines forever. Having no phenomenal existence, It radiates forever. Never being destroyed, It exists forever. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.33

उल्लेखमात्रमपि ते न च नामरूपं  
निर्भिन्नभिन्नमपि ते न हि वस्तु किञ्चित् ।  
निर्लज्जमानस करोषि कथं विषादं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-३३॥

*ullekhamātramapi te na ca nāmarūpaṃ  
nirbhinnabhinnamapi te na hi vastu kiñcit .  
nirlajjamānasa karoṣi kathaṃ viṣādaṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-33..*

[O Self,] You have no name and no form, not even by way of the faintest hint. There is no substance that is either different from You or not different from You. Why, then, O shameless mind, do you grieve? I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.34

किं नाम रोदिषि सखे न जरा न मृत्युः  
किं नाम रोदिषि सखे न च जन्म दुःखम् ।  
किं नाम रोदिषि सखे न च ते विकारो  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-३४॥

*kiṃ nāma rodiṣi sakhe na jarā na mṛtyuḥ  
kiṃ nāma rodiṣi sakhe na ca janma duḥkham .  
kiṃ nāma rodiṣi sakhe na ca te vikāro  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-34..*

Why do you cry, my friend? For you there is no old age and no death. For you there is no birth and no misery. Why do you cry, my friend? For you there is no disease and no change. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.35

किं नाम रोदिषि सखे न च ते स्वरूपं  
किं नाम रोदिषि सखे न च ते विरूपम् ।  
किं नाम रोदिषि सखे न च ते वयांसि  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-३५॥

*kiṃ nāma rodiṣi sakhe na ca te svarūpaṃ  
kiṃ nāma rodiṣi sakhe na ca te virūpam .  
kiṃ nāma rodiṣi sakhe na ca te vayāṃsi  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-35..*

Why do you cry, my friend? You have no form, nor are you deformed. Why do you cry, my friend? There is no aging for you. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.36

किं नाम रोदिषि सखे न च ते वयांसि  
किं नाम रोदिषि सखे न च ते मनांसि ।  
किं नाम रोदिषि सखे न तवेन्द्रियाणि  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-३६॥

*kiṃ nāma rodiṣi sakhe na ca te vayāṃsi  
kiṃ nāma rodiṣi sakhe na ca te manāṃsi .  
kiṃ nāma rodiṣi sakhe na tavendriyāṇi  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-36..*

Why do you cry, my friend? You have no physical or mental organism. Why do you cry, my friend? You have no sense organs at all. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.37

किं नाम रोदिषि सखे न च तेऽस्ति कामः  
किं नाम रोदिषि सखे न च ते प्रलोभः ।  
किं नाम रोदिषि सखे न च ते विमोहो  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-३७॥

*kiṃ nāma rodiṣi sakhe na ca te'sti kāmaḥ  
kiṃ nāma rodiṣi sakhe na ca te pralobhaḥ .  
kiṃ nāma rodiṣi sakhe na ca te vimoho  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-37..*

Why do you cry, my friend? You have no lust and no greed. Why do you cry, my friend? You have no delusion. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.38

ऐश्वर्यमिच्छसि कथं न च ते धनानि  
ऐश्वर्यमिच्छसि कथं न च ते हि पत्नी ।  
ऐश्वर्यमिच्छसि कथं न च ते ममेति  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-३८॥

*aiśvaryamicchasi kathaṃ na ca te dhanāni  
aiśvaryamicchasi kathaṃ na ca te hi patnī .  
aiśvaryamicchasi kathaṃ na ca te mameti  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-38..*

How can you want sovereignty when you have no wealth? Why do you chase wealth when you have no wife? Since you have no sense of ownership, what would you do with wealth? I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.39

लिङ्गप्रपञ्चजनुषी न च ते न मे च  
निर्लज्जमानसमिदं च विभाति भिन्नम् ।  
निर्भेदभेदरहितं न च ते न मे च  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-३९॥

*liṅgaprapañcajanuṣī na ca te na me ca  
nirlajjamānasamidaṃ ca vibhāti bhinnam .  
nirbhedabhedarahitaṃ na ca te na me ca  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-39..*

The creation of the subtle and gross universe is neither for you nor for me. The shameless mind invented the idea of diversity. For you and for me there is neither unity nor diversity. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.40

नो वाणुमात्रमपि ते हि विरागरूपं  
नो वाणुमात्रमपि ते हि सरागरूपम् ।  
नो वाणुमात्रमपि ते हि सकामरूपं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-४०॥

*no vāṇumātramapi te hi virāgarūpaṃ  
no vāṇumātramapi te hi sarāgarūpam .  
no vāṇumātramapi te hi sakāmarūpaṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-40..*

Truly, there is not a trace of attachment or detachment in your real nature. Truly, you have not the slightest form of desire. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.41

ध्याता न ते हि हृदये न च ते समाधि-  
र्ध्यानं न ते हि हृदये न बहिः प्रदेशः ।  
ध्येयं न चेति हृदये न हि वस्तु कालो  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-४१॥

*dhyātā na te hi hṛdaye na ca te samādhi-  
rdhyānaṃ na te hi hṛdaye na bahiḥ pradeśaḥ .  
dhyeyaṃ na ceti hṛdaye na hi vastu kālo  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-41..*

Truly, there is no one meditating within your heart, so you have no samadhi. There is no meditation within your heart, because there is no space outside it. Indeed, there is no object of meditation within your heart, since there is no object and no time. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.42

यत्सारभूतमखिलं कथितं मया ते  
न त्वं न मे न महतो न गुरुर्न न शिष्यः ।  
स्वच्छन्दरूपसहजं परमार्थतत्त्वं  
ज्ञानामृतं समरसं गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ३-४२॥

*yatsārabhūtamakhilaṃ kathitaṃ mayā te  
na tvaṃ na me na mahato na gururna na śiṣyaḥ .  
svacchandarūpasahajaṃ paramārthatattvaṃ  
jñānāmṛtaṃ samarasaṃ gaganopamo'ham ..3-42..*

I have told you the very essence of the Supreme Reality. There is no you, no I, no superior, no teacher, and no disciple. The Ultimate Reality is simple and spontaneous. I am Existence-Knowledge-Bliss, as limitless as space.

### 3.43

कथमिह परमार्थं तत्त्वमानन्दरूपं  
कथमिह परमार्थं नैवमानन्दरूपम् ।  
कथमिह परमार्थं ज्ञानविज्ञानरूपं  
यदि परमहमेकं वर्तते व्योमरूपम् ॥ ३-४३॥

*kathamiha paramārthaṃ tattvamānandarūpaṃ  
kathamiha paramārthaṃ naivamānandarūpam .  
kathamiha paramārthaṃ jñānavijñānarūpaṃ  
yadi paramahamekaṃ vartate vyomarūpam ..3-43..*

If I, the Supreme One, alone exist, like space, how can I say the Supreme Reality is blissful or not blissful? How can I say that It is reached through knowledge or through realization?

### 3.44

दहनपवनहीनं विद्धि विज्ञानमेक-  
मवनिजलविहीनं विद्धि विज्ञानरूपम् ।  
समगमनविहीनं विद्धि विज्ञानमेकं  
गगनमिव विशालं विद्धि विज्ञानमेकम् ॥ ३-४४॥

*dahanapavanahīnaṃ viddhi vijñānameka-  
mavanijalavihīnaṃ viddhi vijñānarūpam .  
samagamanavihīnaṃ viddhi vijñānamekaṃ  
gaganamiva viśālaṃ viddhi vijñānamekam ..3-44..*

Realize that non-dual Consciousness, which has no fire or air, no earth or water. Realize that non-dual Consciousness, which is neither moving nor still. Realize that non-dual Consciousness, which is as vast as space.

### 3.45

न शून्यरूपं न विशून्यरूपं  
न शुद्धरूपं न विशुद्धरूपम् ।  
रूपं विरूपं न भवामि किञ्चित्  
स्वरूपरूपं परमार्थतत्त्वम् ॥ ३-४५॥

*na śūnyarūpaṃ na viśūnyarūpaṃ  
na śuddharūpaṃ na viśuddharūpam .  
rūpaṃ virūpaṃ na bhavāmi kiñcit  
svarūparūpaṃ paramārthatattvam ..3-45..*

Formless I am not, nor do I have form. Purity and impurity are not my nature, nor are beauty and ugliness. That Supreme Reality shining in Its own nature is what I am.

### 3.46

मुञ्च मुञ्च हि संसारं त्यागं मुञ्च हि सर्वथा ।  
त्यागात्यागविषं शुद्धममृतं सहजं ध्रुवम् ॥ ३-४६॥

*muñca muñca hi saṃsāraṃ tyāgaṃ muñca hi sarvathā .  
tyāgātyāgaviṣaṃ śuddhamamṛtaṃ sahajaṃ dhruvam ..3-46..*

Renounce, renounce worldly existence, and then renounce even that renunciation completely. Cast off, as you would poison, the egoistic notion of rejecting or accepting the world. You are pure, simple, steady, and immortal.

### 4.1

<vt>अथ चतुर्थोऽध्यायः</vt>  
नावाहनं नैव विसर्जनं वा  
पुष्पाणि पत्राणि कथं भवन्ति ।  
ध्यानानि मन्त्राणि कथं भवन्ति  
समासमं चैव शिवार्चनं च ॥ ४-१॥

*<vt>Chapter 4</vt>  
nāvāhanaṃ naiva visarjanaṃ vā  
puṣpāṇi patrāṇi kathaṃ bhavanti .  
dhyānāni mantrāṇi kathaṃ bhavanti  
samāsamaṃ caiva śivārcanaṃ ca ..4-1..*

Brahman can be neither invoked nor abandoned, because It has no form. What use, then, is offering flowers and leaves, practicing meditation, or repeating the mantra? How can anyone worship that Supreme Bliss in which both unity and diversity dissolve?

### 4.2

न केवलं बन्धविबन्धमुक्तो  
न केवलं शुद्धविशुद्धमुक्तः ।  
न केवलं योगवियोगमुक्तः  
स वै विमुक्तो गगनोपमोऽहम् ॥ ४-२॥

*na kevalaṃ bandhavibandhamukto  
na kevalaṃ śuddhaviśuddhamuktaḥ .  
na kevalaṃ yogaviyogamuktaḥ  
sa vai vimukto gaganopamo'ham ..4-2..*

Brahman is not merely free from bondage and liberation, purity and impurity, union and separation; It is truly ever free. And I am that Brahman, infinite as space.

### 4.3

सञ्जायते सर्वमिदं हि तथ्यं  
सञ्जायते सर्वमिदं वितथ्यम् ।  
एवं विकल्पो मम नैव जातः  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-३॥

*sañjāyate sarvamidaṃ hi tathyaṃ  
sañjāyate sarvamidaṃ vitathyam .  
evaṃ vikalpo mama naiva jātaḥ  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-3..*

Whether the manifested world is real or unreal, no such doubt ever arises in my mind. By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.4

न साञ्जनं चैव निरञ्जनं वा  
न चान्तरं वापि निरन्तरं वा ।  
अन्तर्विभन्नं न हि मे विभाति  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-४॥

*na sāñjanaṃ caiva nirañjanaṃ vā  
na cāntaraṃ vāpi nirantaraṃ vā .  
antarvibhannaṃ na hi me vibhāti  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-4..*

Neither darkness nor light, neither inside nor outside, nor any other kind of diversity appears in my Self. By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.5

अबोधबोधो मम नैव जातो  
बोधस्वरूपं मम नैव जातम् ।  
निर्बोधबोधं च कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-५॥

*abodhabodho mama naiva jāto  
bodhasvarūpaṃ mama naiva jātam .  
nirbodhabodhaṃ ca kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-5..*

Neither ignorance nor knowledge arises in me. Even knowledge of the Self does not arise in me. How can I claim to have ignorance or knowledge? By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.6

न धर्मयुक्तो न च पापयुक्तो  
न बन्धयुक्तो न च मोक्षयुक्तः ।  
युक्तं त्वयुक्तं न च मे विभाति  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-६॥

*na dharmayukto na ca pāpayukto  
na bandhayukto na ca mokṣayuktaḥ .  
yuktaṃ tvayuktaṃ na ca me vibhāti  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-6..*

Brahman has no connection with virtue and vice or with bondage and freedom. Nothing appears to me as joined or as separated. By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.7

परापरं वा न च मे कदाचित्  
मध्यस्थभावो हि न चारिमित्रम् ।  
हिताहितं चापि कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-७॥

*parāparaṃ vā na ca me kadācit  
madhyasthabhāvo hi na cārimitram .  
hitāhitaṃ cāpi kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-7..*

No one is above me or below me. I have no notion of neutrality, and I have neither enemy nor friend. How can I speak of good and evil? By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.8

नोपासको नैवमुपास्यरूपं  
न चोपदेशो न च मे क्रिया च ।  
संवित्स्वरूपं च कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-८॥

*nopāsako naivamupāsyarūpaṃ  
na copadeśo na ca me kriyā ca .  
saṃvitsvarūpaṃ ca kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-8..*

I am neither the worshipper nor the thing worshipped. Instructions and rituals are not intended for me. How can I describe the nature of absolute Consciousness? By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.9

नो व्यापकं व्याप्यमिहास्ति किञ्चित्  
न चालयं वापि निरालयं वा ।  
अशून्यशून्यं च कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-९॥

*no vyāpakaṃ vyāpyamihāsti kiñcit  
na cālayaṃ vāpi nirālayaṃ vā .  
aśūnyaśūnyaṃ ca kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-9..*

Nothing pervades Brahman, and Brahman pervades nothing. It neither has a dwelling place nor lacks one. How can I call It full or empty? By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.10

न ग्राहको ग्राह्यकमेव किञ्चित्  
न कारणं वा मम नैव कार्यम् ।  
अचिन्त्यचिन्त्यं च कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-१०॥

*na grāhako grāhyakameva kiñcit  
na kāraṇaṃ vā mama naiva kāryam .  
acintyacintyaṃ ca kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-10..*

My Self is neither the perceiver nor the thing perceived. It has no cause and no effect. How can I say It is conceivable or inconceivable? By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.11

न भेदकं वापि न चैव भेद्यं  
न वेदकं वा मम नैव वेद्यम् ।  
गतागतं तात कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-११॥

*na bhedakaṃ vāpi na caiva bhedyaṃ  
na vedakaṃ vā mama naiva vedyam .  
gatāgataṃ tāta kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-11..*

My Self destroys nothing, and nothing destroys It. It is neither a knower nor something knowable. Dear one, how can I describe It as coming or going? By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.12

न चास्ति देहो न च मे विदेहो  
बुद्धिर्मनो मे न हि चेन्द्रियाणि ।  
रागो विरागश्च कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-१२॥

*na cāsti deho na ca me videho  
buddhirmano me na hi cendriyāṇi .  
rāgo virāgaśca kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-12..*

I have no body, nor am I bodiless. I have no senses, no mind, and no intellect. How can I say I have attachment or detachment? By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.13

उल्लेखमात्रं न हि भिन्नमुच्चै-  
रुल्लेखमात्रं न तिरोहितं वै ।  
समासमं मित्र कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-१३॥

*ullekhamātraṃ na hi bhinnamuccai-  
rullekhamātraṃ na tirohitaṃ vai .  
samāsamaṃ mitra kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-13..*

Forceful claims cannot alter Brahman, and denying Brahman does not make It vanish. O friend, how can I say whether Brahman is always the same or not? By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.14

जितेन्द्रियोऽहं त्वजितेन्द्रियो वा  
न संयमो मे नियमो न जातः ।  
जयाजयौ मित्र कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-१४॥

*jitendriyo'haṃ tvajitendriyo vā  
na saṃyamo me niyamo na jātaḥ .  
jayājayau mitra kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-14..*

I have mastered the senses, and then again, I have not mastered them. I have never practiced self-restraint or religious austerities. O friend, how can I speak of success and defeat? By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.15

अमूर्तमूर्तिर्न च मे कदाचि-  
दाद्यन्तमध्यं न च मे कदाचित् ।  
बलाबलं मित्र कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-१५॥

*amūrtamūrtirna ca me kadāci-  
dādyantamadhyaṃ na ca me kadācit .  
balābalaṃ mitra kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-15..*

I have no form, nor am I formless. I have no beginning, middle, or end. Friend, how can I say that I am strong or weak? By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.16

मृतामृतं वापि विषाविषं च  
सञ्जायते तात न मे कदाचित् ।  
अशुद्धशुद्धं च कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-१६॥

*mṛtāmṛtaṃ vāpi viṣāviṣaṃ ca  
sañjāyate tāta na me kadācit .  
aśuddhaśuddhaṃ ca kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-16..*

My dear, death and immortality, poison and nectar, have never come forth from me. How can I say I am pure or impure? By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.17

स्वप्नः प्रबोधो न च योगमुद्रा  
नक्तं दिवा वापि न मे कदाचित् ।  
अतुर्यतुर्यं च कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-१७॥

*svapnaḥ prabodho na ca yogamudrā  
naktaṃ divā vāpi na me kadācit .  
aturyaturyaṃ ca kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-17..*

For me there is no waking and no dreaming, no yoga posture, and no day or night. How can I say I am in the third state [sleep] or in the Fourth [Transcendental]? By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.18

संविद्धि मां सर्वविसर्वमुक्तं  
माया विमाया न च मे कदाचित् ।  
सन्ध्यादिकं कर्म कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-१८॥

*saṃviddhi māṃ sarvavisarvamuktaṃ  
māyā vimāyā na ca me kadācit .  
sandhyādikaṃ karma kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-18..*

Know that I am free from everything, and then again, not free from anything. I have no maya [illusion] and none of its many forms. How can I say I must perform the daily obligatory religious disciplines? By nature I am blissful and free.

### 4.19

संविद्धि मां सर्वसमाधियुक्तं  
संविद्धि मां लक्ष्यविलक्ष्यमुक्तम् ।  
योगं वियोगं च कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-१९॥

*saṃviddhi māṃ sarvasamādhiyuktaṃ  
saṃviddhi māṃ lakṣyavilakṣyamuktam .  
yogaṃ viyogaṃ ca kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-19..*

Understand that I am wholly immersed in Brahman. Understand that I have neither purpose nor purposelessness. How could I talk about joining or parting? Bliss and freedom are my very nature.

### 4.20

मूर्खोऽपि नाहं न च पण्डितोऽहं  
मौनं विमौनं न च मे कदाचित् ।  
तर्कं वितर्कं च कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-२०॥

*mūrkho'pi nāhaṃ na ca paṇḍito'haṃ  
maunaṃ vimaunaṃ na ca me kadācit .  
tarkaṃ vitarkaṃ ca kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-20..*

I am not ignorant and I am not learned. I am not silent and I am not a chatterer. How could I set out Brahman through claims and rebuttals? Bliss and freedom are my very nature.

### 4.21

पिता च माता च कुलं न जाति-  
र्जन्मादि मृत्युर्न च मे कदाचित् ।  
स्नेहं विमोहं च कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-२१॥

*pitā ca mātā ca kulaṃ na jāti-  
rjanmādi mṛtyurna ca me kadācit .  
snehaṃ vimohaṃ ca kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-21..*

I have no father, no mother, no family, and I belong to no lineage. I was never born and I will never die. How could I say that I feel affection or confusion? Bliss and freedom are my very nature.

### 4.22

अस्तं गतो नैव सदोदितोऽहं  
तेजोवितेजो न च मे कदाचित् ।  
सन्ध्यादिकं कर्म कथं वदामि  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-२२॥

*astaṃ gato naiva sadodito'haṃ  
tejovitejo na ca me kadācit .  
sandhyādikaṃ karma kathaṃ vadāmi  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-22..*

I never vanish; I am always here. In me there is no light and no darkness. How could I say that the rules of ritual apply to me? Bliss and freedom are my very nature.

### 4.23

असंशयं विद्धि निराकुलं मां  
असंशयं विद्धि निरन्तरं माम् ।  
असंशयं विद्धि निरञ्जनं मां  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-२३॥

*asaṃśayaṃ viddhi nirākulaṃ māṃ  
asaṃśayaṃ viddhi nirantaraṃ mām .  
asaṃśayaṃ viddhi nirañjanaṃ māṃ  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-23..*

Be sure of this: I am free of confusion. Be sure of this: I am eternal. Be sure of this: I am without stain. Bliss and freedom are my very nature.

### 4.24

ध्यानानि सर्वाणि परित्यजन्ति  
शुभाशुभं कर्म परित्यजन्ति ।  
त्यागामृतं तात पिबन्ति धीराः  
स्वरूपनिर्वाणमनामयोऽहम् ॥ ४-२४॥

*dhyānāni sarvāṇi parityajanti  
śubhāśubhaṃ karma parityajanti .  
tyāgāmṛtaṃ tāta pibanti dhīrāḥ  
svarūpanirvāṇamanāmayo'ham ..4-24..*

My dear one, the wise abandon every kind of meditation and every kind of action, whether good or bad. They drink the nectar of renunciation. Bliss and freedom are my very nature.

### 4.25

विन्दति विन्दति न हि न हि यत्र  
छन्दोलक्षणं न हि न हि तत्र ।  
समरसमग्नो भावितपूतः  
प्रलपति तत्त्वं परमवधूतः ॥ ४-२५॥

*vindati vindati na hi na hi yatra  
chandolakṣaṇaṃ na hi na hi tatra .  
samarasamagno bhāvitapūtaḥ  
pralapati tattvaṃ paramavadhūtaḥ ..4-25..*

Where the intellect cannot go, how could any kind of composition exist? The great Avadhuta, having cleansed himself through meditation and merged into Infinite Bliss, has sung of Brahman without premeditation.

### 5.1

<vt>अथ पञ्चमोऽध्यायः</vt>  
ॐ इति गदितं गगनसमं तत्  
न परापरसारविचार इति ।  
अविलासविलासनिराकरणं  
कथमक्षरबिन्दुसमुच्चरणम् ॥ ५-१॥

*<vt>Chapter 5</vt>  
oṃ iti gaditaṃ gaganasamaṃ tat  
na parāparasāravicāra iti .  
avilāsavilāsanirākaraṇaṃ  
kathamakṣarabindusamuccaraṇam ..5-1..*

The all-pervading Brahman has been pointed to by the syllable Om. Yet neither higher nor lower knowledge can fix what It essentially is. In speaking of Brahman, both noumenon and phenomenon have been set aside. So how could the syllable Om express It?

### 5.2

इति तत्त्वमसिप्रभृतिश्रुतिभिः  
प्रतिपादितमात्मनि तत्त्वमसि ।  
त्वमुपाधिविवर्जितसर्वसमं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-२॥

*iti tattvamasiprabhṛtiśrutibhiḥ  
pratipāditamātmani tattvamasi .  
tvamupādhivivarjitasarvasamaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-2..*

Through their great sayings such as 'Thou art That' and 'I am Brahman,' the Upanishads have proclaimed that your innermost Atman is the Reality. You are the all-inclusive Sameness, without any attribute. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.3

अध ऊर्ध्वविवर्जितसर्वसमं  
बहिरन्तरवर्जितसर्वसमम् ।  
यदि चैकविवर्जितसर्वसमं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-३॥

*adha ūrdhvavivarjitasarvasamaṃ  
bahirantaravarjitasarvasamam .  
yadi caikavivarjitasarvasamaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-3..*

The all-encompassing Brahman has no below and no above, no inside and no outside. Nor can It even be called numerically one. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.4

न हि कल्पितकल्पविचार इति  
न हि कारणकार्यविचार इति ।  
पदसन्धिविवर्जितसर्वसमं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-४॥

*na hi kalpitakalpavicāra iti  
na hi kāraṇakāryavicāra iti .  
padasandhivivarjitasarvasamaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-4..*

Brahman cannot be perceived by keeping prescribed rules and rites. It cannot be investigated through cause and effect. It is beyond inflected words and euphonic combinations. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.5

न हि बोधविबोधसमाधिरिति  
न हि देशविदेशसमाधिरिति ।  
न हि कालविकालसमाधिरिति  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-५॥

*na hi bodhavibodhasamādhiriti  
na hi deśavideśasamādhiriti .  
na hi kālavikālasamādhiriti  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-5..*

Brahman is not the joining of consciousness and unconsciousness. It is not the meeting place of space and outer space. It is not the point where time and timelessness merge. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.6

न हि कुम्भनभो न हि कुम्भ इति  
न हि जीववपुर्न हि जीव इति ।  
न हि कारणकार्यविभाग इति  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-६॥

*na hi kumbhanabho na hi kumbha iti  
na hi jīvavapurna hi jīva iti .  
na hi kāraṇakāryavibhāga iti  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-6..*

Brahman is neither the space inside a jar nor the jar. It is neither the body of the individual soul nor the individual soul itself. It lies beyond the relation of cause and effect. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.7

इह सर्वनिरन्तरमोक्षपदं  
लघुदीर्घविचारविहीन इति ।  
न हि वर्तुलकोणविभाग इति  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-७॥

*iha sarvanirantaramokṣapadaṃ  
laghudīrghavicāravihīna iti .  
na hi vartulakoṇavibhāga iti  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-7..*

The all-pervading Brahman is freedom Itself. Distinctions of short and long, round and angular, do not exist in It. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.8

इह शून्यविशून्यविहीन इति  
इह शुद्धविशुद्धविहीन इति ।  
इह सर्वविसर्वविहीन इति  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-८॥

*iha śūnyaviśūnyavihīna iti  
iha śuddhaviśuddhavihīna iti .  
iha sarvavisarvavihīna iti  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-8..*

Brahman is neither empty nor not empty. It is neither pure nor impure. It is neither everything nor nothing. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.9

न हि भिन्नविभिन्नविचार इति  
बहिरन्तरसन्धिविचार इति ।  
अरिमित्रविवर्जितसर्वसमं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-९॥

*na hi bhinnavibhinnavicāra iti  
bahirantarasandhivicāra iti .  
arimitravivarjitasarvasamaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-9..*

Brahman cannot be perceived as divided or as undivided. It has no outside, no inside, and no place where the two meet. It is the same toward all and has neither enemy nor friend. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.10

न हि शिष्यविशिष्यस्वरूप इति  
न चराचरभेदविचार इति ।  
इह सर्वनिरन्तरमोक्षपदं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-१०॥

*na hi śiṣyaviśiṣyasvarūpa iti  
na carācarabhedavicāra iti .  
iha sarvanirantaramokṣapadaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-10..*

In Brahman there is no difference between someone who is a disciple and someone who is not. In Brahman there is no difference between what moves and what does not. The all-pervading Reality is liberation Itself. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.11

ननु रूपविरूपविहीन इति  
ननु भिन्नविभिन्नविहीन इति ।  
ननु सर्गविसर्गविहीन इति  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-११॥

*nanu rūpavirūpavihīna iti  
nanu bhinnavibhinnavihīna iti .  
nanu sargavisargavihīna iti  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-11..*

Brahman is indeed without form and without formlessness. It is truly neither divided nor undivided. It is also free from creation and destruction. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.12

न गुणागुणपाशनिबन्ध इति  
मृतजीवनकर्म करोमि कथम् ।  
इति शुद्धनिरञ्जनसर्वसमं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-१२॥

*na guṇāguṇapāśanibandha iti  
mṛtajīvanakarma karomi katham .  
iti śuddhanirañjanasarvasamaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-12..*

Since the chains of good and bad qualities do not bind me, how could I be caught up in the doings of those who live and die? Truly, I am the pure, unclouded, all-pervading Reality. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.13

इह भावविभावविहीन इति  
इह कामविकामविहीन इति ।  
इह बोधतमं खलु मोक्षसमं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-१३॥

*iha bhāvavibhāvavihīna iti  
iha kāmavikāmavihīna iti .  
iha bodhatamaṃ khalu mokṣasamaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-13..*

Brahman is free from emotion and agitation, from desire and the absence of desire. This highest Consciousness is indeed one with liberation. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.14

इह तत्त्वनिरन्तरतत्त्वमिति  
न हि सन्धिविसन्धिविहीन इति ।  
यदि सर्वविवर्जितसर्वसमं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-१४॥

*iha tattvanirantaratattvamiti  
na hi sandhivisandhivihīna iti .  
yadi sarvavivarjitasarvasamaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-14..*

The true nature of Brahman is eternal Reality Itself. It neither unites nor separates. Though It stands apart from everything, It pervades everything equally. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.15

अनिकेतकुटी परिवारसमं  
इहसङ्गविसङ्गविहीनपरम् ।  
इह बोधविबोधविहीनपरं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-१५॥

*aniketakuṭī parivārasamaṃ  
ihasaṅgavisaṅgavihīnaparam .  
iha bodhavibodhavihīnaparaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-15..*

Brahman fills the open space, the house, and the family alike. The Supreme Reality has neither attachment nor detachment, neither knowledge nor ignorance. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.16

अविकारविकारमसत्यमिति  
अविलक्षविलक्षमसत्यमिति ।  
यदि केवलमात्मनि सत्यमिति  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-१६॥

*avikāravikāramasatyamiti  
avilakṣavilakṣamasatyamiti .  
yadi kevalamātmani satyamiti  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-16..*

This universe, taken as a transformation of the changeless Brahman, is unreal, and every appearance of the unseen Brahman is unreal. The Atman alone is truly the Reality. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.17

इह सर्वसमं खलु जीव इति  
इह सर्वनिरन्तरजीव इति ।  
इह केवलनिश्चलजीव इति  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-१७॥

*iha sarvasamaṃ khalu jīva iti  
iha sarvanirantarajīva iti .  
iha kevalaniścalajīva iti  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-17..*

Brahman is truly one and the same as the individual soul. It pervades every living being and every unmoving thing equally. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.18

अविवेकविवेकमबोध इति  
अविकल्पविकल्पमबोध इति ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरबोध इति  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-१८॥

*avivekavivekamabodha iti  
avikalpavikalpamabodha iti .  
yadi caikanirantarabodha iti  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-18..*

It makes no sense to reason about Brahman, which is beyond thought. To see many where there is one is pure ignorance. Brahman is truly unbroken consciousness. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.19

न हि मोक्षपदं न हि बन्धपदं  
न हि पुण्यपदं न हि पापपदम् ।  
न हि पूर्णपदं न हि रिक्तपदं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-१९॥

*na hi mokṣapadaṃ na hi bandhapadaṃ  
na hi puṇyapadaṃ na hi pāpapadam .  
na hi pūrṇapadaṃ na hi riktapadaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-19..*

Brahman is not a condition of freedom or of bondage, not a condition of virtue or of vice, not a condition of fullness or of emptiness. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.20

यदि वर्णविवर्णविहीनसमं  
यदि कारणकार्यविहीनसमम् ।  
यदिभेदविभेदविहीनसमं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-२०॥

*yadi varṇavivarṇavihīnasamaṃ  
yadi kāraṇakāryavihīnasamam .  
yadibhedavibhedavihīnasamaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-20..*

The omnipresent Brahman has nothing to do with caste or outcaste. It is free from cause and effect, from oneness and manyness. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.21

इह सर्वनिरन्तरसर्वचिते  
इह केवलनिश्चलसर्वचिते ।  
द्विपदादिविवर्जितसर्वचिते  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-२१॥

*iha sarvanirantarasarvacite  
iha kevalaniścalasarvacite .  
dvipadādivivarjitasarvacite  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-21..*

Brahman, absolute Consciousness, pervades all living beings and all the motionless things of the world. And yet It shines by Itself, apart from humans and every other being. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.22

अतिसर्वनिरन्तरसर्वगतं  
अतिनिर्मलनिश्चलसर्वगतम् ।  
दिनरात्रिविवर्जितसर्वगतं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-२२॥

*atisarvanirantarasarvagataṃ  
atinirmalaniścalasarvagatam .  
dinarātrivivarjitasarvagataṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-22..*

The omnipresent Brahman is beyond everything, and yet It pervades everything equally. It is utterly pure and still. It exists without day or night. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.23

न हि बन्धविबन्धसमागमनं  
न हि योगवियोगसमागमनम् ।  
न हि तर्कवितर्कसमागमनं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-२३॥

*na hi bandhavibandhasamāgamanaṃ  
na hi yogaviyogasamāgamanam .  
na hi tarkavitarkasamāgamanaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-23..*

Bondage and freedom, union and separation, reasoning and inference, and similar pairs of opposites never meet in Brahman. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.24

इह कालविकालनिराकरणं  
अणुमात्रकृशानुनिराकरणम् ।  
न हि केवलसत्यनिराकरणं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-२४॥

*iha kālavikālanirākaraṇaṃ  
aṇumātrakṛśānunirākaraṇam .  
na hi kevalasatyanirākaraṇaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-24..*

In Brahman, time and its divisions such as morning and evening are all denied. The atom and the primal elements such as fire and air are denied as well. But the Ultimate Reality cannot be denied. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.25

इह देहविदेहविहीन इति  
ननु स्वप्नसुषुप्तिविहीनपरम् ।  
अभिधानविधानविहीनपरं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-२५॥

*iha dehavidehavihīna iti  
nanu svapnasuṣuptivihīnaparam .  
abhidhānavidhānavihīnaparaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-25..*

Brahman neither has a body nor is without one. It has no states such as waking, dreaming, and sleeping. It is beyond names and rules. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.26

गगनोपमशुद्धविशालसमं  
अतिसर्वविवर्जितसर्वसमम् ।  
गतसारविसारविकारसमं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-२६॥

*gaganopamaśuddhaviśālasamaṃ  
atisarvavivarjitasarvasamam .  
gatasāravisāravikārasamaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-26..*

The all-pervading Brahman is stainless and vast like space. It goes beyond everything and It fills everything. It is present in what is essential, in what is not essential, and in every modification. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.27

इह धर्मविधर्मविरागतर-  
मिह वस्तुविवस्तुविरागतरम् ।  
इह कामविकामविरागतरं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-२७॥

*iha dharmavidharmavirāgatara-  
miha vastuvivastuvirāgataram .  
iha kāmavikāmavirāgataraṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-27..*

Brahman has no concern with virtue or vice. It is indifferent to wealth and poverty and free from passion and dispassion. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.28

सुखदुःखविवर्जितसर्वसम-  
मिह शोकविशोकविहीनपरम् ।  
गुरुशिष्यविवर्जिततत्त्वपरं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-२८॥

*sukhaduḥkhavivarjitasarvasama-  
miha śokaviśokavihīnaparam .  
guruśiṣyavivarjitatattvaparaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-28..*

The all-pervading Brahman is free from pleasure and pain, sorrow and joy. In that Supreme Reality there is neither guru nor disciple. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.29

न किलाङ्कुरसारविसार इति  
न चलाचलसाम्यविसाम्यमिति ।  
अविचारविचारविहीनमिति  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-२९॥

*na kilāṅkurasāravisāra iti  
na calācalasāmyavisāmyamiti .  
avicāravicāravihīnamiti  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-29..*

In Brahman there is no love-play and no offspring, no strength and no weakness. It has neither motion nor stillness, neither equality nor inequality. It is without discrimination and without the lack of it. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.30

इह सारसमुच्चयसारमिति ।  
कथितं निजभावविभेद इति ।  
विषये करणत्वमसत्यमिति  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-३०॥

*iha sārasamuccayasāramiti .  
kathitaṃ nijabhāvavibheda iti .  
viṣaye karaṇatvamasatyamiti  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-30..*

Brahman is the essence of all essences, and Its nature [attributes] has been described as something apart from Itself. Any action within the world of the senses is unreal. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.31

बहुधा श्रुतयः प्रवदन्ति यतो  
वियदादिरिदं मृगतोयसमम् ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वसमं  
किमु रोदिषि मानसि सर्वसमम् ॥ ५-३१॥

*bahudhā śrutayaḥ pravadanti yato  
viyadādiridaṃ mṛgatoyasamam .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvasamaṃ  
kimu rodiṣi mānasi sarvasamam ..5-31..*

In many ways the Upanishads state that manifested space, earth, and the rest are like water in a mirage. In truth, Brahman alone exists equally in everything. You are that very Brahman, O mind, so why do you weep?

### 5.32

विन्दति विन्दति न हि न हि यत्र  
छन्दोलक्षणं न हि न हि तत्र ।  
समरसमग्नो भावितपूतः  
प्रलपति तत्त्वं परमवधूतः ॥ ५-३२॥

*vindati vindati na hi na hi yatra  
chandolakṣaṇaṃ na hi na hi tatra .  
samarasamagno bhāvitapūtaḥ  
pralapati tattvaṃ paramavadhūtaḥ ..5-32..*

Where the intellect cannot go, how could any kind of composition exist? The great Avadhuta, having cleansed himself through meditation and merged into Infinite Bliss, has sung of Brahman without premeditation.

### 6.1

<vt>अथ षष्ठमोऽध्यायः</vt>  
बहुधा श्रुतयः प्रवदन्ति वयं  
वियदादिरिदं मृगतोयसमम् ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिव-  
मुपमेयमथोह्युपमा च कथम् ॥ ६-१॥

*<vt>Chapter 6</vt>  
bahudhā śrutayaḥ pravadanti vayaṃ  
viyadādiridaṃ mṛgatoyasamam .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśiva-  
mupameyamathohyupamā ca katham ..6-1..*

The Upanishads tell us in many ways that space, earth, and the rest are like water in a mirage. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how could It be compared with anything or have an equal?

### 6.2

अविभक्तिविभक्तिविहीनपरं  
ननु कार्यविकार्यविहीनपरम् ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
यजनं च कथं तपनं च कथम् ॥ ६-२॥

*avibhaktivibhaktivihīnaparaṃ  
nanu kāryavikāryavihīnaparam .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
yajanaṃ ca kathaṃ tapanaṃ ca katham ..6-2..*

Brahman is neither divided nor undivided. It is truly free from action and change. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, where is there room for worship and austerity?

### 6.3

मन एव निरन्तरसर्वगतं  
ह्यविशालविशालविहीनपरम् ।  
मन एव निरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
मनसापि कथं वचसा च कथम् ॥ ६-३॥

*mana eva nirantarasarvagataṃ  
hyaviśālaviśālavihīnaparam .  
mana eva nirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
manasāpi kathaṃ vacasā ca katham ..6-3..*

That Intelligence is truly eternal and omnipresent, and neither vast nor minute. If that Intelligence is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how can the mind grasp It or speech express It?

### 6.4

दिनरात्रिविभेदनिराकरण-  
मुदितानुदितस्य निराकरणम् ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
रविचन्द्रमसौ ज्वलनश्च कथम् ॥ ६-४॥

*dinarātrivibhedanirākaraṇa-  
muditānuditasya nirākaraṇam .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
ravicandramasau jvalanaśca katham ..6-4..*

In the self-luminous Brahman there is no division into day and night, sunrise and sunset. If It alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how could the sun, the moon, or fire light It up?

### 6.5

गतकामविकामविभेद इति  
गतचेष्टविचेष्टविभेद इति ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
बहिरन्तरभिन्नमतिश्च कथम् ॥ ६-५॥

*gatakāmavikāmavibheda iti  
gataceṣṭaviceṣṭavibheda iti .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
bahirantarabhinnamatiśca katham ..6-5..*

The distinctions of desire and desirelessness, action and inaction, are wholly absent from Brahman. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how can there be any idea of outside and inside?

### 6.6

यदि सारविसारविहीन इति  
यदि शून्यविशून्यविहीन इति ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
प्रथमं च कथं चरमं च कथम् ॥ ६-६॥

*yadi sāravisāravihīna iti  
yadi śūnyaviśūnyavihīna iti .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
prathamaṃ ca kathaṃ caramaṃ ca katham ..6-6..*

Brahman has no expansion or contraction, no fullness or emptiness. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, where could a first or a last be placed?

### 6.7

यदिभेदविभेदनिराकरणं  
यदि वेदकवेद्यनिराकरणम् ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
तृतीयं च कथं तुरीयं च कथम् ॥ ६-७॥

*yadibhedavibhedanirākaraṇaṃ  
yadi vedakavedyanirākaraṇam .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
tṛtīyaṃ ca kathaṃ turīyaṃ ca katham ..6-7..*

For Brahman, the notions of difference and sameness, knower and known, are rejected. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how can there be a third state [sleep] or a Fourth [the Transcendental]?

### 6.8

गदिताविदितं न हि सत्यमिति  
विदिताविदितं न हि सत्यमिति ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
विषयेन्द्रियबुद्धिमनांसि कथम् ॥ ६-८॥

*gaditāviditaṃ na hi satyamiti  
viditāviditaṃ na hi satyamiti .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
viṣayendriyabuddhimanāṃsi katham ..6-8..*

To regard Brahman as expressible or inexpressible, knowable or unknowable, is pure fancy. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how can there be objects, senses, mind, and intellect?

### 6.9

गगनं पवनो न हि सत्यमिति  
धरणी दहनो न हि सत्यमिति ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
जलदश्च कथं सलिलं च कथम् ॥ ६-९॥

*gaganaṃ pavano na hi satyamiti  
dharaṇī dahano na hi satyamiti .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
jaladaśca kathaṃ salilaṃ ca katham ..6-9..*

Neither space nor air has reality, neither fire nor earth, neither water nor clouds. Only the undivided Supreme Bliss is real.

### 6.10

यदि कल्पितलोकनिराकरणं  
यदि कल्पितदेवनिराकरणम् ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
गुणदोषविचारमतिश्च कथम् ॥ ६-१०॥

*yadi kalpitalokanirākaraṇaṃ  
yadi kalpitadevanirākaraṇam .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
guṇadoṣavicāramatiśca katham ..6-10..*

Because the various worlds [earth, heaven, and so on] and the gods [Vishnu, Shiva, Brahma, and so on] are nothing but imagination, their existence has been denied. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how can there be merit or demerit, reasoning or intellect?

### 6.11

मरणामरणं हि निराकरणं  
करणाकरणं हि निराकरणम् ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
गमनागमनं हि कथं वदति ॥ ६-११॥

*maraṇāmaraṇaṃ hi nirākaraṇaṃ  
karaṇākaraṇaṃ hi nirākaraṇam .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
gamanāgamanaṃ hi kathaṃ vadati ..6-11..*

In Brahman there is no death and no immortality, no action and no inaction. If Brahman alone is the all-pervading Supreme Bliss, how can anyone speak of Its coming or going?

### 6.12

प्रकृतिः पुरुषो न हि भेद इति  
न हि कारणकार्यविभेद इति ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
पुरुषापुरुषं च कथं वदति ॥ ६-१२॥

*prakṛtiḥ puruṣo na hi bheda iti  
na hi kāraṇakāryavibheda iti .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
puruṣāpuruṣaṃ ca kathaṃ vadati ..6-12..*

In Brahman there is no distinction between Purusha [Spirit] and prakriti [matter], and no cause and effect. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how can anyone call It male or female?

### 6.13

तृतीयं न हि दुःखसमागमनं  
न गुणाद्द्वितीयस्य समागमनम् ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
स्थविरश्च युवा च शिशुश्च कथम् ॥ ६-१३॥

*tṛtīyaṃ na hi duḥkhasamāgamanaṃ  
na guṇāddvitīyasya samāgamanam .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
sthaviraśca yuvā ca śiśuśca katham ..6-13..*

Brahman has no connection with the miseries of old age or the pleasures of youth. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how can there be childhood, youth, or old age?

### 6.14

ननु आश्रमवर्णविहीनपरं  
ननु कारणकर्तृविहीनपरम् ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिव-  
मविनष्टविनष्टमतिश्च कथम् ॥ ६-१४॥

*nanu āśramavarṇavihīnaparaṃ  
nanu kāraṇakartṛvihīnaparam .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśiva-  
mavinaṣṭavinaṣṭamatiśca katham ..6-14..*

Brahman is indeed free of the four stages of life and the four castes. It has no cause and It is not a doer. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how can one think of It as perishable or as imperishable?

### 6.15

ग्रसिताग्रसितं च वितथ्यमिति  
जनिताजनितं च वितथ्यमिति ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिव-  
मविनाशि विनाशि कथं हि भवेत् ॥ ६-१५॥

*grasitāgrasitaṃ ca vitathyamiti  
janitājanitaṃ ca vitathyamiti .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśiva-  
mavināśi vināśi kathaṃ hi bhavet ..6-15..*

To think that Brahman is bound or freed, created or uncreated, is pure fancy. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how can It be either mortal or immortal?

### 6.16

पुरुषापुरुषस्य विनष्टमिति  
वनितावनितस्य विनष्टमिति ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिव-  
मविनोदविनोदमतिश्च कथम् ॥ ६-१६॥

*puruṣāpuruṣasya vinaṣṭamiti  
vanitāvanitasya vinaṣṭamiti .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśiva-  
mavinodavinodamatiśca katham ..6-16..*

The ideas of man and woman, wife and husband, do not exist in Brahman. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how can one feel pain and pleasure?

### 6.17

यदि मोहविषादविहीनपरो  
यदि संशयशोकविहीनपरः ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिव-  
महमत्र ममेति कथं च पुनः ॥ ६-१७॥

*yadi mohaviṣādavihīnaparo  
yadi saṃśayaśokavihīnaparaḥ .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśiva-  
mahamatra mameti kathaṃ ca punaḥ ..6-17..*

Brahman is indeed free from delusion and dejection, doubt and distress. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how can there be 'I' and 'mine'?

### 6.18

महमेति  
ननु धर्मविधर्मविनाश इति  
ननु बन्धविबन्धविनाश इति ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं-  
मिहदुःखविदुःखमतिश्च कथम् ॥ ६-१८॥

*mahameti  
nanu dharmavidharmavināśa iti  
nanu bandhavibandhavināśa iti .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ-  
mihaduḥkhaviduḥkhamatiśca katham ..6-18..*

The distinctions of merit and demerit, bondage and freedom, are truly cancelled in Brahman. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how can there be feelings of misery and happiness?

### 6.19

न हि याज्ञिकयज्ञविभाग इति  
न हुताशनवस्तुविभाग इति ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
वद कर्मफलानि भवन्ति कथम् ॥ ६-१९॥

*na hi yājñikayajñavibhāga iti  
na hutāśanavastuvibhāga iti .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
vada karmaphalāni bhavanti katham ..6-19..*

In Brahman there is no difference between the one who sacrifices and the sacrifice, or between the sacrificial fire and the offering. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, tell me, how can there be any fruits of action?

### 6.20

ननु शोकविशोकविमुक्त इति  
ननु दर्पविदर्पविमुक्त इति ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
ननु रागविरागमतिश्च कथम् ॥ ६-२०॥

*nanu śokaviśokavimukta iti  
nanu darpavidarpavimukta iti .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
nanu rāgavirāgamatiśca katham ..6-20..*

Brahman is indeed free from sorrow and joy, pride and humility. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how can there be any thought of passion or dispassion?

### 6.21

न हि मोहविमोहविकार इति  
न हि लोभविलोभविकार इति ।  
यदि चैकनिरन्तरसर्वशिवं  
ह्यविवेकविवेकमतिश्च कथम् ॥ ६-२१॥

*na hi mohavimohavikāra iti  
na hi lobhavilobhavikāra iti .  
yadi caikanirantarasarvaśivaṃ  
hyavivekavivekamatiśca katham ..6-21..*

Infatuation and temptation, greed and delusion, cannot disturb or alter Brahman. If Brahman alone is the undivided Supreme Bliss, how can there be any thought of discrimination or its absence?

### 6.22

त्वमहं न हि हन्त कदाचिदपि  
कुलजातिविचारमसत्यमिति ।  
अहमेव शिवः परमार्थ इति  
अभिवादनमत्र करोमि कथम् ॥ ६-२२॥

*tvamahaṃ na hi hanta kadācidapi  
kulajātivicāramasatyamiti .  
ahameva śivaḥ paramārtha iti  
abhivādanamatra karomi katham ..6-22..*

See this: neither "you" nor "I" has ever existed. To think of yourself as belonging to a family or a caste is utter nonsense. I truly am Brahman, the Supreme Reality. So how could I bow down to It?

### 6.23

गुरुशिष्यविचारविशीर्ण इति  
उपदेशविचारविशीर्ण इति ।  
अहमेव शिवः परमार्थ इति  
अभिवादनमत्र करोमि कथम् ॥ ६-२३॥

*guruśiṣyavicāraviśīrṇa iti  
upadeśavicāraviśīrṇa iti .  
ahameva śivaḥ paramārtha iti  
abhivādanamatra karomi katham ..6-23..*

Brahman holds no distinction at all between guru and disciple, or between teaching and reflection. I truly am Brahman, the Supreme Reality. So how could I bow down to It?

### 6.24

न हि कल्पितदेहविभाग इति  
न हि कल्पितलोकविभाग इति ।  
अहमेव शिवः परमार्थ इति  
अभिवादनमत्र करोमि कथम् ॥ ६-२४॥

*na hi kalpitadehavibhāga iti  
na hi kalpitalokavibhāga iti .  
ahameva śivaḥ paramārtha iti  
abhivādanamatra karomi katham ..6-24..*

Brahman has no divisions into physical forms or into imagined worlds. I truly am Brahman, the Supreme Reality. So how could I bow down to It?

### 6.25

सरजो विरजो न कदाचिदपि  
ननु निर्मलनिश्चलशुद्ध इति ।  
अहमेव शिवः परमार्थ इति  
अभिवादनमत्र करोमि कथम् ॥ ६-२५॥

*sarajo virajo na kadācidapi  
nanu nirmalaniścalaśuddha iti .  
ahameva śivaḥ paramārtha iti  
abhivādanamatra karomi katham ..6-25..*

Passion and dispassion have no place whatsoever in Brahman. It is radiant, unmoving and pure. I truly am Brahman, the Supreme Reality. So how could I bow down to It?

### 6.26

न हि देहविदेहविकल्प इति  
अनृतं चरितं न हि सत्यमिति ।  
अहमेव शिवः परमार्थ इति  
अभिवादनमत्र करोमि कथम् ॥ ६-२६॥

*na hi dehavidehavikalpa iti  
anṛtaṃ caritaṃ na hi satyamiti .  
ahameva śivaḥ paramārtha iti  
abhivādanamatra karomi katham ..6-26..*

The idea that Brahman has a body, or that It has none, is pure imagination. The actions that life appears to involve are unreal as well. I truly am Brahman, the Supreme Reality. So how could I bow down to It?

### 6.27

विन्दति विन्दति न हि न हि यत्र  
छन्दोलक्षणं न हि न हि तत्र ।  
समरसमग्नो भावितपूतः  
प्रलपति तत्त्वं परमवधूतः ॥ ६-२७॥

*vindati vindati na hi na hi yatra  
chandolakṣaṇaṃ na hi na hi tatra .  
samarasamagno bhāvitapūtaḥ  
pralapati tattvaṃ paramavadhūtaḥ ..6-27..*

How can anything be composed about what the intellect cannot reach? Having purified himself through meditation and merged in Infinite Bliss, the great Avadhuta has sung of Brahman spontaneously.

### 7.1

<vt>अथ सप्तमोऽध्यायः</vt>  
रथ्याकर्पटविरचितकन्थः  
पुण्यापुण्यविवर्जितपन्थः ।  
शून्यागारे तिष्ठति नग्नो  
शुद्धनिरञ्जनसमरसमग्नः ॥ ७-१॥

*<vt>Chapter 7</vt>  
rathyākarpaṭaviracitakanthaḥ  
puṇyāpuṇyavivarjitapanthaḥ .  
śūnyāgāre tiṣṭhati nagno  
śuddhanirañjanasamarasamagnaḥ ..7-1..*

The Avadhuta's patched garment is sewn from rags thrown away beside the road. The path he walks lies beyond virtue and vice. Carefree, this mendicant makes his home in a deserted place, immersed in the pure, untainted bliss of Brahman.

### 7.2

लक्ष्यालक्ष्यविवर्जितलक्ष्यो  
युक्तायुक्तविवर्जितदक्षः ।  
केवलतत्त्वनिरञ्जनपूतो  
वादविवादः कथमवधूतः ॥ ७-२॥

*lakṣyālakṣyavivarjitalakṣyo  
yuktāyuktavivarjitadakṣaḥ .  
kevalatattvanirañjanapūto  
vādavivādaḥ kathamavadhūtaḥ ..7-2..*

The marks of the Avadhuta may show or they may not. He stands beyond right and wrong, yet he is completely honest. His true nature is perfect, pure and without blemish. How could so illumined a soul get caught up in arguments and debates?

### 7.3

आशापाशविबन्धनमुक्ताः  
शौचाचारविवर्जितयुक्ताः ।  
एवं सर्वविवर्जितशान्ता-  
स्तत्त्वं शुद्धनिरञ्जनवन्तः ॥ ७-३॥

*āśāpāśavibandhanamuktāḥ  
śaucācāravivarjitayuktāḥ .  
evaṃ sarvavivarjitaśāntā-  
stattvaṃ śuddhanirañjanavantaḥ ..7-3..*

The alluring trap of desire no longer holds the Avadhuta. Turning away from physical neatness and social courtesies, he stays immersed in the Supreme Spirit. Having given up everything in this way, he becomes one with pure, stainless Brahman.

### 7.4

कथमिह देहविदेहविचारः  
कथमिह रागविरागविचारः ।  
निर्मलनिश्चलगगनाकारं  
स्वयमिह तत्त्वं सहजाकारम् ॥ ७-४॥

*kathamiha dehavidehavicāraḥ  
kathamiha rāgavirāgavicāraḥ .  
nirmalaniścalagaganākāraṃ  
svayamiha tattvaṃ sahajākāram ..7-4..*

In this state of realization, how could the Avadhuta even ask whether he has a body or not, or whether he is attached or not? He is himself the innate Reality, spotless and unmoving, as infinite as space.

### 7.5

कथमिह तत्त्वं विन्दति यत्र  
रूपमरूपं कथमिह तत्र ।  
गगनाकारः परमो यत्र  
विषयीकरणं कथमिह तत्र ॥ ७-५॥

*kathamiha tattvaṃ vindati yatra  
rūpamarūpaṃ kathamiha tatra .  
gaganākāraḥ paramo yatra  
viṣayīkaraṇaṃ kathamiha tatra ..7-5..*

In this state of realization, how could anyone know the Reality, and where? How can there be form or formlessness? If the Supreme Reality pervades everything as space does, how could anyone make It an object?

### 7.6

गगनाकारनिरन्तरहंस-  
स्तत्त्वविशुद्धनिरञ्जनहंसः ।  
एवं कथमिह भिन्नविभिन्नं  
बन्धविबन्धविकारविभिन्नम् ॥ ७-६॥

*gaganākāranirantarahaṃsa-  
stattvaviśuddhanirañjanahaṃsaḥ .  
evaṃ kathamiha bhinnavibhinnaṃ  
bandhavibandhavikāravibhinnam ..7-6..*

Brahman pervades everything as space does, and Its very nature is pure and spotless. So how could It contain division and multiplication, bondage and liberation, or any other change?

### 7.7

केवलतत्त्वनिरन्तरसर्वं  
योगवियोगौ कथमिह गर्वम् ।  
एवं परमनिरन्तरसर्व-  
मेवं कथमिह सारविसारम् ॥ ७-७॥

*kevalatattvanirantarasarvaṃ  
yogaviyogau kathamiha garvam .  
evaṃ paramanirantarasarva-  
mevaṃ kathamiha sāravisāram ..7-7..*

The Absolute Brahman pervades everything equally. How could anyone experience union or separation there? Since the Supreme Brahman is the same in all, how could It vary in strength or weakness?

### 7.8

केवलतत्त्वनिरञ्जनसर्वं  
गगनाकारनिरन्तरशुद्धम् ।  
एवं कथमिह सङ्गविसङ्गं  
सत्यं कथमिह रङ्गविरङ्गम् ॥ ७-८॥

*kevalatattvanirañjanasarvaṃ  
gaganākāranirantaraśuddham .  
evaṃ kathamiha saṅgavisaṅgaṃ  
satyaṃ kathamiha raṅgaviraṅgam ..7-8..*

The Absolute Brahman shines equally in everything, clear and all-pervading like space. How could friendship or hostility, joy or sorrow, really exist in It?

### 7.9

योगवियोगै रहितो योगी  
भोगविभोगै रहितो भोगी ।  
एवं चरति हि मन्दं मन्दं  
मनसा कल्पितसहजानन्दम् ॥ ७-९॥

*yogaviyogai rahito yogī  
bhogavibhogai rahito bhogī .  
evaṃ carati hi mandaṃ mandaṃ  
manasā kalpitasahajānandam ..7-9..*

Whether or not the Avadhuta follows the rules of yoga, he is still a yogi. Whether or not he owns anything, he is still an enjoyer. He moves slowly and calmly, delighting in the natural bliss that rises from his own pure mind.

### 7.10

बोधविबोधैः सततं युक्तो  
द्वैताद्वैतैः कथमिह मुक्तः ।  
सहजो विरजः कथमिह योगी  
शुद्धनिरञ्जनसमरसभोगी ॥ ७-१०॥

*bodhavibodhaiḥ satataṃ yukto  
dvaitādvaitaiḥ kathamiha muktaḥ .  
sahajo virajaḥ kathamiha yogī  
śuddhanirañjanasamarasabhogī ..7-10..*

So long as someone holds both knowledge and ignorance, along with a sense of duality and non-duality, how can he be liberated? And why would anyone try to become a yogi who constantly enjoys the pure, unmixed bliss of Brahman, and whose inborn nature has no desire or ignorance?

### 7.11

भग्नाभग्नविवर्जितभग्नो  
लग्नालग्नविवर्जितलग्नः ।  
एवं कथमिह सारविसारः  
समरसतत्त्वं गगनाकारः ॥ ७-११॥

*bhagnābhagnavivarjitabhagno  
lagnālagnavivarjitalagnaḥ .  
evaṃ kathamiha sāravisāraḥ  
samarasatattvaṃ gaganākāraḥ ..7-11..*

Brahman has no division, since It is beyond whole and part. It clings to nothing, since It is beyond attachment and non-attachment. As It is all-pervading existence, like space, how could It contract or expand?

### 7.12

सततं सर्वविवर्जितयुक्तः  
सर्वं तत्त्वविवर्जितमुक्तः ।  
एवं कथमिह जीवितमरणं  
ध्यानाध्यानैः कथमिह करणम् ॥ ७-१२॥

*satataṃ sarvavivarjitayuktaḥ  
sarvaṃ tattvavivarjitamuktaḥ .  
evaṃ kathamiha jīvitamaraṇaṃ  
dhyānādhyānaiḥ kathamiha karaṇam ..7-12..*

Having given up everything, the Avadhuta remains forever united with Brahman. He has gone beyond all the elements and is free. How could life or death apply to him? What difference does it make whether he meditates or not?

### 7.13

इन्द्रजालमिदं सर्वं यथा मरुमरीचिका ।  
अखण्डितमनाकारो वर्तते केवलः शिवः ॥ ७-१३॥

*indrajālamidaṃ sarvaṃ yathā marumarīcikā .  
akhaṇḍitamanākāro vartate kevalaḥ śivaḥ ..7-13..*

This entire world is an illusion, like a mirage in the desert. Only the indivisible, formless Supreme Beatitude exists.

### 7.14

धर्मादौ मोक्षपर्यन्तं निरीहाः सर्वथा वयम् ।  
कथं रागविरागैश्च कल्पयन्ति विपश्चितः ॥ ७-१४॥

*dharmādau mokṣaparyantaṃ nirīhāḥ sarvathā vayam .  
kathaṃ rāgavirāgaiśca kalpayanti vipaścitaḥ ..7-14..*

We Avadhutas never seek righteousness, wealth, enjoyment or liberation. So how can scholars suppose that we have either attachment or detachment?

### 7.15

विन्दति विन्दति न हि न हि यत्र  
छन्दोलक्षणं न हि न हि तत्र ।  
समरसमग्नो भावितपूतः  
प्रलपति तत्त्वं परमवधूतः ॥ ७-१५॥

*vindati vindati na hi na hi yatra  
chandolakṣaṇaṃ na hi na hi tatra .  
samarasamagno bhāvitapūtaḥ  
pralapati tattvaṃ paramavadhūtaḥ ..7-15..*

How can anything be composed about what the intellect cannot reach? Having purified himself through meditation and merged in Infinite Bliss, the great Avadhuta has sung of Brahman spontaneously.

### 8.1

<vt>अथ अष्टमोऽध्यायः</vt>  
त्वद्यात्रया व्यापकता हता ते  
ध्यानेन चेतःपरता हता ते ।  
स्तुत्या मया वाक्परता हता ते  
क्षमस्व नित्यं त्रिविधापराधान् ॥ ८-१॥

*<vt>Chapter 8</vt>  
tvadyātrayā vyāpakatā hatā te  
dhyānena cetaḥparatā hatā te .  
stutyā mayā vākparatā hatā te  
kṣamasva nityaṃ trividhāparādhān ..8-1..*

O Brahman, in going on pilgrimage to find you, I have denied that you are everywhere. In meditating on you, I have shaped you into a form in my mind, denying that you are formless. In singing hymns, I have described you, denying that you are beyond description. Forgive me these three offenses.

### 8.2

कामैरहतधीर्दान्तो मृदुः शुचिरकिञ्चनः ।  
अनीहो मितभुक् शान्तः स्थिरो मच्छरणो मुनिः ॥ ८-२॥

*kāmairahatadhīrdānto mṛduḥ śucirakiñcanaḥ .  
anīho mitabhuk śāntaḥ sthiro maccharaṇo muniḥ ..8-2..*

A sage is one alone whose intellect desires do not disturb and whose senses are under control; who is gentle, pure, owns nothing, covets nothing, does not crave food, and is serene and steadfast; who has taken refuge in the Self.

### 8.3

अप्रमत्तो गभीरात्मा धृतिमान् जितषड्गुणः ।  
अमानी मानदः कल्पो मैत्रः कारुणिकः कविः ॥ ८-३॥

*apramatto gabhīrātmā dhṛtimān jitaṣaḍguṇaḥ .  
amānī mānadaḥ kalpo maitraḥ kāruṇikaḥ kaviḥ ..8-3..*

The sage stays alert, deep and steady, having mastered the mind and the senses. He is humble and shows respect to everyone. He is courteous, friendly, compassionate and farsighted.

### 8.4

कृपालुरकृतद्रोहस्तितिक्षुः सर्वदेहिनाम् ।  
सत्यसारोऽनवद्यात्मा समः सर्वोपकारकः ॥ ८-४॥

*kṛpālurakṛtadrohastitikṣuḥ sarvadehinām .  
satyasāro'navadyātmā samaḥ sarvopakārakaḥ ..8-4..*

The sage is gracious, harms no one, and bears patiently with all. Firmly grounded in truth and impartial, he does good to everyone. His soul is without blame.

### 8.5

अवधूतलक्षणं वर्णैर्ज्ञातव्यं भगवत्तमैः ।  
वेदवर्णार्थतत्त्वज्ञैर्वेदवेदान्तवादिभिः ॥ ८-५॥

*avadhūtalakṣaṇaṃ varṇairjñātavyaṃ bhagavattamaiḥ .  
vedavarṇārthatattvajñairvedavedāntavādibhiḥ ..8-5..*

The marks of a supreme A-VA-DHU-TA should be learned one syllable at a time from teachers of the Vedas and Vedanta who are skilled in understanding the syllables and meaning of the Vedas.

### 8.6

आशापाशविनिर्मुक्त आदिमध्यान्तनिर्मलः ।  
आनन्दे वर्तते नित्यमकारं तस्य लक्षणम् ॥ ८-६॥

*āśāpāśavinirmukta ādimadhyāntanirmalaḥ .  
ānande vartate nityamakāraṃ tasya lakṣaṇam ..8-6..*

In the word Avadhuta, the syllable A shows that he is free from the snare of hopes and expectations. His life remains pure from beginning through middle to end, and he dwells in bliss at all times.

### 8.7

वासना वर्जिता येन वक्तव्यं च निरामयम् ।  
वर्तमानेषु वर्तेत वकारं तस्य लक्षणम् ॥ ८-७॥

*vāsanā varjitā yena vaktavyaṃ ca nirāmayam .  
vartamāneṣu varteta vakāraṃ tasya lakṣaṇam ..8-7..*

In the word Avadhuta, the syllable VA shows that he has torn out every desire by the roots. His words purify, and he lives at all times in the ever-present Brahman.

### 8.8

धूलिधूसरगात्राणि धूतचित्तो निरामयः ।  
धारणाध्याननिर्मुक्तो धूकारस्तस्य लक्षणम् ॥ ८-८॥

*dhūlidhūsaragātrāṇi dhūtacitto nirāmayaḥ .  
dhāraṇādhyānanirmukto dhūkārastasya lakṣaṇam ..8-8..*

In the word Avadhuta, the syllable DHU shows that although gray dust covers his body because he has no body-consciousness, his mind is pure and healthy. Concentration and meditation are no longer necessary for him.

### 8.9

तत्त्वचिन्ता धृता येन चिन्ताचेष्टाविवर्जितः ।  
तमोऽहंकारनिर्मुक्तस्तकारस्तस्य लक्षणम् ॥ ८-९॥

*tattvacintā dhṛtā yena cintāceṣṭāvivarjitaḥ .  
tamo'haṃkāranirmuktastakārastasya lakṣaṇam ..8-9..*

In the word Avadhuta, the syllable TA shows that he is absorbed in the thought of Brahman, that anxiety does not touch him, and that he feels no need to strive. Egotism and ignorance have left him entirely.

### 8.10

दत्तात्रेयावधूतेन निर्मितानन्दरूपिणा ।  
ये पठन्ति च श‍ृण्वन्ति तेषां नैव पुनर्भवः ॥ ८-१०॥

*dattātreyāvadhūtena nirmitānandarūpiṇā .  
ye paṭhanti ca śṛṇvanti teṣāṃ naiva punarbhavaḥ ..8-10..*

Dattatreya Avadhuta composed this song of bliss. Whoever reads or hears it is released from rebirth.
