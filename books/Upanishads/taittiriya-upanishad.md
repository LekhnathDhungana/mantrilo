# Taittiriya Upanishad

### 1.1.1

प्रथमा शीक्षावल्ली  
ॐ शं नो मित्रः शं वरुणः । शं नो भवत्वर्यमा ।  
शं न इन्द्रो बृहस्पतिः । शं नो विष्णुरुरुक्रमः ।  
नमो ब्रह्मणे । नमस्ते वायो । त्वमेव प्रत्यक्षं ब्रह्मासि ।  
त्वामेव प्रत्यक्षं ब्रह्म वदिष्यामि । ऋतं वदिष्यामि ।  
सत्यं वदिष्यामि । तन्मामवतु । तद्वक्तारमवतु ।  
अवतु माम् । अवतु वक्तारम् ।  
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥ १॥ इति प्रथमोऽनुवाकः ॥

*prathamā śīkṣāvallī  
oṃ śaṃ no mitraḥ śaṃ varuṇaḥ . śaṃ no bhavatvaryamā .  
śaṃ na indro bṛhaspatiḥ . śaṃ no viṣṇururukramaḥ .  
namo brahmaṇe . namaste vāyo . tvameva pratyakṣaṃ brahmāsi .  
tvāmeva pratyakṣaṃ brahma vadiṣyāmi . ṛtaṃ vadiṣyāmi .  
satyaṃ vadiṣyāmi . tanmāmavatu . tadvaktāramavatu .  
avatu mām . avatu vaktāram .  
oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ .. 1.. iti prathamo'nuvākaḥ ..*

Om. May Mitra be kind to us, and may Varuna be kind. May Aryaman be kind to us. May Indra and Brihaspati be kind to us, and may wide-striding Vishnu be kind to us. Homage to Brahman! Homage to you, Vayu! You are truly Brahman made perceptible, and I will proclaim you as perceptible Brahman. I will speak what is right and I will speak what is true. May That protect me, and may That protect the teacher. May That protect me; may It protect the teacher.
Om! Peace! Peace! Peace!

### 1.2.1

शिक्षाशास्त्रार्थसङ्ग्रहः  
ॐ शीक्षां व्याख्यास्यामः । वर्णः स्वरः । मात्रा बलम् ।  
साम सन्तानः । इत्युक्तः शीक्षाध्यायः ॥ १॥  
इति द्वितीयोऽनुवाकः ॥

*śikṣāśāstrārthasaṅgrahaḥ  
oṃ śīkṣāṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ . varṇaḥ svaraḥ . mātrā balam .  
sāma santānaḥ . ityuktaḥ śīkṣādhyāyaḥ .. 1..  
iti dvitīyo'nuvākaḥ ..*

Om! Next we will explain the science of phonetics: sounds, accent or pitch, quality or measure, the effort of articulation, and evenness and continuity in pronouncing the letters. That completes the lesson on phonetics.

### 1.3.1

संहितोपासनम्  
सह नौ यशः । सह नौ ब्रह्मवर्चसम् ।  
अथातः सꣳहिताया उपनिषदम् व्याख्यास्यामः ।  
पञ्चस्वधिकरणेषु ।  
अधिलोकमधिज्यौतिषमधिविद्यमधिप्रजमध्यात्मम् ।  
ता महासꣳहिता इत्याचक्षते । अथाधिलोकम् ।  
पृथिवी पूर्वरूपम् । द्यौरुत्तररूपम् ।  
आकाशः सन्धिः वायुः सन्धानम् । इत्यधिलोकम् ॥ १॥

*saṃhitopāsanam  
saha nau yaśaḥ . saha nau brahmavarcasam .  
athātaḥ sagͫhitāyā upaniṣadam vyākhyāsyāmaḥ .  
pañcasvadhikaraṇeṣu .  
adhilokam adhijyauti ṣamadhividyam adhiprajamadhyātmam .  
tā mahāsagͫhitā ityācakṣate . athādhilokam .  
pṛthivī pūrvarūpam . dyauruttararūpam .  
ākāśaḥ sandhiḥ. vāyuḥ sandhānam . ityadhilokam . .. 1..*

The pupils say: 'May both of us, teacher and disciple, share the glory and radiance that come from holy living and study of scripture.' The teacher says: 'Now we will explain the secret teaching of conjunction.' It rests on five perceptible objects: a) the Universe, b) the Luminaries, c) Learning, d) Progeny, e) the Body. These are called 'the great combinations (or blendings).' The teaching about the universe is this: the earth is the earlier form and the heaven (firmament) the later form. The atmosphere is the junction, and air is the joining link. In this way one should meditate on the Universe.

### 1.3.2

अथाधिजौतिषम् ।  
अग्निः पूर्वरूपम् । आदित्य उत्तररूपम् । आपः सन्धिः ।  
वैद्युतः सन्धानम् । इत्यधिज्यौतिषम् । अथाधिविद्यम् ।  
आचार्यः पूर्वरूपम् । अन्तेवास्युत्तररूपम् । विद्या सन्धिः ।  
प्रवचनꣳसन्धानम् । इत्यधिविद्यम् ॥ २॥

*athādhijautiṣam .  
agniḥ pūrvarūpam . āditya uttararūpam . āpaḥ sandhiḥ .  
vaidyutaḥ sandhānam . ityadhijyautiṣam . athādhividyam .  
ācāryaḥ pūrvarūpam antevāsyuttararūpam . vidyā sandhiḥ .  
pravacanagͫsandhānam . ityadhividyam ... 2..*

Next, the luminaries, or meditations on fire. Fire is the earlier form and the sun the later form. Water lies between them and lightning connects them. In this way one should meditate on light.
Next, knowledge. The teacher is the earlier form and the pupil the later form, learning lies between them, and instruction is what joins them. In this way one should meditate on learning.

### 1.3.3

अथाधिप्रजम् । माता पूर्वरूपम् ।  
पितोत्तररूपम् । प्रजा सन्धिः । प्रजननꣳसन्धानम् ।  
इत्यधिप्रजम् ॥ ३॥

*athādhiprajam . mātā pūrvarūpam .  
pitottararūpam . prajā sandhiḥ . prajananagͫsandhānam .  
ityadhiprajam .. 3..*

Next, progeny. The mother is the earlier form and the father the later form, progeny is the junction, and procreation is the connection. In this way one should meditate on progeny.

### 1.3.4

अथाध्यात्मम् । अधराहनुः पूर्वरूपम् ।  
उत्तराहनूत्तररूपम् । वाक्सन्धिः । जिह्वासन्धानम् ।  
इत्यध्यात्मम् । इतीमामहासꣳहिताः ।  
य एवमेता महासꣳहिता व्याख्याता वे`द ।  
सन्धीयते प्रजया पशुभिः ।  
ब्रह्मवर्चसेनान्नाद्येन सुवर्ग्येण लोकेन ॥ ४॥  
इति तृतीयोऽनुवाकः ॥

*athādhyātmam . adharāhanuḥ pūrvarūpam .  
uttarāhanūttararūpam . vāksandhiḥ . jihvāsandhānam .  
ityadhyātmam . itīmāmahāsagͫhitāḥ .  
ya evametā mahāsagͫhitā vyākhyātā ve`da .  
sandhīyate prajayā paśubhiḥ .  
brahmavarcasenānnādyena suvargyeṇa lokena .. 4..  
iti tṛtīyo'nuvākaḥ ..*

What comes next concerns the individual, or the body. The lower jaw is the earlier form and the upper jaw the later form, speech is the conjunction, and the tongue is the means of joining them. In this way one should meditate on oneself.
These are known as 'the great conjunctions.' Whoever understands them as explained here is joined with progeny, cattle, food, and the like, and with the glory of holy lustre, wealth, and heavenly joys.

### 1.4.1

मेधादिसिद्ध्यर्था आवहन्तीहोममन्त्राः  
यश्छन्दसामृषभो विश्वरूपः ।  
छन्दोभ्योऽध्यमृतात्सम्बभूव ।  
स मेन्द्रो मेधया स्पृणोतु ।  
अमृतस्य देव धारणो भूयासम् ।  
शरीरं मे विचर्षणम् । जिह्वा मे मधुमत्तमा ।  
कर्णाभ्यां भूरिविश्रुवम् ।  
ब्रह्मणः कोशोऽसि मेधया पिहितः ।  
श्रुतं मे गोपाय  ॥ १॥

*medhādisiddhyarthā āvahantīhomamantrāḥ  
yaśchandasāmṛṣabho viśvarūpaḥ .  
chandobhyo'dhyamṛtātsambabhūva .  
sa mendro medhayā spṛṇotu .  
amṛtasya deva dhāraṇo bhūyāsam .  
śarīraṃ me vicarṣaṇam . jihvā me madhumattamā .  
karṇābhyāṃ bhūriviśruvam .  
brahmaṇaḥ kośo'si medhayā pihitaḥ .  
śrutaṃ me gopāya .. 1..*

He whose form is manifold, who stands foremost among the sacred hymns of the Vedas, and who arose from those immortal hymns: may that Indra (Omkara) fill me with vigour of intellect. O Lord, may I come to possess the immortal revelations. May my body be capable and active, and my speech as sweet and pleasing as can be. May I hear abundantly with my ears. You are the sheath of Brahman. Guard my learning.

### 1.4.2

आवहन्ती वितन्वाना । कुर्वाणाऽचीरमात्मनः । वासाꣳसि मम गावश्च ।  
अन्नपाने च सर्वदा । ततो मे श्रियमावह ।  
लोमशां पशुभिः सह स्वाहा ।  
आमायन्तु ब्रह्मचारिणः स्वाहा ।  
विमाऽऽयन्तु ब्रह्मचारिणः स्वाहा ।  
प्रमाऽऽयन्तु ब्रह्मचारिणः स्वाहा ।  
दमायन्तु ब्रह्मचारिणः स्वाहा ।  
शमायन्तु ब्रह्मचारिणः स्वाहा ॥ २॥

*āvahantī vitanvānā . kurvāṇā'cīramātmanaḥ . vāsāgͫsi mama gāvaśca .  
annapāne ca sarvadā . tato me śriyamāvaha .  
lomaśāṃ paśubhiḥ saha svāhā .  
āmāyantu brahmacāriṇaḥ svāhā .  
vimā''yantu brahmacāriṇaḥ svāhā .  
pramā''yantu brahmacāriṇaḥ svāhā .  
damāyantu brahmacāriṇaḥ svāhā .  
śamāyantu brahmacāriṇaḥ svāhā .. 2..*

O Lord, let prosperity then be mine, in the form of hairy animals along with cattle. May fortune provide for me, without delay and for all time, food and drink, cloth and cattle, bringing them continually and in ever-growing measure. Throughout, may celibate students thirsting for knowledge come to me. Svaha! May they come to me from far away and from every direction. May the students eager to gain knowledge of Brahman master their senses. May the students eager to live the knowledge of Brahman be at peace. Svaha!

### 1.4.3

यशो जनेऽसानि स्वाहा । श्रेयान् वस्यसोऽसानि स्वाहा ।  
तं त्वा भग प्रविशानि स्वाहा ।  
स मा भग प्रविश स्वाहा ।  
तस्मिन् सहस्रशाखे । निभगाऽहं त्वयि मृजे स्वाहा ।  
यथाऽऽपः प्रवताऽऽयन्ति । यथा मासा अहर्जरम् ।  
एवं मां ब्रह्मचारिणः । धातरायन्तु सर्वतः स्वाहा ।  
प्रतिवेशोऽसि प्रमाभाहि प्रमापद्यस्व ॥ ३॥  
इति चतुर्थोऽनुवाकः ॥

*yaśo jane'sāni svāhā . śreyān vasyaso'sāni svāhā .  
taṃ tvā bhaga praviśāni svāhā .  
sa mā bhaga praviśa svāhā .  
tasmin sahasraśākhe . nibhagā'haṃ tvayi mṛje svāhā .  
yathā''paḥ pravatā''yanti . yathā māsā aharjaram .  
evaṃ māṃ brahmacāriṇaḥ . dhātarāyantu sarvataḥ svāhā .  
prativeśo'si pramābhāhi pramāpadyasva .. 3..  
iti caturtho'nuvākaḥ ..*

May I succeed among the people. Svaha! May I stand above the wealthy. Svaha! O Lord of prosperity, may I enter into You. Svaha! May You enter into me. In that Self of Yours with its thousand branches, O Lord, may I cleanse myself of all my sins. Svaha! As water runs downhill and months pass into years, so, O creator, may students of Brahma-vidya come to me from everywhere. Svaha! You are the refuge! Shine on me! Come to me!

### 1.5.1

व्याहृत्युपासनम्  
भूर्भुवः सुवरिति वा एतास्तिस्रो व्याहृतयः ।  
तासामुहस्मै तां चतुर्थीम् । माहाचमस्यः प्रवेदयते ।  
मह इति । तद्ब्रह्म । स आत्मा । अङ्गान्यन्या देवताः ।  
भूरिति वा अयं लोकः । भुव इत्यन्तरिक्षम् ।  
सुवरित्यसौ लोकः  ।  
मह इत्यादित्यः । आदित्येन वाव सर्वेलोक महीयन्ते ॥ १॥

*vyāhṛtyupāsanam  
bhūrbhuvaḥ suvariti vā etāstisro vyāhṛtayaḥ .  
tāsāmuhasmai tāṃ caturthīm . māhācamasyaḥ pravedayate .  
maha iti . tadbrahma . sa ātmā . aṅgānyanyā devatāḥ .  
bhūriti vā ayaṃ lokaḥ . bhuva ityantarikṣam .  
suvarityasau lokaḥ .  
maha ityādityaḥ . ādityena vāva sarveloka mahīyante  .. 1..*

Bhuh, Bhuvah, and Suvah are three brief utterances with mystical meaning. Beside these there is a fourth, Mahah, made known by the seer Mahacamasya. That is Brahman. Mahah is like the body, and the other Gods are its limbs. Bhuh is this world. Bhuvah is the sky. Suvah is the next world. Mahah is the sun, for the sun nourishes all the worlds.

### 1.5.2

भूरिति वा अग्निः । भुव इति वायुः । सुवरित्यादित्यः ।  
मह इति चन्द्रमाः । चन्द्रमसा वाव  
सर्वाणि ज्योतीꣳषि महीयन्ते । भूरिति वा ऋचः ।  
भुव इति सामानि ।  
सुवरिति यजूꣳषि  ।  
मह इति ब्रह्म । ब्रह्मणा वाव सर्वेवेदा महीयन्ते ॥ २॥

*bhūriti vā agniḥ . bhuva iti vāyuḥ . Suvarityādityaḥ .  
maha iti candramāḥ . candramasā vāva  
sarvāṇi jyotīgͫṣi mahīyante . bhūriti vā ṛcaḥ .  
bhuva iti sāmāni .  
suvariti yajūgͫṣi .  
maha iti brahma . brahmaṇā vāva sarvevedā mahīyante .. 2..*

Bhuh is fire, Bhuvah is air, Suvah is the sun, and Mahah is the moon, for it is by the moon that all vital powers flourish.
Bhuh is the Rk, Bhuvah is the Saman, Suvah is the Yajus, and Mahah is the Brahman (represented by the syllable Om), for it is by the Brahman that the Vedas flourish.

### 1.5.3

भूरिति वै प्राणः । भुव इत्यपानः । सुवरिति व्यानः ।  
मह इत्यन्नम् । अन्नेन वाव सर्वे प्राण महीयन्ते ।  
ता वा एताश्चतस्रश्चतुर्ध । चतस्रश्चतस्रो व्याहृतयः ।  
ता यो वेद ।  
स वेद ब्रह्म । सर्वेऽस्मैदेवा बलिमावहन्ति ॥ ३॥  
इति पञ्चमोऽनुवाकः ॥

*bhūriti vai prāṇaḥ . bhuva ityapānaḥ . suvariti vyānaḥ .  
maha ityannam . annena vāva sarve prāṇa mahīyante .  
tā vā etāścatasraścaturdha . catasraścatasro vyāhṛtayaḥ .  
tā yo veda .  
sa veda brahma . sarve'smaidevā balimāvahanti .. 3..  
iti pañcamo'nuvākaḥ ..*

Bhuh is Prana. Bhuvah is Apana. Savah is Vyana. Mahah is food, for it is by food that the Pranas flourish.
These four are themselves fourfold, and each of the four Vyahrtis has four forms. Whoever knows these knows Brahman. All the Devas bring offerings to Him.
Bhuh - 1. World  2. Fire  3. Rk.  4. Prana
Bhuvah - 1. Sky  2. Air  3. Sama  4. Apana
Suvah - 1. Next world  2. Sun  3. Yajus  4. Vyana
Mahah - 1. Sun  2. Moon  3. Om  4. Food

### 1.6.1

मनोमयत्वादिगुणकब्रह्मोपासनया स्वाराज्यसिद्धिः  
स य एषोऽन्तहृदय आकाशः ।  
तस्मिन्नयं पुरुषो मनोमयः । अमृतो हिरण्मयः ।  
अन्तरेण तालुके । य एषस्तन इवावलम्बते । सेन्द्रयोनिः ।  
यत्रासौ केशान्तो विवर्तते । व्यपोह्य शीर्षकपाले ॥ १॥

*manomayatvādiguṇakabrahmopāsanayā svārājyasiddhiḥ  
sa ya eṣo'ntahṛdaya ākāśaḥ .  
tasminnayaṃ puruṣo manomayaḥ . amṛto hiraṇmayaḥ .  
antareṇa tāluke . ya eṣastana ivāvalambate . sendrayoniḥ .  
yatrāsau keśānto vivartate . vyapohya śīrṣakapāle .. 1..*

Here, in this space inside the heart, lives the intelligent, imperishable, radiant Purusha or Entity. Between the palates hangs something like a nipple (the uvula). That is the birthplace of Indra, where the root of the hair is parted, opening the skull at its centre.

### 1.6.2

भूरित्यग्नौ प्रतितिष्ठति । भुव इति वायौ  ।  
सुवरित्यादित्ये । मह इति ब्रह्मणि । आप्नोति स्वाराज्यम् ।  
आप्नोति मनसस्पतिम् । वाक्पतिश्चक्षुष्पतिः ।  
श्रोत्रपतिर्विज्ञानपतिः । एतत्ततो भवति ।  
आकाशशरीरं ब्रह्म ।  
सत्यात्म प्राणारामं मन आनन्दम् ।  
शान्तिसमृद्धममृतम् ।  
इति प्राचीन योग्योपास्व ॥ २॥ इति षष्ठोऽनुवाकः ॥

*bhūrityagnau pratitiṣṭhati . bhuva iti vāyau .  
suvarityāditye . maha iti brahmaṇi . āpnoti svārājyam .  
āpnoti manasaspatim . vākpatiścakṣuṣpatiḥ .  
śrotrapatirvijñānapatiḥ . etattato bhavati .  
ākāśaśarīraṃ brahma .  
satyātma prāṇārāmaṃ mana ānandam .  
śāntisamṛddhamamṛtam .  
iti prācīna yogyopāsva .. 2.. iti ṣaṣṭho'nuvākaḥ ..*

As the word Bhuh he stands in fire, as Bhuvah in the air, as Suvah in the sun, and as Mahah in the Brahman. He gains self-radiance and lordship over minds. He becomes Lord of Speech, Lord of the eyes, Lord of the ears, Lord of Knowledge. Then he becomes this Brahman, whose body is space and whose nature is truth, who plays in dynamic life, prana, whose mind is bliss, who is full of peace and immortal. Meditate in this way, O Descendant of ancient Yoga-culture.

### 1.7.1

'Om' indeed signals compliance. With Om they begin singing the Sama verses, and with 'Om Som' they recite the Shastras. The Adhvaryu responds with the syllable 'Om.' Whoever takes Brahman to be non-existent becomes non-existent himself. Whoever takes Brahman to be existent is known by the world to be existent. The Self is the essence of the former (Anandamaya kosha). At this point the following questions come up: Does the ignorant person, on leaving this world, go There? Or does the knower, on leaving this world, obtain That?
He desired: 'I shall become many and be born.' He performed Tapas. Having done so, He created all this, whatever we perceive. Having created it, He entered into it. Having entered it, He became the manifest and the unmanifest, the defined and the undefined, the housed and the houseless, knowledge and ignorance, truth and falsehood, and everything that exists. That is why It is called Existence. In this sense there is the following Vedic verse.

In the beginning this was indeed Non-existence. From it the existent was born. That created Itself by Itself, and so It is called the Self-made, or the Well-made. What was Self-made is taste (joy). Having obtained this taste, a person becomes blessed, for who could breathe out or breathe in if this joy were not there in the cavity of the heart? It is this Brahman that brings us joy. When this Atman reaches fearless oneness with Brahman, who is invisible, bodiless, inexplicable, and without support, he becomes free of fear. But when he makes even the smallest distinction in Brahman, he is in danger. That same Brahman becomes the source of fear for the one who makes a difference and does not reflect. A hundred times this human bliss is the bliss of the human Gandharvas, and it is also the bliss of one who is learned in the Vedas and free from desires. A hundred times the bliss of the human Gandharvas is the unit of bliss of the celestial Gandharvas, and it is the same as the bliss of one learned in the Vedas and free from desires. A hundred times the bliss of the celestial Gandharvas is the unit of joy of the Manes, whose world lasts for a long time, and the same is the joy of one learned in the Vedas and free from desires. A hundred times this joy of the Manes, whose worlds are relatively immortal, is one unit of joy of the Ajanaja gods born in the Deva loka, and it is also the measure of bliss of the Srotriya who has gone beyond all his desires.

A hundred times this joy of the gods born in the deva loka is a unit of joy of the gods (Karma Devas) who attained that state through their special Karma, and it is also the joy of a Srotriya free from desires. A hundred times this joy of the Karma Devas is the simple unit of joy of the gods (office-bearers), and it is also the measure of bliss of the Srotriya who has gone beyond all his desires. A hundred measures of the happiness of the gods (office-bearers) is the simple unit of joy of Indra (the king of Gods), and it is also the measure of bliss of the Srotriya who has gone beyond all his desires.

A hundred times the bliss of Brhaspati is the unit of joy of Prajapati (father of the creatures of the universe), and it is also the measure of bliss of the Srotriya who has gone beyond all his desires. A hundred times the bliss of Prajapati is the unit of joy of Brahman, which is in no way greater than the bliss of the Srotriya (one learned in the Vedas) who, in his experience of the Reality, is without any desire.

annaṃ na nindyāt . tadvratam . prāṇo vā annam .  
śarīramannādam . prāṇe śarīraṃ pratiṣṭhitam .

iti saptamo'nuvākaḥ ..*

Do not speak ill of food; let that be your vow. Prana is food. The body is the eater of food. The body rests on Prana, and Prana rests on the body. So food rests on food. Whoever knows that food rests on food becomes one with Brahman. He comes to have food and to eat food. He grows great in offspring, in wealth of cattle, and in the splendour of Brahmana-hood. He grows great in fame.

### 3.8.1

Do not reject food; let that be your vow. Water is food. Fire is the eater of food. Fire rests on water, and water rests on fire. So food rests on food. Whoever knows that food rests on food stands firmly established forever. He comes to have food and to eat food. He grows great in offspring, in wealth of cattle, and in the splendour of Brahmana-hood. He grows great in fame.

Whoever knows that food rests on food stands firmly established forever. He comes to have food and to eat food. He grows great in offspring, in wealth of cattle, and in the splendour of Brahmana-hood. He grows great in fame.

### 3.10.1

Do not turn away anyone who asks for shelter and lodging. This is the vow. So gather plenty of food by whatever means. People should say: 'Food is ready.' If the food is prepared in the best manner, it is given to him (the guest) in the best manner too. If it is prepared in a middling manner, it is given to him in a middling manner too. If it is prepared in the lowest manner, it is given to him in that lower manner too. Whoever knows this obtains all the rewards mentioned above.

### 3.10.4

Let him worship That as Namah (obeisance), and all desires will come to pay him homage. Let him meditate on That as the Supreme, and he rises to supremacy in life. Let him contemplate That as the 'destructive aspect' of Brahman, and all the enemies who hate him and the rivals he dislikes 'die around him.' The reality at the core of man and the Reality in the Sun are one.
