# Prashnopanishad

### 1.0

ॐ भद्रं कर्णेभिः श‍ृणुयाम देवा  
भद्रं पश्येमाक्षभिर्यजत्राः ।  
स्थिरैरङ्गैस्तुष्तुवाꣳसस्तनूभिर्व्यशेम देवहितं यदायुः ॥  
स्वस्ति न इन्द्रो वृद्धश्रवाः  
स्वस्ति नः पूषा विश्ववेदाः ।  
स्वस्ति नस्तार्क्ष्यो अरिष्टनेमिः  
स्वस्ति नो बृहस्पतिर्दधातु ॥  
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

*oṃ bhadraṃ karṇebhiḥ śṛṇuyāma devā  
bhadraṃ paśyemākṣabhiryajatrāḥ .  
sthirairaṅgaistuṣtuvāgͫsastanūbhirvyaśema devahitaṃ yadāyuḥ ..  
svasti na indro vṛddhaśravāḥ  
svasti naḥ pūṣā viśvavedāḥ .  
svasti nastārkṣyo ariṣṭanemiḥ  
svasti no bṛhaspatirdadhātu ..  
oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ ..*

Om. O gods, may our ears hear what is auspicious! O worshipful gods, may our eyes see what is good! Strong in limb and body, may we sing your praise and enjoy the life Prajapati has allotted to us! Om. Peace! Peace! Peace!

Adoration to the highest Rishis! Adoration to the highest Rishis! Tat sat. Harih, Om!

### 1.1

ॐ सुकेशा च भारद्वाजः शैब्यश्च सत्यकामः सौर्यायणी च गार्ग्यः  
कौसल्यश्चाश्वलायनो भार्गवो वैदर्भिः कबन्धी कात्यायनस्ते हैते  
ब्रह्मपरा ब्रह्मनिष्ठाः परं ब्रह्मान्वेषमाणा एष ह वै तत्सर्वं  
वक्ष्यतीति ते ह समित्पाणयो भगवन्तं पिप्पलादमुपसन्नाः ॥ १.१॥

*oṃ sukeśā ca bhāradvājaḥ śaibyaśca satyakāmaḥ sauryāyaṇī ca gārgyaḥ  
kausalyaścāśvalāyano bhārgavo vaidarbhiḥ kabandhī kātyāyanaste haite  
brahmaparā brahmaniṣṭhāḥ paraṃ brahmānveṣamāṇā eṣa ha vai tatsarvaṃ  
vakṣyatīti te ha samitpāṇayo bhagavantaṃ pippalādamupasannāḥ .. 1.1..*

Om. Sukesa, son of Bharadvaja; Satyakama, son of Sibi; Sauryayani of the family of Garga; Kausalya, son of Asvala; Vaidarbhi of the family of Bhrigu; and Kabandhi, son of Katya: all of these were devoted to Brahman, firm in Brahman, and in search of the Supreme Brahman. Fuel in hand, they went to the venerable Pippalada, believing he would tell them everything about Brahman.

Sukesas Bhâradvâga, Saivya Satyakâma, Sauryâyanin Gârgya, Kausalya Âsvalâyana, Bhârgava Vaidarbhi, and Kabandhin Kâtyâyana were devoted to Brahman, firm in Brahman, and in search of the Highest Brahman. Thinking that the venerable Pippalâda could tell them all of it, they took fuel in their hands, as pupils do, and went to him.

### 1.2

तान्ह स ऋषिरुवाच भूय एव तपसा ब्रह्मचर्येण श्रद्धया  
संवत्सरं संवत्स्यथ यथाकामं प्रश्नान् पृच्छत यदि  
विज्ञास्यामः सर्वं ह वो वक्ष्याम इति ॥ १.२॥

*tānha sa ṛṣiruvāca bhūya eva tapasā brahmacaryeṇa śraddhayā  
saṃvatsaraṃ saṃvatsyatha yathākāmaṃ praśnān pṛcchata yadi  
vijñāsyāmaḥ sarvaṃ ha vo vakṣyāma iti .. 1.2..*

The rishi told them: Live with me one more year, practicing austerity, chastity, and faith. After that, ask whatever you wish. If we know the answers, we will tell you everything.

The Rishi told them: Remain here another year, with penance, abstinence, and faith. Then ask whatever questions you please, and if we know the answers, we will tell you everything.

### 1.3

अथ कबन्धी कात्यायन उपेत्य पप्रच्छ ।  
भगवन् कुते ह वा इमाः प्रजाः प्रजायन्त इति ॥ १.३॥

*atha kabandhī kātyāyana upetya papraccha .  
bhagavan kute ha vā imāḥ prajāḥ prajāyanta iti .. 1.3..*

Then Kabandhi, son of Katya, came to him and asked: Sir, where do these creatures come from?

Then Kabandhin Kâtyâyana came to him and asked: Sir, from where might these creatures be born?

### 1.4

तस्मै स होवाच प्रजाकामो वै प्रजापतिः स तपोऽतप्यत  
स तपस्तप्त्वा स मिथुनमुत्पादयते । रयिं च प्राणं  
चेत्येतौ मे बहुधा प्रजाः करिष्यत इति ॥ १.४॥

*tasmai sa hovāca prajākāmo vai prajāpatiḥ sa tapo'tapyata  
sa tapastaptvā sa mithunamutpādayate . rayiṃ ca prāṇaṃ  
cetyetau me bahudhā prajāḥ kariṣyata iti .. 1.4..*

The teacher told him: Prajapati, the Creator, wished for offspring. He practiced austerities, and having done so, he brought forth a pair, the moon (rayi) and the sun (prana). He said to Himself, "These two will produce creatures for Me in many ways."

He answered: Prajapati, the lord of creatures, wished for creatures (pragâh). He performed penance, and having done so he produced a pair, matter (rayi) and spirit (prâna), thinking that together they would produce creatures for him in many ways.

### 1.5

आदित्यो ह वै प्राणो रयिरेव चन्द्रमा रयिर्वा एतत्  
सर्वं यन्मूर्तं चामूर्तं च तस्मान्मूर्तिरेव रयिः ॥ १.५॥

*ādityo ha vai prāṇo rayireva candramā rayirvā etat  
sarvaṃ yanmūrtaṃ cāmūrtaṃ ca tasmānmūrtireva rayiḥ .. 1.5..*

The sun is indeed prana, life; the moon is rayi, food. Food is truly all of this, what has form and what has none. So everything that has form is indeed food.

The sun is spirit, and the moon is matter. All of this, whatever has a body and whatever has none, is matter; and so body is indeed matter.

### 1.6

अथादित्य उदयन्यत्प्राचीं दिशं प्रविशति तेन प्राच्यान् प्राणान्  
रश्मिषु सन्निधत्ते । यद्दक्षिणां यत् प्रतीचीं यदुदीचीं यदधो  
यदूर्ध्वं यदन्तरा दिशो यत् सर्वं प्रकाशयति तेन सर्वान् प्राणान्  
रश्मिषु सन्निधत्ते ॥ १.६॥

*athāditya udayanyatprācīṃ diśaṃ praviśati tena prācyān prāṇān  
raśmiṣu sannidhatte . yaddakṣiṇāṃ yat pratīcīṃ yadudīcīṃ yadadho  
yadūrdhvaṃ yadantarā diśo yat sarvaṃ prakāśayati tena sarvān prāṇān  
raśmiṣu sannidhatte .. 1.6..*

When the sun rises it enters the eastern quarter and so wraps the living beings of the east in its rays. And when it lights the southern, western, northern, lower, upper, and intermediate quarters, when it lights everything, it wraps all living beings in its rays in the same way.

When Âditya, the sun, rises, he goes toward the East and so takes the Eastern spirits into his rays. And when he lights up the South, the West, the North, the Zenith, the Nadir, the intermediate quarters, and everything, he likewise takes all spirits into his rays.

### 1.7

स एष वैश्वानरो विश्वरूपः प्राणोऽग्निरुदयते ।  
तदेतदृचाऽभ्युक्तम् ॥ १.७॥

*sa eṣa vaiśvānaro viśvarūpaḥ prāṇo'gnirudayate .  
tadetadṛcā'bhyuktam .. 1.7..*

That sun rises each day, the sun that is the soul of all creatures and of all forms, that is life and fire. The following rik describes it: The wise know him who is present in all forms, full of rays, all-knowing, non-dual, the support of all life, the eye of all beings, the giver of heat. There rises the sun, thousand-rayed, existing in a hundred forms, the life of all creatures.

So he rises as Vaisvânara, belonging to all men, taking on all forms, as spirit and as fire. The following verse says this:

### 1.8

विश्वरूपं हरिणं जातवेदसं  
परायणं ज्योतिरेकं तपन्तम् ।  
सहस्ररश्मिः शतधा वर्तमानः  
प्राणः प्रजानामुदयत्येष सूर्यः ॥ १.८॥

*viśvarūpaṃ hariṇaṃ jātavedasaṃ  
parāyaṇaṃ jyotirekaṃ tapantam .  
sahasraraśmiḥ śatadhā vartamānaḥ  
prāṇaḥ prajānāmudayatyeṣa sūryaḥ .. 1.8..*

That sun rises each day, the sun that is the soul of all creatures and of all forms, that is life and fire. The following rik describes it: The wise know him who is present in all forms, full of rays, all-knowing, non-dual, the support of all life, the eye of all beings, the giver of heat. There rises the sun, thousand-rayed, existing in a hundred forms, the life of all creatures.

They knew him who takes on all forms, the golden one, who knows all things, who climbs highest, alone in his splendor, and who warms us. The thousand-rayed one, who abides in a hundred places, the spirit of all creatures, the Sun, rises.

### 1.9

संवत्सरो वै प्रजापतिस्तस्यायने दक्षिणं चोत्तरं च ।  
तद्ये ह वै तदिष्टापूर्ते कृतमित्युपासते ते चान्द्रमसमेव  
लोकमभिजयन्ते । त एव पुनरावर्तन्ते तस्मादेत ऋषयः  
प्रजाकामा दक्षिणं प्रतिपद्यन्ते । एष ह वै रयिर्यः  
पितृयाणः ॥ १.९॥

*saṃvatsaro vai prajāpatistasyāyane dakṣiṇaṃ cottaraṃ ca .  
tadye ha vai tadiṣṭāpūrte kṛtamityupāsate te cāndramasameva  
lokamabhijayante . ta eva punarāvartante tasmādeta ṛṣayaḥ  
prajākāmā dakṣiṇaṃ pratipadyante . eṣa ha vai rayiryaḥ  
pitṛyāṇaḥ .. 1.9..*

The year is truly Prajapati, and it has two paths, the Southern and the Northern. Those who perform sacrifices and pious actions as duties to be done gain only the World of the Moon, and they certainly come back here again. So the rishis who want offspring travel the Southern Path. This Path of the Fathers is rayi, food.

The year is indeed Prajapati, and it has two paths, the Southern and the Northern. Those here who trust in sacrifices and pious gifts as work done gain only the moon as their future world, and they return again. So the Rishis who want offspring go to the South, and that path of the Fathers is matter (rayi).

### 1.10

अथोत्तरेण तपसा ब्रह्मचर्येण श्रद्धया  
विद्ययाऽऽत्मानमन्विष्यादित्यमभिजयन्ते । एतद्वै  
प्राणानामायतनमेतदमृतमभयमेतत् परायणमेतस्मान्न पुनरावर्तन्त  
इत्येष निरोधस्तदेष श्लोकः ॥ १.१०॥

*athottareṇa tapasā brahmacaryeṇa śraddhayā  
vidyayā''tmānamanviṣyādityamabhijayante . etadvai  
prāṇānāmāyatanametadamṛtamabhayametat parāyaṇametasmānna punarāvartanta  
ityeṣa nirodhastadeṣa ślokaḥ .. 1.10..*

But those who seek the Self through austerity, chastity, faith, and knowledge travel the Northern Path and reach the Sun. The Sun is truly the support of all lives. He is immortal and without fear; He is the final goal. From there they do not return. This path is closed to the ignorant. On this there is the following verse:

But those who have sought the Self through penance, abstinence, faith, and knowledge reach Âditya, the sun, by the Northern path. This is the home of the spirits, the immortal, free from danger, the highest. They do not return from there, for it is the end. The Sloka says this:

### 1.11

पञ्चपादं पितरं द्वादशाकृतिं  
दिव आहुः परे अर्धे पुरीषिणम् ।  
अथेमे अन्य उ परे विचक्षणं  
सप्तचक्रे षडर आहुरर्पितमिति ॥ १.११॥

*pañcapādaṃ pitaraṃ dvādaśākṛtiṃ  
diva āhuḥ pare ardhe purīṣiṇam .  
atheme anya u pare vicakṣaṇaṃ  
saptacakre ṣaḍara āhurarpitamiti .. 1.11..*

Some call Him the father with five feet and twelve forms, the giver of rain, who lives in the region above the sky. Others say that the world is set in the all-knowing Sun, which has seven wheels and six spokes.

Some call him the father with five feet (the five seasons) and twelve shapes (the twelve months), the giver of rain in the highest half of heaven. Others say that the sage is placed in the lower half, in the chariot with seven wheels and six spokes.

### 1.12

मासो वै प्रजापतिस्तस्य कृष्णपक्ष एव रयिः शुक्लः प्रणस्तस्मादेत  
ऋषयः शुक्ल इष्टं कुर्वन्तीतर इतरस्मिन् ॥ १.१२॥

*māso vai prajāpatistasya kṛṣṇapakṣa eva rayiḥ śuklaḥ praṇastasmādeta  
ṛṣayaḥ śukla iṣṭaṃ kurvantītara itarasmin .. 1.12..*

The month is truly Prajapati. Its dark half is food, rayi; its bright half is the eater, prana. So some rishis perform sacrifice in the bright half, and some in the other.

The month is Prajapati. Its dark half is matter and its bright half spirit. So some Rishis perform sacrifice in the bright half, and others in the other half.

### 1.13

अहोरात्रो वै प्रजापतिस्तस्याहरेव प्राणो रात्रिरेव रयिः प्राणं वा एते  
प्रस्कन्दन्ति ये दिवा रत्या संयुज्यन्ते ब्रह्मचर्यमेव तद्यद्रात्रौ  
रत्या संयुज्यन्ते ॥ १.१३॥

*ahorātro vai prajāpatistasyāhareva prāṇo rātrireva rayiḥ prāṇaṃ vā ete  
praskandanti ye divā ratyā saṃyujyante brahmacaryameva tadyadrātrau  
ratyā saṃyujyante .. 1.13..*

Day and night are truly Prajapati. Of these, day is the eater, prana, and night is the food, rayi. Those who unite in sexual enjoyment by day truly waste their life; but to unite in sexual enjoyment by night is truly chastity.

Day and Night are Prajapati; its day is spirit and its night matter. Those who unite in love by day waste their spirit, but uniting in love by night is right.

### 1.14

अन्नं वै प्रजापतिस्ततो ह वै तद्रेतस्तस्मादिमाः प्रजाः  
प्रजायन्त इति ॥ १.१४॥

*annaṃ vai prajāpatistato ha vai tadretastasmādimāḥ prajāḥ  
prajāyanta iti .. 1.14..*

Food is truly Prajapati. From it comes semen, and from semen all these creatures are born.

Food is Prajapati. From it comes seed, and from seed these creatures are born.

### 1.15

तद्ये ह वै तत् प्रजापतिव्रतं चरन्ति ते मिथुनमुत्पादयन्ते ।  
तेषामेवैष ब्रह्मलोको येषां तपो ब्रह्मचर्यं येषु सत्यं  
प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ १.१५॥

*tadye ha vai tat prajāpativrataṃ caranti te mithunamutpādayante .  
teṣāmevaiṣa brahmaloko yeṣāṃ tapo brahmacaryaṃ yeṣu satyaṃ  
pratiṣṭhitam .. 1.15..*

Those who follow this rule of Prajapati therefore produce a pair. But Brahmaloka belongs to those who practice austerity and chastity and in whom truth is firmly rooted.

Those who keep this rule of Prajapati, as laid down in § 13, therefore produce a pair, and this Brahma-world here belongs to them. But those in whom penance, abstinence, and truth dwell,

### 1.16

तेषामसौ विरजो ब्रह्मलोको न येषु जिह्ममनृतं न  
माया चेति ॥ १.१६॥  
इति प्रश्नोपनिषदि प्रथमः प्रश्नः ॥

*teṣāmasau virajo brahmaloko na yeṣu jihmamanṛtaṃ na  
māyā ceti .. 1.16..  
iti praśnopaniṣadi prathamaḥ praśnaḥ ..*

The spotless World of Brahma belongs to those who have no crookedness, no falsehood, and no deceit in them.

to them belongs that pure Brahma-world, to those, that is, in whom there is nothing crooked, nothing false, and no guile.

### 2.1

अथ हैनं भार्गवो वैदर्भिः पप्रच्छ । भगवन् कत्येव  
देवाः प्रजां विधारयन्ते कतर एतत् प्रकाशयन्ते कः  
पुनरेषां वरिष्ठ इति ॥ २.१॥

*atha hainaṃ bhārgavo vaidarbhiḥ papraccha . bhagavan katyeva  
devāḥ prajāṃ vidhārayante katara etat prakāśayante kaḥ  
punareṣāṃ variṣṭha iti .. 2.1..*

Then Vaidarbhi of the family of Bhrigu asked him: Sir, how many gods support the body of a created being? How many of them display their power through it? And which of them is supreme?

Then Bhârgava Vaidarbhi asked him: Sir, how many gods sustain what has been created in this way, how many make it manifest, and who is the best of them?

### 2.2

तस्मै स होवाचाकाशो ह वा एष देवो वायुरग्निरापः  
पृथिवी वाङ्मनश्चक्षुः श्रोत्रं च । ते प्रकाश्याभिवदन्ति  
वयमेतद्बाणमवष्टभ्य विधारयामः ॥ २.२॥

*tasmai sa hovācākāśo ha vā eṣa devo vāyuragnirāpaḥ  
pṛthivī vāṅmanaścakṣuḥ śrotraṃ ca . te prakāśyābhivadanti  
vayametadbāṇamavaṣṭabhya vidhārayāmaḥ .. 2.2..*

He told the disciple: Space, akasa, is truly that god, as are wind, fire, water, earth, speech, mind, eye, and ear. Having displayed their glory, these boasted, "We support this body and hold it up."

He answered: That god is the ether, the wind, fire, water, earth, speech, mind, eye, and ear. Having displayed their power, these argue and say: Each of us supports this body and sustains it.

### 2.3

तान् वरिष्ठः प्राण उवाच । मा मोहमापद्यथ अहमेवैतत्  
पञ्चधाऽऽत्मानं प्रविभज्यैतद्बाणमवष्टभ्य विधारयामीति  
तेऽश्रद्दधाना बभूवुः ॥ २.३॥

*tān variṣṭhaḥ prāṇa uvāca . mā mohamāpadyatha ahamevaitat  
pañcadhā''tmānaṃ pravibhajyaitadbāṇamavaṣṭabhya vidhārayāmīti  
te'śraddadhānā babhūvuḥ .. 2.3..*

Prana, the foremost, said to them, "Do not be deluded. I alone, dividing myself into five parts, support this body and hold it up." But they did not believe it.

Then Prâna (breath, spirit, life), as the best, told them: Do not be deceived. I alone, dividing myself fivefold, support this body and sustain it.

### 2.4

सोऽभिमानादूर्ध्वमुत्क्रामत इव तस्मिन्नुत्क्रामत्यथेतरे सर्व  
एवोत्क्रामन्ते तस्मिंश्च प्रतिष्ठमाने सर्व एव प्रतिष्ठन्ते । तद्यथा  
मक्षिका मधुकरराजानमुत्क्रामन्तं सर्व एवोत्क्रमन्ते तस्मिंष्च  
प्रतिष्ठमाने सर्व एव प्रातिष्टन्त एवं वाङ्मनष्चक्षुः श्रोत्रं  
च ते प्रीताः प्राणं स्तुन्वन्ति ॥ २.४॥

*so'bhimānādūrdhvamutkrāmata iva tasminnutkrāmatyathetare sarva  
evotkrāmante tasmiṃśca pratiṣṭhamāne sarva eva pratiṣṭhante . tadyathā  
makṣikā madhukararājānamutkrāmantaṃ sarva evotkramante tasmiṃṣca  
pratiṣṭhamāne sarva eva prātiṣṭanta evaṃ vāṅmanaṣcakṣuḥ śrotraṃ  
ca te prītāḥ prāṇaṃ stunvanti .. 2.4..*

Out of pride, Prana seemed to rise up from the body. As it rose, all the others rose as well, and when it settled down, they all settled down with it. Just as bees go out when their queen goes out and come back when she comes back, so did speech, mind, eye, and ear. Satisfied, they praised prana.

They did not believe it. So, out of pride, he acted as though he were going out from above. As he went out, all the others went out, and as he returned, all the others returned. Just as bees go out when their queen goes out and return when she returns, so did speech, mind, eye, and ear. Satisfied, they praise Prâna, saying:

### 2.5

एषोऽग्निस्तपत्येष सूर्य  
एष पर्जन्यो मघवानेष वायुः  
एष पृथिवी रयिर्देवः  
सदसच्चामृतं च यत् ॥ २.५॥

*eṣo'gnistapatyeṣa sūrya  
eṣa parjanyo maghavāneṣa vāyuḥ  
eṣa pṛthivī rayirdevaḥ  
sadasaccāmṛtaṃ ca yat .. 2.5..*

It burns as fire. It is the sun and the rain. It is Indra, the wind, the earth, and food. It is the shining god. It is being and non-being, and it is immortality.

He is Agni (fire), he shines as Sûrya (sun), he is Parganya (rain), the mighty Indra, he is Vâyu (wind), he is the earth, he is matter, he is God. He is what is, what is not, and what is immortal.

### 2.6

अरा इव रथनाभौ प्राणे सर्वं प्रतिष्ठितम् ।  
ऋचो यजूꣳषि सामानि यज्ञः क्षत्रं ब्रह्म च ॥ २.६॥

*arā iva rathanābhau prāṇe sarvaṃ pratiṣṭhitam .  
ṛco yajūgͫṣi sāmāni yajñaḥ kṣatraṃ brahma ca .. 2.6..*

Like spokes in the hub of a wheel, all things are set in prana, including the Rig Veda, the Yajur Veda, the Sama Veda, the kshattriyas, and the brahmins.

Like spokes in the hub of a wheel, everything is set in Prâna: the verses of the Rig-veda, Yagur-veda, and Sâma-veda, the sacrifice, the Kshatriyas, and the Brâhmans.

### 2.7

प्रजापतिश्चरसि गर्भे त्वमेव प्रतिजायसे ।  
तुभ्यं प्राण प्रजास्त्विमा बलिं हरन्ति  
यः प्राणैः प्रतितिष्ठसि ॥ २.७॥

*prajāpatiścarasi garbhe tvameva pratijāyase .  
tubhyaṃ prāṇa prajāstvimā baliṃ haranti  
yaḥ prāṇaiḥ pratitiṣṭhasi .. 2.7..*

As Prajapati you move about in the womb, and it is indeed you who are born again. To you, O Prana, creatures bring offerings, to you who live in the body with the organs.

As Prajapati, lord of creatures, you move about in the womb, and you indeed are born again. These creatures bring offerings to you, the Prâna, who dwell with the other prânas (the organs of sense).

### 2.8

देवानामसि वह्नितमः पितॄणां प्रथमा स्वधा ।  
ऋषीणां चरितं सत्यमथर्वाङ्गिरसामसि ॥ २.८॥

*devānāmasi vahnitamaḥ pitṝṇāṃ prathamā svadhā .  
ṛṣīṇāṃ caritaṃ satyamatharvāṅgirasāmasi .. 2.8..*

You are the chief bearer of oblations to the gods and the first offering to the departed fathers. You are the true activity of the rishis, of the Atharvangiras.

You are the best carrier for the Gods and the first offering to the Fathers. You are the true work of the Rishis, of the Atharvâṅgiras.

### 2.9

इन्द्रस्त्वं प्राण तेजसा रुद्रोऽसि परिरक्षिता ।  
त्वमन्तरिक्षे चरसि सूर्यस्त्वं ज्योतिषां पतिः ॥ २.९॥

*indrastvaṃ prāṇa tejasā rudro'si parirakṣitā .  
tvamantarikṣe carasi sūryastvaṃ jyotiṣāṃ patiḥ .. 2.9..*

You are Indra, O Prana, and Rudra too in valor. You are the Protector. You move through the sky; you are the sun, the lord of lights.

O Prâna, by your light you are Indra, and as protector you are Rudra. You move through the sky; you are the sun, the lord of lights.

### 2.10

यदा त्वमभिवर्षस्यथेमाः प्राण ते प्रजाः ।  
आनन्दरूपास्तिष्ठन्ति कामायान्नं भविष्यतीति ॥ २.१०॥

*yadā tvamabhivarṣasyathemāḥ prāṇa te prajāḥ .  
ānandarūpāstiṣṭhanti kāmāyānnaṃ bhaviṣyatīti .. 2.10..*

When you pour down rain, O Prana, these creatures of yours rejoice, thinking there will be all the food they want.

When you pour down rain, O Prâna, these creatures of yours rejoice, hoping there will be as much food as they desire.

### 2.11

व्रात्यस्त्वं प्राणैकर्षरत्ता विश्वस्य सत्पतिः ।  
वयमाद्यस्य दातारः पिता त्वं मातरिश्व नः ॥ २.११॥

*vrātyastvaṃ prāṇaikarṣarattā viśvasya satpatiḥ .  
vayamādyasya dātāraḥ pitā tvaṃ mātariśva naḥ .. 2.11..*

You are vratya, O Prana, and the Ekarshi Fire that consumes the butter. You are the Supreme Lord of all. We give the butter you consume, O Matarisva! You are our father.

You are a Vrâtya, O Prâna, the only Rishi, the consumer of everything, the good lord. We give what you are to consume; you, O Mâtarisva, are our father.

### 2.12

या ते तनूर्वाचि प्रतिष्ठिता या श्रोत्रे या च चक्षुषि ।  
या च मनसि सन्तता शिवां तां कुरु मोत्क्रमीः ॥ २.१२॥

*yā te tanūrvāci pratiṣṭhitā yā śrotre yā ca cakṣuṣi .  
yā ca manasi santatā śivāṃ tāṃ kuru motkramīḥ .. 2.12..*

Make gracious that form of yours which lives in speech, in the ear, and in the eye, and which pervades the mind. Do not leave!

Make gracious that body of yours which lives in speech, in the ear, and in the eye, and which pervades the mind. Do not go away!

### 2.13

प्राणस्येदं वशे सर्वं त्रिदिवे यत् प्रतिष्ठितम् ।  
मातेव पुत्रान् रक्षस्व श्रीश्च प्रज्ञां च विधेहि न इति ॥ २.१३॥  
इति प्रश्नोपनिषदि द्वितीयः प्रश्नः ॥

*prāṇasyedaṃ vaśe sarvaṃ tridive yat pratiṣṭhitam .  
māteva putrān rakṣasva śrīśca prajñāṃ ca vidhehi na iti .. 2.13..  
iti praśnopaniṣadi dvitīyaḥ praśnaḥ ..*

All that exists here is under the control of prana, and so is what exists in heaven. Protect us as a mother protects her sons, and grant us prosperity and wisdom.

All this, whatever exists in the three heavens, is in the power of Prâna. Protect us as a mother protects her sons, and give us happiness and wisdom.

### 3.1

अथ हैनं कौशल्यश्चाश्वलायनः पप्रच्छ । भगवन् कुत  
एष प्राणो जायते कथमायात्यस्मिञ्शरीर आत्मानं वा  
प्रविभज्य कथं प्रतिष्ठते केनोत्क्रमते कथं बाह्यमभिधत्ते  
कथमध्यात्ममिति ॥ ३.१॥

*atha hainaṃ kauśalyaścāśvalāyanaḥ papraccha . bhagavan kuta  
eṣa prāṇo jāyate kathamāyātyasmiñśarīra ātmānaṃ vā  
pravibhajya kathaṃ pratiṣṭhate kenotkramate kathaṃ bāhyamabhidhatte  
kathamadhyātmamiti .. 3.1..*

Then Kausalya, son of Asvala, asked Pippalada: Sir, where is this prana born from? How does it enter this body? Having divided itself, how does it remain in the body? How does it leave? How does it support what is outside, and how what is inside?

Then Kausalya Âsvalâyana asked: Sir, from where is that Prâna (spirit) born? How does it enter this body? How does it remain after dividing itself? How does it go out? How does it support what is outside, and how what is inside?

### 3.2

तस्मै स होवाचातिप्रश्नान् पृच्छसि ब्रह्मिष्ठोऽसीति  
तस्मात्तेऽहं ब्रवीमि ॥ ३.२॥

*tasmai sa hovācātipraśnān pṛcchasi brahmiṣṭho'sīti  
tasmātte'haṃ bravīmi .. 3.2..*

The teacher answered him: You ask difficult questions. You must be deeply devoted to Brahman, so I will answer you.

He answered: Your questions are harder, but you are very fond of Brahman, so I will tell you.

### 3.3

आत्मन एष प्राणो जायते । यथैषा पुरुषे  
छायैतस्मिन्नेतदाततं  
मनोकृतेनायात्यस्मिञ्शरीरे ॥ ३.३॥

*ātmana eṣa prāṇo jāyate . yathaiṣā puruṣe  
chāyaitasminnetadātataṃ  
manokṛtenāyātyasmiñśarīre .. 3.3..*

This prana is born of Atman. As a person casts a shadow, so Atman casts this prana. It enters this body through the activity of the mind.

This Prâna (spirit) is born of the Self. Like the shadow thrown on a man, it is spread out over the Brahman. It enters this body through the work of the mind.

### 3.4

यथा सम्रादेवाधिकृतान् विनियुङ्क्ते । एतन् ग्रामानोतान्  
ग्रामानधितिष्ठस्वेत्येवमेवैष प्राण इतरान् प्राणान् पृथक्  
पृथगेव सन्निधत्ते ॥ ३.४॥

*yathā samrādevādhikṛtān viniyuṅkte . etan grāmānotān  
grāmānadhitiṣṭhasvetyevamevaiṣa prāṇa itarān prāṇān pṛthak  
pṛthageva sannidhatte .. 3.4..*

As an emperor instructs his officials, saying, "Govern these villages or those," so this prana assigns the other pranas, each to its own place.

As a king instructs officials, telling them to govern these villages or those, so that Prâna (spirit) arranges the other prânas, each for its own work.

### 3.5

पायूपस्थेऽपानं चक्षुःश्रोत्रे मुखनासिकाभ्यां प्राणः स्वयं  
प्रातिष्ठते मध्ये तु समानः । एष ह्येतद्धुतमन्नं समं नयति  
तस्मादेताः सप्तार्चिषो भवन्ति ॥ ३.५॥

*pāyūpasthe'pānaṃ cakṣuḥśrotre mukhanāsikābhyāṃ prāṇaḥ svayaṃ  
prātiṣṭhate madhye tu samānaḥ . eṣa hyetaddhutamannaṃ samaṃ nayati  
tasmādetāḥ saptārciṣo bhavanti .. 3.5..*

Prana places apana in the organs of excretion and generation. It moves through the mouth and nose itself, and lives in the eye and ear. In the middle is samana, which distributes evenly what is offered as food in the stomach's fire. From this prana fire the seven flames arise.

The Apâna (down-breathing) is in the organs of excretion and generation. The Prâna itself lives in eye and ear, passing through mouth and nose. In the middle is the Samâna (on-breathing), which carries what has been sacrificed as food evenly over the body, and from it the seven lights proceed.

### 3.6

हृदि ह्येष आत्मा । अत्रैतदेकशतं नाडीनां तासां शतं  
शतमेकैकस्या द्वासप्ततिर्द्वासप्ततिः प्रतिशाखानाडीसहस्राणि  
भवन्त्यासु व्यानश्चरति ॥ ३.६॥

*hṛdi hyeṣa ātmā . atraitadekaśataṃ nāḍīnāṃ tāsāṃ śataṃ  
śatamekaikasyā dvāsaptatirdvāsaptatiḥ pratiśākhānāḍīsahasrāṇi  
bhavantyāsu vyānaścarati .. 3.6..*

The atman lives in the heart, where there are one hundred and one arteries (nadi). Each of these has one hundred branches, and each branch in turn has seventy-two thousand smaller vessels. Vyana moves in these.

The Self is in the heart. There are the 101 arteries there, each with a hundred smaller veins, and each of these branches has 72,000. In these the Vyâna (back-breathing) moves.

### 3.7

अथैकयोर्ध्व उदानः पुण्येन पुण्यं लोकं नयति पापेन  
पापमुभाभ्यामेव मनुष्यलोकम् ॥ ३.७॥

*athaikayordhva udānaḥ puṇyena puṇyaṃ lokaṃ nayati pāpena  
pāpamubhābhyāmeva manuṣyalokam .. 3.7..*

Then udana, rising through one of these, carries the departing soul to the virtuous world for its virtuous deeds, to the sinful world for its sinful deeds, and to the world of men for both.

Through one of them the Udâna (out-breathing) leads us upward to the good world through good work, to the bad world through bad work, and to the world of men through both.

### 3.8

आदित्यो ह वै बाह्यः प्राण उदयत्येष ह्येनं चाक्षुषं  
प्राणमनुगृह्णानः । पृथिव्यां या देवता सैषा पुरुषस्य  
अपानमवष्टभ्यान्तरा यदाकाशः स समानो वायुर्व्यानः ॥ ३.८॥

*ādityo ha vai bāhyaḥ prāṇa udayatyeṣa hyenaṃ cākṣuṣaṃ  
prāṇamanugṛhṇānaḥ . pṛthivyāṃ yā devatā saiṣā puruṣasya  
apānamavaṣṭabhyāntarā yadākāśaḥ sa samāno vāyurvyānaḥ .. 3.8..*

The sun is truly the external prana, for it rises in support of the prana in the eye. The deity in the earth controls a person's apana. The space, akasa, between heaven and earth is samana. The air is vyana.

The sun rises as the external Prâna, for it helps the Prâna in the eye. The deity in the earth is there to support a person's Apâna (down-breathing). The ether between sun and earth is the Samâna (on-breathing), and the air is the Vyâna (back-breathing).

### 3.9

तेजो ह वा उदानस्तस्मादुपशान्ततेजाः । पुनर्भवमिन्द्रियैर्मनसि  
सम्पद्यमानैः ॥ ३.९॥

*tejo ha vā udānastasmādupaśāntatejāḥ . punarbhavamindriyairmanasi  
sampadyamānaiḥ .. 3.9..*

Fire is truly udana. So one whose fire has gone out departs for rebirth, with the senses absorbed in the mind.

Light is the Udâna (out-breathing). So one whose light has gone out comes to a new birth with the senses absorbed in the mind.

### 3.10

यच्चित्तस्तेनैष प्राणमायाति । प्राणस्तेजसा युक्तः सहात्मना  
तथासङ्कल्पितं लोकं नयति ॥ ३.१०॥

*yaccittastenaiṣa prāṇamāyāti . prāṇastejasā yuktaḥ sahātmanā  
tathāsaṅkalpitaṃ lokaṃ nayati .. 3.10..*

Whatever one is thinking, with that one enters prana. Prana, joined with fire and together with the soul, leads to whatever world has been shaped by thought.

Whatever his thought at the time of death, with that he goes back to Prâna; and the Prâna, joined with light and together with the self (the jîvâtmâ), leads on to the world he has earned.

### 3.11

य एवं विद्वान् प्राणं वेद न हास्य प्रजा हीयतेऽमृतो  
भवति तदेषः श्लोकः ॥ ३.११॥

*ya evaṃ vidvān prāṇaṃ veda na hāsya prajā hīyate'mṛto  
bhavati tadeṣaḥ ślokaḥ .. 3.11..*

The wise person who knows prana in this way does not lose his offspring and becomes immortal. On this there is the following verse:

Whoever, knowing in this way, knows Prâna, his offspring does not perish, and he becomes immortal. The Sloka says this:

### 3.12

उत्पत्तिमायतिं स्थानं विभुत्वं चैव पञ्चधा ।  
अध्यात्मं चैव प्राणस्य विज्ञायामृतमश्नुते  
विज्ञायामृतमश्नुत इति ॥ ३.१२॥  
इति प्रश्नोपनिषदि तृतीयः प्रश्नः ॥

*utpattimāyatiṃ sthānaṃ vibhutvaṃ caiva pañcadhā .  
adhyātmaṃ caiva prāṇasya vijñāyāmṛtamaśnute  
vijñāyāmṛtamaśnuta iti .. 3.12..  
iti praśnopaniṣadi tṛtīyaḥ praśnaḥ ..*

One who knows prana's origin, its entry, its place, its fivefold distribution, its inner aspect and also its outer one gains immortality; yes, gains immortality.

One who has known the origin, entry, place, fivefold distribution, and inner state of the Prâna gains immortality; yes, gains immortality.

### 4.1

अथ हैनं सौर्यायणि गार्ग्यः पप्रच्छ । भगवन्नेतस्मिन् पुरुषे  
कानि स्वपन्ति कान्यस्मिञ्जाग्रति कतर एष देवः स्वप्नान् पश्यति  
कस्यैतत् सुखं भवति कस्मिन्नु सर्वे सम्प्रतिष्ठिता भवन्तीति ॥ ४.१॥

*atha hainaṃ sauryāyaṇi gārgyaḥ papraccha . bhagavannetasmin puruṣe  
kāni svapanti kānyasmiñjāgrati katara eṣa devaḥ svapnān paśyati  
kasyaitat sukhaṃ bhavati kasminnu sarve sampratiṣṭhitā bhavantīti .. 4.1..*

Next Sauryayani of the family of Garga asked: Sir, what are the things that sleep in a person, and what stays awake in him? Which deity sees dreams? Whose is the happiness? In whom, again, are all these gathered together?

Then Sauryâyanin Gârgya asked: Sir, what sleeps in this person, and what is awake in him? What power (deva) sees dreams? Whose is the happiness? On what do all of these depend?

### 4.2

तस्मै स होवाच यथा गार्ग्य मरीचयोऽर्कस्यास्तं गच्छतः सर्वा  
एतस्मिंस्तेजोमण्डल एकीभवन्ति ताः पुनः पुनरुदयतः प्रचरन्त्येवं  
ह वै तत् सर्वं परे देवे मनस्येकीभवति तेन तर्ह्येष पुरुषो न  
श‍ृणोति न पश्यति न जिघ्रति न रसयते न स्पृशते नाभिवदते  
नादत्ते नानन्दयते न विसृजते नेयायते स्वपितीत्याचक्षते ॥ ४.२॥

*tasmai sa hovāca yathā gārgya marīcayo'rkasyāstaṃ gacchataḥ sarvā  
etasmiṃstejomaṇḍala ekībhavanti tāḥ punaḥ punarudayataḥ pracarantyevaṃ  
ha vai tat sarvaṃ pare deve manasyekībhavati tena tarhyeṣa puruṣo na  
śṛṇoti na paśyati na jighrati na rasayate na spṛśate nābhivadate  
nādatte nānandayate na visṛjate neyāyate svapitītyācakṣate .. 4.2..*

Pippalada answered him: O Gargya, as the sun's rays are gathered into its shining disc when it sets and go forth again when it rises, so all these, the objects and the senses, become one in the higher god, the mind. So at that time a person does not hear, see, smell, taste, or touch; he does not speak, grasp, enjoy, emit, or move about. "He is asleep," people say.

He answered: O Gârgya, as all the rays of the sun are gathered into that disc of light when it sets, and come forth each time it rises again, so all this, all the senses, is gathered into the highest faculty (deva), the mind. So at that time the person does not hear, see, smell, taste, or touch; he does not speak, take, enjoy, evacuate, or move about. "He sleeps," people say.

### 4.3

प्राणाग्नय एवैतस्मिन् पुरे जाग्रति । गार्हपत्यो ह वा एषोऽपानो  
व्यानोऽन्वाहार्यपचनो यद्गार्हपत्यात् प्रणीयते प्रणयनादाहवनीयः  
प्राणः ॥ ४.३॥

*prāṇāgnaya evaitasmin pure jāgrati . gārhapatyo ha vā eṣo'pāno  
vyāno'nvāhāryapacano yadgārhapatyāt praṇīyate praṇayanādāhavanīyaḥ  
prāṇaḥ .. 4.3..*

The prana fires stay awake in this city. Apana is the Garhapatya Fire and vyana the Anvaharyapachana Fire. And prana is the Ahavaniya Fire, named for being taken, since it is taken from the Garhapatya Fire.

The fires of the prânas are, in a sense, awake in that town, the body. The Apâna is the Gârhapatya fire and the Vyâna the Anvâhâryapakana fire. And because it is taken out of the Gârhapatya fire, the fire for taking out, the Prâna is the Âhavanîya fire. The Apâna is identified with the Gârhapatya fire with no reason given until later, when the Prâna is said to be the Âhavanîya fire, taken out of the Gârhapatya, here called pranayana, just as the prâna proceeds from the apâna in sleep. The Vyâna is identified with the Dakshinâgni, the Southern fire, because it issues from the heart through an opening on the right.

### 4.4

*yaducchvāsaniḥśvāsāvetāvāhutī samaṃ nayatīti sa samānaḥ . mano ha

gamayati .. 4.4..*

Samana is so called because it evenly distributes the two oblations, the out-breath and the in-breath; it is the priest. The mind is truly the sacrificer. Udana is the fruit of the sacrifice, because every day, in deep sleep, it leads the sacrificer to Brahman.

Because it carries these two oblations, the out-breathing and the in-breathing, evenly, the Samâna is the Hotri priest. The mind is the sacrificer, and the Udâna is the reward of the sacrifice; every day, in deep sleep, it leads the sacrificer to Brahman.

### 4.5

अत्रैष देवः स्वप्ने महिमानमनुभवति । यद्दृष्टं  
दृष्टमनुपश्यति श्रुतं श्रुतमेवार्थमनुश‍ृणोति

चादृष्टं च श्रुतं चाश्रुतं चानुभूतं चाननुभूतं च  
सच्चासच्च सर्वं पश्यति सर्वः पश्यति ॥ ४.५॥

*atraiṣa devaḥ svapne mahimānamanubhavati . yaddṛṣṭaṃ  
dṛṣṭamanupaśyati śrutaṃ śrutamevārthamanuśṛṇoti  
deśadigantaraiśca pratyanubhūtaṃ punaḥ punaḥ pratyanubhavati dṛṣṭaṃ

saccāsacca sarvaṃ paśyati sarvaḥ paśyati .. 4.5..*

There, in dreams, that god, the mind, experiences glory. Whatever has been seen he sees again; whatever has been heard he hears again; whatever has been experienced in various countries and regions he experiences again. Whether seen or unseen, heard or unheard, real or unreal, he sees it all. He sees all, being all himself.

There that god, the mind, enjoys greatness in sleep. What has been seen he sees again; what has been heard he hears again; what has been enjoyed in various countries and regions he enjoys again. What has been seen and not seen, heard and not heard, enjoyed and not enjoyed, he sees it all; being all, he sees.

### 4.6

स यदा तेजसाऽभिभूतो भवति । अत्रैष देवः स्वप्नान्न  
पश्यत्यथ यदैतस्मिञ्शरीर एतत्सुखं भवति ॥ ४.६॥

*sa yadā tejasā'bhibhūto bhavati . atraiṣa devaḥ svapnānna  
paśyatyatha yadaitasmiñśarīra etatsukhaṃ bhavati .. 4.6..*

When the jiva is overcome by light he sees no dreams, and at that time this happiness arises in the body.

And when he is overpowered by light, that god sees no dreams, and at that time that happiness arises in his body.

### 4.7

स यथा सोभ्य वयांसि वसोवृक्षं सम्प्रतिष्ठन्ते । एवं

*sa yathā sobhya vayāṃsi vasovṛkṣaṃ sampratiṣṭhante . evaṃ

### 4.9

एष हि द्रष्टा स्प्रष्टा श्रोता घ्राता रसयिता मन्ता बोद्धा कर्ता

Whoever knows that indestructible being gains what is highest and indestructible, he who is without shadow, without body, without color, bright. Yes, O friend, whoever knows it becomes all-knowing and becomes all. On this there is this Sloka:

### 4.11

O friend, whoever knows that imperishable Being in which the intelligent self rests, along with the gods, the pranas, and the elements, becomes all-knowing and enters into all.

O friend, whoever knows that indestructible being in which the true knower, the vital spirits (prânas), with all the powers (deva), and the elements rest, being all-knowing, has penetrated all.

### 5.1

अथ हैनं शैब्यः सत्यकामः पप्रच्छ । स यो ह वै  
तद्भगवन्मनुष्येषु प्रायणान्तमोङ्कारमभिध्यायीत । कतमं वाव  
स तेन लोकं जयतीति । तस्मै स होवाच ॥ ५.१॥

*atha hainaṃ śaibyaḥ satyakāmaḥ papraccha . sa yo ha vai  
tadbhagavanmanuṣyeṣu prāyaṇāntamoṅkāramabhidhyāyīta . katamaṃ vāva

Then Satyakama, son of Sibi, asked Pippalada: Sir, if someone among men were to meditate here on the syllable AUM until death, which world would he truly gain by it?

Then Saivya Satyakâma asked him: Sir, if someone among men were to meditate here on the syllable Om until death, what would he gain by it?

### 5.2

एतद्वै सत्यकाम परं चापरं च ब्रह्म यदोङ्कारः ।  
तस्माद्विद्वानेतेनैवायतनेनैकतरमन्वेति ॥ ५.२॥

tasmādvidvānetenaivāyatanenaikataramanveti .. 5.2..*

He answered: O Satyakama, the syllable AUM is the Supreme Brahman and also the other Brahman. So the one who knows it reaches, with its support, either the one or the other.

He answered: O Satyakâma, the syllable Om (AUM) is the highest Brahman and also the other. So the one who knows it reaches, by this same means, one of the two.

### 5.3

स यद्येकमात्रमभिध्यायीत स तेनैव संवेदितस्तूर्णमेव  
जगत्यामभिसम्पद्यते । तमृचो मनुष्यलोकमुपनयन्ते स तत्र

*sa yadyekamātramabhidhyāyīta sa tenaiva saṃveditastūrṇameva

tapasā brahmacaryeṇa śraddhayā sampanno mahimānamanubhavati .. 5.3..*

If he meditates on one letter (matra), then, enlightened by that alone, he soon returns to earth after death. The rik verses lead him to the world of men. By practicing austerity, chastity, and faith he enjoys greatness.

If he meditates on one Mâtrâ (the A), then, enlightened by that alone, he quickly arrives at the earth. The Rik-verses lead him to the world of men, and endowed there with penance, abstinence, and faith, he enjoys greatness.

### 5.4

अथ यदि द्विमात्रेण मनसि सम्पद्यते सोऽन्तरिक्षं  
यजुर्भिरुन्नीयते सोमलोकम् । स सोमलोके विभुतिमनुभूय

yajurbhirunnīyate somalokam . sa somaloke vibhutimanubhūya  
punarāvartate .. 5.4..*

If, again, he meditates on the second letter, he reaches the mind and is carried up by the yajur verses to the intermediate space, the Plane of the Moon. After enjoying greatness on the Plane of the Moon, he comes back here again.

If he meditates with two Mâtrâs (A + U), he reaches the Manas and is led up by the Yagus-verses to the sky, the Soma-world. After enjoying greatness in the Soma-world, he returns again.

### 5.6

तिस्रो मात्रा मृत्युमत्यः प्रयुक्ता  
अन्योन्यसक्ताः अनविप्रयुक्ताः ।

The three Mâtrâs (A + U + M), if used separately and only joined one to another, are mortal. But in acts, whether outer, inner, or intermediate, if well performed, the sage does not tremble.

ऋग्भिरेतं यजुर्भिरन्तरिक्षं

यस्मिन्नताः षोडशकलाः प्रभवन्तीति ॥ ६.२॥

*tasmai sa hovācehaivāntaḥśarīre somya sa puruṣo  
yasminnatāḥ ṣoḍaśakalāḥ prabhavantīti .. 6.2..*

Pippalada told him: That Person, the source from which these sixteen parts arise, is right here inside the body.

He answered: "Friend, the person in whom these sixteen parts arise is here inside the body."

### 6.3

स ईक्षाचक्रे । कस्मिन्नहमुत्क्रान्त उत्क्रान्तो भविष्यामि  
कस्मिन्वा प्रतिष्ठिते प्रतिष्ठास्यामीति ॥ ६.३॥

*sa īkṣācakre . kasminnahamutkrānta utkrānto bhaviṣyāmi  
kasminvā pratiṣṭhite pratiṣṭhāsyāmīti .. 6.3..*

The Purusha considered: "What is it whose departure will mean my departure, and whose staying will mean my staying?"

He considered: What is it whose going will make me go, and whose staying will make me stay?

### 6.4

स प्राणमसृजत प्राणाच्छ्रद्धां खं वायुर्ज्योतिरापः पृथिवीन्द्रियं  
मनः । अन्नमन्नाद्वीर्यं तपो मन्त्राः कर्म लोका लोकेषु च नाम च  
॥ ६.४॥

*sa prāṇamasṛjata prāṇācchraddhāṃ khaṃ vāyurjyotirāpaḥ pṛthivīndriyaṃ  
manaḥ . annamannādvīryaṃ tapo mantrāḥ karma lokā lokeṣu ca nāma ca  
.. 6.4..*

He brought forth prana. From prana came faith, space, air, fire, water, earth, the organs, mind and food. From food came virility, austerity, the Vedic hymns, sacrifice and the worlds, and within the worlds He brought forth names.

He sent forth (created) Prâna (spirit). From Prâna came Sraddhâ (faith), ether, air, light, water, earth, sense, mind and food. From food came vigour, penance, hymns, sacrifice and the worlds, and within the worlds, name as well.

### 6.5

स यथेमा नद्यः स्यन्दमानाः समुद्रायणाः समुद्रं प्राप्यास्तं  
गच्छन्ति भिद्येते तासां नामरूपे समुद्र इत्येवं प्रोच्यते । एवमेवास्य  
परिद्रष्टुरिमाः षोडशकलाः पुरुषायणाः पुरुषं प्राप्यास्तं गच्छन्ति  
भिद्येते चासां नामरूपे पुरुष इत्येवं प्रोच्यते स एषोऽकलोऽमृतो  
भवति तदेष श्लोकः ॥ ६.५॥

*sa yathemā nadyaḥ syandamānāḥ samudrāyaṇāḥ samudraṃ prāpyāstaṃ  
gacchanti bhidyete tāsāṃ nāmarūpe samudra ityevaṃ procyate . evamevāsya  
paridraṣṭurimāḥ ṣoḍaśakalāḥ puruṣāyaṇāḥ puruṣaṃ prāpyāstaṃ gacchanti  
bhidyete cāsāṃ nāmarūpe puruṣa ityevaṃ procyate sa eṣo'kalo'mṛto  
bhavati tadeṣa ślokaḥ .. 6.5..*

Rivers flowing toward the ocean vanish into it once they arrive; their names and forms are destroyed, and they are simply called the ocean. In the same way, these sixteen parts of the seer, whose goal is the Purusha, vanish into the Purusha once they reach Him; their names and forms are destroyed, and they are simply called the Purusha. He becomes partless and immortal. There is this verse about it:

These flowing rivers head for the ocean, and on reaching it they sink into it; their name and form are broken, and people speak only of the ocean. Exactly so, these sixteen parts of the spectator head for the person (purusha), and on reaching the person they sink into him; their name and form are broken, and people speak only of the person, and he becomes partless and immortal. There is this verse about it:

### 6.6

अरा इव रथनाभौ कला यस्मिन्प्रतिष्ठिताः ।  
तं वेद्यं पुरुषं वेद यथ मा वो मृत्युः परिव्यथा इति ॥ ६.६॥

*arā iva rathanābhau kalā yasminpratiṣṭhitāḥ .  
taṃ vedyaṃ puruṣaṃ veda yatha mā vo mṛtyuḥ parivyathā iti .. 6.6..*

The parts rest firmly in the Purusha like spokes in the hub of a wheel. He alone is to be known. Know Him, so that death may not touch you.

Know the person who is to be known, in whom these parts rest like spokes in the hub of a wheel, so that death may not hurt you.

### 6.7

तान् होवाचैतावदेवाहमेतत् परं ब्रह्म वेद । नातः  
परमस्तीति ॥ ६.७॥

*tān hovācaitāvadevāhametat paraṃ brahma veda . nātaḥ  
paramastīti .. 6.7..*

Pippalada told them: This is as far as I know the Supreme Brahman. Nothing is higher than this.

Then he (Pippalâda) told them: "This much do I know of this Highest Brahman. Nothing is higher than it."

### 6.8

ते तमर्चयन्तस्त्वं हि नः पिता योऽस्माकमविद्यायाः परं पारं  
तारयसीति । नमः परमऋषिभ्यो नमः परमऋषिभ्यः ॥ ६.८॥  
इति प्रश्नोपनिषदि षष्ठः प्रश्नः ॥

*te tamarcayantastvaṃ hi naḥ pitā yo'smākamavidyāyāḥ paraṃ pāraṃ  
tārayasīti . namaḥ paramaṛṣibhyo namaḥ paramaṛṣibhyaḥ .. 6.8..  
iti praśnopaniṣadi ṣaṣṭhaḥ praśnaḥ ..*

They worshipped him and said: You truly are our father, for you have carried us across our ignorance to the far shore. Adoration to the supreme rishis! Adoration to the supreme rishis!

Praising him, they said: "You truly are our father, you who carry us from our ignorance to the far shore."
