# Isha Upanishad

### 0

ओं | पूर्णमदः पूर्णमिदं पूर्णात्पूर्णमुदच्यते ।   
पूर्णस्य पूर्णमादाय पूर्णमेवावशिष्यते ॥

*oṃ | pūrṇamadaḥ pūrṇamidaṃ pūrṇātpūrṇamudacyate |  
pūrṇasya pūrṇamādāya pūrṇamevāvaśiṣyate ||*

All that cannot be seen is the whole (Brahman). All that can be seen is the whole (Brahman). From the whole (Brahman) the whole (Hiranyagarbha) came forth. When the whole (the Universe) is taken back into the whole (Brahman), only the whole (Brahman) is left.

### 1

ईशावास्यमिदं सर्वं यत्किञ्च जगत्यां जगत् ।   
तेन त्यक्तेन भुञ्जीथा मा गृधः कस्य स्विद्धनम् ॥ १ ॥

*īśāvāsyamidaṃ sarvaṃ yatkiñca jagatyāṃ jagat |   
tena tyaktena bhuñjīthā mā gṛdhaḥ kasya sviddhanam || 1 ||*

Everything that moves in this universe, and everything that does not, is covered by the Lord (indwelt, pervaded, enveloped, or clothed by Him). Renounce that and enjoy. Do not crave anyone's wealth (or: Do not crave, for whose is wealth?).

### 2

कुर्वन्नेवेह कर्माणि जिजीविषेच्छतं समाः ।   
एवं त्वयि नान्यथेतोऽस्ति न कर्म लिप्यते नरे ॥ २ ॥

*kurvanneveha karmāṇi jijīviṣecchataṃ samāḥ |   
evaṃ tvayi nānyatheto'sti na karma lipyate nare || 2 ||*

Anyone who wants to live a hundred years on this earth should spend them doing Karma. As long as you live as a human being in this way, there is no other way for Karma not to cling to you.

### 3

असुर्या नाम ते लोका अन्धेन तमसावृताः ।   
तांस्ते प्रेत्याभिगच्छन्ति ये के चात्महनो जनाः ॥ ३ ॥

*asuryā nāma te lokā andhena tamasāvṛtāḥ |   
tāṃste pretyābhigacchanti ye ke cātmahano janāḥ || 3 ||*

Those births share the nature of the Asuras and are wrapped in blind darkness. People who kill their Atman go to them after leaving the body.

### 4

अनेजदेकं मनसो जवीयो नैनद्देवा आप्नुवन्पूर्वमर्षत् ।   
तद्धावतोऽन्यानत्येति तिष्ठत्तस्मिन्नपो मातरिश्वा दधाति ॥ ४ ॥

*anejadekaṃ manaso javīyo nainaddevā āpnuvanpūrvamarṣat |   
taddhāvato'nyānatyeti tiṣṭhattasminnapo mātariśvā dadhāti || 4 ||*

It does not move, it is one, and it is swifter than the mind. The Devas (the senses) could not catch it, for it ran ahead of them. While sitting still, it outruns those who chase it. Through it the all-pervading air (Sutratman) sustains the activity of all living beings.

### 5

तदेजति तन्नैजति तद्दूरे तद्वन्तिके ।   
तदन्तरस्य सर्वस्य तदु सर्वस्यास्य बाह्यतः ॥ ५ ॥

*tadejati tannaijati taddūre tadvantike |   
tadantarasya sarvasya tadu sarvasyāsya bāhyataḥ || 5 ||*

It moves and it does not move. It is far away and it is close by. It is inside all this and it is outside all this.

### 6

यस्तु सर्वाणि भूतान्यात्मन्येवानुपश्यति ।   
सर्वभूतेषु चात्मानं ततो न विजुगुप्सते ॥ ६ ॥

*yastu sarvāṇi bhūtānyātmanyevānupaśyati |   
sarvabhūteṣu cātmānaṃ tato na vijugupsate || 6 ||*

Whoever sees all things in his Atman and his Atman in all things feels no revulsion because of that.

### 7

यस्मिन्सर्वाणि भूतान्यात्मैवाभूद्विजानतः ।   
तत्र को मोहः कः शोक एकत्वमनुपश्यतः ॥ ७ ॥

*yasminsarvāṇi bhūtānyātmaivābhūdvijānataḥ |   
tatra ko mohaḥ kaḥ śoka ekatvamanupaśyataḥ || 7 ||*

When all Bhutas have become one with the knower's own Atman, what confusion or sorrow can there be for him who sees this oneness?

### 8

स पर्यगाच्छुक्रमकायमव्रणमस्नाविरंशुद्धम् अपापविद्धम् ।   
कविर्मनीषी परिभूः स्ययम्भूर्याथातथ्यतोऽर्थान्व्यदधाच्छाश्वतीभ्यः समाभ्यः ॥ ८ ॥

*sa paryagācchukramakāyamavraṇamasnāviraṃśuddham apāpaviddham |   
kavirmanīṣī paribhūḥ syayambhūryāthātathyato'rthānvyadadhācchāśvatībhyaḥ samābhyaḥ || 8 ||*

He filled everything. He is radiant, bodiless, unwounded, without muscles, pure, and untouched by sin. Far-seeing, all-knowing, transcendent, and self-born, he assigned to the various eternal creators their proper functions.

### 9

अन्धन्तमः प्रविशन्ति येऽविद्यामुपासते ।   
ततो भूय इव ते तमोय उ विद्यायां रताः ॥ ९ ॥

*andhantamaḥ praviśanti ye'vidyāmupāsate |   
tato bhūya iva te tamoya u vidyāyāṃ ratāḥ || 9 ||*

Those who worship Avidya alone sink into blind darkness, and those who worship Vidya alone sink into a darkness greater still.

### 10

अन्यदेवाहुर्विद्ययाऽन्यदाहुरविद्यया ।   
इति शुश्रुम धीराणां ये नस्तद्विचचक्षिरे ॥ १० ॥

*anyadevāhurvidyayā'nyadāhuravidyayā |   
iti śuśruma dhīrāṇāṃ ye nastadvicacakṣire || 10 ||*

Vidya is said to bring one result and Avidya another. This we have heard from the wise who taught us both Vidya and Avidya.

### 11

विद्यां चाविद्यां च यस्तद्वेदोभयं सह ।   
अविद्यया मृत्युं तीर्त्वा विद्ययामृतमश्नुते ॥ ११ ॥

*vidyāṃ cāvidyāṃ ca yastadvedobhayaṃ saha |   
avidyayā mṛtyuṃ tīrtvā vidyayāmṛtamaśnute || 11 ||*

Whoever knows Vidya and Avidya together passes beyond Death through Avidya and reaches immortality through Vidya.

### 12

अन्धं तमः प्रविशन्ति येऽसम्भूतिमुपासते ।   
ततो भूय इव ते तमो य उ संभूत्यां रताः ॥ १२ ॥

*andhaṃ tamaḥ praviśanti ye'sambhūtimupāsate |   
tato bhūya iva te tamo ya u saṃbhūtyāṃ ratāḥ || 12 ||*

Those who worship the unborn Prakriti sink into blind darkness. Those devoted to the Karya Brahman Hiranyagarbha sink into a greater darkness.

### 13

अन्यदेवाहुः संभवादन्यदाहुरसंभवात् ।   
इति शुश्रुम धीराणां ये नस्तद्विचचक्षिरे ॥ १३ ॥

*anyadevāhuḥ saṃbhavādanyadāhurasaṃbhavāt |   
iti śuśruma dhīrāṇāṃ ye nastadvicacakṣire || 13 ||*

It is said that worshipping Hiranyagarbha brings one result and worshipping Prakriti another. This we have heard from the wise teachers who instructed us in it.

### 14

संभूतिं च विनाशं च यस्तद्वेदोभयं सह ।   
विनाशेन मृत्युं तीर्त्वा संभूत्यामृतमश्नुते ॥ १४ ॥

*saṃbhūtiṃ ca vināśaṃ ca yastadvedobhayaṃ saha |   
vināśena mṛtyuṃ tīrtvā saṃbhūtyāmṛtamaśnute || 14 ||*

Those who worship both the unmanifested Prakriti and Hiranyagarbha (Destruction) pass beyond death through the worship of Hiranyagarbha and reach immortality through the worship of Prakriti.

### 15

हिरण्मयेन पात्रेण सत्यस्यापिहितं मुखम् ।   
तत्त्वं पूषन्नपावृणु सत्यधर्माय दृष्टये ॥ १५ ॥

*hiraṇmayena pātreṇa satyasyāpihitaṃ mukham |   
tattvaṃ pūṣannapāvṛṇu satyadharmāya dṛṣṭaye || 15 ||*

The doorway of the True is covered, as if by a golden vessel. O sun, take away that covering, so that I who have been worshipping 'The True' may see it.

### 16

पूषन्नेकर्षे यम सूर्य प्राजापत्य व्यूह रश्मीन्समूह ।   
तेजः यत्ते रूपं कल्याणतमं तत्ते पश्यामि योऽसावसौ पुरुषः सोऽहमस्मि ॥ १६ ॥

*pūṣannekarṣe yama sūrya prājāpatya vyūha raśmīnsamūha |   
tejaḥ yatte rūpaṃ kalyāṇatamaṃ tatte paśyāmi yo'sāvasau puruṣaḥ so'hamasmi || 16 ||*

O Sun, lone traveller of the Heavens, ruler of all, Surya, son of Prajapati, draw back your rays and gather in your burning light. I see your glorious form. I am he, the Purusha within you.

### 17

वायुरनिलममृतमथेदं भस्मान्तं शरीरम् ।   
ओं । क्रतो स्मर कृतं स्मर क्रतो स्मर कृतं स्मर ॥ १७ ॥

*vāyuranilamamṛtamathedaṃ bhasmāntaṃ śarīram |   
oṃ | krato smara kṛtaṃ smara krato smara kṛtaṃ smara || 17 ||*

Let my Prana dissolve into the all-pervading Air, the eternal Sutratman, and let fire burn this body to ashes. Om. O mind, remember, remember my deeds. O mind, remember, remember my deeds.

### 18

अग्ने नय सुपथा राये अस्मान्विश्वानि देव वयुनानि विद्वान् ।   
युयोध्यस्मज्जुहुराणमेनो भूयिष्ठां ते नम उक्तिं विधेम ॥ १८ ॥

*agne naya supathā rāye asmānviśvāni deva vayunāni vidvān |   
yuyodhyasmajjuhurāṇameno bhūyiṣṭhāṃ te nama uktiṃ vidhema || 18 ||*

O Agni, you know all our deeds, O God; lead us along the good path to enjoy the fruits of what we have done. Take from within us the sin of deceit. We offer you many prostrations in words.
