# Kathopanishad

### 1.1.0

<vt>Invocation</vt>  
ॐ  
॥ अथ कठोपनिषद् ॥  
ॐ सह नाववतु । सह नौ भुनक्तु । सहवीर्यं करवावहै ।  
तेजस्वि नावधीतमस्तु । मा विद्विषावहै ॥  
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

*<vt>Invocation</vt>  
oṃ  
.. atha kaṭhopaniṣad ..  
oṃ saha nāvavatu . saha nau bhunaktu . sahavīryaṃ karavāvahai .  
tejasvi nāvadhītamastu . mā vidviṣāvahai ..  
oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ*

Invocation:
Om. May Brahman guard the two of us! May Brahman grant us both the fruit of Knowledge! May we both gain the strength to acquire Knowledge! May our shared study reveal the Truth! May neither of us bear ill will toward the other! Om. Peace! Peace! Peace!

### 1.1.1

ॐ उशन् ह वै वाजश्रवसः सर्ववेदसं ददौ ।  
तस्य ह नचिकेता नाम पुत्र आस ॥ १॥

*oṃ uśan ha vai vājaśravasaḥ sarvavedasaṃ dadau .  
tasya ha naciketā nāma putra āsa  1-1-1*

Wanting rewards, Vajasravasa carried out the Visvajit sacrifice, giving away everything he owned. He had a son called Nachiketa.

### 1.1.2

तँ ह कुमारँ सन्तं दक्षिणासु  
नीयमानासु श्रद्धाविवेश सोऽमन्यत ॥ २॥

*tam̐ ha kumāram̐ santaṃ dakṣiṇāsu  
nīyamānāsu śraddhāviveśa so'manyata  1-1-2*

As the gifts were being handed out, faith came into the heart of Nachiketa, who was still a boy. He thought to himself: Surely the worlds reached by someone who gives away cows that can no longer drink, eat, give milk, or bear calves are joyless.

### 1.1.3

पीतोदका जग्धतृणा दुग्धदोहा निरिन्द्रियाः ।  
अनन्दा नाम ते लोकास्तान् स गच्छति ता ददत् ॥ ३॥

*pītodakā jagdhatṛṇā dugdhadohā nirindriyāḥ .  
anandā nāma te lokāstān sa gacchati tā dadat  1-1-3*

These cows have had their last drink of water and their last meal of grass. Their milk is spent and their strength is gone. The one who gives them reaches worlds that are truly joyless.

### 1.1.4

स होवाच पितरं तत कस्मै मां दास्यसीति ।  
द्वितीयं तृतीयं तँ होवाच मृत्यवे त्वा ददामीति ॥ ४॥

*sa hovāca pitaraṃ tata kasmai māṃ dāsyasīti .  
dvitīyaṃ tṛtīyaṃ tam̐ hovāca mṛtyave tvā dadāmīti  1-1-4*

He asked his father, 'Father, to whom will you give me?' He asked a second time and a third. His father then answered, 'I will give you to death.'

### 1.1.5

बहूनामेमि प्रथमो बहूनामेमि मध्यमः ।  
किँ स्विद्यमस्य कर्तव्यं यन्मयाऽद्य करिष्यति ॥ ५॥

*bahūnāmemi prathamo bahūnāmemi madhyamaḥ .  
kim̐ svidyamasya kartavyaṃ yanmayā'dya kariṣyati  1-1-5*

Among many I come first, or among many I am in the middle, but I am certainly never last. What purpose of the King of Death will my father accomplish today by handing me over to him?

### 1.1.6

अनुपश्य यथा पूर्वे प्रतिपश्य तथाऽपरे ।  
सस्यमिव मर्त्यः पच्यते सस्यमिवाजायते पुनः ॥ ६॥

*anupaśya yathā pūrve pratipaśya tathā'pare .  
sasyamiva martyaḥ pacyate sasyamivājāyate punaḥ  1-1-6*

Nachiketa said: Look back at how it went for those who came before us, and notice how it goes for those with us now. A mortal ripens like grain, and like grain he sprouts again.

### 1.1.7

वैश्वानरः प्रविशत्यतिथिर्ब्राह्मणो गृहान् ।  
तस्यैताँ शान्तिं कुर्वन्ति हर वैवस्वतोदकम् ॥ ७॥

*vaiśvānaraḥ praviśatyatithirbrāhmaṇo gṛhān .  
tasyaitām̐ śāntiṃ kurvanti hara vaivasvatodakam  1-1-7*

A brahmin guest enters a house like fire. The householder calms him by offering water and a seat. Bring him water, O King of Death!

### 1.1.8

आशाप्रतीक्षे संगतँ सूनृतां  
चेष्टापूर्ते पुत्रपशूँश्च सर्वान् ।  
एतद्वृङ्क्ते पुरुषस्याल्पमेधसो  
यस्यानश्नन्वसति ब्राह्मणो गृहे ॥ ८॥

*āśāpratīkṣe saṃgatam̐ sūnṛtāṃ  
ceṣṭāpūrte putrapaśūm̐śca sarvān .  
etadvṛṅkte puruṣasyālpamedhaso  
yasyānaśnanvasati brāhmaṇo gṛhe  1-1-8*

When a brahmin stays in a house without food, he destroys that foolish householder's hopes and expectations, the benefit of his association with pious people, the merit of his kind words, the good results of his sacrifices and charitable deeds, and his cattle and children as well.

### 1.1.9

तिस्रो रात्रीर्यदवात्सीर्गृहे मे-  
ऽनश्नन् ब्रह्मन्नतिथिर्नमस्यः ।  
नमस्तेऽस्तु ब्रह्मन् स्वस्ति मेऽस्तु  
तस्मात्प्रति त्रीन्वरान्वृणीष्व ॥ ९॥

*tisro rātrīryadavātsīrgṛhe me-  
'naśnan brahmannatithirnamasyaḥ .  
namaste'stu brahman svasti me'stu  
tasmātprati trīnvarānvṛṇīṣva  1-1-9*

Yama said: Salutations to you, O Brahmin! You are an honored guest and have stayed in my house for three nights without eating. So choose three boons now, one for each night, O Brahmin. May all go well with me!

### 1.1.10

शान्तसंकल्पः सुमना यथा स्याद्  
वीतमन्युर्गौतमो माऽभि मृत्यो ।  
त्वत्प्रसृष्टं माऽभिवदेत्प्रतीत  
एतत् त्रयाणां प्रथमं वरं वृणे ॥ १०॥

*śāntasaṃkalpaḥ sumanā yathā syād  
vītamanyurgautamo mā'bhi mṛtyo .  
tvatprasṛṣṭaṃ mā'bhivadetpratīta  
etat trayāṇāṃ prathamaṃ varaṃ vṛṇe  1-1-10*

Nachiketa said: O Death, may my father Gautama be calm, cheerful, and without anger toward me! When you have sent me home, may he recognize me and welcome me! This I choose as the first of the three boons.

### 1.1.11

यथा पुरस्ताद् भविता प्रतीत  
औद्दालकिरारुणिर्मत्प्रसृष्टः ।  
सुखँ रात्रीः शयिता वीतमन्युः  
त्वां ददृशिवान्मृत्युमुखात् प्रमुक्तम् ॥ ११॥

*yathā purastād bhavitā pratīta  
auddālakirāruṇirmatprasṛṣṭaḥ .  
sukham̐ rātrīḥ śayitā vītamanyuḥ  
tvāṃ dadṛśivānmṛtyumukhāt pramuktam  1-1-11*

Yama said: By my grace your father, Auddilaki Aruni, will recognize you and treat you as he did before. Having seen you released from the jaws of death, he will sleep peacefully at night and hold no anger against you.

### 1.1.12

स्वर्गे लोके न भयं किंचनास्ति  
न तत्र त्वं न जरया बिभेति ।  
उभे तीर्त्वाऽशनायापिपासे  
शोकातिगो मोदते स्वर्गलोके ॥ १२॥

*svarge loke na bhayaṃ kiṃcanāsti  
na tatra tvaṃ na jarayā bibheti .  
ubhe tīrtvā'śanāyāpipāse  
śokātigo modate svargaloke  1-1-12*

Nachiketa said: There is no fear in heaven. You are not there, and no one there is afraid of old age. Having passed beyond both hunger and thirst, a person in heaven rejoices, free of grief.

### 1.1.13

स त्वमग्निँ स्वर्ग्यमध्येषि मृत्यो  
प्रब्रूहि त्वँ श्रद्दधानाय मह्यम् ।  
स्वर्गलोका अमृतत्वं भजन्त  
एतद् द्वितीयेन वृणे वरेण ॥ १३॥

*sa tvamagnim̐ svargyamadhyeṣi mṛtyo  
prabrūhi tvam̐ śraddadhānāya mahyam .  
svargalokā amṛtatvaṃ bhajanta  
etad dvitīyena vṛṇe vareṇa  1-1-13*

O Death, you know the fire that leads to heaven. I am eager to learn, so explain to me that fire by which those who dwell in heaven gain immortality. This I ask as my second boon.

### 1.1.14

प्र ते ब्रवीमि तदु मे निबोध  
स्वर्ग्यमग्निं नचिकेतः प्रजानन् ।  
अनन्तलोकाप्तिमथो प्रतिष्ठां  
विद्धि त्वमेतं निहितं गुहायाम् ॥ १४॥

*pra te bravīmi tadu me nibodha  
svargyamagniṃ naciketaḥ prajānan .  
anantalokāptimatho pratiṣṭhāṃ  
viddhi tvametaṃ nihitaṃ guhāyām  1-1-14*

Yama said: I know the Fire-sacrifice that leads to Heaven very well, and I will explain it to you. Listen. Understand that this Fire is the means of reaching Heaven. It is the support of the universe and lies hidden in the hearts of the wise.

### 1.1.15

लोकादिमग्निं तमुवाच तस्मै  
या इष्टका यावतीर्वा यथा वा ।  
स चापि तत्प्रत्यवदद्यथोक्तं  
अथास्य मृत्युः पुनरेवाह तुष्टः ॥ १५॥

*lokādimagniṃ tamuvāca tasmai  
yā iṣṭakā yāvatīrvā yathā vā .  
sa cāpi tatpratyavadadyathoktaṃ  
athāsya mṛtyuḥ punarevāha tuṣṭaḥ  1-1-15*

Yama then described to him the Fire that is the source of the worlds, which bricks should be collected for the altar, how many, and how the sacrificial fire should be lit. Nachiketa repeated all of it just as he had been told. Pleased with him, Yama spoke again.

### 1.1.16

तमब्रवीत् प्रीयमाणो महात्मा  
वरं तवेहाद्य ददामि भूयः ।  
तवैव नाम्ना भविताऽयमग्निः  
सृङ्कां चेमामनेकरूपां गृहाण ॥ १६॥

*tamabravīt prīyamāṇo mahātmā  
varaṃ tavehādya dadāmi bhūyaḥ .  
tavaiva nāmnā bhavitā'yamagniḥ  
sṛṅkāṃ cemāmanekarūpāṃ gṛhāṇa  1-1-16*

Delighted, great-souled Death said to Nachiketa: I will now give you one more boon. This fire will bear your name. Take from me, too, this many-colored chain.

### 1.1.17

त्रिणाचिकेतस्त्रिभिरेत्य सन्धिं  
त्रिकर्मकृत्तरति जन्ममृत्यू ।  
ब्रह्मजज्ञं देवमीड्यं विदित्वा  
निचाय्येमाँ शान्तिमत्यन्तमेति ॥ १७॥

*triṇāciketastribhiretya sandhiṃ  
trikarmakṛttarati janmamṛtyū .  
brahmajajñaṃ devamīḍyaṃ viditvā  
nicāyyemām̐ śāntimatyantameti  1-1-17*

Whoever has performed this Nachiketa sacrifice three times, having been taught by the three, and has also carried out his three duties, passes beyond birth and death. Having known and realized this Fire born of Brahman, all-knowing, radiant, and worthy of worship, he reaches supreme peace.

### 1.1.18

त्रिणाचिकेतस्त्रयमेतद्विदित्वा  
य एवं विद्वाँश्चिनुते नाचिकेतम् ।  
स मृत्युपाशान् पुरतः प्रणोद्य  
शोकातिगो मोदते स्वर्गलोके ॥ १८॥

*triṇāciketastrayametadviditvā  
ya evaṃ vidvām̐ścinute nāciketam .  
sa mṛtyupāśān purataḥ praṇodya  
śokātigo modate svargaloke  1-1-18*

Whoever, knowing the three, has performed the Nachiketa sacrifice three times casts off the chains of death even here, overcomes grief, and rejoices in Heaven.

### 1.1.19

एष तेऽग्निर्नचिकेतः स्वर्ग्यो  
यमवृणीथा द्वितीयेन वरेण ।  
एतमग्निं तवैव प्रवक्ष्यन्ति जनासः  
तृतीयं वरं नचिकेतो वृणीष्व ॥ १९॥

*eṣa te'gnirnaciketaḥ svargyo  
yamavṛṇīthā dvitīyena vareṇa .  
etamagniṃ tavaiva pravakṣyanti janāsaḥ  
tṛtīyaṃ varaṃ naciketo vṛṇīṣva  1-1-19*

This, O Nachiketa, is your Fire-sacrifice that leads to Heaven, which you chose as your second boon. People will call this Fire by your name. Now, O Nachiketa, choose the third boon.

### 1.1.20

येयं प्रेते विचिकित्सा मनुष्ये-  
ऽस्तीत्येके नायमस्तीति चैके ।  
एतद्विद्यामनुशिष्टस्त्वयाऽहं  
वराणामेष वरस्तृतीयः ॥ २०॥

*yeyaṃ prete vicikitsā manuṣye-  
'stītyeke nāyamastīti caike .  
etadvidyāmanuśiṣṭastvayā'haṃ  
varāṇāmeṣa varastṛtīyaḥ  1-1-20*

Nachiketa said: There is this doubt concerning a person who has died. Some say he exists; others say he does not. I would like you to teach me about this. It is the third of my boons.

### 1.1.21

देवैरत्रापि विचिकित्सितं पुरा  
न हि सुविज्ञेयमणुरेष धर्मः ।  
अन्यं वरं नचिकेतो वृणीष्व  
मा मोपरोत्सीरति मा सृजैनम् ॥ २१॥

*devairatrāpi vicikitsitaṃ purā  
na hi suvijñeyamaṇureṣa dharmaḥ .  
anyaṃ varaṃ naciketo vṛṇīṣva  
mā moparotsīrati mā sṛjainam  1-1-21*

Yama said: Even the gods had doubts on this matter long ago. It is hard to understand, for the nature of Atman is subtle. Choose a different boon, O Nachiketa! Do not press me. Let me off from this one.

### 1.1.22

देवैरत्रापि विचिकित्सितं किल  
त्वं च मृत्यो यन्न सुज्ञेयमात्थ ।  
वक्ता चास्य त्वादृगन्यो न लभ्यो  
नान्यो वरस्तुल्य एतस्य कश्चित् ॥ २२॥

*devairatrāpi vicikitsitaṃ kila  
tvaṃ ca mṛtyo yanna sujñeyamāttha .  
vaktā cāsya tvādṛganyo na labhyo  
nānyo varastulya etasya kaścit  1-1-22*

Nachiketa said: O Death, even the gods have doubts on this matter, and you yourself say it is hard to understand. But no other teacher like you can be found, and surely no other boon can compare with this one.

### 1.1.23

शतायुषः पुत्रपौत्रान्वृणीष्वा  
बहून्पशून् हस्तिहिरण्यमश्वान् ।  
भूमेर्महदायतनं वृणीष्व  
स्वयं च जीव शरदो यावदिच्छसि ॥ २३॥

*śatāyuṣaḥ putrapautrānvṛṇīṣvā  
bahūnpaśūn hastihiraṇyamaśvān .  
bhūmermahadāyatanaṃ vṛṇīṣva  
svayaṃ ca jīva śarado yāvadicchasi  1-1-23*

Yama said: Choose sons and grandsons who will live a hundred years. Choose elephants, horses, herds of cattle, and gold. Choose a huge territory on earth, and live here for as many years as you wish.

### 1.1.24

एतत्तुल्यं यदि मन्यसे वरं  
वृणीष्व वित्तं चिरजीविकां च ।  
महाभूमौ नचिकेतस्त्वमेधि  
कामानां त्वा कामभाजं करोमि ॥ २४॥

*etattulyaṃ yadi manyase varaṃ  
vṛṇīṣva vittaṃ cirajīvikāṃ ca .  
mahābhūmau naciketastvamedhi  
kāmānāṃ tvā kāmabhājaṃ karomi  1-1-24*

If you consider any other boon equal to that one, choose it. Choose wealth and long life. Be king of the wide earth, O Nachiketa. I will make you one who enjoys every desire.

### 1.1.25

ये ये कामा दुर्लभा मर्त्यलोके  
सर्वान् कामाँश्छन्दतः प्रार्थयस्व ।  
इमा रामाः सरथाः सतूर्या  
न हीदृशा लम्भनीया मनुष्यैः ।  
आभिर्मत्प्रत्ताभिः परिचारयस्व  
नचिकेतो मरणं माऽनुप्राक्षीः ॥ २५॥

*ye ye kāmā durlabhā martyaloke  
sarvān kāmām̐śchandataḥ prārthayasva .  
imā rāmāḥ sarathāḥ satūryā  
na hīdṛśā lambhanīyā manuṣyaiḥ .  
ābhirmatprattābhiḥ paricārayasva  
naciketo maraṇaṃ mā'nuprākṣīḥ  1-1-25*

Whatever desires are hard to satisfy in this world of mortals, ask for them as you please. Here are lovely maidens with their chariots and musical instruments, such as men cannot obtain. I give them to you, and they will attend you. But do not question me about death.

### 1.1.26

श्वोभावा मर्त्यस्य यदन्तकैतत्  
सर्वेंद्रियाणां जरयंति तेजः ।  
अपि सर्वं जीवितमल्पमेव  
तवैव वाहास्तव नृत्यगीते ॥ २६॥

*śvobhāvā martyasya yadantakaitat  
sarveṃdriyāṇāṃ jarayaṃti tejaḥ .  
api sarvaṃ jīvitamalpameva  
tavaiva vāhāstava nṛtyagīte  1-1-26*

Nachiketa said: But these things last only until tomorrow, O Death. What is more, they wear out the vigor of all the senses. Even the longest life is short. Keep your horses, your dances, and your songs for yourself.

### 1.1.27

न वित्तेन तर्पणीयो मनुष्यो  
लप्स्यामहे वित्तमद्राक्ष्म चेत्त्वा ।  
जीविष्यामो यावदीशिष्यसि त्वं  
वरस्तु मे वरणीयः स एव ॥ २७॥

*na vittena tarpaṇīyo manuṣyo  
lapsyāmahe vittamadrākṣma cettvā .  
jīviṣyāmo yāvadīśiṣyasi tvaṃ  
varastu me varaṇīyaḥ sa eva  1-1-27*

No one can ever be made happy by wealth. Besides, now that I have seen you, I will surely obtain wealth, and I will also live as long as you rule. So I will accept no boon except the one I asked for.

### 1.1.28

अजीर्यताममृतानामुपेत्य  
जीर्यन्मर्त्यः क्वधःस्थः प्रजानन् ।  
अभिध्यायन् वर्णरतिप्रमोदान्  
अतिदीर्घे जीविते को रमेत ॥ २८॥

*ajīryatāmamṛtānāmupetya  
jīryanmartyaḥ kvadhaḥsthaḥ prajānan .  
abhidhyāyan varṇaratipramodān  
atidīrghe jīvite ko rameta  1-1-28*

Which of the decaying mortals here below, having come near the undecaying immortals and learned that they can fulfill his higher needs, would delight in an overlong life, once he has thought over the pleasures of beauty and song?

### 1.1.29

यस्मिन्निदं विचिकित्सन्ति मृत्यो  
यत्साम्पराये महति ब्रूहि नस्तत् ।  
योऽयं वरो गूढमनुप्रविष्टो  
नान्यं तस्मान्नचिकेता वृणीते ॥ २९॥  
॥ इति काठकोपनिषदि प्रथमाध्याये प्रथमा वल्ली ॥

*yasminnidaṃ vicikitsanti mṛtyo  
yatsāmparāye mahati brūhi nastat .  
yo'yaṃ varo gūḍhamanupraviṣṭo  
nānyaṃ tasmānnaciketā vṛṇīte  1-1-29  
iti kāṭhakopaniṣadi prathamādhyāye prathamā vallī*

O Death, tell me about that Great Hereafter which leaves people in doubt.

### 1.2.1

अन्यच्छ्रेयोऽन्यदुतैव प्रेय-  
स्ते उभे नानार्थे पुरुषँ सिनीतः ।  
तयोः श्रेय आददानस्य साधु  
भवति हीयतेऽर्थाद्य उ प्रेयो वृणीते ॥ १॥

*anyacchreyo'nyadutaiva preya-  
ste ubhe nānārthe puruṣam̐ sinītaḥ .  
tayoḥ śreya ādadānasya sādhu  
bhavati hīyate'rthādya u preyo vṛṇīte  1-2-1*

Yama said: The good is one thing and the pleasant is another. Both bind a person, each serving a different need. Whoever takes the good of the two fares well, but whoever chooses the pleasant misses the goal.

### 1.2.2

श्रेयश्च प्रेयश्च मनुष्यमेतः  
तौ सम्परीत्य विविनक्ति धीरः ।  
श्रेयो हि धीरोऽभि प्रेयसो वृणीते  
प्रेयो मन्दो योगक्षेमाद्वृणीते ॥ २॥

*śreyaśca preyaśca manuṣyametaḥ  
tau samparītya vivinakti dhīraḥ .  
śreyo hi dhīro'bhi preyaso vṛṇīte  
preyo mando yogakṣemādvṛṇīte  1-2-2*

The good and the pleasant both come before a person. The calm soul examines them closely and tells them apart. He prefers the good over the pleasant, while the fool, out of greed and avarice, picks the pleasant.

### 1.2.3

स त्वं प्रियान्प्रियरूपांश्च कामान्  
अभिध्यायन्नचिकेतोऽत्यस्राक्षीः ।  
नैतां सृङ्कां वित्तमयीमवाप्तो  
यस्यां मज्जन्ति बहवो मनुष्याः ॥ ३॥

*sa tvaṃ priyānpriyarūpāṃśca kāmān  
abhidhyāyannaciketo'tyasrākṣīḥ .  
naitāṃ sṛṅkāṃ vittamayīmavāpto  
yasyāṃ majjanti bahavo manuṣyāḥ  1-2-3*

O Nachiketa, having thought carefully about pleasures that are delightful or seem so, you have given them all up. You have not taken the road full of wealth, on which many people sink.

### 1.2.4

दूरमेते विपरीते विषूची  
अविद्या या च विद्येति ज्ञाता ।  
विद्याभीप्सिनं नचिकेतसं मन्ये  
न त्वा कामा बहवोऽलोलुपन्त ॥ ४॥

*dūramete viparīte viṣūcī  
avidyā yā ca vidyeti jñātā .  
vidyābhīpsinaṃ naciketasaṃ manye  
na tvā kāmā bahavo'lolupanta  1-2-4*

Ignorance and what is called Knowledge stand far apart and lead to different ends. I consider you, O Nachiketa, someone who wants Knowledge, since even many pleasures could not lure you away.

### 1.2.5

अविद्यायामन्तरे वर्तमानाः  
स्वयं धीराः पण्डितंमन्यमानाः ।  
दन्द्रम्यमाणाः परियन्ति मूढा  
अन्धेनैव नीयमाना यथान्धाः ॥ ५॥

*avidyāyāmantare vartamānāḥ  
svayaṃ dhīrāḥ paṇḍitaṃmanyamānāḥ .  
dandramyamāṇāḥ pariyanti mūḍhā  
andhenaiva nīyamānā yathāndhāḥ  1-2-5*

Fools living in darkness, yet taking themselves to be wise and learned, wander around and around on many crooked paths, like blind people led by the blind.

### 1.2.6

न साम्परायः प्रतिभाति बालं  
प्रमाद्यन्तं वित्तमोहेन मूढम् ।  
अयं लोको नास्ति पर इति मानी  
पुनः पुनर्वशमापद्यते मे ॥ ६॥

*na sāmparāyaḥ pratibhāti bālaṃ  
pramādyantaṃ vittamohena mūḍham .  
ayaṃ loko nāsti para iti mānī  
punaḥ punarvaśamāpadyate me  1-2-6*

The Hereafter never shows itself to someone who lacks discrimination, who is careless and confused by the delusion of wealth. 'Only this world exists,' he thinks, 'and there is no other.' Time after time he falls under my power.

### 1.2.7

श्रवणायापि बहुभिर्यो न लभ्यः  
शृण्वन्तोऽपि बहवो यं न विद्युः ।  
आश्चर्यो वक्ता कुशलोऽस्य लब्धा  
आश्चर्यो ज्ञाता कुशलानुशिष्टः ॥ ७॥

*śravaṇāyāpi bahubhiryo na labhyaḥ  
śṛṇvanto'pi bahavo yaṃ na vidyuḥ .  
āścaryo vaktā kuśalo'sya labdhā  
āścaryo jñātā kuśalānuśiṣṭaḥ  1-2-7*

Many never even hear of Atman, and many who do hear of Him do not understand. Wonderful is the one who explains Him, and rare the one who listens. Rare indeed is the person who experiences Atman when taught by a capable teacher.

### 1.2.8

न नरेणावरेण प्रोक्त एष  
सुविज्ञेयो बहुधा चिन्त्यमानः ।  
अनन्यप्रोक्ते गतिरत्र नास्ति  
अणीयान् ह्यतर्क्यमणुप्रमाणात् ॥ ८॥

*na nareṇāvareṇa prokta eṣa  
suvijñeyo bahudhā cintyamānaḥ .  
ananyaprokte gatiratra nāsti  
aṇīyān hyatarkyamaṇupramāṇāt  1-2-8*

Taught by a lesser person, Atman is not easily understood, because debaters regard It in many different ways. But when the teacher is one who has become one with Atman, no doubt about It can remain. Atman is finer than the finest and cannot be known through argument.

### 1.2.9

नैषा तर्केण मतिरापनेया  
प्रोक्तान्येनैव सुज्ञानाय प्रेष्ठ ।  
यां त्वमापः सत्यधृतिर्बतासि  
त्वादृङ्नो भूयान्नचिकेतः प्रष्टा ॥ ९॥

*naiṣā tarkeṇa matirāpaneyā  
proktānyenaiva sujñānāya preṣṭha .  
yāṃ tvamāpaḥ satyadhṛtirbatāsi  
tvādṛṅno bhūyānnaciketaḥ praṣṭā  1-2-9*

Reasoning cannot gain this Knowledge. When taught by another, dearest one, Atman becomes easy to understand. You have now gained this Knowledge. You are truly a person of real resolve. May we always have a questioner like you!

### 1.2.10

जानाम्यहं शेवधिरित्यनित्यं  
न ह्यध्रुवैः प्राप्यते हि ध्रुवं तत् ।  
ततो मया नाचिकेतश्चितोऽग्निः  
अनित्यैर्द्रव्यैः प्राप्तवानस्मि नित्यम् ॥ १०॥

*jānāmyahaṃ śevadhirityanityaṃ  
na hyadhruvaiḥ prāpyate hi dhruvaṃ tat .  
tato mayā nāciketaścito'gniḥ  
anityairdravyaiḥ prāptavānasmi nityam  1-2-10*

Yama said: I know that the treasure earned by action does not last, for what is eternal cannot be reached through what is not. Even so, I performed the Nachiketa sacrifice with impermanent things and reached this position, which is eternal only in a relative sense.

### 1.2.11

कामस्याप्तिं जगतः प्रतिष्ठां  
क्रतोरानन्त्यमभयस्य पारम् ।  
स्तोममहदुरुगायं प्रतिष्ठां दृष्ट्वा  
धृत्या धीरो नचिकेतोऽत्यस्राक्षीः ॥ ११॥

*kāmasyāptiṃ jagataḥ pratiṣṭhāṃ  
kratorānantyamabhayasya pāram .  
stomamahadurugāyaṃ pratiṣṭhāṃ dṛṣṭvā  
dhṛtyā dhīro naciketo'tyasrākṣīḥ  1-2-11*

You have seen the fulfillment of desires, the foundation of the universe, the rewards of sacrifices, the shore where fear does not exist, what is great and worthy of worship, the wide abode, and the goal. Being wise, you have firmly set it all aside.

### 1.2.12

तं दुर्दर्शं गूढमनुप्रविष्टं  
गुहाहितं गह्वरेष्ठं पुराणम् ।  
अध्यात्मयोगाधिगमेन देवं  
मत्वा धीरो हर्षशोकौ जहाति ॥ १२॥

*taṃ durdarśaṃ gūḍhamanupraviṣṭaṃ  
guhāhitaṃ gahvareṣṭhaṃ purāṇam .  
adhyātmayogādhigamena devaṃ  
matvā dhīro harṣaśokau jahāti  1-2-12*

The ancient, radiant One is hard to see, unmanifest, and hidden; It dwells in the buddhi and rests in the body. A wise person who realizes It through concentration on the Self leaves joy and sorrow far behind.

### 1.2.13

एतच्छ्रुत्वा सम्परिगृह्य मर्त्यः  
प्रवृह्य धर्म्यमणुमेतमाप्य ।  
स मोदते मोदनीयँ हि लब्ध्वा  
विवृतँ सद्म नचिकेतसं मन्ये ॥ १३॥

*etacchrutvā samparigṛhya martyaḥ  
pravṛhya dharmyamaṇumetamāpya .  
sa modate modanīyam̐ hi labdhvā  
vivṛtam̐ sadma naciketasaṃ manye  1-2-13*

A mortal who has heard this and understood it well, who has separated that Atman, the very soul of dharma, from all physical objects and realized the subtle essence, rejoices, for he has gained what is the cause of rejoicing. The Abode of Brahman, I believe, lies open for Nachiketa.

### 1.2.14

अन्यत्र धर्मादन्यत्राधर्मा-  
दन्यत्रास्मात्कृताकृतात् ।  
अन्यत्र भूताच्च भव्याच्च  
यत्तत्पश्यसि तद्वद ॥ १४॥

*anyatra dharmādanyatrādharmā-  
danyatrāsmātkṛtākṛtāt .  
anyatra bhūtācca bhavyācca  
yattatpaśyasi tadvada  1-2-14*

Nachiketa said: Tell me about That which you see as distinct from righteousness and unrighteousness, distinct from all this cause and effect, distinct from what has been and what will be.

### 1.2.15

सर्वे वेदा यत्पदमामनन्ति  
तपाꣳसि सर्वाणि च यद्वदन्ति ।  
यदिच्छन्तो ब्रह्मचर्यं चरन्ति  
तत्ते पदꣳ संग्रहेण ब्रवीम्योमित्येतत् ॥ १५॥

*sarve vedā yatpadamāmananti  
tapāgͫsi sarvāṇi ca yadvadanti .  
yadicchanto brahmacaryaṃ caranti  
tatte padagͫ saṃgraheṇa bravīmyomityetat  1-2-15*

Yama said: I will tell you briefly of the goal proclaimed by all the Vedas, the aim of all austerities, and what people long for when they live in continence. It is Om.

### 1.2.16

एतद्ध्येवाक्षरं ब्रह्म एतद्ध्येवाक्षरं परम् ।  
एतद्ध्येवाक्षरं ज्ञात्वा यो यदिच्छति तस्य तत् ॥ १६॥

*etaddhyevākṣaraṃ brahma etaddhyevākṣaraṃ param .  
etaddhyevākṣaraṃ jñātvā yo yadicchati tasya tat  1-2-16*

This syllable Om is truly Brahman. This syllable is the Highest. Anyone who knows this syllable gains whatever he desires.

### 1.2.17

एतदालम्बनँ श्रेष्ठमेतदालम्बनं परम् ।  
एतदालम्बनं ज्ञात्वा ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ १७॥

*etadālambanam̐ śreṣṭhametadālambanaṃ param .  
etadālambanaṃ jñātvā brahmaloke mahīyate  1-2-17*

This is the finest support; this is the highest support. Anyone who knows this support is honored in the world of Brahma.

### 1.2.18

न जायते म्रियते वा विपश्चिन्  
नायं कुतश्चिन्न बभूव कश्चित् ।  
अजो नित्यः शाश्वतोऽयं पुराणो  
न हन्यते हन्यमाने शरीरे ॥ १८॥

*na jāyate mriyate vā vipaścin  
nāyaṃ kutaścinna babhūva kaścit .  
ajo nityaḥ śāśvato'yaṃ purāṇo  
na hanyate hanyamāne śarīre  1-2-18*

The knowing Self is neither born nor does It die. It did not arise from anything, and nothing arose from It. Unborn, eternal, everlasting, and ancient, It is not killed when the body is killed.

### 1.2.19

हन्ता चेन्मन्यते हन्तुँ हतश्चेन्मन्यते हतम् ।  
उभौ तौ न विजानीतो नायँ हन्ति न हन्यते ॥ १९॥

*hantā cenmanyate hantum̐ hataścenmanyate hatam .  
ubhau tau na vijānīto nāyam̐ hanti na hanyate  1-2-19*

If the killer believes he kills, and the slain believes he is slain, neither of them understands correctly. The Self does not kill and is not killed.

### 1.2.20

अणोरणीयान्महतो महीया-  
नात्माऽस्य जन्तोर्निहितो गुहायाम् ।  
तमक्रतुः पश्यति वीतशोको  
धातुप्रसादान्महिमानमात्मनः ॥ २०॥

*aṇoraṇīyānmahato mahīyā-  
nātmā'sya jantornihito guhāyām .  
tamakratuḥ paśyati vītaśoko  
dhātuprasādānmahimānamātmanaḥ  1-2-20*

Atman, tinier than the tiny and vaster than the vast, lies hidden in the hearts of all living beings. A person without desires, through tranquility of the senses and mind, sees the greatness of the Self and is released from grief.

### 1.2.21

आसीनो दूरं व्रजति शयानो याति सर्वतः ।  
कस्तं मदामदं देवं मदन्यो ज्ञातुमर्हति ॥ २१॥

*āsīno dūraṃ vrajati śayāno yāti sarvataḥ .  
kastaṃ madāmadaṃ devaṃ madanyo jñātumarhati  1-2-21*

Sitting still, It journeys far; lying down, It goes everywhere. Who but I can know that radiant Atman, who rejoices and does not rejoice?

### 1.2.22

अशरीरँ शरीरेष्वनवस्थेष्ववस्थितम् ।  
महान्तं विभुमात्मानं मत्वा धीरो न शोचति ॥ २२॥

*aśarīram̐ śarīreṣvanavastheṣvavasthitam .  
mahāntaṃ vibhumātmānaṃ matvā dhīro na śocati  1-2-22*

Having realized Atman as living within impermanent bodies, yet Itself bodiless, vast, and all-pervading, the wise person does not grieve.

### 1.2.23

नायमात्मा प्रवचनेन लभ्यो  
न मेधया न बहुना श्रुतेन ।  
यमेवैष वृणुते तेन लभ्यः  
तस्यैष आत्मा विवृणुते तनूꣳ स्वाम् ॥ २३॥

*nāyamātmā pravacanena labhyo  
na medhayā na bahunā śrutena .  
yamevaiṣa vṛṇute tena labhyaḥ  
tasyaiṣa ātmā vivṛṇute tanūgͫ svām  1-2-23*

This Atman cannot be reached through study of the Vedas, through intelligence, or through much listening to sacred books. It is reached only by the one It chooses. To that person Atman shows Its own form.

### 1.2.24

नाविरतो दुश्चरितान्नाशान्तो नासमाहितः ।  
नाशान्तमानसो वाऽपि प्रज्ञानेनैनमाप्नुयात् ॥ २४॥

*nāvirato duścaritānnāśānto nāsamāhitaḥ .  
nāśāntamānaso vā'pi prajñānenainamāpnuyāt  1-2-24*

Someone who has not first turned from wickedness, who is not tranquil and restrained, and whose mind is not at peace cannot reach Atman. It is realized only through Knowledge of Reality.

### 1.2.25

यस्य ब्रह्म च क्षत्रं च उभे भवत ओदनः ।  
मृत्युर्यस्योपसेचनं क इत्था वेद यत्र सः ॥ २५॥  
इति काठकोपनिषदि प्रथमाध्याये द्वितीया वल्ली ॥

*yasya brahma ca kṣatraṃ ca ubhe bhavata odanaḥ .  
mṛtyuryasyopasecanaṃ ka itthā veda yatra saḥ 1-2-25  
iti kāṭhakopaniṣadi prathamādhyāye dvitīyā vallī*

Who, then, knows where He is, He for whom Brahmins and kshattriyas are mere food and death itself a seasoning?

### 1.3.1

ऋतं पिबन्तौ सुकृतस्य लोके  
गुहां प्रविष्टौ परमे परार्धे ।  
छायातपौ ब्रह्मविदो वदन्ति  
पञ्चाग्नयो ये च त्रिणाचिकेताः ॥ १॥

*ṛtaṃ pibantau sukṛtasya loke  
guhāṃ praviṣṭau parame parārdhe .  
chāyātapau brahmavido vadanti  
pañcāgnayo ye ca triṇāciketāḥ  1-3-1*

Two dwell within the body, in the intellect, the supreme akasa of the heart, enjoying the certain results of their own actions. The knowers of Brahman describe them as light and shade, and so do householders who have made offerings in the Five Fires and those who have performed the Nachiketa sacrifice three times.

### 1.3.2

यः सेतुरीजानानामक्षरं ब्रह्म यत् परम् ।  
अभयं तितीर्षतां पारं नाचिकेतँ शकेमहि ॥ २॥

*yaḥ seturījānānāmakṣaraṃ brahma yat param .  
abhayaṃ titīrṣatāṃ pāraṃ nāciketam̐ śakemahi  1-3-2*

We know how to perform the Nachiketa sacrifice, the bridge for those who sacrifice. We also know that supreme, imperishable Brahman, sought by those who want to cross to the shore where there is no fear.

### 1.3.3

आत्मानँ रथिनं विद्धि शरीरँ रथमेव तु ।  
बुद्धिं तु सारथिं विद्धि मनः प्रग्रहमेव च ॥ ३॥

*ātmānam̐ rathinaṃ viddhi śarīram̐ rathameva tu .  
buddhiṃ tu sārathiṃ viddhi manaḥ pragrahameva ca  1-3-3*

Understand the atman as the owner of the chariot, the body as the chariot, the intellect as the charioteer, and the mind as the reins.

### 1.3.4

इन्द्रियाणि हयानाहुर्विषयाँ स्तेषु गोचरान् ।  
आत्मेन्द्रियमनोयुक्तं भोक्तेत्याहुर्मनीषिणः ॥ ४॥

*indriyāṇi hayānāhurviṣayām̐ steṣu gocarān .  
ātmendriyamanoyuktaṃ bhoktetyāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ  1-3-4*

The senses, it is said, are the horses, and the objects are the roads. The atman joined with the body, senses, and mind is what the wise call the enjoyer.

### 1.3.5

यस्त्वविज्ञानवान्भवत्ययुक्तेन मनसा सदा ।  
तस्येन्द्रियाण्यवश्यानि दुष्टाश्वा इव सारथेः ॥ ५॥

*yastvavijñānavānbhavatyayuktena manasā sadā .  
tasyendriyāṇyavaśyāni duṣṭāśvā iva sāratheḥ  1-3-5*

When the buddhi, tied to a mind that is forever distracted, loses its discrimination, the senses run out of control, like a charioteer's vicious horses.

### 1.3.6

यस्तु विज्ञानवान्भवति युक्तेन मनसा सदा ।  
तस्येन्द्रियाणि वश्यानि सदश्वा इव सारथेः ॥ ६॥

*yastu vijñānavānbhavati yuktena manasā sadā .  
tasyendriyāṇi vaśyāni sadaśvā iva sāratheḥ  1-3-6*

But when the buddhi is joined to a mind that is always held in check and has discrimination, the senses stay under control, like a charioteer's good horses.

### 1.3.7

यस्त्वविज्ञानवान्भवत्यमनस्कः सदाऽशुचिः ।  
न स तत्पदमाप्नोति संसारं चाधिगच्छति ॥ ७॥

*yastvavijñānavānbhavatyamanaskaḥ sadā'śuciḥ .  
na sa tatpadamāpnoti saṃsāraṃ cādhigacchati  1-3-7*

When the buddhi is joined to a scattered mind, it loses discrimination and stays impure. The embodied soul then never reaches the goal, and instead enters the round of births.

### 1.3.8

यस्तु विज्ञानवान्भवति समनस्कः सदा शुचिः ।  
स तु तत्पदमाप्नोति यस्माद्भूयो न जायते ॥ ८॥

*yastu vijñānavānbhavati samanaskaḥ sadā śuciḥ .  
sa tu tatpadamāpnoti yasmādbhūyo na jāyate  1-3-8*

But when the buddhi is joined to a restrained mind, it has discrimination and stays pure. The embodied soul then reaches the goal from which there is no further birth.

### 1.3.9

विज्ञानसारथिर्यस्तु मनः प्रग्रहवान्नरः ।  
सोऽध्वनः पारमाप्नोति तद्विष्णोः परमं पदम् ॥ ९॥

*vijñānasārathiryastu manaḥ pragrahavānnaraḥ .  
so'dhvanaḥ pāramāpnoti tadviṣṇoḥ paramaṃ padam  1-3-9*

A person who has discrimination as his charioteer and keeps a firm grip on the reins of the mind comes to the end of the road, which is the supreme position of Vishnu.

### 1.3.10

इन्द्रियेभ्यः परा ह्यर्था अर्थेभ्यश्च परं मनः ।  
मनसस्तु परा बुद्धिर्बुद्धेरात्मा महान्परः ॥ १०॥

*indriyebhyaḥ parā hyarthā arthebhyaśca paraṃ manaḥ .  
manasastu parā buddhirbuddherātmā mahānparaḥ  1-3-10*

Past the senses lie the rudiments of their objects. Past those rudiments lies the mind. Past the mind lies the atman known as Mahat (great).

### 1.3.11

महतः परमव्यक्तमव्यक्तात्पुरुषः परः ।  
पुरुषान्न परं किंचित्सा काष्ठा सा परा गतिः ॥ ११॥

*mahataḥ paramavyaktamavyaktātpuruṣaḥ paraḥ .  
puruṣānna paraṃ kiṃcitsā kāṣṭhā sā parā gatiḥ  1-3-11*

Past the great Ātman lies the Unmanifested. Past the Unmanifested lies the Puruṣa (the Cosmic Soul). Past the Puruṣa there is nothing. That is the end, the final goal.

### 1.3.12

एष सर्वेषु भूतेषु गूढोऽऽत्मा न प्रकाशते ।  
दृश्यते त्वग्र्यया बुद्ध्या सूक्ष्मया सूक्ष्मदर्शिभिः ॥ १२॥

*eṣa sarveṣu bhūteṣu gūḍho''tmā na prakāśate .  
dṛśyate tvagryayā buddhyā sūkṣmayā sūkṣmadarśibhiḥ  1-3-12*

That Self lies hidden in all beings and does not shine out, but subtle seers perceive It with their one-pointed, subtle intellects.

### 1.3.13

यच्छेद्वाङ्मनसी प्राज्ञस्तद्यच्छेज्ज्ञान आत्मनि ।  
ज्ञानमात्मनि महति नियच्छेत्तद्यच्छेच्छान्त आत्मनि ॥ १३॥

*yacchedvāṅmanasī prājñastadyacchejjñāna ātmani .  
jñānamātmani mahati niyacchettadyacchecchānta ātmani  1-3-13*

The wise person should dissolve speech into the mind and the mind into the intellect, then dissolve the intellect into the Cosmic Mind and the Cosmic Mind into the Tranquil Self.

### 1.3.14

उत्तिष्ठत जाग्रत  
प्राप्य वरान्निबोधत ।  
क्षुरस्य धारा निशिता दुरत्यया  
दुर्गं पथस्तत्कवयो वदन्ति ॥ १४॥

*uttiṣṭhata jāgrata  
prāpya varānnibodhata .  
kṣurasya dhārā niśitā duratyayā  
durgaṃ pathastatkavayo vadanti  1-3-14*

Get up! Wake up! Go to the great ones and learn. The wise say that this path is like the sharp edge of a razor, hard to walk and difficult to cross.

### 1.3.15

अशब्दमस्पर्शमरूपमव्ययं  
तथाऽरसं नित्यमगन्धवच्च यत् ।  
अनाद्यनन्तं महतः परं ध्रुवं  
निचाय्य तन्मृत्युमुखात् प्रमुच्यते ॥ १५॥

*aśabdam asparśam arūpamavyayaṃ  
tathā'rasaṃ nityamagandhavacca yat .  
anādyanantaṃ mahataḥ paraṃ dhruvaṃ  
nicāyya tanmṛtyumukhāt pramucyate  1-3-15*

Atman has no sound, no touch, no form, no decay, no taste, and no smell, and It is eternal. When one has realized That, which has no beginning or end, lies beyond the Great, and does not change, one is released from the jaws of death.

### 1.3.16

नाचिकेतमुपाख्यानं मृत्युप्रोक्तँ सनातनम् ।  
उक्त्वा श्रुत्वा च मेधावी ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ १६॥

*nāciketamupākhyānaṃ mṛtyuproktam̐ sanātanam .  
uktvā śrutvā ca medhāvī brahmaloke mahīyate  1-3-16*

The wise person who has heard and retold the eternal story of Nachiketa, as Death told it, is honored in the world of Brahman.

### 1.3.17

य इमं परमं गुह्यं श्रावयेद् ब्रह्मसंसदि ।  
प्रयतः श्राद्धकाले वा तदानन्त्याय कल्पते ।  
तदानन्त्याय कल्पत इति ॥ १७॥  
इति काठकोपनिषदि प्रथमाध्याये तृतीया वल्ली ॥

*ya imaṃ paramaṃ guhyaṃ śrāvayed brahmasaṃsadi .  
prayataḥ śrāddhakāle vā tadānantyāya kalpate .  
tadānantyāya kalpata iti  1-3-17  
iti kāṭhakopaniṣadi prathamādhyāye tṛtīyā vallī*

Whoever practices self-control and recites this supreme secret in a gathering of Brahmins or at an after-death ceremony gains infinite rewards from it. Indeed, he gains infinite rewards.

### 2.1.1

पराञ्चि खानि व्यतृणत् स्वयम्भू-  
स्तस्मात्पराङ्पश्यति नान्तरात्मन् ।  
कश्चिद्धीरः प्रत्यगात्मानमैक्ष-  
दावृत्तचक्षुरमृतत्वमिच्छन् ॥ १॥

*parāñci khāni vyatṛṇat svayambhū-  
stasmātparāṅpaśyati nāntarātman .  
kaściddhīraḥ pratyagātmānamaikṣa-  
dāvṛttacakṣuramṛtatvamicchan  2-1-1*

Yama said: The self-existent Supreme Lord damaged the sense-organs by creating them to turn outward. So with them a person perceives only outer objects and not the inner Self. But a calm person who longs for Immortality closes his eyes and sees the inner Self.

### 2.1.2

पराचः कामाननुयन्ति बाला-  
स्ते मृत्योर्यन्ति विततस्य पाशम् ।  
अथ धीरा अमृतत्वं विदित्वा  
ध्रुवमध्रुवेष्विह न प्रार्थयन्ते ॥ २॥

*parācaḥ kāmānanuyanti bālā-  
ste mṛtyoryanti vitatasya pāśam .  
atha dhīrā amṛtatvaṃ viditvā  
dhruvamadhruveṣviha na prārthayante  2-1-2*

Children run after outer pleasures and fall into the wide net of death. Calm souls, knowing what unshakable Immortality is, want nothing uncertain in this world.

### 2.1.3

येन रूपं रसं गन्धं शब्दान् स्पर्शाꣳश्च मैथुनान् ।  
एतेनैव विजानाति किमत्र परिशिष्यते । एतद्वै तत् ॥ ३॥

*yena rūpaṃ rasaṃ gandhaṃ śabdān sparśāgͫśca maithunān .  
etenaiva vijānāti kimatra pariśiṣyate . etadvai tat  2-1-3*

Through Atman one knows form, taste, smell, sounds, touches, and carnal pleasures. What is there that Atman does not know? This, truly, is That.

### 2.1.4

स्वप्नान्तं जागरितान्तं चोभौ येनानुपश्यति ।  
महान्तं विभुमात्मानं मत्वा धीरो न शोचति ॥ ४॥

*svapnāntaṃ jāgaritāntaṃ cobhau yenānupaśyati .  
mahāntaṃ vibhumātmānaṃ matvā dhīro na śocati  2-1-4*

Through Atman one perceives all objects in sleep and in waking. Having realized the vast, all-pervading Atman, the calm soul grieves no more.

### 2.1.5

य इमं मध्वदं वेद आत्मानं जीवमन्तिकात् ।  
ईशानं भूतभव्यस्य न ततो विजुगुप्सते । एतद्वै तत् ॥ ५॥

*ya imaṃ madhvadaṃ veda ātmānaṃ jīvamantikāt .  
īśānaṃ bhūtabhavyasya na tato vijugupsate . etadvai tat  2-1-5*

Whoever knows the individual soul, the one who experiences the fruits of action, as Atman, ever near and Lord of past and future, will not hide himself from others. This, truly, is That.

### 2.1.6

यः पूर्वं तपसो जातमद्भ्यः पूर्वमजायत ।  
गुहां प्रविश्य तिष्ठन्तं यो भूतेभिर्व्यपश्यत । एतद्वै तत् ॥ ६॥

*yaḥ pūrvaṃ tapaso jātamadbhyaḥ pūrvamajāyata .  
guhāṃ praviśya tiṣṭhantaṃ yo bhūtebhirvyapaśyata . etadvai tat  2-1-6*

That person truly knows Brahman who knows the First-born, the child of austerity, made before the waters and living with the elements in the cave of the heart. This, truly, is That.

### 2.1.7

या प्राणेन संभवत्यदितिर्देवतामयी ।  
गुहां प्रविश्य तिष्ठन्तीं या भूतेभिर्व्यजायत । एतद्वै तत् ॥ ७॥

*yā prāṇena saṃbhavatyaditirdevatāmayī .  
guhāṃ praviśya tiṣṭhantīṃ yā bhūtebhirvyajāyata . etadvai tat  2-1-7*

That person truly knows Brahman who knows Aditi, the soul of all deities, born in the form of Prana, created with the elements, and dwelling in the heart once she has entered it. This, truly, is That.

### 2.1.8

अरण्योर्निहितो जातवेदा गर्भ इव सुभृतो गर्भिणीभिः ।  
दिवे दिवे ईड्यो जागृवद्भिर्हविष्मद्भिर्मनुष्येभिरग्निः । एतद्वै तत् ॥ ८॥

*araṇyornihito jātavedā garbha iva subhṛto garbhiṇībhiḥ .  
dive dive īḍyo jāgṛvadbhirhaviṣmadbhirmanuṣyebhiragniḥ . etadvai tat  2-1-8*

Agni lies hidden in the two fire-sticks, well protected, like a child in its mother's womb. People who are awake and those who make offerings in the sacrifices worship him day after day. This, truly, is That.

### 2.1.9

यतश्चोदेति सूर्योऽस्तं यत्र च गच्छति ।  
तं देवाः सर्वेऽर्पितास्तदु नात्येति कश्चन । एतद्वै तत् ॥ ९॥

*yataścodeti sūryo'staṃ yatra ca gacchati .  
taṃ devāḥ sarve'rpitāstadu nātyeti kaścana . etadvai tat  2-1-9*

That from which the sun rises and into which it sets, that which holds all the devas and which no one can ever go beyond: This, truly, is That.

### 2.1.10

यदेवेह तदमुत्र यदमुत्र तदन्विह ।  
मृत्योः स मृत्युमाप्नोति य इह नानेव पश्यति ॥ १०॥

*yadeveha tadamutra yadamutra tadanviha .  
mṛtyoḥ sa mṛtyumāpnoti ya iha nāneva paśyati  2-1-10*

What is here is also there, and what is there is also here. Whoever sees any difference here goes from death to death.

### 2.1.11

मनसैवेदमाप्तव्यं नेह नानाऽस्ति किंचन ।  
मृत्योः स मृत्युं गच्छति य इह नानेव पश्यति ॥ ११॥

*manasaivedamāptavyaṃ neha nānā'sti kiṃcana .  
mṛtyoḥ sa mṛtyuṃ gacchati ya iha nāneva paśyati  2-1-11*

Brahman can be realized through the mind alone, and then one sees no multiplicity in It at all. Whoever sees multiplicity in It goes from death to death. This, truly, is That.

### 2.1.12

अङ्गुष्ठमात्रः पुरुषो मध्य आत्मनि तिष्ठति ।  
ईशानं भूतभव्यस्य न ततो विजुगुप्सते । एतद्वै तत् ॥ १२॥

*aṅguṣṭhamātraḥ puruṣo madhya ātmani tiṣṭhati .  
īśānaṃ bhūtabhavyasya na tato vijugupsate . etadvai tat  2-1-12*

The Purusha, the size of a thumb, lives in the body. He is Lord of the past and the future. Once one knows Him, one no longer hides oneself. This, truly, is That.

### 2.1.13

अङ्गुष्ठमात्रः पुरुषो ज्योतिरिवाधूमकः ।  
ईशानो भूतभव्यस्य स एवाद्य स उ श्वः । एतद्वै तत् ॥ १३॥

*aṅguṣṭhamātraḥ puruṣo jyotirivādhūmakaḥ .  
īśāno bhūtabhavyasya sa evādya sa u śvaḥ . etadvai tat  2-1-13*

The Purusha, the size of a thumb, is like a smokeless flame. Lord of the past and the future, He is the same today and tomorrow. This, truly, is That.

### 2.1.14

यथोदकं दुर्गे वृष्टं पर्वतेषु विधावति ।  
एवं धर्मान् पृथक् पश्यंस्तानेवानुविधावति ॥ १४॥

*yathodakaṃ durge vṛṣṭaṃ parvateṣu vidhāvati .  
evaṃ dharmān pṛthak paśyaṃstānevānuvidhāvati  2-1-14*

Rain falling on a mountain peak runs down the rocks in every direction. In the same way, whoever sees the attributes as different from Brahman runs after them in every direction.

### 2.1.15

यथोदकं शुद्धे शुद्धमासिक्तं तादृगेव भवति ।  
एवं मुनेर्विजानत आत्मा भवति गौतम ॥ १५॥  
इति काठकोपनिषदि द्वितीयाध्याये प्रथमा वल्ली ॥

*yathodakaṃ śuddhe śuddhamāsiktaṃ tādṛgeva bhavati .  
evaṃ munervijānata ātmā bhavati gautama  2-1-15  
iti kāṭhakopaniṣadi dvitīyādhyāye prathamā vallī*

Pure water poured into pure water becomes one with it. So too, O Gautama, is the Self of the sage who knows.

### 2.2.1

पुरमेकादशद्वारमजस्यावक्रचेतसः ।  
अनुष्ठाय न शोचति विमुक्तश्च विमुच्यते । एतद्वै तत् ॥ १॥

*puramekādaśa dvāramajasyāvakracetasaḥ .  
anuṣṭhāya na śocati vimuktaśca vimucyate . etadvai tat  2-2-1*

The unborn Atman of undistorted Consciousness owns a city with eleven gates. Whoever meditates on Him grieves no longer; released from the bonds of ignorance, that person becomes free. This, truly, is That.

### 2.2.2

हँसः शुचिषद्वसुरान्तरिक्षसद्-  
होता वेदिषदतिथिर्दुरोणसत् ।  
नृषद्वरसदृतसद्व्योमसद्  
अब्जा गोजा ऋतजा अद्रिजा ऋतं बृहत् ॥ २॥

*ham̐saḥ śuciṣadvasurāntarikṣasad-  
hotā vediṣadatithirduroṇasat .  
nṛṣadvarasadṛtasadvyomasad  
abjā gojā ṛtajā adrijā ṛtaṃ bṛhat  2-2-2*

He is the sun that lives in the bright heavens, the air in the space between, the fire on earth, the guest in the house. He lives in human beings, in the gods, in truth, and in the sky. He is born in water, on earth, in the sacrifice, and on the mountains. He is the True and the Great.

### 2.2.3

ऊर्ध्वं प्राणमुन्नयत्यपानं प्रत्यगस्यति ।  
मध्ये वामनमासीनं विश्वे देवा उपासते ॥ ३॥

*ūrdhvaṃ prāṇamunnayatyapānaṃ pratyagasyati .  
madhye vāmanamāsīnaṃ viśve devā upāsate  2-2-3*

It is He who sends prana upward and draws apana downward. All the devas worship that adorable One seated in the middle.

### 2.2.4

अस्य विस्रंसमानस्य शरीरस्थस्य देहिनः ।  
देहाद्विमुच्यमानस्य किमत्र परिशिष्यते । एतद्वै तत् ॥ ४॥

*asya visraṃsamānasya śarīrasthasya dehinaḥ .  
dehādvimucyamānasya kimatra pariśiṣyate . etadvai tat  2-2-4*

When the soul that lives in the body and identifies with it is torn from the body and set free from it, what is left? This, truly, is That?

### 2.2.5

न प्राणेन नापानेन मर्त्यो जीवति कश्चन ।  
इतरेण तु जीवन्ति यस्मिन्नेतावुपाश्रितौ ॥ ५॥

*na prāṇena nāpānena martyo jīvati kaścana .  
itareṇa tu jīvanti yasminnetāvupāśritau  2-2-5*

No mortal lives by prana, which rises, or by apana, which descends. People live by something else, on which these two depend.

### 2.2.6

हन्त त इदं प्रवक्ष्यामि गुह्यं ब्रह्म सनातनम् ।  
यथा च मरणं प्राप्य आत्मा भवति गौतम ॥ ६॥

*hanta ta idaṃ pravakṣyāmi guhyaṃ brahma sanātanam .  
yathā ca maraṇaṃ prāpya ātmā bhavati gautama  2-2-6*

Well then, Gautama, I will tell you about this deep and eternal Brahman, and also what becomes of the atman after it meets death.

### 2.2.7

योनिमन्ये प्रपद्यन्ते शरीरत्वाय देहिनः ।  
स्थाणुमन्येऽनुसंयन्ति यथाकर्म यथाश्रुतम् ॥ ७॥

*yonimanye prapadyante śarīratvāya dehinaḥ .  
sthāṇumanye'nusaṃyanti yathākarma yathāśrutam  2-2-7*

Depending on their work and their knowledge, some jivas enter the womb to take form as organic beings, and some go into non-organic matter.

### 2.2.8

य एष सुप्तेषु जागर्ति कामं कामं पुरुषो निर्मिमाणः ।  
तदेव शुक्रं तद्ब्रह्म तदेवामृतमुच्यते ।  
तस्मिँल्लोकाः श्रिताः सर्वे तदु नात्येति कश्चन । एतद्वै तत् ॥ ८॥

*ya eṣa supteṣu jāgarti kāmaṃ kāmaṃ puruṣo nirmimāṇaḥ .  
tadeva śukraṃ tadbrahma tadevāmṛtamucyate .  
tasmim̐llokāḥ śritāḥ sarve tadu nātyeti kaścana . etadvai tat  2-2-8*

The Purusha who stays awake while the sense-organs sleep, fashioning one lovely form after another, is the Pure. That is Brahman, and That alone is called the Immortal. All worlds are held in Him, and none can go beyond. This, truly, is That.

### 2.2.9

अग्निर्यथैको भुवनं प्रविष्टो  
रूपं रूपं प्रतिरूपो बभूव ।  
एकस्तथा सर्वभूतान्तरात्मा  
रूपं रूपं प्रतिरूपो बहिश्च ॥ ९॥

*agniryathaiko bhuvanaṃ praviṣṭo  
rūpaṃ rūpaṃ pratirūpo babhūva .  
ekastathā sarvabhūtāntarātmā  
rūpaṃ rūpaṃ pratirūpo bahiśca  2-2-9*

The one non-dual fire, once it has entered the world, takes on different forms according to what it burns. So too the one non-dual Atman, living in all beings, takes on different forms according to what It enters. And It also exists outside them.

### 2.2.10

वायुर्यथैको भुवनं प्रविष्टो  
रूपं रूपं प्रतिरूपो बभूव ।  
एकस्तथा सर्वभूतान्तरात्मा  
रूपं रूपं प्रतिरूपो बहिश्च ॥ १०॥

*vāyuryathaiko bhuvanaṃ praviṣṭo  
rūpaṃ rūpaṃ pratirūpo babhūva .  
ekastathā sarvabhūtāntarātmā  
rūpaṃ rūpaṃ pratirūpo bahiśca  2-2-10*

The one non-dual air, once it has entered the world, takes on different forms according to what it enters. So too the one non-dual Atman, living in all beings, takes on different forms according to what It enters. And It also exists outside them.

### 2.2.11

सूर्यो यथा सर्वलोकस्य चक्षुः  
न लिप्यते चाक्षुषैर्बाह्यदोषैः ।  
एकस्तथा सर्वभूतान्तरात्मा  
न लिप्यते लोकदुःखेन बाह्यः ॥ ११॥

*sūryo yathā sarvalokasya cakṣuḥ  
na lipyate cākṣuṣairbāhyadoṣaiḥ .  
ekastathā sarvabhūtāntarātmā  
na lipyate lokaduḥkhena bāhyaḥ  2-2-11*

The sun helps every eye to see, yet it is untouched by flaws in the eyes or in the outer things it reveals. In the same way the one Atman, living in all beings, is never stained by the world's misery, since It stands outside it.

### 2.2.12

एको वशी सर्वभूतान्तरात्मा  
एकं रूपं बहुधा यः करोति ।  
तमात्मस्थं येऽनुपश्यन्ति धीराः  
तेषां सुखं शाश्वतं नेतरेषाम् ॥ १२॥

*eko vaśī sarvabhūtāntarātmā  
ekaṃ rūpaṃ bahudhā yaḥ karoti .  
tamātmasthaṃ ye'nupaśyanti dhīrāḥ  
teṣāṃ sukhaṃ śāśvataṃ netareṣām  2-2-12*

There is one Supreme Ruler, the innermost Self of all beings, who turns His one form into many. Lasting happiness belongs to the wise who perceive Him within themselves, and to no one else.

### 2.2.13

नित्योऽनित्यानां चेतनश्चेतनानाम्  
एको बहूनां यो विदधाति कामान् ।  
तमात्मस्थं येऽनुपश्यन्ति धीराः  
तेषां शान्तिः शाश्वती नेतरेषाम् ॥ १३॥

*nityo'nityānāṃ cetanaścetanānām  
eko bahūnāṃ yo vidadhāti kāmān .  
tamātmasthaṃ ye'nupaśyanti dhīrāḥ  
teṣāṃ śāntiḥ śāśvatī netareṣām  2-2-13*

There is One who is the eternal Reality among passing things and the one truly conscious Entity among conscious things, and who, though non-dual, fulfills the desires of many. Lasting peace belongs to the wise who perceive Him within themselves, and to no one else.

### 2.2.14

तदेतदिति मन्यन्तेऽनिर्देश्यं परमं सुखम् ।  
कथं नु तद्विजानीयां किमु भाति विभाति वा ॥ १४॥

*tadetaditi manyante'nirdeśyaṃ paramaṃ sukham .  
kathaṃ nu tadvijānīyāṃ kimu bhāti vibhāti vā  2-2-14*

"This is That": so the sages realize that indescribable Supreme Joy. How may I come to realize It? Does It shine by Its own light? Does It shine brightly, or does It not?

### 2.2.15

न तत्र सूर्यो भाति न चन्द्रतारकं  
नेमा विद्युतो भान्ति कुतोऽयमग्निः ।  
तमेव भान्तमनुभाति सर्वं  
तस्य भासा सर्वमिदं विभाति ॥ १५॥  
इति काठकोपनिषदि द्वितीयाध्याये द्वितीया वल्ली ॥

*na tatra sūryo bhāti na candratārakaṃ  
nemā vidyuto bhānti kuto'yamagniḥ .  
tameva bhāntamanubhāti sarvaṃ  
tasya bhāsā sarvamidaṃ vibhāti  2-2-15  
iti kāṭhakopaniṣadi dvitīyādhyāye dvitīyā vallī*

The sun does not shine there, nor do the moon and stars, nor these flashes of lightning, let alone this fire. When He shines, everything shines after Him. His light lights up all of this.

### 2.3.1

ऊर्ध्वमूलोऽवाक्शाख एषोऽश्वत्थः सनातनः ।  
तदेव शुक्रं तद्ब्रह्म तदेवामृतमुच्यते ।  
तस्मिँल्लोकाः श्रिताः सर्वे तदु नात्येति कश्चन । एतद्वै तत् ॥ १॥

*ūrdhvamūlo'vākśākha eṣo'śvatthaḥ sanātanaḥ .  
tadeva śukraṃ tadbrahma tadevāmṛtamucyate .  
tasmim̐llokāḥ śritāḥ sarve tadu nātyeti kaścana . etadvai tat  2-3-1*

This is the eternal Asvattha Tree, with its root above and its branches below. That root is called the Bright; That is Brahman, and That alone is the Immortal. All worlds are held in That, and none can go beyond. This, truly, is That.

### 2.3.2

यदिदं किं च जगत् सर्वं प्राण एजति निःसृतम् ।  
महद्भयं वज्रमुद्यतं य एतद्विदुरमृतास्ते भवन्ति ॥ २॥

*yadidaṃ kiṃ ca jagat sarvaṃ prāṇa ejati niḥsṛtam .  
mahadbhayaṃ vajramudyataṃ ya etadviduramṛtāste bhavanti  2-3-2*

All that exists, the entire universe, vibrates because it has come forth from Brahman, which is its Ground. That Brahman is a great terror, like a thunderbolt about to strike. Those who know It become immortal.

### 2.3.3

भयादस्याग्निस्तपति भयात्तपति सूर्यः ।  
भयादिन्द्रश्च वायुश्च मृत्युर्धावति पञ्चमः ॥ ३॥

*bhayādasyāgnistapati bhayāttapati sūryaḥ .  
bhayādindraśca vāyuśca mṛtyurdhāvati pañcamaḥ  2-3-3*

Out of terror of Brahman fire burns; out of terror of It the sun shines; out of terror of It Indra, Vayu, and Death, the fifth, run.

### 2.3.4

इह चेदशकद्बोद्धुं प्राक्षरीरस्य विस्रसः ।  
ततः सर्गेषु लोकेषु शरीरत्वाय कल्पते ॥ ४॥

*iha cedaśakadboddhuṃ prākṣarīrasya visrasaḥ .  
tataḥ sargeṣu lokeṣu śarīratvāya kalpate  2-3-4*

If a person can realize Brahman here, before the body falls apart, that person is liberated. If not, he takes a body again in the created worlds.

### 2.3.5

यथाऽऽदर्शे तथाऽऽत्मनि यथा स्वप्ने तथा पितृलोके ।  
यथाऽप्सु परीव ददृशे तथा गन्धर्वलोके  
छायातपयोरिव ब्रह्मलोके ॥ ५॥

*yathā''darśe tathā''tmani yathā svapne tathā pitṛloke .  
yathā'psu parīva dadṛśe tathā gandharvaloke  
chāyātapayoriva brahmaloke  2-3-5*

The buddhi shows Brahman as a mirror would, and the World of the Fathers as a dream would. The World of the Gandharvas shows It as water would, and the World of Brahma as light and shade.

### 2.3.6

इन्द्रियाणां पृथग्भावमुदयास्तमयौ च यत् ।  
पृथगुत्पद्यमानानां मत्वा धीरो न शोचति ॥ ६॥

*indriyāṇāṃ pṛthagbhāvamudayāstamayau ca yat .  
pṛthagutpadyamānānāṃ matvā dhīro na śocati  2-3-6*

Understanding that the senses arise separately, that they are distinct from Atman, and that their rising and setting belong to them alone, a wise person grieves no more.

### 2.3.7

इन्द्रियेभ्यः परं मनो मनसः सत्त्वमुत्तमम् ।  
सत्त्वादधि महानात्मा महतोऽव्यक्तमुत्तमम् ॥ ७॥

*indriyebhyaḥ paraṃ mano manasaḥ sattvamuttamam .  
sattvādadhi mahānātmā mahato'vyaktamuttamam  2-3-7*

Past the senses is the mind. Past the mind is the intellect. Higher than the intellect is the Great Atman. Higher than the Great Atman is the Unmanifest.

### 2.3.8

अव्यक्तात्तु परः पुरुषो व्यापकोऽलिङ्ग एव च ।  
यं ज्ञात्वा मुच्यते जन्तुरमृतत्वं च गच्छति ॥ ८॥

*avyaktāttu paraḥ puruṣo vyāpako'liṅga eva ca .  
yaṃ jñātvā mucyate janturamṛtatvaṃ ca gacchati  2-3-8*

Past the Unmanifest is the Person, all-pervading and imperceptible. Realizing Him, the embodied self is liberated and reaches Immortality.

### 2.3.9

न संदृशे तिष्ठति रूपमस्य  
न चक्षुषा पश्यति कश्चनैनम् ।  
हृदा मनीषा मनसाऽभिक्लृप्तो  
य एतद्विदुरमृतास्ते भवन्ति ॥ ९॥

*na saṃdṛśe tiṣṭhati rūpamasya  
na cakṣuṣā paśyati kaścanainam .  
hṛdā manīṣā manasā'bhiklṛpto  
ya etadviduramṛtāste bhavanti  2-3-9*

His form is not something that can be seen; no one looks on Him with the eye. He can be known when revealed by an intellect free of doubt and by constant meditation. Those who know this become immortal.

### 2.3.10

यदा पञ्चावतिष्ठन्ते ज्ञानानि मनसा सह ।  
बुद्धिश्च न विचेष्टते तामाहुः परमां गतिम् ॥ १०॥

*yadā pañcāvatiṣṭhante jñānāni manasā saha .  
buddhiśca na viceṣṭate tāmāhuḥ paramāṃ gatim  2-3-10*

When the five instruments of knowledge come to rest together with the mind, and the intellect stops moving, that is called the Supreme State.

### 2.3.11

तां योगमिति मन्यन्ते स्थिरामिन्द्रियधारणाम् ।  
अप्रमत्तस्तदा भवति योगो हि प्रभवाप्ययौ ॥ ११॥

*tāṃ yogamiti manyante sthirāmindriyadhāraṇām .  
apramattastadā bhavati yogo hi prabhavāpyayau  2-3-11*

This firm control of the senses is what is called yoga. One must then stay watchful, for yoga can both help and harm.

### 2.3.12

नैव वाचा न मनसा प्राप्तुं शक्यो न चक्षुषा ।  
अस्तीति ब्रुवतोऽन्यत्र कथं तदुपलभ्यते ॥ १२॥

*naiva vācā na manasā prāptuṃ śakyo na cakṣuṣā .  
astīti bruvato'nyatra kathaṃ tadupalabhyate  2-3-12*

Atman cannot be reached by speech, by the mind, or by the eye. How can It be realized except through the affirmation of one who says, "He is"?

### 2.3.13

अस्तीत्येवोपलब्धव्यस्तत्त्वभावेन चोभयोः ।  
अस्तीत्येवोपलब्धस्य तत्त्वभावः प्रसीदति ॥ १३॥

*astītyevopalabdha vyastattvabhāvena cobhayoḥ .  
astītyevopalabdhasya tattvabhāvaḥ prasīdati  2-3-13*

He should be realized first as Existence limited by upadhis, and then in His true transcendental nature. Of these two aspects, Atman realized as Existence leads the knower to the realization of His true nature.

### 2.3.14

यदा सर्वे प्रमुच्यन्ते कामा येऽस्य हृदि श्रिताः ।  
अथ मर्त्योऽमृतो भवत्यत्र ब्रह्म समश्नुते ॥ १४॥

*yadā sarve pramucyante kāmā ye'sya hṛdi śritāḥ .  
atha martyo'mṛto bhavatyatra brahma samaśnute  2-3-14*

When every desire living in the heart falls away, the mortal becomes immortal and attains Brahman here.

### 2.3.15

यदा सर्वे प्रभिद्यन्ते हृदयस्येह ग्रन्थयः ।  
अथ मर्त्योऽमृतो भवत्येतावद्ध्यनुशासनम् ॥ १५॥

*yadā sarve prabhidyante hṛdayasyeha granthayaḥ .  
atha martyo'mṛto bhavatyetāvaddhyanuśāsanam  2-3-15*

When every tie of the heart is cut here on earth, the mortal becomes immortal. That is the whole of the teaching.

### 2.3.16

शतं चैका च हृदयस्य नाड्य-  
स्तासां मूर्धानमभिनिःसृतैका ।  
तयोर्ध्वमायन्नमृतत्वमेति  
विष्वङ्ङन्या उत्क्रमणे भवन्ति ॥ १६॥

*śataṃ caikā ca hṛdayasya nāḍya-  
stāsāṃ mūrdhānamabhiniḥsṛtaikā .  
tayordhvamāyannamṛtatvameti  
viṣvaṅṅanyā utkramaṇe bhavanti  2-3-16*

The heart has one hundred and one arteries, and one of them passes through the crown of the head. Rising through it at death, a person reaches immortality. But when his prana leaves by the other arteries, going off in different directions, he is born again in the world.

### 2.3.17

अङ्गुष्ठमात्रः पुरुषोऽन्तरात्मा  
सदा जनानां हृदये संनिविष्टः ।  
तं स्वाच्छरीरात्प्रवृहेन्मुञ्जादिवेषीकां धैर्येण ।  
तं विद्याच्छुक्रममृतं तं विद्याच्छुक्रममृतमिति ॥ १७॥

*aṅguṣṭhamātraḥ puruṣo'ntarātmā  
sadā janānāṃ hṛdaye saṃniviṣṭaḥ .  
taṃ svāccharīrātpravṛhenmuñjādiveṣīkāṃ dhairyeṇa .  
taṃ vidyācchukramamṛtaṃ taṃ vidyācchukramamṛtamiti  2-3-17*

The Purusha, no bigger than a thumb, the inner Self, lives always in human hearts. Let a person steadily draw Him out from the body, as one draws the tender stalk from a blade of grass. Let him know that Self as the Bright, as the Immortal; yes, as the Bright, as the Immortal.

### 2.3.18

मृत्युप्रोक्तां नचिकेतोऽथ लब्ध्वा  
विद्यामेतां योगविधिं च कृत्स्नम् ।  
ब्रह्मप्राप्तो विरजोऽभूद्विमृत्यु-  
रन्योऽप्येवं यो विदध्यात्ममेव ॥ १८॥

*mṛtyuproktāṃ naciketo'tha labdhvā  
vidyāmetāṃ yogavidhiṃ ca kṛtsnam .  
brahmaprāpto virajo'bhūdvimṛtyu-  
ranyo'pyevaṃ yo vidadhyātmameva  2-3-18*

After receiving this wisdom from the King of Death along with the entire process of yoga, Nachiketa became free of impurity and death and attained Brahman. So it will be for anyone else who knows the innermost Self in this way.

### 2.3.19

ॐ सह नाववतु । सह नौ भुनक्तु । सहवीर्यं करवावहै ।  
तेजस्विनावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै ॥ १९॥  
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥  
इति काठकोपनिषदि द्वितीयाध्याये तृतीया वल्ली ॥

*om saha nāvavatu . saha nau bhunaktu . saha vīryaṃ karavāvahai .  
tejasvināvadhītamastu mā vidviṣāvahai  
oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ  
iti kāṭhakopaniṣadi dvitīyādhyāye tṛtīyā vallī*

Peace Chant:
Om. May Brahman guard the two of us! May Brahman grant us both the fruit of Knowledge! May we both gain the strength to acquire Knowledge! May our shared study reveal the Truth! May neither of us bear ill will toward the other! Om. Peace! Peace! Peace!
