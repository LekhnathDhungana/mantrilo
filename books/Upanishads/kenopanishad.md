# Kenopanishad

### 1.0

केनोपनिषत्  
ॐ आप्यायन्तु ममाङ्गानि वाक्प्राणश्चक्षुः  
श्रोत्रमथो बलमिन्द्रियाणि च सर्वाणि ।  
सर्वं ब्रह्मौपनिषदं  
माऽहं ब्रह्म निराकुर्यां मा मा ब्रह्म  
निराकरोदनिराकरणमस्त्वनिराकरणं मेऽस्तु ।  
तदात्मनि निरते य  
उपनिषत्सु धर्मास्ते मयि सन्तु ते मयि सन्तु ।  
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

*Kenopaniṣat  
oṃ āpyāyantu mamāṅgāni vākprāṇaścakṣuḥ  
śrotramatho balamindriyāṇi ca sarvāṇi .  
sarvaṃ brahmaupaniṣadaṃ  
mā'haṃ brahma nirākuryāṃ mā mā brahma  
nirākarodanirākaraṇamastvanirākaraṇaṃ me'stu .  
tadātmani nirate ya  
upaniṣatsu dharmāste mayi santu te mayi santu .  
oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ ..*

May my limbs, speech, prana (vital air), eye, ear, and the strength of all my senses become vigorous. Everything is the Brahman of the Upanisads. May I never deny Brahman.
May Brahman never reject me. May there be no denial of Brahman. May there be no rejection by Brahman. May all the virtues declared by the Upanisads rest in me, who delight in the Atman! May they rest in me!
Om Peace! Peace!! Peace!!!

### 1.1

ॐ केनेषितं पतति प्रेषितं मनः  
केन प्राणः प्रथमः प्रैति युक्तः ।  
केनेषितां वाचमिमां वदन्ति  
चक्षुः श्रोत्रं क उ देवो युनक्ति ॥ १॥

*oṃ keneṣitaṃ patati preṣitaṃ manaḥ  
kena prāṇaḥ prathamaḥ praiti yuktaḥ .  
keneṣitāṃ vācamimāṃ vadanti  
cakṣuḥ śrotraṃ ka u devo yunakti .. 1..*

The disciple asked: Om. Whose will sends the mind toward its object? At whose command does the prana, the foremost, carry out its work? By whose will do people speak? What god directs the eyes and ears?

### 1.2

श्रोत्रस्य श्रोत्रं मनसो मनो यद्  
वाचो ह वाचं स उ प्राणस्य प्राणः ।  
चक्षुषश्चक्षुरतिमुच्य धीराः  
प्रेत्यास्माल्लोकादमृता भवन्ति ॥ २॥

*śrotrasya śrotraṃ manaso mano yad  
vāco ha vācaṃ sa u prāṇasya prāṇaḥ .  
cakṣuṣaścakṣuratimucya dhīrāḥ  
pretyāsmāllokādamṛtā bhavanti .. 2..*

The teacher replied: It is the "ear" of the ear, the "mind" of the mind, the "tongue" of the tongue (the "speech" of speech), and likewise the "life" of life and the "eye" of the eye. Giving up the sense of Self, or "I"-ness, in these and rising beyond the life of the senses, the wise become immortal.

### 1.3

न तत्र चक्षुर्गच्छति न वाग्गच्छति नो मनः ।  
न विद्मो न विजानीमो यथैतदनुशिष्यात् ॥ ३॥

*na tatra cakṣurgacchati na vāggacchati no manaḥ .  
na vidmo na vijānīmo yathaitadanuśiṣyāt .. 3..*

Neither the eye reaches there, nor speech, nor the mind. We do not know That, and we do not know how anyone could be taught about That.

### 1.4

अन्यदेव तद्विदितादथो अविदितादधि ।  
इति शुश्रुम पूर्वेषां ये नस्तद्व्याचचक्षिरे ॥ ४॥

*anyadeva tadviditādatho aviditādadhi .  
iti śuśruma pūrveṣāṃ ye nastadvyācacakṣire .. 4..*

It is other than the known, and It is above the unknown. This we have heard from the teachers of old who explained It to us.

### 1.5

यद्वाचाऽनभ्युदितं येन वागभ्युद्यते ।  
तदेव ब्रह्म त्वं विद्धि नेदं यदिदमुपासते ॥ ५॥

*yadvācā'nabhyuditaṃ yena vāgabhyudyate .  
tadeva brahma tvaṃ viddhi nedaṃ yadidamupāsate .. 5..*

What speech cannot express, but what gives expression to speech: know That alone as Brahman, and not what people here worship.

### 1.6

यन्मनसा न मनुते येनाहुर्मनो मतम् ।  
तदेव ब्रह्म त्वं विद्धि नेदं यदिदमुपासते ॥ ६॥

*yanmanasā na manute yenāhurmano matam .  
tadeva brahma tvaṃ viddhi nedaṃ yadidamupāsate .. 6..*

What the mind cannot grasp, but by which, it is said, the mind itself is grasped: know That alone as Brahman, and not what people here worship.

### 1.7

यच्चक्षुषा न पश्यति येन चक्षूँषि पश्यति ।  
तदेव ब्रह्म त्वं विद्धि नेदं यदिदमुपासते ॥ ७॥

*yaccakṣuṣā na paśyati yena cakṣūm̐ṣi paśyati .  
tadeva brahma tvaṃ viddhi nedaṃ yadidamupāsate .. 7..*

What the eye cannot see, but by which the eyes are able to see (by which sight is perceived): know That alone as Brahman, and not what people here worship.

### 1.8

यच्छ्रोत्रेण न शृणोति येन श्रोत्रमिदं श्रुतम् ।  
तदेव ब्रह्म त्वं विद्धि नेदं यदिदमुपासते ॥ ८॥

*yacchrotreṇa na śṛṇoti yena śrotramidaṃ śrutam .  
tadeva brahma tvaṃ viddhi nedaṃ yadidamupāsate .. 8..*

What the ear cannot hear, but by which the ears are able to hear (by which hearing is perceived): know That alone as Brahman, and not what people here worship.

### 1.9

यत्प्राणेन न प्राणिति येन प्राणः प्रणीयते ।  
तदेव ब्रह्म त्वं विद्धि नेदं यदिदमुपासते ॥ ९॥  
॥ इति केनोपनिषदि प्रथमः खण्डः ॥

*yatprāṇena na prāṇiti yena prāṇaḥ praṇīyate .  
tadeva brahma tvaṃ viddhi nedaṃ yadidamupāsate .. 9..  
.. iti kenopaniṣadi prathamaḥ khaṇḍaḥ ..*

What no one breathes with the breath, but by which breath itself is breathed: know That to be Brahman, and not this that people worship here.

### 2.1

यदि मन्यसे सुवेदेति दहरमेवापि var दभ्रमेवापि  
नूनं त्वं वेत्थ ब्रह्मणो रूपम् ।  
यदस्य त्वं यदस्य देवेष्वथ नु  
मीमाँस्यमेव ते मन्ये विदितम् ॥ १॥

*yadi manyase suvedeti daharamevāpi var dabhramevāpi  
nūnaṃ tvaṃ vettha brahmaṇo rūpam .  
yadasya tvaṃ yadasya deveṣvatha nu  
mīmām̐syameva te manye viditam .. 1..*

The teacher said: If you suppose, "I know Brahman well," then you surely know very little of Its form; you know only Its form as limited by humans or by the gods. So Brahman is still something you should inquire into.

### 2.2

नाहं मन्ये सुवेदेति नो न वेदेति वेद च ।  
यो नस्तद्वेद तद्वेद नो न वेदेति वेद च ॥ २॥

*nāhaṃ manye suvedeti no na vedeti veda ca .  
yo nastadveda tadveda no na vedeti veda ca .. 2..*

The disciple said: I think I know Brahman. I do not suppose that I know It well, nor do I suppose that I do not know It. Whoever among us understands the meaning of "Neither do I not know, nor do I know" knows Brahman.

### 2.3

यस्यामतं तस्य मतं मतं यस्य न वेद सः ।  
अविज्ञातं विजानतां विज्ञातमविजानताम् ॥ ३॥

*yasyāmataṃ tasya mataṃ mataṃ yasya na veda saḥ .  
avijñātaṃ vijānatāṃ vijñātamavijānatām .. 3..*

The one who does not claim to know Brahman knows It; the one who claims to know It does not. It is unknown to those who think they know It, and known to those who think they do not.

### 2.4

प्रतिबोधविदितं मतममृतत्वं हि विन्दते ।  
आत्मना विन्दते वीर्यं विद्यया विन्दतेऽमृतम् ॥ ४॥

*pratibodhaviditaṃ matamamṛtatvaṃ hi vindate .  
ātmanā vindate vīryaṃ vidyayā vindate'mṛtam .. 4..*

Brahman is known when It is realized in every state of mind, for through such Knowledge one reaches Immortality. Through Atman one gains strength; through Knowledge, Immortality.

### 2.5

इह चेदवेदीदथ सत्यमस्ति  
न चेदिहावेदीन्महती विनष्टिः ।  
भूतेषु भूतेषु विचित्य धीराः  
प्रेत्यास्माल्लोकादमृता भवन्ति ॥ ५॥  
॥ इति केनोपनिषदि द्वितीयः खण्डः ॥

*iha cedavedīdatha satyamasti  
na cedihāvedīnmahatī vinaṣṭiḥ .  
bhūteṣu bhūteṣu vicitya dhīrāḥ  
pretyāsmāllokādamṛtā bhavanti .. 5..  
.. iti kenopaniṣadi dvitīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ ..*

If a person knows Atman here, he reaches the true goal of life. If he does not know It here, great ruin awaits him. Having realized the Self in every being, the wise leave the world behind and become immortal.

### 3.1

ब्रह्म ह देवेभ्यो विजिग्ये तस्य ह ब्रह्मणो  
विजये देवा अमहीयन्त ॥ १॥

*brahma ha devebhyo vijigye tasya ha brahmaṇo  
vijaye devā amahīyanta .. 1..*

The story goes that Brahman won a victory for the gods, and through this victory of Brahman the gods grew exultant. They told themselves, "This victory is truly ours. This glory is ours alone."

### 3.2

त ऐक्षन्तास्माकमेवायं विजयोऽस्माकमेवायं महिमेति ।  
तद्धैषां विजज्ञौ तेभ्यो ह प्रादुर्बभूव तन्न व्यजानत  
किमिदं यक्षमिति ॥ २॥

*ta aikṣantāsmākamevāyaṃ vijayo'smākamevāyaṃ mahimeti .  
taddhaiṣāṃ vijajñau tebhyo ha prādurbabhūva tanna vyajānata  
kimidaṃ yakṣamiti .. 2..*

Brahman, of course, understood all this and appeared before them. But they did not recognize who that adorable Spirit was.

### 3.3

तेऽग्निमब्रुवञ्जातवेद एतद्विजानीहि  
किमिदं यक्षमिति तथेति ॥ ३॥

*te'gnimabruvañjātaveda etadvijānīhi  
kimidaṃ yakṣamiti tatheti .. 3..*

They spoke to Agni: "O Jātaveda, please find out who this yaksha is." "Yes," said Agni.

### 3.4

तदभ्यद्रवत्तमभ्यवदत्कोऽसीत्यग्निर्वा  
अहमस्मीत्यब्रवीज्जातवेदा वा अहमस्मीति ॥ ४॥

*tadabhyadravattamabhyavadatko'sītyagnirvā  
ahamasmītyabravījjātavedā vā ahamasmīti .. 4..*

Agni hurried to the yaksha, who asked who he was. Agni answered, "I am indeed Agni. I am also called Jātaveda (nearly all-knowing)."

### 3.5

तस्मिꣳस्त्वयि किं वीर्यमित्यपीदꣳ सर्वं  
दहेयं यदिदं पृथिव्यामिति ॥ ५॥

*tasmigͫstvayi kiṃ vīryamityapīdagͫ sarvaṃ  
daheyaṃ yadidaṃ pṛthivyāmiti .. 5..*

"And what power do you have, you who are so renowned?" asked the yaksha. "I can burn up everything there is on this earth," Agni answered.

### 3.6

तस्मै तृणं निदधावेतद्दहेति ।  
तदुपप्रेयाय सर्वजवेन तन्न शशाक दग्धुं स तत एव  
निववृते नैतदशकं विज्ञातुं यदेतद्यक्षमिति ॥ ६॥

*tasmai tṛṇaṃ nidadhāvetaddaheti .  
tadupapreyāya sarvajavena tanna śaśāka dagdhuṃ sa tata eva  
nivavṛte naitadaśakaṃ vijñātuṃ yadetadyakṣamiti .. 6..*

The yaksha set a straw in front of him and said, "Burn this!" Agni rushed at it with all his speed but could not burn it. So he left and went back to the gods, saying, "I could not find out who the yaksha was."

### 3.7

अथ वायुमब्रुवन्वायवेतद्विजानीहि  
किमेतद्यक्षमिति तथेति ॥ ७॥

*atha vāyumabruvanvāyavetadvijānīhi  
kimetadyakṣamiti tatheti .. 7..*

They then spoke to Vāyu: "O Vāyu, please find out who this yaksha is." "Yes," said Vāyu.

### 3.8

तदभ्यद्रवत्तमभ्यवदत्कोऽसीति वायुर्वा  
अहमस्मीत्यब्रवीन्मातरिश्वा वा अहमस्मीति ॥ ८॥

*tadabhyadravattamabhyavadatko'sīti vāyurvā  
ahamasmītyabravīnmātariśvā vā ahamasmīti .. 8..*

Vāyu hurried to the yaksha, who asked who he was. Vāyu answered, "I am indeed Vāyu. I am also called Mātarishvā (the one who courses through the atmosphere)."

### 3.9

तस्मिँस्त्वयि किं वीर्यमित्यपीदँ  
सर्वमाददीय यदिदं पृथिव्यामिति ॥ ९॥

*tasmim̐stvayi kiṃ vīryamityapīdam̐  
sarvamādadīya yadidaṃ pṛthivyāmiti .. 9..*

"And what power do you have, you who are so renowned?" asked the yaksha. "I can truly blow away everything there is on this earth," Vāyu answered.

### 3.10

तस्मै तृणं निदधावेतदादत्स्वेति  
तदुपप्रेयाय सर्वजवेन तन्न शशाकादातुं स तत एव  
निववृते नैतदशकं विज्ञातुं यदेतद्यक्षमिति ॥ १०॥

*tasmai tṛṇaṃ nidadhāvetadādatsveti  
tadupapreyāya sarvajavena tanna śaśākādātuṃ sa tata eva  
nivavṛte naitadaśakaṃ vijñātuṃ yadetadyakṣamiti .. 10..*

The yaksha set a straw in front of him and said, "Blow this away!" Vāyu rushed at it with all his speed but could not blow it away. So he left and went back to the gods, saying, "I could not find out who the yaksha was."

### 3.11

अथेन्द्रमब्रुवन्मघवन्नेतद्विजानीहि किमेतद्यक्षमिति तथेति  
तदभ्यद्रवत्तस्मात्तिरोदधे ॥ ११॥

*athendramabruvanmaghavannetadvijānīhi kimetadyakṣamiti tatheti  
tadabhyadravattasmāttirodadhe .. 11..*

The gods then spoke to Indra: "O Maghavan, please find out who this yaksha is." "Yes," said Indra, and he hurried to the yaksha. But the yaksha vanished from his sight.

### 3.12

स तस्मिन्नेवाकाशे स्त्रियमाजगाम बहुशोभमानामुमाँ  
हैमवतीं ताँहोवाच किमेतद्यक्षमिति ॥ १२॥  
॥ इति केनोपनिषदि तृतीयः खण्डः ॥

*sa tasminnevākāśe striyamājagāma bahuśobhamānāmumām̐  
haimavatīṃ tām̐hovāca kimetadyakṣamiti .. 12..  
.. iti kenopaniṣadi tṛtīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ ..*

In that very place Indra saw a woman, the marvelously radiant Umā, daughter of the snow-covered mountain Himavat. He asked her, "Who could this yaksha be?"

### 4.1

सा ब्रह्मेति होवाच ब्रह्मणो वा एतद्विजये महीयध्वमिति  
ततो हैव विदाञ्चकार ब्रह्मेति ॥ १॥

*sā brahmeti hovāca brahmaṇo vā etadvijaye mahīyadhvamiti  
tato haiva vidāñcakāra brahmeti .. 1..*

"That yaksha was Brahman," she said. "It was through the victory of Brahman that you gained this glory." From Umā's words Indra understood that the yaksha was Brahman.

### 4.2

तस्माद्वा एते देवा अतितरामिवान्यान्देवान्यदग्निर्वायुरिन्द्रस्ते  
ह्येनन्नेदिष्ठं पस्पर्शुस्ते ह्येनत्प्रथमो विदाञ्चकार ब्रह्मेति ॥ २॥

*tasmādvā ete devā atitarāmivānyāndevānyadagnirvāyurindraste  
hyenannediṣṭhaṃ pasparśuste hyenatprathamo vidāñcakāra brahmeti .. 2..*

That is why these gods, Agni, Vāyu, and Indra, surpass the other gods: they came closest to the yaksha and were the first to know Him as Brahman.

### 4.3

तस्माद्वा इन्द्रोऽतितरामिवान्यान्देवान्स  
ह्येनन्नेदिष्ठं पस्पर्श स ह्येनत्प्रथमो विदाञ्चकार ब्रह्मेति ॥ ३॥

*tasmādvā indro'titarāmivānyāndevānsa  
hyenannediṣṭhaṃ pasparśa sa hyenatprathamo vidāñcakāra brahmeti .. 3..*

And that is why Indra surpasses the other gods: he came closest to the yaksha, and he was the first to know Him as Brahman.

### 4.4

तस्यैष आदेशो यदेतद्विद्युतो व्यद्युतदा३  
इतीन् न्यमीमिषदा३ इत्यधिदैवतम् ॥ ४॥

*tasyaiṣa ādeśo yadetadvidyuto vyadyutadā  
itīn nyamīmiṣadā3 ityadhidaivatam .. 4..*

This is the teaching about That (Brahman): It is like a flash of lightning; It is like the blink of an eye. This refers to the ādihidaivatam (Its aspect as cosmic manifestation).

### 4.5

अथाध्यात्मं यदेतद्गच्छतीव च मनोऽनेन  
चैतदुपस्मरत्यभीक्ष्णँ सङ्कल्पः ॥ ५॥

*athādhyātmaṃ yadetadgacchatīva ca mano'nena  
caitadupasmaratyabhīkṣṇam̐ saṅkalpaḥ .. 5..*

Now for the description from the standpoint of His manifestation as Ᾱtman within the body: "as one thinks of Brahman with the mind, and as swiftly as the mind wills."

### 4.6

तद्ध तद्वनं नाम तद्वनमित्युपासितव्यं स य एतदेवं वेदाभि  
हैनꣳ सर्वाणि भूतानि संवाञ्छन्ति ॥ ६॥

*taddha tadvanaṃ nāma tadvanamityupāsitavyaṃ sa ya etadevaṃ vedābhi  
hainagͫ sarvāṇi bhūtāni saṃvāñchanti .. 6..*

That Brahman is called Tadvana, the One adored by all. It should be worshipped under the name Tadvana. All beings love the one who knows Brahman in this way.

### 4.7

उपनिषदं भो ब्रूहीत्युक्ता त उपनिषद्ब्राह्मीं वाव त  
उपनिषदमब्रूमेति ॥ ७॥

*upaniṣadaṃ bho brūhītyuktā ta upaniṣadbrāhmīṃ vāva ta  
upaniṣadamabrūmeti .. 7..*

"Sir, teach me the saving knowledge of the Upanishad." "The saving knowledge of the Upanishad has been given to you. We have truly given you the saving knowledge of Brahman."

### 4.8

तस्यै तपो दमः कर्मेति प्रतिष्ठा वेदाः सर्वाङ्गानि  
सत्यमायतनम् ॥ ८॥

*tasyai tapo damaḥ karmeti pratiṣṭhā vedāḥ sarvāṅgāni  
satyamāyatanam .. 8..*

Austerity, self-restraint, and dedicated work are the foundations of It, the saving knowledge of the Upaniṣads. The Vedas are its limbs, and Truth is its home.

### 4.9

यो वा एतामेवं वेदापहत्य पाप्मानमनन्ते स्वर्गे  
लोके ज्येये प्रतितिष्ठति प्रतितिष्ठति ॥ ९॥  
॥ इति केनोपनिषदि चतुर्थः खण्डः ॥

*yo vā etāmevaṃ vedāpahatya pāpmānamanante svarge  
loke jyeye pratitiṣṭhati pratitiṣṭhati .. 9..  
.. iti kenopaniṣadi caturthaḥ khaṇḍaḥ ..*

Whoever realizes this knowledge of Brahman in this way destroys sin and becomes firmly established in Brahman, the infinite, the blissful, the highest.

### 4.10

ॐ आप्यायन्तु ममाङ्गानि वाक्प्राणश्चक्षुः  
श्रोत्रमथो बलमिन्द्रियाणि च सर्वाणि ।  
सर्वं ब्रह्मौपनिषदं  
माऽहं ब्रह्म निराकुर्यां मा मा ब्रह्म  
निराकरोदनिराकरणमस्त्वनिराकरणं मेऽस्तु ।  
तदात्मनि निरते य  
उपनिषत्सु धर्मास्ते मयि सन्तु ते मयि सन्तु ।  
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

*oṃ āpyāyantu mamāṅgāni vākprāṇaścakṣuḥ  
śrotramatho balamindriyāṇi ca sarvāṇi .  
sarvaṃ brahmaupaniṣadaṃ  
mā'haṃ brahma nirākuryāṃ mā mā brahma  
nirākarodanirākaraṇamastvanirākaraṇaṃ me'stu .  
tadātmani nirate ya  
upaniṣatsu dharmāste mayi santu te mayi santu .  
oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ ..*

Om. May Brahman protect us both, teacher and disciple! May Brahman grant us both the fruit of Knowledge! May we both gain the energy to acquire Knowledge! May what we both study reveal the Truth! May we hold no ill will toward each other!
Om. Peace! Peace! Peace!
