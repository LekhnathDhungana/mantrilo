# Upadesa Saaram

### 1

कर्तुराज्ञया प्राप्यते फलम् ।  
कर्म किं परं कर्म तज्जडम् ॥१॥

*karturājñayā prāpyate phalam .  
karma kiṁ paraṁ karma tajjaḍam ..1..*

Actions bear fruit according to the law or will of the Creator. So how can action be supreme? It cannot. Action is inert.

### 2

कृतिमहोदधौ पतनकारणम् ।  
फलमशाश्वतं गतिनिरोधकम् ॥२॥

*kṛtimahodadhau patanakāraṇam .  
phalamaśāśvataṁ gatinirodhakam ..2..*

What actions produce does not last; it fades away. Still, the seeds of those actions build up into an ocean of Karma that stands in the way of a seeker's progress.

### 3

ईश्वरार्पितं नेच्छया कृतम् ।  
चित्तशोधकं मुक्तिसाधकम् ॥३॥

*īśvarārpitaṁ necchayā kṛtam .  
cittaśodhakaṁ muktisādhakam ..3..*

When work is offered to the Almighty and carried out with no expectation of its results, it purifies the mind and in this way brings one to Liberation or Realization.

### 4

कायवाङ्मनः कार्यमुत्तमम् ।  
पूजनं जपश्चिन्तनं क्रमात् ॥४॥

*kāyavāṅmanaḥ kāryamuttamam .  
pūjanaṁ japaścintanaṁ kramāt ..4..*

Of the acts done through body, voice, and mind, namely puja (ritual worship), japa (chanting), and contemplation (inner meditation), each one ranks above the one before it.

### 5

जगत ईशधीयुक्तसेवनम् ।  
अष्टमूर्तिभृद्देवपूजनम् ॥५॥

*jagata īśadhīyuktasevanam .  
aṣṭamūrtibhṛddevapūjanam ..5..*

To worship any of the eight forms while regarding them all as forms of God is good worship (puja) of God.

### 6

उत्तमस्तवादुच्चमन्दतः ।  
चित्तजं जपध्यानमुत्तमम् ॥६॥

*uttamastavāducca mandataḥ .  
cittajaṁ japadhyānamuttamam ..6..*

Singing the Lord's praises is good. Loud japa is better than that, and soft japa is better than loud. But silent, mental japa is the best of all.

### 7

आज्यधारया स्रोतसा समम् ।  
सरलचिन्तनं विरलतः परम् ॥७॥

*ājyadhārayā srotasā samam .  
saralacintanaṁ viralataḥ param ..7..*

Meditation that runs on without a break, like a steady flow of oil or a stream of water, is better than meditation that keeps getting interrupted.

### 8

भेदभावनात्सोऽहमित्यसौ ।  
भावनाऽभिदा पावनी मता ॥८॥

*bhedabhāvanātso'hamityasau .  
bhāvanā'bhidā pāvanī matā ..8..*

Meditating without duality, as 'I am HE,' ranks above any meditation that supposes a separation between the Bhakta (devotee) and the beloved Lord.

### 9

भावशून्यसद्भावसुस्थितिः ।  
भावनाबलाद्भक्तिरुत्तमा ॥९॥

*bhāvaśūnyasadbhāvasusthitiḥ .  
bhāvanābalādbhaktiruttamā ..9..*

Step by step one reaches a state of void with no thought in it, and abiding in the background state of 'I AM' becomes complete. This is supreme devotion.

### 10

हृत्स्थले मनः स्वस्थता क्रिया ।  
भक्तियोगबोधाश्च निश्चितम् ॥१०॥

*hṛtsthale manaḥ svasthatā kriyā .  
bhaktiyogabodhāśca niścitam ..10..*

To settle the mind in the Heart (the Source) is true Karma (action), Bhakti (devotion), Yoga (action), and Jnana (knowledge).

### 11

वायुरोधनाल्लीयते मनः ।  
जालपक्षिवद्रोधसाधनम् ॥११॥

*Vāyurodhanāllīyate manaḥ .  
Jālapakṣiva drodha sādhanam ..11..*

Breath-control holds the movement of the mind in check, just as a net holds back a bird caught in it.

### 12

चित्तवायवश्चित्क्रियायुताः ।  
शाखयोर्द्वयी शक्तिमूलका ॥१२॥

*Cittavāyavaścitkriyāyutāḥ .  
Śākhayordvayī śaktimūlakā ..12..*

The mind and the pranas, which carry out the various thoughts and actions within the body, both spring from a single common source, the Supreme.

### 13

लयविनाशने उभयरोधने ।  
लयगतं पुनर्भवति नो मृतम् ॥१३॥

*Layavināśane ubhayarodhane .  
Layagataṃ punarbhavati no mṛtam ..13..*

The mind can be controlled in two ways: by absorption or abeyance, or by complete destruction. In the first way the mind is merely absorbed in the void and held in abeyance for a time, so it rises again. In the second way the mind is destroyed and can never rise again.

### 14

प्राणबन्धनाल्लीनमानसम् ।  
एकचिन्तनान्नाशमेत्यदः ॥१४॥

*Prāṇa bandhanāllīna mānasam .  
Eka cintanānnāśamet yadaḥ ..14..*

Once breath control has suspended the mind, the mind must then be destroyed completely through one-pointed attention on that ONE reality.

### 15

नष्टमानसोत्कृष्टयोगिनः ।  
कृत्यमस्ति किं स्वस्थितिं यतः ॥१५॥

*Naṣṭamānasotkṛṣṭayoginaḥ .  
Kṛtyamasti kiṃ svasthitiṃ yataḥ ..15..*

For an exalted Yogi whose mind is completely destroyed and who stays forever established in the Self, what action is left to do?

### 16

दृश्यवारितं चित्तमात्मनः ।  
चित्त्वदर्शनं तत्त्वदर्शनम् ॥१६॥

*Dṛśyavāritaṃ cittamātmanaḥ .  
Cittvadarśanaṃ tattvadarśanam ..16..*

Truth is revealed when attention turns away from objects and rests on the Self alone.

### 17

मानसं तु किं मार्गणे कृते ।  
नैव मानसं मार्ग आर्जवात् ॥१७॥

*Mānasaṃ tu kiṃ mārgaṇe kṛte .  
Naiva mānasaṃ mārga ārjavāt ..17..*

On inquiring 'What is the mind?', one discovers that no mind exists. This is the direct path to Reality.

### 18

वृत्तयस्त्वहं वृत्तिमाश्रिताः ।  
वृत्तयो मनो विद्ध्यहं मनः ॥१८॥

*Vṛttayastvahaṃ vṛttimāśritāḥ .  
Vṛttayo mano viddhyahaṃ manaḥ ..18..*

The mind is only a bundle of thoughts, and those thoughts rest on the I-thought alone. So the mind is simply this I-thought.

### 19

अहमयं कुतो भवति चिन्वतः ।  
अयि पतत्यहं निजविचारणम् ॥१९॥

*Ahamayaṃ kuto bhavati cinvataḥ .  
Ayi patatyahaṃ nijavicāraṇam ..19..*

Ask yourself, 'Where does this I-thought come from?' Under deep inquiry the I-thought vanishes. This is Self-Inquiry.

### 20

अहमि नाशभाज्यहमहंतया ।  
स्फुरति हृत्स्वयं परमपूर्णसत् ॥२०॥

*Ahami nāśa bhājyaham ahaṃtayā .  
Sphurati hṛtsvayaṃ paramapūrṇasat ..20..*

Once the I-thought or Ego is destroyed, the real I rises of its own accord in the spiritual Heart and shines as 'I-I' in complete fullness.

### 21

इदमहं पदाऽभिख्यमन्वहम् ।  
अहमिलीनकेऽप्यलयसत्तया ॥२१॥

*Idamahaṃ padā'bhikhya manvaham .  
Ahamilīnake'pyalayasattayā ..21..*

This unbroken, continuous 'I-I' is the real I, since the I-thought (the egoic I) disappears and dissolves in deep sleep.

### 22

विग्रहेन्द्रियप्राणधीतमः ।  
नाहमेकसत्तज्जडं ह्यसत् ॥२२॥

*Vigrahendriya prāṇadhītamaḥ .  
Nāhameka sattajjaḍaṃ hyasat ..22..*

This real 'I-I' is the sole Truth or Reality. The body, the senses, the vital air (prana), the breath, the intellect, and ignorance cannot be counted as real, for they are all gross, inert, insentient, and illusory.

### 23

सत्त्वभासिका चित्क्ववेतरा ।  
सत्तया हि चिच्चित्तया ह्यहम् ॥२३॥

*Sattvabhāsikā citkvavetarā .  
Sattayā hi ciccittayā hyaham ..23..*

Is there anything other than Consciousness that lights up existence? In fact, existence itself is Consciousness, and Consciousness is 'I AM.'

### 24

ईशजीवयोर्वेषधीभिदा ।  
सत्स्वभावतो वस्तु केवलम् ॥२४॥

*Īśajīvayorveṣadhībhidā .  
Satsvabhāvato vastu kevalam ..24..*

The jiva (the individual) and Ishwara (Unity) differ only in their equipment (the gross and subtle bodies). From the standpoint of True Nature, the Absolute Reality alone exists.

### 25

वेषहानतः स्वात्मदर्शनम् ।  
ईशदर्शनं स्वात्मरूपतः ॥२५॥

*Veṣahānataḥ svātmadarśanam .  
Īśadarśanaṃ svātmarūpataḥ ..25..*

When the many conditionings of body and mind and the identifications with name and form fall away, the Self is realized. Seeing the Lord as the Self is true Self-Realization.

### 26

आत्मसंस्थितिः स्वात्मदर्शनम् ।  
आत्मनिर्द्वयादात्मनिष्ठता ॥२६॥

*Ātmasaṃsthitiḥ svātmadarśanam .  
Ātma nirdvayādātmaniṣṭhatā ..26..*

Because the Self is non-dual, being established in the Self alone is the seeing of the Self, and that alone is abiding in the Self.

### 27

ज्ञानवर्जिताऽज्ञानहीनचित् ।  
ज्ञानमस्ति किं ज्ञातुमन्तरम् ॥२७॥

*Jñānavarjitā'jñānahīnacit .  
Jñānamasti kiṃ jñātumantaram ..27..*

Consciousness goes beyond all dualities and holds neither the thought of knowledge nor the thought of ignorance. To know the Self, is there any knowledge besides Self-Awareness itself?

### 28

किं स्वरूपमित्यात्मदर्शने ।  
अव्ययाऽभवाऽऽपूर्णचित्सुखम् ॥२८॥

*Kiṃ svarūpamityātmadarśane .  
Avyayā'bhavā''pūrṇacitsukham ..28..*

'What is the nature of Me, the Self?' By inquiring in this way, one realizes the Self as Indestructible, Unborn, Perfect, and of the nature of Consciousness and bliss.

### 29

बन्धमुक्त्यतीतं परं सुखम् ।  
विन्दतीह जीवस्तु दैविकः ॥२९॥

*Bandha muktyatītaṃ paraṃ sukham .  
Vindatīha jīvastu daivikaḥ ..29..*

Whoever has realized the Divine State, his own real nature, gains supreme happiness and bliss, beyond bondage and freedom, here in this very world.

### 30

अहमपेतकं निजविभानकम् ।  
महदिदंतपो रमणवागियम् ॥३०॥

*Ahamapetakaṃ nijavibhānakam .  
Mahadidaṃtapo ramaṇavāgiyam ..30..*

Self-Inquiry free of ego is a great penance. Realize this truth as voiced by Sri Ramana.
