## Charges and renewal

Mantrilo paid self-service plans are monthly subscriptions. Before you confirm a purchase, Stripe Checkout shows the applicable price, currency, billing interval, and taxes where applicable. The subscription renews and charges the payment method on file at the end of each billing period unless you cancel. If a promotion changes the initial charge, review the recurring price and when it takes effect before confirming checkout.

Starter and Growth have INR prices for eligible India workspaces. Contact billing support to confirm eligibility or request an India Business quote. Stripe collects the billing address for INR checkout and may ask your bank to authenticate the payment and authorize recurring charges. See the instructions Stripe and your bank provide when you subscribe.

## Managing or cancelling your subscription

If **Manage subscription & invoices** appears under **Usage & billing** in the Developer Console, open it and review the cancellation date in Stripe before confirming. You can also request cancellation through [verified billing contact]. Include the account email or workspace ID and ask for confirmation of the effective date. Contact billing support for an INR plan change.

Deleting your account in **Settings** is a separate action. It cancels a qualifying active subscription immediately and removes the workspace and API keys. To keep the workspace, use the billing portal to cancel.
