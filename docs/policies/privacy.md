This policy covers the Mantrilo website, Developer Console, and hosted API. If a developer submits personal information on behalf of their own users, the developer's privacy notice also applies to its collection and use of that information.

## Accounts and subscriptions

We use your email address, or phone number where phone sign-in is offered, to authenticate you and send account messages. We use your name, workspace name, and alert preference to set up and administer your workspace. Firebase Authentication handles sign-in and account emails. If email codes are enabled, Resend delivers those codes.

Stripe processes payments. We provide Stripe with a workspace identifier and an email address when available. Enter payment details and any required billing address in Stripe Checkout. Mantrilo uses billing references and subscription status to manage access, renewals, cancellations, and support. For India pricing, we use the billing country to determine eligibility. See [Stripe's privacy notice](https://stripe.com/privacy) for information on its processing.

## API usage and astrology

We use workspace and key identifiers, operation names, timestamps, statuses, and request or audio usage counts to enforce limits, show usage, and troubleshoot. Our hosting and security systems may process request metadata such as IP addresses and URLs.

Astrology operations can use birth dates and times, time zones, coordinates, or place queries. Compatibility calculations can use two birth profiles. We use those fields to perform the requested operation. Our application usage records omit astrology inputs and results. Some operations send inputs to FreeAstroAPI for calculation or place search. Its processing and retention may differ from our application usage records. If you integrate the API into your product, inform your users about this processing and send the fields the operation calls for. Use synthetic birth details while developing and testing.

## Website and browser storage

The contact form prepares a draft in your browser. If you send it by email, we use the information you share to respond. The site remembers your display theme in browser storage and may keep a broad referral category for the current tab. Links to certain Console pages include the public page path and referral category so we can measure Console visits and signup starts. The Console uses cookies for session and request security.

## Service providers

We use Google Cloud and Firebase to host the service, authenticate users, and store workspace data. We use Stripe for subscriptions and payments and FreeAstroAPI for some astrology operations. When email code sign-in is enabled, Resend delivers the code. See the [service provider list](subprocessors.md) for the types of information each provider handles.

## Retention, deletion, and requests

In the Developer Console, open **Settings → Delete account** to permanently remove a standard customer account. This removes the active workspace, its API keys and stored workspace usage, and the sign-in account. Integrations using those keys stop working. Account deletion also cancels a qualifying active subscription immediately. Use **Usage & billing** if you want to cancel your subscription and retain your account.
