Mantrilo acts as processor or subprocessor for that processing. Mantrilo's Privacy Policy describes its separate handling of account, security, and billing data.

## Instructions and use

Mantrilo will process covered data on the customer's documented instructions, including the service agreement and permitted API requests, to provide, support, and secure the requested service. Mantrilo will inform the customer if it believes an instruction violates applicable data protection law. If a law requires processing outside the customer's instructions, Mantrilo will notify the customer before processing where the law permits notice.

The customer determines what data is necessary and is responsible for applicable notices, permissions, and lawful grounds for sending it. It will limit each request to the fields needed for the operation.

## Safeguards and assistance

Mantrilo will restrict access to authorized people who need it for the service and are bound by confidentiality. It will apply the technical and organizational measures in the security schedule and assist the customer with individual rights requests, security obligations, impact assessments, and legally required regulator consultations, taking account of the processing and information available.

Mantrilo will notify the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting covered data. It will provide available information about the incident and update the customer as the investigation proceeds.
